#### Sabbasaṅgāhikavāro ^2-18-1-0

Kati khandhā, kati āyatanāni, kati dhātuyo, kati saccāni, kati indriyāni, kati hetū, kati āhārā, kati phassā, kati vedanā, kati saññā, kati cetanā, kati cittāni?

## Dhammahadayavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-18|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare dhammahadayavibhaṅge pāḷiparicchedo tāva evaṃ veditabbo – ettha hi āditova khandhādīnaṃ dvādasannaṃ koṭṭhāsānaṃ vasena sabbasaṅgāhikavāro nāma vutto, dutiyo tesaṃyeva dhammānaṃ kāmadhātuādīsu uppattānuppattidassanavāro, tatiyo tattheva pariyāpannāpariyāpannadassanavāro, catuttho tīsu bhūmīsu uppattikkhaṇe vijjamānāvijjamānadhammadassanavāro, pañcamo tesaṃ dhammānaṃ bhūmantaravasena dassanavāro, chaṭṭho gatīsu uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇadassanavāro, sattamo abhiññeyyādivāro, aṭṭhamo sārammaṇānārammaṇavāro, navamo khandhādidhammānaṃ diṭṭhasutādivasena saṅgahetvā dassanavāro, dasamo kusalattikādivasena saṅgahetvā dassanavāroti evaṃ dasahi vārehi pāḷi paricchinnā. Yathāpucchaṃ vissajjanañhettha sabbaññubyākaraṇaṃ nāma; khandhādīnaṃ pana pabhedo khandhavibhaṅgādīsu vuttanayeneva veditabbo. Iminā ca vibhaṅgena aṭṭhārasahi vibhaṅgehi maṇḍitaṃ vibhaṅgapakaraṇaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ.

### Sabbasaṅgāhikavāro

[[#^toc-18-1|↑↑↑]]

Paṭhame sabbasaṅgāhikavāre khandhā, āyatanāni, dhātuyo, saccāni, indriyāni, hetū, āhārā, phassā, vedanā, saññā, cetanā, cittānīti dvādasa koṭṭhāsā pucchitvā vissajjitā. Tattha "avīcito yāva bhavaggaṃ etthantare kati khandhā"ti pucchite "ekoti vā…pe… cattāroti vā chāti vā avatvā pañcāti vattuṃ samattho añño natthī"ti attano ñāṇabalaṃ dīpento bhagavā "pañcakkhandhā"ti pucchānurūpaṃ vissajjanaṃ āha; yathāpucchaṃ vissajjanañhi sabbaññubyākaraṇaṃ nāmāti vuccati. "Dvādasāyatanāni, aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo, cattāri saccāni, bāvīsatindriyāni, nava hetū, cattāro āhārā, satta phassā, satta vedanā, satta saññā, satta cetanā, satta cittānī"tiādīsupi eseva nayo. Rūpakkhandhādīnaṃ pabhedo khandhavibhaṅgādīsu vuttanayeneva veditabbo.

### Uppattānuppattivāro

[[#^toc-18-2|↑↑↑]]

Dutiyavāre ye dhammā kāmabhave kāmadhātusambhūtānañca sattānaṃ uppajjanti – kāmadhātuyaṃ pariyāpannā vā apariyāpannā vā – te sabbe saṅgahetvā "kāmadhātuyā pañcakkhandhā"tiādi vuttaṃ; rūpadhātuādīsupi eseva nayo. Yasmā pana rūpadhātupariyāpannānaṃ sattānaṃ ghānāyatanādīnaṃ abhāvena gandhāyatanādīni āyatanādikiccaṃ na karonti, tasmā "rūpadhātuyā cha āyatanāni, nava dhātuyo"tiādi vuttaṃ. Yasmā ca okāsavasena vā sattuppattivasena vā apariyāpannadhātu nāma natthi, tasmā "apariyāpannadhātuyā"ti avatvā yaṃ yaṃ apariyāpannaṃ taṃ tadeva dassetuṃ "apariyāpanne kati khandhā"tiādi vuttaṃ.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

## Dhammahadayavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-18|↑↑↑]]

Dhammahadayavibhaṅge avīcito yāva bhavaggā sabbe dhammā khandhādīhi dvādasahi koṭṭhāsehi saṅgahetvā tesaṃ uppattānuppatti, pariyāpannāpariyāpannatā, upapattikkhaṇe pātubhāvo, gatīsu uppādakakammaṃ āyuppamāṇañca, abhiññeyyapariññeyyapahātabbabhāvetabbasacchikātabbabhāvo ca dassito, "natthi koci bhavo nicco"ti ca vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā sabbasmiṃ bhave khandhāyatanadhātumattameva passantena, dukkhasaccaṃ pariññeyyaṃ, samudayasaccaṃ pahātabbaṃ, maggasaccaṃ bhāvetabbaṃ, nirodhasaccaṃ sacchikātabbanti ñatvā, akusalahetū pajahitvā kusalahetū bhāvetvā, bhavesu nikantiṃ akarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Sabbasaṅgāhikavāro

[[#^toc-18-1|↑↑↑]]

Sabbasaṅgāhikavāre avīcito yāva bhavaggaṃ etthantare sabbe dhammā pañcakkhandhā, dvādasāyatanāni, aṭṭhārasa dhātuyotiādinā dvādasahi koṭṭhāsehi saṅgahitā; nava hetūsu ca lobho doso moho tayo akusalahetū, alobho adoso amoho tayo kusalahetū vuttā. Bhagavā hi yathāpucchaṃ vissajjanena sabbaññubyākaraṇaṃ dīpesi. Tasmā yoginā attano santāne uppajjamānaṃ yaṃ kiñci dhammaṃ "ayaṃ khandho, idaṃ āyatanaṃ, ayaṃ dhātū"ti koṭṭhāsato sallakkhetvā, tayo akusalahetū anuppādento tayo kusalahetū upasampādetvā, cakkhusamphassādike satta phasse satta vedanā satta saññā satta cetanā satta cittāni ca dhammamattato passantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Uppattānuppattivāro

Uppattānuppattivāre kāmadhātuyā nava hetū, rūpadhātuyā arūpadhātuyā ca dosavajjitā aṭṭha hetū vuttā – tattha lobho ca moho ca akusalahetū labbhantiyeva; rūpadhātupariyāpannānaṃ pana sattānaṃ ghānāyatanādīnaṃ abhāvena cha āyatanāni nava dhātuyo, arūpadhātuyā ekaṃ cittaṃ. Apariyāpanne pana akusalahetu nāma natthi, tayo kusalahetū tayo abyākatahetūti cha hetū eva. Tasmā yoginā "rūpārūpadhātūsupi lobho ca moho ca anubandhantī"ti ñatvā sukhumesupi bhavesu nikantiṃ akaronto, yattha akusalahetu natthi taṃ apariyāpannaṃ maggaphalaṃ ārabbha vīriyaṃ upasampādentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Analysis of the Heart of the Teaching ^2-18-0

#### The Section Comprising Everything ^2-18-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-978]]

How many aggregates are there, how many sense-fields, how many elements, how many truths, how many faculties, how many roots, how many nutriments, how many contacts, how many feelings, how many perceptions, how many intentions, how many minds?
There are five aggregates, twelve sense-fields, eighteen elements, four truths, twenty-two faculties, nine roots, four nutriments, seven contacts, seven feelings, seven perceptions, seven intentions, seven minds. ^2-978

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-979]]

Therein, what are the five aggregates? The aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — these are called "the five aggregates". ^2-979

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-980]]

Therein, what are the twelve sense-fields? The eye sense-field, the sight sense-field, the ear sense-field, the sound sense-field, the nose sense-field, the smell sense-field, the tongue sense-field, the taste sense-field, the body sense-field, the tangible sense-field, the mind sense-field, the mental-object sense-field — these are called "the twelve sense-fields". ^2-980

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-981]]

Therein, what are the eighteen elements? The eye element, the sight element, the eye-consciousness element, the ear element, the sound element, the ear-consciousness element, the nose element, the smell element, the nose-consciousness element, the tongue element, the taste element, the tongue-consciousness element, the body element, the tangible element, the body-consciousness element, the mind element, the mental-object element, the mind-consciousness element — these are called "the eighteen elements". ^2-981

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-982]]

Therein, what are the four truths? The truth of suffering, the truth of the origin, the truth of cessation, the truth of the path — these are called "the four truths". ^2-982

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-983]]

Therein, what are the twenty-two faculties? The eye faculty, the ear faculty, the nose faculty, the tongue faculty, the body faculty, the mind faculty, the faculty of femininity, the faculty of masculinity, the life faculty, the happiness faculty, the pain faculty, the joy faculty, the grief faculty, the equanimity faculty, the faculty of confidence, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known", the faculty of final knowledge, the faculty of one who has fully known — these are called "the twenty-two faculties". ^2-983

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-984]]

Therein, what are the nine roots? Three wholesome roots, three unwholesome roots, three neutral roots.
Therein, what are the three wholesome roots? Non-greed is a wholesome root, non-hate is a wholesome root, non-delusion is a wholesome root — these are the three wholesome roots.
Therein, what are the three unwholesome roots? Greed is an unwholesome root, hate is an unwholesome root, delusion is an unwholesome root — these are the three unwholesome roots.
Therein, what are the three neutral roots? Non-greed, non-hate, and non-delusion in the results of wholesome things or in functional neutral things — these are the three neutral roots. These are called "the nine roots". ^2-984

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-985]]

Therein, what are the four nutriments? The nutriment of solid food, the nutriment of contact, the nutriment of mental intention, the nutriment of consciousness — these are called "the four nutriments". ^2-985

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-986]]

Therein, what are the seven contacts? Eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, mind-element contact, mind-consciousness-element contact — these are called "the seven contacts". ^2-986

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-987]]

Therein, what are the seven feelings? Feeling born of eye-contact, feeling born of ear-contact, feeling born of nose-contact, feeling born of tongue-contact, feeling born of body-contact, feeling born of mind-element contact, feeling born of mind-consciousness-element contact — these are called "the seven feelings". ^2-987

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-988]]

Therein, what are the seven perceptions? Perception born of eye-contact, perception born of ear-contact, perception born of nose-contact, perception born of tongue-contact, perception born of body-contact, perception born of mind-element contact, perception born of mind-consciousness-element contact — these are called "the seven perceptions". ^2-988

Therein, what are the seven intentions? Intention born of eye-contact, intention born of ear-contact, intention born of nose-contact, intention born of tongue-contact, intention born of body-contact, intention born of mind-element contact, intention born of mind-consciousness-element contact — these are called "the seven intentions". ^2-989

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-990]]

Therein, what are the seven minds? Eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element — these are called "the seven minds". ^2-990

##### The Material-Form Element ^2-18-2-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-993]]

In the material-form element, how many aggregates are there, how many sense-fields, how many elements, how many truths, how many faculties… how many minds?
In the material-form element there are five aggregates, six sense-fields, nine elements, three truths, fourteen faculties, eight roots, three nutriments, four contacts, four feelings, four perceptions, four intentions, four minds. ^2-993

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-994]]

Therein, what are the five aggregates in the material-form element? The aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — these are called "the five aggregates in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the six sense-fields in the material-form element? The eye sense-field, the sight sense-field, the ear sense-field, the sound sense-field, the mind sense-field, the mental-object sense-field — these are called "the six sense-fields in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the nine elements in the material-form element? The eye element, the sight element, the eye-consciousness element, the ear element, the sound element, the ear-consciousness element, the mind element, the mental-object element, the mind-consciousness element — these are called "the nine elements in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the three truths in the material-form element? The truth of suffering, the truth of the origin, the truth of the path — these are called "the three truths in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the fourteen faculties in the material-form element? The eye faculty, the ear faculty, the mind faculty, the life faculty, the joy faculty, the equanimity faculty, the faculty of confidence, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known", the faculty of final knowledge, the faculty of one who has fully known — these are called "the fourteen faculties in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the eight roots in the material-form element? Three wholesome roots, two unwholesome roots, three neutral roots.
Therein, what are the three wholesome roots? Non-greed is a wholesome root, non-hate is a wholesome root, non-delusion is a wholesome root — these are the three wholesome roots.
Therein, what are the two unwholesome roots? Greed is an unwholesome root, delusion is an unwholesome root — these are the two unwholesome roots.
Therein, what are the three neutral roots? Non-greed, non-hate, and non-delusion in the results of wholesome things or in functional neutral things — these are the three neutral roots. These are called the eight roots in the material-form element.
Therein, what are the three nutriments in the material-form element? The nutriment of contact, the nutriment of mental intention, the nutriment of consciousness — these are called "the three nutriments in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the four contacts in the material-form element? Eye-contact, ear-contact, mind-element contact, mind-consciousness-element contact — these are called "the four contacts in the material-form element".
Therein, what are the four feelings in the material-form element… the four perceptions… the four intentions… the four minds? Eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element — these are called "the four minds in the material-form element". ^2-994
