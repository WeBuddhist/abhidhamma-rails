## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Jhāna)

[[#^toc-12-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe āgatanayeneva tanti ṭhapitā. Tasmā tattha kusalavipākakiriyavasena niddiṭṭhānaṃ sabbesampi jhānānaṃ cittuppādakaṇḍe vuttanayeneva attho veditabbo; suddhikanavakādibhedopi sabbo tattha vuttasadisoyevāti.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Jhāna)

[[#^toc-12-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye kusalavipākakiriyavasena sabbāni jhānāni cittuppādakaṇḍe vuttanayeneva niddiṭṭhāni, suddhikanavakādibhedopi tattha vuttasadisoyeva. Tasmā yoginā "kiriyavipākamattameta"nti anādaraṃ pahāya, kusalāni jhānāni yathābhūtaṃ sallakkhetvā, suddhikanavakādibhedaṃ yathāsattiṃ upasampādetvā, samathabhāvanāvasena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

There are four meditative absorptions: the first meditative absorption, the second meditative absorption, the third meditative absorption, the fourth meditative absorption. ^2-623

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-624]]

Therein, what is the first meditative absorption? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is the five-factored meditative absorption: thought, examination, joy, happiness, one-pointedness of mind. This is called "the first meditative absorption". The remaining things are associated with the meditative absorption.
Therein, what is the second meditative absorption? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — with the stilling of thought and examination… he enters and dwells in the second meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is the three-factored meditative absorption: joy, happiness, one-pointedness of mind. This is called "the second meditative absorption". The remaining things are associated with the meditative absorption.
Therein, what is the third meditative absorption? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — with the fading away of joy… he enters and dwells in the third meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is the two-factored meditative absorption: happiness, one-pointedness of mind. This is called "the third meditative absorption". The remaining things are associated with the meditative absorption.
Therein, what is the fourth meditative absorption? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — with the abandoning of happiness… he enters and dwells in the fourth meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is the two-factored meditative absorption: equanimity, one-pointedness of mind. This is called "the fourth meditative absorption". The remaining things are associated with the meditative absorption.

##### Fine-Material-Sphere Results ^2-12-2-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-630]]

There are four meditative absorptions: the first meditative absorption, the second meditative absorption, the third meditative absorption, the fourth meditative absorption. ^2-630

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-631]]

Therein, what is the first meditative absorption? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is contact… there is non-distraction. These things are wholesome. When, because that same fine-material-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, as its result — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is the five-factored meditative absorption: thought, examination, joy, happiness, one-pointedness of mind. This is called "the first meditative absorption". The remaining things are associated with the meditative absorption.
Therein, what is the second meditative absorption? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — with the stilling of thought and examination… he enters and dwells in the second meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is contact… there is non-distraction. These things are wholesome. When, because that same fine-material-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, as its result — with the stilling of thought and examination… the second meditative absorption… the third meditative absorption… the fourth meditative absorption… the first meditative absorption… he enters and dwells in the fifth meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is the two-factored meditative absorption: equanimity, one-pointedness of mind. This is called "the fifth meditative absorption". The remaining things are associated with the meditative absorption…. ^2-631
