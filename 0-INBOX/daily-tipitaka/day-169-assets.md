##### Rūpadhātu ^2-18-4-2-0

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Dhammadassanavāro – Bhūmantaradassanavāro

[[#^toc-18-4|↑↑↑]]

Catutthavāre kāmadhātuyā, rūpadhātuyā, asaññasattānaṃ, arūpadhātuyā ca upapattikkhaṇe vijjamānāvijjamānā dhammā dassitā. Tattha ekādasāyatanānīti saddāyatanavajjāni; tañhi ekantena paṭisandhiyaṃ nuppajjati. Iminā nayena sabbattha attho veditabbo. Sattake "devānaṃ asurāna"nti gativasena avatvā avisesena "gabbhaseyyakāna"nti vuttaṃ; tasmā yattha yattha gabbhaseyyakā sambhavanti tattha tattha tesaṃ sattāyatanāni veditabbāni, tathā dhātuyo. Sesamettha uttānatthameva. Pañcamavāre pana tesaṃyeva dhammānaṃ kāmāvacarādibhūmantaravasena pariyāpannāpariyāpannavasena ca dassanaṃ kataṃ; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ taṃ dhammasaṅgahaṭṭhakathāyaṃ vuttameva.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Dhammadassanavāro – Bhūmantaradassanavāro

[[#^toc-18-4|↑↑↑]]

Dhammadassanavāre upapattikkhaṇe sabbesaṃ ekaṃ dukkhasaccameva pātubhavati; saddāyatanaṃ ekantena paṭisandhiyaṃ nuppajjati; kassaci tayo hetū, kassaci dve hetū pātubhavanti, avasesā ahetukā; asaññasattā pana acittakā pātubhavanti. Bhūmantaradassanavāre avīcinirayato yāva paranimmitavasavattī deve kāmāvacarā, maggā ca maggaphalāni ca asaṅkhatā ca dhātu apariyāpannāti vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "paṭisandhi nāma dukkhasaccamevā"ti sallakkhetvā upapattiyaṃ anabhinanditvā, ñāṇasampayuttāya paṭisandhiyā laddhāya paññindriyaṃ bhāvetvā, apariyāpanne maggaphale asaṅkhatāya ca dhātuyā cittaṃ namentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1013]]

At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, for whom do three roots appear? For the gods of the sense sphere, for humans of the first eon, and for womb-born beings who have roots and are associated with knowledge, at the moment of rebirth three roots appear: non-greed as a resultant root, non-hate as a resultant root, non-delusion as a resultant root. At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, these three roots appear for them.
At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, for whom do two roots appear? For the gods of the sense sphere, for humans of the first eon, and for womb-born beings who have roots but are dissociated from knowledge, at the moment of rebirth two roots appear: non-greed as a resultant root, non-hate as a resultant root. At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, these two roots appear for them. The remaining beings appear without roots. ^2-1013

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1014]]

At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, which four nutriments appear for all? The nutriment of solid food, the nutriment of contact, the nutriment of mental intention, the nutriment of consciousness — at the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, these four nutriments appear for all.
At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, which one contact appears for all? Mind-consciousness-element contact — at the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, this one contact appears for all.
At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, which one feeling… one perception… one intention… one mind appears for all? The mind-consciousness element — at the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, this one mind appears for all. ^2-1014

##### The Material-Form Element ^2-18-4-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1015]]

At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, how many aggregates appear… how many minds appear?
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element — setting aside the non-percipient gods — five aggregates appear, five sense-fields appear, five elements appear, one truth appears, ten faculties appear, three roots appear, three nutriments appear, one contact appears, one feeling… one perception… one intention… one mind appears. ^2-1015

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1016]]

At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which five aggregates appear? The aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, these five aggregates appear.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which five sense-fields appear? The eye sense-field, the sight sense-field, the ear sense-field, the mind sense-field, the mental-object sense-field — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, these five sense-fields appear.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which five elements appear? The eye element, the sight element, the ear element, the mind-consciousness element, the mental-object element — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, these five elements appear.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which one truth appears? The truth of suffering — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, this one truth appears.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which ten faculties appear? The eye faculty, the ear faculty, the mind faculty, the life faculty, the joy faculty or the equanimity faculty, the faculty of confidence, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, these ten faculties appear.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which three roots appear? Non-greed as a resultant root, non-hate as a resultant root, non-delusion as a resultant root — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, these three roots appear.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which three nutriments appear? The nutriment of contact, the nutriment of mental intention, the nutriment of consciousness — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, these three nutriments appear.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which one contact appears? Mind-consciousness-element contact — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, this one contact appears.
At the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, which one feeling… one perception… one intention… one mind appears? The mind-consciousness element — at the moment of rebirth in the material-form element, this one mind appears. ^2-1016

##### The Non-Percipient Beings ^2-18-4-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1017]]

At the moment of rebirth of the non-percipient gods, how many aggregates appear… how many minds appear?
At the moment of rebirth of the non-percipient gods, one aggregate appears — the aggregate of material form; two sense-fields appear — the sight sense-field and the mental-object sense-field; two elements appear — the sight element and the mental-object element; one truth appears — the truth of suffering; one faculty appears — the material life faculty. The non-percipient gods appear without roots, without nutriment, without contact, without feeling, without perception, without intention, without mind. ^2-1017
