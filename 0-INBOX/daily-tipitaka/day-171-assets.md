## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Āyuppamāṇaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyuppamāṇe manussānaṃ vassasataṃ appaṃ vā bhiyyoti dutiyaṃ vassasataṃ appatvā vīsāya vā tiṃsāya vā…pe… saṭṭhiyā vā vassehi adhikampi vassasataṃ. Brahmapārisajjā brahmapurohitā mahābrahmāno paṭhamajjhānabhūmiyaṃ, parittābhā appamāṇābhā ābhassarā dutiyajjhānabhūmiyaṃ, parittasubhā appamāṇasubhā subhakiṇhā tatiyajjhānabhūmiyaṃ ekatale vasanti; āyuantaraṃ pana nesaṃ nānā. Catutthajjhāne ārammaṇamanasikārachandapaṇidhiadhimokkhābhinīhārapaññānānattatāya upapattinānattaṃ hoti. Asaññasattā pana ekacce titthāyatane pabbajitvā "cittaṃ nissāya rajjanadussanamuyhanāni nāma hontī"ti citte dosaṃ disvā "acittakabhāvo nāma sobhano, diṭṭhadhammanibbānameta"nti saññāvirāgaṃ janetvā tatrūpagaṃ samāpattiṃ bhāvetvā tattha nibbattanti, pañca kappasatāni ṭhatvā kāmāvacarasaññuppādena tato cavanti. Manussesu gihibhāve ṭhitāyeva sotāpannāpi honti, sakadāgāmiphalampi anāgāmiphalampi arahattaphalampi pāpuṇanti. Aṭṭhasamāpattilābhīnaṃ paguṇajjhānaṃ, sabbesu paguṇesu patthanā, patthanāya asati maraṇasamaye samāpannā samāpatti upapattiṃ niyameti. Vehapphalā, akaniṭṭhā, nevasaññānāsaññāyatananti ime tayo devalokā seṭṭhabhavā nāma; tattha nibbattaanāgāmino tattha tattheva parinibbāyanti. Avasāne ca "natthi koci bhavo nicco"ti dīpetvā jarāmaraṇamokkhāya maggabhāvanā thomitā.

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Sattamādīsu catūsu vāresu sattamavāre salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena abhiññeyyatā veditabbā, ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena pariññeyyatā; sā ca "rūpakkhandho abhiññeyyo pariññeyyo na pahātabbo"tiādīsu ñātatīraṇapariññāvaseneva, "samudayasaccaṃ abhiññeyyaṃ pariññeyyaṃ pahātabba"ntiādīsu pahānapariññāvasena veditabbā. Aṭṭhamavāre rūpādiārammaṇānaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ vasena sārammaṇānārammaṇatā veditabbā. Navamavāre khandhādidhammā diṭṭhasutamutaviññātavasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; so uttānatthoyeva. Dasamavāre kusalattikavedanāttikavipākattikaupādinnattikavitakkattikānaṃ rūpadukalokiyadukānañca vasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ siyā taṃ sabbaṃ tattha tattha pañhāpucchakavāre vuttameva. Ettāvatā dhammahadayavibhaṅgavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.

#### Āyuppamāṇaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyuppamāṇe manussānaṃ vassasataṃ appaṃ vā bhiyyo, devabrahmānaṃ pana dīghaṃ āyu vuttaṃ; tāva dīghāyukāpi sattā āyusaṅkhayā cavanti. Asaññasattā ca citte dosaṃ disvā "acittakabhāvo nāma sobhano, diṭṭhadhammanibbānameta"nti saññāvirāgaṃ janetvā tattha nibbattanti, pañcakappasatāvasāne puna cavanti. Manussesu pana gihibhāve ṭhitāyeva sotāpannāpi honti, sakadāgāmiphalampi anāgāmiphalampi arahattaphalampi pāpuṇanti; aṭṭhasamāpattilābhīnañca paguṇajjhānaṃ, patthanā, maraṇasamaye samāpannā samāpatti ca upapattiṃ niyameti. Tasmā yoginā appakaṃ manussāyuṃ ñatvā pamādaṃ pahāya, acittakabhāvaṃ nibbānanti agaṇhanto, gihibhāvepi maggaphalāni pattabbānīti ussāhaṃ janetvā, jhānaṃ paguṇaṃ katvā, sabbāsave pariññāya anāsavo parinibbātuṃ maggaṃ bhāventena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Abhiññeyyādivāre sabbe khandhādayo abhiññeyyā pariññeyyā vuttā; samudayasaccaṃ tayo akusalahetū domanassindriyañca pahātabbāni, maggasaccaṃ anaññātaññassāmītindriyañca bhāvetabbāni, nirodhasaccaṃ aññātāvindriyañca sacchikātabbāni. Abhiññeyyatā salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena, pariññeyyatā ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena veditabbā. Sārammaṇānārammaṇavāre rūpakkhandho anārammaṇo, cattāro khandhā sārammaṇā; diṭṭhasutādivāre cattāro khandhā na diṭṭhā na sutā na mutā, viññātāva; tikādivāre samudayasaccaṃ akusalaṃ, maggasaccaṃ kusalaṃ, nirodhasaccaṃ abyākataṃ. Tasmā yoginā rūpārūpadhamme salakkhaṇato pariggahetvā ñātapariññāya jānitvā tīraṇapariññāya tīrayitvā, pahānapariññāya samudayaṃ akusalahetū ca pajahitvā, maggaṃ bhāvetvā nirodhaṃ sacchikarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### The Section on What Is to Be Directly Known and the Rest ^2-18-7-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1030]]

Of the five aggregates, how many are to be directly known, how many are to be fully understood, how many are to be abandoned, how many are to be developed, how many are to be realised, how many are neither to be abandoned, nor to be developed, nor to be realised… of the seven minds, how many are to be directly known, how many are to be fully understood, how many are to be abandoned, how many are to be developed, how many are to be realised, how many are neither to be abandoned, nor to be developed, nor to be realised? ^2-1030

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1031]]

The aggregate of material form is to be directly known and fully understood; it is not to be abandoned, not to be developed, not to be realised. Four aggregates are to be directly known and fully understood; they may be to be abandoned, may be to be developed, may be to be realised, or may be neither to be abandoned, nor to be developed, nor to be realised.
Ten sense-fields are to be directly known and fully understood; they are not to be abandoned, not to be developed, not to be realised. Two sense-fields are to be directly known and fully understood; they may be to be abandoned, may be to be developed, may be to be realised, or may be neither to be abandoned, nor to be developed, nor to be realised.
Sixteen elements are to be directly known and fully understood; they are not to be abandoned, not to be developed, not to be realised. Two elements are to be directly known and fully understood; they may be to be abandoned, may be to be developed, may be to be realised, or may be neither to be abandoned, nor to be developed, nor to be realised.
The truth of the origin is to be directly known, fully understood, and abandoned; it is not to be developed, not to be realised. The truth of the path is to be directly known and fully understood; it is not to be abandoned; it is to be developed; it is not to be realised. The truth of cessation is to be directly known and fully understood; it is not to be abandoned, not to be developed; it is to be realised. The truth of suffering is to be directly known and fully understood; it may be to be abandoned, it is not to be developed, not to be realised, or it may be not to be abandoned.
Nine faculties are to be directly known and fully understood; they are not to be abandoned, not to be developed, not to be realised. The grief faculty is to be directly known, fully understood, and abandoned; it is not to be developed, not to be realised. The faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known" is to be directly known and fully understood; it is not to be abandoned; it is to be developed; it is not to be realised. The faculty of final knowledge is to be directly known and fully understood; it is not to be abandoned; it may be to be developed, or may be to be realised. Of the five aggregates, how many have objects, how many are without objects… of the seven minds, how many have objects, how many are without objects? ^2-1032

The aggregate of material form is without an object. Four aggregates have objects.
