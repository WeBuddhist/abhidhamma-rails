### Pañhāpucchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchane pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ "katikusalā? Katiakusalā? Katiabyākatā"tiādinā nayena yaṃ labbhati yañca na labbhati taṃ sabbaṃ pucchitvā, vissajjane "rūpakkhandho abyākato"tiādinā nayena yaṃ labbhati tadeva uddhaṭaṃ.
