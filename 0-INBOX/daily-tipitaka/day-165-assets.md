##### (2) Bāhirassa upādāya ^2-17-12-2-0

Tattha katamāni aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritāni bāhirassa upādāya? Iminā asmīti hoti, iminā itthasmīti hoti, iminā evasmīti hoti, iminā aññathāsmīti hoti, iminā bhavissanti hoti, iminā itthaṃ bhavissanti hoti, iminā evaṃ bhavissanti hoti, iminā aññathā bhavissanti hoti, iminā asasmīti hoti, iminā sātasmīti hoti, iminā siyanti hoti, iminā itthaṃ siyanti hoti, iminā evaṃ siyanti hoti, iminā aññathā siyanti hoti, iminā apāhaṃ siyanti hoti, iminā apāhaṃ itthaṃ siyanti hoti, iminā apāhaṃ evaṃ siyanti hoti, iminā apāhaṃ aññathā siyanti hoti. ^2-975

(1) Kathañca iminā asmīti hoti? Kañci dhammaṃ avakāriṃ karitvā rūpaṃ… vedanaṃ… saññaṃ… saṅkhāre… viññāṇaṃ iminā asmīti chandaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā asmīti mānaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā asmīti diṭṭhiṃ paṭilabhati. Tasmiṃ sati imāni papañcitāni honti – iminā itthasmīti vā, iminā evasmīti vā, iminā aññathāsmīti vā.
(2) Kathañca iminā itthasmīti hoti? Iminā khattiyosmīti vā, iminā brāhmaṇosmīti vā, iminā vessosmīti vā, iminā suddosmīti vā, iminā gahaṭṭhosmīti vā, iminā pabbajitosmīti vā, iminā devosmīti vā, iminā manussosmīti vā, iminā rūpīsmīti vā, iminā arūpīsmīti vā, iminā saññīsmīti vā, iminā asaññīsmīti vā, iminā nevasaññīnāsaññīsmīti vā – evaṃ iminā itthasmīti hoti.
(3) Kathañca iminā evasmīti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā tathāhaṃ khattiyosmīti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā tathāhaṃ brāhmaṇosmīti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā tathāhaṃ nevasaññīnāsaññīsmīti vā – evaṃ iminā evasmīti hoti.
(4) Kathañca iminā aññathāsmīti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā nāhaṃ tathā khattiyosmīti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā nāhaṃ tathā brāhmaṇosmīti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā nāhaṃ tathā nevasaññīnāsaññīsmīti vā – evaṃ iminā aññathāsmīti hoti.
(5) Kathañca iminā bhavissanti hoti? Kañci dhammaṃ avakāriṃ karitvā rūpaṃ… vedanaṃ… saññaṃ… saṅkhāre… viññāṇaṃ iminā bhavissanti chandaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā bhavissanti mānaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā bhavissanti diṭṭhiṃ paṭilabhati. Tasmiṃ sati imāni papañcitāni honti – iminā itthaṃ bhavissanti vā, iminā evaṃ bhavissanti vā, iminā aññathā bhavissanti vā.
(6) Kathañca iminā itthaṃ bhavissanti hoti? Iminā khattiyo bhavissanti vā…pe… iminā arūpī bhavissanti vā, iminā saññī bhavissanti vā, iminā asaññī bhavissanti vā, iminā nevasaññīnāsaññī bhavissanti vā – evaṃ iminā itthaṃ bhavissanti hoti.
(7) Kathañca iminā evaṃ bhavissanti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā tathāhaṃ khattiyo bhavissanti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā tathāhaṃ brāhmaṇo bhavissanti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā tathāhaṃ nevasaññīnāsaññī bhavissanti vā – evaṃ iminā evaṃ bhavissanti hoti.
(8) Kathañca iminā aññathā bhavissanti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā nāhaṃ tathā khattiyo bhavissanti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā nāhaṃ tathā brāhmaṇo bhavissanti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā nāhaṃ tathā nevasaññīnāsaññī bhavissanti vā – evaṃ iminā aññathā bhavissanti hoti.
(9) Kathañca iminā asasmīti hoti? Kañci dhammaṃ avakāriṃ [avakārī (sī.)] karitvā rūpaṃ…pe… vedanaṃ… saññaṃ… saṅkhāre… viññāṇaṃ iminā niccosmi dhuvosmi sassatosmi avipariṇāmadhammosmīti – evaṃ iminā asasmīti hoti.
(10) Kathañca iminā sātasmīti hoti? Kañci dhammaṃ avakāriṃ karitvā rūpaṃ…pe… vedanaṃ… saññaṃ… saṅkhāre… viññāṇaṃ iminā ucchijjissāmi vinassissāmi na bhavissāmīti – evaṃ iminā sātasmīti hoti.
(11) Kathañca iminā siyanti hoti? Kañci dhammaṃ avakāriṃ karitvā rūpaṃ…pe… vedanaṃ… saññaṃ… saṅkhāre… viññāṇaṃ iminā siyanti chandaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā siyanti mānaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā siyanti diṭṭhiṃ paṭilabhati. Tasmiṃ sati imāni papañcitāni honti – iminā itthaṃ siyanti vā, iminā evaṃ siyanti vā, iminā aññathā siyanti vā.
(12) Kathañca iminā itthaṃ siyanti hoti? Iminā khattiyo siyanti vā, iminā brāhmaṇo siyanti vā, iminā vesso siyanti vā, iminā suddo siyanti vā, iminā gahaṭṭho siyanti vā, iminā pabbajito siyanti vā, iminā devo siyanti vā, iminā manusso siyanti vā, iminā rūpī siyanti vā, iminā arūpī siyanti vā, iminā saññī siyanti vā, iminā asaññī siyanti vā, iminā nevasaññīnāsaññī siyanti vā – evaṃ iminā itthaṃ siyanti hoti.
(13) Kathañca iminā evaṃ siyanti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā tathāhaṃ khattiyo siyanti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā tathāhaṃ brāhmaṇo siyanti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā tathāhaṃ nevasaññīnāsaññī siyanti vā – evaṃ iminā evaṃ siyanti hoti.
(14) Kathañca iminā aññathā siyanti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā nāhaṃ tathā khattiyo siyanti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā nāhaṃ tathā brāhmaṇo siyanti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā nāhaṃ tathā nevasaññīnāsaññī siyanti vā – evaṃ iminā aññathā siyanti hoti.
(15) Kathañca iminā apāhaṃ siyanti hoti? Kañci dhammaṃ avakāriṃ karitvā rūpaṃ…pe… vedanaṃ… saññaṃ… saṅkhāre… viññāṇaṃ iminā apāhaṃ siyanti chandaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā apāhaṃ siyanti mānaṃ paṭilabhati, iminā apāhaṃ siyanti diṭṭhiṃ paṭilabhati. Tasmiṃ sati imāni papañcitāni honti – iminā apāhaṃ itthaṃ siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ evaṃ siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ aññathā siyanti vā.
(16) Kathañca iminā apāhaṃ itthaṃ siyanti hoti? Iminā apāhaṃ khattiyo siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ brāhmaṇo siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ vesso siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ suddo siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ gahaṭṭho siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ pabbajito siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ devo siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ manusso siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ rūpī siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ arūpī siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ saññī siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ asaññī siyanti vā, iminā apāhaṃ nevasaññīnāsaññī siyanti vā – evaṃ iminā apāhaṃ itthaṃ siyanti hoti.
(17) Kathañca iminā apāhaṃ evaṃ siyanti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā apāhaṃ tathā khattiyo siyanti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā apāhaṃ tathā brāhmaṇo siyanti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā apāhaṃ tathā nevasaññīnāsaññī siyanti vā – evaṃ iminā apāhaṃ evaṃ siyanti hoti.
(18) Kathañca iminā apāhaṃ aññathā siyanti hoti? Parapuggalaṃ upanidhāya yathā so khattiyo iminā apāhaṃ na tathā khattiyo siyanti vā, yathā so brāhmaṇo iminā apāhaṃ na tathā brāhmaṇo siyanti vā…pe… yathā so nevasaññīnāsaññī iminā apāhaṃ na tathā nevasaññīnāsaññī siyanti vā – evaṃ iminā apāhaṃ aññathā siyanti hoti.
Imāni aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritāni bāhirassa upādāya.
Iti imāni aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritāni ajjhattikassa upādāya, imāni aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritāni bāhirassa upādāya, tadekajjhaṃ abhisaññūhitvā abhisaṅkhipitvā chattiṃsa taṇhāvicaritāni honti. Iti evarūpāni atītāni chattiṃsa taṇhāvicaritāni, anāgatāni chattiṃsa taṇhāvicaritāni, paccuppannāni chattiṃsa taṇhāvicaritāni, tadekajjhaṃ abhisaññūhitvā abhisaṅkhipitvā aṭṭhataṇhāvicaritasataṃ hoti. ^2-976

Tattha katamāni dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni brahmajāle veyyākaraṇe vuttāni bhagavatā? Cattāro sassatavādā, cattāro ekaccasassatikā, cattāro antānantikā, cattāro amarāvikkhepikā, dve adhiccasamuppannikā, soḷasa saññīvādā, aṭṭha asaññīvādā, aṭṭha nevasaññīnāsaññīvādā, satta ucchedavādā, pañca diṭṭhadhammanibbānavādā – imāni dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni brahmajāle veyyākaraṇe vuttāni bhagavatāti.
Khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgo niṭṭhito. ^2-977

### Taṇhāvicaritaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-12|↑↑↑]]

Taṇhāvicaritānīti taṇhāsamudācārā taṇhāpavattiyo. Ajjhattikassa upādāyāti ajjhattikaṃ khandhapañcakaṃ upādāya; tattha kañci dhammaṃ anavakāriṃ katvā samūhato gahetvā taṇhāmānadiṭṭhivasena ‘asmī’ti hoti, tasmiṃ sati ‘khattiyo ahaṃ’ti anupanidhāya ‘itthasmī’ti, ‘yathā so khattiyo tathā aha’nti samato upanidhāya ‘evasmī’ti, ‘tato hīno vā seṭṭho vā aha’nti asamato upanidhāya ‘aññathāsmī’ti hoti; bhavissantiādīni anāgatavasena, asmi sātasmīti sassatucchedavasena, siyantiādīni saṃsayaparivitakkavasena, apāhaṃ siyantiādīni patthanākappanavasena vuttāni – dve diṭṭhisīsā, cattāro suddhasīsā, dvādasa sīsamūlakāti aṭṭhārasa. Bāhirassa upādāyāti bāhiraṃ khandhapañcakaṃ upādāya, rūpādīsu ekadesato gahetvā ‘iminā chattena vā khaggena vā khattiyo aha’nti evaṃ aṭṭhārasa; tadekajjhaṃ chattiṃsa, atītānāgatapaccuppannavasena aṭṭhataṇhāvicaritasataṃ hoti. Mānadiṭṭhiyo pana na vinā taṇhāya, tasmā tadekaṭṭhavasena idha vuttā, taṇhāsīsena vā papañcattayampi uddiṭṭhaṃ. Dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni pana brahmajāle veyyākaraṇe satthārā sayaṃ āhacca bhāsitāni, tattha vuttanayeneva pabhedo ca attho ca veditabbo.

### Taṇhāvicaritaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-12|↑↑↑]]

Taṇhāvicaritaniddese pañcakkhandhe samūhato gahetvā ‘asmī’ti, ‘itthasmi, evasmi, aññathāsmī’ti, bāhire ca ‘iminā chattena vā khaggena vā aha’nti taṇhāmānadiṭṭhipapañcavasena aṭṭhataṇhāvicaritasataṃ vuttaṃ, dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni ca brahmajāle. ##### (2) Concerning what is external ^2-17-12-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-975]]

Therein, what are the eighteen wanderings of craving concerning what is external? There is "by this I am"; there is "by this I am thus"; there is "by this I am just so"; there is "by this I am otherwise"; there is "by this I shall be"; there is "by this I shall be thus"; there is "by this I shall be just so"; there is "by this I shall be otherwise"; there is "by this I am lasting"; there is "by this I am perishing"; there is "by this may I be"; there is "by this may I be thus"; there is "by this may I be just so"; there is "by this may I be otherwise"; there is "by this would that I might be"; there is "by this would that I might be thus"; there is "by this would that I might be just so"; there is "by this would that I might be otherwise". ^2-975

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-976]]

(1) And how is there "by this I am"? Having taken up some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one obtains the desire "by this I am", one obtains the conceit "by this I am", one obtains the view "by this I am". When that exists, there are these proliferations: "by this I am thus", or "by this I am just so", or "by this I am otherwise".
(2) And how is there "by this I am thus"? "By this I am a noble", or "by this I am a brahmin", or "by this I am a merchant", or "by this I am a worker", or "by this I am a householder", or "by this I am a renunciant", or "by this I am a god", or "by this I am a human being", or "by this I have material form", or "by this I am immaterial", or "by this I am percipient", or "by this I am non-percipient", or "by this I am neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this I am thus".
(3) And how is there "by this I am just so"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this so I am a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this so I am a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this so I am neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this I am just so".
(4) And how is there "by this I am otherwise"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this I am not so a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this I am not so a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this I am not so neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this I am otherwise".
(5) And how is there "by this I shall be"? Having taken up some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one obtains the desire "by this I shall be", one obtains the conceit "by this I shall be", one obtains the view "by this I shall be". When that exists, there are these proliferations: "by this I shall be thus", or "by this I shall be just so", or "by this I shall be otherwise".
(6) And how is there "by this I shall be thus"? "By this I shall be a noble"… or "by this I shall be immaterial", or "by this I shall be percipient", or "by this I shall be non-percipient", or "by this I shall be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this I shall be thus".
(7) And how is there "by this I shall be just so"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this so I shall be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this so I shall be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this so I shall be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this I shall be just so".
(8) And how is there "by this I shall be otherwise"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this I shall not so be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this I shall not so be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this I shall not so be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this I shall be otherwise".
(9) And how is there "by this I am lasting"? Having taken up some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one thinks, "by this I am permanent, I am stable, I am eternal, I am not subject to change" — thus there is "by this I am lasting".
(10) And how is there "by this I am perishing"? Having taken up some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one thinks, "by this I shall be cut off, I shall be destroyed, I shall not be" — thus there is "by this I am perishing".
(11) And how is there "by this may I be"? Having taken up some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one obtains the desire "by this may I be", one obtains the conceit "by this may I be", one obtains the view "by this may I be". When that exists, there are these proliferations: "by this may I be thus", or "by this may I be just so", or "by this may I be otherwise".
(12) And how is there "by this may I be thus"? "By this may I be a noble", or "by this may I be a brahmin", or "by this may I be a merchant", or "by this may I be a worker", or "by this may I be a householder", or "by this may I be a renunciant", or "by this may I be a god", or "by this may I be a human being", or "by this may I have material form", or "by this may I be immaterial", or "by this may I be percipient", or "by this may I be non-percipient", or "by this may I be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this may I be thus".
(13) And how is there "by this may I be just so"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this so may I be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this so may I be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this so may I be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this may I be just so".
(14) And how is there "by this may I be otherwise"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this may I not so be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this may I not so be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this may I not so be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this may I be otherwise".

(16) And how is there "by this would that I might be thus"? (17) And how is there "by this would that I might be just so"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this so would that I might be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this so would that I might be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this so would that I might be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this would that I might be just so".
(18) And how is there "by this would that I might be otherwise"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, by this would that I might not so be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, by this would that I might not so be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, by this would that I might not so be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "by this would that I might be otherwise".

Therein, what are the sixty-two grounds of views spoken of by the Blessed One in the exposition of the Brahmajāla?
