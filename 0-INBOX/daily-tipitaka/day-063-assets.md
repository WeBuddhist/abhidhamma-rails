## Pāli source verses

1216. Katame dhammā no upādā? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho, viññāṇakkhandho, cattāro ca mahābhūtā, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu – ime dhammā no upādā. ^1-1216

1217. Katame dhammā upādiṇṇā? Sāsavā kusalākusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ vipākā kāmāvacarā, rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā; vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho; yañca rūpaṃ kammassa katattā – ime dhammā upādiṇṇā . ^1-1217

1218. Katame dhammā anupādiṇṇā? Sāsavā kusalākusalā dhammā kāmāvacarā, rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā; vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho; ye ca dhammā kiriyā neva kusalā nākusalā na ca kammavipākā, yañca rūpaṃ na kammassa katattā, apariyāpannā maggā ca, maggaphalāni ca, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu – ime dhammā anupādiṇṇā. ^1-1218

##### Upādānagocchakaṃ ^1-3-2-11-0

1219. Katame dhammā upādānā? Cattāri upādānāni – kāmupādānaṃ, diṭṭhupādānaṃ, sīlabbatupādānaṃ, attavādupādānaṃ [kāmūpādānaṃ diṭṭhūpādānaṃ sīlabbatūpādānaṃ attavādūpādānaṃ (sī.)]. ^1-1219

1220. Tattha katamaṃ kāmupādānaṃ? Yo kāmesu kāmacchando kāmarāgo kāmanandī kāmataṇhā kāmasineho kāmapariḷāho kāmamucchā kāmajjhosānaṃ – idaṃ vuccati kāmupādānaṃ. ^1-1220

1221. Tattha katamaṃ diṭṭhupādānaṃ? Natthi dinnaṃ, natthi yiṭṭhaṃ, natthi hutaṃ, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṃ kammānaṃ phalaṃ vipāko, natthi ayaṃ loko, natthi paro loko, natthi mātā, natthi pitā, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi loke samaṇabrāhmaṇā sammaggatā [samaggatā (ka.)] sammāpaṭipannā, ye imañca lokaṃ parañca lokaṃ sayaṃ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedentīti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ diṭṭhigahanaṃ diṭṭhikantāro diṭṭhivisūkāyikaṃ diṭṭhivipphanditaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ gāho patiṭṭhāho abhiniveso parāmāso kummaggo micchāpatho micchattaṃ titthāyatanaṃ vipariyāsaggāho – idaṃ vuccati diṭṭhupādānaṃ. Ṭhapetvā sīlabbatupādānañca attavādupādānañca sabbāpi micchādiṭṭhi diṭṭhupādānaṃ. ^1-1221

1222. Tattha katamaṃ sīlabbatupādānaṃ? Ito bahiddhā samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṃ sīlena suddhi, vatena suddhi, sīlabbatena suddhīti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ diṭṭhigahanaṃ diṭṭhikantāro diṭṭhivisūkāyikaṃ diṭṭhivipphanditaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ gāho patiṭṭhāho abhiniveso parāmāso kummaggo micchāpatho micchattaṃ titthāyatanaṃ vipariyāsaggāho – idaṃ vuccati sīlabbatupādānaṃ. ^1-1222

1223. Tattha katamaṃ attavādupādānaṃ? Idha assutavā puthujjano ariyānaṃ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akovido ariyadhamme avinīto sappurisānaṃ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akovido sappurisadhamme avinīto rūpaṃ attato samanupassati, rūpavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā rūpaṃ, rūpasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Vedanaṃ…pe… saññaṃ…pe… saṅkhāre…pe… viññāṇaṃ attato samanupassati, viññāṇavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā viññāṇaṃ viññāṇasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ diṭṭhigahanaṃ diṭṭhikantāro diṭṭhivisūkāyikaṃ diṭṭhivipphanditaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ gāho patiṭṭhāho abhiniveso parāmāso kummaggo micchāpatho micchattaṃ titthāyatanaṃ vipariyāsaggāho – idaṃ vuccati attavādupādānaṃ.
Ime dhammā upādānā. ^1-1223

1224. Katame dhammā no upādānā? Te dhamme ṭhapetvā avasesā kusalākusalābyākatā dhammā kāmāvacarā, rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā, apariyāpannā; vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho; sabbañca rūpaṃ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu – ime dhammā no upādānā. ^1-1224

1226. Katame dhammā anupādāniyā? Apariyāpannā maggā ca, maggaphalāni ca, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu – ime dhammā anupādāniyā. ^1-1226

1227. Katame dhammā upādānasampayuttā? Tehi dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho – ime dhammā upādānasampayuttā. ^1-1227

1229. Katame dhammā upādānā ceva upādāniyā ca? Tāneva upādānāni upādānā ceva upādāniyā ca. ^1-1229

1231. Katame dhammā upādānā ceva upādānasampayuttā ca? Diṭṭhupādānaṃ kāmupādānena upādānañceva upādānasampayuttañca, kāmupādānaṃ diṭṭhupādānena upādānañceva upādānasampayuttañca, sīlabbatupādānaṃ kāmupādānena upādānañceva upādānasampayuttañca, kāmupādānaṃ sīlabbatupādānena upādānañceva upādānasampayuttañca , attavādupādānaṃ kāmupādānena upādānañceva upādānasampayuttañca, kāmupādānaṃ attavādupādānena upādānañceva upādānasampayuttañca – ime dhammā upādānā ceva upādānasampayuttā ca. ^1-1231

1232. Katame dhammā upādānasampayuttā ceva no ca upādānā? Tehi dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā, te dhamme ṭhapetvā vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho – ime dhammā upādānasampayuttā ceva no ca upādānā. ^1-1232

1233. Katame dhammā upādānavippayuttā upādāniyā? Tehi dhammehi ye dhammā vippayuttā sāsavā kusalākusalābyākatā dhammā kāmāvacarā , rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā; rūpakkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho – ime dhammā upādānavippayuttā upādāniyā. ^1-1233

##### Kilesagocchakaṃ ^1-3-2-12-0

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Mahantaradukaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-4-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaradukanikkhepakathāyaṃ kenaci viññeyyadukaniddese cakkhuviññeyyāti cakkhuviññāṇena vijānitabbā. Sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Kenaci viññeyyāti cakkhuviññāṇādīsu kenaci ekena vijānitabbā; kenaci na viññeyyāti teneva na vijānitabbā. Rūpaṃ pana cakkhuviññeyyaṃ saddo na cakkhuviññeyyoti imamatthaṃ gahetvā vīsati dukā vibhattāti veditabbā.

#### Upādānagocchakaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-4-2-11|↑↑↑]]

Upādānagocchakanikkhepakathāyaṃ upādānānaṃ nikkhepo āsavagocchakanikkhepakathāyaṃ vuttanayeneva veditabbo. Vipākattike kiñcāpi rūpadhammāpi kammasamuṭṭhānā atthi, anārammaṇattā pana te kammasarikkhakā na hontīti sārammaṇā arūpadhammāva kammasarikkhakattā vipākāti vuttā, bījasarikkhakaṃ phalaṃ viyāti.

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-4-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesagocchakanikkhepakathāyaṃ yattha dve tayo kilesā ekato uppajjantīti tattha dasadhā kilesānaṃ ekato uppatti hoti. Lobho chadhā ekato uppajjati, paṭigho dvidhā, moho dvidhāti veditabbo. Lobho tāva asaṅkhārike diṭṭhivippayutte mohauddhaccaahirikānottappehi, sasaṅkhārike mohathinauddhaccaahirikānottappehi, pe, diṭṭhisampayutte asaṅkhārike mohauddhaccadiṭṭhiahirikānottappehi, sasaṅkhārike mohadiṭṭhithinauddhaccaahirikānottappehi saddhinti chadhā ekato uppajjatīti.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

#### Mahantaradukaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-3-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaraduke "kenaci viññeyyā kenaci na viññeyyā"tiādinā vīsati dukā nikkhittā — rūpaṃ cakkhuviññeyyaṃ na sotaviññeyyaṃ, saddo sotaviññeyyo na cakkhuviññeyyoti. Iti dhammā nānāviññāṇehi nānāgocarā. Tasmā yoginā channaṃ dvārānaṃ visaye yathāvato ñatvā, ekekasmiṃ dvāre "idha diṭṭhaṃ, idha suta"nti sallakkhetvā, indriyasaṃvaraṃ pūretvā, saṅkhāre "anattā"ti passitabbaṃ.

#### Upādānagocchakaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-3-2-11|↑↑↑]]

Upādānagocchake cattāri upādānāni nikkhittāni — kāmupādānaṃ, diṭṭhupādānaṃ, sīlabbatupādānaṃ, attavādupādānaṃ. Ete taṇhādiṭṭhivasena ārammaṇaṃ daḷhaṃ gaṇhanti, punabbhavassa ca indhanaṃ honti. Tasmā yoginā "kiṃ upādiyāmī"ti disvā, visesena attavādupādānaṃ "sabbe dhammā anattā"ti paññāya sithilaṃ katvā, upādānakkhayāya paṭipajjitabbaṃ; anupādāya hi cittaṃ vimuccati.

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-3-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesagocchake dasa kilesavatthūni nikkhittāni — lobho doso moho māno diṭṭhi vicikicchā thinaṃ uddhaccaṃ ahirikaṃ anottappaṃ. Ete cittaṃ kilissanti saṃkilesenti; tehi ekato uppajjamānehi cittaṃ malinaṃ hoti. Tasmā yoginā kilese uppattikkhaṇeyeva satiyā pariggahetvā, sīlena vikkhambhetvā, samādhinā tanukatvā, paññāya samugghātetvā, cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ; cittavodānā hi visuddhi.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, the volitional formations aggregate, the consciousness aggregate, the four great essential elements, and the unconditioned element – these states are not derived. ^1-1216
The tainted results of wholesome and unwholesome states belonging to the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and immaterial sphere; the feeling aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate; and matter produced by kamma – these states are grasped by kamma. ^1-1217
The tainted wholesome and unwholesome states belonging to the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and immaterial sphere; the feeling aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate; and functional states that are neither wholesome nor unwholesome nor kamma-results; and matter not produced by kamma; and the unincluded paths and path-fruits; and the unconditioned element – these states are not grasped by kamma. ^1-1218
Which phenomena are clingings? Four clingings: sensual clinging, clinging to views, clinging to rites and rituals, clinging to a doctrine of self. ^1-1219
What is sensual clinging? That desire for sensual pleasures, lust for sensual pleasures, delight in sensual pleasures, craving for sensual pleasures, affection for sensual pleasures, agitation due to sensual pleasures, infatuation with sensual pleasures, obsession with sensual pleasures – this is called sensual clinging. ^1-1220
What is clinging to views? The view that there is no giving, no offering, no sacrifice; no fruit or result of good and bad actions; no this world, no other world; no mother, no father; no spontaneously born beings; no recluses and brahmins in the world who are rightly gone, rightly practiced, who have themselves realized and proclaimed this world and the other world by direct knowledge – such a view, wrong view, thicket of views, wilderness of views, agitation of views, fickleness of views, fetter of views, grasp, holding, adherence, clinging, wrong path, false way, wrongness, heretical tenet, perverse grasp – this is called clinging to views. Excluding clinging to rites and rituals and clinging to a doctrine of self, all wrong views are clinging to views. ^1-1221
What is clinging to rites and rituals? Among recluses and brahmins outside [this dispensation], the view that purification is through virtue, purification is through vows, purification is through rites and rituals – such a view, wrong view, thicket of views, wilderness of views, agitation of views, fickleness of views, fetter of views, grasp, holding, adherence, clinging, wrong path, false way, wrongness, heretical tenet, perverse grasp – this is called clinging to rites and rituals. ^1-1222
These phenomena are clingings. ^1-1223
Which phenomena are not clingings? Excluding those phenomena, the remaining wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate phenomena in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and unincluded; the aggregate of feeling... consciousness; and all matter, and the unconditioned element – these phenomena are not clingings. ^1-1224
Which phenomena are objects of clinging? Wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate phenomena with taints in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and immaterial sphere; the aggregate of form... consciousness – these phenomena are objects of clinging. ^1-1225
Which phenomena are not objects of clinging? The unincluded paths and their fruits, and the unconditioned element – these phenomena are not objects of clinging. ^1-1226
Which phenomena are associated with clinging? The mental aggregates (feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness) that are associated with clinging – these phenomena are associated with clinging. ^1-1227
Which phenomena are dissociated from clinging? The mental aggregates (feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness) that are dissociated from clinging; all physical form, and the unconditioned element – these phenomena are dissociated from clinging. ^1-1228
Which phenomena are both clinging and objects of clinging? The clingings themselves are both clinging and objects of clinging. ^1-1229
Which phenomena are objects of clinging but not clinging itself? Excluding the clingings themselves, the remaining defiled wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate phenomena pertaining to the sense-sphere, form-sphere, and formless-sphere; the five aggregates (form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness) – these phenomena are objects of clinging but not clinging itself. ^1-1230
Which phenomena are both clinging and associated with clinging? Clinging to views associated with sensual clinging is both clinging and associated with clinging; sensual clinging associated with clinging to views is both clinging and associated with clinging; clinging to rites and rituals associated with sensual clinging is both clinging and associated with clinging; sensual clinging associated with clinging to rites and rituals is both clinging and associated with clinging; clinging to a doctrine of self associated with sensual clinging is both clinging and associated with clinging; sensual clinging associated with clinging to a doctrine of self is both clinging and associated with clinging – these phenomena are both clinging and associated with clinging. ^1-1231
Which phenomena are associated with clinging but not clinging itself? Excluding the clingings themselves, the mental aggregates (feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness) that are associated with clinging – these phenomena are associated with clinging but not clinging itself. ^1-1232
Which phenomena are dissociated from clinging yet are objects of clinging? Those phenomena that are dissociated from clinging, namely, defiled wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate phenomena pertaining to the sense-sphere, form-sphere, and formless-sphere; the five aggregates (form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness) – these phenomena are dissociated from clinging yet are objects of clinging. ^1-1233
The second recitation section in the Summary Chapter. ^1-1234
What are defilements? The ten bases of defilements are: greed, hatred, delusion, conceit, wrong view, skeptical doubt, sloth, restlessness, shamelessness, and fearlessness of wrongdoing. ^1-1235
Therein, what is greed? That which is lust, attachment, desire, infatuation, clinging, covetousness; craving for sensuality, existence, non-existence, fine-material, immaterial, cessation, forms, sounds, smells, tastes, tangibles, mental objects; the flood, bond, knot, clinging, hindrance, latent disposition, root of suffering, covetousness, greed, the unwholesome root. This is called greed. ^1-1236
