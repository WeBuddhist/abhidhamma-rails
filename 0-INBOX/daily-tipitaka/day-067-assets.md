## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-4-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhidukanikkhepakathāyaṃ micchattattike ānantarikānīti anantarāyena phaladāyakāni; mātughātakakammādīnametaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Tesu ekasmimpi kamme kate taṃ paṭibāhitvā aññaṃ kammaṃ attano vipākassa okāsaṃ kātuṃ na sakkoti. Yā ca micchādiṭṭhi niyatāti ahetukavādaakiriyavādanatthikavādesu aññatarā – taṃ gahetvā ṭhitaṃ puggalaṃ buddhasatampi buddhasahassampi bodhetuṃ na sakkonti.

### Suttantikadukanikkhepaṃ

[[#^toc-4-3|↑↑↑]]

Suttantikadukanikkhepakathāyaṃ mātikākathāyaṃ atthato vivecitattā yāni nesaṃ niddesapadāni tesampi heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva suviññeyyattā yebhuyyena uttānatthāneva. Tattha vijjūpamaduke cakkhumā puriso meghandhakāre rattiṃ maggaṃ paṭipajji; vijjuyā niccharitvā andhakāraviddhaṃsanakālo viya sotāpattimaggobhāsena uppajjitvā saccacchādakatamassa vinoditakālo; dutiya- tatiyagamanābhinīhāro viya sakadāgāmi- anāgāmimaggattāya vipassanārambho. Vajirassa pana pāsāṇo vā maṇi vā abhejjo nāma natthi – evameva arahattamaggassa avajjhakileso nāma natthi, sabbakilese vinivijjhati vajiraṃ viyāti adhivacanadukādinaṃ niddesapadāni nānābhedehi vibhattāni.

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ (Nikkhepa)

[[#^toc-3-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhiduke dassanena bhāvanāya ca pahātabbā, tesañca hetū, micchattaniyatā sammattaniyatā aniyatā cāti piṭṭhibhūtā dukā nikkhittā. Tattha tīṇi saṃyojanāni — sakkāyadiṭṭhi vicikicchā sīlabbataparāmāso — tadekaṭṭhā ca kilesā dassanena pahātabbā, sesā rāgadosamohādayo bhāvanāya. Ānantarikāni pana kammāni anantarāyena phaladāyakattā aññena kusalakammena appaṭibāhiyāni, niyatā ca micchādiṭṭhi ahetuka-akiriya-natthikavādabhedā buddhasatenapi abodhanīyā. Tasmā yoginā ānantarikakammā ceva niyatamicchādiṭṭhito ca accantaṃ viramitvā, sammādiṭṭhiṃ purakkhatvā, sotāpattimaggena tīṇi saṃyojanāni uparimaggehi ca sesakilese anukkamena samucchindantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Suttantikadukanikkhepaṃ

[[#^toc-3-3|↑↑↑]]

Suttantikadukesu vijjābhāgi-avijjābhāgi-bāla-paṇḍita-kaṇha-sukka-tapanīya-atapanīyādayo dvācattālīsa dukā nikkhittā. Tattha ahirikānottappāni bālabhāvassa mūlāni, kāyaduccaritādīni ca katattā pacchā tapanīyāni; hiri-ottappā pana paṇḍitakarā, kāyasucaritādīni ca akatattā atapanīyāni. Tasmā yoginā ahirikānottappe pahāya hiri-ottappe patiṭṭhāya, kaṇhe tapanīye ca dhamme vajjetvā sukke atapanīye ca dhamme sampādetabbā. Vijjābhāgiyā ca vipassanā bhāvetabbā — yathā meghandhakāre vijju niccharitvā maggaṃ pakāseti, evameva sotāpatti-sakadāgāmi-anāgāmimaggobhāsā saccacchādakatamaṃ vinodenti; yathā ca vajiraṃ sabbaṃ vinivijjhati, evaṃ arahattamaggo asesato sabbakilese samucchindati, tena ca pahīnā kilesā puna na paccudāvattanti. Iti anukkamena vaḍḍhentena cittaṃ accantaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Apart from those phenomena, the remaining wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate phenomena in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and the not-included; the feeling aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate, all matter, and the unconditioned element—these phenomena do not lead to liberation. ^1-1296
Which phenomena are fixed?
The five heinous actions, fixed wrong view, and the four not-included paths—these phenomena are fixed. ^1-1297
Which phenomena are not fixed?
Apart from those phenomena, the remaining wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate phenomena in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and the not-included; the feeling aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate, all matter, and the unconditioned element—these phenomena are not fixed. ^1-1298
Which phenomena have a superior?
Phenomena subject to taints, wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate, in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere; the form aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate—these phenomena have a superior. ^1-1299
Which phenomena have no superior?
The not-included paths, the path fruits, and the unconditioned element—these phenomena have no superior. ^1-1300
Which phenomena are with danger?
The three unwholesome roots: greed, hatred, delusion; and defilements associated with them; the feeling aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate associated with them; bodily action, verbal action, and mental action arising from them—these phenomena are with danger. ^1-1301
Which phenomena are without danger?
Wholesome and indeterminate phenomena in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and the not-included; the feeling aggregate... up to... the consciousness aggregate, all matter, and the unconditioned element—these phenomena are without danger.

#### Exposition of Suttanta Dyads ^1-3-3-0
Which are the states partaking of knowledge? States associated with knowledge – these states partake of knowledge. ^1-1303
Which are the states partaking of ignorance? States associated with ignorance – these states partake of ignorance. ^1-1304
Which are the states like lightning? Wisdom in the three lower noble paths – these states are like lightning. ^1-1305
Which are the states like a diamond? Wisdom in the Arahant path – these states are like a diamond. ^1-1306
Which are the states that are foolish? Shamelessness and fearlessness of wrongdoing – these states are foolish. All unwholesome states are foolish. ^1-1307
Which are the states that are wise? Shame and fear of wrongdoing – these states are wise. All wholesome states are wise. ^1-1308
Which are the states that are dark? Shamelessness and fearlessness of wrongdoing – these states are dark. All unwholesome states are dark. ^1-1309
Which are the states that are bright? Shame and fear of wrongdoing – these states are bright. All wholesome states are bright. ^1-1310
What are the states that are to be tormented? Bodily misconduct, verbal misconduct, mental misconduct – these states are to be tormented. All unwholesome states are to be tormented. ^1-1311
What are the states that are not to be tormented? Bodily good conduct, verbal good conduct, mental good conduct – these states are not to be tormented. All wholesome states are not to be tormented. ^1-1312
What are the states that are designations? The reckoning, appellation, concept, usage, name, naming, nomenclature, expression, indication, and utterance of those various states – these states are designations. All states are paths of designation. ^1-1313
What are the states that are expressions? The reckoning, appellation, concept, usage, name, naming, nomenclature, expression, indication, and utterance of those various states – these states are expressions. All states are paths of expression. ^1-1314
What are the states that are concepts? The reckoning, appellation, concept, usage, name, naming, nomenclature, expression, indication, and utterance of those various states – these states are concepts. All states are paths of concepts. ^1-1315
Therein, what is name? The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, the volitional formations aggregate, the consciousness aggregate, and the unconditioned element – this is called name. ^1-1316
Therein, what is form? The four great primary elements, and form derived from the four great primary elements – this is called form. ^1-1317
Therein, what is ignorance? That which is not knowing, not seeing...pe... the fetter of ignorance, delusion, the root of unwholesome states – this is called ignorance. ^1-1318
Therein, what is craving for existence? That which is desire for existence in states of being...pe... clinging to existence – this is called craving for existence. ^1-1319
Therein, what is view of existence? The view 'there will be a self and a world,' such a view, a wrong view...pe... a perverted grasp – this is called view of existence. ^1-1320
What is the view of non-existence? The view, 'There will be no self and no world,' such a view, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called the view of non-existence. ^1-1321
What is the view of eternalism? The view, 'The self and the world are eternal,' such a view, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called the view of eternalism. ^1-1322
What is the view of annihilationism? The view, 'The self and the world will be annihilated,' such a view, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called the view of annihilationism. ^1-1323
What is the view of finitude? The view, 'The self and the world are finite,' such a view, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called the view of finitude. ^1-1324
What is the view of infinitude? The view, 'The self and the world are infinite,' such a view, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called the view of infinitude. ^1-1325
What is speculation about the past? The view that arises concerning the past, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called speculation about the past. ^1-1326
What is speculation about the future? The view that arises concerning the future, wrong view, perverted grasping – this is called speculation about the future. ^1-1327
What is shamelessness? That which is not ashamed of what should be ashamed of, is not ashamed of committing evil, unwholesome states – this is called shamelessness. ^1-1328
