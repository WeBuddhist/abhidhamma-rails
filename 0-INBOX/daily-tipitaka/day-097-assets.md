182. Tattha katamā kāmadhātu? Kāmapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko saṅkappo appanā byappanā cetaso abhiniropanā micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati kāmadhātu. Heṭṭhato avīcinirayaṃ pariyantaṃ karitvā uparito paranimmitavasavattī deve anto karitvā yaṃ etasmiṃ antare etthāvacarā ettha pariyāpannā khandhadhātuāyatanā rūpā [rūpaṃ (syā.)] vedanā saññā saṅkhārā viññāṇaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "kāmadhātu".
Tattha katamā byāpādadhātu? Byāpādapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko…pe… micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "byāpādadhātu". Dasasu vā āghātavatthūsu cittassa āghāto paṭighāto paṭighaṃ paṭivirodho kopo pakopo sampakopo doso padoso sampadoso cittassa byāpatti manopadoso kodho kujjhanā kujjhitattaṃ doso dussanā dussitattaṃ byāpatti byāpajjanā virodho paṭivirodho caṇḍikkaṃ asuropo anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "byāpādadhātu".

Idhekacco pāṇinā vā leḍḍunā vā daṇḍena vā satthena vā rajjuyā vā aññataraññatarena satte viheṭheti, yā evarūpā heṭhanā viheṭhanā hiṃsanā vihiṃsanā rosanā virosanā parūpaghāto – ayaṃ vuccati "vihiṃsādhātu".

Tattha katamā abyāpādadhātu? Abyāpādapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko…pe… sammāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "abyāpādadhātu". Yā sattesu metti mettāyanā mettāyitattaṃ mettācetovimutti – ayaṃ vuccati "abyāpādadhātu".

Imā cha dhātuyo.
Iti imāni tīṇi chakkāni tadekajjhaṃ abhisaññūhitvā abhisaṅkhipitvā aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo honti.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Imasmiṃ dhātūnaṃ suttantabhājanīye bhagavā sabbā dhātuyo chahi chahi dhātūhi saṅkhipitvā tīhi chakkehi dassento cha dhātuyotiādimāha. Tattha chāti gaṇanaparicchedo, dhātuyoti paricchinnadhammanidassanaṃ; dhātvaṭṭho nāma sabhāvaṭṭho, sabhāvaṭṭho nāma suññataṭṭho, suññataṭṭho nāma nissattaṭṭho. Iti pathavīdhātūti patiṭṭhānadhātu, āpodhātūti ābandhanadhātu, tejodhātūti paripācanadhātu, vāyodhātūti vitthambhanadhātu, ākāsadhātūti asamphuṭṭhadhātu, viññāṇadhātūti vijānanadhātu. Iminā kiṃ dassitaṃ hoti? Dhātumanasikāro. Imasmiṃ pana dhātumanasikāre kammaṃ katvā vipassanaṃ paṭṭhapetvā uttamatthaṃ arahattaṃ pāpuṇitukāmena catupārisuddhisīlaṃ sodhetabbaṃ, dasa pubbapalibodhā chinditabbā, ācariyassa santike sattavidhena uggahakosallena dasavidhena ca manasikārakosallena kammaṭṭhānaṃ uggaṇhitabbaṃ, tato pañcaṅgasamannāgate senāsane vivittokāsagatena vaṇṇasaṇṭhānadisokāsaparicchedavasena kesādīsu ekeko koṭṭhāso 'acetano abyākato suñño nissatto'ti manasikātabbo. Tiṇṇaṃ pana chakkānaṃ vasena aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo pariggahitāva honti; iti imasmiṃ suttantabhājanīye soḷasa dhātuyo kāmāvacarā, dve tebhūmikāti evamettha sammasanacārova kathitoti veditabbo.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sarūpeneva sabbāpi dhātuyo dassento aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo — cakkhudhātu rūpadhātūtiādimāha; tattha uddesavāre atthādivasena vinicchayo veditabbo. Saṅkhato hi cakkhudhātu jātito cakkhupasādavasena eko dhammotveva saṅkhaṃ gacchati, tathā sotaghānajivhākāyarūpasaddagandharasadhātuyo; phoṭṭhabbadhātu pana pathavītejovāyovasena tayo dhammā; cakkhuviññāṇadhātu kusalākusalavipākavasena dve dhammā, tathā sotaghānajivhākāyaviññāṇadhātuyo; manodhātu pañcadvārāvajjanakusalākusalavipākasampaṭicchanavasena tayo dhammā; dhammadhātu tiṇṇaṃ arūpakkhandhānaṃ soḷasannaṃ sukhumarūpānaṃ asaṅkhatāya ca dhātuyā vasena vīsati dhammā; manoviññāṇadhātu sesakusalākusalābyākataviññāṇavasena chasattati dhammāti saṅkhaṃ gacchati. Niddesavāre pana 'cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe cā'ti vuttattā cakkhuviññāṇadhātuyā cakkhu nissayapaccayo hoti, rūpaṃ ārammaṇapaccayo, kiriyamanodhātu vigatapaccayo, tayo arūpakkhandhā sahajātapaccayoti ime cattāro paṭicca esā uppajjati nāma; sotañca paṭiccātiādīsupi eseva nayo.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye tīhi chakkehi dhātuyo saṅkhipitvā dassitā; tesu paṭhamena dhātumanasikāro dassito. Imasmiṃ pana dhātumanasikāre kammaṃ katvā vipassanaṃ paṭṭhapetvā uttamatthaṃ arahattaṃ pāpuṇitukāmena catupārisuddhisīlaṃ sodhetabbaṃ — sīlavato hi kammaṭṭhānabhāvanā ijjhati; visuddhasīlena ca sīle patiṭṭhāya dasa pubbapalibodhā chinditabbā, chinnapalibodhena sattavidhena uggahakosallena dasavidhena ca manasikārakosallena kammaṭṭhānaṃ nijjaṭaṃ paguṇaṃ kātabbaṃ, kesādīsu ekekakoṭṭhāso "acetano abyākato suñño nissatto"ti manasikātabbo. Tatiyacchakke ca kāmadhātu byāpādadhātu vihiṃsādhātūti tayo akusalavitakkā, nekkhammadhātu abyāpādadhātu avihiṃsādhātūti tayo kusalā dassitā; abyāpādo ca mettāya adhivacanaṃ, avihiṃsā karuṇāya, nekkhammaṃ sabbassāpi kusalassa. Byāpādo hi sattesu uppannoyeva kammapathaṃ bhindati, tathā vihiṃsā. Tasmā yoginā kāmabyāpādavihiṃsāvitakke pahāya, nekkhammaṃ mettaṃ karuṇañca upasampādetvā, sīle patiṭṭhitena dhātumanasikārena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sarūpeneva aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo dassitā; vijānanasabhāve viññāṇe jīvasaññīnaṃ jīvasaññāsamūhanatthaṃ veneyyajjhāsayavasena ca tā pakāsitā. Sabbā eva hi saṅkhatā dhātuyo pubbantāparantavivittato, dhuvasubhasukhattabhāvasuññato, paccayāyattavuttito ca daṭṭhabbā. Visesato ca dhammadhātuyā vedanākkhandho sallamiva sūlamiva ca daṭṭhabbo, puthujjanānaṃ saññā āsādukkhajananato rittamuṭṭhi viya ayathābhuccanimittaggāhakato vanamigo viya, saṅkhārā aṅgārakāsuyaṃ khipanakapuriso viya rājapurisānubandhacorā viya visarukkhabījāni viya, rūpaṃ nānāvidhūpaddavanimittato khuracakkaṃ viya; manoviññāṇadhātu ca vanamakkaṭo viya assakhaḷuṅko viya raṅganaṭo viya duddamanato daṭṭhabbā; asaṅkhatā pana dhātu amatato santato khemato ca daṭṭhabbā. Tasmā yoginā dhātūsu jīvasaññaṃ attagāhañca pahāya, imā upamā manasikatvā saṅkhatadhātūsu nibbidaṃ upasampādetvā, duddamaṃ manoviññāṇadhātuṃ satiyā damayanto, asaṅkhatāya khemāya amatāya dhātuyā adhimuccanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `AI_translation/english/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

182. Therein, what is the element of sense desire? Thinking connected with sense desire – thought, intention, fixing, focusing, directing the mind, wrong intention – this is called the element of sense desire. Taking the Avīci hell as the lower limit and the gods who wield power over others' creations as the upper limit, whatever within this range moves here and is included here – the aggregates, elements, and sense-fields, material form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness – this is called "the element of sense desire".
Therein, what is the element of ill will? Thinking connected with ill will – thought… wrong intention – this is called "the element of ill will". Or the mind's irritation over the ten grounds for resentment: hostility, resistance, opposition; anger, rage, fury; hatred, ill feeling, malice; the mind's vexation, mental resentment; anger, being angry, the state of being angry; hatred, hating, the state of hating; vexation, being vexed; opposition, hostility; ferocity, harshness, and the mind's displeasure – this is called "the element of ill will".
Therein, what is the element of cruelty? Thinking connected with cruelty – thought… wrong intention – this is called "the element of cruelty". Here someone harms living beings with a hand, a clod, a stick, a knife, a rope, or with one thing or another; such tormenting, harassing, hurting, injuring, provoking, and attacking of others – this is called "the element of cruelty".
Therein, what is the element of renunciation? Thinking connected with renunciation – thought… right intention – this is called "the element of renunciation". Indeed, all wholesome states are "the element of renunciation".
Therein, what is the element of goodwill? Thinking connected with goodwill – thought… right intention – this is called "the element of goodwill". Loving-kindness towards living beings, the act of loving, the state of loving, the liberation of mind through loving-kindness – this is called "the element of goodwill".
Therein, what is the element of harmlessness? Thinking connected with harmlessness – thought, intention, fixing, focusing, directing the mind, right intention – this is called "the element of harmlessness". Compassion towards living beings, the act of compassion, the state of compassion, the liberation of mind through compassion – this is called "the element of harmlessness".
These are the six elements.
Thus these three sets of six, gathered and grouped together, make eighteen elements.
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-182

#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-3-2-0

183. There are eighteen elements – the eye element, the element of sights, the eye-consciousness element; the ear element, the element of sounds, the ear-consciousness element; the nose element, the element of smells, the nose-consciousness element; the tongue element, the element of tastes, the tongue-consciousness element; the body element, the element of things touched, the body-consciousness element; the mind element, the element of mental objects, and the mind-consciousness element. ^2-183

184. Therein, what is the eye element? The eye that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the eye element".
Therein, what is the element of sights? The sight that is the shining of colour derived from the four great elements… this is the element of sights – this is called "the element of sights".
Therein, what is the eye-consciousness element? Depending on the eye and sights there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding eye-consciousness element – this is called "the eye-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the ear element? The ear that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the ear element".
Therein, what is the element of sounds? The sound that is derived from the four great elements, invisible and offering resistance… this is the element of sounds – this is called "the element of sounds".
Therein, what is the ear-consciousness element? Depending on the ear and sounds there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding ear-consciousness element – this is called "the ear-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the nose element? The nose that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the nose element".
Therein, what is the element of smells? The smell that is derived from the four great elements, invisible and offering resistance… this is the element of smells – this is called "the element of smells".
Therein, what is the nose-consciousness element? Depending on the nose and smells there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding nose-consciousness element – this is called "the nose-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the tongue element? The tongue that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the tongue element".
Therein, what is the element of tastes? The taste that is derived from the four great elements, invisible and offering resistance… this is the element of tastes – this is called "the element of tastes".
Therein, what is the tongue-consciousness element? Depending on the tongue and tastes there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding tongue-consciousness element – this is called "the tongue-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the body element? The body that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the body element".
Therein, what is the element of things touched? The earth element… this is the element of things touched – this is called "the element of things touched".
Therein, what is the body-consciousness element? Depending on the body and things touched there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding body-consciousness element – this is called "the body-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the mind element? Immediately after the arising and ceasing of the eye-consciousness element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind element; immediately after the arising and ceasing of the ear-consciousness element… of the nose-consciousness element… of the tongue-consciousness element… of the body-consciousness element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind element; or, at the first turning of attention towards any object whatever there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind element – this is called "the mind element".
Therein, what is the element of mental objects? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the material form that is invisible and offers no resistance and is included in the sense-field of mental objects, and the unconditioned element.
Therein, what is the aggregate of feeling? As a single class, the aggregate of feeling is joined with contact. As a twofold class: there is feeling with a root cause, and feeling without one. As a threefold class: there is wholesome feeling, unwholesome feeling, and neutral feeling… thus the aggregate of feeling is classified up to ten ways… thus the aggregate of feeling is classified in many ways. This is called "the aggregate of feeling".
Therein, what is the aggregate of perception? As a single class, the aggregate of perception is joined with contact. As a twofold class: there is perception with a root cause, and perception without one. As a threefold class: there is wholesome perception, unwholesome perception, and neutral perception… thus the aggregate of perception is classified up to ten ways… thus the aggregate of perception is classified in many ways. This is called "the aggregate of perception".
Therein, what is the aggregate of mental formations? As a single class, the aggregate of mental formations is joined with mind. As a twofold class: there are formations that are root causes, and formations that are not. As a threefold class: there are wholesome formations, unwholesome formations, and neutral formations… thus the aggregate of mental formations is classified up to ten ways… thus the aggregate of mental formations is classified in many ways. This is called "the aggregate of mental formations".
Therein, what is the material form that is invisible, offers no resistance, and is included in the sense-field of mental objects? The faculty of femininity… physical food – this is called the material form that is "invisible, offers no resistance, and is included in the sense-field of mental objects".
Therein, what is the unconditioned element? The ending of desire, the ending of hatred, and the ending of delusion – this is called "the unconditioned element". This is called "the element of mental objects".
Therein, what is the mind-consciousness element? Immediately after the arising and ceasing of the eye-consciousness element there arises the mind element, and immediately after the arising and ceasing of the mind element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity… and the corresponding mind-consciousness element; immediately after the arising and ceasing of the ear-consciousness element… of the nose-consciousness element… of the tongue-consciousness element… of the body-consciousness element there arises the mind element, and immediately after the arising and ceasing of that mind element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity… and the corresponding mind-consciousness element; and depending on the mind and mental objects there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind-consciousness element – this is called "the mind-consciousness element".
