| Day | Date | Section | Verses |

| day-028 | 10 Jun (Thu) | 1. Cittuppādakaṇḍaṃ | 455–477 |

Note: vv. 455–468 cover ##### Kusalavipākamanodhātu (^1-1-7-2-0), the wholesome-resultant mind-element (the "receiving" consciousness). At v. 469 the range crosses into ##### Kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātusomanassasahagatā (^1-1-7-3-0), the joy-accompanied mind-consciousness element (the "investigating" consciousness); vv. 469–477 run through it to pīti.

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Kusalavipākamanodhātu

[[#^toc-2-7-2|↑↑↑]]

Manodhātu sabhāvasuññatanissattaṭṭhena manoyeva dhātu; cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ anantaraṃ rūpādivijānanalakkhaṇā, rūpādīnaṃ sampaṭicchanarasā, tathābhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā, cakkhuviññāṇādiapagamapadaṭṭhānā. Vatthu nibaddhaṃ hadayavatthumeva; dvārārammaṇāni pana anibaddhāni – pañcadvāre ārammaṇānaṃ saṅkamantesu sampaṭicchanaṃ hutvā vipaccati, kusalavipākesu cakkhuviññāṇādīsu niruddhesu taṃsamanantarā tāneva ṭhānappattāni rūpārammaṇādīni sampaṭicchati. Jhānaṅgampi pañcaviññāṇasote patitvā gatattāyeva na uddhaṭaṃ; maggaṅgāni ca alābhatoyeva.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1387]]

#### Kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātusomanassasahagatā

[[#^toc-2-7-3|↑↑↑]]

Paṭhamā kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu iṭṭhārammaṇasmiṃyeva pavattati, tasmā somanassavedanā hoti. Dvīsu ṭhānesu vipaccati – pañcadvāre kusalavipākacakkhuviññāṇādianantaraṃ vipākamanodhātuyā taṃ ārammaṇaṃ sampaṭicchitvā niruddhāya santīraṇakiccaṃ sādhayamānā, chasu dvāresu ca balavārammaṇe javanena gahitārammaṇaṃ gahetvā tadārammaṇabhāvena. Tadārammaṇaṃ patiṭṭhamānaṃ bhavaṅgassa vāraṃ adatvā ekaṃ dve cittavāre pavattitvā bhavaṅgameva otarati. Ubhayatthāpi pañcaviññāṇasote patitvā gatattāyeva jhānaṅgamaggaṅgāni na uddhaṭāni.

### Kusalavipākamanodhātu

[[#^toc-1-7-2|↑↑↑]]

Kusalavipākamanodhātu iṭṭhārammaṇe sampaṭicchanavasena uppajjati. Sampaṭicchanaṃ ārammaṇaṃ paṭiggaṇhanalakkhaṇaṃ; imehi dhammānupassanāya "idaṃ vipākaṃ" ti ñatvā ārammaṇaggahaṇe upekkhāṃ bhāveti.

### Kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātusomanassasahagatā

[[#^toc-1-7-3|↑↑↑]]

Kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu somanassasahagatā santīraṇavasena uppajjati. Somanassena vipākaṃ anubhavantassa ciттassa "idaṃ kammaphalaṃ" ti sammapajānanaṃ cittapariyodāpanāya hoti.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Full range vv. 455–477 lives in the translation file (block IDs ^1-455 … ^1-477). First and last verses copied here for §4:

**v. 455.** When, due to a wholesome kamma of the sense sphere having been done and accumulated, a resultant mind-element accompanied by equanimity has arisen, having a visible object or…pe… a tangible object or whatever it takes as object, then on that occasion there is contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, initial application, sustained application, equanimity, one-pointedness of mind, mind faculty, faculty of equanimity, life faculty; or whatever other dependently arisen formless phenomena there are on that occasion—these phenomena are indeterminate. ^1-455

**v. 477.** What is rapture at that time? That which at that time is rapture, joy, gladness, delight, mirth, exhilaration, thrill, elation, satisfaction of mind – this is rapture at that time. ^1-477
