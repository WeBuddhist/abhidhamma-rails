## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhidukaatthuddhāraniddese ānantarikāni kammāni, niyatamicchādiṭṭhi ca sammattaniyamatāya paccaṭṭhitā. Atthuddhārakaṇḍo ca tepiṭakassa buddhavacanassa atthuddhārabhūto yāva saraṇadukā nikkhittaṃ, sakale abhidhammapiṭake nayamaggaṃ mahāpakaraṇe pañhuddhāraṃ gaṇanacāraṃ asallakkhentenāpi aṭṭhakathākaṇḍatoyeva samānetuṃ vaṭṭatīti. Aṭṭhasāliniyā dhammasaṅgahaaṭṭhakathāya aṭṭhakathākaṇḍavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitāti.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhidukaatthuddhāre ānantarikāni kammāni niyatamicchādiṭṭhi ca sammattaniyāmapaṭibaddhabhāvena, saraṇadukāvasānā ca dukā uddhaṭā. Iti ayaṃ atthuddhārakaṇḍo sakalassa tepiṭakabuddhavacanassa atthaṃ uddharitvā ṭhito. Tasmā yoginā niyatamicchādiṭṭhiṃ ānantarikakammañca sabbaso vajjetvā, sammattaniyāmaṃ ariyamaggaṃ okkamanto, buddhadhammasaṅghesu saraṇagamanaṃ patiṭṭhāpetvā, cittaṃ accantaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Which phenomena are associated with happiness? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by joy, four unwholesome consciousnesses, six resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, five functional consciousnesses, the third and fourth fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the third and fourth supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant types), excluding happiness itself that arises therein – these phenomena are associated with happiness. ^1-1597

Which phenomena are not associated with happiness? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by equanimity, eight unwholesome consciousnesses, ten resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, seven resultant unwholesome consciousnesses, six functional consciousnesses, the fourth fine-material jhāna (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the four immaterial consciousnesses (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the fourth supramundane jhāna (wholesome and resultant types), happiness itself, all material phenomena, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not associated with happiness. ^1-1598

Which phenomena are associated with equanimity? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by equanimity, six unwholesome consciousnesses, ten resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, six resultant unwholesome consciousnesses, six functional consciousnesses, the fourth fine-material jhāna (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the four immaterial consciousnesses (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the fourth supramundane jhāna (wholesome and resultant types), excluding equanimity itself that arises therein – these phenomena are associated with equanimity. ^1-1599

Which phenomena are not associated with equanimity? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by joy, six unwholesome consciousnesses, six resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, one resultant unwholesome consciousness, five functional consciousnesses, the third and fourth fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the third and fourth supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant types), equanimity itself, all material phenomena, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not associated with equanimity. ^1-1600

Which phenomena belong to the sense-sphere? All sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, all unwholesome consciousnesses, all resultant sense-sphere consciousnesses, all functional sense-sphere consciousnesses, and all material phenomena – these phenomena belong to the sense-sphere. ^1-1601

Which phenomena do not belong to the sense-sphere? All fine-material sphere phenomena, all immaterial sphere phenomena, and all unincluded phenomena – these phenomena do not belong to the sense-sphere. ^1-1602

Which phenomena are fine-material sphere? The fourfold and fivefold fine-material absorptions, whether wholesome, resultant, or functional – these phenomena are fine-material sphere. ^1-1603

Which phenomena are not fine-material sphere? Sense-sphere, immaterial-sphere, and unincluded phenomena – these phenomena are not fine-material sphere. ^1-1604

Which phenomena are immaterial sphere? The four immaterial attainments, whether wholesome, resultant, or functional – these phenomena are immaterial sphere. ^1-1605

Which phenomena are not immaterial sphere? Sense-sphere, fine-material sphere, and unincluded phenomena – these phenomena are not immaterial sphere. ^1-1606

Which phenomena are included? Wholesome, unwholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate phenomena in the three planes, and all matter – these phenomena are included. ^1-1607

Which phenomena are unincluded? The four transcendent paths, the four transcendent fruits, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are unincluded. ^1-1608

Which phenomena lead to liberation? The four transcendent paths – these phenomena lead to liberation. ^1-1609

Which phenomena do not lead to liberation? Wholesome and unwholesome phenomena in the three planes, resultant phenomena in the four planes, functional indeterminate phenomena in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena do not lead to liberation. ^1-1610

Which phenomena are fixed? The four consciousnesses associated with wrong view and the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure – these phenomena are sometimes fixed, sometimes unfixed. The four transcendent paths – these phenomena are fixed. ^1-1611

Which phenomena are unfixed? The four consciousnesses rooted in greed dissociated from wrong view, the consciousness accompanied by doubt, the consciousness accompanied by restlessness, wholesome phenomena in the three planes, resultant phenomena in the four planes, functional indeterminate phenomena in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are unfixed. ^1-1612

Which phenomena are with superior? Wholesome, unwholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate phenomena in the three planes, and all matter 
– these phenomena are with superior. ^1-1613

Which phenomena are without superior? The four supramundane paths, the four fruitions of recluseship, and Nibbāna 
– these phenomena are without superior. ^1-1614

Which phenomena are with refuge/defilement? The twelve unwholesome consciousness-arisings 
– these phenomena are with refuge/defilement. ^1-1615

Which phenomena are without refuge/defilement? Wholesome and resultant phenomena in the four planes, functional indeterminate phenomena in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna 
– these phenomena are without refuge/defilement.
The summary of meanings is concluded.
The treatise Dhammasaṅgaṇī is concluded. ^1-1616
