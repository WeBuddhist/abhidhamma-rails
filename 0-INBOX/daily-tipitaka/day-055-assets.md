1007. Tattha katamā sakkāyadiṭṭhi? Idha assutavā puthujjano ariyānaṃ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akovido ariyadhamme avinīto sappurisānaṃ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akovido sappurisadhamme avinīto rūpaṃ attato samanupassati, rūpavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā rūpaṃ, rūpasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Vedanaṃ attato samanupassati, vedanāvantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā vedanaṃ, vedanāya vā attānaṃ. Saññaṃ attato samanupassati, saññāvantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā saññaṃ, saññāya vā attānaṃ. Saṅkhāre attato samanupassati, saṅkhāravantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā saṅkhāre, saṅkhāresu vā attānaṃ. Viññāṇaṃ attato samanupassati, viññāṇavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā viññāṇaṃ, viññāṇasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ diṭṭhigahanaṃ diṭṭhikantāro diṭṭhivisūkāyikaṃ [diṭṭhivisūkāyitaṃ (sī.)] diṭṭhivipphanditaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ gāho paṭiggāho abhiniveso parāmāso kummaggo micchāpatho micchattaṃ titthāyatanaṃ vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati sakkāyadiṭṭhi. ^1-1007

1008. Tattha katamā vicikicchā? Yā evarūpā kaṅkhā kaṅkhāyanā kaṅkhāyitattaṃ vimati vicikicchā dveḷhakaṃ dvedhāpatho saṃsayo anekaṃsaggāho āsappanā parisappanā apariyogāhanā thambhitattaṃ cittassa manovilekho – ayaṃ vuccati vicikicchā. ^1-1008

1009. Tattha katamo sīlabbataparāmāso? ## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

### Tikanikkhepaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1|↑↑↑]]

Tikanikkhepakathāyaṃ tikanikkhepaṃ ārabhibbā kusalapadaniddese tīṇīti gaṇanaparicchedo. Kusalāni ca tāni mūlāni ca, kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ hetupaccayapabhavajanakasamuṭṭhānanibbattakaṭṭhena mūlānīti kusalamūlāni. Evaṃ atthavasena dassetvā nāmavasena dassetuṃ alobho adoso amohoti āha. Taṃsampayuttoti tehi sampayutto, taṃsamuṭṭhānanti tehi samuṭṭhitaṃ. Tīhi ṭhānesu catubhūmakakusalaṃ pariyādiyitvā dassesi dhammarājā. Akusalepi eseva nayo; dassanena pahātabbahetukesupi eseva desanānayo veditabbo.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

### Tikanikkhepaṃ

[[#^toc-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Tikanikkhepe paṭhamaṃ kusalattikaṃ nikkhittaṃ — "katame dhammā kusalā? Tīṇi kusalamūlāni alobho adoso amoho"ti. Alobho dānassa, adoso mettāsīlassa, amoho paññābhāvanāya mūlaṃ; tabbiparītā lobhadosamohā akusalamūlāni. Aparo tiko "dassanena pahātabbā, bhāvanāya pahātabbā, neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbā"ti pahānabhūmiṃ dasseti. Tasmā yoginā tīṇi akusalamūlāni pahānāya, tīṇi kusalamūlāni upasampadāya ca ussahitvā, dassanena bhāvanāya ca pahātabbe kilese kamena samugghātetuṃ maggo bhāvetabbo; evaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpeti.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Which phenomena are wholesome? The three wholesome roots—non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion; the feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, formation aggregate, and consciousness aggregate associated with them; and the bodily kamma, verbal kamma, and mental kamma arisen from them—these phenomena are wholesome. ^1-985
Which phenomena are unwholesome? The three unwholesome roots—greed, hatred, delusion; and the defilements co-existent with them; the feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, formation aggregate, and consciousness aggregate associated with them; and the bodily kamma, verbal kamma, and mental kamma arisen from them—these phenomena are unwholesome. ^1-986
Which phenomena are indeterminate? The resultants of wholesome and unwholesome phenomena in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and supramundane; the feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, formation aggregate, and consciousness aggregate; and those functional phenomena that are neither wholesome nor unwholesome nor kamma-resultants; and all matter, and the unconditioned element—these phenomena are indeterminate. ^1-987
Which phenomena are associated with pleasant feeling? In the plane of pleasant feeling, in the sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and supramundane, excluding pleasant feeling; the perception aggregate, formation aggregate, and consciousness aggregate associated with it—these phenomena are associated with pleasant feeling. ^1-988
Which phenomena are associated with painful feeling? In the plane of painful feeling, in the sense sphere, excluding painful feeling; the perception aggregate, formation aggregate, and consciousness aggregate associated with it—these phenomena are associated with painful feeling. ^1-989
The aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness, in the sense, fine-material, immaterial, and transcendent spheres, associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling, excluding the feeling itself. ^1-990
The results of wholesome and unwholesome phenomena in the sense, fine-material, immaterial, and transcendent spheres, namely, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness. ^1-991
Wholesome and unwholesome phenomena in the sense, fine-material, immaterial, and transcendent spheres, namely, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness. ^1-992
Functional phenomena that are neither wholesome nor unwholesome and not kamma-results; all material phenomena; and the unconditioned element. ^1-993
The results of wholesome and unwholesome phenomena, subject to taints, in the sense, fine-material, and immaterial spheres, namely, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness; and matter born of kamma. ^1-994
Wholesome and unwholesome phenomena, subject to taints, in the sense, fine-material, and immaterial spheres, namely, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness; functional phenomena that are neither wholesome nor unwholesome and not kamma-results; and matter not born of kamma. ^1-995
The transcendent paths, their fruits, and the unconditioned element. ^1-996
The three unwholesome roots – greed, hatred, delusion; associated defilements; the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness associated with them; and bodily, verbal, and mental action arising from them. ^1-997
Wholesome and indeterminate phenomena, subject to taints, in the sense, fine-material, and immaterial spheres, namely, the aggregate of matter, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness. ^1-998
The transcendent paths, their fruits, and the unconditioned element. ^1-999
The feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, volitional formations aggregate, and consciousness aggregate, associated with them, in the sphere of initial and sustained application (sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and supramundane), excluding initial application and sustained application. These phenomena are accompanied by initial application and sustained application. ^1-1000
The feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, volitional formations aggregate, and consciousness aggregate, associated with them, in the sphere of only sustained application (fine-material sphere and supramundane), excluding sustained application. These phenomena are without initial application, only sustained application. ^1-1001
The feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, volitional formations aggregate, and consciousness aggregate, in the sphere without initial and sustained application (sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and supramundane); all material phenomena; and the unconditioned element. These phenomena are without initial application and without sustained application. ^1-1002
The feeling aggregate, perception aggregate, volitional formations aggregate, and consciousness aggregate, associated with them, in the sphere of joy (sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and supramundane), excluding joy. These phenomena are accompanied by joy. ^1-1003
The perception aggregate, volitional formations aggregate, and consciousness aggregate, associated with them, in the sphere of happiness (sense sphere, fine-material sphere, and supramundane), excluding happiness. These phenomena are accompanied by happiness. ^1-1004
The perception aggregate, volitional formations aggregate, and consciousness aggregate, associated with them, in the sphere of equanimity (sense sphere, fine-material sphere, immaterial sphere, and supramundane), excluding equanimity. These phenomena are accompanied by equanimity. ^1-1005
The three fetters: personality view, doubt, and adherence to rites and rituals. ^1-1006
Here, an uninstructed worldling, not seeing noble ones, unskilled in the Dhamma of noble ones, untrained in the Dhamma of noble ones; not seeing good men, unskilled in the Dhamma of good men, untrained in the Dhamma of good men, regards form as self, or self as having form, or form as in self, or self as in form; regards feeling as self, or self as having feeling, or feeling as in self, or self as in feeling; regards perception as self, or self as having perception, or perception as in self, or self as in perception; regards volitional formations as self, or self as having volitional formations, or volitional formations as in self, or self as in volitional formations; regards consciousness as self, or self as having consciousness, or consciousness as in self, or self as in consciousness. Whatever such view, wrong view, thicket of views, wilderness of views, distortion of views, vacillation of views, fetter of views, grasping, clinging, adherence, obsession, wrong path, false way, wrongness, sectarian position, inverted grasp – this is called personality view. ^1-1007
Doubts, is perplexed about, the Teacher; doubts, is perplexed about, the Dhamma; doubts, is perplexed about, the Saṅgha; doubts, is perplexed about, the training; doubts, is perplexed about, the past; doubts, is perplexed about, the future; doubts, is perplexed about, both past and future; doubts, is perplexed about, dependent arising and dependently arisen phenomena. Whatever such wavering, state of wavering, perplexity, doubt, two-way path, uncertainty, grasping at many sides, vacillation, wandering, lack of penetration, stiffness of mind, mental defilement – this is called doubt. ^1-1008
Among recluses and brahmins outside this doctrine, the view 'purification by virtue, purification by rites, purification by virtue and rites'. Whatever such view, wrong view, thicket of views, wilderness of views, distortion of views, vacillation of views, fetter of views, grasping, clinging, adherence, obsession, wrong path, false way, wrongness, sectarian position, inverted grasp – this is called adherence to rites and rituals. ^1-1009
These three fetters; and defilements associated with them; the feeling aggregate... up to the consciousness aggregate associated with them; bodily action, vocal action, mental action arising from them – these phenomena are to be abandoned by seeing. ^1-1010
