##### (14) Cha diṭṭhiyo ^2-17-7-14-0

Tattha katamā cha diṭṭhiyo? "Atthi me attā"ti vā assa saccato thetato diṭṭhi uppajjati, "natthi me attā"ti vā assa saccato thetato diṭṭhi uppajjati, "attanā vā attānaṃ sañjānāmī"ti vā assa saccato thetato diṭṭhi uppajjati, "attanā vā anattānaṃ sañjānāmī"ti vā assa saccato thetato diṭṭhi uppajjati, "anattanā vā attānaṃ sañjānāmī"ti vā assa saccato thetato diṭṭhi uppajjati; atha vā panassa evaṃdiṭṭhi hoti – "so me ayaṃ attā vado vedeyyo tatra tatra dīgharattaṃ kalyāṇapāpakānaṃ kammānaṃ vipākaṃ paccanubhoti. Na so jāto nāhosi, na so jāto na bhavissati, nicco dhuvo sassato avipariṇāmadhammo"ti vā panassa saccato thetato diṭṭhi uppajjati. Imā cha diṭṭhiyo.
Chakkaṃ. ^2-948

#### Sattakaniddeso ^2-17-8-0

##### (1) Sattānusayā ^2-17-8-1-0

Tattha katame sattānusayā? Kāmarāgānusayo, paṭighānusayo, mānānusayo, diṭṭhānusayo, vicikicchānusayo, bhavarāgānusayo, avijjānusayo – ime satta anusayā. ^2-949

##### (2) Satta saṃyojanāni ^2-17-8-2-0

Tattha katamāni satta saṃyojanāni? Kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ, mānasaṃyojanaṃ, diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ, vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ, bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ – imāni satta saṃyojanāni. ^2-U69

##### (3) Satta pariyuṭṭhānāni ^2-17-8-3-0

Tattha katamāni satta pariyuṭṭhānāni? Kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhānaṃ, paṭighapariyuṭṭhānaṃ, mānapariyuṭṭhānaṃ, diṭṭhipariyuṭṭhānaṃ, vicikicchāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ, bhavarāgapariyuṭṭhānaṃ, avijjāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ – imāni satta pariyuṭṭhānāni. ^2-U70

##### (4) Satta asaddhammā ^2-17-8-4-0

Tattha katame satta asaddhammā? Assaddho hoti, ahiriko hoti, anottappī hoti, appassuto hoti, kusīto hoti, muṭṭhassatī hoti, duppañño hoti – ime satta asaddhammā. ^2-950

##### (5) Satta duccaritāni ^2-17-8-5-0

Tattha katamāni satta duccaritāni? Pāṇātipāto, adinnādānaṃ, kāmesumicchācāro, musāvādo, pisuṇā vācā, pharusā vācā, samphappalāpo – imāni satta duccaritāni. ^2-U71

##### (6) Satta mānā ^2-17-8-6-0

Tattha katame satta mānā? Māno, atimāno, mānātimāno, omāno, adhimāno, asmimāno, micchāmāno – ime satta mānā. ^2-U72

##### (7) Satta diṭṭhiyo ^2-17-8-7-0

(Ka) tattha katamā satta diṭṭhiyo? Idhekacco samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā evaṃvādī hoti evaṃdiṭṭhi – "yato kho, bho, ayaṃ attā rūpī cātumahābhūtiko [cātummahābhūtiko (sī. syā.)] mātāpettikasambhavo kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho bho, ayaṃ attā sammā samucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti.
(Kha) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. ‘Neso natthī’ti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā sammā samucchinno hoti. Atthi kho bho añño attā dibbo rūpī kāmāvacaro kabaḷīkārabhakkho. Taṃ tvaṃ na jānāsi na passasi. Tamahaṃ jānāmi passāmi. So kho, bho, attā yato kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sammāsamucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti.
(Ga) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. ‘Neso natthī’ti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā sammā samucchinno hoti. Atthi kho, bho, añño attā dibbo rūpī manomayo sabbaṅgapaccaṅgī ahīnindriyo. Taṃ tvaṃ na jānāsi na passasi. Tamahaṃ jānāmi passāmi. So kho, bho, attā yato kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sammā samucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti.
(Gha) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. ‘Neso natthī’ti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā sammā samucchinno hoti. Atthi kho, bho, añño attā sabbaso rūpasaññānaṃ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṃ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṃ amanasikārā ananto ākāsoti ākāsānañcāyatanūpago. Taṃ tvaṃ na jānāsi na passasi. Tamahaṃ jānāmi passāmi. So kho, bho, attā yato kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sammā samucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti.
(Ṅa) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. ‘Neso natthī’ti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā sammā samucchinno hoti. Atthi kho, bho, añño attā sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ samatikkamma anantaṃ viññāṇanti viññāṇañcāyatanūpago. Taṃ tvaṃ na jānāsi na passasi. Tamahaṃ jānāmi passāmi. So kho, bho, attā yato kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sammā samucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti.
(Ca) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. ‘Neso natthī’ti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā sammā samucchinno hoti. Atthi kho, bho, añño attā sabbaso viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ samatikkamma natthi kiñcīti ākiñcaññāyatanūpago. Taṃ tvaṃ na jānāsi na passasi. Tamahaṃ jānāmi passāmi. So kho, bho, attā yato kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sammā samucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti.
(Cha) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho pana eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. ‘Neso natthī’ti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā sammā samucchinno hoti. Atthi kho, bho, añño attā sabbaso ākiñcaññāyatanaṃ samatikkamma nevasaññānāsaññāyatanūpago. Taṃ tvaṃ na jānāsi na passasi. Tamahaṃ jānāmi passāmi. So kho, bho, attā yato kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṃ maraṇā, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sammā samucchinno hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa ucchedaṃ vināsaṃ vibhavaṃ paññapenti. Imā satta diṭṭhiyo.
Sattakaṃ. ^2-951

#### Aṭṭhakaniddeso ^2-17-9-0

##### (1) Aṭṭha kilesavatthūni ^2-17-9-1-0

Tattha katamāni aṭṭha kilesavatthūni? Lobho, doso, moho, māno, diṭṭhi, vicikicchā, thinaṃ, uddhaccaṃ – imāni aṭṭha kilesavatthūni. ^2-952

##### (2) Aṭṭha kusītavatthūni ^2-17-9-2-0

Tattha katamāni aṭṭha kusītavatthūni?
(Ka) idha bhikkhunā kammaṃ kātabbaṃ hoti. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "kammaṃ kho me kātabbaṃ bhavissati. Kammaṃ kho pana me karontassa kāyo kilamissati. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ paṭhamaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Kha) puna caparaṃ bhikkhunā kammaṃ kataṃ hoti. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "ahaṃ kho kammaṃ akāsiṃ. Kammaṃ kho pana me karontassa kāyo kilanto. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ dutiyaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Ga) puna caparaṃ bhikkhunā maggo gantabbo hoti. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "maggo kho me gantabbo bhavissati. Maggaṃ kho pana me gacchantassa kāyo kilamissati. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ tatiyaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Gha) puna caparaṃ bhikkhunā maggo gato hoti. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "ahaṃ kho maggaṃ agamāsiṃ. Maggaṃ kho pana me gacchantassa kāyo kilanto. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ catutthaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Ṅa) puna caparaṃ bhikkhu gāmaṃ vā nigamaṃ vā piṇḍāya caranto na labhati lūkhassa vā paṇītassa vā bhojanassa yāvadatthaṃ pāripūriṃ. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "ahaṃ kho gāmaṃ vā nigamaṃ vā piṇḍāya caranto nālatthaṃ lūkhassa vā paṇītassa vā bhojanassa yāvadatthaṃ pāripūriṃ. Tassa me kāyo kilanto akammañño. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ pañcamaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Ca) puna caparaṃ bhikkhu gāmaṃ vā nigamaṃ vā piṇḍāya caranto labhati lūkhassa vā paṇītassa vā bhojanassa yāvadatthaṃ pāripūriṃ. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "ahaṃ kho gāmaṃ vā nigamaṃ vā piṇḍāya caranto alatthaṃ lūkhassa vā paṇītassa vā bhojanassa yāvadatthaṃ pāripūriṃ. Tassa me kāyo kilanto akammañño māsācitaṃ maññe. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ chaṭṭhaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Cha) puna caparaṃ bhikkhuno uppanno hoti appamattako ābādho. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "uppanno kho me ayaṃ appamattako ābādho. Atthi kappo nipajjituṃ. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ sattamaṃ kusītavatthu.
(Ja) puna caparaṃ bhikkhu gilānā vuṭṭhito [gilānavuṭṭhito (saddanībhi) a. ni. 6.16 pāḷiyā ṭīkā passitabbā] hoti aciravuṭṭhito gelaññā. Tassa evaṃ hoti – "ahaṃ kho gilānā vuṭṭhito aciravuṭṭhito gelaññā. Tassa me kāyo dubbalo akammañño. Handāhaṃ nipajjāmī"ti. So nipajjati; na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā, anadhigatassa adhigamāya, asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya. Idaṃ aṭṭhamaṃ kusītavatthu. Imāni aṭṭha kusītavatthūni. ^2-953

### Chakkaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-7|↑↑↑]]

Chakkaniddese yasmā kuddho vā kodhavasena sandiṭṭhiparāmāsī vā sandiṭṭhiparāmāsitāya kalahaṃ viggahaṃ vivādaṃ āpajjati, tasmā kodhādayo vivādamūlānīti vuttā. Manāpiyesu rūpādīsu kāmagehasitā chandarāgā, amanāpiyesu virodhavatthūni. Agāravesu yo satthari dharamāne upaṭṭhānaṃ na yāti, parinibbute cetiyaṃ vandituṃ na gacchati, ayaṃ satthari agāravo; yo sakkaccaṃ dhammaṃ na suṇāti, ayaṃ dhamme agāravo; yo vuḍḍhe bhikkhū ghaṭṭento gacchati, ayaṃ saṅghe agāravo; tisso sikkhā apūrayamāno sikkhāya, appamādalakkhaṇaṃ ananubrūhayamāno appamāde, duvidhaṃ paṭisanthāraṃ akaronto paṭisanthāre agāravo nāma. Kammabhassaniddāsaṅgaṇikāsaṃsaggapapañcārāmatā parihānakarā dhammā. Cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā somanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ domanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ upekkhāṭṭhāniyaṃ rūpaṃ upavicaratīti somanassupavicārādayo; gehasitāni kāmaguṇanissitāni, upekkhā panettha aññāṇupekkhā. ‘Atthi me attā’ti sassatadiṭṭhi, ‘natthi me attā’ti ucchedadiṭṭhi, ‘attanā vā attānaṃ sañjānāmī’tiādayopi sassatucchedadiṭṭhiyova, ‘vado vedeyyo… nicco dhuvo sassato avipariṇāmadhammo’ti sassatadiṭṭhiyā abhinivesākārā; evamayaṃ sabbāsavadiṭṭhi nāma kathitā.

### Sattakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-8|↑↑↑]]

Sattakaniddese thāmagataṭṭhena appahīnaṭṭhena ca anusentīti anusayā; vaṭṭasmiṃ satte saṃyojenti ghaṭentīti saṃyojanāni; samudācāravasena pariyuṭṭhahantīti pariyuṭṭhānāni, kāmarāgova pariyuṭṭhānaṃ kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhānaṃ. Asataṃ dhammā, lāmakaṭṭhena vā asantā dhammāti asaddhammā; rāgādīhi dosehi duṭṭhāni caritānīti duccaritāni; tena tenākārena maññantīti mānā. Diṭṭhiniddese satta ucchedavādā kathitā – paṭhamo rūpakāyasīsena cātumahābhūtikaṃ mātāpettikasambhavaṃ manussattabhāvaṃ attāti vadati, dutiyo taṃ paṭikkhipitvā dibbaṃ kāmāvacaraṃ kabaḷīkārabhakkhaṃ, tatiyo dibbaṃ rūpiṃ manomayaṃ sabbaṅgapaccaṅgiṃ ahīnindriyaṃ, tato paraṃ ākāsānañcāyatanūpagādayo arūpattabhāve attāti vadanti, sabbepi ca ‘kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassatī’ti gaṇhanti.

### Aṭṭhakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-9|↑↑↑]]

Aṭṭhakaniddese kilesāyeva kilesavatthūni; kusītavatthūnīti kusītassa alasassa kosajjakāraṇāni – kammaṃ kātabbaṃ hoti, kataṃ hoti, maggo gantabbo hoti, gato hoti, piṇḍapātaṃ na labhati, labhati ‘māsācitaṃ maññe’ti garuko hoti, appamattako ābādho uppanno hoti, gilānā vuṭṭhito hoti, sabbattha ‘handāhaṃ nipajjāmī’ti osīdanaṃ, appattassa pattiyā duvidhampi vīriyaṃ nārabhati. Lokassa dhammāti lokadhammā, etehi vimutto nāma natthi, buddhānampi honti eva; lābhe sārāgo alābhe paṭivirodho, yase ayase pasaṃsāya nindāya sukhe dukkhe ca uppanno sārāgo vā virodho vā cittaṃ paṭihanatīti paṭighāto. Purisadosesu codito ‘na sarāmī’ti assatibhāvena nibbeṭheti, codakaṃyeva paṭippharati, paccāropeti, aññenāññaṃ paṭicaratīti aññena vacanena aññaṃ paṭicchādeti bahiddhā kathaṃ apanāmeti, kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātukaroti, bāhāvikkhepakaṃ bhaṇati. Asaññīvādesu lābhino kasiṇarūpaṃ attāti gahetvā rūpīti diṭṭhi uppajjati, alābhino takkamatteneva; diṭṭhigatiko hi ummattako viya yaṃ vā taṃ vā gaṇhāti.

### Chakkaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-7|↑↑↑]]

Chakkaniddese kodhādayo kalahaviggahavivādānaṃ mūlāni, satthari dhamme saṅghe sikkhāya appamāde paṭisanthāre ca agāravo, kammabhassaniddāsaṅgaṇikāsaṃsaggapapañcārāmatā parihānakarā dhammā vuttā. Tasmā yoginā vivādamūlāni agāravaṃ parihāniyadhamme ca pahāya, parinibbutepi satthari cetiyaṃ vandanto sakkaccaṃ dhammaṃ suṇanto vuḍḍhesu gāravaṃ karonto tisso sikkhā pūrento appamādaṃ anubrūhento duvidhaṃ paṭisanthāraṃ karonto, cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā somanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ domanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ upekkhāṭṭhāniyaṃ upavicarantaṃ gehasitaṃ cittaṃ sallakkhetvā, ‘atthi me attā’tiādikaṃ sabbāsavadiṭṭhiṃ anupādiyantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Sattakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-8|↑↑↑]]

Sattakaniddese anusayā thāmagataṭṭhena appahīnaṭṭhena anusenti, saṃyojanāni vaṭṭasmiṃ satte saṃyojenti, pariyuṭṭhānāni samudācāravasena pariyuṭṭhahanti, asaddhammā ca asataṃ lāmakānaṃ dhammā vuttā. Tasmā yoginā appahīnaṃ anusayaṃ, vaṭṭasmiṃ ghaṭentaṃ saṃyojanaṃ, samudācārappattaṃ pariyuṭṭhānañca yathāsakaṃ sallakkhetvā, asaddhamme satta duccaritāni ca akaronto, ‘ayaṃ attā rūpī cātumahābhūtiko kāyassa bhedā ucchijjatī’tiādikaṃ ucchedadiṭṭhiṃ pahāya, saddhamme patiṭṭhahantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Aṭṭhakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-9|↑↑↑]]

Aṭṭhakaniddese kusītavatthūni ‘handāhaṃ nipajjāmī’ti osīdanakāraṇāni, aṭṭhasu lokadhammesu sārāgo virodho ca cittaṃ paṭihanati, purisadosā ca codite nibbeṭhanapaṭippharaṇādayo vuttā. Tasmā yoginā kammaṃ katvā maggaṃ gantvā piṇḍapātaṃ labhitvā alabhitvā vā gilānā vuṭṭhitopi ‘handāhaṃ nipajjāmī’ti osīdanaṃ pahāya appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya vīriyaṃ ārabhanto, lābhālābhayasāyasanindāpasaṃsāsukhadukkhesu ‘buddhānampi ete honti evā’ti cittaṃ appaṭihaññamānaṃ katvā, codito ‘na sarāmī’ti anibbeṭhetvā codakaṃ appaṭippharitvā kopaṃ apātukaronto āpattiṃ paṭikarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

##### (14) The six views ^2-17-7-14-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-948]]

Therein, what are the six views? The view arises in one as true and established, "There is a self for me"; or the view arises in one as true and established, "There is no self for me"; or the view arises in one as true and established, "By self I perceive self"; or the view arises in one as true and established, "By self I perceive non-self"; or the view arises in one as true and established, "By non-self I perceive self"; or else one holds a view like this: "It is this self of mine that speaks and feels, that experiences here and there the result of good and evil actions. It was not born and did not exist before; it was not born and will not exist; it is permanent, stable, eternal, not subject to change" — the view arises in one as true and established. These are the six views.
The Sixes. ^2-948

#### The Exposition of the Sevens ^2-17-8-0

##### (1) The seven underlying tendencies ^2-17-8-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-949]]

Therein, what are the seven underlying tendencies? The underlying tendency to sensual lust, the underlying tendency to aversion, the underlying tendency to conceit, the underlying tendency to views, the underlying tendency to doubt, the underlying tendency to lust for existence, the underlying tendency to ignorance — these are the seven underlying tendencies. ^2-949

##### (2) The seven fetters ^2-17-8-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-U69]]

Therein, what are the seven fetters? The fetter of sensual lust, the fetter of aversion, the fetter of conceit, the fetter of views, the fetter of doubt, the fetter of lust for existence, the fetter of ignorance — these are the seven fetters. ^2-U69

##### (3) The seven obsessions ^2-17-8-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-U70]]

Therein, what are the seven obsessions? The obsession of sensual lust, the obsession of aversion, the obsession of conceit, the obsession of views, the obsession of doubt, the obsession of lust for existence, the obsession of ignorance — these are the seven obsessions. ^2-U70

##### (4) The seven bad qualities ^2-17-8-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-950]]

Therein, what are the seven bad qualities? One is without confidence, one is shameless, one is without moral dread, one is of little learning, one is lazy, one is forgetful, one is without wisdom — these are the seven bad qualities. ^2-950

##### (5) The seven kinds of misconduct ^2-17-8-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-U71]]

Therein, what are the seven kinds of misconduct? Killing living beings, taking what is not given, sexual misconduct, false speech, divisive speech, harsh speech, idle chatter — these are the seven kinds of misconduct. ^2-U71

##### (6) The seven conceits ^2-17-8-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-U72]]

Therein, what are the seven conceits? Conceit, exaggerated conceit, conceit beyond conceit, self-belittling conceit, over-estimating conceit, the conceit "I am", wrong conceit — these are the seven conceits. ^2-U72

##### (7) The seven views ^2-17-8-7-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-951]]

(a) Therein, what are the seven views? Here some ascetic or brahmin speaks thus and holds this view: "Since, sir, this self, having material form, made of the four great elements, produced by mother and father, is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being.
(b) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, is not to that extent completely cut off. There is, sir, another self — divine, having material form, belonging to the sense sphere, feeding on solid food. That you do not know or see; that I know and see. Since, sir, that self is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being.
(c) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, is not to that extent completely cut off. There is, sir, another self — divine, having material form, made of mind, complete in all its limbs, not lacking in faculties. That you do not know or see; that I know and see. Since, sir, that self is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being.
(d) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, is not to that extent completely cut off. There is, sir, another self which, completely passing beyond perceptions of material form, with the disappearance of perceptions of resistance, not attending to perceptions of diversity, aware that 'space is infinite', has reached the field of infinite space. That you do not know or see; that I know and see. Since, sir, that self is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being.
(e) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, is not to that extent completely cut off. There is, sir, another self which, completely passing beyond the field of infinite space, aware that 'consciousness is infinite', has reached the field of infinite consciousness. That you do not know or see; that I know and see. Since, sir, that self is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being.
(f) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, is not to that extent completely cut off. There is, sir, another self which, completely passing beyond the field of infinite consciousness, aware that 'there is nothing', has reached the field of nothingness. That you do not know or see; that I know and see. Since, sir, that self is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being.
(g) Another says to him: "There is that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, is not to that extent completely cut off. There is, sir, another self which, completely passing beyond the field of nothingness, has reached the field of neither-perception-nor-non-perception. That you do not know or see; that I know and see. Since, sir, that self is cut off and destroyed with the breakup of the body and does not exist after death, to that extent, sir, this self is completely cut off." Thus some declare the cutting off, destruction, and non-existence of an existing being. These are the seven views.
The Sevens. ^2-951

#### The Exposition of the Eights ^2-17-9-0

##### (1) The eight grounds of defilement ^2-17-9-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-952]]

Therein, what are the eight grounds of defilement? Greed, hate, delusion, conceit, views, doubt, dullness, restlessness — these are the eight grounds of defilement. ^2-952

##### (2) The eight grounds of laziness ^2-17-9-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-953]]

Therein, what are the eight grounds of laziness?
(a) Here a monk has work to do. He thinks: "I shall have work to do. But while doing the work my body will get tired. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the first ground of laziness.
(b) Further, a monk has done work. He thinks: "I have done work. But while doing the work my body got tired. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the second ground of laziness.
(c) Further, a monk has a journey to make. He thinks: "I shall have a journey to make. But while making the journey my body will get tired. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the third ground of laziness.
(d) Further, a monk has made a journey. He thinks: "I have made a journey. But while making the journey my body got tired. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the fourth ground of laziness.
(e) Further, a monk, walking for alms in a village or town, does not get his fill of coarse or fine food as much as he needs. He thinks: "Walking for alms in a village or town, I did not get my fill of coarse or fine food as much as I needed. My body is tired and unworkable. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the fifth ground of laziness.
(f) Further, a monk, walking for alms in a village or town, gets his fill of coarse or fine food as much as he needs. He thinks: "Walking for alms in a village or town, I got my fill of coarse or fine food as much as I needed. My body is heavy and unworkable, like a load of soaked beans, I think. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the sixth ground of laziness.
(g) Further, a slight illness has arisen in a monk. He thinks: "This slight illness has arisen in me. There is reason enough to lie down. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the seventh ground of laziness.
(h) Further, a monk has recovered from illness, has recently recovered from sickness. He thinks: "I have recovered from illness, recently recovered from sickness. My body is weak and unworkable. Well then, let me lie down." He lies down; he does not initiate energy for reaching the unreached, attaining the unattained, realising the unrealised. This is the eighth ground of laziness. These are the eight grounds of laziness. ^2-953
