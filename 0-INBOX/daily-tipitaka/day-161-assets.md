##### (15) Pañca diṭṭhadhammanibbānavādā ^2-17-6-15-0

Tattha katame pañca diṭṭhadhammanibbānavādā?
(Ka) idhekacco samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā evaṃvādī hoti evaṃdiṭṭhi [evaṃdiṭṭhi (syā.)] – "yato kho, bho, ayaṃ attā pañcahi kāmaguṇehi samappito samaṅgībhūto paricāreti, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānaṃ paññapenti.
(Kha) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi neso natthīti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hoti. Taṃ kissa hetu? Kāmā hi, bho, aniccā dukkhā vipariṇāmadhammā. Tesaṃ vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā uppajjanti sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā. Yato kho, bho, ayaṃ attā vivicceva kāmehi…pe… paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānaṃ paññapenti.
(Ga) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. Neso natthīti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hoti. Taṃ kissa hetu? Yadeva tattha vitakkitaṃ vicāritaṃ etena etaṃ oḷārikaṃ akkhāyati. Yato kho, bho, ayaṃ attā vitakkavicārānaṃ vūpasamā…pe… dutiyaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānaṃ paññapenti.
(Gha) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. Neso natthīti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hoti. Taṃ kissa hetu? Yadeva tattha pītigataṃ cetaso uppilāvitaṃ, etena etaṃ oḷārikaṃ akkhāyati. Yato kho, bho, ayaṃ attā pītiyā ca virāgā…pe… tatiyaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānaṃ paññapenti.
(Ṅa) tamañño evamāha – "atthi kho, bho, eso attā yaṃ tvaṃ vadesi. Neso natthīti vadāmi. No ca kho, bho, ayaṃ attā ettāvatā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hoti. Taṃ kissa hetu? Yadeva tattha sukhapīti cetaso ābhogo, etena etaṃ oḷārikaṃ akkhāyati. Yato kho, bho, ayaṃ attā sukhassa ca pahānā…pe… catutthaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati, ettāvatā kho, bho, ayaṃ attā paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappatto hotī"ti. Ittheke sato sattassa paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānaṃ paññapenti. Ime pañca diṭṭhadhammanibbānavādā.
Pañcakaṃ. ^2-943

##### (1) Cha vivādamūlāni ^2-17-7-1-0

##### (2) Cha chandarāgā ^2-17-7-2-0

### Pañcakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-6|↑↑↑]]

Pañcakaniddese yasmā yesaṃ sakkāyadiṭṭhiādīni appahīnāni, te bhavaggepi nibbatte etāni ākaḍḍhitvā kāmabhaveyeva pātenti, tasmā orambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni; etāni gacchantaṃ na vārenti, gataṃ pana ānenti, rūparāgādīni pana gacchantaṃ na vārenti, āgantuṃ pana na denti. Rāgādayo pañca lagganaṭṭhena saṅgā, anupaviṭṭhaṭṭhena sallā. Cetokhilā cittassa thaddhabhāvā kacavarabhāvā khāṇukabhāvā, yehi satthari dhamme saṅghe sikkhāya ca kaṅkhati, nādhimuccati, na sampasīdati; cetasovinibandhā cittaṃ bandhitvā muṭṭhiyaṃ katvā viya gaṇhanti – kāme kāye rūpe avītarāgatā, udarāvadehakaṃ bhuñjitvā seyyasukhapassasukhamiddhasukhānuyogo, ‘devo vā bhavissāmī’ti paṇidhāya brahmacariyacaraṇaṃ. Kusaladhamme āvarantīti nīvaraṇāni. Diṭṭhīsu saññīvādā asaññīvādā nevasaññīnāsaññīvādā ucchedavādā diṭṭhadhammanibbānavādā ca saṅgahitā; tattha kāmaguṇesu samappito paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappattoti vadanti, dutiyādivāresu pana kāmā hutvā abhāvaṭṭhena aniccā, tesaṃ vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā uppajjanti, paṭhamajjhānaṃ vitakkavicārehi oḷārikaṃ sakaṇṭakaṃ viya khāyatīti uttarottaraṃ paṭikkhipanti. Akkhantiyāti anadhivāsanāya – tassā ādīnavesu puggalo bahuno janassa appiyo amanāpo verabahulo vajjabahulo hoti; ājīvakabhayaṃ asilokabhayaṃ parisasārajjabhayañca katapāpassa uppajjati.

### Chakkaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-7|↑↑↑]]

Chakkaniddese yasmā kuddho vā kodhavasena sandiṭṭhiparāmāsī vā sandiṭṭhiparāmāsitāya kalahaṃ viggahaṃ vivādaṃ āpajjati, tasmā kodhādayo vivādamūlānīti vuttā. Manāpiyesu rūpādīsu kāmagehasitā chandarāgā, amanāpiyesu virodhavatthūni. Agāravesu yo satthari dharamāne upaṭṭhānaṃ na yāti, parinibbute cetiyaṃ vandituṃ na gacchati, ayaṃ satthari agāravo; yo sakkaccaṃ dhammaṃ na suṇāti, ayaṃ dhamme agāravo; yo vuḍḍhe bhikkhū ghaṭṭento gacchati, ayaṃ saṅghe agāravo; tisso sikkhā apūrayamāno sikkhāya, appamādalakkhaṇaṃ ananubrūhayamāno appamāde, duvidhaṃ paṭisanthāraṃ akaronto paṭisanthāre agāravo nāma. Kammabhassaniddāsaṅgaṇikāsaṃsaggapapañcārāmatā parihānakarā dhammā. Cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā somanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ domanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ upekkhāṭṭhāniyaṃ rūpaṃ upavicaratīti somanassupavicārādayo; gehasitāni kāmaguṇanissitāni, upekkhā panettha aññāṇupekkhā. ‘Atthi me attā’ti sassatadiṭṭhi, ‘natthi me attā’ti ucchedadiṭṭhi, ‘attanā vā attānaṃ sañjānāmī’tiādayopi sassatucchedadiṭṭhiyova, ‘vado vedeyyo… nicco dhuvo sassato avipariṇāmadhammo’ti sassatadiṭṭhiyā abhinivesākārā; evamayaṃ sabbāsavadiṭṭhi nāma kathitā.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

##### (15) The five doctrines of Nibbāna in this very life ^2-17-6-15-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-943]]

Therein, what are the five doctrines of Nibbāna in this very life?
(a) Here some ascetic or brahmin speaks thus and holds this view: "When this self, sir, furnished and provided with the five strands of sense pleasure, amuses itself, to that extent, sir, this self has reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life." Thus some declare the highest Nibbāna in this very life of an existing being.
(b) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, has not to that extent reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life. Why is that? Because, sir, sense pleasures are impermanent, painful, and subject to change; with their change and alteration arise sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair. When this self, sir, secluded from sense pleasures… enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption, to that extent, sir, this self has reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life." Thus some declare the highest Nibbāna in this very life of an existing being.
(c) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, has not to that extent reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life. Why is that? Because whatever there is of thought and examination there, by that it is reckoned gross. When this self, sir, with the stilling of thought and examination… enters and dwells in the second meditative absorption, to that extent, sir, this self has reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life." Thus some declare the highest Nibbāna in this very life of an existing being.
(d) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, has not to that extent reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life. Why is that? Because whatever there is of joy and mental elation there, by that it is reckoned gross. When this self, sir, with the fading away of joy… enters and dwells in the third meditative absorption, to that extent, sir, this self has reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life." Thus some declare the highest Nibbāna in this very life of an existing being.
(e) Another says to him: "There is, sir, that self of which you speak; I do not say it does not exist. But this self, sir, has not to that extent reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life. Why is that? Because whatever there is of the mind's engagement with happiness and joy there, by that it is reckoned gross. When this self, sir, with the abandoning of happiness… enters and dwells in the fourth meditative absorption, to that extent, sir, this self has reached the highest Nibbāna in this very life." Thus some declare the highest Nibbāna in this very life of an existing being. These are the five doctrines of Nibbāna in this very life.
