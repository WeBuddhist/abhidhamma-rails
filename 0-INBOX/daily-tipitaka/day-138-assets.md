[[#^toc-13-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva mettādīnaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana sabbāpi tīsu tikesu navatabbārammaṇā eva, ajjhattārammaṇattike bahiddhārammaṇā. Imasmiṃ appamaññāvibhaṅge suttantabhājanīyepi abhidhammabhājanīyepi pañhāpucchakepi lokiyā eva appamaññāyo kathitā; tayopi hi ete nayā lokiyattā ekaparicchedā eva, evaṃ teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassito.

Of the four measureless states, how many are wholesome, how many unwholesome, how many neutral… how many involve conflict, how many are free from conflict? ^2-700

##### The Triads ^2-13-3-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-701]]

They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. Three measureless states are associated with pleasant feeling; equanimity is associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They may be results; they may be things subject to result; they may be neither results nor things subject to result. They may be taken up at birth and able to be clung to; they may be not taken up at birth but able to be clung to. They are undefiled but connected with defilement.
