##### Arūpadhātu ^2-18-2-3-0

Arūpadhātuyā kati khandhā…pe… kati cittāni?
Arūpadhātuyā cattāro khandhā, dve āyatanāni, dve dhātuyo, tīṇi saccāni, ekādasindriyāni, aṭṭha hetū, tayo āhārā, eko phasso, ekā vedanā, ekā saññā, ekā cetanā, ekaṃ cittaṃ. ^2-995

Tattha katame arūpadhātuyā cattāro khandhā? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho, viññāṇakkhandho – ime vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā cattāro khandhā".
Tattha katamāni arūpadhātuyā dve āyatanāni? Manāyatanaṃ, dhammāyatanaṃ – imāni vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā dve āyatanāni".
Tattha katamā arūpadhātuyā dve dhātuyo? Manoviññāṇadhātu, dhammadhātu – imā vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā dve dhātuyo".
Tattha katamāni arūpadhātuyā tīṇi saccāni? Dukkhasaccaṃ, samudayasaccaṃ, maggasaccaṃ – imāni vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā tīṇi saccāni".
Tattha katamāni arūpadhātuyā ekādasindriyāni? Manindriyaṃ, jīvitindriyaṃ, somanassindriyaṃ, upekkhindriyaṃ, saddhindriyaṃ, vīriyindriyaṃ, satindriyaṃ, samādhindriyaṃ, paññindriyaṃ, aññindriyaṃ, aññātāvindriyaṃ – imāni vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā ekādasindriyāni".
Tattha katame arūpadhātuyā aṭṭha hetū? Tayo kusalahetū, dve akusalahetū, tayo abyākatahetū…pe… ime vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā aṭṭha hetū".
Tattha katame arūpadhātuyā tayo āhārā? Phassāhāro, manosañcetanāhāro, viññāṇāhāro – ime vuccanti "arūpadhātuyā tayo āhārā".
Tattha katamo arūpadhātuyā eko phasso? Manoviññāṇadhātusamphasso – ayaṃ vuccati "arūpadhātuyā eko phasso".
Tattha katamā arūpadhātuyā ekā vedanā…pe… ekā saññā…pe… ekā cetanā…pe… ekaṃ cittaṃ? Manoviññāṇadhātu – idaṃ vuccati "arūpadhātuyā ekaṃ cittaṃ". ^2-996

##### Apariyāpannaṃ ^2-18-2-4-0

Apariyāpanne kati khandhā…pe… kati cittāni?
Apariyāpanne cattāro khandhā, dve āyatanāni, dve dhātuyo, dve saccāni, dvādasindriyāni, cha hetū, tayo āhārā, eko phasso, ekā vedanā, ekā saññā, ekā cetanā, ekaṃ cittaṃ. ^2-997

Tattha katame apariyāpanne cattāro khandhā? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho, viññāṇakkhandho – ime vuccanti "apariyāpanne cattāro khandhā".
Tattha katamāni apariyāpanne dve āyatanāni? Manāyatanaṃ, dhammāyatanaṃ – imāni vuccanti "apariyāpanne dve āyatanāni".
Tattha katamā apariyāpanne dve dhātuyo? Manoviññāṇadhātu, dhammadhātu – imā vuccanti "apariyāpanne dve dhātuyo".
Tattha katamāni apariyāpanne dve saccāni? Maggasaccaṃ, nirodhasaccaṃ – imāni vuccanti "apariyāpanne dve saccāni".
Tattha katamāni apariyāpanne dvādasindriyāni? Manindriyaṃ, jīvitindriyaṃ, somanassindriyaṃ, upekkhindriyaṃ, saddhindriyaṃ, vīriyindriyaṃ, satindriyaṃ, samādhindriyaṃ, paññindriyaṃ, anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, aññindriyaṃ, aññātāvindriyaṃ – imāni vuccanti "apariyāpanne dvādasindriyāni".
Tattha katame apariyāpanne cha hetū? Tayo kusalahetū, tayo abyākatahetū.
Tattha katame tayo kusalahetū? Alobho kusalahetu, adoso kusalahetu, amoho kusalahetu – ime tayo kusalahetū.
Tattha katame tayo abyākatahetū? Kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ vipākato alobho, adoso, amoho – ime tayo abyākatahetū. Ime vuccanti "apariyāpanne cha hetū".
Tattha katame apariyāpanne tayo āhārā? Phassāhāro, manosañcetanāhāro, viññāṇāhāro – ime vuccanti "apariyāpanne tayo āhārā".
Tattha katamo apariyāpanne eko phasso? Manoviññāṇadhātusamphasso – ayaṃ vuccati "apariyāpanne eko phasso".
Tattha katamā apariyāpanne ekā vedanā…pe… ekā saññā…pe… ekā cetanā…pe… ekaṃ cittaṃ? Manoviññāṇadhātu – idaṃ vuccati "apariyāpanne ekaṃ cittaṃ". ^2-998

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Uppattānuppattivāro

[[#^toc-18-2|↑↑↑]]

Dutiyavāre ye dhammā kāmabhave kāmadhātusambhūtānañca sattānaṃ uppajjanti – kāmadhātuyaṃ pariyāpannā vā apariyāpannā vā – te sabbe saṅgahetvā "kāmadhātuyā pañcakkhandhā"tiādi vuttaṃ; rūpadhātuādīsupi eseva nayo. Yasmā pana rūpadhātupariyāpannānaṃ sattānaṃ ghānāyatanādīnaṃ abhāvena gandhāyatanādīni āyatanādikiccaṃ na karonti, tasmā "rūpadhātuyā cha āyatanāni, nava dhātuyo"tiādi vuttaṃ. Yasmā ca okāsavasena vā sattuppattivasena vā apariyāpannadhātu nāma natthi, tasmā "apariyāpannadhātuyā"ti avatvā yaṃ yaṃ apariyāpannaṃ taṃ tadeva dassetuṃ "apariyāpanne kati khandhā"tiādi vuttaṃ.

### Pariyāpannāpariyāpannavāro

[[#^toc-18-3|↑↑↑]]

Tatiyavāre "pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati kāmadhātupariyāpannā, kati na kāmadhātupariyāpannā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati kāmadhātupariyāpannā, kati na kāmadhātupariyāpannā"ti kāmadhātu-rūpadhātu-arūpadhātuvasena ceva pariyāpannāpariyāpannavasena ca pucchā katā. Tattha kāmadhātupariyāpannāti kāmadhātubhajanaṭṭhena pariyāpannā; taṃnissitā tadantogadhā kāmadhātutveva saṅkhaṃ gatāti attho. Sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Pariyāpannāti bhavavasena okāsavasena ca paricchinnā; apariyāpannāti tathā aparicchinnā.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Uppattānuppattivāro

[[#^toc-18-2|↑↑↑]]

Uppattānuppattivāre kāmadhātuyā nava hetū, rūpadhātuyā arūpadhātuyā ca dosavajjitā aṭṭha hetū vuttā – tattha lobho ca moho ca akusalahetū labbhantiyeva; rūpadhātupariyāpannānaṃ pana sattānaṃ ghānāyatanādīnaṃ abhāvena cha āyatanāni nava dhātuyo, arūpadhātuyā ekaṃ cittaṃ. Apariyāpanne pana akusalahetu nāma natthi, tayo kusalahetū tayo abyākatahetūti cha hetū eva. Tasmā yoginā "rūpārūpadhātūsupi lobho ca moho ca anubandhantī"ti ñatvā sukhumesupi bhavesu nikantiṃ akaronto, yattha akusalahetu natthi taṃ apariyāpannaṃ maggaphalaṃ ārabbha vīriyaṃ upasampādentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pariyāpannāpariyāpannavāro

[[#^toc-18-3|↑↑↑]]

Pariyāpannāpariyāpannavāre pariyāpannāti bhavavasena okāsavasena ca paricchinnā, taṃnissitā tadantogadhā; apariyāpannāti tathā aparicchinnā. Tattha tayo akusalahetū kabaḷīkāro ca āhāro pariyāpannā eva, maggasaccaṃ nirodhasaccañca apariyāpannā. Tasmā yoginā bhavavasena okāsavasena ca paricchinne dhamme "bhavantogadhā ime"ti disvā tesu anallīyitvā, akusalahetū pajahitvā, aparicchinnaṃ maggasaccaṃ bhāvetvā nirodhasaccaṃ sacchikarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

##### The Immaterial Element ^2-18-2-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-995]]

In the immaterial element, how many aggregates are there… how many minds?
In the immaterial element there are four aggregates, two sense-fields, two elements, three truths, eleven faculties, eight roots, three nutriments, one contact, one feeling, one perception, one intention, one mind. ^2-995

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-996]]

Therein, what are the four aggregates in the immaterial element? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — these are called "the four aggregates in the immaterial element".
Therein, what are the two sense-fields in the immaterial element? The mind sense-field, the mental-object sense-field — these are called "the two sense-fields in the immaterial element".
Therein, what are the two elements in the immaterial element? The mind-consciousness element, the mental-object element — these are called "the two elements in the immaterial element".
Therein, what are the three truths in the immaterial element? The truth of suffering, the truth of the origin, the truth of the path — these are called "the three truths in the immaterial element".
Therein, what are the eleven faculties in the immaterial element? The mind faculty, the life faculty, the joy faculty, the equanimity faculty, the faculty of confidence, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of final knowledge, the faculty of one who has fully known — these are called "the eleven faculties in the immaterial element".
Therein, what are the eight roots in the immaterial element? Three wholesome roots, two unwholesome roots, three neutral roots… these are called "the eight roots in the immaterial element".
Therein, what are the three nutriments in the immaterial element? The nutriment of contact, the nutriment of mental intention, the nutriment of consciousness — these are called "the three nutriments in the immaterial element".
Therein, what is the one contact in the immaterial element? Mind-consciousness-element contact — this is called "the one contact in the immaterial element".
Therein, what is the one feeling in the immaterial element… the one perception… the one intention… the one mind? The mind-consciousness element — this is called "the one mind in the immaterial element". ^2-996

##### The Not-Included ^2-18-2-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-997]]

In what is not included in any sphere, how many aggregates are there… how many minds?
In what is not included there are four aggregates, two sense-fields, two elements, two truths, twelve faculties, six roots, three nutriments, one contact, one feeling, one perception, one intention, one mind. ^2-997

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-998]]

Therein, what are the four aggregates in what is not included? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — these are called "the four aggregates in what is not included".
Therein, what are the two sense-fields in what is not included? The mind sense-field, the mental-object sense-field — these are called "the two sense-fields in what is not included".
Therein, what are the two elements in what is not included? The mind-consciousness element, the mental-object element — these are called "the two elements in what is not included".
Therein, what are the two truths in what is not included? The truth of the path, the truth of cessation — these are called "the two truths in what is not included".
Therein, what are the twelve faculties in what is not included? The mind faculty, the life faculty, the joy faculty, the equanimity faculty, the faculty of confidence, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known", the faculty of final knowledge, the faculty of one who has fully known — these are called "the twelve faculties in what is not included".
Therein, what are the six roots in what is not included? Three wholesome roots, three neutral roots.
Therein, what are the three wholesome roots? Non-greed is a wholesome root, non-hate is a wholesome root, non-delusion is a wholesome root — these are the three wholesome roots.
Therein, what are the three neutral roots? Non-greed, non-hate, and non-delusion in the results of wholesome things — these are the three neutral roots. These are called "the six roots in what is not included".
Therein, what are the three nutriments in what is not included? The nutriment of contact, the nutriment of mental intention, the nutriment of consciousness — these are called "the three nutriments in what is not included".
Therein, what is the one contact in what is not included? Mind-consciousness-element contact — this is called "the one contact in what is not included".
Therein, what is the one feeling in what is not included… the one perception… the one intention… the one mind? The mind-consciousness element — this is called "the one mind in what is not included". ^2-998

#### The Section on Included and Not Included ^2-18-3-0

##### The Sense-Pleasure Element ^2-18-3-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-999]]

Of the five aggregates, how many are included in the sense-pleasure element, how many are not included in the sense-pleasure element… of the seven minds, how many are included in the sense-pleasure element, how many are not included in the sense-pleasure element? ^2-999

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1000]]

The aggregate of material form is included in the sense-pleasure element; four aggregates may be included in the sense-pleasure element, or may be not included in the sense-pleasure element.
Ten sense-fields are included in the sense-pleasure element; two sense-fields may be included in the sense-pleasure element, or may be not included in the sense-pleasure element.
Sixteen elements are included in the sense-pleasure element; two elements may be included in the sense-pleasure element, or may be not included in the sense-pleasure element.
The truth of the origin is included in the sense-pleasure element; two truths are not included in the sense-pleasure element; the truth of suffering may be included in the sense-pleasure element, or may be not included in the sense-pleasure element.
