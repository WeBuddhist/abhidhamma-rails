#### Diṭṭhasutādidassanavāro ^2-18-9-0

##### Kusalattikaṃ ^2-18-10-1-0

Pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati kusalā, kati akusalā, kati abyākatā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati kusalā, kati akusalā, kati abyākatā?

Dve dhātuyo siyā kusalā, siyā akusalā, siyā abyākatā. Samudayasaccaṃ akusalaṃ. Maggasaccaṃ kusalaṃ. Nirodhasaccaṃ abyākataṃ. Dukkhasaccaṃ siyā kusalaṃ, siyā akusalaṃ, siyā abyākataṃ.
Dasindriyā abyākatā. Domanassindriyaṃ akusalaṃ. Anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ kusalaṃ. Cattārindriyā siyā kusalā, siyā abyākatā. Cha indriyā siyā kusalā, siyā akusalā, siyā abyākatā.
Tayo kusalahetū kusalā. Tayo akusalahetū akusalā. Tayo abyākatahetū abyākatā. Kabaḷīkāro āhāro abyākato. Tayo āhārā siyā kusalā, siyā akusalā, siyā abyākatā. Cha phassā abyākatā. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphasso siyā kusalo, siyā akusalo, siyā abyākato. Cha vedanā… cha saññā… cha cetanā… cha cittā abyākatā. Manoviññāṇadhātu siyā kusalā, siyā akusalā, siyā abyākatā. ^2-1038

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Sattamādīsu catūsu vāresu sattamavāre salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena abhiññeyyatā veditabbā, ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena pariññeyyatā; sā ca "rūpakkhandho abhiññeyyo pariññeyyo na pahātabbo"tiādīsu ñātatīraṇapariññāvaseneva, "samudayasaccaṃ abhiññeyyaṃ pariññeyyaṃ pahātabba"ntiādīsu pahānapariññāvasena veditabbā. Aṭṭhamavāre rūpādiārammaṇānaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ vasena sārammaṇānārammaṇatā veditabbā. Navamavāre khandhādidhammā diṭṭhasutamutaviññātavasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; so uttānatthoyeva. Dasamavāre kusalattikavedanāttikavipākattikaupādinnattikavitakkattikānaṃ rūpadukalokiyadukānañca vasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ siyā taṃ sabbaṃ tattha tattha pañhāpucchakavāre vuttameva. Ettāvatā dhammahadayavibhaṅgavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Abhiññeyyādivāre sabbe khandhādayo abhiññeyyā pariññeyyā vuttā; samudayasaccaṃ tayo akusalahetū domanassindriyañca pahātabbāni, maggasaccaṃ anaññātaññassāmītindriyañca bhāvetabbāni, nirodhasaccaṃ aññātāvindriyañca sacchikātabbāni. Abhiññeyyatā salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena, pariññeyyatā ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena veditabbā. Sārammaṇānārammaṇavāre rūpakkhandho anārammaṇo, cattāro khandhā sārammaṇā; diṭṭhasutādivāre cattāro khandhā na diṭṭhā na sutā na mutā, viññātāva; tikādivāre samudayasaccaṃ akusalaṃ, maggasaccaṃ kusalaṃ, nirodhasaccaṃ abyākataṃ. Tasmā yoginā rūpārūpadhamme salakkhaṇato pariggahetvā ñātapariññāya jānitvā tīraṇapariññāya tīrayitvā, pahānapariññāya samudayaṃ akusalahetū ca pajahitvā, maggaṃ bhāvetvā nirodhaṃ sacchikarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### The Section Showing the Seen, the Heard, and the Rest ^2-18-9-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1036]]

Of the five aggregates, how many are seen, how many are heard, how many are sensed, how many are known, how many are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known… of the seven minds, how many are seen, how many are heard, how many are sensed, how many are known, how many are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known? ^2-1036

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1037]]

The aggregate of material form may be seen, may be heard, may be sensed, may be known, or may be neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. Four aggregates are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known.
The sight sense-field is seen, not heard, not sensed, but known. The sound sense-field is not seen, but heard, not sensed, but known. The smell sense-field… the taste sense-field… the tangible sense-field are not seen, not heard, but sensed and known. Seven sense-fields are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known.
The sight element is seen, not heard, not sensed, but known. The sound element is not seen, but heard, not sensed, but known. The smell element… the taste element… the tangible element are not seen, not heard, but sensed and known. Thirteen elements are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known.
Three truths are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. The truth of suffering may be seen, may be heard, may be sensed, may be known, or may be neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known.
The twenty-two faculties are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. The nine roots are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. The four nutriments are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. The seven contacts are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. The seven feelings… the seven perceptions… the seven intentions… the seven minds are neither seen, nor heard, nor sensed, but known. ^2-1037

#### The Section Showing the Triads and the Rest ^2-18-10-0

##### The Wholesome Triad ^2-18-10-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1038]]

Of the five aggregates, how many are wholesome, how many unwholesome, how many neutral… of the seven minds, how many are wholesome, how many unwholesome, how many neutral?
The aggregate of material form is neutral. Four aggregates may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral. Ten sense-fields are neutral. Two sense-fields may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral. Sixteen elements are neutral. Two elements may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral. The truth of the origin is unwholesome. The truth of the path is wholesome. The truth of cessation is neutral. The truth of suffering may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral.
Ten faculties are neutral. The grief faculty is unwholesome. The faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known" is wholesome. Four faculties may be wholesome, or may be neutral. Six faculties may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral.
Three wholesome roots are wholesome. Three unwholesome roots are unwholesome. Three neutral roots are neutral. The nutriment of solid food is neutral. Three nutriments may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral. Six contacts are neutral. Mind-consciousness-element contact may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral. Six feelings… six perceptions… six intentions… six minds are neutral. The mind-consciousness element may be wholesome, may be unwholesome, or may be neutral. ^2-1038
