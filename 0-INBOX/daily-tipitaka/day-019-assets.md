## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

### Lokuttarakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Evaṃ bhavattayasampattinibbattakaṃ kusalaṃ dassetvā idāni sabbabhavasamatikkamanāya lokuttarakusalaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. 'Lokaṃ taratīti lokuttaraṃ' – vaṭṭato niyyātīti niyyānikaṃ apacayagāmiṃ ca. Jhānaṃ bhāvetīti ekacittakkhaṇikaṃ appanājhānaṃ uppādeti vaḍḍheti. Diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānāya paṭhamāya bhūmiyā pattiyāti sotāpattiphalaṃ, samucchedavivekavasena viviccā. Paṭipadāya nānattaṃ dassetuṃ dukkhapaṭipadā dandhābhiññā, sukhāpaṭipadā khippābhiññāoti vuttaṃ. Phassādīsu anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvoti cattāro dhammā adhikā – maggaṭṭhakaṃ hoti. Etaṃ lokuttarakusalaṃ tebhūmakakusalaṃ upādāya apacayagāmī.

#### Suddhikapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-2-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttarajhānaṃ paṭipadāya saddhiṃyeva garuṃ katvā dassetuṃ suddhikapaṭipadā vuttā – suddhikanayaṃ pahāya paṭipadābhedameva ādito dassitaṃ. Tattha dukkhapaṭipadā khippābhiññā dandhābhiññā, sukhapaṭipadā khippābhiññā dandhābhiññāti catubbidhā paṭipadā: yo āditova kilese vikkhambhento dukkhena sasaṅkhārena sappayogena kilamanto vikkhambheti, tassa dukkhā paṭipadā; yo pana vikkhambhita kileso vipassanāparivāsaṃ vasanto cirena maggaṃ pāpuṇāti, tassa dandhābhiññā hoti. Catukkapañcakajhānanayena ca dve nayā vibhattā. Lokuttarajhānassa viseso: phassādīsu kāmāvacarakusalato adhikā dhammā – anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, sammāvācā, sammākammanto, sammāājīvoti cattāro; tena idha maggaṭṭhaṅgiko hoti. Suññatavāro pākatikoyeva.

### Suddhikapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-1-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Suddhikapaṭipadāya lokuttarapaṭhamajjhānādīnaṃ maggacittāni vibhajiyanti. Sammādiṭṭhiyā diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānaṃ katvā, aṭṭhaṅgikamaggassa bhāvanāya, dassanabhūmiyaṃ pākaṭaṃ sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo cāti maggaṭṭhakaṃ paripūreti. Evaṃ suddhikā paṭipadā lokuttarakusalassa ādhāro.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

#### Wholesome Supramundane Consciousness ^1-1-5-0

##### Pure Practice ^1-1-5-1-0

**v. 277.** Which states are wholesome? When one develops the supramundane jhāna, leading out (of saṃsāra), leading to the cessation of accumulation (of kamma), for the abandonment of wrong views, for the attainment of the first stage (of enlightenment), quite secluded from sensual desires…pe… one enters and abides in the first jhāna, with painful practice and slow direct knowledge. At that time, contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, initial application, sustained application, zest, pleasure, one-pointedness of mind, the faculties of faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, wisdom, mind, joy, life, and the faculty "I shall know the unknown", right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration, the powers of faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, wisdom, shame, and fear of wrongdoing, non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion, non-covetousness, non-ill will, right view, shame, fear of wrongdoing, bodily tranquillity, mental tranquillity, bodily lightness, mental lightness, bodily malleability, mental malleability, bodily wieldiness, mental wieldiness, bodily proficiency, mental proficiency, bodily rectitude, mental rectitude, mindfulness, clear comprehension, calm, insight, exertion, non-distraction arise; or whatever other dependently arisen formless states are present at that time — these states are wholesome. ^1-277

**v. 278.** What is contact at that time? Whatever contact, touching, complete touching, the state of having completely touched at that time — this is contact at that time. ^1-278

**v. 279.** What is feeling at that time? Whatever mental pleasantness, mental pleasure, pleasant pleasure born of mind-consciousness element contact, pleasant and pleasurable feeling born of mind-contact at that time — this is feeling at that time. ^1-279

(vv. 280–302 follow the same Padabhājanī definition pattern — each citta-factor of v. 277 is defined in turn: perception, volition, consciousness, initial application, sustained application, rapture, happiness, one-pointedness of mind, then the eight faculties saddhā/vīriya/sati/samādhi/paññā/manas/somanassa/jīvita and the supramundane-only ninth anaññātaññassāmītindriya, then the eight path factors sammādiṭṭhi/saṅkappa/vācā/kammanta/ājīva/vāyāma/sati.)

**v. 303.** What on that occasion is Right Mindfulness? At that time, mindfulness, recollection, repeated recollection, remembering, holding in mind, non-floating away from the object, non-forgetfulness; mindfulness, the mindfulness faculty, the power of mindfulness, Right Mindfulness, the enlightenment factor of mindfulness, a path factor, included in the path — this is the Right Mindfulness that there then is. ^1-303
