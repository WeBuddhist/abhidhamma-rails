## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Abyākataniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abyākataṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe āgatapaṭipāṭiyāva vibhattaṃ. Sabbavāresu cettha avijjāmūlakā nayā parihīnā; kasmā? Avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbassa abhāvato — kusalacittesu hi avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbaṃ kusalamūlaṃ atthi, cakkhuviññāṇādīsu natthi; sahetukesu pana kiñcāpi atthi, evaṃ santepi idha pacchinnattā na gahitaṃ. Visesato panettha cakkhuviññāṇādīsu taṇhāṭṭhānaṃ upādānaṭṭhānañca parihīnaṃ, taṇhāṭṭhānārahassa balavadhammassa abhāvā adhimokkharahitattā ca; sesāhetukesu taṇhāṭṭhānameva parihīnaṃ; sahetukesu pana pasādasabbhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne pasādena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Evamettha kusalākusalavipākesu cakkhuviññāṇādīsu saṅkhāraviññāṇanāmachaṭṭhāyatanaphassavedanāmūlakā cha cha nayā, sesāhetukesu adhimokkhamūlakena saddhiṃ satta satta, sahetukesu pasādamūlakena saddhiṃ aṭṭha aṭṭha nayā veditabbā. Cakkhuviññāṇādīsu ca catunnampi catukkānaṃ ādivārova vutto; tatiyacatutthavārā asambhavatoyeva na vuttā — rūpamissakā hi te, na ca cakkhuviññāṇādīni rūpaṃ samuṭṭhāpenti. Arūpāvacaravipākasmiṃ pana vāradvayameva labbhati.

#### Abyākataniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abyākataniddese vipākakiriyacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Cakkhuviññāṇādīsu avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbassa abhāvā avijjāmūlakā nayā parihīnā; taṇhāṭṭhānārahassa balavadhammassa abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhānaṃ upādānaṭṭhānañca parihīnaṃ; sahetukesu pana pasādena taṇhāṭṭhāne padaṃ pūritaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "sabbaṃ me cittaṃ kusalaṃ vā akusalaṃ vā"ti ekantasaññaṃ pahāya, dassanasavanādīsu abyākatacittānaṃ suddhavipākabhāvadassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "diṭṭhe diṭṭhamattaṃ sute sutamattaṃ — na tāva ettha rāgo na doso"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `AI_translation/english/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

317. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because sense-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind-consciousness element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object, or a sound as its object, or a smell as its object, or a taste as its object, or a tangible thing as its object, or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-317

318. Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition"… this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental experience that is neither comfortable nor uncomfortable, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant experience born of mind-contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has feeling as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-318

319. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because sense-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind-consciousness element has arisen, accompanied by joy, associated with knowledge… accompanied by joy, associated with knowledge, with prompting… accompanied by joy, dissociated from knowledge… accompanied by joy, dissociated from knowledge, with prompting… accompanied by equanimity, associated with knowledge… accompanied by equanimity, associated with knowledge, with prompting… accompanied by equanimity, dissociated from knowledge… accompanied by equanimity, dissociated from knowledge, with prompting, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, confidence; with confidence as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-319

320. Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition"… this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the confidence that has feeling as condition? The confidence, trusting, reliance, full confidence — this is called "the confidence that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has confidence as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has confidence as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-320

321. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when one develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — secluded from sense pleasures… one enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion there is contact… there is non-distraction — these things are wholesome.
When, because that same fine-material-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, as its result — secluded from sense pleasures… one enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption on the earth device — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, confidence; with confidence as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-321

Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-322

On the occasion when, because unwholesome action has been done and accumulated, resultant eye-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… ear-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sound as its object… nose-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a smell as its object… tongue-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a taste as its object… body-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by pain, with a tangible thing as its object — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-324

325. Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding body-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition"… this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Bodily discomfort, bodily pain, the uncomfortable painful experience born of body-contact, the uncomfortable painful feeling born of body-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has feeling as condition? Setting aside feeling: the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has feeling as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-325

326. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because unwholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object, or a sound as its object, or a smell as its object, or a taste as its object, or a tangible thing as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-326

327. Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called the formation.
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition"… this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental experience that is neither comfortable nor uncomfortable, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant experience born of mind-contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has feeling as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-327
