## Pāli source verses

1508. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇavippayuttā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā nīvaraṇavippayuttā. ^1-1508

1509. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇā ceva nīvaraṇiyā ca? Tāneva nīvaraṇāni nīvaraṇā ceva nīvaraṇiyā ca. ^1-1509

1510. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇiyā ceva no ca nīvaraṇā? Ṭhapetvā nīvaraṇe, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā nīvaraṇiyā ceva no ca nīvaraṇā. Anīvaraṇiyā dhammā na vattabbā – nīvaraṇā ceva nīvaraṇiyā cātipi, nīvaraṇiyā ceva no ca nīvaraṇātipi. ^1-1510

1511. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇā ceva nīvaraṇasampayuttā ca? Yattha dve tīṇi nīvaraṇāni ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā nīvaraṇā ceva nīvaraṇasampayuttā ca. ^1-1511

1512. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇasampayuttā ceva no ca nīvaraṇā? Ṭhapetvā nīvaraṇe, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā nīvaraṇasampayuttā ceva no ca nīvaraṇā. Nīvaraṇavippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – nīvaraṇā ceva nīvaraṇasampayuttā cātipi, nīvaraṇasampayuttā ceva no ca nīvaraṇātipi. ^1-1512

1513. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇavippayuttā nīvaraṇiyā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā nīvaraṇavippayuttā nīvaraṇiyā. ^1-1513

1514. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇavippayuttā anīvaraṇiyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā nīvaraṇavippayuttā anīvaraṇiyā. Nīvaraṇasampayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – nīvaraṇavippayuttā nīvaraṇiyātipi, nīvaraṇavippayuttā anīvaraṇiyātipi. ^1-1514

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Nīvaraṇagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Nīvaraṇagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yattha dve tīṇi nīvaraṇāni ekato uppajjantīti aṭṭhadhā nīvaraṇānaṃ ekato uppatti veditabbā. Kāmacchando duvidhā ekato uppajjati – asaṅkhārikacittesu uddhaccanīvaraṇaavijjānīvaraṇehi, sasaṅkhārikesu thinamiddhauddhaccaavijjānīvaraṇehi saddhiṃ. Byāpādo catudhā ekato uppajjati; uddhaccaṃ ekadhā; vicikicchā ekadhāva ekato uppajjatīti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1931]]

#### Parāmāsagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Parāmāsagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese parāmāsānaṃ atthuddhāraṃ āsavagocchakānaṃ vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Tattha upādiṇṇaanupādiṇṇavibhāgena anupādiṇṇadhamme tejussadattā taṇhāmānadiṭṭhivasena gahaṇassa paccayā na hontīti, asaṃkiliṭṭhaasaṃkilesikesupi eseva nayoti vuttaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1692]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1693]]

#### Mahantaradukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaradukaatthuddhāraniddese ñāṇasampayuttacittuppādā sekkhāsekkhānaṃ attanā paṭividdhamaggānaṃ paccavekkhaṇakāle maggārammaṇā, maggena pana asahajātattā na maggahetukā, maggaṃ garuṃ katvā paccavekkhaṇakāle ārammaṇādhipativasena maggādhipatino. Dvādasavidhe rūpāvacaracatutthajjhāne sabbatthapādakacatutthādīni nava jhānāni neva maggārammaṇāni na maggahetukāni na maggādhipatīni. Atītārammaṇattike, ajjhattārammaṇattike ca viññāṇañcāyatanādiniddeso vuttanayeneva veditabbo.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

#### Nīvaraṇagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Nīvaraṇaatthuddhāre nīvaraṇāni ekato uppattivasena uddhaṭāni — kāmacchando duvidhā (asaṅkhārike uddhaccaavijjānīvaraṇehi, sasaṅkhārike thinamiddhauddhaccaavijjānīvaraṇehi saha), byāpādo catudhā, uddhaccaṃ vicikicchā ca ekadhā ekato uppajjati. Etāni cittaṃ āvaritvā samādhiṃ paññañca nivārenti. Tasmā yoginā "imasmiṃ cittuppāde ime nīvaraṇā ekato uppajjantī"ti ñatvā, kāmacchandaṃ asubhena, byāpādaṃ mettāya, thinamiddhaṃ ālokasaññāya, uddhaccakukkuccaṃ samathena, vicikicchaṃ dhammavavatthānena vinodetvā, nīvaraṇavimuttaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Parāmāsagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Parāmāsaatthuddhāre parāmāso (diṭṭhivasena micchābhiniveso) āsavānaṃ vuttanayeneva uddhaṭo. Anupādiṇṇadhammā tejussadattā taṇhāmānadiṭṭhivasena gahaṇassa paccayā na honti; asaṃkiliṭṭhaasaṃkilesikesupi eseva nayo. Tasmā yoginā diṭṭhiparāmāsaṃ "idha gaṇhātī"ti ñatvā, sabbadhammesu "netaṃ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā"ti aparāmasanto, anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Mahantaradukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaradukaatthuddhāre sārammaṇādayo dukā uddhaṭā. Sekhāsekhānaṃ maggapaccavekkhaṇakāle ñāṇasampayuttacittuppādā maggārammaṇā ceva, maggaṃ garuṃ katvā ārammaṇādhipatino ca honti; na pana maggahetukā, maggena asahajātattā. Tasmā yoginā attanā paṭividdhaṃ maggaphalaṃ garuṃ katvā paccavekkhitvā, maggaṃ ādhipateyyaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā, taṃ bhāvanaṃ vaḍḍhento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Which dhammas are dissociated from hindrances? Wholesome consciousness in the four planes, resultant consciousness in the four planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, and matter, and Nibbāna – these dhammas are dissociated from hindrances. ^1-1508

Which dhammas are hindrances and also liable to be hindered? The hindrances themselves are hindrances and also liable to be hindered. ^1-1509

Which dhammas are liable to be hindered but are not hindrances themselves? Excluding the hindrances, the remaining unwholesome states, wholesome consciousness in the three planes, resultant consciousness in the three planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, and all matter – these dhammas are liable to be hindered but are not hindrances themselves. Dhammas not liable to be hindered should not be said to be hindrances and also liable to be hindered, or liable to be hindered but not hindrances themselves. ^1-1510

Which dhammas are hindrances and associated with hindrances? Where two or three hindrances arise together – these dhammas are hindrances and associated with hindrances. ^1-1511

Which dhammas are associated with hindrances but are not hindrances themselves? Excluding the hindrances, the remaining unwholesome states – these dhammas are associated with hindrances but are not hindrances themselves. Dhammas dissociated from hindrances should not be said to be hindrances and associated with hindrances, or associated with hindrances but not hindrances themselves. ^1-1512

Which dhammas are dissociated from hindrances but liable to be hindered? Wholesome consciousness in the three planes, resultant consciousness in the three planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, and all matter – these dhammas are dissociated from hindrances but liable to be hindered. ^1-1513

Which dhammas are dissociated from hindrances and not liable to be hindered? The four supramundane paths, and the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbāna – these dhammas are dissociated from hindrances and not liable to be hindered. Dhammas associated with hindrances should not be said to be dissociated from hindrances but liable to be hindered, or dissociated from hindrances and not liable to be hindered. ^1-1514

##### Group concerning Misapprehension/Clinging ^1-4-2-9-0

Which dhammas are misapprehensions/clinging? Wrong view arises in the four types of consciousness associated with wrong view – these dhammas are misapprehensions/clinging. ^1-1515

Which dhammas are not misapprehensions/clinging? Excluding misapprehension/clinging, the remaining unwholesome states, wholesome consciousness in the four planes, resultant consciousness in the four planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, and matter, and Nibbāna – these dhammas are not misapprehensions/clinging. ^1-1516

Which phenomena are clung to? Wholesome, unwholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate phenomena in the three realms, and all matter – these phenomena are clung to. ^1-1517

Which phenomena are not clung to? The four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbana – these phenomena are not clung to. ^1-1518

Which phenomena are associated with wrong view? The four consciousnesses associated with wrong view, excluding the wrong view arising therein – these phenomena are associated with wrong view. ^1-1519

Which phenomena are disassociated from wrong view? The four consciousnesses rooted in greed and disassociated from wrong view, the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, the consciousness accompanied by doubt, the consciousness accompanied by restlessness, wholesome and resultant phenomena in the four realms, functional indeterminate phenomena in the three realms, matter, and Nibbana – these phenomena are disassociated from wrong view. Wrong view itself is not to be classified as associated with wrong view, nor as disassociated from wrong view. ^1-1520

Which phenomena are wrong view and are clung to? Wrong view itself is wrong view and is clung to. ^1-1521

Which phenomena are clung to but are not wrong view? All unwholesome phenomena except wrong view, wholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate phenomena in the three realms, and all matter – these phenomena are clung to but are not wrong view. Phenomena that are not clung to are not to be classified as wrong view and clung to, nor as clung to but not wrong view. ^1-1522

Which phenomena are disassociated from wrong view and are clung to? The four consciousnesses rooted in greed and disassociated from wrong view, the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, the consciousness accompanied by doubt, the consciousness accompanied by restlessness, wholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate phenomena in the three realms, and all matter – these phenomena are disassociated from wrong view and are clung to. ^1-1523

Which phenomena are disassociated from wrong view and are not clung to? The four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbana – these phenomena are disassociated from wrong view and are not clung to. Wrong view itself and phenomena associated with wrong view are not to be classified as disassociated from wrong view and clung to, nor as disassociated from wrong view and not clung to. ^1-1524

##### Mahantaradukaṃ ^1-4-2-10-0

Which phenomena have an object? Wholesome, unwholesome, and resultant phenomena in the four realms, and functional indeterminate phenomena in the three realms – these phenomena have an object. ^1-1525

What phenomena are without object? Matter and Nibbāna – these phenomena are without object. ^1-1526

What phenomena are consciousness? Eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, mind-element, mind-consciousness element – these phenomena are consciousness. ^1-1527

What phenomena are not consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not consciousness. ^1-1528

What phenomena are mental factors? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations – these phenomena are mental factors. ^1-1529

What phenomena are not mental factors? Consciousness, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not mental factors. ^1-1530

What phenomena are associated with consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations – these phenomena are associated with consciousness. ^1-1531

What phenomena are dissociated from consciousness? Matter and Nibbāna – these phenomena are dissociated from consciousness. Consciousness should not be said to be associated with consciousness, nor dissociated from consciousness. ^1-1532

What phenomena are conjoined with consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations – these phenomena are conjoined with consciousness. ^1-1533

What phenomena are disjoined from consciousness? Matter and Nibbāna – these phenomena are disjoined from consciousness. Consciousness should not be said to be conjoined with consciousness, nor disjoined from consciousness. ^1-1534

What phenomena are originated by consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, bodily intimation, verbal intimation, and whatever other matter there is that is mind-born, mind-caused, mind-originated – visual object, sound object, smell object, taste object, tangible object, space element, water element, lightness of matter, pliancy of matter, adaptability of matter, growth of matter, continuity of matter, nutriment – these phenomena are originated by consciousness. ^1-1535

What phenomena are not mind-originated? Consciousness, remaining matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not mind-originated. ^1-1536

What phenomena are co-existent with mind? The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, the formations aggregate, bodily intimation, and verbal intimation – these phenomena are co-existent with mind. ^1-1537

What phenomena are not co-existent with mind? Consciousness, remaining matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not co-existent with mind. ^1-1538

What phenomena accompany mind? The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, the formations aggregate, bodily intimation, and verbal intimation – these phenomena accompany mind. ^1-1539

What phenomena do not accompany mind? Consciousness, remaining matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena do not accompany mind. ^1-1540

What phenomena originate from and are associated with mind? The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, and the formations aggregate – these phenomena originate from and are associated with mind. ^1-1541

What phenomena do not originate from and are associated with mind? Consciousness, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena do not originate from and are associated with mind. ^1-1542

What phenomena are co-existent with, originate from, and are associated with mind? The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, and the formations aggregate – these phenomena are co-existent with, originate from, and are associated with mind. ^1-1543

What phenomena are not co-existent with, originate from, and are associated with mind? Consciousness, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not co-existent with, originate from, and are associated with mind. ^1-1544

What phenomena accompany, originate from, and are associated with mind? The feeling aggregate, the perception aggregate, and the formations aggregate – these phenomena accompany, originate from, and are associated with mind. ^1-1545

Which phenomena do not arise in conjunction with, are not dependent on, and do not follow consciousness? Consciousness, matter, and Nibbana – these phenomena do not arise in conjunction with, are not dependent on, and do not follow consciousness. ^1-1546
