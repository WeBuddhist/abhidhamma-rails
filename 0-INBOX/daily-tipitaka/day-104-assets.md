### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye paṭhamaṃ bāvīsati indriyāni uddisitvā "tattha katamaṃ cakkhundriya"nti niddesavāro āraddho. Tattha "yaṃ cakkhu catunnaṃ mahābhūtānaṃ upādāya pasādo"tiādi sabbaṃ dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ padabhājane vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Vīriyindriyasamādhindriyaniddesādīsu ca sammāvāyāmo micchāvāyāmo sammāsamādhi micchāsamādhīti ādīni na vuttāni; kasmā? Sabbasaṅgāhakattā — sabbasaṅgāhakāni hi idha indriyāni kathitāni. Desanākkamo cettha evaṃ veditabbo: ajjhattadhammaṃ pariññāya ariyabhūmipaṭilābho hotīti attabhāvapariyāpannāni cakkhundriyādīni paṭhamaṃ desitāni; yaṃ dhammaṃ upādāya itthīti vā purisoti vā saṅkhaṃ gacchati, tassa nidassanatthaṃ tato itthindriyaṃ purisindriyañca; so duvidhopi jīvitindriyapaṭibaddhavuttīti ñāpanatthaṃ tato jīvitindriyaṃ; yaṃ kiñci vedayitaṃ sabbaṃ taṃ sukhadukkhanti ñāpanatthaṃ tato sukhindriyādīni; taṃnirodhatthaṃ pana ete dhammā bhāvetabbāti paṭipattidassanatthaṃ tato saddhādīni; paṭipattiyā amoghabhāvadassanatthaṃ tato anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, tasseva phalattā tato aññindriyaṃ, ante ca paramassāsabhūtaṃ aññātāvindriyaṃ desitaṃ. Bhedo cettha jīvitindriyasseva — taṃ hi rūpajīvitindriyaṃ arūpajīvitindriyanti duvidhaṃ hoti, sesānaṃ abhedo. Kiccato ca cakkhundriyassa attano tikkhamandādibhāvena cakkhuviññāṇādidhammānaṃ attākārānuvattāpanaṃ kiccaṃ, manindriyassa sahajātadhammānaṃ attano vasavattāpanaṃ, jīvitindriyassa sahajātadhammānupālanaṃ, saddhādīnaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanaṃ, aññātāvindriyassa sabbakiccesu ussukkappahānanti. Bhūmito pana dasa indriyāni lokiyāni kāmāvacarāneva, tīṇi lokuttarāni, nava lokiyalokuttaramissakānīti niddesavāre siddhaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake sabbesampi indriyānaṃ kusalākusalābyākatādivibhāgo pāḷinayānusāreneva veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana "sattindriyā anārammaṇā"ti cakkhusotaghānajivhākāyaitthipurisindriyāni sandhāya vuttaṃ; jīvitindriyaṃ pana arūpamissakattā idha anābhaṭaṃ. "Dvindriyā"ti sukhadukkhadvayaṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ — taṃ hi ekantaparittārammaṇaṃ. Domanassindriyaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha pavattikāle parittārammaṇaṃ hoti, rūpāvacarārūpāvacare ārabbha pavattikāle mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ ārabbha pavattikāle navattabbārammaṇaṃ. "Navindriyā siyā parittārammaṇā"ti manindriyajīvitindriyasomanassindriyaupekkhindriyāni ceva saddhādipañcakañca sandhāya vuttaṃ. "Cattāri indriyānī"ti sukhadukkhadomanassaaññātāvindriyāni — tāni hi maggārammaṇattike na bhajanti; maggahetukanti ca sahajātahetuṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ, vīriyavīmaṃsājeṭṭhakakāle siyā maggādhipati, chandacittajeṭṭhakakāle siyā navattabbā. "Dasindriyā siyā uppannā, siyā uppādino"ti satta rūpindriyāni tīṇi ca vipākindriyāni sandhāya vuttaṃ; "tīṇindriyāni bahiddhārammaṇānī"ti tīṇi lokuttarindriyāni. Iti imasmimpi pañhāpucchake dasindriyāni kāmāvacarāni, tīṇi lokuttarāni, nava lokiyalokuttaramissakāneva kathitāni; ayampi abhidhammabhājanīyena saddhiṃ ekaparicchedova hoti. Evamayaṃ indriyavibhaṅgo dveparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassitoti.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye ekamekassa indriyassa kiccaṃ dassitaṃ. Cakkhundriyādīnaṃ tāva attano tikkhamandādibhāvena cakkhuviññāṇādidhammānaṃ attākārānuvattāpanaṃ kiccaṃ; manindriyassa sahajātadhammānaṃ attano vasavattāpanaṃ; jīvitindriyassa sahajātadhammānupālanaṃ; sukhadukkhasomanassadomanassindriyānaṃ sahajātadhamme abhibhavitvā yathāsakaṃ oḷārikākārānupāpanaṃ; upekkhindriyassa santapaṇītamajjhattākārānupāpanaṃ; saddhādīnaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanaṃ sampayuttadhammānañca pasannākārādibhāvasampāpanaṃ; anaññātaññassāmītindriyassa saṃyojanattayappahānaṃ; aññindriyassa kāmarāgabyāpādādītanukaraṇapahānaṃ; aññātāvindriyassa sabbakiccesu ussukkappahānanti. Desanākkamopi paṭipattiṃ dasseti — attabhāvapariyāpannehi cakkhādīhi ārabhitvā, "yaṃ kiñci vedayitaṃ sabbaṃ taṃ sukhadukkha"nti ñāpetvā, taṃnirodhatthaṃ bhāvetabbāti saddhādīni desitāni, ante ca paramassāsabhūtaṃ aññātāvindriyaṃ. Tasmā yoginā sukhadukkhasomanassadomanassindriyānaṃ oḷārikākārena vasavattitaṃ pahāya, saddhāvīriyasatisamādhipaññindriyānaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanakiccaṃ upasampādetvā, "imāya paṭipattiyā esa dhammo paṭhamaṃ attani pātubhavatī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake bāvīsatindriyānaṃ kusalākusalābyākatabhāvo ārammaṇādivibhāgo ca pāḷinayānusārena veditabbo. Tattha satta rūpindriyāni anārammaṇāni; sukhadukkhadvayaṃ ekantaparittārammaṇaṃ; domanassindriyaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha parittārammaṇaṃ, mahaggate ārabbha mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ ārabbha navattabbārammaṇaṃ; tīṇi lokuttarindriyāni bahiddhārammaṇāni. Bhūmito ca dasindriyāni kāmāvacarāni, tīṇi lokuttarāni, nava pana lokiyalokuttaramissakāni — manindriyajīvitindriyasomanassindriyaupekkhindriyāni ceva saddhāvīriyasatisamādhipaññindriyapañcakañca. Tasmā yoginā "aññāni me lokiyakāle indriyāni, aññāni lokuttarakāle"ti vikappaṃ pahāya, tesuyeva navasu missakindriyesu — visesato saddhādipañcake — nirantarabhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, "yeheva ajja appamatto kusalaṃ upasampādemi, teheva parato dukkhassantaṃ nigacchāmī"ti sallakkhento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Analysis of the Faculties ^2-5-0
#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-5-1-0

219. There are twenty-two faculties – the eye faculty, the ear faculty, the nose faculty, the tongue faculty, the body faculty, the mind faculty, the faculty of femininity, the faculty of masculinity, the life faculty, the faculty of pleasure, the faculty of pain, the faculty of gladness, the faculty of sadness, the faculty of equanimity, the faculty of confidence, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known", the faculty of final knowledge, and the faculty of one who has fully known. ^2-219

220. Therein, what is the eye faculty? The eye that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the eye faculty".
Therein, what is the ear faculty… the nose faculty… the tongue faculty… the body faculty? The body that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the body faculty".
Therein, what is the mind faculty? As a single class, the mind faculty is joined with contact. As a twofold class: there is mind with a root cause, and mind without a root cause. As a threefold class: there is wholesome mind, unwholesome mind, and neutral mind. As a fourfold class: there is mind belonging to the sense sphere, to the fine-material sphere, to the immaterial sphere, and mind not included in any sphere. As a fivefold class: there is mind joined with the faculty of pleasure, joined with the faculty of pain, joined with the faculty of gladness, joined with the faculty of sadness, and joined with the faculty of equanimity. As a sixfold class: there is eye-consciousness… mind-consciousness. This is the mind faculty as a sixfold class.
As a sevenfold class: there is eye-consciousness… body-consciousness, the mind element, and the mind-consciousness element. This is the mind faculty as a sevenfold class.
As an eightfold class: there is eye-consciousness… body-consciousness – which may be accompanied by pleasure or by pain – the mind element, and the mind-consciousness element. This is the mind faculty as an eightfold class.
As a ninefold class: there is eye-consciousness… body-consciousness, the mind element, and the mind-consciousness element – which may be wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral. This is the mind faculty as a ninefold class.
As a tenfold class: there is eye-consciousness… body-consciousness – which may be accompanied by pleasure or by pain – the mind element, and the mind-consciousness element – which may be wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral. This is the mind faculty as a tenfold class… thus the mind faculty is classified in many ways. This is called "the mind faculty".
Therein, what is the faculty of femininity? A woman's feminine features, feminine marks, feminine ways, feminine manner, femininity, the state of being a woman – this is called "the faculty of femininity".
Therein, what is the faculty of masculinity? A man's masculine features, masculine marks, masculine ways, masculine manner, masculinity, the state of being a man – this is called "the faculty of masculinity".
Therein, what is the life faculty? The life faculty is twofold: there is the material life faculty, and there is the immaterial life faculty.
Therein, what is the material life faculty? The life span of material states, their persistence, their keeping going, their being kept going, their continuing, their proceeding, their maintenance, their life, the life faculty – this is called "the material life faculty".
Therein, what is the immaterial life faculty? The life span of immaterial states, their persistence, their keeping going, their being kept going, their continuing, their proceeding, their maintenance, their life, the life faculty – this is called "the immaterial life faculty". This is called "the life faculty".
Therein, what is the faculty of pleasure? Bodily comfort, bodily pleasure; the comfortable, pleasant experience born of body-contact; the comfortable, pleasant feeling born of body-contact – this is called "the faculty of pleasure".
Therein, what is the faculty of pain? Bodily discomfort, bodily pain; the uncomfortable, painful experience born of body-contact; the uncomfortable, painful feeling born of body-contact – this is called "the faculty of pain".
Therein, what is the faculty of gladness? Mental comfort, mental pleasure; the comfortable, pleasant experience born of mind-contact; the comfortable, pleasant feeling born of mind-contact – this is called "the faculty of gladness".
Therein, what is the faculty of sadness? Mental discomfort, mental pain; the uncomfortable, painful experience born of mind-contact; the uncomfortable, painful feeling born of mind-contact – this is called "the faculty of sadness".
Therein, what is the faculty of equanimity? What is mentally neither comfortable nor uncomfortable; the experience born of mind-contact that is neither painful nor pleasant; the feeling born of mind-contact that is neither painful nor pleasant – this is called "the faculty of equanimity".
Therein, what is the faculty of confidence? The confidence, the trusting, the reliance, the deep trust; confidence, the faculty of confidence, the power of confidence – this is called "the faculty of confidence".
Therein, what is the faculty of energy? The mental arousing of energy, the exertion, the endeavour, the striving, the effort, the zeal, the persistence, the vigour, the steadiness, the unslackening endeavour, the not putting down of desire, the not putting down of the burden, the firm taking up of the burden; energy, the faculty of energy, the power of energy – this is called "the faculty of energy".
Therein, what is the faculty of mindfulness? The mindfulness, the remembering, the recollecting; mindfulness, the bearing in mind, the keeping in mind, the non-drifting, the non-forgetting; mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness – this is called "the faculty of mindfulness".
Therein, what is the faculty of concentration? The steadiness of mind, the stability, the standing firm, the non-scattering, the non-distraction, the undistracted state of mind; calm, the faculty of concentration, the power of concentration, right concentration – this is called "the faculty of concentration".
Therein, what is the faculty of wisdom? The wisdom, the understanding… the non-delusion, the investigation of states, right view – this is called "the faculty of wisdom".
Therein, what is the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known"? The wisdom, the understanding… the non-delusion, the investigation of states, the right view, the awakening factor of investigation of states that is a path factor, included in the path, for the realizing of those states not yet known, not yet seen, not yet reached, not yet found, not yet realized – this is called "the faculty of 'I shall know what is not yet known'".
Therein, what is the faculty of final knowledge? The wisdom, the understanding… the non-delusion, the investigation of states, the right view, the awakening factor of investigation of states that is a path factor, included in the path, for the realizing of those states that are known, seen, reached, found, and realized – this is called the faculty of final knowledge.
Therein, what is the faculty of one who has fully known? The wisdom, the understanding… the non-delusion, the investigation of states, the right view, the awakening factor of investigation of states that is a path factor, included in the path, of one who has fully known those states that are seen, reached, found, and realized – this is called "the faculty of one who has fully known".
The Classification According to the Abhidhamma is finished. ^2-220
