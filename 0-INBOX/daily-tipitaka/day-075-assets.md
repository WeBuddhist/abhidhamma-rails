## Pāli source verses

1547. Katame dhammā ajjhattikā? Cakkhāyatanaṃ…pe… manāyatanaṃ – ime dhammā ajjhattikā. ^1-1547

1548. Katame dhammā bāhirā? Rūpāyatanaṃ…pe… dhammāyatanaṃ – ime dhammā bāhirā. ^1-1548

1549. Katame dhammā upādā? Cakkhāyatanaṃ…pe… kabaḷīkāro āhāro – ime dhammā upādā. ^1-1549

1550. Katame dhammā no upādā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, cattāro ca mahābhūtā, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no upādā. ^1-1550

1551. Katame dhammā upādiṇṇā? Tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, yañca rūpaṃ kammassa katattā – ime dhammā upādiṇṇā. ^1-1551

1552. Katame dhammā anupādiṇṇā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, yañca rūpaṃ na kammassa katattā, cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā anupādiṇṇā. ^1-1552

##### Upādānagocchakaṃ ^1-4-2-11-0

1553. Katame dhammā upādānā? Cattāri upādānāni – kāmupādānaṃ , diṭṭhupādānaṃ, sīlabbatupādānaṃ, attavādupādānaṃ. Kāmupādānaṃ aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Diṭṭhupādānañca sīlabbatupādānañca attavādupādānañca catūsu diṭṭhigatasampayuttesu cittuppādesu uppajjanti – ime dhammā upādānā. ^1-1553

1554. Katame dhammā no upādānā? Ṭhapetvā upādāne avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no upādānā. ^1-1554

1555. Katame dhammā upādāniyā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā upādāniyā. ^1-1555

1556. Katame dhammā anupādāniyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā anupādāniyā. ^1-1556

1557. Katame dhammā upādānasampayuttā? Cattāro diṭṭhigatasampayuttalobhasahagatacittuppādā, cattāro diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatacittuppādā, etthuppannaṃ lobhaṃ ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā upādānasampayuttā. ^1-1557

1558. Katame dhammā upādānavippayuttā? Catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppanno lobho, dve domanassasahagatacittuppādā, vicikicchāsahagato cittuppādo, uddhaccasahagato cittuppādo, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā upādānavippayuttā. ^1-1558

1559. Katame dhammā upādānā ceva upādāniyā ca? Tāneva upādānāni upādānā ceva upādāniyā ca. ^1-1559

1560. Katame dhammā upādāniyā ceva no ca upādānā? Ṭhapetvā upādāne avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā upādāniyā ceva no ca upādānā . Anupādāniyā dhammā na vattabbā – upādānā ceva upādāniyā cātipi, upādāniyā ceva no ca upādānātipi. ^1-1560

1561. Katame dhammā upādānā ceva upādānasampayuttā ca? Yattha diṭṭhi ca lobho ca ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā upādānā ceva upādānasampayuttā ca. ^1-1561

1562. Katame dhammā upādānasampayuttā ceva no ca upādānā? Aṭṭha lobhasahagatacittuppādā, etthuppanne upādāne ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā upādānasampayuttā ceva no ca upādānā. Upādānavippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – upādānā ceva upādānasampayuttā cātipi, upādānasampayuttā ceva no ca upādānātipi. ^1-1562

1563. Katame dhammā upādānavippayuttā upādāniyā? Catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppanno lobho, dve domanassasahagatacittuppādā, vicikicchāsahagato cittuppādo, uddhaccasahagato cittuppādo, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā upādānavippayuttā upādāniyā. ^1-1563

1564. Katame dhammā upādānavippayuttā anupādāniyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā upādānavippayuttā anupādāniyā. Upādānasampayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – upādānavippayuttā upādāniyātipi, upādānavippayuttā anupādāniyātipi. ^1-1564

##### Kilesagocchakaṃ ^1-4-2-12-0

1565. Katame dhammā kilesā? Dasa kilesavatthūni – lobho, doso, moho, māno, diṭṭhi, vicikicchā, thinaṃ, uddhaccaṃ, ahirikaṃ, anottappaṃ. Lobho aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Doso dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Moho sabbākusalesu uppajjati. Māno catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Diṭṭhi catūsu diṭṭhigatasampayuttesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Vicikicchā vicikicchāsahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Thinaṃ sasaṅkhārikesu akusalesu uppajjati. Uddhaccañca ahirikañca anottappañca sabbākusalesu uppajjanti – ime dhammā kilesā. ^1-1565

1566. Katame dhammā no kilesā? Ṭhapetvā kilese avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no kilesā. ^1-1566

1567. Katame dhammā saṃkilesikā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā saṃkilesikā. ^1-1567

1568. Katame dhammā asaṃkilesikā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā asaṃkilesikā. ^1-1568

1569. Katame dhammā saṃkiliṭṭhā? Dvādasa akusalacittuppādā – ime dhammā saṃkiliṭṭhā. ^1-1569

1570. Katame dhammā asaṃkiliṭṭhā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā asaṃkiliṭṭhā. ^1-1570

1571. Katame dhammā kilesasampayuttā? Dvādasa akusalacittuppādā – ime dhammā kilesasampayuttā. ^1-1571

1572. Katame dhammā kilesavippayuttā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā kilesavippayuttā. ^1-1572

1573. Katame dhammā kilesā ceva saṃkilesikā ca? Teva kilesā kilesā ceva saṃkilesikā ca. ^1-1573

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Mahantaradukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaradukaatthuddhāraniddese ñāṇasampayuttacittuppādā sekkhāsekkhānaṃ attanā paṭividdhamaggānaṃ paccavekkhaṇakāle maggārammaṇā, maggena pana asahajātattā na maggahetukā, maggaṃ garuṃ katvā paccavekkhaṇakāle ārammaṇādhipativasena maggādhipatino. Dvādasavidhe rūpāvacaracatutthajjhāne sabbatthapādakacatutthādīni nava jhānāni neva maggārammaṇāni na maggahetukāni na maggādhipatīni. Atītārammaṇattike, ajjhattārammaṇattike ca viññāṇañcāyatanādiniddeso vuttanayeneva veditabbo.

#### Upādānagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-11|↑↑↑]]

Upādānagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese upādānānaṃ atthuddhāraṃ āsavagocchakānaṃ vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Tattha parittārammaṇattike dvipañcaviññāṇāni cakkhupasādādayo nissāya niyameneva parittadhamme ārabbha pavattantīti parittārammaṇāni. Aṭṭha pana sahetukavipākacittuppādā tadārammaṇavasena paṭisandhibhavaṅgacutivasena ca parittadhammeyeva ārabbha pavattantīti.

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yattha dve tayo kilesā ekato uppajjantīti dasadhā kilesānaṃ ekato uppatti hoti. Lobho chadhā, paṭigho dvidhā, moho dvidhā ekato uppajjati. Lobho tāva asaṅkhārike diṭṭhivippayutte mohauddhaccaahirikānottappehi pe diṭṭhisampayutte sasaṅkhārike mohadiṭṭhithinauddhaccaahirikānottappehi saddhinti chadhā ekato uppajjati. Sesaṃ sabbattha uttānatthamevāti.

#### Mahantaradukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaradukaatthuddhāre sārammaṇādayo dukā uddhaṭā. Sekhāsekhānaṃ maggapaccavekkhaṇakāle ñāṇasampayuttacittuppādā maggārammaṇā ceva, maggaṃ garuṃ katvā ārammaṇādhipatino ca honti; na pana maggahetukā, maggena asahajātattā. #### Upādānagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-11|↑↑↑]]

Upādānaatthuddhāre cattāri upādānāni (kāmupādānaṃ diṭṭhupādānaṃ sīlabbatupādānaṃ attavādupādānaṃ) āsavānaṃ vuttanayeneva uddhaṭāni. Aṭṭha sahetukavipākacittuppādā tadārammaṇapaṭisandhibhavaṅgacutivasena parittadhammeyeva ārabbha pavattanti. Tasmā yoginā catubbidhaṃ upādānaṃ "idha upādiyatī"ti ñatvā, kāmesu diṭṭhīsu sīlabbate attavāde ca gahaṇaṃ maggañāṇena pahāya, anupādāno cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ; anupādāya hi cittaṃ vimuccati.

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesaatthuddhāre dasa kilesā ekato uppattivasena uddhaṭā — lobho chadhā, paṭigho dvidhā, moho ca dvidhā ekato uppajjati. Lobho tāva asaṅkhārike diṭṭhivippayutte mohauddhaccaahirikānottappehi saddhiṃ…pe… uppajjati. Ete kilesā cittaṃ santāpenti kilissanti ca. Tasmā yoginā "imasmiṃ cittuppāde ime kilesā ekato uppajjantī"ti kilesajālaṃ pariññāya, lobhadosamohe vipassanāmaggañāṇehi khepetvā, nikkilesaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Which phenomena are internal? The eye-base... up to... the mind-base – these phenomena are internal. ^1-1547

Which phenomena are external? The visible-form-base... up to... the dhamma-base – these phenomena are external. ^1-1548

Which phenomena are objects of clinging? The eye-base... up to... edible food – these phenomena are objects of clinging. ^1-1549

Which phenomena are not objects of clinging? Wholesome states in the four planes, unwholesome states, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, the four great essential matters, and Nibbana – these phenomena are not objects of clinging. ^1-1550

Which phenomena are clung-to? Resultant states in the three planes, and matter produced by kamma – these phenomena are clung-to. ^1-1551

Which phenomena are not clung-to? Wholesome states in the three planes, unwholesome states, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter not produced by kamma, the four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbana – these phenomena are not clung-to. ^1-1552

##### Upādānagocchakaṃ (The Section on Clinging) ^1-4-2-11-0

Which phenomena are clingings? The four clingings: sensual clinging, clinging to views, clinging to rites and rituals, clinging to a doctrine of self. Sensual clinging arises in the eight consciousnesses accompanied by greed. Clinging to views, clinging to rites and rituals, and clinging to a doctrine of self arise in the four consciousnesses associated with wrong view – these phenomena are clingings. ^1-1553

Which phenomena are not clingings? Apart from the clingings, the remaining unwholesome states, wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter, and Nibbana – these phenomena are not clingings. ^1-1554

Which phenomena are objects of clinging? Wholesome states in the three planes, unwholesome states, resultant states in the three planes, functional states in the three planes, and all material phenomena – these phenomena are objects of clinging. ^1-1555

Which phenomena are not objects of clinging? The four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not objects of clinging. ^1-1556

Which phenomena are associated with clinging? The four consciousnesses rooted in greed associated with wrong view, and the four consciousnesses rooted in greed dissociated from wrong view, excluding the greed arising therein – these phenomena are associated with clinging. ^1-1557

Which phenomena are dissociated from clinging? The greed arising in the four consciousnesses rooted in greed dissociated from wrong view, the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, the consciousness accompanied by doubt, the consciousness accompanied by restlessness, wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional states in the three planes, material phenomena, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are dissociated from clinging. ^1-1558

Which phenomena are both clinging and objects of clinging? The clinging factors themselves are both clinging and objects of clinging. ^1-1559

Which phenomena are objects of clinging but not clinging itself? All remaining unwholesome states, excluding the clinging factors, wholesome states in the three planes, resultant states in the three planes, functional states in the three planes, and all material phenomena – these phenomena are objects of clinging but not clinging itself. Phenomena that are not objects of clinging cannot be said to be both clinging and objects of clinging, nor objects of clinging but not clinging itself. ^1-1560

Which phenomena are both clinging and associated with clinging? Where wrong view and greed arise together – these phenomena are both clinging and associated with clinging. ^1-1561

Which phenomena are associated with clinging but not clinging itself? The eight consciousnesses rooted in greed, excluding the clinging factors arising therein – these phenomena are associated with clinging but not clinging itself. Phenomena dissociated from clinging cannot be said to be both clinging and associated with clinging, nor associated with clinging but not clinging itself. ^1-1562

Which phenomena are dissociated from clinging but are objects of clinging? The greed arising in the four consciousnesses rooted in greed dissociated from wrong view, the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, the consciousness accompanied by doubt, the consciousness accompanied by restlessness, wholesome states in the three planes, resultant states in the three planes, functional states in the three planes, and all material phenomena – these phenomena are dissociated from clinging but are objects of clinging. ^1-1563

Which phenomena are dissociated from clinging and are not objects of clinging? The four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are dissociated from clinging and are not objects of clinging. Phenomena associated with clinging cannot be said to be dissociated from clinging but objects of clinging, nor dissociated from clinging and not objects of clinging. ^1-1564

##### The Section on Defilements ^1-4-2-12-0

What are defilements? The ten bases of defilements: greed, hatred, delusion, conceit, wrong view, doubt, sloth, restlessness, shamelessness, fearlessness of wrongdoing. Greed arises in the eight consciousnesses accompanied by greed. Hatred arises in the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure. Delusion arises in all unwholesome states. Conceit arises in the four consciousnesses accompanied by greed, dissociated from wrong view. Wrong view arises in the four consciousnesses associated with wrong view. Doubt arises in the consciousness accompanied by doubt. Sloth arises in prompted unwholesome states. Restlessness, shamelessness, and fearlessness of wrongdoing arise in all unwholesome states. These states are defilements. ^1-1565

What are not defilements? Excluding defilements, the remaining unwholesome states, wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna. These states are not defilements. ^1-1566

What are defiling things? Wholesome states in the three planes, unwholesome states, resultant states in the three planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, and all matter. These states are defiling. ^1-1567

What are non-defiling things? The four supramundane paths, the four fruition states, and Nibbāna. These states are non-defiling. ^1-1568

What are defiled things? The twelve unwholesome consciousnesses. These states are defiled. ^1-1569

What are undefiled things? Wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna. These states are undefiled. ^1-1570

What are associated with defilements? The twelve unwholesome consciousnesses. These states are associated with defilements. ^1-1571

What are dissociated from defilements? Wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna. These states are dissociated from defilements. ^1-1572

What are defilements and defiling? The defilements themselves are defilements and defiling. ^1-1573
