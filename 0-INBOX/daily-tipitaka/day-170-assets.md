#### Bhūmantaradassanavāro ^2-18-5-0

Kāmāvacarā dhammā, na kāmāvacarā dhammā, rūpāvacarā dhammā, na rūpāvacarā dhammā, arūpāvacarā dhammā, na arūpāvacarā dhammā, pariyāpannā dhammā, apariyāpannā dhammā.
Katame dhammā kāmāvacarā? Heṭṭhato avīcinirayaṃ pariyantaṃ karitvā, uparito paranimmitavasavattī deve antokaritvā, yaṃ etasmiṃ antare etthāvacarā ettha pariyāpannā khandhadhātuāyatanā; rūpaṃ, vedanā, saññā, saṅkhārā, viññāṇaṃ – ime dhammā kāmāvacarā.
Katame dhammā na kāmāvacarā? Rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā, apariyāpannā – ime dhammā na kāmāvacarā.
Katame dhammā rūpāvacarā? Heṭṭhato brahmalokaṃ pariyantaṃ karitvā, uparito akaniṭṭhe deve antokaritvā, yaṃ etasmiṃ antare etthāvacarā ettha pariyāpannā samāpannassa vā upapannassa vā diṭṭhadhammasukhavihārissa vā cittacetasikā dhammā – ime dhammā rūpāvacarā.
Katame dhammā na rūpāvacarā? Kāmāvacarā, arūpāvacarā, apariyāpannā – ime dhammā na rūpāvacarā.
Katame dhammā arūpāvacarā? Heṭṭhato ākāsānañcāyatanūpage deve pariyantaṃ karitvā, uparito nevasaññānāsaññāyatanūpage deve antokaritvā, yaṃ etasmiṃ antare etthāvacarā ettha pariyāpannā samāpannassa vā upapannassa vā diṭṭhadhammasukhavihārissa vā cittacetasikā dhammā – ime dhammā arūpāvacarā.
Katame dhammā na arūpāvacarā? Kāmāvacarā, rūpāvacarā, apariyāpannā – ime dhammā na arūpāvacarā.
Katame dhammā pariyāpannā? Sāsavā kusalākusalabyākatā dhammā kāmāvacarā, rūpāvacarā, arūpāvacarā, rūpakkhandho, vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho, viññāṇakkhandho – ime dhammā pariyāpannā.
Katame dhammā apariyāpannā? Maggā ca, maggaphalāni ca, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu – ime dhammā apariyāpannā. ^2-1020

Tena māsena dvādasamāsiyo saṃvaccharo. Tena saṃvaccharena dibbāni pañca vassasatāni cātummahārājikānaṃ devānaṃ āyuppamāṇaṃ. Manussānaṃ gaṇanāya kittakaṃ hoti? Navuti vassasatasahassāni.

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Dhammadassanavāro – Bhūmantaradassanavāro

[[#^toc-18-4|↑↑↑]]

Catutthavāre kāmadhātuyā, rūpadhātuyā, asaññasattānaṃ, arūpadhātuyā ca upapattikkhaṇe vijjamānāvijjamānā dhammā dassitā. Tattha ekādasāyatanānīti saddāyatanavajjāni; tañhi ekantena paṭisandhiyaṃ nuppajjati. Iminā nayena sabbattha attho veditabbo. Sattake "devānaṃ asurāna"nti gativasena avatvā avisesena "gabbhaseyyakāna"nti vuttaṃ; tasmā yattha yattha gabbhaseyyakā sambhavanti tattha tattha tesaṃ sattāyatanāni veditabbāni, tathā dhātuyo. Sesamettha uttānatthameva. Pañcamavāre pana tesaṃyeva dhammānaṃ kāmāvacarādibhūmantaravasena pariyāpannāpariyāpannavasena ca dassanaṃ kataṃ; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ taṃ dhammasaṅgahaṭṭhakathāyaṃ vuttameva.

### Uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavāro

[[#^toc-18-5|↑↑↑]]

Chaṭṭhavāre gatīsu uppādakakammañca āyuppamāṇañca dassitaṃ – kena kammena kattha upapajjanti, upapannānañca kittakaṃ āyuppamāṇanti. Ettha cha kāmāvacarā, nava brahmalokā, pañca suddhāvāsā, cattāro arūpā asaññasattavehapphalehi saddhiṃ chabbīsati devalokā; manussalokena saddhiṃ sattavīsati. Catūsu pana apāyesu bhummadevesu ca kammameva pamāṇaṃ; yāva kammaṃ na khīyati, na tāva cavanti.

#### Uppādakakammaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Uppādakakamme pañcahi kāmaguṇehi nānappakārehi vā iddhivisesehi dibbantīti devā; te sammutidevā upapattidevā visuddhidevāti tividhā. Sammutidevāti lokasammutiyā devā – rājāno deviyo kumārā; upapattidevāti devaloke uppannattā upapattiyā devā; visuddhidevāti sabbesaṃ devānaṃ pūjārahā sabbakilesavisuddhiyā devā, arahanto. Dānaṃ datvā, sīlaṃ samādiyitvā, cātuddasādīsu aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgataṃ uposathaṃ upavasitvā appekacce khattiyamahāsālādīnaṃ manussānaṃ, appekacce cātumahārājikādīnaṃ channaṃ kāmāvacaradevānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapajjanti; yasmā dānādipuññakammaṃ manussasobhagyatāya paccayo, adhimattabhāvepi ca nānappakārabhedato nānappakārassa khattiyamahāsālādibhāvassa paccayo, tasmā tassa vasena upapattibhedo dassito. Sahabyatanti sahabhāvaṃ, sabhāgā hutvā nibbattantīti attho. Tuṭṭhā pahaṭṭhāti tusitā; yathārucite bhoge nimminitvā ramantīti nimmānaratī; parehi nimmitesu bhogesu vasaṃ vattentīti paranimmitavasavattī.

#### Āyuppamāṇaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyuppamāṇe manussānaṃ vassasataṃ appaṃ vā bhiyyoti dutiyaṃ vassasataṃ appatvā vīsāya vā tiṃsāya vā…pe… saṭṭhiyā vā vassehi adhikampi vassasataṃ. Brahmapārisajjā brahmapurohitā mahābrahmāno paṭhamajjhānabhūmiyaṃ, parittābhā appamāṇābhā ābhassarā dutiyajjhānabhūmiyaṃ, parittasubhā appamāṇasubhā subhakiṇhā tatiyajjhānabhūmiyaṃ ekatale vasanti; āyuantaraṃ pana nesaṃ nānā. Catutthajjhāne ārammaṇamanasikārachandapaṇidhiadhimokkhābhinīhārapaññānānattatāya upapattinānattaṃ hoti. Asaññasattā pana ekacce titthāyatane pabbajitvā "cittaṃ nissāya rajjanadussanamuyhanāni nāma hontī"ti citte dosaṃ disvā "acittakabhāvo nāma sobhano, diṭṭhadhammanibbānameta"nti saññāvirāgaṃ janetvā tatrūpagaṃ samāpattiṃ bhāvetvā tattha nibbattanti, pañca kappasatāni ṭhatvā kāmāvacarasaññuppādena tato cavanti. Manussesu gihibhāve ṭhitāyeva sotāpannāpi honti, sakadāgāmiphalampi anāgāmiphalampi arahattaphalampi pāpuṇanti. Aṭṭhasamāpattilābhīnaṃ paguṇajjhānaṃ, sabbesu paguṇesu patthanā, patthanāya asati maraṇasamaye samāpannā samāpatti upapattiṃ niyameti. Vehapphalā, akaniṭṭhā, nevasaññānāsaññāyatananti ime tayo devalokā seṭṭhabhavā nāma; tattha nibbattaanāgāmino tattha tattheva parinibbāyanti. Avasāne ca "natthi koci bhavo nicco"ti dīpetvā jarāmaraṇamokkhāya maggabhāvanā thomitā.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Dhammadassanavāro – Bhūmantaradassanavāro

[[#^toc-18-4|↑↑↑]]

Dhammadassanavāre upapattikkhaṇe sabbesaṃ ekaṃ dukkhasaccameva pātubhavati; saddāyatanaṃ ekantena paṭisandhiyaṃ nuppajjati; kassaci tayo hetū, kassaci dve hetū pātubhavanti, avasesā ahetukā; asaññasattā pana acittakā pātubhavanti. Bhūmantaradassanavāre avīcinirayato yāva paranimmitavasavattī deve kāmāvacarā, maggā ca maggaphalāni ca asaṅkhatā ca dhātu apariyāpannāti vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "paṭisandhi nāma dukkhasaccamevā"ti sallakkhetvā upapattiyaṃ anabhinanditvā, ñāṇasampayuttāya paṭisandhiyā laddhāya paññindriyaṃ bhāvetvā, apariyāpanne maggaphale asaṅkhatāya ca dhātuyā cittaṃ namentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavāro

[[#^toc-18-5|↑↑↑]]

Uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavāre chabbīsati devalokā, manussalokena saddhiṃ sattavīsati dassitā; niraye ca sesaapāyesu ca kammameva pamāṇaṃ, yāva kammaṃ na khīyati na tāva cavanti. Puññatejena ukkhittāpi bhavaggaṃ sampattāpi punāgacchanti duggatiṃ; tāva dīghāyukāpi sattā āyusaṅkhayā cavanti. Tasmā yoginā duccaritakammaṃ akaronto, dānasīlauposathādipuññaṃ upasampādetvāpi tattha nikantiṃ akatvā, jarāmaraṇamokkhāya uttamaṃ maggaṃ bhāventena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Uppādakakammaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Uppādakakamme dānaṃ datvā, sīlaṃ samādiyitvā, cātuddasādīsu aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgataṃ uposathaṃ upavasitvā appekacce khattiyabrāhmaṇagahapatimahāsālānaṃ, appekacce cātumahārājikādīnaṃ devānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapajjanti; dānādipuññakammañca adhimattabhāvepi nānappakārabhedato nānappakārassa upapattibhedassa paccayo hoti. Visuddhidevā pana sabbakilesavisuddhiyā sabbesaṃ devānaṃ pūjārahā arahanto. Tasmā yoginā maccheraṃ dussīlyañca pahāya dānaṃ sīlaṃ uposathakammañca sakkaccaṃ upasampādetvā, upapattidevabhāvamattena asantuṭṭho sabbakilesavisuddhiyā visuddhidevabhāvaṃ patthentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Āyuppamāṇaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyuppamāṇe manussānaṃ vassasataṃ appaṃ vā bhiyyo, devabrahmānaṃ pana dīghaṃ āyu vuttaṃ; tāva dīghāyukāpi sattā āyusaṅkhayā cavanti. Asaññasattā ca citte dosaṃ disvā "acittakabhāvo nāma sobhano, diṭṭhadhammanibbānameta"nti saññāvirāgaṃ janetvā tattha nibbattanti, pañcakappasatāvasāne puna cavanti. Manussesu pana gihibhāve ṭhitāyeva sotāpannāpi honti, sakadāgāmiphalampi anāgāmiphalampi arahattaphalampi pāpuṇanti; aṭṭhasamāpattilābhīnañca paguṇajjhānaṃ, patthanā, maraṇasamaye samāpannā samāpatti ca upapattiṃ niyameti. Tasmā yoginā appakaṃ manussāyuṃ ñatvā pamādaṃ pahāya, acittakabhāvaṃ nibbānanti agaṇhanto, gihibhāvepi maggaphalāni pattabbānīti ussāhaṃ janetvā, jhānaṃ paguṇaṃ katvā, sabbāsave pariññāya anāsavo parinibbātuṃ maggaṃ bhāventena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

#### The Section Showing the Different Planes ^2-18-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1020]]

There are things of the sense sphere and things not of the sense sphere; things of the fine-material sphere and things not of the fine-material sphere; things of the immaterial sphere and things not of the immaterial sphere; things included in a sphere and things not included in any sphere.
Which things are of the sense sphere? Taking as the lower limit the Avīci hell below, and taking as the upper limit the gods who wield power over others' creations, the aggregates, elements, and sense-fields that move in this interval and are included here — material form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness — these things are of the sense sphere.
Which things are not of the sense sphere? Things of the fine-material sphere, things of the immaterial sphere, and things not included in any sphere — these things are not of the sense sphere.
Which things are of the fine-material sphere? Taking as the lower limit the Brahmā world below, and taking as the upper limit the Akaniṭṭha gods above, the mind and mental factors of one who has attained, or one who has been reborn there, or one dwelling happily in this very life, that move in this interval and are included here — these things are of the fine-material sphere.
Which things are not of the fine-material sphere? Things of the sense sphere, things of the immaterial sphere, and things not included in any sphere — these things are not of the fine-material sphere.
Which things are of the immaterial sphere? Taking as the lower limit the gods who have reached the field of infinite space below, and taking as the upper limit the gods who have reached the field of neither-perception-nor-non-perception above, the mind and mental factors of one who has attained, or one who has been reborn there, or one dwelling happily in this very life, that move in this interval and are included here — these things are of the immaterial sphere.
Which things are not of the immaterial sphere? Things of the sense sphere, things of the fine-material sphere, and things not included in any sphere — these things are not of the immaterial sphere.
Which things are included? The wholesome, unwholesome, and neutral things subject to mental taints, of the sense sphere, the fine-material sphere, and the immaterial sphere: the aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — these things are included.
Which things are not included? The paths, the fruits of the paths, and the unconditioned element — these things are not included. ^2-1020

#### The Section on Generating Action and the Measure of Life ^2-18-6-0

##### The Generating Action ^2-18-6-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1021]]

"Gods": there are three kinds of gods — gods by convention, gods by rebirth, gods by purity.
Gods by convention are kings, queens, and princes.
Gods by rebirth are the gods from the realm of the Four Great Kings upwards.
Gods by purity are the arahants.
Having given gifts, undertaken virtue, and kept the observance day, where are beings reborn? Having given gifts, undertaken virtue, and kept the observance day, some are reborn in the company of wealthy nobles, some are reborn in the company of wealthy brahmins, some are reborn in the company of wealthy householders, some are reborn in the company of the gods of the Four Great Kings, some are reborn in the company of the gods of the Thirty-Three, some are reborn in the company of the Yāma gods, some are reborn in the company of the Tusita gods, some are reborn in the company of the gods who delight in creating, some are reborn in the company of the gods who wield power over others' creations. ^2-1021

##### The Measure of Life ^2-18-6-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1022]]

What is the measure of life of human beings? One hundred years, or a little more. ^2-1022

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1023]]

What is the measure of life of the gods of the Four Great Kings? Fifty human years are one day and night of the gods of the Four Great Kings. Thirty such nights make a month; twelve such months make a year; five hundred such divine years are the measure of life of the gods of the Four Great Kings. How much is that in human reckoning? Nine million years.
What is the measure of life of the gods of the Thirty-Three? One hundred human years are one day and night of the gods of the Thirty-Three. Thirty such nights make a month; twelve such months make a year; one thousand such divine years are the measure of life of the gods of the Thirty-Three. How much is that in human reckoning? Thirty-six million years.
What is the measure of life of the Yāma gods? Two hundred human years are one day and night of the Yāma gods. Thirty such nights make a month; twelve such months make a year; two thousand such divine years are the measure of life of the Yāma gods. How much is that in human reckoning? One hundred and forty-four million years.
What is the measure of life of the Tusita gods? Four hundred human years are one day and night of the Tusita gods. Thirty such nights make a month; twelve such months make a year; four thousand such divine years are the measure of life of the Tusita gods. How much is that in human reckoning? Five hundred and seventy-six million years.
What is the measure of life of the gods who delight in creating? Eight hundred human years are one day and night of the gods who delight in creating. Thirty such nights make a month; twelve such months make a year; eight thousand such divine years are the measure of life of the gods who delight in creating. How much is that in human reckoning? Two thousand three hundred and four million years.
What is the measure of life of the gods who wield power over others' creations? Sixteen hundred human years are one day and night of the gods who wield power over others' creations. Thirty such nights make a month; twelve such months make a year; sixteen thousand such divine years are the measure of life of the gods who wield power over others' creations. How much is that in human reckoning? Nine thousand two hundred and sixteen million years.
These six sense-sphere realms, all abounding in every sense pleasure;
How long is the life of all of them counted as one?
Twelve hundred and twenty-eight crores of years,
And fifty hundred thousand — so it is proclaimed by way of years. ^2-1023

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1024]]

Having developed the first meditative absorption to a limited degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the first meditative absorption to a limited degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of Brahmā's retinue. What is their measure of life? A third part of an eon.
Having developed the first meditative absorption to a middling degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the first meditative absorption to a middling degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods who are Brahmā's ministers. What is their measure of life? Half an eon.
Having developed the first meditative absorption to a refined degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the first meditative absorption to a refined degree, they are reborn in the company of the Great Brahmā gods. What is their measure of life? An eon. ^2-1024

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1025]]

Having developed the second meditative absorption to a limited degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the second meditative absorption to a limited degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of limited radiance. What is their measure of life? Two eons.
Having developed the second meditative absorption to a middling degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the second meditative absorption to a middling degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of measureless radiance. What is their measure of life? Four eons.
Having developed the second meditative absorption to a refined degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the second meditative absorption to a refined degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of streaming radiance. What is their measure of life? Eight eons. ^2-1025

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1026]]

Having developed the third meditative absorption to a limited degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the third meditative absorption to a limited degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of limited beauty. What is their measure of life? Sixteen eons.
Having developed the third meditative absorption to a middling degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the third meditative absorption to a middling degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of measureless beauty. What is their measure of life? Thirty-two eons.
Having developed the third meditative absorption to a refined degree, where are they reborn? Having developed the third meditative absorption to a refined degree, they are reborn in the company of the gods of complete beauty. What is their measure of life? Sixty-four eons. ^2-1026

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1027]]

Having developed the fourth meditative absorption — according to the difference of object, the difference of attention, the difference of desire, the difference of aspiration, the difference of decision, the difference of resolve, the difference of wisdom — some are reborn in the company of the non-percipient gods, some in the company of the Vehapphala gods, some in the company of the Aviha gods, some in the company of the Atappa gods, some in the company of the Sudassa gods, some in the company of the Sudassī gods, some in the company of the Akaniṭṭha gods, some in the company of the gods who have reached the field of infinite space, some in the company of the gods who have reached the field of infinite consciousness, some in the company of the gods who have reached the field of nothingness, some in the company of the gods who have reached the field of neither-perception-nor-non-perception.
What is the measure of life of the non-percipient gods and the Vehapphala gods? Five hundred eons.
What is the measure of life of the Aviha gods? A thousand eons.
What is the measure of life of the Atappa gods? Two thousand eons.
What is the measure of life of the Sudassa gods? Four thousand eons.
What is the measure of life of the Sudassī gods? Eight thousand eons.
What is the measure of life of the Akaniṭṭha gods? Sixteen thousand eons. ^2-1027
