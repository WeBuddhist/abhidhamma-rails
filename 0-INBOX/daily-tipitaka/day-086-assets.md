## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayopi vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Kevalañhi saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti, dukesu ca sukhasahagatadukādayopi. Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā 'phassasampayutto'ti avatvā 'cittasampayutto'ti vuttaṃ; dukesu cettha hetudukādayopi labbhanti, tikā saññākkhandhasadisā eva. Viññāṇakkhandhaniddese cakkhusamphassajādibhāvaṃ avatvā 'cakkhuviññāṇa'ntiādi vuttaṃ — na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ 'manosamphassaja'nti niddisituṃ; sesaṃ saññākkhandhe vuttasadisameva. Imesaṃ tiṇṇampi khandhānaṃ niddeseyeva vedanākkhandhaniddesato atirekatikadukā laddhā, tesaṃ vasena vārappabhedo veditabbo.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayo vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti; saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā "cittasampayutto"ti vuttaṃ; viññāṇakkhandhaniddese "cakkhuviññāṇa"ntiādi vuttaṃ, na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ manosamphassajanti niddisituṃ. Iti ekasmiṃ nayamhi gahite sesā tenupāyena gahitāva honti. Tasmā yoginā ekasmiṃ khandhe sammasanañāṇaṃ paṭiladdhaṃ sesesupi upanetvā, "sabbe pañca khandhā aniccā dukkhā anattā"ti ekarasena passanto, khandhesu attagāhaṃ pahāya, sabbaso anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

92. What is the aggregate of formations? As a single category: formations are associated with mind.
As two categories: there is a formation that is a root-cause, and a formation that is not a root-cause.
As three categories: there is a formation that is wholesome, a formation that is unwholesome, and a formation that is indeterminate.
As four categories: there is a formation belonging to the sense sphere, to the fine-material sphere, to the immaterial sphere, and a formation that is unincluded.
As five categories: volition associated with the pleasure faculty, the pain faculty, the joy faculty, the displeasure faculty, and the equanimity faculty. Thus, as five categories, this is the aggregate of formations.
As six categories: volition born of eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, and mind-contact. Thus, as six categories, this is the aggregate of formations.
As seven categories: volition born of eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, mind-element-contact, and mind-consciousness-element-contact. Thus, as seven categories, this is the aggregate of formations.
As eight categories: volition born of eye-contact … up to … body-contact — the last accompanied by pleasure or by pain — together with volition born of mind-element-contact and mind-consciousness-element-contact. Thus, as eight categories, this is the aggregate of formations.
As nine categories: volition born of eye-contact … up to … mind-element-contact and mind-consciousness-element-contact, each being wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate. Thus, as nine categories, this is the aggregate of formations.
As ten categories: volition born of eye-contact … up to … body-contact — the last accompanied by pleasure or by pain — together with volition born of mind-element-contact and mind-consciousness-element-contact, each being wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate. Thus, as ten categories, this is the aggregate of formations. ^2-92
