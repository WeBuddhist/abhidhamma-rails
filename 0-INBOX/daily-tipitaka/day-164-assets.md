#### Dasakaniddeso ^2-17-11-0

##### (1) Dasa kilesavatthūni ^2-17-11-1-0

Tattha katamāni dasa kilesavatthūni? Lobho, doso, moho, māno, diṭṭhi, vicikicchā, thinaṃ, uddhaccaṃ, ahirikaṃ, anottappaṃ – imāni dasa kilesavatthūni. ^2-966

##### (2) Dasa āghātavatthūni ^2-17-11-2-0

Tattha katamāni dasa āghātavatthūni? "Anatthaṃ me acarī"ti āghāto jāyati, "anatthaṃ me caratī"ti āghāto jāyati, "anatthaṃ me carissatī"ti āghāto jāyati, "piyassa me manāpassa anatthaṃ acari"…pe… anatthaṃ carati…pe… anatthaṃ carissatīti āghāto jāyati, appiyassa me amanāpassa atthaṃ acari…pe… atthaṃ carati…pe… atthaṃ carissatīti āghāto jāyati, aṭṭhāne vā pana āghāto jāyati – imāni dasa āghātavatthūni. ^2-967

##### (3) Dasa akusalakammapathā ^2-17-11-3-0

Tattha katame dasa akusalakammapathā? Pāṇātipāto, adinnādānaṃ, kāmesumicchācāro, musāvādo, pisuṇā vācā, pharusā vācā, samphappalāpo, abhijjhā, byāpādo, micchādiṭṭhi – ime dasa akusalakammapathā. ^2-968

##### (4) Dasa saṃyojanāni ^2-17-11-4-0

Tattha katamāni dasa saṃyojanāni? Kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ, mānasaṃyojanaṃ, diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ, vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ, sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ, bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, issāsaṃyojanaṃ, macchariyasaṃyojanaṃ, avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ – imāni dasa saṃyojanāni. ^2-969

##### (5) Dasa micchattā ^2-17-11-5-0

Tattha katame dasa micchattā? Micchādiṭṭhi, micchāsaṅkappo, micchāvācā, micchākammanto, micchāājīvo, micchāvāyāmo, micchāsati, micchāsamādhi, micchāñāṇaṃ, micchāvimutti – ime dasa micchattā. ^2-970

##### (6) Dasavatthukā micchādiṭṭhi ^2-17-11-6-0

Tattha katamā dasavatthukā micchādiṭṭhi? Natthi dinnaṃ, natthi yiṭṭhaṃ, natthi hutaṃ, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṃ kammānaṃ phalaṃ vipāko, natthi ayaṃ loko, natthi paro loko, natthi mātā, natthi pitā, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi loke samaṇabrāhmaṇā sammaggatā [samaggatā (ka.)] sammā paṭipannā ye imañca lokaṃ parañca lokaṃ sayaṃ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedentīti – ayaṃ dasavatthukā micchādiṭṭhi. ^2-971

##### (7) Dasavatthukā antaggāhikā diṭṭhi ^2-17-11-7-0

Tattha katamā dasavatthukā antaggāhikā diṭṭhi? Sassato lokoti vā, asassato lokoti vā, antavā lokoti vā, anantavā lokoti vā, taṃ jīvaṃ taṃ sarīranti vā, aññaṃ jīvaṃ aññaṃ sarīranti vā, hoti tathāgato paraṃ maraṇāti vā, na hoti tathāgato paraṃ maraṇāti vā, hoti ca na ca hoti tathāgato paraṃ maraṇāti vā, neva hoti na na hoti tathāgato paraṃ maraṇāti vā – ayaṃ dasavatthukā antaggāhikā diṭṭhi.
Dasakaṃ. ^2-972

### Dasakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-11|↑↑↑]]

Dasakaniddese kilesā eva kilesavatthūni. Āghātavatthūni panettha "anatthaṃ me acarī"tiādīnaṃ vasena avikopetabbe khāṇukaṇṭakādimhipi aṭṭhāne uppannāghātena saddhiṃ dasa vuttāni. Micchattesu micchāñāṇanti pāpakiriyāsu upāyacintāvasena pāpaṃ katvā ‘sukataṃ mayā’ti paccavekkhaṇākārena uppanno moho, micchāvimuttīti avimuttasseva sato vimuttasaññitā. Dasa akusalakammapathā, dasa saṃyojanāni, dasavatthukā micchādiṭṭhi, dasavatthukā antaggāhikā diṭṭhi ca heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva uttānatthā.

### Taṇhāvicaritaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-12|↑↑↑]]

Taṇhāvicaritānīti taṇhāsamudācārā taṇhāpavattiyo. Ajjhattikassa upādāyāti ajjhattikaṃ khandhapañcakaṃ upādāya; tattha kañci dhammaṃ anavakāriṃ katvā samūhato gahetvā taṇhāmānadiṭṭhivasena ‘asmī’ti hoti, tasmiṃ sati ‘khattiyo ahaṃ’ti anupanidhāya ‘itthasmī’ti, ‘yathā so khattiyo tathā aha’nti samato upanidhāya ‘evasmī’ti, ‘tato hīno vā seṭṭho vā aha’nti asamato upanidhāya ‘aññathāsmī’ti hoti; bhavissantiādīni anāgatavasena, asmi sātasmīti sassatucchedavasena, siyantiādīni saṃsayaparivitakkavasena, apāhaṃ siyantiādīni patthanākappanavasena vuttāni – dve diṭṭhisīsā, cattāro suddhasīsā, dvādasa sīsamūlakāti aṭṭhārasa. Bāhirassa upādāyāti bāhiraṃ khandhapañcakaṃ upādāya, rūpādīsu ekadesato gahetvā ‘iminā chattena vā khaggena vā khattiyo aha’nti evaṃ aṭṭhārasa; tadekajjhaṃ chattiṃsa, atītānāgatapaccuppannavasena aṭṭhataṇhāvicaritasataṃ hoti. Mānadiṭṭhiyo pana na vinā taṇhāya, tasmā tadekaṭṭhavasena idha vuttā, taṇhāsīsena vā papañcattayampi uddiṭṭhaṃ. Dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni pana brahmajāle veyyākaraṇe satthārā sayaṃ āhacca bhāsitāni, tattha vuttanayeneva pabhedo ca attho ca veditabbo.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

#### The Exposition of the Tens ^2-17-11-0

##### (1) The ten grounds of defilement ^2-17-11-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-966]]

Therein, what are the ten grounds of defilement? Greed, hate, delusion, conceit, views, doubt, dullness, restlessness, shamelessness, lack of moral dread — these are the ten grounds of defilement. ^2-966

##### (2) The ten grounds of resentment ^2-17-11-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-967]]

Therein, what are the ten grounds of resentment? Resentment arises: "He has done me harm"; resentment arises: "He is doing me harm"; resentment arises: "He will do me harm"; "He has done harm… is doing harm… will do harm to one dear and pleasing to me" — resentment arises; "He has done good… is doing good… will do good to one disliked and displeasing to me" — resentment arises; or resentment arises groundlessly — these are the ten grounds of resentment. ^2-967

##### (3) The ten courses of unwholesome action ^2-17-11-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-968]]

Therein, what are the ten courses of unwholesome action? Killing living beings, taking what is not given, sexual misconduct, false speech, divisive speech, harsh speech, idle chatter, covetousness, ill will, wrong view — these are the ten courses of unwholesome action. ^2-968

##### (4) The ten fetters ^2-17-11-4-0

Therein, what are the ten fetters? The fetter of sensual lust, the fetter of aversion, the fetter of conceit, the fetter of views, the fetter of doubt, the fetter of wrong grasping of virtue and observances, the fetter of lust for existence, the fetter of envy, the fetter of stinginess, the fetter of ignorance — these are the ten fetters. ^2-969

##### (5) The ten wrongnesses ^2-17-11-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-970]]

Therein, what are the ten wrongnesses? Wrong view, wrong intention, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, wrong concentration, wrong knowledge, wrong liberation — these are the ten wrongnesses. ^2-970

##### (6) The wrong view with ten grounds ^2-17-11-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-971]]

Therein, what is the wrong view with ten grounds? "There is nothing given, there is nothing offered, there is nothing sacrificed; there is no fruit and result of well-done and badly-done actions; there is no this world, there is no other world; there is no mother, there is no father; there are no spontaneously reborn beings; there are in the world no ascetics and brahmins of right conduct and right practice who declare this world and the other world, having realised them for themselves by direct knowledge" — this is the wrong view with ten grounds. ^2-971

##### (7) The extremist view with ten grounds ^2-17-11-7-0

Therein, what is the extremist view with ten grounds? "The world is eternal", or "the world is not eternal", or "the world is finite", or "the world is infinite", or "the soul and the body are the same", or "the soul is one thing and the body another", or "a Tathāgata exists after death", or "a Tathāgata does not exist after death", or "a Tathāgata both exists and does not exist after death", or "a Tathāgata neither exists nor does not exist after death" — this is the extremist view with ten grounds.

#### The Exposition of the Wanderings of Craving ^2-17-12-0

##### (1) Concerning what is internal ^2-17-12-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-973]]

Therein, what are the eighteen wanderings of craving concerning what is internal? There is "I am"; there is "I am thus"; there is "I am just so"; there is "I am otherwise"; there is "I shall be"; there is "I shall be thus"; there is "I shall be just so"; there is "I shall be otherwise"; there is "I am lasting"; there is "I am perishing"; there is "may I be"; there is "may I be thus"; there is "may I be just so"; there is "may I be otherwise"; there is "would that I might be"; there is "would that I might be thus"; there is "would that I might be just so"; there is "would that I might be otherwise". ^2-973

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-974]]

And how is there "I am"? Not having discarded some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one obtains the desire "I am", one obtains the conceit "I am", one obtains the view "I am". When that exists, there are these proliferations: "I am thus", or "I am just so", or "I am otherwise".
(2) And how is there "I am thus"? "I am a noble", or "I am a brahmin", or "I am a merchant", or "I am a worker", or "I am a householder", or "I am a renunciant", or "I am a god", or "I am a human being", or "I have material form", or "I am immaterial", or "I am percipient", or "I am non-percipient", or "I am neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "I am thus".
(3) And how is there "I am just so"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, so I am a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, so I am a brahmin", or "as he is a merchant, so I am a merchant", or "as he is a worker, so I am a worker", or "as he is a householder, so I am a householder", or "as he is a renunciant, so I am a renunciant", or "as he is a god, so I am a god", or "as he is a human being, so I am a human being", or "as he has material form, so I have material form", or "as he is immaterial, so I am immaterial", or "as he is percipient, so I am percipient", or "as he is non-percipient, so I am non-percipient", or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, so I am neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "I am just so".
(4) And how is there "I am otherwise"? Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, I am not so a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, I am not so a brahmin", or "as he is a merchant, I am not so a merchant", or "as he is a worker, I am not so a worker", or "as he is a householder, I am not so a householder", or "as he is a renunciant, I am not so a renunciant", or "as he is a god, I am not so a god", or "as he is a human being, I am not so a human being", or "as he has material form, I do not so have material form", or "as he is immaterial, I am not so immaterial", or "as he is percipient, I am not so percipient", or "as he is non-percipient, I am not so non-percipient", or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, I am not so neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "I am otherwise".
(5) And how is there "I shall be"? Not having discarded some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one obtains the desire "I shall be", one obtains the conceit "I shall be", one obtains the view "I shall be". When that exists, there are these proliferations: "I shall be thus", or "I shall be just so", or "I shall be otherwise".
(6) And how is there "I shall be thus"? "I shall be a noble", or "I shall be a brahmin", or "I shall be a merchant", or "I shall be a worker", or "I shall be a householder", or "I shall be a renunciant", or "I shall be a god", or "I shall be a human being", or "I shall have material form", or "I shall be immaterial", or "I shall be percipient", or "I shall be non-percipient", or "I shall be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "I shall be thus".

Comparing oneself with another person: "As he is a noble, I shall not so be a noble", or "as he is a brahmin, I shall not so be a brahmin"… or "as he is neither percipient nor non-percipient, I shall not so be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "I shall be otherwise".
(9) And how is there "I am lasting"? Not having discarded some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one thinks, "I am permanent, I am stable, I am eternal, I am not subject to change" — thus there is "I am lasting".
(10) And how is there "I am perishing"? Not having discarded some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one thinks, "I shall be cut off, I shall be destroyed, I shall not be" — thus there is "I am perishing".
(11) And how is there "may I be"? Not having discarded some thing — material form… feeling… perception… formations… consciousness — one obtains the desire "may I be", one obtains the conceit "may I be", one obtains the view "may I be". When that exists, there are these proliferations: "may I be thus", or "may I be just so", or "may I be otherwise".

"May I be a noble", or "may I be a brahmin", or "may I be a merchant", or "may I be a worker", or "may I be a householder", or "may I be a renunciant", or "may I be a god", or "may I be a human being", or "may I have material form", or "may I be immaterial", or "may I be percipient", or "may I be non-percipient", or "may I be neither percipient nor non-percipient" — thus there is "may I be thus".
