## Pāli source verses

1574. Katame dhammā saṃkilesikā ceva no ca kilesā? Ṭhapetvā kilese avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā saṃkilesikā ceva no ca kilesā. Asaṃkilesikā dhammā na vattabbā – kilesā ceva saṃkilesikā cātipi, saṃkilesikā ceva no ca kilesātipi. ^1-1574

1575. Katame dhammā kilesā ceva saṃkiliṭṭhā ca? Teva kilesā kilesā ceva saṃkiliṭṭhā ca. ^1-1575

1576. Katame dhammā saṃkiliṭṭhā ceva no ca kilesā? Ṭhapetvā kilese avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā saṃkiliṭṭhā ceva no ca kilesā . Asaṃkiliṭṭhā dhammā na vattabbā – kilesā ceva saṃkiliṭṭhā cātipi, saṃkiliṭṭhā ceva no ca kilesātipi. ^1-1576

1578. Katame dhammā kilesasampayuttā ceva no ca kilesā? Ṭhapetvā kilese avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā kilesasampayuttā ceva no ca kilesā. Kilesavippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – kilesā ceva kilesasampayuttā cātipi, kilesasampayuttā ceva no ca kilesātipi. ^1-1578

1579. Katame dhammā kilesavippayuttā saṃkilesikā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā kilesavippayuttā saṃkilesikā. ^1-1579

1580. Katame dhammā kilesavippayuttā asaṃkilesikā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā , cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā kilesavippayuttā asaṃkilesikā. Kilesasampayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – kilesavippayuttā saṃkilesikātipi, kilesavippayuttā asaṃkilesikātipi. ^1-1580

##### Piṭṭhidukaṃ ^1-4-2-13-0

Cattāro diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatacittuppādā, dve domanassasahagatacittuppādā – ime dhammā siyā dassanena pahātabbā, siyā na dassanena pahātabbā. ^1-1581

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yattha dve tayo kilesā ekato uppajjantīti dasadhā kilesānaṃ ekato uppatti hoti. Lobho chadhā, paṭigho dvidhā, moho dvidhā ekato uppajjati. Lobho tāva asaṅkhārike diṭṭhivippayutte mohauddhaccaahirikānottappehi pe diṭṭhisampayutte sasaṅkhārike mohadiṭṭhithinauddhaccaahirikānottappehi saddhinti chadhā ekato uppajjati. Sesaṃ sabbattha uttānatthamevāti.

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhidukaatthuddhāraniddese ānantarikāni kammāni, niyatamicchādiṭṭhi ca sammattaniyamatāya paccaṭṭhitā. Atthuddhārakaṇḍo ca tepiṭakassa buddhavacanassa atthuddhārabhūto yāva saraṇadukā nikkhittaṃ, sakale abhidhammapiṭake nayamaggaṃ mahāpakaraṇe pañhuddhāraṃ gaṇanacāraṃ asallakkhentenāpi aṭṭhakathākaṇḍatoyeva samānetuṃ vaṭṭatīti. Aṭṭhasāliniyā dhammasaṅgahaaṭṭhakathāya aṭṭhakathākaṇḍavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitāti.

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesaatthuddhāre dasa kilesā ekato uppattivasena uddhaṭā — lobho chadhā, paṭigho dvidhā, moho ca dvidhā ekato uppajjati. Lobho tāva asaṅkhārike diṭṭhivippayutte mohauddhaccaahirikānottappehi saddhiṃ…pe… uppajjati. Ete kilesā cittaṃ santāpenti kilissanti ca. Tasmā yoginā "imasmiṃ cittuppāde ime kilesā ekato uppajjantī"ti kilesajālaṃ pariññāya, lobhadosamohe vipassanāmaggañāṇehi khepetvā, nikkilesaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhidukaatthuddhāre ānantarikāni kammāni niyatamicchādiṭṭhi ca sammattaniyāmapaṭibaddhabhāvena, saraṇadukāvasānā ca dukā uddhaṭā. Iti ayaṃ atthuddhārakaṇḍo sakalassa tepiṭakabuddhavacanassa atthaṃ uddharitvā ṭhito. Tasmā yoginā niyatamicchādiṭṭhiṃ ānantarikakammañca sabbaso vajjetvā, sammattaniyāmaṃ ariyamaggaṃ okkamanto, buddhadhammasaṅghesu saraṇagamanaṃ patiṭṭhāpetvā, cittaṃ accantaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Which dhammas are defiling but not defilements? All unwholesome states except the defilements; wholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate states in the three planes; and all material phenomena. These dhammas are defiling but not defilements. Undefiling dhammas are not to be called defilements and defiling, nor defiling but not defilements. ^1-1574

Which dhammas are defilements and defiled? The defilements themselves are defilements and defiled. ^1-1575

Which dhammas are defiled but not defilements? All unwholesome states except the defilements. These dhammas are defiled but not defilements. Undefiled dhammas are not to be called defilements and defiled, nor defiled but not defilements. ^1-1576

Which dhammas are defilements and associated with defilements? Where two or three defilements arise together. These dhammas are defilements and associated with defilements. ^1-1577

Which dhammas are associated with defilements but not defilements? All unwholesome states except the defilements. These dhammas are associated with defilements but not defilements. Dhammas dissociated from defilements are not to be called defilements and associated with defilements, nor associated with defilements but not defilements. ^1-1578

Which dhammas are dissociated from defilements but defiling? Wholesome, resultant, and functional indeterminate states in the three planes; and all material phenomena. These dhammas are dissociated from defilements but defiling. ^1-1579

Which dhammas are dissociated from defilements and undefiling? The four supramundane paths, the four fruits of recluseship, and Nibbana. These dhammas are dissociated from defilements and undefiling. Dhammas associated with defilements are not to be called dissociated from defilements and defiling, nor dissociated from defilements and undefiling. ^1-1580

##### The Pair of Eradication ^1-4-2-13-0

Which dhammas are to be abandoned by seeing? The four mind-moments associated with wrong view, and the mind-moment accompanied by doubt. These dhammas are to be abandoned by seeing. The four mind-moments accompanied by greed dissociated from wrong view, and the two mind-moments accompanied by displeasure. These dhammas may or may not be abandoned by seeing. ^1-1581

Which dhammas are not to be abandoned by seeing? The mind-moment accompanied by restlessness; wholesome states in the four planes; resultant states in the four planes; functional indeterminate states in the three planes; material phenomena; and Nibbana. These dhammas are not to be abandoned by seeing. ^1-1582

Which phenomena are to be abandoned by development? The mind-state accompanied by restlessness – these phenomena are to be abandoned by development. The four mind-states accompanied by greed disconnected from wrong view, and the two mind-states accompanied by displeasure – these phenomena are sometimes to be abandoned by development, sometimes not to be abandoned by development. ^1-1583

Which phenomena are not to be abandoned by development? The four mind-states accompanied by wrong view, the mind-state accompanied by doubt, wholesome consciousness in the four planes, resultant consciousness in the four planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, materiality, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not to be abandoned by development. ^1-1584

Which phenomena have causes to be abandoned by insight? The four mind-states accompanied by wrong view, the mind-state accompanied by doubt, excluding the delusion arising in them – these phenomena have causes to be abandoned by insight. The four mind-states accompanied by greed disconnected from wrong view, and the two mind-states accompanied by displeasure – these phenomena are sometimes having causes to be abandoned by insight, sometimes not having causes to be abandoned by insight. ^1-1585

Which phenomena do not have causes to be abandoned by insight? The delusion accompanied by doubt, the mind-state accompanied by restlessness, wholesome consciousness in the four planes, resultant consciousness in the four planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, materiality, and Nibbāna – these phenomena do not have causes to be abandoned by insight. ^1-1586

Which phenomena have causes to be abandoned by development? The mind-state accompanied by restlessness, excluding the delusion arising in it – these phenomena have causes to be abandoned by development. The four mind-states accompanied by greed disconnected from wrong view, and the two mind-states accompanied by displeasure – these phenomena are sometimes having causes to be abandoned by development, sometimes not having causes to be abandoned by development. ^1-1587

Which phenomena do not have causes to be abandoned by development? The four mind-states accompanied by wrong view, the mind-state accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, wholesome consciousness in the four planes, resultant consciousness in the four planes, functional consciousness in the three planes, materiality, and Nibbāna – these phenomena do not have causes to be abandoned by development. ^1-1588

Which phenomena are accompanied by initial application? Wholesome sense-sphere consciousness, unwholesome consciousness, eleven resultant consciousnesses of wholesome sense-sphere kamma, two resultant consciousnesses of unwholesome kamma, eleven functional consciousnesses, the first fine-material jhāna (wholesome, resultant, and functional), and the first supramundane jhāna (wholesome and resultant), excluding the initial application itself arising in them – these phenomena are accompanied by initial application. ^1-1589

Which phenomena are not accompanied by initial application? The ten five-sense consciousnesses, the third and fourth fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional), the four immaterial-sphere consciousnesses (wholesome, resultant, and functional), the third and fourth supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant), initial application itself, materiality, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not accompanied by initial application. ^1-1590

Which phenomena are accompanied by sustained application? Wholesome sense-sphere consciousness, unwholesome consciousness, eleven resultant consciousnesses of wholesome sense-sphere kamma, two resultant consciousnesses of unwholesome kamma, eleven functional consciousnesses, the first and second fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional), and the first and second supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant), excluding the sustained application itself arising in them – these phenomena are accompanied by sustained application. ^1-1591

Which phenomena are not accompanied by sustained application? The ten five-sense consciousnesses, the third and fourth fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional), the four immaterial-sphere consciousnesses (wholesome, resultant, and functional), the third and fourth supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant), sustained application itself, materiality, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not accompanied by sustained application. ^1-1592

Which phenomena are accompanied by rapture? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by joy, four unwholesome consciousnesses, five resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, five functional consciousnesses, the second and third fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the second and third supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant types), excluding rapture itself that arises therein – these phenomena are accompanied by rapture. ^1-1593

Which phenomena are not accompanied by rapture? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by equanimity, eight unwholesome consciousnesses, eleven resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, seven resultant unwholesome consciousnesses, six functional consciousnesses, the fourth fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the four immaterial consciousnesses (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the fourth supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant types), rapture itself, all material phenomena, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not accompanied by rapture. ^1-1594

Which phenomena are associated with rapture? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by joy, four unwholesome consciousnesses, five resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, five functional consciousnesses, the second and third fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the second and third supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant types), excluding rapture itself that arises therein – these phenomena are associated with rapture. ^1-1595

Which phenomena are not associated with rapture? Four sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses accompanied by equanimity, eight unwholesome consciousnesses, eleven resultant sense-sphere wholesome consciousnesses, seven resultant unwholesome consciousnesses, six functional consciousnesses, the fourth fine-material jhānas (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the four immaterial consciousnesses (wholesome, resultant, and functional types), the fourth supramundane jhānas (wholesome and resultant types), rapture itself, all material phenomena, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not associated with rapture. ^1-1596
