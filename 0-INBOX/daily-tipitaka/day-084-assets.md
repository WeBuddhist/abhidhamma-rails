## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayopi vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Kevalañhi saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti, dukesu ca sukhasahagatadukādayopi. Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā 'phassasampayutto'ti avatvā 'cittasampayutto'ti vuttaṃ; dukesu cettha hetudukādayopi labbhanti, tikā saññākkhandhasadisā eva. Viññāṇakkhandhaniddese cakkhusamphassajādibhāvaṃ avatvā 'cakkhuviññāṇa'ntiādi vuttaṃ — na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ 'manosamphassaja'nti niddisituṃ; sesaṃ saññākkhandhe vuttasadisameva. Imesaṃ tiṇṇampi khandhānaṃ niddeseyeva vedanākkhandhaniddesato atirekatikadukā laddhā, tesaṃ vasena vārappabhedo veditabbo.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayo vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti; saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā "cittasampayutto"ti vuttaṃ; viññāṇakkhandhaniddese "cakkhuviññāṇa"ntiādi vuttaṃ, na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ manosamphassajanti niddisituṃ. Iti ekasmiṃ nayamhi gahite sesā tenupāyena gahitāva honti. Tasmā yoginā ekasmiṃ khandhe sammasanañāṇaṃ paṭiladdhaṃ sesesupi upanetvā, "sabbe pañca khandhā aniccā dukkhā anattā"ti ekarasena passanto, khandhesu attagāhaṃ pahāya, sabbaso anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.
