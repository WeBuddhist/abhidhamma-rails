## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Ekakamātikādi (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-1|↑↑↑]]

Ekavidhenāti ekappakārena, ekakoṭṭhāsena vā. Ñāṇañca taṃ vatthu ca nānappakārānaṃ sampattīnanti ñāṇavatthu; teneva "yāthāvakavatthuvibhāvanā paññā – evaṃ ekavidhena ñāṇavatthū"ti vuttaṃ. Pañca viññāṇāti cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca. "Na hetu ahetukā"tiādīhi dhammasaṅgahamātikāvasena, "aniccā jarābhibhūtā"tiādīhi amātikāvasenāti duvidhehi aṭṭhasattatiyā padehi ekakamātikā nikkhittā; dukānurūpehi pañcatiṃsāya dukehi dukamātikā. Tikamātikā pana "cintāmayā paññā"tiādīhi catūhi bāhirattikehi, "vipākā paññā"tiādīhi cuddasahi mātikātikehi, vitakkattike pītittike ca niyamitapaññāvasena vuttehīti aṭṭhāsītiyā tikehi nikkhittā. Catukkamātikā "kammassakatañāṇa"ntiādīhi ekavīsatiyā catukkehi, sattakamātikā "sattasattati ñāṇavatthūnī"ti saṅkhepato vuttehi ekādasahi sattakehi nikkhittā.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Ekakamātikādi (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-1|↑↑↑]]

Ekakamātikāyaṃ pañca viññāṇā "aniccā jarābhibhūtā"tiādīhi amātikāpadehi nikkhittā, tikamātikāyaṃ "cintāmayā paññā"tiādayo, catukkamātikāyaṃ "kammassakatañāṇa"ntiādayo. Tasmā yoginā "cakkhuviññāṇādīni mama niccāni sukhāvahānī"ti gāhaṃ pahāya, kammassakatañāṇena pāpaṃ akaronto, cintāmayādipaññaṃ upasampādetvā, pañca viññāṇāni aniccāni jarābhibhūtānīti yāthāvato vibhāventena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

**Format note.** Track-folder copy of the one-shot pass (promoted 2026-09-01). It carries an `![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-N]]` transclusion line above each verse and does **not** print the verse number; the `**v. N.**` label in the day file's §4 is supplied by the composer.

The ground of knowledge by way of four:
(1) Knowledge that action is one's own, knowledge in conformity with the truths, the knowledge of one endowed with the path, the knowledge of one endowed with the fruit
(2) Knowledge of suffering, knowledge of the origin of suffering, knowledge of the cessation of suffering, knowledge of the way leading to the cessation of suffering
(3) Wisdom of the sense sphere, wisdom of the fine-material sphere, wisdom of the immaterial sphere, wisdom not included in any sphere
(4) Knowledge of the teaching, inferential knowledge, knowledge of encompassing others' minds, conventional knowledge
(5) There is wisdom for accumulation and not for dismantling; there is wisdom for dismantling and not for accumulation; there is wisdom both for accumulation and for dismantling; there is wisdom neither for accumulation nor for dismantling
(6) There is wisdom for disenchantment and not for penetration; there is wisdom for penetration and not for disenchantment; there is wisdom both for disenchantment and for penetration; there is wisdom neither for disenchantment nor for penetration
(7) Wisdom that shares in decline, wisdom that shares in stability, wisdom that shares in distinction, wisdom that shares in breaking through
(8) The four analytical knowledges
(9) The four modes of practice
(10) The four objects
(11) Knowledge of aging and death, knowledge of the origin of aging and death, knowledge of the cessation of aging and death, knowledge of the way leading to the cessation of aging and death
(12-21) Knowledge of birth… knowledge of existence… knowledge of clinging… knowledge of craving… knowledge of feeling… knowledge of contact… knowledge of the six sense-fields… knowledge of mentality and material form… knowledge of consciousness… knowledge of formations, knowledge of the origin of formations, knowledge of the cessation of formations, knowledge of the way leading to the cessation of formations. Thus is the ground of knowledge by way of four. ^2-754
