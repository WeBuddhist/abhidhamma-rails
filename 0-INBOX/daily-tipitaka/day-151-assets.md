## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Dasakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-10|↑↑↑]]

Dasavidhena ñāṇavatthuniddese aṭṭhānanti hetupaṭikkhepo, anavakāsoti paccayapaṭikkhepo; kāraṇañhi tadāyattavuttitāya attano phalassa ṭhānanti ca avakāsoti ca vuccati. Yaṃ yaṃ hi puthujjano niccaṃ sukhaṃ attāti gaṇhāti, taṃ taṃ ariyasāvako aniccaṃ dukkhaṃ anattāti gaṇhanto gāhaṃ viniveṭheti; mātughātādīni pañca ānantariyakammāni puthujjano karoti, na ariyasāvako; ekissā lokadhātuyā dve sammāsambuddhā apubbaṃ acarimaṃ nuppajjanti anacchariyattā desanāya visesābhāvato vivādabhāvato ca; yathā nimbabījādīni amadhurameva phalaṃ nibbattenti, evaṃ kāyaduccaritādīni amadhurameva vipākaṃ nibbattenti. Dutiyabalaniddese gatiupadhikālapayogasampattīhi paṭibāhitaṃ pāpakammaṃ vipākaṃ adatvā catasso vipattiyo āgamma deti, catūhi vipattīhi paṭibāhitaṃ kalyāṇakammaṃ catasso sampattiyo āgamma deti; idaṃ tathāgatassa kammantaravipākantarajānanañāṇaṃ akampiyaṭṭhena dutiyabalaṃ. Tatiyabalaniddese maggoti vā paṭipadāti vā kammassevetaṃ nāmaṃ; sakalagāmavāsikesu ekato ekaṃ migaṃ jīvitā voropentesupi taṃ kammaṃ āyūhanakkhaṇeyeva nānā hoti, taṃ tathāgato ‘esa niraye nibbattissati, esa tiracchānayoniyaṃ, esa pettivisaye’ti nirayagāminiṃ…pe… nibbānagāminiṃ paṭipadaṃ pajānāti. Catutthabalaniddese khandhāyatanadhātunānattaṃ, anekadhātunānādhātulokanānattañca na kevalaṃ upādinnakasaṅkhāralokassa, anupādinnakasaṅkhāralokassāpi pajānāti; sabbaññubuddhānaṃyeva hi etaṃ balaṃ, na aññesaṃ. Pañcamabalaniddese hīnādhimuttikā hīnādhimuttike, paṇītādhimuttikā paṇītādhimuttike sevanti bhajanti payirupāsanti; taṃ ajjhāsayadhātu niyāmeti. Chaṭṭhabalaniddese sattānaṃ āsayaṃ anusayaṃ caritaṃ adhimuttaṃ, apparajakkhamahārajakkhatikkhindriyamudindriyasvākāradvākārasuviññāpayaduviññāpayabhabbābhabbabhāvañca pajānāti; indriyaparopariyattiñāṇañca āsayānusayañāṇañca ekato hutvā ekaṃ balañāṇaṃ nāma jātaṃ. Sattamabalaniddese cattāro jhāyī, cattāri jhānāni, vimokkhā, savitakkasavicārādayo samādhī, samāpattiyo, hānabhāgiyo visesabhāgiyo ca dhammo, vodānampi vuṭṭhānaṃ, tamhā tamhā samādhimhā vuṭṭhānampi vuṭṭhānanti vibhattaṃ. Aṭṭhamabalaniddese anekavihitaṃ pubbenivāsaṃ, navamabalaniddese dibbena cakkhunāti sabbaṃ visuddhimagge vitthāritameva; dasamabalaniddese cetovimuttinti phalasamādhiṃ, paññāvimuttinti phalañāṇaṃ. Paravādī pana "dasabalañāṇaṃ nāma pāṭiyekkaṃ natthi, sabbaññutañāṇassevāyaṃ pabhedo"ti āha; taṃ na tathā daṭṭhabbaṃ. Dasabalañāṇañhi sakasakakiccameva jānāti – paṭhamaṃ kāraṇākāraṇameva, dutiyaṃ kammantaravipākantarameva, tatiyaṃ kammaparicchedameva, catutthaṃ dhātunānattakaraṇameva, pañcamaṃ sattānaṃ ajjhāsayādhimuttimeva, chaṭṭhaṃ indriyānaṃ tikkhamudubhāvameva, sattamaṃ jhānādīhi saddhiṃ tesaṃ saṃkilesādimeva, aṭṭhamaṃ pubbenivutthakhandhasantatimeva, navamaṃ sattānaṃ cutipaṭisandhimeva, dasamaṃ saccaparicchedameva; sabbaññutañāṇaṃ pana etehi jānitabbañca tato uttaritarañca pajānāti, etesaṃ pana kiccaṃ na sabbaṃ karoti – jhānaṃ hutvā appetuṃ, iddhi hutvā vikubbituṃ, maggo hutvā kilese khepetuṃ na sakkoti. Paṭipāṭiyā satta ñāṇāni kāmāvacarāni, tato dve rūpāvacarāni, avasāne ekaṃ lokuttaraṃ; sabbaññutañāṇaṃ pana savitakkasavicārameva kāmāvacarameva lokiyameva. Iti aññadeva dasabalañāṇaṃ, aññaṃ sabbaññutañāṇaṃ.

### Dasakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-10|↑↑↑]]

Dasakaniddese yathā nimbabījakosātakībījādīni amadhurameva phalaṃ nibbattenti, evaṃ kāyaduccaritādīni amadhurameva vipākaṃ nibbattenti, kāyasucaritādīni madhurameva vuttāni. Catūhi sampattīhi paṭibāhitampi pāpakammaṃ payogavipattiṃ āgamma vipaccati, catūhi vipattīhi paṭibāhitampi kalyāṇakammaṃ payogasampattiṃ āgamma vipaccati; yo pana bahumpi kusalākusalaṃ kammaṃ katvā kalyāṇamittaṃ nissāya arahattaṃ pāpuṇāti, etassa kammavipāko ‘nāhosi’ nāma. Sīlavantānaṃ sīlavantasevanameva, paññavantānaṃ paññavantasevanameva hoti, taṃ ajjhāsayadhātu niyāmeti; heṭṭhimaṃ heṭṭhimañhi paguṇajjhānaṃ uparimassa uparimassa padaṭṭhānaṃ hotīti vodānampi vuṭṭhānaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "idaṃ niccaṃ, idaṃ sukhaṃ, eso me attā"ti gāhaṃ pahāya, pāṇātipātādīni dasa akusalakammāni akaronto, sammāpayoge patiṭṭhāya tīṇi sucaritāni pūretvā kusalaṃ upasampādetvā, paṇītādhimuttike kalyāṇamitte sevanto bhajanto payirupāsanto, tathāgatassa dasabalañāṇe cittaṃ pasādento, sabbasaṅkhāre aniccato dukkhato anattato passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

Here the Tathāgata knows: "There are some evil undertakings of action which, blocked by a fortunate destination, do not ripen. There are some evil undertakings of action which, blocked by a fortunate body, do not ripen. There are some evil undertakings of action which, blocked by fortunate time, do not ripen. The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view therein — this is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the result of past, future, and present undertakings of action, according to circumstance and according to cause. ^2-810

(3) Therein, what is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the way that leads everywhere? Here the Tathāgata knows: "This is the path, this is the way that leads to hell"; he knows: "This is the path, this is the way that leads to the animal womb"; he knows: "This is the path, this is the way that leads to the realm of ghosts"; he knows: "This is the path, this is the way that leads to the human world"; he knows: "This is the path, this is the way that leads to the world of the gods"; he knows: "This is the path, this is the way that leads to Nibbāna." The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view therein — this is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the way that leads everywhere. ^2-811

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-812]]

(4) Therein, what is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the world with its many and different elements? Here the Tathāgata knows the diversity of the aggregates, knows the diversity of the sense-fields, knows the diversity of the elements, knows the diversity of the world with its many and different elements. The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view therein — this is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the world with its many and different elements. ^2-812

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-813]]

(5) Therein, what is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the different inclinations of beings? Here the Tathāgata knows: "There are beings of inferior inclination; there are beings of refined inclination. Beings of inferior inclination resort to, frequent, and attend upon beings of inferior inclination. Beings of refined inclination resort to, frequent, and attend upon beings of refined inclination.
"In the past too, beings of inferior inclination resorted to, frequented, and attended upon beings of inferior inclination; beings of refined inclination resorted to, frequented, and attended upon beings of refined inclination.
"In the future too, beings of inferior inclination will resort to, frequent, and attend upon beings of inferior inclination; beings of refined inclination will resort to, frequent, and attend upon beings of refined inclination." The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view therein — this is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the different inclinations of beings. ^2-813

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-814]]

(6) Therein, what is the Tathāgata's knowledge, as it really is, of the higher and lower state of the faculties of other beings, of other persons? Here the Tathāgata knows the disposition of beings, knows their underlying tendencies, knows their conduct, knows their inclination; he knows beings with little dust in their eyes and much dust in their eyes, with sharp faculties and dull faculties, of good character and bad character, easy to instruct and hard to instruct, capable and incapable. ^2-814

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-815]]

And what is the disposition of beings? "The world is eternal", or "the world is not eternal", or "the world is finite", or "the world is infinite", or "the soul and the body are the same", or "the soul is one thing and the body another", or "a Tathāgata exists after death", or "a Tathāgata does not exist after death", or "a Tathāgata both exists and does not exist after death", or "a Tathāgata neither exists nor does not exist after death" — thus beings rely either on the view of existence or on the view of non-existence. Or, avoiding both these extremes, they have gained the conforming acceptance with regard to things that are dependently arisen through specific conditionality, and the knowledge of them as they really are. This is the disposition of beings. ^2-815
