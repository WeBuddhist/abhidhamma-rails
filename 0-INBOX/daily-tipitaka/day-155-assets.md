##### (60) Hīnassa hīnohamasmīti māno ^2-17-2-35-0

Tattha katamo hīnassa hīnohamasmīti māno? Idhekacco hīno hoti jātiyā vā gottena vā kolaputtiyena vā…pe… aññataraññatarena vatthunā parehi sadisaṃ attānaṃ dahati; so taṃ nissāya omānaṃ jappeti. Yo evarūpo omāno omaññanā omaññitattaṃ hīḷanā ohīḷanā ohīḷitattaṃ attuññā attavaññā attaparibhavo – ayaṃ vuccati "hīnassa hīnohamasmīti māno". ^2-877

##### (61) Māno ^2-17-2-36-0

Tattha katamo māno? Yo māno maññanā maññitattaṃ unnati unnāmo dhajo sampaggāho ketukamyatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "māno". ^2-878

##### (62) Atimāno ^2-17-2-37-0

Tattha katamo atimāno? Idhekacco jātiyā vā gottena vā kolaputtiyena vā…pe… aññataraññatarena vatthunā parehi attānaṃ atimaññati. Yo evarūpo māno maññanā maññitattaṃ unnati unnāmo dhajo sampaggāho ketukamyatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "atimāno". ^2-879

##### (63) Mānātimāno ^2-17-2-38-0

Tattha katamo mānātimāno? Idhekacco jātiyā vā gottena vā kolaputtiyena vā…pe… aññataraññatarena vatthunā pubbakālaṃ parehi sadisaṃ attānaṃ dahati, aparakālaṃ attānaṃ seyyaṃ dahati. Yo evarūpo māno maññanā maññitattaṃ unnati unnāmo dhajo sampaggāho ketukamyatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "mānātimāno". ^2-880

##### (64) Omāno ^2-17-2-39-0

Idhekacco jātiyā vā gottena vā kolaputtiyena vā vaṇṇapokkharatāya vā dhanena vā ajjhenena vā kammāyatanena vā sippāyatanena vā vijjāṭṭhānena vā sutena vā paṭibhānena vā aññataraññatarena vatthunā omānaṃ jappeti. Yo evarūpo omāno omaññanā omaññitattaṃ hīḷanā ohīḷanā ohīḷitattaṃ attuññā attavaññā attaparibhavo – ayaṃ vuccati "omāno". ^2-881

##### (65) Adhimāno ^2-17-2-40-0

##### (66) Asmimāno ^2-17-2-41-0

#### Dukaniddeso ^2-17-3-0

##### (1) Kodho ca upanāho ca ^2-17-3-1-0

(Ka) tattha katamo kodho? Yo kodho kujjhanā kujjhitattaṃ doso dussanā dussitattaṃ byāpatti byāpajjanā byāpajjitattaṃ virodho paṭivirodho caṇḍikkaṃ asuropo anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "kodho".
(Kha) tattha katamo upanāho? Pubbakālaṃ kodho, aparakālaṃ upanāho. Yo evarūpo upanāho upanayhanā upanayhitattaṃ aṭṭhapanā ṭhapanā saṇṭhapanā anusaṃsandanā anuppabandhanā daḷhīkammaṃ kodhassa – ayaṃ vuccati "upanāho". ^2-891

##### (2) Makkho ca paḷāso ca ^2-17-3-2-0

(Ka) tattha katamo makkho? Yo makkho makkhāyanā makkhāyitattaṃ [makkhiyanā makkhiyitattaṃ (sī. ka.)] niṭṭhuriyaṃ niṭṭhuriyakammaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "makkho".
(Kha) tattha katamo paḷāso? Yo paḷāso paḷāsāyanā [palāsāyanā palāsāyitattaṃ (syā.)] paḷāsāhāro vivādaṭṭhānaṃ yugaggāho appaṭinissaggo – ayaṃ vuccati "paḷāso". ^2-892

##### (3) Issā ca macchariyañca ^2-17-3-3-0

(Ka) tattha katamā issā? Yā paralābhasakkāragarukāramānanavandanapūjanāsu issā issāyanā issāyitattaṃ usūyā usūyanā usūyitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "issā".
(Kha) tattha katamaṃ macchariyaṃ? Pañca macchariyāni – āvāsamacchariyaṃ, kulamacchariyaṃ, lābhamacchariyaṃ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṃ, dhammamacchariyaṃ. Yaṃ evarūpaṃ maccheraṃ maccharāyanā maccharāyitattaṃ vevicchaṃ kadariyaṃ kaṭukañcukatā aggahitattaṃ [paggahitattaṃ (sī. ka.) dha. sa. 1127] cittassa – idaṃ vuccati "macchariyaṃ". ^2-893

##### (4) Māyā ca sāṭheyyañca ^2-17-3-4-0

(Ka) tattha katamā māyā? Idhekacco kāyena duccaritaṃ caritvā, vācāya duccaritaṃ caritvā, manasā duccaritaṃ caritvā tassa paṭicchādanahetuṃ pāpikaṃ icchaṃ paṇidahati. "Mā maṃ jaññā"ti icchati. "Mā maṃ jaññā"ti saṅkappeti. "Mā maṃ jaññā"ti vācaṃ bhāsati. "Mā maṃ jaññā"ti kāyena parakkamati. Yā evarūpā māyā māyāvitā accāsarā vañcanā nikati vikiraṇā pariharaṇā gūhanā parigūhanā chādanā paṭicchādanā anuttānīkammaṃ anāvikammaṃ vocchādanā pāpakiriyā – ayaṃ vuccati "māyā".
(Kha) tattha katamaṃ sāṭheyyaṃ? Idhekacco saṭho hoti parisaṭho. Yaṃ tattha saṭhaṃ saṭhatā sāṭheyyaṃ kakkaratā kakkariyaṃ [kakkhaḷatā kakkhaḷiyaṃ (syā.)] parikkhattatā pārikkhattiyaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "sāṭheyyaṃ". ^2-894

##### (5) Avijjā ca bhavataṇhā ca ^2-17-3-5-0

(Ka) tattha katamā avijjā? Yaṃ aññāṇaṃ adassanaṃ…pe… avijjālaṅgī moho akusalamūlaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "avijjā".
(Kha) tattha katamā bhavataṇhā? Yo bhavesu bhavacchando bhavarāgo bhavanandī bhavataṇhā bhavasineho bhavapariḷāho bhavamucchā bhavajjhosānaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "bhavataṇhā". ^2-895

##### (6) Bhavadiṭṭhi ca vibhavadiṭṭhi ca ^2-17-3-6-0

(Ka) tattha katamā bhavadiṭṭhi? "Bhavissati attā ca loko cā"ti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati "bhavadiṭṭhi".
(Kha) tattha katamā vibhavadiṭṭhi? "Na bhavissati attā ca loko cā"ti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati vibhavadiṭṭhi. ^2-896

##### (7) Sassatadiṭṭhi ca ucchedadiṭṭhi ca ^2-17-3-7-0

(Kha) tattha katamā ucchedadiṭṭhi? "Ucchijjissati attā ca loko cā"ti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati "ucchedadiṭṭhi". ^2-897

##### (8) Antavādiṭṭhi ca anantavādiṭṭhi ca ^2-17-3-8-0

(Ka) tattha katamā antavādiṭṭhi? "Antavā attā ca loko cā"ti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati "antavādiṭṭhi".

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Jātiṃ paṭiccāti jātiyā sati taṃ jātiṃ nissāya uppanno majjanākārappavatto māno jātimado nāma, svāyaṃ khattiyādīnaṃ catunnampi vaṇṇānaṃ uppajjati; gottārogyayobbanajīvitalābhasakkārādīsupi ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanavasena uppanno māno tena tena nāmena vutto, evaṃ savatthukaṃ mānaṃ kathetvā avatthukaṃ nibbattitamānaṃ dassetuṃ "tattha katamo mado"ti vuttaṃ. Pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggalakkhaṇo, yathā kakaṇṭako thokaṃ gantvā thokaṃ tiṭṭhati evaṃ dānādikusalakiriyāya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā; thambho cittassa uddhumātabhāvalakkhaṇo, sārambho karaṇuttariyalakkhaṇo, atricchatā sakalābhe asantuṭṭhi paralābhe ca patthanā, mahicchatā pāpicchatā ca asantaguṇasambhāvanatā paṭiggahaṇe ca amattaññutā – tayo hi pūretuṃ na sakkā, aggi upādānena, samuddo udakena, mahiccho paccayehi. Siṅgaṃ kilesasiṅgāratā, tintiṇaṃ kilesavasena khīyanākāro, cāpalyaṃ cīvarapattādimaṇḍanā, asabhāgavutti mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappassa apaccupaṭṭhāpanaṃ; tandīvijambhitābhattasammadehi kilesavasena kāyālasiyaṃ, cetaso līnattena cittassa gilānākāro kathito. Kuhanā paccayapaṭisevanasāmantajappanairiyāpathavasena tividhaṃ vimhāpanaṃ, lapanā nemittikatā nippesikatā lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā ca micchājīvassa ākārā; nippesikatā hi veḷupesikā viya parassa guṇaṃ nippeseti nipuñchati. Seyyasadisahīnamānā jātiādivatthuvaseneva, seyyassa seyyohamasmītiādayo nava mānā puggalaṃ nissāya kathitā, tesu yāthāvamānā arahattamaggavajjhā, ayāthāvamānā sotāpattimaggavajjhā. Adhimāno appatte pattasaññitā, so ariyasāvakassa dussīlassa ca nuppajjati, parisuddhasīlassa pana āraddhavipassakassa dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantassa uppajjati, uccamālaṅkavāsī mahānāgatthero viya. Asmimāno ‘ahaṃ rūpa’ntiādinā uppannamāno, micchāmāno pāpakena kammāyatanādinā; ñātijanapadavitakkā gehasitapemena uppannā, amaravitakko amaro maccho viya ekasmiṃ pakkhe asaṇṭhahanato, parānuddayatālābhasakkārasilokānavaññattipaṭisaṃyuttā ca vitakkā gehasitā eva.

### Dukaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-3|↑↑↑]]

Dukesu pubbakālaṃ kodhaṃ upanayhatīti aparakālakodho upanāho nāma, so upanandhanalakkhaṇo veraṃ appaṭinissajjanaraso, yena samannāgato puggalo ādittapūtialātaṃ viya jalateva. Makkho paraguṇamakkhanalakkhaṇo, gūthapaharaṇakaṃ gūtho viya attano kāraṇaṃ paṭhamataraṃ makkheti, parena sukatānaṃ kiriyānaṃ avacchādanapaccupaṭṭhāno; paḷāso yugaggāhalakkhaṇo paraguṇehi attano guṇānaṃ samakaraṇaraso. Māyā katapaṭicchādanalakkhaṇā, yāya samannāgato puggalo bhasmāpaṭicchanno viya aṅgāro, udakapaṭicchanno viya khāṇu hoti; sāṭheyyaṃ attano avijjamānaguṇappakāsanalakkhaṇaṃ. Anajjavena kāyavacīcittavaṅkatā kathitā, amaddavaṃ amudutāsaṅkhātā ujucittatā, akkhantiādayo khantiādipaṭipakkhato veditabbā. Ajjhattasaṃyojanaṃ kāmabhave bandhanaṃ, bahiddhāsaṃyojanaṃ rūpārūpabhavesu bandhanaṃ, yasmā kāmabhave cutipaṭisandhiyo bahukā tattha ālayopi bahu hoti; tattha ekekaṃ pañcavidhaṃ, tasmā orambhāgiyāni uddhambhāgiyānīti vuttāni.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Ekakaniddese jātigottārogyayobbanādīni nissāya ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanākārappavatto māno mado vutto, pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggo, dānādīsu kusalesu kakaṇṭako viya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā, mahicchapuggalo ca aggi viya samuddo viya paccayehi pūretuṃ na sakkā, kuhanālapanādayo micchājīvassa ākārā, adhimāno ca āraddhavipassakassapi ‘arahā aha’nti uppajjati. Tasmā yoginā jātiādimadaṃ, thambhasārambhaatricchatāmahicchatāpāpicchatādayo, kuhanālapanānemittikatānippesikatālābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatāsaṅkhātaṃ micchājīvañca pahāya, paṭiggahaṇe ca paribhoge ca mattaññutaṃ, dānādīsu kusalesu sātaccakiriyataṃ, mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappaṃ upasampādetvā, dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantopi ‘arahā aha’nti amaññamāno, ñātijanapadādivitakke gehasite uppanne sallakkhetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Dukaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-3|↑↑↑]]

Dukaniddese upanāho veraṃ appaṭinissajjanaraso, makkho parena sukatānaṃ kiriyānaṃ avacchādanapaccupaṭṭhāno, māyā katapaṭicchādanalakkhaṇā, sāṭheyyaṃ avijjamānaguṇappakāsanalakkhaṇaṃ vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā pubbakālakodhaṃ aparāparaṃ anubandhantaṃ upanāhaṃ, paraguṇamakkhanaṃ makkhaṃ, yugaggāhaṃ paḷāsaṃ, katapāpapaṭicchādanaṃ māyaṃ, asantaguṇadīpanaṃ sāṭheyyañca pahāya, veraṃ paṭinissajjanto parena sukatā kiriyā anavacchādento katampi pāpaṃ uttānīkaronto, khantisoraccaajjavamaddavāni upasampādetvā, kāmabhave rūpārūpabhavesu ca ālayaṃ bandhanaṃ sallakkhentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

##### (60) The conceit of one who is worse, "I am worse" ^2-17-2-35-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-877]]

Therein, what is the conceit of one who is worse, "I am worse"? Here someone is worse on account of birth, or clan, or good family… or on one ground or another, and he regards himself as equal to others; relying on that, he nurses self-belittling. Whatever self-belittling of this kind there is, belittling oneself, the state of belittling oneself, the despising, the looking down, the state of looking down on oneself, self-contempt, self-scorn, self-disparagement — this is called "the conceit of one who is worse, 'I am worse'". ^2-877

##### (61) Conceit ^2-17-2-36-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-878]]

Therein, what is conceit? The conceit, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "conceit". ^2-878

##### (62) Exaggerated conceit ^2-17-2-37-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-879]]

Therein, what is exaggerated conceit? Here someone despises others on account of birth, or clan, or good family… or on one ground or another. Whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "exaggerated conceit". ^2-879

##### (63) Conceit beyond conceit ^2-17-2-38-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-880]]

Therein, what is conceit beyond conceit? Here someone, on account of birth, or clan, or good family… or on one ground or another, at an earlier time regards himself as equal to others, and at a later time regards himself as better. Whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "conceit beyond conceit". ^2-880

##### (64) Self-belittling conceit ^2-17-2-39-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-881]]

Therein, what is self-belittling conceit? Here someone nurses self-belittling on account of birth, or clan, or good family, or beauty of complexion, or wealth, or study, or field of work, or field of craft, or branch of science, or learning, or quick understanding, or on one ground or another. Whatever self-belittling of this kind there is, belittling oneself, the state of belittling oneself, the despising, the looking down, the state of looking down on oneself, self-contempt, self-scorn, self-disparagement — this is called "self-belittling conceit". ^2-881

##### (65) Over-estimating conceit ^2-17-2-40-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-882]]

Therein, what is over-estimating conceit? Perceiving what has not been reached as reached, perceiving what has not been done as done, perceiving what has not been attained as attained, perceiving what has not been realised as realised; whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "over-estimating conceit". ^2-882

##### (66) The conceit "I am" ^2-17-2-41-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-883]]

Therein, what is the conceit "I am"? The conceit "I am" with regard to material form, the desire "I am", the underlying tendency "I am"; with regard to feeling… perception… formations… the conceit "I am" with regard to consciousness, the desire "I am", the underlying tendency "I am"; whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "the conceit 'I am'". ^2-883

##### (67) Wrong conceit ^2-17-2-42-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-884]]

Therein, what is wrong conceit? Here someone nurses conceit on account of an evil field of work, or an evil field of craft, or an evil branch of science, or evil learning, or evil quick understanding, or evil virtue, or an evil observance, or evil virtue-and-observance, or an evil view, or on one ground or another; whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "wrong conceit". ^2-884

##### (68) Thoughts about relatives ^2-17-2-43-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-885]]

Therein, what are thoughts about relatives? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with the household life, concerning relatives — this is called "thoughts about relatives". ^2-885

##### (70) Thoughts about not dying ^2-17-2-45-0

##### (71) Thought connected with sympathy for others ^2-17-2-46-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-888]]

The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with the household life therein — this is called "thought connected with sympathy for others". ^2-888

Therein, what is thought connected with not being despised? #### The Exposition of the Twos ^2-17-3-0

##### (1) Anger and resentment ^2-17-3-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-891]]

(a) Therein, what is anger? The anger, being angry, the state of anger; the hate, hating, state of hating; the malice, being malicious, state of being malicious; the opposition, hostility, fierceness, rudeness, the mind's dissatisfaction — this is called "anger".
(b) Therein, what is resentment? First there is anger, afterwards resentment. Whatever resentment of this kind there is, resenting, the state of resentment; the keeping up, maintaining, and fixing of anger, its continuing on, its being carried on, its being made firm — this is called "resentment". ^2-891

##### (2) Contempt and domineering ^2-17-3-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-892]]

(a) Therein, what is contempt? The contempt, being contemptuous, the state of contempt, disdain, an act of disdain — this is called "contempt".
(b) Therein, what is domineering? The domineering, being domineering, the taking up of a position for dispute, holding one's own side, refusing to give up — this is called "domineering". ^2-892

##### (3) Envy and stinginess ^2-17-3-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-893]]

(a) Therein, what is envy? The envy at the gain, honour, respect, esteem, salutation, and veneration of others, the being envious, the state of envy; the jealousy, being jealous, the state of jealousy — this is called "envy".
(b) Therein, what is stinginess? The five kinds of stinginess: stinginess about dwellings, stinginess about families, stinginess about gains, stinginess about praise, stinginess about the teaching. Whatever stinginess of this kind there is, being stingy, the state of stinginess; meanness, miserliness, tight-fistedness, the ungenerous grip of the mind — this is called "stinginess". ^2-893

##### (4) Deceit and treachery ^2-17-3-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-894]]

(a) Therein, what is deceit? Here someone, having done misconduct with the body, having done misconduct with speech, having done misconduct with the mind, forms an evil wish in order to conceal it. He wishes, "May no one know me"; he intends, "May no one know me"; he speaks words, "May no one know me"; he strives with the body, "May no one know me". Whatever deceit of this kind there is, being deceitful, the going beyond bounds, the cheating, the fraud, the distracting, the evading, the hiding, the concealing, the covering up, the covering over, the not laying open, the not making plain, the keeping covered, the evil act — this is called "deceit".
(b) Therein, what is treachery? Here someone is treacherous, thoroughly treacherous. The treacherousness therein, being treacherous, the state of treachery; the roughness, the crookedness, the studied elegance, the state of studied elegance — this is called "treachery". ^2-894

##### (5) Ignorance and craving for existence ^2-17-3-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-895]]

(a) Therein, what is ignorance? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "ignorance".
(b) Therein, what is craving for existence? The desire for existence with regard to states of existence, the lust for existence, the delight in existence, the craving for existence, the affection for existence, the burning for existence, the infatuation with existence, the holding fast to existence — this is called "craving for existence". ^2-895

##### (6) The view of existence and the view of non-existence ^2-17-3-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-896]]

(a) Therein, what is the view of existence? "The self and the world will exist" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the view of existence".
(b) Therein, what is the view of non-existence? "The self and the world will not exist" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called the view of non-existence. ^2-896

##### (7) The eternalist view and the annihilationist view ^2-17-3-7-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-897]]

(a) Therein, what is the eternalist view? "The self and the world are eternal" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the eternalist view".
(b) Therein, what is the annihilationist view? "The self and the world will be cut off" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the annihilationist view". ^2-897

##### (8) The finite view and the infinite view ^2-17-3-8-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-898]]

(a) Therein, what is the view that things are finite? "The self and the world are finite" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the view that things are finite".
(b) Therein, what is the view that things are infinite? "The self and the world are infinite" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the view that things are infinite". ^2-898

##### (9) Speculation about the past and about the future ^2-17-3-9-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-899]]

(a) Therein, what is speculation about the past? The view that arises concerning the past, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "speculation about the past".
(b) Therein, what is speculation about the future? The view that arises concerning the future, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "speculation about the future". ^2-899
