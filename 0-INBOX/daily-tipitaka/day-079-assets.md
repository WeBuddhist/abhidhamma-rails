## Pāli source verses

Tattha katamā saññā bahiddhā? Yā saññā tesaṃ tesaṃ parasattānaṃ parapuggalānaṃ ajjhattaṃ paccattaṃ niyakā pāṭipuggalikā upādinnā, cakkhusamphassajā saññā sotasamphassajā saññā ghānasamphassajā saññā jivhāsamphassajā saññā kāyasamphassajā saññā manosamphassajā saññā – ayaṃ vuccati saññā bahiddhā. ^2-16

17. Tattha katamā saññā oḷārikā sukhumā? Paṭighasamphassajā saññā oḷārikā, adhivacanasamphassajā saññā sukhumā. Akusalā saññā oḷārikā, kusalābyākatā saññā sukhumā. ##### Saṅkhārakkhandho ^2-1-1-4-0

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Saññākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhaniddese 'yā kāci saññā'ti catubhūmikasaññaṃ pariyādiyati; cakkhusamphassajā saññātiādīni atītādivasena niddiṭṭhasaññaṃ sabhāvato dassetuṃ vuttāni. Tattha cakkhusamphassato cakkhusamphassasmiṃ vā jātā cakkhusamphassajā nāma, sesāsupi eseva nayo. Purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva; manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi. Oḷārikadukaniddese 'paṭighasamphassajā'ti sappaṭighe cakkhupasādādayo vatthuṃ katvā sappaṭighe rūpādayo ārabbha uppanno phasso paṭighasamphasso nāma, tato tasmiṃ vā jātā paṭighasamphassajā nāma. Cakkhuviññāṇañhi 'mano' nāma; tena sahajāto phasso manosamphasso, tasmā vā jātāti manosamphassajā.

#### Saṅkhārakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese 'ye keci saṅkhārā'ti catubhūmikasaṅkhāre pariyādiyati. 'Cetanā'ti heṭṭhimakoṭiyā padhānasaṅkhāravasena vuttaṃ — heṭṭhimakoṭiyā hi antamaso cakkhuviññāṇena saddhiṃ pāḷiyaṃ āgatā cattāro saṅkhārā uppajjanti, tesu cetanā padhānā āyūhanaṭṭhena pākaṭattā, tasmā ayameva gahitā; taṃsampayuttasaṅkhārā pana tāya gahitāya gahitāva honti. Idhāpi purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva, manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi; sabbā catubhūmikacetanā. Sesaṃ vedanākkhandhasadisameva.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Saññākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhaniddese cakkhusamphassajādivasena cha saññā vuttā — purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva, manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi. Paṭighasamphassajā nāma sappaṭighe vatthumhi sappaṭighe ārammaṇe ca uppannā. Saññā hi nimittaṃ gaṇhāti; tato ca papañco vaḍḍhati. Tasmā yoginā dvāresu ārammaṇaṃ āpāthagataṃ "saññāmattaṃ ida"nti ñatvā, nimittaggāhena anusārena uppajjamānaṃ kāmasaññaṃ byāpādasaññaṃ vihiṃsāsaññañca pahāya, nekkhammasaññaṃ abyāpādasaññaṃ avihiṃsāsaññañca upasampādetvā, "aniccā saññā"ti anupassanena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Saṅkhārakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese "cetanā"ti heṭṭhimakoṭiyā padhānasaṅkhāravasena vuttaṃ — cetanā hi āyūhanaṭṭhena pākaṭattā padhānā, tāya gahitāya taṃsampayuttasaṅkhārā gahitāva honti. Cetanā ca kammaṃ nāma; tasmā ayaṃ khandho kammassa mūlaṃ dasseti. Tasmā yoginā "cetanāhaṃ, bhikkhave, kammaṃ vadāmī"ti ñatvā, akusalacetanāya kāyavacīmanoduccaritaṃ pahāya, kusalacetanāya dānasīlabhāvanākammaṃ upasampādetvā, sabbasaṅkhāre "aniccā vipariṇāmadhammā"ti passanto saṅkhāresu nibbindanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

Formations associated with painful feeling is far from formations associated with pleasant and neutral feeling; formations associated with pleasant and neutral feeling is far from formations associated with painful feeling; formations associated with pleasant feeling is far from formations associated with painful and neutral feeling; formations associated with painful and neutral feeling is far from formations associated with pleasant feeling; formations associated with neutral feeling is far from formations associated with pleasant-and-painful feeling; formations associated with pleasant-and-painful feeling is far from formations associated with neutral feeling. The formations of one who has not attained absorption is far from the formations of one who has attained absorption, and the formations of one who has attained absorption is far from the formations of one who has not. Formations affected by taints is far from formations unaffected by taints, and formations unaffected by taints is far from formations affected by taints — this is called far formations.

Formations affected by taints is near formations affected by taints, and formations unaffected by taints is near formations unaffected by taints. This is called near formations.
