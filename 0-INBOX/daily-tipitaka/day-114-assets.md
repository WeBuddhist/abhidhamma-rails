## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Kusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni imināva nayena kusalacittādīsupi paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ "katame dhammā kusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Yathā pana akusale paṭhamaṃ mātikaṃ nikkhipitvā pacchā niddeso kato, na tathā idha; kasmā? Appanāvāre nānattasambhavato — lokiyakusalādīsu hi tesaṃ dhammānaṃ dukkhasaccapariyāpannattā "evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassā"ti appanā hoti, lokuttarakusalādīsu pana "evametesaṃ dhammāna"nti. Tasmā ettha sādhāraṇato mātikaṃ ṭhapetuṃ na sakkāti pāṭiyekkaṃ tesaṃ tesaṃ kusalādīnaṃ mātikaṃ uddisitvāva niddeso kato. Tattha yasmā ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjā natthi, tasmā taṃ avatvā, avijjā viya akusalānaṃ kusalānaṃ mūlato kusalamūlaṃ vuttaṃ; taṇhupādānānañca abhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne taṇhā viya ārammaṇe ajjhogāḷho pasādo, upādānaṭṭhāne ca upādānaṃ viya daḷhanipātī adhimokkho vutto. Sesaṃ heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

#### Kusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Kusalaniddese kusalacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjāya abhāvā avijjāṭṭhāne kusalamūlaṃ vuttaṃ; taṇhupādānānaṃ abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhāne ārammaṇe ajjhogāḷho pasādo, upādānaṭṭhāne ca daḷhanipātī adhimokkho vutto. Tasmā yoginā "akusalesuyeva paccayākāro vattatī"ti ekaṃsagāhaṃ pahāya, kusalacittepi paccayapaṭipāṭidassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "yathā avijjā akusalassa mūlaṃ, evaṃ alobhādosāmohā kusalassa mūlānī"ti sallakkhento, tāneva mūlāni vaḍḍhento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

293. Therein, what are the wholesome roots? Non-greed, non-hate, non-delusion.
Therein, what is non-greed? The non-greed, absence of greed, state of being without greed, non-passion, absence of passion, state of being without passion, non-covetousness, the non-greed that is a wholesome root — this is called "non-greed".
Therein, what is non-hate? The non-hate, absence of hating, state of being without hate, non-ill-will, freedom from ill-will, the non-hate that is a wholesome root — this is called "non-hate".
Therein, what is non-delusion? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view — this is called "non-delusion". These are called "the wholesome roots".
Therein, what is the formation that has a wholesome root as condition? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation that has a wholesome root as condition".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition… the mentality that has consciousness as condition… the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition… the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition… the feeling that has contact as condition… this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the confidence that has feeling as condition? The confidence, trusting, reliance, full confidence — this is called "the confidence that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has confidence as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has confidence as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-293

305. Therein, what are the wholesome roots? Non-greed, non-hate, non-delusion.
Therein, what is non-greed… non-hate… non-delusion? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view, the awakening factor of investigation of things, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "non-delusion". These are called "the wholesome roots".
Therein, what is the formation that has a wholesome root as condition? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation that has a wholesome root as condition"… this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental comfort, mental happiness, the comfortable happy experience born of mind-contact, the comfortable happy feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the confidence that has feeling as condition? The confidence, trusting, reliance, full confidence — this is called "the confidence that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has confidence as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has confidence as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… this is called "the aging and death that have birth as condition".
