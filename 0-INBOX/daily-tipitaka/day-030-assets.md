## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Rūpāvacaravipākā

[[#^toc-2-7-6|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacaravipākacittāni kusalānugatikaṃ katvā bhājitāni – yathā hatthiassapabbatādīnaṃ chāyā attano mūlasadisāva honti, tathā rūpāvacaravipākaṃ attano kusalasadisameva hoti, na sadisampi asadisampi. Kāmāvacarakammañca yadā kadāci vipākaṃ deti, rūpāvacaraṃ pana anantarāyena dutiyasmiṃyeva attabhāve vipākaṃ detītipi kusalānugatikaṃ katvā bhājitaṃ. Paṭipadādibhedo hīnapaṇītamajjhimabhāvo ca etesaṃ jhānāgamanato veditabbo. Chandādīnaṃ aññataraṃ dhuraṃ katvā anuppādanīyattā niradhipatikāneva etāni; sesaṃ kusale vuttanayeneva.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1473]]

#### Arūpāvacaravipākā

[[#^toc-2-7-7|↑↑↑]]

Arūpāvacaravipākacittānipi rūpāvacarasadisanayeneva kusalānugatikāni katvā bhājitāni – attano kusalasadisamevāti. Kāmāvacaravipākaṃ viya attano kusalena sadisampi asadisampi na honti; anantarāyena dutiyasmiṃyeva attabhāve vipākaṃ denti. Paṭipadādibhedo ca hīnapaṇītamajjhimabhāvo ca arūpajhānāgamanato veditabbo. Chandādīnaṃ dhuraṃ katvā anuppādanīyattā niradhipatikāneva etāni; sesaṃ sabbaṃ kusale vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1473]]

### Lokuttaravipāka-paṭhamamaggavipākā

[[#^toc-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttaravipākampi kusalasadisattā kusalānugatikameva katvā bhājitaṃ. Tebhūmakakusalaṃ cutipaṭisandhivasena vaṭṭaṃ ācināti vaḍḍhetīti 'katattā upacitattā'ti tattha vuttaṃ; lokuttaraṃ pana tena ācitampi apacināti, sayampi cutipaṭisandhivasena na ācināti – tenettha 'katattā bhāvitattā'ti vuttaṃ. Maggāgamanaṃ dhuraṃ phalassa āgataṭṭhāne; suññataanimittaappaṇihitanāmāni maggānugatikaṃ phalaṃ labhati. Tebhūmakakusalānañhi añño āyūhanakālo añño vipaccanakālo – na sakkā attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ labhāpetuṃ. Lokuttarāni pana tāya paññāya avūpasantāya apaṇṇakaṃ maggānantarameva vipākaṃ paṭilabhanti – tasmā attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ labhāpetuṃ sakkonti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1475]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1477]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1479]]

#### Suddhikapaṭipadā (Lokuttaravipāka)

[[#^toc-2-8-1|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttaravipākaṃ paṭipadābhedena ādito dassetuṃ suddhikapaṭipadā vuttā – kusalasuddhikapaṭipadāya sadiseneva paṭipadāmeva garuṃ katvā pabhedabhājanāya ādito āraddhaṃ. Tattha lokuttaravipāke kusalato viseso – 'katattā upacitattā'ti avatvā 'katattā bhāvitattā'ti vuttaṃ; tebhūmakakusalaṃ cutipaṭisandhivasena vaṭṭaṃ ācināti vaḍḍheti, lokuttaraṃ pana tena ācitampi apacināti sayampi cutipaṭisandhivasena na ācināti. Catubbidhā paṭipadā ca catukkapañcakajhānanayā ca yojitā; vipākacittāni suññataṃ animittaṃ appaṇihitanti tīsu nāmesu vattanti.

## Lokuttaravipāka-paṭhamamaggavipākā

[[#^toc-1-8|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttaravipāke lokuttaramaggakusalakammānaṃ phalaṭṭhena phalacittāni abyākatā. Paṭhamaphalacittaṃ sotāpattimaggassa phalanti. Yathā maggacittaṃ kilesānaṃ samugghāte niyyānikaṃ hoti, tatheva phalacittaṃ maggassa samīpabhāvena maggena pahīnānaṃ kilesānaṃ nuppattabbābhāvaṃ dasseti. Lokuttaravipākāni dassanena yogino punappunaṃ maggabhāvanāya saddhaṃ paggaṇhāti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-13-3-0]]

### Suddhikapaṭipadā — Vipāka

[[#^toc-1-8-1|↑↑↑]]

Suddhikapaṭipadāya lokuttaraphalacittaṃ vibhajiyati. Phalacittaṃ maggacittassa anantaruppannaṃ hoti; evaṃ maggabhāvanāya phalaṃ dassayantassa yogino vipassanāupakkilesānaṃ pahānaṃ paññāpeti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-13-3-0]]

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Full range vv. 499–508 lives in the translation file (block IDs ^1-499 … ^1-508). First and last verses copied here for §4:

**v. 499.** Which states are indeterminate? When one cultivates the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere, and, secluded from sensual pleasures...pe... attains and abides in the first jhāna with earth kasiṇa, at that time, contact...pe... non-distraction...pe... these states are wholesome. As the result of that very fine-material sphere wholesome kamma, having been wrought and accumulated, secluded from sensual pleasures...pe... one attains and abides in the first jhāna with earth kasiṇa, at that time, contact...pe... non-distraction...pe... these states are indeterminate. ^1-499

**v. 508.** But when, as the result of this wholesome jhāna, the corresponding jhāna is attained, with painful practice, slow comprehension, and emptiness... with painful practice, slow comprehension, and signless... with painful practice, slow comprehension, and desireless, then there is contact... non-distraction... these states are indeterminate. ^1-508
