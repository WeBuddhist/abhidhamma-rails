## Pāli source verses

1442. Katame dhammā na hetū? Ṭhapetvā hetū, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā na hetū. ^1-1442

1443. Katame dhammā sahetukā? Vicikicchāsahagataṃ uddhaccasahagataṃ mohaṃ ṭhapetvā avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, kāmāvacarassa vipākato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, kāmāvacarakiriyato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ – ime dhammā sahetukā. ^1-1443

1444. Katame dhammā ahetukā? Vicikicchāsahagato moho, uddhaccasahagato moho, dvepañcaviññāṇāni, tisso ca manodhātuyo, pañca ca ahetukamanoviññāṇadhātuyo, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā ahetukā. ^1-1444

1445. Katame dhammā hetusampayuttā? Vicikicchāsahagataṃ uddhaccasahagataṃ mohaṃ ṭhapetvā avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, kāmāvacarassa vipākato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, kāmāvacarakiriyato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ – ime dhammā hetusampayuttā. ^1-1445

1446. Katame dhammā hetuvippayuttā? Vicikicchāsahagato moho, uddhaccasahagato moho, dvepañcaviññāṇāni tisso ca manodhātuyo pañca ca ahetukamanoviññāṇadhātuyo, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā hetuvippayuttā. ^1-1446

1447. Katame dhammā hetū ceva sahetukā ca? Yattha dve tayo hetū ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā hetū ceva sahetukā ca. ^1-1447

1448. Katame dhammā sahetukā ceva na ca hetū? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, kāmāvacarassa vipākato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, kāmāvacarakiriyato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, etthuppanne hetū ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā sahetukā ceva na ca hetū. Ahetukā dhammā na vattabbā – hetū ceva sahetukā cātipi, sahetukā ceva na ca hetūtipi. ^1-1448

1449. Katame dhammā hetū ceva hetusampayuttā ca? Yattha dve tayo hetū ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā hetū ceva hetusampayuttā ca. ^1-1449

1450. Katame dhammā hetusampayuttā ceva na ca hetū? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, kāmāvacarassa vipākato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, kāmāvacarakiriyato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, etthuppanne hetū ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā hetusampayuttā ceva na ca hetū. Hetuvippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – hetū ceva hetusampayuttā cātipi, hetusampayuttā ceva na ca hetūtipi. ^1-1450

1451. Katame dhammā na hetū sahetukā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, kāmāvacarassa vipākato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, kāmāvacarakiriyato ahetuke cittuppāde ṭhapetvā tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, etthuppanne hetū ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā na hetū sahetukā. ^1-1451

1452. Katame dhammā na hetū ahetukā? Dvepañcaviññāṇāni, tisso ca manodhātuyo, pañca ca ahetukamanoviññāṇadhātuyo, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā na hetū ahetukā . Hetū dhammā na vattabbā – na hetū sahetukātipi, na hetū ahetukātipi. ^1-1452

##### Cūḷantaradukaṃ ^1-4-2-2-0

1453. Katame dhammā sappaccayā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā sappaccayā. ^1-1453

1454. Katame dhammā appaccayā? Nibbānaṃ – ime dhammā appaccayā. ^1-1454

1455. Katame dhammā saṅkhatā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā saṅkhatā. ^1-1455

1456. Katame dhammā asaṅkhatā? Nibbānaṃ – ime dhammā asaṅkhatā. ^1-1456

1457. Katame dhammā sanidassanā? Rūpāyatanaṃ – ime dhammā sanidassanā. ^1-1457

1458. Katame dhammā anidassanā? Cakkhāyatanaṃ …pe… phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, yañca rūpaṃ anidassanaṃ appaṭighaṃ dhammāyatanapariyāpannaṃ, nibbānañca – ime dhammā anidassanā. ^1-1458

1459. Katame dhammā sappaṭighā? Cakkhāyatanaṃ…pe… phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṃ – ime dhammā sappaṭighā. ^1-1459

1460. Katame dhammā appaṭighā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, yañca rūpaṃ anidassanaṃ appaṭighaṃ dhammāyatanapariyāpannaṃ, nibbānañca – ime dhammā appaṭighā. ^1-1460

1461. Katame dhammā rūpino? Cattāro ca mahābhūtā, catunnañca mahābhūtānaṃ upādāya rūpaṃ – ime dhammā rūpino. ^1-1461

1462. Katame dhammā arūpino? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, nibbānañca – ime dhammā arūpino. ^1-1462

1463. Katame dhammā lokiyā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā lokiyā. ^1-1463

1464. Katame dhammā lokuttarā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā lokuttarā. Sabbe [sabbeva (syā.)] dhammā kenaci viññeyyā, kenaci na viññeyyā. ^1-1464

##### Āsavagocchakaṃ ^1-4-2-3-0

1465. Katame dhammā āsavā? Cattāro āsavā – kāmāsavo, bhavāsavo, diṭṭhāsavo, avijjāsavo. Kāmāsavo aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati bhavāsavo catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati diṭṭhāsavo catūsu diṭṭhigatasampayuttesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Avijjāsavo sabbākusalesu uppajjati – ime dhammā āsavā. ^1-1465

1466. Katame dhammā no āsavā? Ṭhapetvā āsave avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ , catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no āsavā. ^1-1466

1467. Katame dhammā sāsavā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā sāsavā. ^1-1467

1468. Katame dhammā anāsavā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā anāsavā. ^1-1468

1469. Katame dhammā āsavasampayuttā? Dve domanassasahagatacittuppādā etthuppannaṃ mohaṃ ṭhapetvā, vicikicchāsahagataṃ uddhaccasahagataṃ mohaṃ ṭhapetvā, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā āsavasampayuttā. ^1-1469

1470. Katame dhammā āsavavippayuttā? Dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppanno moho, vicikicchāsahagato moho, uddhaccasahagato moho, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā āsavavippayuttā. ^1-1470

1471. Katame dhammā āsavā ceva sāsavā ca? Teva āsavā āsavā ceva sāsavā ca. ^1-1471

1472. Katame dhammā sāsavā ceva no ca āsavā? Ṭhapetvā āsave, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā sāsavā ceva no ca āsavā. Anāsavā dhammā na vattabbā – āsavā ceva sāsavā cātipi, sāsavā ceva no ca āsavātipi. ^1-1472

1473. Katame dhammā āsavā ceva āsavasampayuttā ca? Yattha dve tayo āsavā ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā āsavā ceva āsavasampayuttā ca. ^1-1473

1474. Katame dhammā āsavasampayuttā ceva no ca āsavā? Ṭhapetvā āsave, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā āsavasampayuttā ceva no ca āsavā. Āsavavippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – āsavā ceva āsavasampayuttā cātipi, āsavasampayuttā ceva no ca āsavātipi. ^1-1474

1475. Katame dhammā āsavavippayuttā sāsavā? Dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppanno moho, vicikicchāsahagato moho, uddhaccasahagato moho, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā āsavavippayuttā sāsavā. ^1-1475

1476. Katame dhammā āsavavippayuttā anāsavā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā āsavavippayuttā anāsavā. Āsavasampayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – āsavavippayuttā sāsavātipi, āsavavippayuttā anāsavātipi. ^1-1476

[[#^toc-5-2-1|↑↑↑]]

#### Cūḷantaradukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Cūḷantaradukaatthuddhāraniddese saṅkhatadukaniddese asaṅkhatadhātuṃ sandhāya yo eva so dhammoti ekavacananiddeso kato. Kenaci viññeyyadukaniddese cakkhuviññeyyāti cakkhuviññāṇena vijānitabbā, sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Manoviññāṇena kenaci viññeyyā ceva aviññeyyā cāti ayamattho atthi – vavatthānābhāvato pana ettha dukā na vuttāti.

#### Āsavagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Āsavagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yattha dve tayo āsavā ekato uppajjantīti tividhena āsavānaṃ ekato uppatti veditabbā. Catūsu diṭṭhivippayuttesu avijjāsavena, diṭṭhisampayuttesu diṭṭhāsavaavijjāsavehi saddhinti kāmāsavo duvidhena ekato uppajjati. Bhavāsavo catūsu diṭṭhivippayuttesu avijjāsavena saddhiṃ ekadhāva ekato uppajjati. Iminā nayena tikadukānaṃ atthuddhāraṃ veditabbaṃ.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

#### Hetugocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Hetuatthuddhāre tayo kusalahetū (alobho adoso amoho) tayo akusalahetū (lobho doso moho) ca uppattiṭṭhānato uddhaṭā — "catūsu bhūmīsu kusalesu uppajjantī"tiādinā. Yathā mūle sati rukkho patiṭṭhāti, mūle chinne patati, evaṃ hetumhi sati phalaṃ pavattati. Tasmā yoginā "kiṃmūlako ayaṃ dhammo"ti hetuṃ pariggahetvā, akusalahetumūlaṃ "idha uppajjatī"ti ñatvā paññāya samucchinditvā, kusalahetumūlaṃ tesu tesu ṭhānesu vaḍḍhetvā, cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Cūḷantaradukaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Cūḷantaradukaatthuddhāre sappaccaya-saṅkhatādayo dukā uddhaṭā — yaṃ sappaccayaṃ taṃ saṅkhataṃ, tañca aniccaṃ; ekāva asaṅkhatā dhātu (nibbānaṃ) appaccayā accutā santā. Kenaci viññeyyā dhammā cakkhuviññāṇādīhi vijānitabbā, sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Tasmā yoginā sabbaṃ saṅkhataṃ "aniccaṃ dukkhaṃ anattā"ti vipassitvā, saṅkhatesu nibbindanto, asaṅkhataṃ santipadaṃ ninnacittena adhimuccitvā, cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Āsavagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Āsavaatthuddhāre cattāro āsavā (kāmāsavo bhavāsavo diṭṭhāsavo avijjāsavo) ekato uppattivasena uddhaṭā — "yattha dve tayo āsavā ekato uppajjantī"ti. Catūsu diṭṭhivippayuttesu kāmāsavo avijjāsavena saha, diṭṭhisampayuttesu diṭṭhāsavaavijjāsavehi saha uppajjati; bhavāsavo avijjāsavena saddhiṃ ekato uppajjati. Tasmā yoginā "imasmiṃ cittuppāde ime āsavā ekato uppajjantī"ti ñatvā, kāmesu bhavesu ca chandarāgaṃ, micchādiṭṭhiṃ, avijjañca satisampajaññena maggabhāvanāya khepetvā, anāsavaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ; khīṇāsavo hi arahā vuccati.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

What are the phenomena that are not roots? Excluding the roots, wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, functional in the three planes, and matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not roots. ^1-1442

What are the phenomena that are associated with roots? The remaining unwholesome states, excluding the delusion accompanied by doubt and the delusion accompanied by restlessness; wholesome in the four planes; resultant in the four planes, excluding the rootless consciousnesses of the sensuous resultant; functional in the three planes, excluding the rootless consciousnesses of the sensuous functional – these phenomena are associated with roots. ^1-1443

What are the phenomena that are rootless? The delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the two five-fold consciousnesses, the three mind-elements, the five rootless mind-consciousness-elements, and matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are rootless. ^1-1444

Which phenomena are associated with roots? Excluding delusion accompanied by doubt and delusion accompanied by restlessness, the remaining unwholesome; wholesome in the four planes; resultant in the four planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere; functional indeterminate in the three planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere – these phenomena are associated with roots. ^1-1445

Which phenomena are dissociated from roots? Delusion accompanied by doubt, delusion accompanied by restlessness, the two sets of fivefold sense consciousness, the three mind-elements, the five rootless mind-consciousness elements, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are dissociated from roots. ^1-1446

Which phenomena are roots and are rooted? Where two or three roots arise together – these phenomena are roots and are rooted. ^1-1447

Which phenomena are rooted but are not roots? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere; functional indeterminate in the three planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere, excluding the roots that arise therein – these phenomena are rooted but are not roots. Rootless phenomena should not be called "roots and rooted" nor "rooted but not roots." ^1-1448

Which phenomena are roots and are associated with roots? Where two or three roots arise together – these phenomena are roots and are associated with roots. ^1-1449

Which phenomena are associated with roots but are not roots? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere; functional indeterminate in the three planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere, excluding the roots that arise therein – these phenomena are associated with roots but are not roots. Phenomena dissociated from roots should not be called "roots and associated with roots" nor "associated with roots but not roots." ^1-1450

Which phenomena are not roots but are rooted? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere; functional indeterminate in the three planes, excluding rootless consciousness of the sense sphere, excluding the roots that arise therein – these phenomena are not roots but are rooted. ^1-1451

Which phenomena are not roots and are rootless? The two sets of fivefold sense consciousness, the three mind-elements, the five rootless mind-consciousness elements, matter, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not roots and are rootless. Roots should not be called "not roots but rooted" nor "not roots and rootless." ^1-1452

##### Minor Intermediate Dyad ^1-4-2-2-0

Which phenomena are with conditions? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, functional indeterminate in the three planes, and all matter – these phenomena are with conditions. ^1-1453

What are the unconditioned phenomena? Nibbana – these are the unconditioned phenomena. ^1-1454

What are the conditioned phenomena? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, functional in the three planes, and all matter – these are the conditioned phenomena. ^1-1455

What are the unconditioned phenomena? Nibbana – these are the unconditioned phenomena. ^1-1456

What are the visible phenomena? The visible object – these are the visible phenomena. ^1-1457

What are the invisible phenomena? The eye-base...pe... the tangible-base, wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, functional in the three planes, and that matter which is invisible, non-impinging, included in the dhamma-base, and Nibbana – these are the invisible phenomena. ^1-1458

What are the impinging phenomena? The eye-base...pe... the tangible-base – these are the impinging phenomena. ^1-1459

What are the non-impinging phenomena? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, functional in the three planes, and that matter which is invisible, non-impinging, included in the dhamma-base, and Nibbana – these are the non-impinging phenomena. ^1-1460

What are the material phenomena? The four great primaries, and derivative matter dependent on the four great primaries – these are the material phenomena. ^1-1461

What are the immaterial phenomena? Wholesome in the four planes, unwholesome, resultant in the four planes, functional in the three planes, and Nibbana – these are the immaterial phenomena. ^1-1462

What are the mundane phenomena? Wholesome in the three planes, unwholesome, resultant in the three planes, functional in the three planes, and all matter – these are the mundane phenomena. ^1-1463

What are the transcendent states? The four supramundane paths, the four fruition states, and Nibbāna 

– these states are transcendent. All states are knowable by some, not knowable by some. ^1-1464

##### The Section on Taints ^1-4-2-3-0

What are the taints? The four taints: taint of sensual desire, taint of existence, taint of views, taint of ignorance. The taint of sensual desire arises in the eight consciousnesses accompanied by greed. The taint of existence arises in the four consciousnesses accompanied by greed dissociated from wrong view. The taint of views arises in the four consciousnesses associated with wrong view. The taint of ignorance arises in all unwholesome states. These states are taints. ^1-1465

What are not taints? Excluding the taints, the remaining unwholesome states, wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna – these states are not taints. ^1-1466

What are tainted states? Wholesome states in the three planes, unwholesome states, resultant states in the three planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, and all matter – these states are tainted. ^1-1467

What are untainted states? The four supramundane paths, the four fruition states, and Nibbāna – these states are untainted. ^1-1468

What are states concomitant with taints? Excluding the delusion arising in the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, excluding the delusion accompanied by doubt and restlessness, the remaining unwholesome states – these states are concomitant with taints. ^1-1469

What are states dissociated from taints? The delusion arising in the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, the delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, wholesome states in the four planes, resultant states in the four planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, matter, and Nibbāna – these states are dissociated from taints. ^1-1470

What are states that are both taints and tainted? Those same taints are both taints and tainted. ^1-1471

What are states that are tainted but not taints? Excluding the taints, the remaining unwholesome states, wholesome states in the three planes, resultant states in the three planes, functional indeterminate states in the three planes, and all matter – these states are tainted but not taints. Untainted states are not to be called “both taints and tainted” nor “tainted but not taints.” ^1-1472

Which phenomena are both cankers and associated with cankers? Where two or three cankers arise together – these phenomena are both cankers and associated with cankers. ^1-1473

Which phenomena are associated with cankers but are not cankers themselves? Excluding the cankers, the remaining unwholesome phenomena – these phenomena are associated with cankers but are not cankers themselves. Phenomena disjoined from cankers are not to be called 'both cankers and associated with cankers' nor 'associated with cankers but not cankers themselves'. ^1-1474

Which phenomena are disjoined from cankers but still have cankers? Ignorance arising in the two consciousnesses accompanied by displeasure, ignorance accompanied by doubt, ignorance accompanied by restlessness, wholesome phenomena in the three planes, resultant phenomena in the three planes, functional phenomena in the three planes, and all material phenomena – these phenomena are disjoined from cankers but still have cankers. ^1-1475

Which phenomena are disjoined from cankers and are without cankers? The four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are disjoined from cankers and are without cankers. Phenomena associated with cankers are not to be called 'disjoined from cankers but having cankers' nor 'disjoined from cankers and without cankers'. ^1-1476
