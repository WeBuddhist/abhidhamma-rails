## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Chandādhipateyyasuddhikasuññatā

[[#^toc-2-8-8|↑↑↑]]

Chandādhipateyyaṃ suññatañca missayitvā lokuttaravipākaṃ dassetuṃ chandādhipateyyasuddhikasuññatā vuttā. Tebhūmakakusalaṃ parittaaggiṭṭhāne uṇhabhāvanibbutakālo viya – kammakkhaṇe aññattha vipākakkhaṇanti nānākkhaṇatāya attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ na labhāpeti; lokuttaraṃ pana mahantaṃ aggikkhandhaṃ viya – tāya paññāya avūpasantāya maggānantarameva phalaṃ uppajjati, tasmā taṃ attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ labhāpeti. Chandādīnaṃ catunnaṃ adhipatiyānaṃ saddhiṃ suññatādiṭṭhānesu yojitā vīsati mahānayā chandādhipateyyādivasena catugguṇā honti; evaṃ catūhi nayasahassehi bhājetvā paṭhamamaggavipāko dasito.

### Chandādhipateyyasuddhikasuññatā

[[#^toc-1-8-8|↑↑↑]]

Chandādhipateyyasuddhikasuññatāya chandaṃ adhipatiṃ kattvā bhāvitaṃ suññatamaggassa phalacittaṃ vibhajiyati. Evaṃ suññatārammaṇassa maggassa chandādhipateyyanayena phalacittaṃ nidasseti.

Which states are indeterminate?
When one cultivates supramundane jhāna, leading to liberation, leading to cessation of accumulation, for the abandonment of wrong views, for the attainment of the first stage of awakening, secluded from sensual pleasures... the first jhāna, entering and abiding with difficult practice, slow direct knowledge, desireless, dominated by desire, at that time, contact... non-distraction... these states are wholesome.
As the resultant of that very supramundane wholesome jhāna having been done and developed, secluded from sensual pleasures... the first jhāna, entering and abiding with difficult practice, slow direct knowledge, desireless, dominated by desire, at that time, contact... non-distraction... these states are indeterminate. ^1-540
