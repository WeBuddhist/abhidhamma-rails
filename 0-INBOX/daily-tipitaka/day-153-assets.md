## Khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-17|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgepi paṭhamaṃ mātikaṃ ṭhapetvā nikkhittapadānukkamena niddeso kato. Jātimadotiādayo ekakā, kodho ca upanāho cātiādayo dukā, akusalamūlādayo tikā, āsavacatukkādayo catukkā, orambhāgiyasaṃyojanādayo pañcakā, vivādamūlādayo chakkā, anusayādayo sattakā, kilesavatthuādayo aṭṭhakā, āghātavatthuādayo navakā, kilesavatthuādayo dasakā, ajjhattikassa upādāya aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritānītiādayo aṭṭhārasakā ca nikkhittāti sabbānipi etāni aṭṭha kilesasatāni nikkhittānīti veditabbāni. Avasāne ca dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni brahmajāle veyyākaraṇe vuttanayeneva pabhedato ca atthato ca veditabbānīti dassetvā vibhaṅgo niṭṭhāpito.

### Ekakamātikādi (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-1|↑↑↑]]

Tatrāyaṃ nikkhepaparicchedo – ādito tāva jātimadotiādayo tesattati ekakā nikkhittā, tato kodho ca upanāho cātiādayo aṭṭhārasa dukā, akusalamūlādayo pañcatiṃsa tikā, āsavacatukkādayo cuddasa catukkā, orambhāgiyasaṃyojanādayo pannarasa pañcakā, vivādamūlādayo cuddasa chakkā, anusayādayo satta sattakā, kilesavatthuādayo aṭṭha aṭṭhakā, āghātavatthuādayo nava navakā, kilesavatthuādayo satta dasakā, ajjhattikassa upādāya aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritānītiādayo cha aṭṭhārasakā. Ayaṃ tāva nikkhepaparicchedo; idāni yathānikkhittāya mātikāya "tattha katamo jātimado"tiādinā nayena niddesavāro āraddho.

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Jātiṃ paṭiccāti jātiyā sati taṃ jātiṃ nissāya uppanno majjanākārappavatto māno jātimado nāma, svāyaṃ khattiyādīnaṃ catunnampi vaṇṇānaṃ uppajjati; gottārogyayobbanajīvitalābhasakkārādīsupi ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanavasena uppanno māno tena tena nāmena vutto, evaṃ savatthukaṃ mānaṃ kathetvā avatthukaṃ nibbattitamānaṃ dassetuṃ "tattha katamo mado"ti vuttaṃ. Pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggalakkhaṇo, yathā kakaṇṭako thokaṃ gantvā thokaṃ tiṭṭhati evaṃ dānādikusalakiriyāya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā; thambho cittassa uddhumātabhāvalakkhaṇo, sārambho karaṇuttariyalakkhaṇo, atricchatā sakalābhe asantuṭṭhi paralābhe ca patthanā, mahicchatā pāpicchatā ca asantaguṇasambhāvanatā paṭiggahaṇe ca amattaññutā – tayo hi pūretuṃ na sakkā, aggi upādānena, samuddo udakena, mahiccho paccayehi. Siṅgaṃ kilesasiṅgāratā, tintiṇaṃ kilesavasena khīyanākāro, cāpalyaṃ cīvarapattādimaṇḍanā, asabhāgavutti mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappassa apaccupaṭṭhāpanaṃ; tandīvijambhitābhattasammadehi kilesavasena kāyālasiyaṃ, cetaso līnattena cittassa gilānākāro kathito. Kuhanā paccayapaṭisevanasāmantajappanairiyāpathavasena tividhaṃ vimhāpanaṃ, lapanā nemittikatā nippesikatā lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā ca micchājīvassa ākārā; nippesikatā hi veḷupesikā viya parassa guṇaṃ nippeseti nipuñchati. Seyyasadisahīnamānā jātiādivatthuvaseneva, seyyassa seyyohamasmītiādayo nava mānā puggalaṃ nissāya kathitā, tesu yāthāvamānā arahattamaggavajjhā, ayāthāvamānā sotāpattimaggavajjhā. Adhimāno appatte pattasaññitā, so ariyasāvakassa dussīlassa ca nuppajjati, parisuddhasīlassa pana āraddhavipassakassa dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantassa uppajjati, uccamālaṅkavāsī mahānāgatthero viya. Asmimāno ‘ahaṃ rūpa’ntiādinā uppannamāno, micchāmāno pāpakena kammāyatanādinā; ñātijanapadavitakkā gehasitapemena uppannā, amaravitakko amaro maccho viya ekasmiṃ pakkhe asaṇṭhahanato, parānuddayatālābhasakkārasilokānavaññattipaṭisaṃyuttā ca vitakkā gehasitā eva.

## Khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-17|↑↑↑]]

Khuddakavatthuvibhaṅge jātimadādayo ekakā yāva taṇhāvicaritāni sabbānipi aṭṭha kilesasatāni nikkhittāni, avasāne ca dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni brahmajāle vuttāni dassitāni. Tasmā yoginā ‘ime kilesā khuddakā appamattakā’ti appamajjitvā, ekakato yāva aṭṭhārasakā nikkhittaṃ ekekaṃ kilesavatthuṃ attano santāne uppajjamānaṃ sallakkhetvā, taṃ taṃ akusalaṃ akaronto tappaṭipakkhaṃ kusalaṃ upasampādetvā, dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni anupādiyantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Ekakamātikādi (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-1|↑↑↑]]

Mātikāyaṃ jātimado gottamado ārogyamadotiādayo tesattati ekakā, kodho ca upanāho cātiādayo aṭṭhārasa dukā, akusalamūlādayo pañcatiṃsa tikā, āsavādayo cuddasa catukkā nikkhittā. Tasmā yoginā ‘ahaṃ kilesehi visuddho’ti mānaṃ pahāya, imaṃ nikkhepaparicchedaṃ anussaranto ekekaṃ kilesaṃ nāmato jānitvā, tassa uppattiyaṃ satiṃ upaṭṭhāpentena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Ekakaniddese jātigottārogyayobbanādīni nissāya ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanākārappavatto māno mado vutto, pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggo, dānādīsu kusalesu kakaṇṭako viya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā, mahicchapuggalo ca aggi viya samuddo viya paccayehi pūretuṃ na sakkā, kuhanālapanādayo micchājīvassa ākārā, adhimāno ca āraddhavipassakassapi ‘arahā aha’nti uppajjati. Tasmā yoginā jātiādimadaṃ, thambhasārambhaatricchatāmahicchatāpāpicchatādayo, kuhanālapanānemittikatānippesikatālābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatāsaṅkhātaṃ micchājīvañca pahāya, paṭiggahaṇe ca paribhoge ca mattaññutaṃ, dānādīsu kusalesu sātaccakiriyataṃ, mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappaṃ upasampādetvā, dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantopi ‘arahā aha’nti amaññamāno, ñātijanapadādivitakke gehasite uppanne sallakkhetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### The Exposition of the Ones ^2-17-2-0

##### (1) Intoxication with birth ^2-17-2-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-843]]

Therein, what is intoxication with birth? On account of birth: the intoxication, being intoxicated, state of intoxication; the conceit, conceiving, state of conceiving; the loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "intoxication with birth". ^2-843

##### (2-27) Intoxication with clan and the rest ^2-17-2-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-844]]

Therein, what is intoxication with clan? On account of clan… on account of health… on account of youth… on account of life… on account of gain… on account of honour… on account of respect… on account of being put first… on account of one's following… on account of wealth… on account of beauty… on account of learning… on account of quick understanding… on account of seniority… on account of being an alms-goer… on account of not being despised… on account of deportment… on account of success… on account of fame… on account of virtue… on account of meditative absorption… on account of craft… on account of height… on account of build… on account of figure… on account of completeness: the intoxication, being intoxicated, state of intoxication; the conceit, conceiving, state of conceiving; the loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "intoxication with completeness". ^2-844

##### (29) Heedlessness ^2-17-2-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-846]]

Therein, what is heedlessness? Letting the mind loose in bodily misconduct, verbal misconduct, mental misconduct, or the five strands of sense pleasure, and repeatedly letting it loose; acting without respect for the development of wholesome things, acting without perseverance, acting inconstantly, sluggish behaviour, putting down desire, putting down the responsibility, non-practice, non-development, non-cultivation, non-resolution, non-application, heedlessness; whatever heedlessness of this kind there is, being heedless, the state of heedlessness — this is called "heedlessness". ^2-846

##### (32) Excessive desire ^2-17-2-7-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-849]]

Therein, what is excessive desire?
