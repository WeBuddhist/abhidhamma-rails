#### Suttantikadukanikkhepaṃ ^1-3-3-0
1329. Tattha katamaṃ anottappaṃ? Yaṃ na ottappati ottappitabbena, na ottappati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – idaṃ vuccati anottappaṃ. ^1-1329
1330. Tattha katamā hirī? Yaṃ hirīyati hiriyitabbena, hirīyati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – ayaṃ vuccati hirī. ^1-1330
1331. Tattha katamaṃ ottappaṃ? Yaṃ ottappati ottappitabbena, ottappati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – idaṃ vuccati ottappaṃ. ^1-1331
1332. Tattha katamā dovacassatā? Sahadhammike vuccamāne dovacassāyaṃ dovacassiyaṃ dovacassatā vippaṭikūlaggāhitā vipaccanīkasātatā anādariyaṃ anādaratā agāravatā appaṭissavatā – ayaṃ vuccati dovacassatā. ^1-1332
1333. Tattha katamā pāpamittatā? Ye te puggalā assaddhā dussīlā appassutā maccharino duppaññā, yā tesaṃ sevanā nisevanā saṃsevanā bhajanā sambhajanā bhatti sambhatti taṃsampavaṅkatā – ayaṃ vuccati pāpamittatā. ^1-1333
1334. Tattha katamā sovacassatā? Sahadhammike vuccamāne sovacassāyaṃ sovacassiyaṃ sovacassatā appaṭikūlaggāhitā avipaccanīkasātatā sagāravatā sādariyaṃ sādaratā sappaṭissavatā – ayaṃ vuccati sovacassatā. ^1-1334
1335. Tattha katamā kalyāṇamittatā? Ye te puggalā saddhā sīlavanto bahussutā cāgavanto paññavanto, yā tesaṃ sevanā nisevanā saṃsevanā bhajanā sambhajanā bhatti sambhatti taṃsampavaṅkatā – ayaṃ vuccati kalyāṇamittatā. ^1-1335
1336. Tattha katamā āpattikusalatā? Pañcapi āpattikkhandhā āpattiyo, sattapi āpattikkhandhā āpattiyo. Yā tāsaṃ āpattīnaṃ āpattikusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati āpattikusalatā. ^1-1336
1337. Tattha katamā āpattivuṭṭhānakusalatā? Yā tāhi āpattīhi vuṭṭhānakusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati āpattivuṭṭhānakusalatā. ^1-1337
1338. Tattha katamā samāpattikusalatā? Atthi savitakkasavicārā samāpatti, atthi avitakkavicāramattā samāpatti, atthi avitakkaavicārā samāpatti. Yā tāsaṃ samāpattīnaṃ samāpattikusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati samāpattikusalatā. ^1-1338
1339. Tattha katamā samāpattivuṭṭhānakusalatā? Yā tāhi samāpattīhi vuṭṭhānakusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati samāpattivuṭṭhānakusalatā. ^1-1339
1340. Tattha katamā dhātukusalatā? Aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo cakkhudhātu rūpadhātu cakkhuviññāṇadhātu, sotadhātu saddadhātu sotaviññāṇadhātu, ghānadhātu gandhadhātu ghānaviññāṇadhātu, jivhādhātu rasadhātu jivhāviññāṇadhātu, kāyadhātu phoṭṭhabbadhātu kāyaviññāṇadhātu, manodhātu dhammadhātu manoviññāṇadhātu. Yā tāsaṃ dhātūnaṃ dhātukusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati dhātukusalatā. ^1-1340
1341. Tattha katamā manasikārakusalatā? Yā tāsaṃ dhātūnaṃ manasikārakusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati manasikārakusalatā. ^1-1341
1342. Tattha katamā āyatanakusalatā? Dvādasāyatanāni – cakkhāyatanaṃ, rūpāyatanaṃ, sotāyatanaṃ, saddāyatanaṃ, ghānāyatanaṃ, gandhāyatanaṃ, jivhāyatanaṃ, rasāyatanaṃ, kāyāyatanaṃ, phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṃ, manāyatanaṃ, dhammāyatanaṃ. Yā tesaṃ āyatanānaṃ āyatanakusalatā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati āyatanakusalatā. ^1-1342
1343. Tattha katamā paṭiccasamuppādakusalatā? Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā, saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ, viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ, nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ, saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso, phassapaccayā vedanā, vedanāpaccayā taṇhā, taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ, upādānapaccayā bhavo, bhavapaccayā jāti, jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti; evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotīti. Yā tattha paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati paṭiccasamuppādakusalatā. ^1-1343
1344. Tattha katamā ṭhānakusalatā? Ye ye dhammā yesaṃ yesaṃ dhammānaṃ hetū paccayā uppādāya [upādāya (sī. ka.)] taṃ taṃ ṭhānanti, yā tattha paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati ṭhānakusalatā. ^1-1344
1345. Tattha katamā aṭṭhānakusalatā? Ye ye dhammā yesaṃ yesaṃ dhammānaṃ na hetū na paccayā uppādāya taṃ taṃ aṭṭhānanti, yā tattha paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati aṭṭhānakusalatā. ^1-1345
1346. Tattha katamo ajjavo? Yā ajjavatā ajimhatā avaṅkatā akuṭilatā – ayaṃ vuccati ajjavo. ^1-1346
1347. Tattha katamo maddavo? Yā mudutā maddavatā akakkhaḷatā akathinatā nīcacittatā – ayaṃ vuccati maddavo. ^1-1347
1348. Tattha katamā khanti? Yā khanti khamanatā adhivāsanatā acaṇḍikkaṃ anasuropo attamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati khanti. ^1-1348
1349. Tattha katamaṃ soraccaṃ? Yo kāyiko avītikkamo, vācasiko avītikkamo, kāyikavācasiko avītikkamo – idaṃ vuccati soraccaṃ. Sabbopi sīlasaṃvaro soraccaṃ. ^1-1349
1350. Tattha katamaṃ sākhalyaṃ? Yā sā vācā aṇḍakā kakkasā parakaṭukā parābhisajjanī kodhasāmantā asamādhisaṃvattanikā, tathārūpiṃ vācaṃ pahāya yā sā vācā neḷā kaṇṇasukhā pemanīyā hadayaṅgamā porī bahujanakantā bahujanamanāpā tathārūpiṃ vācaṃ bhāsitā hoti; yā tattha saṇhavācatā sakhilavācatā apharusavācatā – idaṃ vuccati sākhalyaṃ. ^1-1350
1351. Tattha katamo paṭisanthāro? Dve paṭisanthārā – āmisapaṭisanthāro ca dhammapaṭisanthāro ca. Idhekacco paṭisanthārako hoti āmisapaṭisanthārena vā dhammapaṭisanthārena vā – ayaṃ vuccati paṭisanthāro. ^1-1351
1352. Tattha katamā indriyesu aguttadvāratā? Idhekacco cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā nimittaggāhī hoti anubyañjanaggāhī. Yatvādhikaraṇamenaṃ cakkhundriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādomanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssaveyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya na paṭipajjati, na rakkhati cakkhundriyaṃ, cakkhundriye na saṃvaraṃ āpajjati. Sotena saddaṃ sutvā…pe… ghānena gandhaṃ ghāyitvā…pe… jivhāya rasaṃ sāyitvā…pe… kāyena phoṭṭhabbaṃ phusitvā…pe… manasā dhammaṃ viññāya nimittaggāhī hoti anubyañjanaggāhī. Yatvādhikaraṇamenaṃ manindriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādomanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssaveyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya na paṭipajjati, na rakkhati manindriyaṃ, manindriye na saṃvaraṃ āpajjati. Yā imesaṃ channaṃ indriyānaṃ agutti agopanā anārakkho asaṃvaro – ayaṃ vuccati indriyesu aguttadvāratā. ^1-1352

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

### Suttantikadukanikkhepaṃ

[[#^toc-4-3|↑↑↑]]

Suttantikadukanikkhepakathāyaṃ mātikākathāyaṃ atthato vivecitattā yāni nesaṃ niddesapadāni tesampi heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva suviññeyyattā yebhuyyena uttānatthāneva. Tattha vijjūpamaduke cakkhumā puriso meghandhakāre rattiṃ maggaṃ paṭipajji; vijjuyā niccharitvā andhakāraviddhaṃsanakālo viya sotāpattimaggobhāsena uppajjitvā saccacchādakatamassa vinoditakālo; dutiya- tatiyagamanābhinīhāro viya sakadāgāmi- anāgāmimaggattāya vipassanārambho. Vajirassa pana pāsāṇo vā maṇi vā abhejjo nāma natthi – evameva arahattamaggassa avajjhakileso nāma natthi, sabbakilese vinivijjhati vajiraṃ viyāti adhivacanadukādinaṃ niddesapadāni nānābhedehi vibhattāni.

#### Suttantikadukanikkhepaṃ

[[#^toc-3-3|↑↑↑]]

Suttantikadukesu vijjābhāgi-avijjābhāgi-bāla-paṇḍita-kaṇha-sukka-tapanīya-atapanīyādayo dvācattālīsa dukā nikkhittā. Tattha ahirikānottappāni bālabhāvassa mūlāni, kāyaduccaritādīni ca katattā pacchā tapanīyāni; hiri-ottappā pana paṇḍitakarā, kāyasucaritādīni ca akatattā atapanīyāni. Tasmā yoginā ahirikānottappe pahāya hiri-ottappe patiṭṭhāya, kaṇhe tapanīye ca dhamme vajjetvā sukke atapanīye ca dhamme sampādetabbā. Vijjābhāgiyā ca vipassanā bhāvetabbā — yathā meghandhakāre vijju niccharitvā maggaṃ pakāseti, evameva sotāpatti-sakadāgāmi-anāgāmimaggobhāsā saccacchādakatamaṃ vinodenti; yathā ca vajiraṃ sabbaṃ vinivijjhati, evaṃ arahattamaggo asesato sabbakilese samucchindati, tena ca pahīnā kilesā puna na paccudāvattanti. Iti anukkamena vaḍḍhentena cittaṃ accantaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

What is fearlessness of wrongdoing? That which is not afraid of what should be feared, is not afraid of committing evil, unwholesome states – this is called fearlessness of wrongdoing. ^1-1329
What is moral shame? That which is ashamed of what should be ashamed of, is ashamed of committing evil, unwholesome states – this is called moral shame. ^1-1330
What is fear of wrongdoing? The fear of what should be feared, the fear of committing evil unwholesome phenomena – this is called fear of wrongdoing. ^1-1331
What is unruliness? When spoken to by fellow Dhamma practitioners, unruliness, taking advice adversely, habitual antagonism, disrespect, lack of deference, lack of reverence, not heeding – this is called unruliness. ^1-1332
What is bad friendship? The association, frequent association, close companionship, devotion, or inclination towards those individuals who are faithless, immoral, unlearned, stingy, unwise – this is called bad friendship. ^1-1333
What is docility? When spoken to by fellow Dhamma practitioners, docility, not taking advice adversely, not habitually antagonistic, respect, deference, reverence, heeding – this is called docility. ^1-1334
What is good friendship? The association, frequent association, close companionship, devotion, or inclination towards those individuals who are faithful, virtuous, learned, generous, wise – this is called good friendship. ^1-1335
What is skill in offenses? The five categories of offenses, the seven categories of offenses. The wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view concerning those offenses – this is called skill in offenses. ^1-1336
What is skill in rising from offenses? The wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view concerning rising from those offenses – this is called skill in rising from offenses. ^1-1337
What is skill in attainments? There is attainment with initial and sustained application, there is attainment with only sustained application, there is attainment without initial and sustained application. The wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view concerning those attainments – this is called skill in attainments. ^1-1338
What is skill in rising from attainments? The wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view concerning rising from those attainments – this is called skill in rising from attainments. ^1-1339
What is skill in elements? The eighteen elements: eye element, form element, eye-consciousness element; ear element, sound element, ear-consciousness element; nose element, smell element, nose-consciousness element; tongue element, taste element, tongue-consciousness element; body element, tangible element, body-consciousness element; mind element, phenomena element, mind-consciousness element. The wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view concerning those elements – this is called skill in elements. ^1-1340
What is skillfulness in attention?
That wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, which is skillfulness in attending to those elements—this is called skillfulness in attention. ^1-1341
What is skillfulness in sense bases?
The twelve sense bases: eye base, visible form base, ear base, sound base, nose base, smell base, tongue base, taste base, body base, tangible base, mind base, mental object base. That wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, which is skillfulness in these sense bases—this is called skillfulness in sense bases. ^1-1342
What is skillfulness in dependent origination?
Ignorance conditions formations; formations condition consciousness; consciousness conditions mind-and-matter; mind-and-matter conditions the six sense bases; the six sense bases condition contact; contact conditions feeling; feeling conditions craving; craving conditions clinging; clinging conditions existence; existence conditions birth; birth conditions decay-and-death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, displeasure, and despair. Thus arises this entire mass of suffering. That wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, concerning this—this is called skillfulness in dependent origination. ^1-1343
What is skillfulness in what is possible?
That which is the understanding that whatever phenomena are causes and conditions for the arising of whatever other phenomena, that is the possible. That wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, concerning this—this is called skillfulness in what is possible. ^1-1344
What is skillfulness in what is impossible?
That which is the understanding that whatever phenomena are not causes and conditions for the arising of whatever other phenomena, that is the impossible. That wisdom, understanding, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, concerning this—this is called skillfulness in what is impossible. ^1-1345
What is uprightness?
That which is straightforwardness, not being crooked, not being devious, not being dishonest—this is called uprightness. ^1-1346
What is gentleness?
That which is softness, gentleness, not harshness, not rigidity, humble-mindedness—this is called gentleness. ^1-1347
What is patience?
That which is patience, endurance, tolerance, absence of fierceness, absence of resentment, contentment of mind—this is called patience. ^1-1348
What is temperance in conduct?
That which is bodily non-transgression, verbal non-transgression, bodily and verbal non-transgression—this is called temperance in conduct. All moral restraint is temperance in conduct. ^1-1349
What is amiability in speech?
That speech which is harsh, rough, hurtful to others, abusive to others, bordering on anger, not conducive to concentration—having abandoned such speech, that speech which is gentle, pleasing to the ear, affectionate, heartwarming, courteous, agreeable to many, pleasant to many, is spoken; that which is polished speech, friendly speech, gentle speech—this is called amiability in speech. ^1-1350
What is hospitality?
There are two types of hospitality: material hospitality and Dharma hospitality. Here, some person is hospitable with material hospitality or Dharma hospitality—this is called hospitality. ^1-1351
What is unguardedness of the sense doors?
Here, perceiving forms with the eye, sounds with the ear, odors with the nose, flavors with the tongue, tangible objects with the body, or mental objects with the mind, one grasps at their general appearance and details. Whereby, while the respective faculty dwells unguarded, evil unwholesome states of covetousness and displeasure might flow in; one does not practice for its restraint, does not protect that faculty, does not undertake restraint in that faculty. The unguardedness, non-protection, non-guarding, non-restraint of these six faculties—this is called unguardedness of the sense doors. ^1-1352
