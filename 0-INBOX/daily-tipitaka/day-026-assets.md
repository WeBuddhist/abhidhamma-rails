## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

### Dvādasa akusalāni

[[#^toc-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Idāni akusaladhammapadaṃ bhājetvā dassetuṃ katame dhammā akusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Akusalassāyaṃ bhūmibhedo natthi – tasmā ekantaṃ kāmāvacarampi samānaṃ 'kāmāvacara'nti na vuttaṃ. Dvādasasu akusalacittesu diṭṭhigatasampayuttā cattāro, diṭṭhivippayuttā cattāro, paṭighasampayuttā dve, vicikicchāsahagatā ca uddhaccasahagatā cāti dve apaṇṇakā – evaṃ dvādasa akusalacittuppādā. Tesu micchādiṭṭhi ayāthāvaditthilakkhaṇā ayonisomanasikārapadaṭṭhānā; lobho ārammaṇaggahaṇalakkhaṇo; moho aññāṇalakkhaṇo sabbākusalānaṃ mūlaṃ. Assaddhiyacitte pasādo natthi, asatiyacitte sati natthi, andhabālacitte paññā natthi – tasmā saddhāsatipaññā na gahitā. Ṭhapetvā uddhaccasahagataṃ, sesāni ekādaseva kamme āyūhite catūsu apāyesu paṭisandhiṃ ākaḍḍhanti.

### Abyākatavipāko

[[#^toc-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni abyākatapadaṃ bhājetvā dassetuṃ katame dhammā abyākatātiādi āraddhaṃ. Catubbidhaṃ abyākataṃ – vipākaṃ kiriyaṃ rūpaṃ nibbānanti. Tattha kusalavipākaṃ parittaṃ ahetukaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīhi ārabhitvā dassetuṃ kāmāvacarassa kusalassa kammassa katattā upacitattā cakkhuviññāṇaṃ uppajjatītiādi vuttaṃ. Tattha cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhusannissitarūpavijānanalakkhaṇaṃ; manodhātu rūpādivijānanalakkhaṇā sampaṭicchanarasā; manoviññāṇadhātu santīraṇādikiccā chaḷārammaṇavijānanalakkhaṇā hadayavatthupadaṭṭhānā. Tato aṭṭha mahāvipākacittāni dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā abyākatātiādi āraddhaṃ; tesaṃ attho heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva, viseso pana alobho abyākatamūlanti dassetuṃ vuttaṃ. Rūpāvacarārūpāvacaravipākāni kusalasadisattā kusalānugatikaṃ katvā bhājitāni.

#### Kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni

[[#^toc-2-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇānaṃ vatthudvārārammaṇāni nibaddhāneva honti, vatthādisaṅkamanaṃ natthi. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhusannissitarūpavijānanalakkhaṇaṃ, rūpamattārammaṇarasaṃ, rūpābhimukhabhāvapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ; eseva nayo sotaviññāṇādīsupi. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhupasādaṃ vatthuṃ katvā iṭṭhe iṭṭhamajjhatte ca catusamuṭṭhānikarūpārammaṇe dassanakiccaṃ sādhayati; sotaviññāṇādīni sotapasādādīni vatthuṃ katvā savanaghāyanasāyanaphusanakiccāni. Cakkhudvārādīsu catūsu upekkhāvedanā bhājitā, kāyaviññāṇe pana ghaṭṭanādhikyena iṭṭhe sukhasahagataṃ aniṭṭhe dukkhasahagataṃ uppajjati.

## Dvādasa akusalāni

[[#^toc-1-6|↑↑↑]]

Dvādasākusalacittuppāde lobhasahagataṃ diṭṭhigatasampayuttaṃ asaṅkhārikādīni, dosamohasahagataṃ ca dvādasa akusalacittāni saṅgahīyanti. Tesu lobho ārammaṇaggahaṇalakkhaṇo maccheramalassa mūlaṃ; doso āghātajanapajahanalakkhaṇo; moho sabbākusalānaṃ mūlaṃ; ahirikaṃ kāyaduccaritādīhi ajigucchanalakkhaṇaṃ; anottappaṃ kāyaduccaritādīhi asārajjanalakkhaṇaṃ. Imesampi akusalacittānaṃ sabhāvaṃ yathāvato ñatvā, hirīottappānaṃ sukkādhammānaṃ bhāvanāya, lobhassa paṭipakkhena alobhena, dosassa paṭipakkhena adosena, mohassa paṭipakkhena amohenāti tīhi kusalamūlehi akusalacittānaṃ vikkhambhanaṃ katvā kusalacittaṃ bhāveti.

## Abyākatavipāko

[[#^toc-1-7|↑↑↑]]

Abyākatavipākacittuppāde kusalavipākacittāni saṅgahīyanti — ahetukāni pañcaviññāṇādivasena, sahetukāni aṭṭhamahāvipākādivasena. Vipākacittāni na kusalāni na akusalāni — kammaphalānaṃ phalaṭṭhena abyākatā. Imesu vipākacittesu phassavedanānaṃ uppādaṭṭhāne cittassa pāṭikulyamanasikāraṃ vā somanassaupekkhādīnaṃ sabhāvaṃ ñatvā, vipāke saṃkilitthacittassa abyāpajjhabhāvena upekkhāṃ bhāveti — "idaṃ kammavipākaṃ, na me attā" ti dassanaṃ sacittapariyodāpanaṃ hoti.

### Kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni

[[#^toc-1-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca ahetukakusalavipākacittāni saṅgahiyanti. Imehi cakkhuviññāṇādīhi iṭṭhārammaṇaṃ vijāniyati — kusalaṃ kammaphalato uppannaṃ; tasmā iṭṭhārammaṇe uppajjamāne cittassa saṃvegaṃ vā pasādaṃ vā uppādetvā kusalakammānaṃ āsevanāya ussāhī hoti.

**v. 420.** What is the aggregate of formations at that time? Contact, volition, initial application, sustained application, one-pointedness, energy faculty, concentration faculty, life faculty, wrong intention, wrong effort, wrong concentration, power of energy, power of concentration, power of shamelessness, power of fearlessness of wrongdoing, hatred, delusion, ill will, shamelessness, fearlessness of wrongdoing, calm, exertion, non-distraction; or whatever other dependently arisen formless phenomena are present at that time, excluding the aggregate of feeling, excluding the aggregate of perception, excluding the aggregate of consciousness—this is the aggregate of formations at that time... these phenomena are unwholesome. ^1-420

**v. 432.** What is contact at that time? That which at that time is contact, touching, complete touching, the state of having completely touched—this is contact at that time. ^1-432
