##### (33) Mahicchatā ^2-17-2-8-0

Tattha katamā mahicchatā? Itarītaracīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārehi pañcahi vā kāmaguṇehi asantuṭṭhassa bhiyyokamyatā, yā evarūpā icchā icchāgatā mahicchatā rāgo sārāgo cittassa sārāgo – ayaṃ vuccati "mahicchatā". ^2-850

##### (34) Pāpicchatā ^2-17-2-9-0

Tattha katamā pāpicchatā? Idhekacco assaddho samāno "saddhoti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, dussīlo samāno "sīlavāti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, appassuto samāno "bahussutoti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, saṅgaṇikārāmo samāno "pavivittoti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, kusīto samāno "āraddhavīriyoti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, muṭṭhassatī samāno "upaṭṭhitassatīti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, asamāhito samāno "samāhitoti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, duppañño samāno "paññavāti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati, akhīṇāsavo samāno "khīṇāsavoti maṃ jano jānātū"ti icchati – yā evarūpā icchā icchāgatā pāpicchatā rāgo sārāgo cittassa sārāgo – ayaṃ vuccati "pāpicchatā". ^2-851

##### (35) Siṅgaṃ ^2-17-2-10-0

Tattha katamaṃ siṅgaṃ? Yaṃ siṅgaṃ siṅgāratā cāturatā cāturiyaṃ parikkhattatā pārikkhattiyaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "siṅgaṃ". ^2-852

##### (36) Tintiṇaṃ ^2-17-2-11-0

Tattha katamaṃ tintiṇaṃ? Yaṃ tintiṇaṃ tintiṇāyanā tintiṇāyitattaṃ loluppaṃ loluppāyanā loluppāyitattaṃ pucchañjikatā sādhukamyatā – idaṃ vuccati "tintiṇaṃ". ^2-853

##### (37) Cāpalyaṃ ^2-17-2-12-0

Tattha katamaṃ cāpalyaṃ? Cīvaramaṇḍanā pattamaṇḍanā senāsanamaṇḍanā imassa vā pūtikāyassa bāhirānaṃ vā parikkhārānaṃ maṇḍanā vibhūsanā keḷanā parikeḷanā giddhikatā giddhikattaṃ capalatā cāpalyaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "cāpalyaṃ". ^2-854

##### (38) Asabhāgavutti ^2-17-2-13-0

Tattha katamaṃ asabhāgavutti? Mātari vā pitari vā jeṭṭhe vā bhātari vā ācariyesu vā upajjhāye vā buddhe vā sāvakesu vā aññataraññataresu garuṭṭhāniyesu vippaṭikūlaggāhitā vipaccanīkasātatā anādariyaṃ anādariyatā agāravatā appatissavatā – ayaṃ vuccati "asabhāgavutti". ^2-855

##### (39) Arati ^2-17-2-14-0

Tattha katamā arati? Pantesu vā senāsanesu aññataraññataresu vā adhikusalesu dhammesu arati aratitā anabhirati anabhiramaṇā ukkaṇṭhitā paritassitā – ayaṃ vuccati "arati". ^2-856

##### (40) Tandī ^2-17-2-15-0

Tattha katamā tandī? Yā tandī tandiyanā tandimanakatā ālasyaṃ ālasyāyanā ālasyāyitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "tandī". ^2-857

##### (41) Vijambhitā ^2-17-2-16-0

Tattha katamā vijambhitā? Yā kāyassa jambhanā vijambhanā ānamanā vinamanā sannamanā paṇamanā byādhiyakaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "vijambhitā". ^2-858

##### (42) Bhattasammado ^2-17-2-17-0

Tattha katamo bhattasammado? Yā bhuttāvissa bhattamucchā bhattakilamatho bhattapariḷāho kāyaduṭṭhullaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "bhattasammado". ^2-859

##### (43) Cetaso ca līnattaṃ ^2-17-2-18-0

Tattha katamaṃ cetaso ca līnattaṃ? Yā cittassa akalyatā akammaññatā olīyanā sallīyanā līnaṃ līyanā līyitattaṃ thinaṃ thīyanā thīyitattaṃ cittassa – idaṃ vuccati "cetaso ca līnattaṃ". ^2-860

##### (44) Kuhanā ^2-17-2-19-0

Tattha katamā kuhanā? Lābhasakkārasilokasannissitassa pāpicchassa icchāpakatassa paccayapaṭisevanasaṅkhātena [paccayapaṭisedhanasaṅkhātena (sī.)] vā sāmantajappitena vā iriyāpathassa vā aṭhapanā [āṭhapanā (ka.)] ṭhapanā saṇṭhapanā bhākuṭitā bhākuṭiyaṃ kuhanā kuhāyanā kuhitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "kuhanā". ^2-861

##### (45) Lapanā ^2-17-2-20-0

Tattha katamā lapanā? Lābhasakkārasilokasannissitassa pāpicchassa icchāpakatassa yā paresaṃ ālapanā lapanā sallapanā ullapanā samullapanā unnahanā samunnahanā ukkācanā samukkācanā anuppiyabhāṇitā cāṭukamyatā muggasūpyatā pāribhaṭayatā – ayaṃ vuccati "lapanā". ^2-862

##### (46) Nemittikatā ^2-17-2-21-0

Tattha katamā nemittikatā? Lābhasakkārasilokasannissitassa pāpicchassa icchāpakatassa yaṃ paresaṃ nimittaṃ nimittakammaṃ obhāso obhāsakammaṃ sāmantajappā parikathā – ayaṃ vuccati "nemittikatā". ^2-863

##### (47) Nippesikatā ^2-17-2-22-0

Tattha katamā nippesikatā? Lābhasakkārasilokasannissitassa pāpicchassa icchāpakatassa yā paresaṃ akkosanā vambhanā garahaṇā ukkhepanā samukkhepanā khipanā saṅkhipanā pāpanā sampāpanā avaṇṇahārikā parapiṭṭhimaṃsikatā – ayaṃ vuccati "nippesikatā". ^2-864

##### (48) Lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā ^2-17-2-23-0

Tattha katamā lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā? Lābhasakkārasilokasannissito pāpiccho icchāpakato ito laddhaṃ āmisaṃ amutra harati amutra vā laddhaṃ āmisaṃ idha āharati, yā evarūpā āmisassa eṭṭhi gaveṭṭhi pariyeṭṭhi esanā gavesanā pariyesanā – ayaṃ vuccati "lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā". ^2-865

##### (49) Seyyohamasmīti māno ^2-17-2-24-0

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Jātiṃ paṭiccāti jātiyā sati taṃ jātiṃ nissāya uppanno majjanākārappavatto māno jātimado nāma, svāyaṃ khattiyādīnaṃ catunnampi vaṇṇānaṃ uppajjati; gottārogyayobbanajīvitalābhasakkārādīsupi ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanavasena uppanno māno tena tena nāmena vutto, evaṃ savatthukaṃ mānaṃ kathetvā avatthukaṃ nibbattitamānaṃ dassetuṃ "tattha katamo mado"ti vuttaṃ. Pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggalakkhaṇo, yathā kakaṇṭako thokaṃ gantvā thokaṃ tiṭṭhati evaṃ dānādikusalakiriyāya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā; thambho cittassa uddhumātabhāvalakkhaṇo, sārambho karaṇuttariyalakkhaṇo, atricchatā sakalābhe asantuṭṭhi paralābhe ca patthanā, mahicchatā pāpicchatā ca asantaguṇasambhāvanatā paṭiggahaṇe ca amattaññutā – tayo hi pūretuṃ na sakkā, aggi upādānena, samuddo udakena, mahiccho paccayehi. Siṅgaṃ kilesasiṅgāratā, tintiṇaṃ kilesavasena khīyanākāro, cāpalyaṃ cīvarapattādimaṇḍanā, asabhāgavutti mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappassa apaccupaṭṭhāpanaṃ; tandīvijambhitābhattasammadehi kilesavasena kāyālasiyaṃ, cetaso līnattena cittassa gilānākāro kathito. Kuhanā paccayapaṭisevanasāmantajappanairiyāpathavasena tividhaṃ vimhāpanaṃ, lapanā nemittikatā nippesikatā lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā ca micchājīvassa ākārā; nippesikatā hi veḷupesikā viya parassa guṇaṃ nippeseti nipuñchati. Seyyasadisahīnamānā jātiādivatthuvaseneva, seyyassa seyyohamasmītiādayo nava mānā puggalaṃ nissāya kathitā, tesu yāthāvamānā arahattamaggavajjhā, ayāthāvamānā sotāpattimaggavajjhā. Adhimāno appatte pattasaññitā, so ariyasāvakassa dussīlassa ca nuppajjati, parisuddhasīlassa pana āraddhavipassakassa dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantassa uppajjati, uccamālaṅkavāsī mahānāgatthero viya. Asmimāno ‘ahaṃ rūpa’ntiādinā uppannamāno, micchāmāno pāpakena kammāyatanādinā; ñātijanapadavitakkā gehasitapemena uppannā, amaravitakko amaro maccho viya ekasmiṃ pakkhe asaṇṭhahanato, parānuddayatālābhasakkārasilokānavaññattipaṭisaṃyuttā ca vitakkā gehasitā eva.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Ekakaniddese jātigottārogyayobbanādīni nissāya ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanākārappavatto māno mado vutto, pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggo, dānādīsu kusalesu kakaṇṭako viya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā, mahicchapuggalo ca aggi viya samuddo viya paccayehi pūretuṃ na sakkā, kuhanālapanādayo micchājīvassa ākārā, adhimāno ca āraddhavipassakassapi ‘arahā aha’nti uppajjati. Tasmā yoginā jātiādimadaṃ, thambhasārambhaatricchatāmahicchatāpāpicchatādayo, kuhanālapanānemittikatānippesikatālābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatāsaṅkhātaṃ micchājīvañca pahāya, paṭiggahaṇe ca paribhoge ca mattaññutaṃ, dānādīsu kusalesu sātaccakiriyataṃ, mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappaṃ upasampādetvā, dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantopi ‘arahā aha’nti amaññamāno, ñātijanapadādivitakke gehasite uppanne sallakkhetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

##### (33) Great desire ^2-17-2-8-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-850]]

Therein, what is great desire? The wanting of more by one discontented with whatever robes, alms-food, dwelling place, and medicinal supports for the sick he has, or with the five strands of sense pleasure; whatever desire of this kind there is, being desirous, great desire, lust, passion, passion of the mind — this is called "great desire". ^2-850

##### (34) Evil desire ^2-17-2-9-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-851]]

Therein, what is evil desire? Here someone without confidence wishes, "May people know me as one with confidence"; being of bad conduct, he wishes, "May people know me as virtuous"; being of little learning, he wishes, "May people know me as learned"; delighting in company, he wishes, "May people know me as secluded"; being lazy, he wishes, "May people know me as energetic"; being forgetful, he wishes, "May people know me as one of established mindfulness"; being unconcentrated, he wishes, "May people know me as concentrated"; being without wisdom, he wishes, "May people know me as wise"; not having destroyed the mental taints, he wishes, "May people know me as one who has destroyed the mental taints" — whatever desire of this kind there is, being desirous, evil desire, lust, passion, passion of the mind — this is called "evil desire". ^2-851

##### (35) Affectation ^2-17-2-10-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-852]]

Therein, what is affectation? The affectation, being affected, cleverness, artfulness, studied elegance, the state of studied elegance — this is called "affectation". ^2-852

##### (36) Greediness ^2-17-2-11-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-853]]

Therein, what is greediness? The greediness, being greedy, the state of greediness; covetousness, being covetous, the state of covetousness; itching desire, the wish for what is nice — this is called "greediness". ^2-853

##### (37) Frivolity ^2-17-2-12-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-854]]

Therein, what is frivolity? Adorning the robes, adorning the bowl, adorning the dwelling place, adorning and beautifying this putrid body or one's outward requisites; playing, playing about, delighting, the state of delighting, fickleness, frivolity — this is called "frivolity". ^2-854

##### (38) Discordant behaviour ^2-17-2-13-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-855]]

Therein, what is discordant behaviour? Towards mother, or father, or elder brother, or teachers, or preceptor, or the Buddha, or the disciples, or towards any others who stand in the place of respect: contrary grasping, delight in opposition, disrespect, disrespectfulness, lack of reverence, lack of deference — this is called "discordant behaviour". ^2-855

##### (39) Discontent ^2-17-2-14-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-856]]

Therein, what is discontent? Discontent with remote dwelling places or with any of the higher wholesome things; being discontented, dissatisfaction, not delighting, restlessness, anxiety — this is called "discontent". ^2-856

##### (40) Sloth ^2-17-2-15-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-857]]

Therein, what is sloth? The sloth, being slothful, mental sluggishness; laziness, being lazy, the state of laziness — this is called "sloth". ^2-857

##### (41) Stretching oneself ^2-17-2-16-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-858]]

Therein, what is stretching oneself? The stretching of the body, stretching out, bending forward, bending sideways, bending together, bending back, restlessness of body — this is called "stretching oneself". ^2-858

##### (42) Drowsiness after meals ^2-17-2-17-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-859]]

Therein, what is drowsiness after meals? The faintness after food of one who has eaten, the weariness after food, the burning after food, the heaviness of the body — this is called "drowsiness after meals". ^2-859

##### (43) Sluggishness of mind ^2-17-2-18-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-860]]

Therein, what is sluggishness of mind? The mind's lack of readiness, its unworkableness, its sagging, its slumping; sloth, being slothful, the mind's slothfulness; dullness, being dull, the mind's state of dullness — this is called "sluggishness of mind". ^2-860

##### (44) Trickery ^2-17-2-19-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-861]]

Therein, what is trickery? For one relying on gain, honour, and renown, of evil desires, driven by desire: the setting up, arranging, and composing of deportment, the frowning, the frowning look, by what is reckoned as the use of requisites, or by roundabout talk — the trickery, being tricky, the state of trickery — this is called "trickery". ^2-861

##### (45) Wheedling talk ^2-17-2-20-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-862]]

Therein, what is wheedling talk? For one relying on gain, honour, and renown, of evil desires, driven by desire: the addressing of others, the talking, the talking along, the talking up, the talking round, the flattering, the excessive flattering, the coaxing, the excessive coaxing, the constant pleasant talk, the fawning, the half-truthful talk, the fondling of children — this is called "wheedling talk". ^2-862

##### (46) Hinting ^2-17-2-21-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-863]]

Therein, what is hinting? For one relying on gain, honour, and renown, of evil desires, driven by desire: the sign made to others, the making of signs, the suggestion, the making of suggestions, the roundabout talk, the indirect talk — this is called "hinting". ^2-863

##### (47) Belittling ^2-17-2-22-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-864]]

Therein, what is belittling? For one relying on gain, honour, and renown, of evil desires, driven by desire: the abusing of others, the disparaging, the blaming, the deriding, the ridiculing, the mocking, the scoffing, the discrediting, the defaming, the carrying about of criticism, the backbiting — this is called "belittling". ^2-864

##### (48) Chasing gain with gain ^2-17-2-23-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-865]]

Therein, what is chasing gain with gain? One relying on gain, honour, and renown, of evil desires, driven by desire, carries goods received here to there, or brings goods received there to here; whatever seeking, seeking out, and seeking around, searching, searching out, and searching around for goods of this kind there is — this is called "chasing gain with gain". ^2-865

##### (49) The conceit "I am better" ^2-17-2-24-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-866]]

Therein, what is the conceit "I am better"? Here someone nurses conceit on account of birth, or clan, or good family, or beauty of complexion, or wealth, or study, or field of work, or field of craft, or branch of science, or learning, or quick understanding, or on one ground or another; whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "the conceit 'I am better'". ^2-866

##### (51) The conceit "I am worse" ^2-17-2-26-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-868]]

Therein, what is the conceit "I am worse"? Here someone nurses self-belittling on account of birth, or clan, or good family, or beauty of complexion, or wealth, or study, or field of work, or field of craft, or branch of science, or learning, or quick understanding, or on one ground or another; whatever self-belittling of this kind there is, belittling oneself, the state of belittling oneself, the despising, the looking down, the state of looking down on oneself, self-contempt, self-scorn, self-disparagement — this is called "the conceit 'I am worse'". ^2-868

##### (52) The conceit of one who is better, "I am better" ^2-17-2-27-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-869]]

Therein, what is the conceit of one who is better, "I am better"? Here someone is better on account of birth, or clan, or good family, or beauty of complexion, or wealth, or study, or field of work, or field of craft, or branch of science, or learning, or quick understanding, or on one ground or another, and he regards himself as better than others; relying on that, he nurses conceit. Whatever conceit of this kind there is, conceiving, state of conceiving, loftiness, haughtiness, the banner, arrogance, the mind's desire for a flag — this is called "the conceit of one who is better, 'I am better'". ^2-869
