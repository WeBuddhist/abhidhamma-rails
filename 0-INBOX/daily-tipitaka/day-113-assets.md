#### Akusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Akusalaniddese yasmā idha mātikā "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"ti akusaladhammavasena nikkhittā, tasmā nikkhepapaṭipāṭiyāva paṭhamaṃ kusalaṃ anāmasitvā "katame dhammā akusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Yasmā ekacittakkhaṇe taṇhāya ca kāmupādānassa ca sambhavo natthi, tasmā yaṃ ettha taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ labbhati, tadeva dassetuṃ "diṭṭhi diṭṭhigata"ntiādi vuttaṃ. Bhavaniddese ca upādānassa saṅkhārakkhandhe saṅgahitattā "ṭhapetvā upādānaṃ vedanākkhandho saññākkhandho saṅkhārakkhandho viññāṇakkhandho"ti vuttaṃ; itarathā hi upādānasseva upādānapaccayattaṃ āpajjeyya. Jātiādiniddesesu ca arūpadhammānaṃ jātiādibhāvato "khaṇḍiccaṃ pāliccaṃ valittacatā cuti cavanatā"ti na vuttaṃ. Dutiyavāre "ṭhapetvā phassa"nti phassassa nāmato nīharaṇatthaṃ vuttaṃ. Tatiyavāre cittasamuṭṭhānarūpe pavattamāne tenupatthaddhānaṃ cakkhāyatanādīnaṃ upacitattā "cakkhāyatanassa upacayo"tiādi vuttaṃ. Catutthavāre mahābhūtarūpapaccayā cakkhāyatanādīni, hadayarūpapaccayā chaṭṭhāyatanaṃ, nāmapaccayā ca pacchājātasahajātādivasena sabbāni pavattantīti "nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ cakkhāyatana"ntiādi vuttaṃ. Sesākusalacittesu ca diṭṭhivippayuttesu taṇhāpaccayā upādānassa abhāvā upādānaṭṭhāne daḷhanipātinā adhimokkhena padaṃ pūritaṃ; domanassasahagatesu vedanāpaccayā taṇhāya abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhāne balavakilesena paṭighena; vicikicchāsampayutte sanniṭṭhānābhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne vicikicchāya, upādānaṭṭhānaṃ pana parihīnameva; uddhaccasampayutte taṇhāṭṭhāne uddhaccena, upādānaṭṭhāne ca adhimokkheneva. Adhimuccanavasena hi adhimokkho; adhimuccati vā tena ārammaṇe cittaṃ nibbicikicchatāya sanniṭṭhānaṃ gacchatītipi adhimokkho.

#### Kusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni imināva nayena kusalacittādīsupi paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ "katame dhammā kusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Yathā pana akusale paṭhamaṃ mātikaṃ nikkhipitvā pacchā niddeso kato, na tathā idha; kasmā? Appanāvāre nānattasambhavato — lokiyakusalādīsu hi tesaṃ dhammānaṃ dukkhasaccapariyāpannattā "evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassā"ti appanā hoti, lokuttarakusalādīsu pana "evametesaṃ dhammāna"nti. Tasmā ettha sādhāraṇato mātikaṃ ṭhapetuṃ na sakkāti pāṭiyekkaṃ tesaṃ tesaṃ kusalādīnaṃ mātikaṃ uddisitvāva niddeso kato. Tattha yasmā ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjā natthi, tasmā taṃ avatvā, avijjā viya akusalānaṃ kusalānaṃ mūlato kusalamūlaṃ vuttaṃ; taṇhupādānānañca abhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne taṇhā viya ārammaṇe ajjhogāḷho pasādo, upādānaṭṭhāne ca upādānaṃ viya daḷhanipātī adhimokkho vutto. Sesaṃ heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Akusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Akusalaniddese akusalacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Ekacittakkhaṇe taṇhāya ca kāmupādānassa ca asambhavā diṭṭhivasena upādānaṃ dassitaṃ; diṭṭhivippayuttesu upādānaṭṭhāne adhimokkhena, domanassasahagatesu taṇhāṭṭhāne paṭighena, vicikicchāsampayutte vicikicchāya, uddhaccasampayutte uddhaccena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Adhimuccati tena ārammaṇe cittaṃ nibbicikicchatāya sanniṭṭhānaṃ gacchatīti adhimokkho. Tasmā yoginā "akasmā me idaṃ akusalacittaṃ uppannaṃ, ahetuka"nti sammohaṃ pahāya, ekamekasmiṃ akusalacitte avijjāsaṅkhārādīnaṃ paṭipāṭidassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "sahetukaṃ idaṃ paccayasamuppannaṃ, paccaye chinne nirujjhatī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[#^toc-6-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Kusalaniddese kusalacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjāya abhāvā avijjāṭṭhāne kusalamūlaṃ vuttaṃ; taṇhupādānānaṃ abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhāne ārammaṇe ajjhogāḷho pasādo, upādānaṭṭhāne ca daḷhanipātī adhimokkho vutto. ## English translation (from `AI_translation/english/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

281. Therein, what is ignorance? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "ignorance".
Therein, what is the formation that has ignorance as condition? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called the formation that has ignorance as condition….
Therein, what is the decision that has craving as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has craving as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-281

282. Which things are unwholesome? On the occasion when an unwholesome mind has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, associated with a view, with a sight as its object, or a sound as its object, or a smell as its object, or a taste as its object, or a tangible thing as its object, or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with ignorance as condition, a formation; with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, craving; with craving as condition, clinging; with clinging as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-282

283. Therein, what is ignorance? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called ignorance….
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental experience that is neither comfortable nor uncomfortable, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant experience born of mind-contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-283
