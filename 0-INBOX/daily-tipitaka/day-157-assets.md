##### (6) Tayo āsavā ^2-17-4-6-0

Tattha katame tayo āsavā? Kāmāsavo, bhavāsavo, avijjāsavo.
(Ka) tattha katamo kāmāsavo? Yo kāmesu kāmacchando kāmarāgo kāmanandī kāmataṇhā kāmasineho kāmapariḷāho kāmamucchā kāmajjhosānaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "kāmāsavo".
(Kha) tattha katamo bhavāsavo? Yo bhavesu bhavacchando…pe… bhavajjhosānaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "bhavāsavo".
(Ga) tattha katamo avijjāsavo? Dukkhe aññāṇaṃ…pe… avijjālaṅgī moho akusalamūlaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "avijjāsavo". Ime tayo āsavā. ^2-914

##### (7) Tīṇi saṃyojanāni ^2-17-4-7-0

Tattha katamāni tīṇi saṃyojanāni? Sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāso.
(Ka) tattha katamā sakkāyadiṭṭhi? Idha assutavā puthujjano ariyānaṃ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akovido ariyadhamme avinīto, sappurisānaṃ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akovido sappurisadhamme avinīto – rūpaṃ attato samanupassati rūpavantaṃ vā attānaṃ attani vā rūpaṃ rūpasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Vedanaṃ…pe… saññaṃ…pe… saṅkhāre…pe… viññāṇaṃ attato samanupassati viññāṇavantaṃ vā attānaṃ attani vā viññāṇaṃ viññāṇasmiṃ vā attānaṃ. Yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati "sakkāyadiṭṭhi".
(Kha) tattha katamā vicikicchā? (Ga) tattha katamo sīlabbataparāmāso? "Ito bahiddhā samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṃ sīlena suddhi vatena suddhi sīlabbatena suddhī"ti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati "sīlabbataparāmāso". Imāni tīṇi saṃyojanāni. ^2-915

##### (8) Tisso taṇhā ^2-17-4-8-0

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Tikaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-4|↑↑↑]]

Tikaniddese tīhi akusalamūlehi vaṭṭamūlasamudācāro kathito; vitakkanavasena vitakko, sañjānanavasena saññā, sabhāvaṭṭhena dhātu. Esanāsu kāmagavesanarāgo kāmesanā, bhavagavesanarāgo bhavesanā, diṭṭhigatikasammatassa brahmacariyassa gavesanā diṭṭhi brahmacariyesanā, tadekaṭṭhaṃ kammampi esanā eva. Vidhāti seyyādivasena vidahanato māno; tamoti vicikicchāsīsena avijjā, yāya atītaṃ anāgataṃ paccuppannaṃ addhānaṃ ārabbha kaṅkhati. Titthāyatanesu ‘pubbekatahetū’ti nigaṇṭhasamayo, ‘issaranimmānahetū’ti brāhmaṇasamayo, ‘ahetu appaccayā’ti ājīvakasamayo. Rāgādayo satte bandhanato kiñcanā, cittaṃ malinaṃ karaṇato malāni, pakkhalanapātahetuto visamāni, anudahanaṭṭhena aggī – daharabhikkhunī rāgena jhāyitvā kālamakāsi, manopadosikā devā dosena, khiḍḍāpadosikā mohena – kasaṭā nirojāti kasāvā. Assādadiṭṭhi sassatadiṭṭhi, attānudiṭṭhi sakkāyadiṭṭhi, micchādiṭṭhi ucchedadiṭṭhi. Upārambho paravajjānupassanalakkhaṇo, yena samannāgato puggalo tunnakāro sāṭakaṃ pasāretvā chiddameva olokento viya parassa aguṇesuyeva patiṭṭhāti; ayonisomanasikāro anicce niccanti dukkhe sukhanti pavatto uppathamanasikāralakkhaṇo, yassa vasena puggalo saccāni yāthāvato āvajjituṃ na sakkoti.

##### (6) The three mental taints ^2-17-4-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-914]]

Therein, what are the three mental taints? The taint of sense pleasure, the taint of existence, the taint of ignorance.
(a) Therein, what is the taint of sense pleasure? The desire for sense pleasures with regard to sense pleasures, the lust for sense pleasures, the delight in sense pleasures, the craving for sense pleasures, the affection for sense pleasures, the burning for sense pleasures, the infatuation with sense pleasures, the holding fast to sense pleasures — this is called "the taint of sense pleasure".
(b) Therein, what is the taint of existence? The desire for existence with regard to states of existence… the holding fast to existence — this is called "the taint of existence".
(c) Therein, what is the taint of ignorance? Not knowing suffering… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "the taint of ignorance". These are the three mental taints. ^2-914

##### (7) The three fetters ^2-17-4-7-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-915]]

Therein, what are the three fetters? The view of a self in the body, doubt, wrong grasping of virtue and observances.
(a) Therein, what is the view of a self in the body? Here an unlearned ordinary person — who has no regard for the noble ones, is unskilled in the teaching of the noble ones, untrained in the teaching of the noble ones; who has no regard for good people, is unskilled in the teaching of good people, untrained in the teaching of good people — regards material form as self, or self as possessing material form, or material form as in self, or self as in material form. He regards feeling… perception… formations… consciousness as self, or self as possessing consciousness, or consciousness as in self, or self as in consciousness. Whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the view of a self in the body".
(b) Therein, what is doubt? One doubts and is perplexed about the Teacher; one doubts and is perplexed about the teaching; one doubts and is perplexed about the Order; one doubts and is perplexed about the training; one doubts and is perplexed about the past; one doubts and is perplexed about the future; one doubts and is perplexed about the past and the future; one doubts and is perplexed about things that are dependently arisen through specific conditionality. Whatever doubt of this kind there is, doubting, state of doubting, rigidity of the mind, mental confusion — this is called "doubt".
(c) Therein, what is wrong grasping of virtue and observances? "Purity comes through virtue, purity comes through observances, purity comes through virtue-and-observances of ascetics and brahmins outside this teaching" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "wrong grasping of virtue and observances". These are the three fetters. ^2-915

##### (8) The three cravings ^2-17-4-8-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-916]]

Therein, what are the three cravings? Craving for sense pleasures, craving for existence, craving for non-existence.
Therein, what is craving for existence? The lust, passion, passion of the mind accompanied by the view of existence — this is called "craving for existence".
Therein, what is craving for non-existence? The lust, passion, passion of the mind accompanied by the annihilationist view — this is called "craving for non-existence". The remaining craving is craving for sense pleasures.
Therein, what is craving for sense pleasures? The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of sense pleasure — this is called "craving for sense pleasures".
The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of material form and the element of the immaterial — this is called "craving for existence".
The lust, passion, passion of the mind accompanied by the annihilationist view — this is called "craving for non-existence". These are the three cravings. ^2-916

##### (9) Another three cravings ^2-17-4-9-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-917]]

Therein, what are another three cravings? Craving for sense pleasures, craving for material form, craving for the immaterial.
(a) Therein, what is craving for sense pleasures? The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of sense pleasure — this is called "craving for sense pleasures".
(b) Therein, what is craving for material form? The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of material form — this is called "craving for material form".
(c) Therein, what is craving for the immaterial? The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of the immaterial — this is called "craving for the immaterial". These are the three cravings. ^2-917

##### (10) Yet another three cravings ^2-17-4-10-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-918]]

Therein, what are yet another three cravings? Craving for material form, craving for the immaterial, craving for cessation.
(a) Therein, what is craving for material form? The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of material form — this is called "craving for material form".
(b) Therein, what is craving for the immaterial? The lust, passion, passion of the mind connected with the element of the immaterial — this is called "craving for the immaterial".
(c) Therein, what is craving for cessation? The lust, passion, passion of the mind accompanied by the annihilationist view — this is called "craving for cessation". These are the three cravings. ^2-918

##### (11) The three quests ^2-17-4-11-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-919]]

Therein, what are the three quests? The quest for sense pleasures, the quest for existence, the quest for a holy life.
(a) Therein, what is the quest for sense pleasures? The desire for sense pleasures with regard to sense pleasures… the holding fast to sense pleasures — this is called "the quest for sense pleasures".
(b) Therein, what is the quest for existence? The desire for existence with regard to states of existence… the holding fast to existence — this is called "the quest for existence".
(c) Therein, what is the quest for a holy life? "The world is eternal", or "the world is not eternal"… or "a Tathāgata neither exists nor does not exist after death" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "the quest for a holy life".
(a) Therein, what is the quest for sense pleasures? Lust for sense pleasures and the unwholesome bodily action, verbal action, and mental action that stand together with it — this is called "the quest for sense pleasures".
(b) Therein, what is the quest for existence? Lust for existence and the unwholesome bodily action, verbal action, and mental action that stand together with it — this is called "the quest for existence".
(c) Therein, what is the quest for a holy life? The extremist view and the unwholesome bodily action, verbal action, and mental action that stand together with it — this is called "the quest for a holy life". These are the three quests. ^2-919

##### (13) The three fears ^2-17-4-13-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-921]]

Therein, what are the three fears? The fear of birth, the fear of aging, the fear of death.
(a) Therein, what is the fear of birth? On account of birth: the fear, fearfulness, terror, horripilation, alarm of mind — this is called "the fear of birth".
(b) Therein, what is the fear of aging? On account of aging: the fear, fearfulness, terror, horripilation, alarm of mind — this is called "the fear of aging".
(c) Therein, what is the fear of death? On account of death: the fear, fearfulness, terror, horripilation, alarm of mind — this is called "the fear of death". These are the three fears. ^2-921

##### (14) The three darknesses ^2-17-4-14-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-922]]

Therein, what are the three darknesses? Concerning the past one doubts, is perplexed, does not resolve, does not settle in confidence; concerning the future one doubts, is perplexed, does not resolve, does not settle in confidence; concerning the present one doubts, is perplexed, does not resolve, does not settle in confidence — these are the three darknesses. ^2-922

##### (15) The three sectarian grounds ^2-17-4-15-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-923]]

Therein, what are the three sectarian grounds? Here some ascetic or brahmin speaks thus and holds this view: "Whatever this person experiences, whether pleasant or painful or neither painful nor pleasant, all that is caused by what was done in the past." Here some ascetic or brahmin speaks thus and holds this view: "Whatever this person experiences, whether pleasant or painful or neither painful nor pleasant, all that is caused by a supreme being's creation." Here some ascetic or brahmin speaks thus and holds this view: "Whatever this person experiences, whether pleasant or painful or neither painful nor pleasant, all that is without cause and without condition." These are the three sectarian grounds. ^2-923
