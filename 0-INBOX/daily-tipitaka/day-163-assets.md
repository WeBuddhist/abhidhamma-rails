##### (3) Aṭṭhasu lokadhammesu cittassa paṭighāto ^2-17-9-3-0

Tattha katamesu aṭṭhasu lokadhammesu cittassa paṭighāto? Lābhe sārāgo, alābhe paṭivirodho, yase sārāgo, ayase paṭivirodho, pasaṃsāya sārāgo, nindāya paṭivirodho, sukhe sārāgo, dukkhe paṭivirodho – imesu aṭṭhasu lokadhammesu cittassa paṭighāto. ^2-954

##### (4) Aṭṭhaanariyavohārā ^2-17-9-4-0

Tattha katame aṭṭha anariyavohārā? Adiṭṭhe diṭṭhavāditā, assute sutavāditā, amute mutavāditā, aviññāte viññātavāditā, diṭṭhe adiṭṭhavāditā, sute assutavāditā, mute amutavāditā, viññāte aviññātavāditā – ime aṭṭha anariyavohārā. ^2-955

##### (5) Aṭṭha micchattā ^2-17-9-5-0

Tattha katame aṭṭha micchattā? Micchādiṭṭhi, micchāsaṅkappo, micchāvācā, micchākammanto, micchāājīvo, micchāvāyāmo, micchāsati, micchāsamādhi – ime aṭṭha micchattā. ^2-956

##### (6) Aṭṭha purisadosā ^2-17-9-6-0

Tattha katame aṭṭha purisadosā? (Ka) idha bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno [codiyamāno (sī. syā.) a. ni. 8.14] "na sarāmi na sarāmī"ti assatiyāva nibbeṭheti. Ayaṃ paṭhamo purisadoso.
(Kha) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno codakaṃyeva paṭippharati – "kiṃ nu kho tuyhaṃ bālassa abyattassa bhaṇitena! Tvampi nāma maṃ bhaṇitabbaṃ maññasī"ti! Ayaṃ dutiyo purisadoso.
(Ga) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno codakaṃyeva [codakasseva (syā.) a. ni. 8.14] paccāropeti – "tvampi khosi itthannāmaṃ āpattiṃ āpanno. Tvaṃ tāva paṭhamaṃ paṭikarohī"ti. Ayaṃ tatiyo purisadoso.
(Gha) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno aññenāññaṃ paṭicarati, bahiddhā kathaṃ apanāmeti, kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātukaroti. Ayaṃ catuttho purisadoso.
(Ṅa) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno saṅghamajjhe bāhāvikkhepakaṃ bhaṇati. Ayaṃ pañcamo purisadoso.
(Ca) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno anādiyitvā saṅghaṃ, anādiyitvā codakaṃ, sāpattikova [āpattikova (ka.) a. ni. 8.14] yenakāmaṃ pakkamati. Ayaṃ chaṭṭho purisadoso.
(Cha) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno "nevāhaṃ āpannomhi, na panāhaṃ anāpannomhī"ti tuṇhībhūto saṅghaṃ viheseti. Ayaṃ sattamo purisadoso.
(Ja) puna caparaṃ bhikkhū bhikkhuṃ āpattiyā codenti. So bhikkhu bhikkhūhi āpattiyā codiyamāno evamāha – "kiṃ nu kho tumhe āyasmanto atibāḷhaṃ mayi byāvaṭā. Idānāhaṃ sikkhaṃ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattissāmī"ti. So sikkhaṃ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattitvā evamāha – "idāni kho tumhe āyasmanto attamanā hothā"ti. Ayaṃ aṭṭhamo purisadoso. Ime aṭṭha purisadosā. ^2-957

##### (7) Aṭṭha asaññīvādā ^2-17-9-7-0

Tattha katame aṭṭha asaññīvādā? "Rūpī attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – asaññīti naṃ paññapenti; arūpī attā…pe… rūpī ca arūpī ca…pe… nevarūpīnārūpī…pe… "antavā attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – asaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "anantavā attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – asaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "antavā ca anantavā ca attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – asaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "nevantavā nānantavā attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – asaññīti naṃ paññapenti. Ime aṭṭha asaññīvādā. ^2-958

##### (8) Aṭṭha nevasaññīnāsaññīvādā ^2-17-9-8-0

Tattha katame aṭṭha nevasaññīnāsaññīvādā? "Rūpī attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "arūpī attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "rūpī ca arūpī ca attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "nevarūpīnārūpī attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "antavā attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "anantavā attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "antavā ca anantavā ca attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti; "nevantavā nānantavā attā hoti arogo paraṃ maraṇā"ti – nevasaññīnāsaññīti naṃ paññapenti. Ime aṭṭha nevasaññīnāsaññīvādā.
Aṭṭhakaṃ. ^2-959

#### Navakaniddeso ^2-17-10-0

##### (1) Nava āghātavatthūni ^2-17-10-1-0

Tattha katamāni nava āghātavatthūni? "Anatthaṃ me acarī"ti āghāto jāyati; "anatthaṃ me caratī"ti āghāto jāyati; "anatthaṃ me carissatī"ti āghāto jāyati; "piyassa me manāpassa anatthaṃ acari"…pe… anatthaṃ carati…pe… anatthaṃ carissatīti āghāto jāyati; appiyassa me amanāpassa atthaṃ acari…pe… atthaṃ carati…pe… atthaṃ carissatīti āghāto jāyati. Imāni nava āghātavatthūni. ^2-960

##### (2) Nava purisamalāni ^2-17-10-2-0

Tattha katamāni nava purisamalāni? Kodho, makkho, issā, macchariyaṃ, māyā, sāṭheyyaṃ, musāvādo, pāpicchatā, micchādiṭṭhi – imāni nava purisamalāni. ^2-961

##### (3) Navavidhā mānā ^2-17-10-3-0

Tattha katame navavidhā mānā? "Seyyassa seyyohamasmī"ti māno, "seyyassa sadisohamasmī"ti māno, "seyyassa hīnohamasmī"ti māno, "sadisassa seyyohamasmī"ti māno, "sadisassa sadisohamasmī"ti māno, "sadisassa hīnohamasmī"ti māno, "hīnassa seyyohamasmī"ti māno, "hīnassa sadisohamasmī"ti māno, "hīnassa hīnohamasmī"ti māno – ime navavidhā mānā. ^2-962

##### (4) Nava taṇhāmūlakā dhammā ^2-17-10-4-0

Tattha katame nava taṇhāmūlakā dhammā? Taṇhaṃ paṭicca pariyesanā, pariyesanaṃ paṭicca lābho, lābhaṃ paṭicca vinicchayo, vinicchayaṃ paṭicca chandarāgo, chandarāgaṃ paṭicca ajjhosānaṃ, ajjhosānaṃ paṭicca pariggaho, pariggahaṃ paṭicca macchariyaṃ, macchariyaṃ paṭicca ārakkho, ārakkhādhikaraṇaṃ daṇḍādāna-satthādāna-kalaha-viggahavivāda-tuvaṃtuvaṃ-pesuñña-musāvādā aneke pāpakā akusalā dhammā sambhavanti – ime nava taṇhāmūlakā dhammā. ^2-963

##### (5) Nava iñjitāni ^2-17-10-5-0

Tattha katamāni nava iñjitāni? "Asmī"ti iñjitametaṃ, "ahamasmī"ti iñjitametaṃ, "ayamahamasmī"ti iñjitametaṃ "bhavissa"nti iñjitametaṃ, "rūpī bhavissa"nti iñjitametaṃ, "arūpī bhavissa"nti iñjitametaṃ, "saññī bhavissa"nti iñjitametaṃ, "asaññī bhavissa"nti iñjitametaṃ, "nevasaññīnāsaññī bhavissa"nti iñjitametaṃ – imāni nava iñjitāni. ^2-964

##### (6-9) Nava maññitādīni ^2-17-10-6-0

Tattha katamāni nava maññitāni… nava phanditāni… nava papañcitāni… nava saṅkhatāni? "Asmī"ti saṅkhatametaṃ, "ahamasmī"ti saṅkhatametaṃ, "ayamahamasmī"ti saṅkhatametaṃ, "bhavissa"nti saṅkhatametaṃ, "rūpī bhavissa"nti saṅkhatametaṃ, "arūpī bhavissa"nti saṅkhatametaṃ, "saññī bhavissa"nti saṅkhatametaṃ, "asaññī bhavissa"nti saṅkhatametaṃ, "nevasaññīnāsaññī bhavissa"nti saṅkhatametaṃ – imāni nava saṅkhatāni.
Navakaṃ. ^2-965

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Aṭṭhakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-9|↑↑↑]]

Aṭṭhakaniddese kilesāyeva kilesavatthūni; kusītavatthūnīti kusītassa alasassa kosajjakāraṇāni – kammaṃ kātabbaṃ hoti, kataṃ hoti, maggo gantabbo hoti, gato hoti, piṇḍapātaṃ na labhati, labhati ‘māsācitaṃ maññe’ti garuko hoti, appamattako ābādho uppanno hoti, gilānā vuṭṭhito hoti, sabbattha ‘handāhaṃ nipajjāmī’ti osīdanaṃ, appattassa pattiyā duvidhampi vīriyaṃ nārabhati. Lokassa dhammāti lokadhammā, etehi vimutto nāma natthi, buddhānampi honti eva; lābhe sārāgo alābhe paṭivirodho, yase ayase pasaṃsāya nindāya sukhe dukkhe ca uppanno sārāgo vā virodho vā cittaṃ paṭihanatīti paṭighāto. Purisadosesu codito ‘na sarāmī’ti assatibhāvena nibbeṭheti, codakaṃyeva paṭippharati, paccāropeti, aññenāññaṃ paṭicaratīti aññena vacanena aññaṃ paṭicchādeti bahiddhā kathaṃ apanāmeti, kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātukaroti, bāhāvikkhepakaṃ bhaṇati. Asaññīvādesu lābhino kasiṇarūpaṃ attāti gahetvā rūpīti diṭṭhi uppajjati, alābhino takkamatteneva; diṭṭhigatiko hi ummattako viya yaṃ vā taṃ vā gaṇhāti.

### Navakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-10|↑↑↑]]

Navakaniddese nava āghātavatthūni sattesu uppattivaseneva kathitāni; purisānaṃ malānīti purisamalāni; navavidhāti navappabhedā mānā. Taṇhaṃ paṭicca rūpādiārammaṇapariyesanā, pariyesanaṃ paṭicca lābho, lābhaṃ paṭicca vinicchayo – idha vitakkoyeva, lābhaṃ labhitvā hi ‘ettakaṃ mayhaṃ bhavissati, ettakaṃ parassa, ettakaṃ nidahissāmī’ti vitakkeneva vinicchināti – vinicchayaṃ paṭicca chandarāgo, chandarāgaṃ paṭicca ajjhosānaṃ ‘ahaṃ mama’nti balavasanniṭṭhānaṃ, ajjhosānaṃ paṭicca pariggaho, pariggahaṃ paṭicca macchariyaṃ parehi sādhāraṇabhāvassa asahanatā, macchariyaṃ paṭicca ārakkho, ārakkhādhikaraṇaṃ daṇḍādānasatthādānakalahaviggahavivādatuvaṃtuvapesuññamusāvādā. Iñjitādīhi sabbapadehi mānova kathito; māno hi iñjanato iñjitaṃ, maññanato maññitaṃ, phandanato phanditaṃ, papañcanato papañcitaṃ, tehi tehi kāraṇehi saṅkhatattā saṅkhatanti ca vuccati.

### Navakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-10|↑↑↑]]

Navakaniddese taṇhaṃ paṭicca pariyesanā, pariyesanaṃ paṭicca lābho, lābhaṃ paṭicca vinicchayo, tato chandarāgo ajjhosānaṃ pariggaho macchariyaṃ ārakkho, ārakkhādhikaraṇaṃ daṇḍādānasatthādānakalahaviggahavivādā uppajjantīti nava taṇhāmūlakā dhammā, iñjitādīhi ca mānova kathito. Tasmā yoginā nava āghātavatthūni sattesu uppajjamānāni sallakkhetvā, taṇhāmūlakaṃ imaṃ paṭipāṭiṃ ārakkhato daṇḍādānakalahato pubbeyeva pariyesanāvinicchayakāle chinditvā, parehi sādhāraṇabhāvaṃ sahanto macchariyaṃ pahāya, ‘asmī’ti ‘aha’nti iñjantaṃ maññantaṃ phandantaṃ papañcentaṃ mānaṃ passantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

##### (3) The mind's resistance to the eight worldly conditions ^2-17-9-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-954]]

Therein, what is the mind's resistance to the eight worldly conditions? Passion at gain, opposition at loss; passion at fame, opposition at disrepute; passion at praise, opposition at blame; passion at happiness, opposition at suffering — this is the mind's resistance to the eight worldly conditions. ^2-954

##### (4) The eight ignoble ways of speech ^2-17-9-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-955]]

Therein, what are the eight ignoble ways of speech? Saying one has seen what one has not seen, saying one has heard what one has not heard, saying one has sensed what one has not sensed, saying one has known what one has not known, saying one has not seen what one has seen, saying one has not heard what one has heard, saying one has not sensed what one has sensed, saying one has not known what one has known — these are the eight ignoble ways of speech. ^2-955

##### (5) The eight wrongnesses ^2-17-9-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-956]]

Therein, what are the eight wrongnesses? Wrong view, wrong intention, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, wrong concentration — these are the eight wrongnesses. ^2-956

##### (6) The eight faults of a person ^2-17-9-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-957]]

Therein, what are the eight faults of a person? (a) Here monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, evades it through sheer forgetfulness: "I do not remember, I do not remember." This is the first fault of a person.
(b) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, snaps back at the very one who charges him: "What is the use of the talk of a fool and incompetent like you! Do you really think you have something to say to me?" This is the second fault of a person.
(c) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, throws the charge back at the very one who charges him: "You too have committed such-and-such an offence. You make amends first." This is the third fault of a person.
(d) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, answers one thing with another, leads the talk aside, and displays anger, hate, and displeasure. This is the fourth fault of a person.
(e) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, speaks waving his arms about in the midst of the Order. This is the fifth fault of a person.
(f) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, disregarding the Order and disregarding the one who charges him, goes off wherever he likes while still bearing the offence. This is the sixth fault of a person.
(g) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, saying "I have neither committed an offence nor not committed one", vexes the Order by remaining silent. This is the seventh fault of a person.
(h) Further, monks charge a monk with an offence. That monk, being charged with the offence by the monks, says: "Why are you venerable ones so extremely busy about me? Now I shall renounce the training and return to the lower life." Having renounced the training and returned to the lower life, he says: "Now, venerable ones, be satisfied." This is the eighth fault of a person. These are the eight faults of a person. ^2-957

##### (7) The eight doctrines of the non-percipient ^2-17-9-7-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-958]]

Therein, what are the eight doctrines of the non-percipient? "The self has material form and is unimpaired after death" — they declare it non-percipient; "the self is immaterial…"; "the self both has material form and is immaterial…"; "the self neither has material form nor is immaterial…"; "the self is finite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it non-percipient; "the self is infinite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it non-percipient; "the self is both finite and infinite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it non-percipient; "the self is neither finite nor infinite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it non-percipient. These are the eight doctrines of the non-percipient. ^2-958

##### (8) The eight doctrines of the neither-percipient-nor-non-percipient ^2-17-9-8-0

Therein, what are the eight doctrines of the neither-percipient-nor-non-percipient? "The self has material form and is unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self is immaterial and unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self both has material form and is immaterial and is unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self neither has material form nor is immaterial and is unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self is finite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self is infinite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self is both finite and infinite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient; "the self is neither finite nor infinite and unimpaired after death" — they declare it neither percipient nor non-percipient. These are the eight doctrines of the neither-percipient-nor-non-percipient.

#### The Exposition of the Nines ^2-17-10-0

##### (1) The nine grounds of resentment ^2-17-10-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-960]]

Therein, what are the nine grounds of resentment? Resentment arises: "He has done me harm"; resentment arises: "He is doing me harm"; resentment arises: "He will do me harm"; "He has done harm… is doing harm… will do harm to one dear and pleasing to me" — resentment arises; "He has done good… is doing good… will do good to one disliked and displeasing to me" — resentment arises. These are the nine grounds of resentment. ^2-960

##### (2) The nine stains of a person ^2-17-10-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-961]]

Therein, what are the nine stains of a person? Anger, contempt, envy, stinginess, deceit, treachery, false speech, evil desire, wrong view — these are the nine stains of a person. ^2-961

##### (3) The nine kinds of conceit ^2-17-10-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-962]]

Therein, what are the nine kinds of conceit? The conceit of one who is better, "I am better"; the conceit of one who is better, "I am equal"; the conceit of one who is better, "I am worse"; the conceit of one who is equal, "I am better"; the conceit of one who is equal, "I am equal"; the conceit of one who is equal, "I am worse"; the conceit of one who is worse, "I am better"; the conceit of one who is worse, "I am equal"; the conceit of one who is worse, "I am worse" — these are the nine kinds of conceit. ^2-962

##### (4) The nine things rooted in craving ^2-17-10-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-963]]

Therein, what are the nine things rooted in craving? Because of craving there is seeking; because of seeking there is gain; because of gain there is deciding; because of deciding there is desire-and-lust; because of desire-and-lust there is holding fast; because of holding fast there is taking possession; because of taking possession there is stinginess; because of stinginess there is guarding; and with guarding as the occasion, there come to be the taking up of sticks and weapons, quarrels, contention, disputes, accusations, divisive speech, false speech, and many other evil, unwholesome things. These are the nine things rooted in craving. ^2-963

##### (5) The nine agitations ^2-17-10-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-964]]

Therein, what are the nine agitations? "I am" is an agitation; "I am this" is an agitation; "this I am" is an agitation; "I shall be" is an agitation; "I shall have material form" is an agitation; "I shall be immaterial" is an agitation; "I shall be percipient" is an agitation; "I shall be non-percipient" is an agitation; "I shall be neither percipient nor non-percipient" is an agitation — these are the nine agitations. ^2-964

##### (6-9) The nine imaginings and the rest ^2-17-10-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-965]]

Therein, what are the nine imaginings… the nine waverings… the nine proliferations… the nine constructions? "I am" is a construction; "I am this" is a construction; "this I am" is a construction; "I shall be" is a construction; "I shall have material form" is a construction; "I shall be immaterial" is a construction; "I shall be percipient" is a construction; "I shall be non-percipient" is a construction; "I shall be neither percipient nor non-percipient" is a construction — these are the nine constructions.
The Nines. ^2-965
