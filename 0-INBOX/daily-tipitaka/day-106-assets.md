### Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅgo ^2-6-0
#### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ ^2-6-1-0

225. Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā, saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ, viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ, nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ, saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso, phassapaccayā vedanā, vedanāpaccayā taṇhā, taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ, upādānapaccayā bhavo, bhavapaccayā jāti, jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti. Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti. ^2-225

226. Tattha katamā avijjā? Dukkhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhasamudaye aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya aññāṇaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "avijjā".
Tattha katame avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā? Puññābhisaṅkhāro, apuññābhisaṅkhāro, āneñjābhisaṅkhāro, kāyasaṅkhāro, vacīsaṅkhāro, cittasaṅkhāro.
Tattha katamo puññābhisaṅkhāro? Kusalā cetanā kāmāvacarā rūpāvacarā dānamayā sīlamayā bhāvanāmayā – ayaṃ vuccati "puññābhisaṅkhāro".
Tattha katamo apuññābhisaṅkhāro? Akusalā cetanā kāmāvacarā – ayaṃ vuccati "apuññābhisaṅkhāro".
Tattha katamo āneñjābhisaṅkhāro? Kusalā cetanā arūpāvacarā – ayaṃ vuccati "āneñjābhisaṅkhāro".
Tattha katamo kāyasaṅkhāro? Kāyasañcetanā kāyasaṅkhāro, vacīsañcetanā vacīsaṅkhāro, manosañcetanā cittasaṅkhāro. Ime vuccanti "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā". ^2-226

227. Tattha katamaṃ saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ? Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ, sotaviññāṇaṃ, ghānaviññāṇaṃ, jivhāviññāṇaṃ, kāyaviññāṇaṃ, manoviññāṇaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ". ^2-227

228. Tattha katamaṃ viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ? Atthi nāmaṃ, atthi rūpaṃ. Tattha katamaṃ nāmaṃ? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho – idaṃ vuccati "nāmaṃ". Tattha katamaṃ rūpaṃ? Cattāro mahābhūtā, catunnañca mahābhūtānaṃ upādāya rūpaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "rūpaṃ". Iti idañca nāmaṃ, idañca rūpaṃ. Idaṃ vuccati "viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ". ^2-228

229. Tattha katamaṃ nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ? Cakkhāyatanaṃ, sotāyatanaṃ, ghānāyatanaṃ, jivhāyatanaṃ, kāyāyatanaṃ, manāyatanaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ". ^2-229

230. Tattha katamo saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso? Cakkhusamphasso sotasamphasso ghānasamphasso jivhāsamphasso kāyasamphasso manosamphasso – ayaṃ vuccati "saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso". ^2-230

231. Tattha katamā phassapaccayā vedanā? Cakkhusamphassajā vedanā, sotasamphassajā vedanā, ghānasamphassajā vedanā, jivhāsamphassajā vedanā, kāyasamphassajā vedanā, manosamphassajā vedanā – ayaṃ vuccati "phassapaccayā vedanā". ^2-231

232. Tattha katamā vedanāpaccayā taṇhā? Rūpataṇhā, saddataṇhā, gandhataṇhā, rasataṇhā, phoṭṭhabbataṇhā, dhammataṇhā – ayaṃ vuccati "vedanāpaccayā taṇhā". ^2-232

233. Tattha katamaṃ taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ? Kāmupādānaṃ, diṭṭhupādānaṃ, sīlabbatupādānaṃ, attavādupādānaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ". ^2-233

234. Tattha katamo upādānapaccayā bhavo? Bhavo duvidhena – atthi kammabhavo, atthi upapattibhavo. Tattha katamo kammabhavo? Puññābhisaṅkhāro, apuññābhisaṅkhāro, āneñjābhisaṅkhāro – ayaṃ vuccati "kammabhavo". Sabbampi bhavagāmikammaṃ kammabhavo.
Tattha katamo upapattibhavo? Kāmabhavo, rūpabhavo, arūpabhavo, saññābhavo, asaññābhavo, nevasaññānāsaññābhavo, ekavokārabhavo, catuvokārabhavo, pañcavokārabhavo – ayaṃ vuccati "upapattibhavo". Iti ayañca kammabhavo, ayañca upapattibhavo. Ayaṃ vuccati "upādānapaccayā bhavo". ^2-234

235. Tattha katamā bhavapaccayā jāti? Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhi tamhi sattanikāye jāti sañjāti okkanti abhinibbatti, khandhānaṃ pātubhāvo, āyatanānaṃ paṭilābho – ayaṃ vuccati "bhavapaccayā jāti". ^2-235

236. Tattha katamaṃ jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ? Atthi jarā, atthi maraṇaṃ. Tattha katamā jarā? Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhi tamhi sattanikāye jarā jīraṇatā khaṇḍiccaṃ pāliccaṃ valittacatā āyuno saṃhāni indriyānaṃ paripāko – ayaṃ vuccati "jarā".
Tattha katamaṃ maraṇaṃ? Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhā tamhā sattanikāyā cuti cavanatā bhedo antaradhānaṃ maccu maraṇaṃ kālakiriyā [kālaṃkiriyā (ka.)] khandhānaṃ bhedo kaḷevarassa nikkhepo jīvitindriyassupacchedo – idaṃ vuccati "maraṇaṃ". Iti ayañca jarā, idañca maraṇaṃ. Idaṃ vuccati "jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ". ^2-236

237. Tattha katamo soko? Ñātibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, bhogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, rogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, sīlabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, diṭṭhibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, aññataraññatarena byasanena samannāgatassa, aññataraññatarena dukkhadhammena phuṭṭhassa soko socanā socitattaṃ antosoko antoparisoko cetaso parijjhāyanā domanassaṃ sokasallaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "soko". ^2-237

238. Tattha katamo paridevo? Ñātibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, bhogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, rogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, sīlabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, diṭṭhibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, aññataraññatarena byasanena samannāgatassa, aññataraññatarena dukkhadhammena phuṭṭhassa ādevo paridevo ādevanā paridevanā ādevitattaṃ paridevitattaṃ vācā palāpo vippalāpo lālappo lālappanā lālappitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati paridevo". ^2-238

239. Tattha katamaṃ dukkhaṃ? Yaṃ kāyikaṃ asātaṃ kāyikaṃ dukkhaṃ kāyasamphassajaṃ asātaṃ dukkhaṃ vedayitaṃ kāyasamphassajā asātā dukkhā vedanā – idaṃ vuccati "dukkhaṃ". ^2-239

240. Tattha katamaṃ domanassaṃ? Yaṃ cetasikaṃ asātaṃ, cetasikaṃ dukkhaṃ, cetosamphassajaṃ asātaṃ dukkhaṃ vedayitaṃ, cetosamphassajā asātā dukkhā vedanā – idaṃ vuccati "domanassaṃ". ^2-240

241. Tattha katamo upāyāso? Ñātibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, bhogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, rogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, sīlabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, diṭṭhibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa, aññataraññatarena byasanena samannāgatassa, aññataraññatarena dukkhadhammena phuṭṭhassa āyāso upāyāso āyāsitattaṃ upāyāsitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "upāyāso". ^2-241

242. Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotīti, evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa saṅgati hoti, samāgamo hoti, samodhānaṃ hoti, pātubhāvo hoti. Tena vuccati "evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotī"ti.
Suttantabhājanīyaṃ. ^2-242

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭicca)

[[#^toc-6-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā"tiādinā nayena dvādasaṅgiko paccayākāro nānācittavasena vibhatto. Tattha desanābhedato, atthalakkhaṇekavidhādito, aṅgānañca vavatthānāti vinicchayo viññātabbo. Atthato tāva pūretuṃ ayuttaṭṭhena kāyaduccaritādi avindiyaṃ nāma, taṃ vindatīti avijjā; tabbiparītato kāyasucaritādi vindiyaṃ nāma, taṃ na vindatītipi avijjā; khandhānaṃ rāsaṭṭhaṃ, āyatanānaṃ āyatanaṭṭhaṃ, dhātūnaṃ suññaṭṭhaṃ, saccānaṃ tathaṭṭhaṃ, indriyānaṃ ādhipateyyaṭṭhaṃ aviditaṃ karotītipi avijjā. Yaṃ paṭicca phalameti so paccayo; upakārakaṭṭho paccayaṭṭho. Saṅkhatamabhisaṅkharontīti saṅkhārā; vijānātīti viññāṇaṃ, namatīti nāmaṃ, ruppatīti rūpaṃ, āye tanoti āyatañca nayatīti āyatanaṃ, phusatīti phasso, vedayatīti vedanā, paritassatīti taṇhā, upādiyatīti upādānaṃ, bhavati bhāvayati cāti bhavo, jananaṃ jāti, jīraṇaṃ jarā, maranti etenāti maraṇaṃ. Lakkhaṇādito ca aññāṇalakkhaṇā avijjā sammohanarasā chādanapaccupaṭṭhānā āsavapadaṭṭhānā, abhisaṅkharaṇalakkhaṇā saṅkhārā āyūhanarasā cetanāpaccupaṭṭhānā avijjāpadaṭṭhānā, vijānanalakkhaṇaṃ viññāṇaṃ pubbaṅgamarasaṃ paṭisandhipaccupaṭṭhānaṃ saṅkhārapadaṭṭhānanti eteneva nayena sesaṅgānipi veditabbāni. "Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotī"ti ettha kevalassāti asammissassa sakalassa vā, dukkhakkhandhassāti dukkhasamūhassa — na sattassa, na sukhasubhādīnaṃ; samudayoti nibbatti.

#### Paccayacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Sabbapaṭhame avijjāmūlake naye paccayacatukke cattāro vārā. Paṭhamo nāmarūpaṭṭhāne nāmassa, saḷāyatanaṭṭhāne ca chaṭṭhāyatanassa vuttattā aparipuṇṇaaṅgadvayayutto dvādasaṅgikavāro nāma; dutiyo nāmarūpaṭṭhāne nāmasseva vuttattā, saḷāyatanaṭṭhāne ca na kassaci vuttattā aparipuṇṇaekaṅgayutto ekādasaṅgikavāro nāma; tatiyo saḷāyatanaṭṭhāne chaṭṭhāyatanassa vuttattā paripuṇṇaekaṅgayutto dvādasaṅgikavāro nāma; catuttho pana paripuṇṇadvādasaṅgikoyeva. Aṭṭhakathāyaṃ vuttaṃ — "paṭhamo sabbasaṅgāhikaṭṭhena, dutiyo paccayavisesaṭṭhena, tatiyo gabbhaseyyakasattānaṃ vasena, catuttho opapātikasattānaṃ vasena gahito"ti; tathā aparipuṇṇaparipuṇṇāyatanavasena, rūpabhavakāmabhavavasena ca. Visesato paṭhamakā dve vārā arūpabhave paccayākāradassanatthaṃ vuttā, tatiyo rūpabhave, catuttho kāmabhave. Tattha "yaṃ yattha aññathā vuttaṃ, avuttañcāpi yaṃ yahiṃ; yaṃ yathā paccayo yassa, taṃ sabbamupalakkhaye"ti gāthāya nayo veditabbo. Saṅkhārassa hi avijjā sampayuttadhammasādhāraṇehi sahajātaaññamaññanissayasampayuttaatthiavigatapaccayehi chahi hetupaccayena cāti sattadhā paccayo; yasmā pana parato hetucatukkādīni tīṇi catukkāni avigatasampayuttaaññamaññapaccayavasena vuttāni, tasmā idha tāni apanetvā avasesānaṃ vasena catudhāva paccayoti veditabbo.

## English translation (from `AI_translation/english/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

### Analysis of Dependent Origination ^2-6-0
#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-6-1-0

225. With ignorance as condition, formations; with formations as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality and material form; with mentality and material form as condition, the six sense-fields; with the six sense-fields as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, craving; with craving as condition, clinging; with clinging as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair come to be. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-225

226. Therein, what is ignorance? Not knowing suffering, not knowing the origin of suffering, not knowing the cessation of suffering, not knowing the way leading to the cessation of suffering — this is called "ignorance".
Therein, what are the formations that have ignorance as condition? The meritorious formation, the demeritorious formation, the imperturbable formation, the bodily formation, the verbal formation, the mental formation.
Therein, what is the meritorious formation? Wholesome intention belonging to the sense sphere or the fine-material sphere, made of giving, made of virtue, made of meditative development — this is called "the meritorious formation".
Therein, what is the demeritorious formation? Unwholesome intention belonging to the sense sphere — this is called "the demeritorious formation".
Therein, what is the imperturbable formation? Wholesome intention belonging to the immaterial sphere — this is called "the imperturbable formation".
Therein, what is the bodily formation? Bodily intention is the bodily formation; verbal intention is the verbal formation; mental intention is the mental formation. These are called "the formations that have ignorance as condition". ^2-226

227. Therein, what is the consciousness that has formations as condition? Eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, mind-consciousness — this is called "the consciousness that has formations as condition". ^2-227

228. Therein, what are the mentality and material form that have consciousness as condition? There is mentality, there is material form. Therein, what is mentality? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations — this is called "mentality". Therein, what is material form? The four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements — this is called "material form". So this is mentality and this is material form. This is called "the mentality and material form that have consciousness as condition". ^2-228

229. Therein, what are the six sense-fields that have mentality and material form as condition? The eye sense-field, the ear sense-field, the nose sense-field, the tongue sense-field, the body sense-field, the mind sense-field — this is called "the six sense-fields that have mentality and material form as condition". ^2-229

230. Therein, what is the contact that has the six sense-fields as condition? Eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, mind-contact — this is called "the contact that has the six sense-fields as condition". ^2-230

231. Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Feeling born of eye-contact, feeling born of ear-contact, feeling born of nose-contact, feeling born of tongue-contact, feeling born of body-contact, feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition". ^2-231

232. Therein, what is the craving that has feeling as condition? Craving for sights, craving for sounds, craving for smells, craving for tastes, craving for tangible things, craving for mental objects — this is called "the craving that has feeling as condition". ^2-232

233. Therein, what is the clinging that has craving as condition? Clinging to sense pleasures, clinging to views, clinging to rules and observances, clinging to a doctrine of self — this is called "the clinging that has craving as condition". ^2-233

234. Therein, what is the existence that has clinging as condition? Existence is twofold: there is action-existence and there is rebirth-existence. Therein, what is action-existence? The meritorious formation, the demeritorious formation, the imperturbable formation — this is called "action-existence". All action that leads to existence is action-existence.
Therein, what is rebirth-existence? Sense-sphere existence, fine-material existence, immaterial existence, percipient existence, non-percipient existence, neither-percipient-nor-non-percipient existence, one-constituent existence, four-constituent existence, five-constituent existence — this is called "rebirth-existence". So this is action-existence and this is rebirth-existence. This is called "the existence that has clinging as condition". ^2-234

235. Therein, what is the birth that has existence as condition? The birth of the various beings in the various classes of beings, their being born, their descent, their coming forth, the appearance of the aggregates, the obtaining of the sense-fields — this is called "the birth that has existence as condition". ^2-235

236. Therein, what are the aging and death that have birth as condition? There is aging, there is death. Therein, what is aging? The aging of the various beings in the various classes of beings, their growing old, broken teeth, grey hair, wrinkled skin, the decline of the life span, the ripening of the faculties — this is called "aging".
Therein, what is death? The passing away of the various beings from the various classes of beings, their passing on, breakup, disappearance, dying, death, the completion of their time, the breakup of the aggregates, the laying down of the body, the cutting off of the life faculty — this is called "death". So this is aging and this is death. This is called "the aging and death that have birth as condition". ^2-236

237. Therein, what is sorrow? For one touched by loss of relatives, touched by loss of wealth, touched by loss through illness, touched by loss of virtue, or touched by loss of view; for one who meets with some misfortune or other, touched by some painful thing or other — the sorrow, sorrowing, state of sorrowing, inner sorrow, inner deep sorrow, the burning of the mind, grief, the dart of sorrow — this is called "sorrow". ^2-237

238. Therein, what is lamentation? For one touched by loss of relatives, touched by loss of wealth, touched by loss through illness, touched by loss of virtue, or touched by loss of view; for one who meets with some misfortune or other, touched by some painful thing or other — the crying, lamenting, act of crying, act of lamenting, state of crying, state of lamenting, wordy talk, babbling, wailing, the act of wailing, the state of wailing — this is called "lamentation". ^2-238

239. Therein, what is pain? Bodily discomfort, bodily pain, the uncomfortable painful experience born of body-contact, the uncomfortable painful feeling born of body-contact — this is called "pain". ^2-239

240. Therein, what is grief? Mental discomfort, mental pain, the uncomfortable painful experience born of mind-contact, the uncomfortable painful feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "grief". ^2-240

241. Therein, what is despair? For one touched by loss of relatives, touched by loss of wealth, touched by loss through illness, touched by loss of virtue, or touched by loss of view; for one who meets with some misfortune or other, touched by some painful thing or other — the distress, despair, state of distress, state of despair — this is called "despair". ^2-241

242. "Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering": thus there is the coming together of this entire mass of suffering, its meeting, its combination, its appearance. Therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering".
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-242
