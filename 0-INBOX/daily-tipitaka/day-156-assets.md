## Pāli source verses

##### (10) Ahirikañca anottappañca ^2-17-3-10-0

(Ka) tattha katamaṃ ahirikaṃ? Yaṃ na hirīyati hiriyitabbena, na hirīyati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – idaṃ vuccati "ahirikaṃ".
(Kha) tattha katamaṃ anottappaṃ? Yaṃ na ottappati ottappitabbena na ottappati pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samāpattiyā – idaṃ vuccati "anottappaṃ". ^2-900

##### (11) Dovacassatā ca pāpamittatā ca ^2-17-3-11-0

(Ka) tattha katamā dovacassatā? Sahadhammike vuccamāne dovacassāyaṃ dovacassiyaṃ dovacassatā vippaṭikulaggāhitā vipaccanīkasātatā anādariyaṃ anādaratā agāravatā appatissavatā – ayaṃ vuccati "dovacassatā".
(Kha) tattha katamā pāpamittatā? Ye te puggalā assaddhā dussīlā appassutā maccharino duppaññā, yā tesaṃ sevanā nisevanā saṃsevanā bhajanā sambhajanā bhatti sambhatti sampavaṅkatā – ayaṃ vuccati "pāpamittatā". ^2-901

##### (12) Anajjavo ca amaddavo ca ^2-17-3-12-0

(Ka) tattha katamo anajjavo? Yo anajjavo anajjavatā jimhatā vaṅkatā kuṭilatā – ayaṃ vuccati "anajjavo".
(Kha) tattha katamo amaddavo? Yā amudutā amaddavatā kakkhaḷiyaṃ phārusiyaṃ kakkhaḷatā kaṭhinatā [kathinatā (syā. ka.)] ujucittatā amudutā – ayaṃ vuccati "amaddavo". ^2-902

##### (13) Akkhanti ca asoraccañca ^2-17-3-13-0

(Ka) tattha katamā akkhanti? Yā akkhanti akkhamanatā anadhivāsanatā caṇḍikkaṃ asuropo anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "akkhanti".
(Kha) tattha katamaṃ asoraccaṃ? Kāyiko vītikkamo vācasiko vītikkamo kāyikavācasiko vītikkamo – idaṃ vuccati "asoraccaṃ". Sabbampi dussīlyaṃ asoraccaṃ. ^2-903

##### (14) Asākhalyañca appaṭisanthāro ca ^2-17-3-14-0

(Ka) tattha katamaṃ asākhalyaṃ? Yā sā vācā kaṇḍakā kakkasā parakaṭukā parābhisajjanī kodhasāmantā asamādhisaṃvattanikā tathārūpiṃ vācaṃ bhāsitā hoti. Yā tattha asaṇhavācatā asakhilavācatā pharusavācatā – idaṃ vuccati "asākhalyaṃ".
(Kha) tattha katamo appaṭisanthāro [appaṭisandhāro (ka.)]? Dve paṭisanthārā – āmisapaṭisanthāro ca dhammapaṭisanthāro ca. Idhekacco appaṭisanthārako hoti āmisapaṭisanthārena vā dhammapaṭisanthārena vā – ayaṃ vuccati "appaṭisanthāro". ^2-904

##### (15) Indriyesu aguttadvāratā ca bhojane amattaññutā ca ^2-17-3-15-0

(Ka) tattha katamā indriyesu aguttadvāratā? Idhekacco cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā nimittaggāhī hoti anubyañjanaggāhī. Yatvādhikaraṇamenaṃ cakkhundriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādomanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssaveyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya na paṭipajjati, na rakkhati cakkhundriyaṃ, cakkhundriye na saṃvaraṃ āpajjati. Sotena saddaṃ sutvā…pe… ghānena gandhaṃ ghāyitvā…pe… jivhāya rasaṃ sāyitvā…pe… kāyena phoṭṭhabbaṃ phusitvā…pe… manasā dhammaṃ viññāya nimittaggāhī hoti anubyañjanaggāhī. Yatvādhikaraṇamenaṃ manindriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādomanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssaveyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya na paṭipajjati, na rakkhati manindriyaṃ, manindriye na saṃvaraṃ āpajjati. Yā imesaṃ channaṃ indriyānaṃ agutti agopanā anārakkho asaṃvaro – ayaṃ vuccati "indriyesu aguttadvāratā".
(Kha) tattha katamā bhojane amattaññutā? Idhekacco appaṭisaṅkhā ayoniso āhāraṃ āhāreti davāya madāya maṇḍanāya vibhūsanāya. Yā tattha asantuṭṭhitā amattaññutā appaṭisaṅkhā bhojane – ayaṃ vuccati "bhojane amattaññutā." ^2-905

##### (16) Muṭṭhassaccañca asampajaññañca ^2-17-3-16-0

(Ka) tattha katamaṃ muṭṭhassaccaṃ? Yā assati ananussati appaṭissati assati assaraṇatā adhāraṇatā pilāpanatā sammusanatā – idaṃ vuccati "muṭṭhassaccaṃ".
(Kha) tattha katamaṃ asampajaññaṃ? Yaṃ aññāṇaṃ adassanaṃ…pe… avijjālaṅgī moho akusalamūlaṃ – idaṃ vuccati "asampajaññaṃ". ^2-906

##### (17) Sīlavipatti ca diṭṭhivipatti ca ^2-17-3-17-0

(Ka) tattha katamā sīlavipatti? Yo kāyiko vītikkamo vācasiko vītikkamo kāyikavācasiko vītikkamo – ayaṃ vuccati "sīlavipatti". Sabbampi dussīlyaṃ sīlavipatti.
(Kha) tattha katamā diṭṭhivipatti? "Natthi dinnaṃ, natthi yiṭṭhaṃ…pe… ye imañca lokaṃ parañca lokaṃ sayaṃ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedentī"ti – yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṃ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho – ayaṃ vuccati "diṭṭhivipatti". Sabbāpi micchādiṭṭhi diṭṭhivipatti. ^2-907

##### (18) Ajjhattasaṃyojanañca bahiddhāsaṃyojanañca ^2-17-3-18-0

(Ka) tattha katamaṃ ajjhattasaṃyojanaṃ? Pañcorambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni – ajjhattasaṃyojanaṃ. (Kha) pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni – bahiddhāsaṃyojanaṃ.
Dukaṃ. ^2-908

#### Tikaniddeso ^2-17-4-0

##### (1) Tīṇi akusalamūlāni ^2-17-4-1-0

Tattha katamāni tīṇi akusalamūlāni? Lobho, doso, moho.
(Ka) tattha katamo lobho? Yo rāgo sārāgo anunayo anurodho nandī nandirāgo cittassa sārāgo icchā mucchā ajjhosānaṃ gedho parigedho saṅgo paṅko ejā māyā janikā sañjananī sibbinī jālinī saritā visattikā sotaṃ visaṭā [visadā (sī. ka.) dha. sa. 1141] āyūhanī [āyūhinī (ka.)] dutiyā paṇidhi bhavanetti vanaṃ vanatho santhavo [sandhavo (ka.)] sineho apekkhā paṭibandhu āsā āsīsanā āsīsitattaṃ [āsiṃsanā āsiṃsitattaṃ (sī. syā.)] rūpāsā saddāsā gandhāsā rasāsā phoṭṭhabbāsā lābhāsā dhanāsā puttāsā jīvitāsā jappā abhijappā jappanā jappitattaṃ loluppaṃ loluppāyanā loluppāyitattaṃ pucchañjikatā sādhukamyatā adhammarāgo visamalobho nikanti nikāmanā patthanā pihanā sampatthanā kāmataṇhā bhavataṇhā vibhavataṇhā rūpataṇhā arūpataṇhā nirodhataṇhā saddataṇhā rūpataṇhā gandhataṇhā rasataṇhā phoṭṭhabbataṇhā dhammataṇhā ogho yogo gantho upādānaṃ āvaraṇaṃ nīvaraṇaṃ chadanaṃ bandhanaṃ upakkileso anusayo pariyuṭṭhānaṃ latā vevicchaṃ dukkhamūlaṃ dukkhanidānaṃ dukkhappabhavo mārapāso mārabaḷisaṃ māravisayo taṇhānadī taṇhājālaṃ taṇhāgaddulaṃ taṇhāsamuddo abhijjhā lobho akusalamūlaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "lobho".
(Kha) tattha katamo doso? "Anatthaṃ me acarī"ti āghāto jāyati, "anatthaṃ me caratī"ti āghāto jāyati, "anatthaṃ me carissatī"ti āghāto jāyati, "piyassa me manāpassa anatthaṃ acari"…pe… anatthaṃ carati…pe… "anatthaṃ carissatī"ti āghāto jāyati, "appiyassa me amanāpassa atthaṃ acari"…pe… atthaṃ carati…pe… "atthaṃ carissatī"ti āghāto jāyati, aṭṭhāne vā pana āghāto jāyati. Yo evarūpo cittassa āghāto paṭighāto paṭighaṃ paṭivirodho kopo pakopo sampakopo doso padoso sampadoso cittassa byāpatti manopadoso kodho kujjhanā kujjhitattaṃ doso dussanā dussitattaṃ byāpatti byāpajjanā byāpajjitattaṃ virodho paṭivirodho caṇḍikkaṃ asuropo anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuccati "doso".
(Ga) tattha katamo moho? Dukkhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhasamudaye aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhe aññāṇaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya aññāṇaṃ, pubbante aññāṇaṃ, aparante aññāṇaṃ, pubbantāparante aññāṇaṃ, idappaccayatā paṭiccasamuppannesu dhammesu aññāṇaṃ, yaṃ evarūpaṃ aññāṇaṃ adassanaṃ…pe… avijjālaṅgī moho akusalamūlaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "moho". Imāni tīṇi akusalamūlāni. ^2-909

##### (2) Tayo akusalavitakkā ^2-17-4-2-0

Tattha katame tayo akusalavitakkā? Kāmavitakko, byāpādavitakko, vihiṃsāvitakko.
(Ka) tattha katamo kāmavitakko? Kāmapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "kāmavitakko".
(Kha) tattha katamo byāpādavitakko? Byāpādapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "byāpādavitakko".
(Ga) tattha katamo vihiṃsāvitakko? Vihiṃsāpaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "vihiṃsāvitakko". Ime tayo akusalavitakkā. ^2-910

##### (3) Tisso akusalasaññā ^2-17-4-3-0

Tattha katamā tisso akusalasaññā? Kāmasaññā, byāpādasaññā, vihiṃsāsaññā.
(Ka) tattha katamā kāmasaññā? Kāmapaṭisaṃyuttā saññā sañjānanā sañjānitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "kāmasaññā".
(Kha) tattha katamā byāpādasaññā? Byāpādapaṭisaṃyuttā saññā sañjānanā sañjānitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "byāpādasaññā".
(Ga) tattha katamā vihiṃsāsaññā? Vihiṃsāpaṭisaṃyuttā saññā sañjānanā sañjānitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "vihiṃsāsaññā". Imā tisso akusalasaññā. ^2-911

##### (4) Tisso akusaladhātuyo ^2-17-4-4-0

Tattha katamā tisso akusaladhātuyo? Kāmadhātu, byāpādadhātu, vihiṃsādhātu.
Tattha katamā kāmadhātu? Kāmavitakko kāmadhātu. Byāpādavitakko byāpādadhātu. Vihiṃsāvitakko vihiṃsādhātu.
(Ka) tattha katamo kāmavitakko? Kāmapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "kāmavitakko".
(Kha) tattha katamo byāpādavitakko? Byāpādapaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "byāpādavitakko".
(Ga) tattha katamo vihiṃsāvitakko? Vihiṃsāpaṭisaṃyutto takko vitakko micchāsaṅkappo – ayaṃ vuccati "vihiṃsāvitakko". Imā tisso akusaladhātuyo. ^2-912

##### (5) Tīṇi duccaritāni ^2-17-4-5-0

Tattha katamāni tīṇi duccaritāni? Kāyaduccaritaṃ, vacīduccaritaṃ, manoduccaritaṃ.
(Ka) tattha katamaṃ kāyaduccaritaṃ? Pāṇātipāto, adinnādānaṃ, kāmesumicchācāro – idaṃ vuccati "kāyaduccaritaṃ".
(Kha) tattha katamaṃ vacīduccaritaṃ? Musāvādo, pisuṇā vācā, pharusā vācā, samphappalāpo – idaṃ vuccati "vacīduccaritaṃ".
(Ga) tattha katamaṃ manoduccaritaṃ? Abhijjhā, byāpādo, micchādiṭṭhi – idaṃ vuccati "manoduccaritaṃ".
(Ka-ga) tattha katamaṃ kāyaduccaritaṃ? Akusalaṃ kāyakammaṃ kāyaduccaritaṃ, akusalaṃ vacīkammaṃ vacīduccaritaṃ, akusalaṃ manokammaṃ manoduccaritaṃ.
Tattha katamaṃ akusalaṃ kāyakammaṃ? Akusalā kāyasañcetanā akusalaṃ kāyakammaṃ, akusalā vacīsañcetanā akusalaṃ vacīkammaṃ, akusalā manosañcetanā akusalaṃ manokammaṃ. Imāni tīṇi duccaritāni. ^2-913

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Dukaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-3|↑↑↑]]

Dukesu pubbakālaṃ kodhaṃ upanayhatīti aparakālakodho upanāho nāma, so upanandhanalakkhaṇo veraṃ appaṭinissajjanaraso, yena samannāgato puggalo ādittapūtialātaṃ viya jalateva. Makkho paraguṇamakkhanalakkhaṇo, gūthapaharaṇakaṃ gūtho viya attano kāraṇaṃ paṭhamataraṃ makkheti, parena sukatānaṃ kiriyānaṃ avacchādanapaccupaṭṭhāno; paḷāso yugaggāhalakkhaṇo paraguṇehi attano guṇānaṃ samakaraṇaraso. Māyā katapaṭicchādanalakkhaṇā, yāya samannāgato puggalo bhasmāpaṭicchanno viya aṅgāro, udakapaṭicchanno viya khāṇu hoti; sāṭheyyaṃ attano avijjamānaguṇappakāsanalakkhaṇaṃ. Anajjavena kāyavacīcittavaṅkatā kathitā, amaddavaṃ amudutāsaṅkhātā ujucittatā, akkhantiādayo khantiādipaṭipakkhato veditabbā. Ajjhattasaṃyojanaṃ kāmabhave bandhanaṃ, bahiddhāsaṃyojanaṃ rūpārūpabhavesu bandhanaṃ, yasmā kāmabhave cutipaṭisandhiyo bahukā tattha ālayopi bahu hoti; tattha ekekaṃ pañcavidhaṃ, tasmā orambhāgiyāni uddhambhāgiyānīti vuttāni.

### Tikaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-4|↑↑↑]]

Tikaniddese tīhi akusalamūlehi vaṭṭamūlasamudācāro kathito; vitakkanavasena vitakko, sañjānanavasena saññā, sabhāvaṭṭhena dhātu. Esanāsu kāmagavesanarāgo kāmesanā, bhavagavesanarāgo bhavesanā, diṭṭhigatikasammatassa brahmacariyassa gavesanā diṭṭhi brahmacariyesanā, tadekaṭṭhaṃ kammampi esanā eva. Vidhāti seyyādivasena vidahanato māno; tamoti vicikicchāsīsena avijjā, yāya atītaṃ anāgataṃ paccuppannaṃ addhānaṃ ārabbha kaṅkhati. Titthāyatanesu ‘pubbekatahetū’ti nigaṇṭhasamayo, ‘issaranimmānahetū’ti brāhmaṇasamayo, ‘ahetu appaccayā’ti ājīvakasamayo. Rāgādayo satte bandhanato kiñcanā, cittaṃ malinaṃ karaṇato malāni, pakkhalanapātahetuto visamāni, anudahanaṭṭhena aggī – daharabhikkhunī rāgena jhāyitvā kālamakāsi, manopadosikā devā dosena, khiḍḍāpadosikā mohena – kasaṭā nirojāti kasāvā. Assādadiṭṭhi sassatadiṭṭhi, attānudiṭṭhi sakkāyadiṭṭhi, micchādiṭṭhi ucchedadiṭṭhi. Upārambho paravajjānupassanalakkhaṇo, yena samannāgato puggalo tunnakāro sāṭakaṃ pasāretvā chiddameva olokento viya parassa aguṇesuyeva patiṭṭhāti; ayonisomanasikāro anicce niccanti dukkhe sukhanti pavatto uppathamanasikāralakkhaṇo, yassa vasena puggalo saccāni yāthāvato āvajjituṃ na sakkoti.

### Dukaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-3|↑↑↑]]

Dukaniddese upanāho veraṃ appaṭinissajjanaraso, makkho parena sukatānaṃ kiriyānaṃ avacchādanapaccupaṭṭhāno, māyā katapaṭicchādanalakkhaṇā, sāṭheyyaṃ avijjamānaguṇappakāsanalakkhaṇaṃ vuttaṃ. ### Tikaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-4|↑↑↑]]

Tikaniddese rāgadosamohā satte anudahanaṭṭhena aggī, cittaṃ malinaṃ karaṇato malāni, kasaṭā nirojāti kasāvā vuttā, upārambho ca paravajjānupassanalakkhaṇo, ayonisomanasikāro anicce niccanti uppathamanasikāralakkhaṇo. Tasmā yoginā akusalavitakkasaññādhātuyo kāyavacīmanoduccaritāni ca akaronto, tunnakāro viya parassa chiddameva olokentaṃ upārambhaṃ pahāya, ariyānaṃ dassanakāmo saddhammaṃ sotukāmo hutvā, ayonisomanasikāraṃ vajjetvā aniccaṃ aniccato dukkhaṃ dukkhato yoniso manasikarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

##### (10) Shamelessness and lack of moral dread ^2-17-3-10-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-900]]

(a) Therein, what is shamelessness? Not being ashamed of what one should be ashamed of, not being ashamed of taking up evil, unwholesome things — this is called "shamelessness".
(b) Therein, what is lack of moral dread? Not dreading what one should dread, not dreading taking up evil, unwholesome things — this is called "lack of moral dread". ^2-900

##### (11) Being hard to correct and evil friendship ^2-17-3-11-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-901]]

(a) Therein, what is being hard to correct? When one is addressed in accordance with the teaching: the being hard to speak to, the difficulty in correcting, the state of being hard to correct, contrary grasping, delight in opposition, disrespect, disrespectfulness, lack of reverence, lack of deference — this is called "being hard to correct".
(b) Therein, what is evil friendship? Those persons who are without confidence, of bad conduct, of little learning, stingy, and without wisdom — the resorting to them, the frequenting, the close frequenting, the associating with them, the fully associating, the devotion, the full devotion, the being inclined towards them — this is called "evil friendship". ^2-901

##### (12) Crookedness and harshness ^2-17-3-12-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-902]]

(a) Therein, what is crookedness? The crookedness, being crooked, the deviousness, the twistedness, the windingness — this is called "crookedness".
(b) Therein, what is harshness? The unpliability, harshness; hardness, roughness, rigidity, stiffness, unyieldingness of mind, unpliability — this is called "harshness". ^2-902

##### (13) Impatience and immoderation ^2-17-3-13-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-903]]

(a) Therein, what is impatience? The impatience, being impatient, the not enduring, the fierceness, the rudeness, the mind's dissatisfaction — this is called "impatience".
(b) Therein, what is immoderation? Transgression in body, transgression in speech, transgression in body and speech — this is called "immoderation". All bad conduct is immoderation. ^2-903

##### (14) Rough speech and lack of hospitality ^2-17-3-14-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-904]]

(a) Therein, what is rough speech? Whatever speech is prickly, rough, bitter to others, offensive to others, bordering on anger, not conducive to concentration — being a speaker of such speech; the ungentleness of speech therein, the unkindness of speech, the harshness of speech — this is called "rough speech".
(b) Therein, what is lack of hospitality? There are two kinds of hospitality: hospitality with material things and hospitality with the teaching. Here someone shows no hospitality, whether with material things or with the teaching — this is called "lack of hospitality". ^2-904

##### (15) Not guarding the doors of the faculties and not knowing moderation in food ^2-17-3-15-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-905]]

(a) Therein, what is not guarding the doors of the faculties? Here someone, having seen a sight with the eye, grasps at its signs and grasps at its details. Since, if he dwelt with the eye faculty unrestrained, covetousness and grief, evil, unwholesome things, might flow in upon him, he does not practise for its restraint, he does not guard the eye faculty, he does not undertake restraint of the eye faculty. Having heard a sound with the ear… having smelled a smell with the nose… having tasted a taste with the tongue… having touched a tangible thing with the body… having known a mental object with the mind, he grasps at its signs and grasps at its details. Since, if he dwelt with the mind faculty unrestrained, covetousness and grief, evil, unwholesome things, might flow in upon him, he does not practise for its restraint, he does not guard the mind faculty, he does not undertake restraint of the mind faculty. The lack of guarding, lack of protection, lack of watching over, lack of restraint of these six faculties — this is called "not guarding the doors of the faculties".
(b) Therein, what is not knowing moderation in food? Here someone takes food without reflection and unwisely — for amusement, for intoxication, for adornment, for beautification. The lack of contentment there, the lack of moderation, the lack of reflection with regard to food — this is called "not knowing moderation in food". ^2-905

##### (16) Forgetfulness and lack of clear comprehension ^2-17-3-16-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-906]]

(a) Therein, what is forgetfulness? The absence of mindfulness, absence of recollection, absence of remembering; the absence of mindfulness, the not bearing in mind, the floating away, the forgetting — this is called "forgetfulness".
(b) Therein, what is lack of clear comprehension? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "lack of clear comprehension". ^2-906

##### (17) Failure in virtue and failure in view ^2-17-3-17-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-907]]

(a) Therein, what is failure in virtue? Transgression in body, transgression in speech, transgression in body and speech — this is called "failure in virtue". All bad conduct is failure in virtue.
(b) Therein, what is failure in view? "There is nothing given, there is nothing offered… there are in the world no ascetics and brahmins of right conduct and right practice who declare this world and the other world, having realised them for themselves by direct knowledge" — whatever view of this kind there is, resort to views… the distorted grip — this is called "failure in view". All wrong view is failure in view. ^2-907

##### (18) The internal fetter and the external fetter ^2-17-3-18-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-908]]

(a) Therein, what is the internal fetter? The five lower fetters are the internal fetter. (b) The five higher fetters are the external fetter.
The Twos. ^2-908

#### The Exposition of the Threes ^2-17-4-0

##### (1) The three unwholesome roots ^2-17-4-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-909]]

Therein, what are the three unwholesome roots? Greed, hate, delusion.
(a) Therein, what is greed? The lust, passion, compliance, attraction, delight, delight-and-lust, passion of the mind; wishing, infatuation, holding fast; greed, thorough greed; attachment, the mire, allure, deception; the begetter, the generator, the seamstress, the netter, the river, the clinging vine, the stream, the spreading; the toiler, the companion, the aspiration, what leads on to existence; the forest, the jungle; intimacy, affection, regard, kinship; wishing, the act of wishing, the state of wishing; wishing for sights, wishing for sounds, wishing for smells, wishing for tastes, wishing for tangible things, wishing for gain, wishing for wealth, wishing for children, wishing for life; muttering desire, excessive muttering desire, the act of muttering desire, the state of muttering desire; greediness, being greedy, the state of greediness; itching desire, the wish for what is nice; unrighteous lust, uneven greed; longing, the act of longing, aspiration, envy of what is good, full aspiration; craving for sense pleasures, craving for existence, craving for non-existence, craving for material form, craving for the immaterial, craving for cessation; craving for sounds, craving for sights, craving for smells, craving for tastes, craving for tangible things, craving for mental objects; the flood, the yoke, the tie, the clinging; the obstruction, the hindrance, the covering, the bond; the corruption, the underlying tendency, the obsession; the creeper, the meanness; the root of suffering, the source of suffering, the origin of suffering; Māra's snare, Māra's hook, Māra's range; the river of craving, the net of craving, the leash of craving, the ocean of craving; covetousness, greed, the unwholesome root — this is called "greed".
(b) Therein, what is hate? Resentment arises: "He has done me harm"; resentment arises: "He is doing me harm"; resentment arises: "He will do me harm"; "He has done harm… is doing harm… will do harm to one dear and pleasing to me" — resentment arises; "He has done good… is doing good… will do good to one disliked and displeasing to me" — resentment arises; or resentment arises groundlessly. Whatever resentment of the mind of this kind there is, repugnance, resistance, opposition, anger, being angered, great anger; hate, hating, great hating; the mind's malice, mental hatred; wrath, being wrathful, wrathfulness; hate, hating, state of hating; malice, being malicious, state of being malicious; opposition, hostility, fierceness, rudeness, the mind's dissatisfaction — this is called "hate".
(c) Therein, what is delusion? Not knowing suffering, not knowing the origin of suffering, not knowing the cessation of suffering, not knowing the way leading to the cessation of suffering; not knowing the past, not knowing the future, not knowing the past and the future; not knowing things that are dependently arisen through specific conditionality. Whatever lack of knowledge of this kind there is, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "delusion". These are the three unwholesome roots. ^2-909

##### (2) The three unwholesome thoughts ^2-17-4-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-910]]

Therein, what are the three unwholesome thoughts? The thought of sense pleasure, the thought of ill will, the thought of cruelty.
(a) Therein, what is the thought of sense pleasure? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with sense pleasure — this is called "the thought of sense pleasure".
(b) Therein, what is the thought of ill will? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with ill will — this is called "the thought of ill will".
(c) Therein, what is the thought of cruelty? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with cruelty — this is called "the thought of cruelty". These are the three unwholesome thoughts. ^2-910

##### (3) The three unwholesome perceptions ^2-17-4-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-911]]

Therein, what are the three unwholesome perceptions? The perception of sense pleasure, the perception of ill will, the perception of cruelty.
(a) Therein, what is the perception of sense pleasure? The perception, perceiving, state of having perceived connected with sense pleasure — this is called "the perception of sense pleasure".
(b) Therein, what is the perception of ill will? The perception, perceiving, state of having perceived connected with ill will — this is called "the perception of ill will".
(c) Therein, what is the perception of cruelty? The perception, perceiving, state of having perceived connected with cruelty — this is called "the perception of cruelty". These are the three unwholesome perceptions. ^2-911

##### (4) The three unwholesome elements ^2-17-4-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-912]]

Therein, what are the three unwholesome elements? The element of sense pleasure, the element of ill will, the element of cruelty.
Therein, what is the element of sense pleasure? The thought of sense pleasure is the element of sense pleasure. The thought of ill will is the element of ill will. The thought of cruelty is the element of cruelty.
(a) Therein, what is the thought of sense pleasure? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with sense pleasure — this is called "the thought of sense pleasure".
(b) Therein, what is the thought of ill will? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with ill will — this is called "the thought of ill will".
(c) Therein, what is the thought of cruelty? The thinking, thought, wrong intention connected with cruelty — this is called "the thought of cruelty". These are the three unwholesome elements. ^2-912

##### (5) The three kinds of misconduct ^2-17-4-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-913]]

Therein, what are the three kinds of misconduct? Bodily misconduct, verbal misconduct, mental misconduct.
(a) Therein, what is bodily misconduct? Killing living beings, taking what is not given, sexual misconduct — this is called "bodily misconduct".
(b) Therein, what is verbal misconduct? False speech, divisive speech, harsh speech, idle chatter — this is called "verbal misconduct".
(c) Therein, what is mental misconduct? Covetousness, ill will, wrong view — this is called "mental misconduct".
(a–c) Therein, what is bodily misconduct? Unwholesome bodily action is bodily misconduct; unwholesome verbal action is verbal misconduct; unwholesome mental action is mental misconduct.
Therein, what is unwholesome bodily action? Unwholesome bodily intention is unwholesome bodily action; unwholesome verbal intention is unwholesome verbal action; unwholesome mental intention is unwholesome mental action. These are the three kinds of misconduct. ^2-913
