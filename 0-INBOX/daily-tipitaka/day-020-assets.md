304. Katamo tasmiṃ samaye sammāsamādhi hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye cittassa ṭhiti saṇṭhiti avaṭṭhiti avisāhāro avikkhepo avisāhaṭamānasatā samatho samādhindriyaṃ samādhibalaṃ sammāsamādhi samādhisambojjhaṅgo maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammāsamādhi hoti. ^1-304

305. Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye saddhābalaṃ hoti? Yā tasmiṃ samaye saddhā saddahanā okappanā abhippasādo saddhā saddhindriyaṃ saddhābalaṃ – idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye saddhābalaṃ hoti. ^1-305

306. Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye vīriyabalaṃ hoti? #### Suddhikapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-2-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttarajhānaṃ paṭipadāya saddhiṃyeva garuṃ katvā dassetuṃ suddhikapaṭipadā vuttā – suddhikanayaṃ pahāya paṭipadābhedameva ādito dassitaṃ. Tattha dukkhapaṭipadā khippābhiññā dandhābhiññā, sukhapaṭipadā khippābhiññā dandhābhiññāti catubbidhā paṭipadā: yo āditova kilese vikkhambhento dukkhena sasaṅkhārena sappayogena kilamanto vikkhambheti, tassa dukkhā paṭipadā; yo pana vikkhambhita kileso vipassanāparivāsaṃ vasanto cirena maggaṃ pāpuṇāti, tassa dandhābhiññā hoti. Catukkapañcakajhānanayena ca dve nayā vibhattā. Lokuttarajhānassa viseso: phassādīsu kāmāvacarakusalato adhikā dhammā – anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, sammāvācā, sammākammanto, sammāājīvoti cattāro; tena idha maggaṭṭhaṅgiko hoti. Suññatavāro pākatikoyeva.

### Suddhikapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-1-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Suddhikapaṭipadāya lokuttarapaṭhamajjhānādīnaṃ maggacittāni vibhajiyanti. Sammādiṭṭhiyā diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānaṃ katvā, aṭṭhaṅgikamaggassa bhāvanāya, dassanabhūmiyaṃ pākaṭaṃ sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo cāti maggaṭṭhakaṃ paripūreti. Evaṃ suddhikā paṭipadā lokuttarakusalassa ādhāro.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

(Verses 304–334 continue the Padabhājanī definitions inside Pure Practice / Suddhikapaṭipadā. v.304 closes the eightfold path (Right Concentration), 305–309 are the five powers, 310–311 the powers of shame and fear of wrongdoing, 312–314 the three wholesome roots, 315–319 the cetasika repeats (non-covetousness, non-ill-will, right view, shame, fear of wrongdoing), 320–331 the six pairs of mental-body / consciousness qualities (tranquillity, lightness, malleability, wieldiness, proficiency, rectitude), 332–333 mindfulness and clear comprehension, and 334 calm / tranquility.)

**v. 304.** What on that occasion is Right Concentration? At that time, the standing, firm standing, steadfast standing of the mind; non-distraction, non-scattering, non-distractedness of mind, tranquility; the concentration faculty, the power of concentration, Right Concentration, the enlightenment factor of concentration, a path factor, included in the path — this is the Right Concentration that there then is. ^1-304

(vv. 305–333 — see the en-dhammasangani-ai.md translation file for the per-verse definitions of each power, root, repeated cetasika, six-pair quality, and mindfulness/clear-comprehension entry.)

**v. 334.** What is tranquility at that time? That which, at that time, is the stability, steadfastness, non-wavering, non-distraction, non-scatteredness of mind, non-scattered mental state; tranquility, the faculty of concentration, the power of concentration, right concentration, the enlightenment factor of concentration, a path factor, included in the path — this is tranquility at that time. ^1-334
