## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Sampayuttacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Sampayuttacatukkepi "avijjāpaccayā"ti ettāvatā sahajātādipaccayavasena saṅkhārassa avijjāpaccayataṃ dassetvā, puna "avijjāsampayutto"ti sampayuttapaccayatā dassitā; sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Yasmā pana arūpīnaṃ dhammānaṃ rūpadhammehi sampayogo natthi, tasmā "viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpa"ntiādīsu tatiyacatutthavārapadesu "viññāṇasampayuttaṃ nāma"ntiādinā nayena yaṃ labbhati, tadeva gahitanti esa sampayuttacatukke viseso. Yathā cettha, evaṃ "etenevānusārena, sabbametaṃ nayaṃ ito; viseso yo ca taṃ jaññā, catukkesu paresupī"ti vuttanayena sesacatukkesupi viseso veditabbo.

#### Akusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Akusalaniddese yasmā idha mātikā "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"ti akusaladhammavasena nikkhittā, tasmā nikkhepapaṭipāṭiyāva paṭhamaṃ kusalaṃ anāmasitvā "katame dhammā akusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Yasmā ekacittakkhaṇe taṇhāya ca kāmupādānassa ca sambhavo natthi, tasmā yaṃ ettha taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ labbhati, tadeva dassetuṃ "diṭṭhi diṭṭhigata"ntiādi vuttaṃ. Bhavaniddese ca upādānassa saṅkhārakkhandhe saṅgahitattā "ṭhapetvā upādānaṃ vedanākkhandho saññākkhandho saṅkhārakkhandho viññāṇakkhandho"ti vuttaṃ; itarathā hi upādānasseva upādānapaccayattaṃ āpajjeyya. Jātiādiniddesesu ca arūpadhammānaṃ jātiādibhāvato "khaṇḍiccaṃ pāliccaṃ valittacatā cuti cavanatā"ti na vuttaṃ. Dutiyavāre "ṭhapetvā phassa"nti phassassa nāmato nīharaṇatthaṃ vuttaṃ. Tatiyavāre cittasamuṭṭhānarūpe pavattamāne tenupatthaddhānaṃ cakkhāyatanādīnaṃ upacitattā "cakkhāyatanassa upacayo"tiādi vuttaṃ. Catutthavāre mahābhūtarūpapaccayā cakkhāyatanādīni, hadayarūpapaccayā chaṭṭhāyatanaṃ, nāmapaccayā ca pacchājātasahajātādivasena sabbāni pavattantīti "nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ cakkhāyatana"ntiādi vuttaṃ. Sesākusalacittesu ca diṭṭhivippayuttesu taṇhāpaccayā upādānassa abhāvā upādānaṭṭhāne daḷhanipātinā adhimokkhena padaṃ pūritaṃ; domanassasahagatesu vedanāpaccayā taṇhāya abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhāne balavakilesena paṭighena; vicikicchāsampayutte sanniṭṭhānābhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne vicikicchāya, upādānaṭṭhānaṃ pana parihīnameva; uddhaccasampayutte taṇhāṭṭhāne uddhaccena, upādānaṭṭhāne ca adhimokkheneva. Adhimuccanavasena hi adhimokkho; adhimuccati vā tena ārammaṇe cittaṃ nibbicikicchatāya sanniṭṭhānaṃ gacchatītipi adhimokkho.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Sampayuttacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Sampayuttacatukke "avijjāsampayutto"ti sampayuttapaccayatā dassitā. Yasmā ca arūpīnaṃ dhammānaṃ rūpadhammehi sampayogo natthi, tasmā tatiyacatutthavāresu "viññāṇasampayuttaṃ nāma"nti yaṃ labbhati tadeva gahitaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "aññā me avijjā, aññaṃ cittaṃ, visuṃ visuṃ ṭhitā"ti visaṃyogasaññaṃ pahāya, ekuppādaekanirodhaekavatthukaekārammaṇabhāvena sampayogadassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "sampayuttāva ete dhammā ekato uppajjanti ekato nirujjhantī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Akusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Akusalaniddese akusalacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Ekacittakkhaṇe taṇhāya ca kāmupādānassa ca asambhavā diṭṭhivasena upādānaṃ dassitaṃ; diṭṭhivippayuttesu upādānaṭṭhāne adhimokkhena, domanassasahagatesu taṇhāṭṭhāne paṭighena, vicikicchāsampayutte vicikicchāya, uddhaccasampayutte uddhaccena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Adhimuccati tena ārammaṇe cittaṃ nibbicikicchatāya sanniṭṭhānaṃ gacchatīti adhimokkho. Tasmā yoginā "akasmā me idaṃ akusalacittaṃ uppannaṃ, ahetuka"nti sammohaṃ pahāya, ekamekasmiṃ akusalacitte avijjāsaṅkhārādīnaṃ paṭipāṭidassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "sahetukaṃ idaṃ paccayasamuppannaṃ, paccaye chinne nirujjhatī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `AI_translation/english/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

265. Therein, what is ignorance? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "ignorance".
Therein, what is the formation that has ignorance as condition, associated with ignorance? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation that has ignorance as condition, associated with ignorance".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition, associated with the formation? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition, associated with the formation".
Therein, what is the mentality that has consciousness as condition, associated with consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations — this is called "the mentality that has consciousness as condition, associated with consciousness".
Therein, what is the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition, associated with mentality? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition, associated with mentality".
Therein, what is the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition, associated with the sixth sense-field? The contact, touching, being in contact, state of being in contact — this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition, associated with the sixth sense-field".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition, associated with contact? Mental comfort, mental happiness, the comfortable happy experience born of mind-contact, the comfortable happy feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition, associated with contact".
Therein, what is the craving that has feeling as condition, associated with feeling? The lust, passion… passion of the mind — this is called "the craving that has feeling as condition, associated with feeling".
Therein, what is the clinging that has craving as condition, associated with craving? The view, resort to views… the sectarian domain, the distorted grip — this is called "the clinging that has craving as condition, associated with craving"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-265
