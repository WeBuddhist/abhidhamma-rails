## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Appamaññā)

[[#^toc-13-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye padabhājanīyaṃ dassento, yasmā idaṃ kammaṭṭhānaṃ dosacaritassa sappāyaṃ, tasmā mettāya vatthubhūtaṃ puggalaṃ dassetuṃ "seyyathāpi nāma ekaṃ puggalaṃ piyaṃ manāpa"nti vuttaṃ. Piyatāya hi byāpādassa pahānaṃ hoti, manāpatāya udāsīnatā na saṇṭhāti hirottappañca paccupaṭṭhāti, tato hirottappānupālitā mettā na parihāyati; evaṃ appanāppattāya vasībhāvaṃ upagatāya mettāya majjhattaverisaṅkhātepi sabbe satte anukkamena pharati. Karuṇāya pana duggataṃ durupetaṃ, muditāya jotibhāve ṭhitaṃ piyaṃ manāpaṃ, upekkhāya neva manāpaṃ na amanāpaṃ puggalaṃ vatthuṃ katvā dassitaṃ. Sesaṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe vuttaṃ, bhāvanāvidhānampi visuddhimagge vitthārato kathitaṃ.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Appamaññā)

[[#^toc-13-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye idaṃ appamaññākammaṭṭhānaṃ dosacaritassa sappāyaṃ vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "ayaṃ paro"ti byāpādagāhaṃ pahāya, piyaṃ manāpaṃ puggalaṃ ādiṃ katvā anukkamena majjhattaverisaṅkhāte sabbe satte mettākaruṇāmuditāupekkhāhi pharitvā, hirottappānupālitaṃ averaṃ kusalaṃ upasampādetvā, udāsīnatāya asaṇṭhānena parihānābhāvena ca cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Appamaññā)

[[#^toc-13-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye appamaññāyo kusalatopi vipākatopi kiriyatopi cittuppādakaṇḍe bhājitanayeneva bhājitā. Tasmā yoginā tāsaṃ kusalākusalavibhāgaṃ tattha vuttanayeneva ñatvā, akusalaṃ pahāya kusalaṃ appamaññaṃ upasampādetvā, cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

"With a mind accompanied by equanimity": therein, what is equanimity? The equanimity towards beings, being equanimous, the state of equanimity, the liberation of mind through equanimity — this is called "equanimity".
Therein, what is the mind? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the mind". This mind is accompanied by this equanimity, arisen together with it, conjoined with it, associated with it. Therefore it is said, "with a mind accompanied by equanimity". ^2-679

Therein, what is loving-kindness? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the path for rebirth in the fine-material sphere — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption accompanied by loving-kindness — on that occasion there is contact… there is non-distraction. These things are wholesome. The remaining things are associated with loving-kindness.
