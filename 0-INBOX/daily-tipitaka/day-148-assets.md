## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Tikaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-5|↑↑↑]]

Tividhena ñāṇavatthuniddese yogavihitesu kammāyatanesu sippāyatanesu vijjāṭṭhānesu kammassakataṃ vā saccānulomikaṃ vā parato assutvā sayameva cintento paṭilabhati, ayaṃ cintāmayā paññā, sā pana abhiññātānaṃ mahāsattānameva uppajjati; parato sutvā paṭiladdhā sutamayā; samāpannassa antosamāpattiyaṃ pavattā bhāvanāmayā. Dānacetanāsīlacetanāsampayuttā paññā pubbacetanā muñcacetanā aparacetanāti tividhena uppajjamānā dānamayā sīlamayā nāma. Pañcasīladasasīlaṃ upādāya pātimokkhasaṃvaro adhisīlaṃ, vaṭṭapādikā aṭṭha samāpattiyo upādāya vipassanāpādikā adhicittaṃ, kammassakatapaññaṃ upādāya vipassanāmaggaphalapaññā adhipaññā, yā uppanneyeva tathāgate uppajjanti. Akusalānaṃ anuppattippahānesu kusalānañca uppattiṭṭhitīsu paññā āyakosallaṃ, tabbiparītā apāyakosallaṃ, accāyikakicce vā bhaye vā tatrūpāyā paññā upāyakosallaṃ.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Tikaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-5|↑↑↑]]

Tikaniddese paññavā ‘mayhaṃ evaṃ manasikaroto anuppannā akusalā dhammā uppajjanti, uppannā pavaḍḍhantī’ti pajānitvā anuppannānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ uppajjituṃ na deti, uppanne pajahati; anuppanne kusale uppādeti, uppanne bhāvanāpāripūriṃ pāpeti. Dānamayā sīlamayā ca paññā ‘dānaṃ dassāmi, sīlaṃ pūressāmī’ti cintentassa, dentassa pūrentassa, datvā pūretvā paccavekkhantassa pubbamuñcaaparacetanāvasena tividhena uppajjati; pañcasīladasasīlaṃ upādāya pātimokkhasaṃvaro adhisīlaṃ, kammassakatapaññaṃ upādāya vipassanāmaggaphalapaññā adhipaññā. Tasmā yoginā "pañcasīlamattena ala"nti abhimānaṃ pahāya, apāyakosallena akusalaṃ uppajjituṃ adatvā, āyakosallena dānasīlapātimokkhasaṃvaraṃ pubbamuñcaaparacetanāhi upasampādetvā, adhicittādhipaññāsannissitāya vipassanāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

(1a) Therein, what is wisdom made of reflection? In the fields of work arranged with skill, or the fields of craft arranged with skill, or the branches of science arranged with skill, whether concerning ownership of action or in conformity with the truths — "material form is impermanent", or "feeling… perception… formations… consciousness is impermanent" — whatever conforming acceptance, view, preference, understanding, consideration, acceptance upon reflection on things of such a kind one obtains without hearing it from another: this is called "wisdom made of reflection".
(1b) Therein, what is wisdom made of learning? In the fields of work arranged with skill, or the fields of craft arranged with skill, or the branches of science arranged with skill, whether concerning ownership of action or in conformity with the truths — "material form is impermanent", or "feeling… perception… formations… consciousness is impermanent" — whatever conforming acceptance, view, preference, understanding, consideration, acceptance upon reflection on things of such a kind one obtains by hearing it from another: this is called "wisdom made of learning".
(1c) All wisdom of one who has entered an attainment is wisdom made of meditative development. ^2-768

(2a) Therein, what is wisdom made of giving? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view that arises concerning giving, in relation to giving: this is called "wisdom made of giving".
(2b) Therein, what is wisdom made of virtue? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view that arises concerning virtue, in relation to virtue: this is called "wisdom made of virtue".
(2c) All wisdom of one who has entered an attainment is wisdom made of meditative development. ^2-769

(3b) Therein, what is wisdom concerning the higher mind? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view that arises in one entering a fine-material-sphere or immaterial-sphere attainment: this is called "wisdom concerning the higher mind".
(3c) Therein, what is wisdom concerning higher wisdom? Wisdom in the four paths and the four fruits: this is called "wisdom concerning higher wisdom". ^2-770

(4b) Therein, what is skill in loss? "When attending to these things, wholesome things not yet arisen do not arise, and wholesome things that have arisen cease. Or, when attending to these things, unwholesome things not yet arisen arise, and unwholesome things that have arisen lead to increase and expansion" — the wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view therein: this is called "skill in loss".
(4c) All wisdom about the means in each case is skill in means. ^2-771
