## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

### Lokuttarakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Evaṃ bhavattayasampattinibbattakaṃ kusalaṃ dassetvā idāni sabbabhavasamatikkamanāya lokuttarakusalaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. 'Lokaṃ taratīti lokuttaraṃ' – vaṭṭato niyyātīti niyyānikaṃ apacayagāmiṃ ca. Jhānaṃ bhāvetīti ekacittakkhaṇikaṃ appanājhānaṃ uppādeti vaḍḍheti. Diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānāya paṭhamāya bhūmiyā pattiyāti sotāpattiphalaṃ, samucchedavivekavasena viviccā. Paṭipadāya nānattaṃ dassetuṃ dukkhapaṭipadā dandhābhiññā, sukhāpaṭipadā khippābhiññāoti vuttaṃ. Phassādīsu anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvoti cattāro dhammā adhikā – maggaṭṭhakaṃ hoti. Etaṃ lokuttarakusalaṃ tebhūmakakusalaṃ upādāya apacayagāmī.

#### Suddhikapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-2-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttarajhānaṃ paṭipadāya saddhiṃyeva garuṃ katvā dassetuṃ suddhikapaṭipadā vuttā – suddhikanayaṃ pahāya paṭipadābhedameva ādito dassitaṃ. Tattha dukkhapaṭipadā khippābhiññā dandhābhiññā, sukhapaṭipadā khippābhiññā dandhābhiññāti catubbidhā paṭipadā: yo āditova kilese vikkhambhento dukkhena sasaṅkhārena sappayogena kilamanto vikkhambheti, tassa dukkhā paṭipadā; yo pana vikkhambhita kileso vipassanāparivāsaṃ vasanto cirena maggaṃ pāpuṇāti, tassa dandhābhiññā hoti. Catukkapañcakajhānanayena ca dve nayā vibhattā. Lokuttarajhānassa viseso: phassādīsu kāmāvacarakusalato adhikā dhammā – anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, sammāvācā, sammākammanto, sammāājīvoti cattāro; tena idha maggaṭṭhaṅgiko hoti. Suññatavāro pākatikoyeva.

#### Suññataṃ

[[#^toc-2-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttaramaggo 'suññata'nti nāmaṃ āgamanato saguṇato ārammaṇatoti tīhi kāraṇehi labhati. Abhidhammapariyāyena pana idha āgamanatoyeva nāmaṃ labhati – anattato abhinivisitvā anattato saṅkhāre passantassa vuṭṭhānagāminī vipassanā āgamanīyaṭṭhāne ṭhatvā attano maggassa 'suññata'nti nāmaṃ deti. Saguṇato nāmaṃ labhati yasmā so rāgādīhi suñño; ārammaṇato labhati yasmā nibbānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā uppanno, rāgādīhi suññattā ca nibbānaṃ suññatanti vuccati. Suttantapariyāyena ubhayampi labhati, abhidhammo pana nippariyāyadesanā āgamanameva dhuraṃ katvā dassetvā tīni vimokkhanāmānaṃ animittamaggassa abhāvaṃ ca dīpeti. Catukkapañcakajhānāni paṭhamato pañcamaṃ yāva suddhikasuññatavasena dassitāni.

#### Suññatamūlakapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-2-5-3|↑↑↑]]

Suññatañca catubbidhāya paṭipadāya ca missaṃ lokuttarajhānaṃ dassetuṃ suññatapaṭipadā vuttā. Puggalajjhāsayena ceva desanāvilāsena ca evaṃ suddhikasuññatāya suññatapaṭipadāya ca pabhedena bhājetvā dasitaṃ; yesaṃ sattānaṃ paṭipadābhedo sappāyo, tesaṃ suññatañceva paṭipadāñca missayitvā lokuttaraṃ deseti. Catukkapañcakajhānāni catasso paṭipadā ca missayitvā dasa nayā nibbattanti – ekaṃ koṭṭhāsaṃ catukkapañcakavasena dve nayā, pañcasu koṭṭhāsesu dasa nayā bhājitā.

#### Appaṇihitaṃ

[[#^toc-2-5-4|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttaramaggo 'appaṇihita'nti nāmaṃ āgamanato saguṇato ārammaṇatoti tīheva kāraṇehi labhati. Abhidhammapariyāyena pana āgamanatoyeva nāmaṃ labhati – dukkhato abhinivisitvā dukkhato saṅkhāre passantassa vuṭṭhānagāminī vipassanā tebhūmakasaṅkhāresu paṇidhiṃ sosetvā pariyādiyitvā vissajjeti; ayaṃ vipassanā appaṇihitā nāma hoti, sā āgamanīyaṭṭhāne ṭhatvā attano maggassa 'appaṇihita'nti nāmaṃ deti. Saguṇato labhati yasmā tattha rāgadosamohapaṇidhayo natthi; ārammaṇato labhati yasmā nibbānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā uppanno, tesaṃ paṇidhīnaṃ abhāvā ca nibbānaṃ appaṇihitanti vuccati. Animittamaggo abhidhammapariyāyena āgamanato nāmaṃ dātuṃ na sakkotīti na gahito. Catukkapañcakajhānāni suddhikaappaṇihitavasena dassitāni.

#### Appaṇihitamūlakapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-2-5-5|↑↑↑]]

Appaṇihitañca catubbidhāya paṭipadāya ca missaṃ lokuttarajhānaṃ dassetuṃ appaṇihitapaṭipadā vuttā. Suññatapaṭipadāsadiseneva puggalajjhāsayena ceva desanāvilāsena ca suddhikaappaṇihitāya appaṇihitapaṭipadāya ca pabhedena bhājetvā dasitaṃ. Catukkapañcakajhānāni catasso paṭipadā ca missayitvā dasa nayā nibbattanti – pañcasu koṭṭhāsesu sabbesupīti ekena koṭṭhāsena dasa nayā bhājitā. Evaṃ suddhikasuññatāya suññatapaṭipadāya suddhikaappaṇihitāya appaṇihitapaṭipadāyāti pañcasu koṭṭhāsesu puggalajjhāsayavasena desanāvilāsena ca lokuttarajhānaṃ bhājetvā dassesi dhammarājā.

### Suddhikapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-1-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Suddhikapaṭipadāya lokuttarapaṭhamajjhānādīnaṃ maggacittāni vibhajiyanti. Sammādiṭṭhiyā diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānaṃ katvā, aṭṭhaṅgikamaggassa bhāvanāya, dassanabhūmiyaṃ pākaṭaṃ sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo cāti maggaṭṭhakaṃ paripūreti. Evaṃ suddhikā paṭipadā lokuttarakusalassa ādhāro.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-7-1-0]]

### Suññataṃ

[[#^toc-1-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Suññataṃ lokuttare suññatasamādhi vibhajiyati. Sabbāsaṅkhārānaṃ attato suññatāti ñatvā maggaṃ bhāveti; suññatārammaṇo lokuttarasamādhi nibbānaṃ sacchikaroti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-7-1-0]]

### Suññatamūlakapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-1-5-3|↑↑↑]]

Suññatamūlakapaṭipadāya lokuttaramaggaṃ suññatārammaṇaṃ kattvā suddhikapaṭipadāhi saṅgahītaṃ bhāveti. Evaṃ suññatānupassanāmūlakaṃ maggabhāvanaṃ bhāvetvā lokuttarakusalasampatti paripūreti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-7-2-0]]

### Appaṇihitaṃ

[[#^toc-1-5-4|↑↑↑]]

Appaṇihitaṃ lokuttare appaṇihitasamādhi vibhajiyati. Taṇhāpraṇidhirahitena cetasā nibbānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā maggaṃ bhāveti; appaṇihitasamādhi taṇhāprahīne citte nibbānasacchikiriyāya bhavati.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-7-1-0]]

### Appaṇihitamūlakapaṭipadā

[[#^toc-1-5-5|↑↑↑]]

Appaṇihitamūlakapaṭipadāya lokuttaramaggaṃ appaṇihitaṃ ārammaṇaṃ kattvā bhāveti. Evaṃ appaṇihitānupassanāmūlakaṃ maggabhāvanaṃ bhāvetvā lokuttarakusalasampatti paripūreti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-7-2-0]]

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

What is insight at that time? That which, at that time, is wisdom, understanding, investigation, thorough investigation, investigation of phenomena, discernment, observation, repeated observation, sagacity, skill, proficiency, analysis, thought, examination, insight, intelligence, leadership, insight, clear comprehension, goad; wisdom, the faculty of wisdom, the power of wisdom, the weapon of wisdom, the palace of wisdom, the light of wisdom, the radiance of wisdom, the lamp of wisdom, the gem of wisdom, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, the enlightenment factor of investigation of phenomena, a path factor, included in the path – this is insight at that time. ^1-335

…

What are wholesome states? When one develops supramundane jhána, leading to liberation, tending to the destruction (of defilements), for the abandoning of wrong views, for the attainment of the first stage (of enlightenment), having secluded oneself from sense pleasures...pe... he enters and abides in the first jhána, with painful practice, swift comprehension, and which is undirected—at that time, contact arises...pe... non-distraction arises...pe... these states are wholesome. ^1-353
