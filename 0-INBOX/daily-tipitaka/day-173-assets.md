##### Upādinnattikaṃ ^2-18-10-4-0

Pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati upādinnupādāniyā, kati anupādinnupādāniyā, kati anupādinnaanupādāniyā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati upādinnupādāniyā, kati anupādinnupādāniyā, kati anupādinnaanupādāniyā?
Rūpakkhandho siyā upādinnupādāniyo, siyā anupādinnupādāniyo 4. Cattāro khandhā siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā.
Pañcāyatanā upādinnupādāniyā. Saddāyatanaṃ anupādinnupādāniyaṃ. Cattāro āyatanā siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā. Dvāyatanā siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā.
Dasa dhātuyo upādinnupādāniyā. Saddadhātu anupādinnupādāniyā. Pañca dhātuyo siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā. Dve dhātuyo siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā.
Samudayasaccaṃ anupādinnupādāniyaṃ. Dve saccā anupādinnaanupādāniyā. Dukkhasaccaṃ siyā upādinnupādāniyaṃ, siyā anupādinnupādāniyaṃ.
Navindriyā upādinnupādāniyā. Domanassindriyaṃ anupādinnupādāniyaṃ. Tīṇindriyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā. Navindriyā siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā. Tayo akusalahetū anupādinnupādāniyā. Tayo kusalahetū siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā. Tayo abyākatahetū siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā.
Kabaḷīkāro āhāro siyā upādinnupādāniyo, siyā anupādinnupādāniyo. Tayo āhārā siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā.
Pañca phassā upādinnupādāniyā. Manodhātusamphasso siyā upādinnupādāniyo, siyā anupādinnupādāniyo. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphasso siyā upādinnupādāniyo, siyā anupādinnupādāniyo, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyo. Pañca vedanā… pañca saññā… pañca cetanā… pañca cittā upādinnupādāniyā. Manodhātu siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā. Manoviññāṇadhātu siyā upādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnupādāniyā, siyā anupādinnaanupādāniyā. ^2-1041

##### Vitakkattikaṃ ^2-18-10-5-0

Pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati savitakkasavicārā, kati avitakkavicāramattā, kati avitakkaavicārā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati savitakkasavicārā, kati avitakkavicāramattā, kati avitakkaavicārā?
Rūpakkhandho avitakkaavicāro. Tayo khandhā siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā. Saṅkhārakkhandho siyā savitakkasavicāro, siyā avitakkavicāramatto, siyā avitakkaavicāro siyā na vattabbo savitakkasavicārotipi, avitakkavicāramattotipi, avitakkaavicārotipi.
Dasāyatanā avitakkaavicārā. Manāyatanaṃ siyā savitakkasavicāraṃ, siyā avitakkavicāramattaṃ, siyā avitakkaavicāraṃ. Dhammāyatanaṃ siyā savitakkasavicāraṃ, siyā avitakkavicāramattaṃ, siyā avitakkaavicāraṃ, siyā na vattabbaṃ savitakkasavicārantipi, avitakkavicāramattantipi, avitakkaavicārantipi.
Pannarasa dhātuyo avitakkaavicārā. Manodhātu savitakkasavicārā. Manoviññāṇadhātu siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā. Dhammadhātu siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā, siyā na vattabbā savitakkasavicārātipi, avitakkavicāramattātipi, avitakkaavicārātipi.
Samudayasaccaṃ savitakkasavicāraṃ. Nirodhasaccaṃ avitakkaavicāraṃ. Maggasaccaṃ siyā savitakkasavicāraṃ, siyā avitakkavicāramattaṃ, siyā avitakkaavicāraṃ. Dukkhasaccaṃ siyā savitakkasavicāraṃ, siyā avitakkavicāramattaṃ, siyā avitakkaavicāraṃ, siyā na vattabbaṃ savitakkasavicārantipi, avitakkavicāramattantipi, avitakkaavicārantipi.
Navindriyā avitakkaavicārā. Domanassindriyaṃ savitakkasavicāraṃ. Upekkhindriyaṃ siyā savitakkasavicāraṃ, siyā avitakkaavicāraṃ. Ekādasindriyā siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā.
Tayo akusalahetū savitakkasavicārā. Cha hetū siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā. Kabaḷīkāro āhāro avitakkaavicāro. Tayo āhārā siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā. Pañca phassā avitakkaavicārā. Manodhātusamphasso savitakkasavicāro. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphasso siyā savitakkasavicāro, siyā avitakkavicāramatto, siyā avitakkaavicāro. Pañca vedanā… pañca saññā… pañca cetanā… pañca cittā avitakkaavicārā manodhātu savitakkasavicārā, manoviññāṇadhātu siyā savitakkasavicārā, siyā avitakkavicāramattā, siyā avitakkaavicārā. ^2-1042

##### (1) Rūpadukaṃ ^2-18-10-6-0

Pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati rūpā, kati arūpā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati rūpā, kati arūpā?
Rūpakkhandho rūpaṃ. Cattāro khandhā arūpā. Dasāyatanā rūpā. Manāyatanaṃ arūpaṃ. Dhammāyatanaṃ siyā rūpaṃ, siyā arūpaṃ. Dasa dhātuyo rūpā. Satta dhātuyo arūpā. Dhammadhātu siyā rūpā, siyā arūpā. Tīṇi saccāni arūpā. Dukkhasaccaṃ siyā rūpaṃ, siyā arūpaṃ. Sattindriyā rūpā. Cuddasindriyā arūpā. Jīvitindriyaṃ siyā rūpaṃ, siyā arūpaṃ. Nava hetū arūpā. Kabaḷīkāro āhāro rūpaṃ. Tayo āhārā arūpā. Satta phassā arūpā. Satta vedanā… satta saññā… satta cetanā satta cittā arūpā. ^2-1043

##### (2) Lokiyadukaṃ ^2-18-10-7-0

Pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati lokiyā, kati lokuttarā? Dvādasannaṃ āyatanānaṃ kati lokiyā, kati lokuttarā? Aṭṭhārasannaṃ dhātūnaṃ kati lokiyā, kati lokuttarā? Catunnaṃ saccānaṃ kati lokiyā, kati lokuttarā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati lokiyā, kati lokuttarā?
Rūpakkhandho lokiyo. Cattāro khandhā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Dasāyatanā lokiyā. Dve āyatanā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Soḷasa dhātuyo lokiyā. Dve dhātuyo siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Dve saccā lokiyā. Dve saccā lokuttarā.
Dasindriyā lokiyā. Tīṇindriyā lokuttarā. Navindriyā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Tayo akusalahetū lokiyā. Cha hetū siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Kabaḷīkāro āhāro lokiyo. Tayo āhārā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Cha phassā lokiyā. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphasso siyā lokiyo, siyā lokuttaro. Cha vedanā lokiyā. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphassajā vedanā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Cha saññā lokiyā. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphassajā saññā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Cha cetanā lokiyā. Manoviññāṇadhātusamphassajā cetanā siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarā. Cha cittā lokiyā. Manoviññāṇadhātu siyā lokiyā, siyā lokuttarāti.
Abhiññā dve sārammaṇā, diṭṭhā kusalavedanā;
Vipākā ca upādinnā, vitakkaṃ rūpalokiyāti.

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Sattamādīsu catūsu vāresu sattamavāre salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena abhiññeyyatā veditabbā, ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena pariññeyyatā; sā ca "rūpakkhandho abhiññeyyo pariññeyyo na pahātabbo"tiādīsu ñātatīraṇapariññāvaseneva, "samudayasaccaṃ abhiññeyyaṃ pariññeyyaṃ pahātabba"ntiādīsu pahānapariññāvasena veditabbā. Aṭṭhamavāre rūpādiārammaṇānaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ vasena sārammaṇānārammaṇatā veditabbā. Navamavāre khandhādidhammā diṭṭhasutamutaviññātavasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; so uttānatthoyeva. Dasamavāre kusalattikavedanāttikavipākattikaupādinnattikavitakkattikānaṃ rūpadukalokiyadukānañca vasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ siyā taṃ sabbaṃ tattha tattha pañhāpucchakavāre vuttameva. Ettāvatā dhammahadayavibhaṅgavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.

## Dhammahadayavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-18|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare dhammahadayavibhaṅge pāḷiparicchedo tāva evaṃ veditabbo – ettha hi āditova khandhādīnaṃ dvādasannaṃ koṭṭhāsānaṃ vasena sabbasaṅgāhikavāro nāma vutto, dutiyo tesaṃyeva dhammānaṃ kāmadhātuādīsu uppattānuppattidassanavāro, tatiyo tattheva pariyāpannāpariyāpannadassanavāro, catuttho tīsu bhūmīsu uppattikkhaṇe vijjamānāvijjamānadhammadassanavāro, pañcamo tesaṃ dhammānaṃ bhūmantaravasena dassanavāro, chaṭṭho gatīsu uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇadassanavāro, sattamo abhiññeyyādivāro, aṭṭhamo sārammaṇānārammaṇavāro, navamo khandhādidhammānaṃ diṭṭhasutādivasena saṅgahetvā dassanavāro, dasamo kusalattikādivasena saṅgahetvā dassanavāroti evaṃ dasahi vārehi pāḷi paricchinnā. Yathāpucchaṃ vissajjanañhettha sabbaññubyākaraṇaṃ nāma; khandhādīnaṃ pana pabhedo khandhavibhaṅgādīsu vuttanayeneva veditabbo. Iminā ca vibhaṅgena aṭṭhārasahi vibhaṅgehi maṇḍitaṃ vibhaṅgapakaraṇaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ.

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Abhiññeyyādivāre sabbe khandhādayo abhiññeyyā pariññeyyā vuttā; samudayasaccaṃ tayo akusalahetū domanassindriyañca pahātabbāni, maggasaccaṃ anaññātaññassāmītindriyañca bhāvetabbāni, nirodhasaccaṃ aññātāvindriyañca sacchikātabbāni. Abhiññeyyatā salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena, pariññeyyatā ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena veditabbā. Sārammaṇānārammaṇavāre rūpakkhandho anārammaṇo, cattāro khandhā sārammaṇā; diṭṭhasutādivāre cattāro khandhā na diṭṭhā na sutā na mutā, viññātāva; tikādivāre samudayasaccaṃ akusalaṃ, maggasaccaṃ kusalaṃ, nirodhasaccaṃ abyākataṃ. Tasmā yoginā rūpārūpadhamme salakkhaṇato pariggahetvā ñātapariññāya jānitvā tīraṇapariññāya tīrayitvā, pahānapariññāya samudayaṃ akusalahetū ca pajahitvā, maggaṃ bhāvetvā nirodhaṃ sacchikarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Dhammahadayavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-18|↑↑↑]]

Dhammahadayavibhaṅge avīcito yāva bhavaggā sabbe dhammā khandhādīhi dvādasahi koṭṭhāsehi saṅgahetvā tesaṃ uppattānuppatti, pariyāpannāpariyāpannatā, upapattikkhaṇe pātubhāvo, gatīsu uppādakakammaṃ āyuppamāṇañca, abhiññeyyapariññeyyapahātabbabhāvetabbasacchikātabbabhāvo ca dassito, "natthi koci bhavo nicco"ti ca vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā sabbasmiṃ bhave khandhāyatanadhātumattameva passantena, dukkhasaccaṃ pariññeyyaṃ, samudayasaccaṃ pahātabbaṃ, maggasaccaṃ bhāvetabbaṃ, nirodhasaccaṃ sacchikātabbanti ñatvā, akusalahetū pajahitvā kusalahetū bhāvetvā, bhavesu nikantiṃ akarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

##### The Taken-Up Triad ^2-18-10-4-0

##### The Thought Triad ^2-18-10-5-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-1042]]

Of the five aggregates, how many are with thought and with examination, how many are without thought but with examination only, how many are without thought and without examination… of the seven minds, how many are with thought and with examination, how many are without thought but with examination only, how many are without thought and without examination?
The aggregate of material form is without thought and without examination. Three aggregates may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination. The aggregate of mental formations may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, may be without thought and without examination, or may not be describable as with thought and with examination, as without thought but with examination only, or as without thought and without examination.
Ten sense-fields are without thought and without examination. The mind sense-field may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination. The mental-object sense-field may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, may be without thought and without examination, or may not be describable as with thought and with examination, as without thought but with examination only, or as without thought and without examination.
Fifteen elements are without thought and without examination. The mind element is with thought and with examination. The mind-consciousness element may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination. The mental-object element may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, may be without thought and without examination, or may not be describable as with thought and with examination, as without thought but with examination only, or as without thought and without examination.
