## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Idāni aparena pariyāyena ekacittakkhaṇe paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ puna "katame dhammā kusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha "avijjāpaccayā"ti upanissayapaccayataṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ; teneva niddesavāre "tattha katamā avijjā"ti avibhajitvā "tattha katamo avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"ti vibhattaṃ — kusalacetanāsaṅkhāto hi saṅkhāroyeva tasmiṃ samaye cittena sahajāto hoti, na avijjā. Tattha lokiyakusalassa heṭṭhā suttantabhājanīye vuttanayeneva avijjā paccayo hoti; yasmā pana appahīnāvijjo avijjāya pahānatthaṃ lokuttaraṃ bhāveti, tasmā tassāpi samatikkamavasena paccayo hoti — avijjāvatoyeva hi kusalāyūhanaṃ hoti, na itarassa. Tebhūmakakusale ca sammohavasenapi samatikkamabhāvanāvasenapi āyūhanaṃ labbhati, lokuttare samucchedabhāvanāvasena. Ayaṃ pana viseso — yathā heṭṭhā ekekakusale catunnaṃ catukkānaṃ vasena nava soḷasakā laddhā, tathā idha na labbhanti; avijjāya avigatasampayuttaaññamaññapaccayābhāvato upanissayavasena panettha paṭhamacatukkameva labbhati, tampi paṭhamavārameva dassetvā saṃkhittaṃ.

#### Kusalamūlakavipākaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Idāni abyākatesupi apareneva nayena paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ "katame dhammā abyākatā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha "kusalamūlapaccayā"ti idampi upanissayapaccayataṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ — kusalavipākassa hi kusalamūlaṃ, akusalavipākassa ca akusalamūlaṃ upanissayapaccayo hoti; nānākkhaṇikakammapaccaye pana vattabbameva natthi, tasmā esa upanissayapaccayena ceva nānākkhaṇikakammapaccayena ca paccayo hoti. Teneva niddesavāre "tattha katamaṃ kusalamūla"nti avibhajitvā "tattha katamo kusalamūlapaccayā saṅkhāro"ti vibhattaṃ; akusalavipākepi eseva nayo, tenevettha akusalamūlakavipākaniddesopi iminā saddhiṃ ekaparicchedova hoti. Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddese viya ca imasmimpi vipākaniddese paṭhamaṃ paccayacatukkameva labbhati, tampi paṭhamavāraṃ dassetvā saṃkhittaṃ. Kiriyādhammānaṃ pana yasmā neva avijjā na kusalākusalamūlāni upanissayapaccayataṃ labhanti, tasmā kiriyavasena paccayākāro na vutto. Evamayaṃ paccayākāro suttantaabhidhammabhājanīyavasena dveparivaṭṭameva nīharitvā bhājetvā dassito hoti.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddese avijjā kusalassa upanissayapaccayavasena paccayo dassito. Na hi ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjā uppajjati; appahīnāvijjo pana avijjāya pahānatthaṃ lokuttaraṃ bhāveti, tasmā samatikkamavasena avijjā tassāpi paccayo hoti — avijjāvatoyeva hi kusalāyūhanaṃ hoti, na itarassa. Tasmā yoginā "avijjāvā ahaṃ, kiṃ me kusalabhāvanāyā"ti nibbedaṃ pahāya, "avijjāya pahānatthameva ayaṃ bhāvanā"ti ñatvā vīriyaṃ upasampādetvā, attano avijjaṃyeva bhāvanāya kāraṇaṃ karonto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Kusalamūlakavipākaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Vipākaniddese kusalamūlaṃ kusalavipākassa, akusalamūlañca akusalavipākassa upanissayapaccayavasena nānākkhaṇikakammapaccayavasena ca paccayo dassito. Kiriyadhammānaṃ pana neva avijjā na kusalākusalamūlāni upanissayapaccayataṃ labhantīti kiriyavasena paccayākāro na vutto. Tasmā yoginā "yaṃ kiñci me sukhaṃ vā dukkhaṃ vā uppajjati, taṃ adhiccasamuppanna"nti ahetukadiṭṭhiṃ pahāya, mūlato phalaṃ phalato ca mūlaṃ anugantvā dassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "yaṃ ajja ropemi, tasseva phalaṃ parato paccanubhomī"ti appamatto hutvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

On the occasion when one develops the meditative absorption that is beyond the world, leading out, leading to dismantling, for the abandoning of views, for reaching the first stage — secluded from sense pleasures… one enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption, of difficult practice and slow direct knowledge — on that occasion, with ignorance as condition, a formation; with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, confidence; with confidence as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of these things.

Therein, what is the formation that has an unwholesome root as condition? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation that has an unwholesome root as condition".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition".
Therein, what is the mentality that has consciousness as condition? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations — this is called "the mentality that has consciousness as condition".
Therein, what is the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition".
Therein, what is the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition? The contact, touching, being in contact, state of being in contact — this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental experience that is neither comfortable nor uncomfortable, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant experience born of mind-contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has feeling as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition".
Therein, what is the birth that has existence as condition? The birth of the various states, their being born, arising, coming forth, appearance — this is called "the birth that has existence as condition".
Therein, what are the aging and death that have birth as condition? There is aging, there is death. Therein, what is aging? The aging of the various states, their growing old, the decline of their life span — this is called aging. Therein, what is death? The destruction of the various states, their vanishing, breakup, disintegration, impermanence, disappearance — this is called "death". So this is aging and this is death. This is called "the aging and death that have birth as condition".
