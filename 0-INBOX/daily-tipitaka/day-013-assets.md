## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Catukkanayo

[[#^toc-2-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacarakusale catukkanayo nāma savitakkasavicārādibhedo catubbidho jhānapabhedo. Tattha paṭhamajjhānaṃ vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṃ savicāraṃ vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ upasampajja viharati pathavīkasiṇādīsu aññataraṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā. Evaṃ dutiyādīni jhānanukkamena catukkajjhānāni nibbattenti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1011]]

#### Pañcakanayo

[[#^toc-2-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañcakanayo pana puggalajjhāsayavasena ceva desanāvilāsena ca bhājito. Tattha vitakkavicārānaṃ visuṃ vūpasamanavasena paṭhamajjhānato paraṃ avitakkaṃ vicāramattaṃ dutiyajjhānaṃ, tato avitakkaavicāraṃ tatiyajjhānanti evaṃ pañca jhāniyāni nibbattenti. Ayamettha viseso – yaṃ catukkanaye dutiyaṃ taṃ idha tatiyanti evamānupubbiyaṃ veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1075]]

#### Catasso paṭipadā (Rūpāvacara)

[[#^toc-2-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacarajjhānaṃ nāma paṭipadākamena sijjhati. Sā pana dukkhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā, dukkhā paṭipadā khippābhiññā, sukhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā, sukhā paṭipadā khippābhiññāti catubbidhā. Tattha ādito kilese vikkhambhentassa kasirapaṭipattitāya dukkhā, appanāparivāse maggapātubhāvamandatāya dandhābhiññāti evaṃ catasso paṭipadā yathārahaṃ veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1092]]

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

### Catukkanayo

[[#^toc-1-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Catukkanaye cattāri rūpajjhānāni catukkapaṭipadāya vibhajiyanti. Paṭhamajjhānaṃ vitakkavicārapītisukhekaggatāsahitaṃ hoti; dutiyajjhānaṃ vitakkavicārehi vūpasantaṃ ajjhattaṃ sampasādanaṃ hoti; tatiyajjhānaṃ pītiyā ca virāgā upekkhako hoti; catutthajjhānaṃ sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhasassa ca pahānā adukkhamasukhaṃ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṃ paṭilabhati. Catukkanayena vā jhānaṃ bhāvetvā kilesehi vimocanaṃ yathānusandhi paṭilabhati.

### Pañcakanayo

[[#^toc-1-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañcakanaye pañca rūpajjhānāni pañcakapaṭipadāya vibhajiyanti. Tattha vitakkamattaṃ paṭhamaṃ pahāya vitakkavirahitaṃ dutiyajjhānaṃ, tato vicāravirahitaṃ tatiyajjhānaṃ, tato pītivirahitaṃ catutthajjhānaṃ, tato sukhassa ca pahānā pañcamajjhānaṃ paṭilabhati. Evaṃ pañcakanayena vitakkavicārapītisukhānaṃ anupubbavivekena jhānaṃ bhāvetabbaṃ, yena upakkilesānaṃ anupubbasodhanā hoti.

### Catasso paṭipadā — Soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Paṭipadācatukke cattāro paṭipadā vibhajiyanti: dukkhā dandhābhiññā, dukkhā khippābhiññā, sukhā dandhābhiññā, sukhā khippābhiññāti. Imesu catūsu paṭipadāsu yā yassa sattassa sappāyā hoti tāya paṭipadāya jhānaṃ bhāvetabbaṃ. Indriyānaṃ tīkhabhāvena vā mudubhāvena jhānakkhippaṃ vā dandhaṃ paṭilabhati; tasmā sattassa indriyaparopariyattaṃ ñatvā anucchavikā paṭipadā sevitabbā.
