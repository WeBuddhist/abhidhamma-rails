### Saccavibhaṅgo ^2-4-0

#### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ ^2-4-1-0

189. Cattāri ariyasaccāni – dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhasamudayaṃ [dukkhasamudayo (syā.)] ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhaṃ [dukkhanirodho (syā.)] ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṃ. ^2-189

##### Dukkhasaccaṃ ^2-4-1-1-0

190. Tattha katamaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ? Jātipi dukkhā, jarāpi dukkhā, maraṇampi dukkhaṃ, sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsāpi dukkhā, appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho, yaṃ picchaṃ na labhati tampi dukkhaṃ, saṃkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā. ^2-190

191. Tattha katamā jāti? Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhi tamhi sattanikāye jāti sañjāti okkanti abhinibbatti khandhānaṃ pātubhāvo āyatanānaṃ paṭilābho – ayaṃ vuccati "jāti". ^2-191

192. Tattha katamā jarā? Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhi tamhi sattanikāye jarā jīraṇatā khaṇḍiccaṃ pāliccaṃ valittacatā āyuno saṃhāni indriyānaṃ paripāko – ayaṃ vuccati "jarā". ^2-192

193. Tattha katamaṃ maraṇaṃ? Yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhā tamhā sattanikāyā cuti cavanatā bhedo antaradhānaṃ maccu maraṇaṃ kālakiriyā khandhānaṃ bhedo kaḷevarassa nikkhepo jīvitindriyassupacchedo – idaṃ vuccati "maraṇaṃ". ^2-193

194. Tattha katamo soko? Ñātibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa bhogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa rogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa sīlabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa diṭṭhibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa aññataraññatarena byasanena samannāgatassa aññataraññatarena dukkhadhammena phuṭṭhassa soko socanā socitattaṃ antosoko antoparisoko cetaso parijjhāyanā domanassaṃ sokasallaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "soko". ^2-194

195. Tattha katamo paridevo? Ñātibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa bhogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa rogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa sīlabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa diṭṭhibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa aññataraññatarena byasanena samannāgatassa aññataraññatarena dukkhadhammena phuṭṭhassa ādevo paridevo ādevanā paridevanā ādevitattaṃ paridevitattaṃ vācā palāpo vippalāpo lālappo lālappanā lālappitattaṃ [lālapo lālapanā lālapitattaṃ (syā.)] – ayaṃ vuccati "paridevo". ^2-195

196. Tattha katamaṃ dukkhaṃ? Yaṃ kāyikaṃ asātaṃ kāyikaṃ dukkhaṃ kāyasamphassajaṃ asātaṃ dukkhaṃ vedayitaṃ kāyasamphassajā asātā dukkhā vedanā – idaṃ vuccati "dukkhaṃ". ^2-196

197. Tattha katamaṃ domanassaṃ? Yaṃ cetasikaṃ asātaṃ cetasikaṃ dukkhaṃ cetosamphassajaṃ asātaṃ dukkhaṃ vedayitaṃ cetosamphassajā asātā dukkhā vedanā – idaṃ vuccati "domanassaṃ". ^2-197

198. Tattha katamo upāyāso? Ñātibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa bhogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa rogabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa sīlabyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa diṭṭhibyasanena vā phuṭṭhassa aññataraññatarena byasanena samannāgatassa aññataraññatarena dukkhadhammena phuṭṭhassa āyāso upāyāso āyāsitattaṃ upāyāsitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "upāyāso". ^2-198

199. Tattha katamo appiyehi sampayogo dukkho? Idha yassa te honti aniṭṭhā akantā amanāpā rūpā saddā gandhā rasā phoṭṭhabbā, ye vā panassa te honti anatthakāmā ahitakāmā aphāsukakāmā ayogakkhemakāmā; yā tehi saṅgati samāgamo samodhānaṃ missībhāvo – ayaṃ vuccati "appiyehi sampayogo dukkho". ^2-199

200. Tattha katamo piyehi vippayogo dukkho? Idha yassa te honti iṭṭhā kantā manāpā rūpā saddā gandhā rasā phoṭṭhabbā, ye vā panassa te honti atthakāmā hitakāmā phāsukakāmā yogakkhemakāmā mātā vā pitā vā bhātā vā bhaginī vā mittā vā amaccā vā ñātī vā sālohitā vā; yā tehi asaṅgati asamāgamo asamodhānaṃ amissībhāvo – ayaṃ vuccati "piyehi vippayogo dukkho". ^2-200

201. Tattha katamaṃ yampicchaṃ na labhati tampi dukkhaṃ? Jātidhammānaṃ sattānaṃ evaṃ icchā uppajjati – "aho vata, mayaṃ na jātidhammā assāma; na ca, vata, no jāti āgaccheyyā"ti! Na kho panetaṃ icchāya pattabbaṃ. Idampi "yampicchaṃ na labhati tampi dukkhaṃ".
Jarādhammānaṃ sattānaṃ…pe… byādhidhammānaṃ sattānaṃ…pe… maraṇadhammānaṃ sattānaṃ…pe… sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsadhammānaṃ sattānaṃ evaṃ icchā uppajjati – "aho vata, mayaṃ na sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsadhammā assāma; na ca, vata, no sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā āgaccheyyu"nti! Na kho panetaṃ icchāya pattabbaṃ. Idampi "yampicchaṃ na labhati tampi dukkhaṃ". ^2-201

202. Tattha katame saṃkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā? Seyyathidaṃ – rūpupādānakkhandho, vedanupādānakkhandho, saññupādānakkhandho, saṅkhārupādānakkhandho, viññāṇupādānakkhandho. Ime vuccanti "saṃkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā".
Idaṃ vuccati "dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ". ^2-202

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Sacca)

[[#^toc-4-1|↑↑↑]]

Saccānaṃ suttantabhājanīye 'dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasacca'nti uddesavāro. Tattha vibhāgato, nibbacanalakkhaṇādippabhedato, atthatthuddhārato, anūnādhikato, kamato, saccañāṇakiccato, antogadhānaṃ pabhedato, upamāto, catukkato, suññatekavidhādīhi, sabhāgavisabhāgato ca viññunā vinicchayo veditabbo. Lakkhaṇādippabhedato hi bādhanalakkhaṇaṃ dukkhasaccaṃ santāpanarasaṃ pavattipaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, pabhavalakkhaṇaṃ samudayasaccaṃ anupacchedakaraṇarasaṃ palibodhapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, santilakkhaṇaṃ nirodhasaccaṃ accutirasaṃ animittapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, niyyānalakkhaṇaṃ maggasaccaṃ kilesappahānakaraṇarasaṃ vuṭṭhānapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ. Kamato pana oḷārikattā sabbasattasādhāraṇattā ca suviññeyyanti dukkhasaccaṃ paṭhamaṃ vuttaṃ, tasseva hetudassanatthaṃ tadanantaraṃ samudayasaccaṃ, hetunirodhā phalanirodhoti ñāpanatthaṃ tato nirodhasaccaṃ, tadadhigamupāyadassanatthaṃ ante maggasaccaṃ; bhavasukhassādagadhitānaṃ vā sattānaṃ saṃvegajananatthaṃ paṭhamaṃ dukkhamāha, saṃviggamānasānaṃ dukkhanissaraṇagavesīnaṃ assāsajananatthaṃ nirodhaṃ, tato nirodhasampāpakaṃ magganti.

#### Dukkhasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Idāni saṅkhepato uddiṭṭhāni dukkhādīni vibhajitvā dassetuṃ 'tattha katamaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ? Jātipi dukkhā'ti niddesavāro āraddho. Tattha ayaṃ mātikā — jāti veditabbā, jātiyā dukkhaṭṭho veditabbo; jarā, maraṇaṃ, soko, paridevo, dukkhaṃ, domanassaṃ, upāyāso, appiyasampayogo, piyavippayogo veditabbo, tesañca dukkhaṭṭho; icchā veditabbā, icchāya dukkhaṭṭho; khandhā veditabbā, khandhānaṃ dukkhaṭṭho veditabbo. Idañhi dukkhaṃ nāma anekaṃ nānappakāraṃ, seyyathidaṃ — dukkhadukkhaṃ, vipariṇāmadukkhaṃ, saṅkhāradukkhaṃ, paṭicchannadukkhaṃ, appaṭicchannadukkhaṃ, pariyāyadukkhaṃ, nippariyāyadukkhanti. Tattha pariyāyadukkhaṃ nippariyāyadukkhanti imasmiṃ padadvaye ṭhatvā dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ kathetabbaṃ. Ariyasaccañca nāmetaṃ pāḷiyaṃ saṅkhepatopi āgacchati vitthāratopi; saṅkhepato āgataṭṭhāne saṅkhepenapi vitthārenapi kathetuṃ vaṭṭati, vitthārato āgataṭṭhāne pana vitthāreneva, na saṅkhepena. Taṃ idaṃ imasmiṃ ṭhāne vitthārena āgatanti vitthāreneva kathetabbaṃ; tasmā jātiādīni 'yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhi tamhi sattanikāye jāti sañjātī'tiādinā padabhājanīyassa vasena veditabbāni.

#### Samudayasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Samudayasaccaniddese yāyaṃ taṇhāti yā ayaṃ taṇhā. Ponobbhavikāti punabbhavakaraṇaṃ punobbhavo, punobbhavo sīlamassāti ponobbhavikā; apica punabbhavaṃ deti, punabbhavāya saṃvattati, punappunaṃ bhave nibbattetīti ponobbhavikā. Abhinandanasaṅkhātena nandirāgena sahagatāti nandirāgasahagatā, nandirāgena saddhiṃ atthato ekattameva gatāti vuttaṃ hoti. Tatratatrābhinandinīti yatra yatra attabhāvo tatratatrābhinandinī, rūpādīsu vā ārammaṇesu tatratatrābhinandinī. Kāmataṇhāti kāme taṇhā, pañcakāmaguṇikarāgassetaṃ adhivacanaṃ; bhave taṇhā bhavataṇhā, bhavapatthanāvasena uppannassa sassatadiṭṭhisahagatassa rūpārūpabhavarāgassetaṃ nāmaṃ. Idāni tassā taṇhāya vatthuṃ vitthārato dassetuṃ 'sā kho panesā'tiādi vuttaṃ; tattha uppajjatīti jāyati, nivisatīti punappunaṃ pavattivasena patiṭṭhahati, yaṃ loke piyarūpaṃ sātarūpanti yaṃ lokasmiṃ piyasabhāvañceva madhurasabhāvañca. Lokasmiñhi cakkhādīsu mamattena abhiniviṭṭhā sattā attano cakkhuṃ suvaṇṇavimāne ugghāṭitamaṇisīhapañjaraṃ viya maññanti, sotaṃ rajatapanāḷikaṃ viya, ghānaṃ vaṭṭetvā ṭhapitaharitālavaṭṭiṃ viya, jivhaṃ rattakambalapaṭalaṃ viya, kāyaṃ suvaṇṇatoraṇaṃ viya, manaṃ aññesaṃ manena asadisaṃ uḷāraṃ maññantīti evaṃ tesu tesu vatthūsu taṇhāya uppatti nivesanañca veditabbaṃ.

#### Nirodhasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Nirodhasaccaniddese 'yo tasseva dukkhassā'ti vattabbe yasmā samudayanirodheneva dukkhaṃ nirujjhati no aññathā, tasmā taṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ samudayanirodhena dassento 'yo tassāyeva taṇhāyā'tiādimāha. Sīhasamānavuttino hi tathāgatā; te dukkhaṃ nirodhentā dukkhanirodhañca dassentā hetumhi paṭipajjanti, na phale; suvānavuttino pana aññatitthiyā, te attakilamathānuyogena phale paṭipajjanti, na hetumhi. Tattha asesavirāganirodho cāgo paṭinissaggo mutti anālayoti sabbāni nibbānavevacanāneva — nibbānañhi āgamma taṇhā asesā virajjati nirujjhati, cajiyati paṭinissajjiyati muccati na alliyati. Ekameva hi nibbānaṃ; nāmāni panassa sabbasaṅkhatānaṃ nāmapaṭipakkhavasena anekāni, seyyathidaṃ — rāgakkhayo dosakkhayo mohakkhayo taṇhākkhayo anuppādo appavattaṃ animittaṃ ajātaṃ ajaraṃ amataṃ asokantiādīni. Yathā ca puriso khette jātaṃ tittaalābuvalliṃ aggato paṭṭhāya mūlaṃ pariyesitvā chindeyya, sā milāyitvā appavattiṃ gaccheyya, tato tasmiṃ khette tittaalābu niruddhāti vucceyya; yathā ca aṭavito core ānetvā nagarassa dakkhiṇadvāre ghāteyyuṃ, tato aṭaviyaṃ corā matāti vucceyyuṃ — evameva cakkhādīsu taṇhā ariyamaggena mūlacchinnā nibbānaṃ āgamma appavattiṃ gacchati, evaṃ gatā ca tesu vatthūsu na paññāyatīti.

#### Maggasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Maggasaccaniddese ayamevāti aññamaggapaṭikkhepanatthaṃ niyamanaṃ. Ariyoti taṃtaṃmaggavajjhehi kilesehi ārakattā ariyabhāvakarattā ariyaphalapaṭilābhakarattā ca ariyo. Aṭṭhaṅgāni assāti aṭṭhaṅgiko — svāyaṃ caturaṅgikā viya senā, pañcaṅgikaṃ viya tūriyaṃ aṅgamattameva hoti, aṅgavinimutto natthi. Nibbānatthikehi maggīyati, nibbānaṃ vā maggati, kilese vā mārento gacchatīti maggo. Samudayasaccaniddese yāyaṃ taṇhāti yā ayaṃ taṇhā. #### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-4-1-0

189. There are four noble truths – the noble truth of suffering, the noble truth of the origin of suffering, the noble truth of the ending of suffering, and the noble truth of the way leading to the ending of suffering. ^2-189

##### The Truth of Suffering ^2-4-1-1-0

190. Therein, what is the noble truth of suffering? Birth is suffering, aging is suffering, death is suffering; sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and despair are suffering; association with what is disliked is suffering; separation from what is loved is suffering; not getting what one wants is also suffering; in brief, the five aggregates that are clung to are suffering. ^2-190

191. Therein, what is birth? The birth of this or that being into this or that order of beings – the being born, the descent, the coming forth, the appearance of the aggregates, the acquiring of the sense-fields – this is called "birth". ^2-191

192. Therein, what is aging? The aging of this or that being in this or that order of beings – the growing old, brokenness of teeth, greyness of hair, wrinkling of skin, the decline of the life span, the ripening of the faculties – this is called "aging". ^2-192
