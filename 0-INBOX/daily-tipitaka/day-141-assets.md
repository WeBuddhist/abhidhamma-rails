## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-1|↑↑↑]]

Saṅgahavāre atthoti saṅkhepato hetuphalaṃ; pabhedato pana yaṃkiñci paccayasamuppannaṃ, nibbānaṃ, bhāsitattho, vipāko, kiriyāti ime pañca dhammā attho. Dhammoti saṅkhepato paccayo; pabhedato pana yo koci phalanibbattako hetu, ariyamaggo, bhāsitaṃ, kusalaṃ, akusalanti ime pañca dhammā dhammo. Niruttipaṭisambhidā tasmiṃ atthe ca dhamme ca sabhāvaniruttiyā abhilāpe saddaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattā saddārammaṇā nāma, na paññattiārammaṇā; ñāṇesu ñāṇaṃ paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā. Imā pana catassopi sekkhabhūmiyañca asekkhabhūmiyañcāti dvīsu ṭhānesu pabhedaṃ gacchanti, adhigamena pariyattiyā savanena paripucchāya pubbayogenāti pañcahi kāraṇehi visadā honti. Pabhedavāro pana saccavārahetuvāradhammavārapaccayākāravārapariyattivāravasena pañcavidho; tattha ca yasmā bhāsitaṃ ñatvā tassattho ñāyati, tasmā uppaṭipāṭiyā paṭhamaṃ dhammapaṭisambhidā niddiṭṭhā.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye tisso paṭisambhidā lokiyā, atthapaṭisambhidā pana nibbānārammaṇānaṃ maggaphalañāṇānaṃ vasena lokiyalokuttaramissakā. Kusalākusalavipākakiriyānaṃ vasena catūhi vārehi vibhattaṃ; kusalākusalavāresu ekekasmiṃ cittaniddese catasso catasso, vipākakiriyavāresu pana vipākakiriyānaṃ atthena saṅgahitattā dhammapaṭisambhidaṃ chaḍḍetvā tisso tissova paṭisambhidā vibhattā. Paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā itarāsaṃ kiccaṃ jānāti, sayaṃ pana tāsaṃ kiccaṃ kātuṃ na sakkoti, bahussutadhammakathiko viya appassutadhammakathikassa. Pariyosāne tāsaṃ uppattiṭṭhānabhūtaṃ khettaṃ dassitaṃ – sekkhānaṃ kāmāvacarakusalato catūsu ñāṇasampayuttesu cittuppādesu, asekkhānaṃ kiriyato catūsu; atthapaṭisambhidā pana maggaphalakālepi uppajjati.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye attho hetuphalaṃ, dhammo paccayo, nirutti sabhāvanirutti, paṭibhānaṃ ñāṇesu ñāṇanti dassitaṃ; yasmā ca bhāsitaṃ ñatvā tassattho ñāyati, tasmā bhāsitaṃ paṭhamaṃ vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā bhāsitassa savanamatteneva santuṭṭhiṃ pahāya, "ayaṃ imassa bhāsitassa attho"ti bhāsitaṃ bhāsitatthañca paccavekkhanto, paccayaṃ disvā paccayuppannaṃ, phalaṃ disvā hetuṃ sallakkhento, sakkaccaṃ dhammassavanaṃ paripucchañca upasampādetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā itarāsaṃ kiccaṃ jānāti, sayaṃ pana taṃ kātuṃ na sakkoti, bahussuto mandassaro viya sarasampannassa appassutassa; sekkhānañca kusalacittesu, asekkhānaṃ kiriyacittesu paṭisambhidā uppajjanti. Tasmā yoginā "sarasampattiyā kathanamattena tipiṭakadharo"ti vā "jānanamatteneva kiccaṃ kata"nti vā vipallāsagāhaṃ pahāya, jānanasamatthatañca karaṇasamatthatañca pāṭiyekkaṃ sallakkhetvā, ñāṇasampayuttehi kusalacittehi dhammaṃ paccavekkhanaṃ upasampādento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

##### The Scripture Section ^2-15-1-6-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-724]]

There are four analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of principles, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding.
Therein, what is the analytical knowledge of principles? Here a monk knows the teaching — the discourses, the mixed prose-and-verse, the expositions, the verses, the inspired utterances, the "thus it was said" texts, the birth stories, the marvellous accounts, the question-and-answer texts. This is called "the analytical knowledge of principles". He knows the meaning of each thing that has been spoken — "this is the meaning of this saying, this is the meaning of this saying". This is called "the analytical knowledge of meaning". Knowledge with regard to the expression of the language of those principles is the analytical knowledge of language. Knowledge with regard to knowledges is the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. This is the scripture section.
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-724

#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-15-2-0

##### The Wholesome Section ^2-15-2-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-725]]

There are four analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of principles, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding.
Which things are wholesome? On the occasion when a wholesome mind of the sense sphere has arisen, accompanied by joy, associated with knowledge, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion there is contact… there is non-distraction. These things are wholesome. Knowledge with regard to these things is the analytical knowledge of principles. Knowledge of their result is the analytical knowledge of meaning. The language by which those things are made known — knowledge with regard to the expression of the language of those principles is the analytical knowledge of language. The knowledge by which one knows those knowledges — "these knowledges illuminate this meaning" — knowledge with regard to knowledges is the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. ^2-725
