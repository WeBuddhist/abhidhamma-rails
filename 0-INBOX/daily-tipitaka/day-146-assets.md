### Ekakamātikādi (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-1|↑↑↑]]

Ekavidhenāti ekappakārena, ekakoṭṭhāsena vā. Ñāṇañca taṃ vatthu ca nānappakārānaṃ sampattīnanti ñāṇavatthu; teneva "yāthāvakavatthuvibhāvanā paññā – evaṃ ekavidhena ñāṇavatthū"ti vuttaṃ. Pañca viññāṇāti cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca. "Na hetu ahetukā"tiādīhi dhammasaṅgahamātikāvasena, "aniccā jarābhibhūtā"tiādīhi amātikāvasenāti duvidhehi aṭṭhasattatiyā padehi ekakamātikā nikkhittā; dukānurūpehi pañcatiṃsāya dukehi dukamātikā. Tikamātikā pana "cintāmayā paññā"tiādīhi catūhi bāhirattikehi, "vipākā paññā"tiādīhi cuddasahi mātikātikehi, vitakkattike pītittike ca niyamitapaññāvasena vuttehīti aṭṭhāsītiyā tikehi nikkhittā. Catukkamātikā "kammassakatañāṇa"ntiādīhi ekavīsatiyā catukkehi, sattakamātikā "sattasattati ñāṇavatthūnī"ti saṅkhepato vuttehi ekādasahi sattakehi nikkhittā.

### Dasakamātikā (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-2|↑↑↑]]

Dasakamātikā "dasa tathāgatassa tathāgatabalānī"tiādinā ekeneva dasakena nikkhittā. Tathāgatabalānīti aññehi asādhāraṇāni tathāgatasseva balāni. Duvidhaṃ tathāgatassa balaṃ – kāyabalañca ñāṇabalañca; yaṃ dasannaṃ chaddantānaṃ balaṃ taṃ ekassa tathāgatassa kāyabalaṃ, idha pana ñāṇabalameva adhippetaṃ, ñāṇañhi akampiyaṭṭhena upatthambhakaṭṭhena ca balanti vuttaṃ. Yathā nisabho catūhi pādehi pathaviṃ uppīḷetvā acalaṭṭhānena tiṭṭhati, evaṃ tathāgato dasahi balehi samannāgato catūhi vesārajjapādehi aṭṭhaparisapathaviṃ uppīḷetvā akampiyo tiṭṭhanto āsabhaṃ ṭhānaṃ paṭijānāti, parisāsu sīhanādaṃ nadati, brahmacakkaṃ pavatteti; taṃ pana dhammacakkaṃ paṭivedhañāṇañca desanāñāṇañcāti duvidhaṃ. Ṭhānañca ṭhānatoti kāraṇañca kāraṇato; kammasamādānānaṃ gatiupadhikālapayogā vipākassa ṭhānaṃ, kammaṃ hetu; saṃkilesanti hānabhāgiyadhammaṃ, vodānanti visesabhāgiyadhammaṃ. Tathāgato paṭhamaṃ ṭhānāṭṭhānañāṇena veneyyānaṃ kilesāvaraṇābhāvaṃ passati…pe… āsavakkhayañāṇānubhāvena āsavakkhayāya dhammaṃ deseti; tasmā iminā anukkamena imāni dasabalāni vuttāni.

### Ekakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-3|↑↑↑]]

"Pañcaviññāṇā na hetumevā"tiādinā nayena āraddhe niddesavāre na hetumevāti sādhāraṇahetupaṭikkhepaniddeso; aniccamevāti hutvā abhāvaṭṭhena aniccāyeva. Uppannavatthukā uppannārammaṇāti anāgatapaṭikkhepo, purejātavatthukā purejātārammaṇāti sahuppattipaṭikkhepo. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhupasādameva vatthuṃ katvā rūpañca ārammaṇaṃ katvā uppajjati – nibaddhavatthu nibaddhadvāraṃ nibaddhārammaṇameva; evamete aññamaññassa gocaravisayaṃ na paccanubhonti. Na kañci dhammaṃ paṭivijānāti aññatra abhinipātamattāti cakkhuviññāṇaṃ dassanamattameva hoti; kusalākusalapaṭivijānanaṃ, iriyāpathakappanaṃ, samādhisamāpajjanaṃ, vipassanā, nibbānārammaṇatā, sabbopi panesa pabhedo manodvārikajavaneyeva labbhati, na pañcadvārikena. Supinaṃ dhātukkhobhato anubhūtapubbato devatopasaṃhārato pubbanimittato vā kapimiddhapareto passati; taṃ sekkhaputhujjanāva passanti appahīnavipallāsattā, asekkhā na passanti. Evaṃ pañcannaṃ viññāṇānaṃ yāthāvaṭṭhaṃ vatthuṃ vibhāvetīti yāthāvakavatthuvibhāvanā paññā, tassā vasena ekavidhena ñāṇavatthu.

### Dasakamātikā (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-2|↑↑↑]]

Dasakamātikāyaṃ tathāgatabalāni aññehi asādhāraṇāni vuttāni; tattha kammasamādānānaṃ kammaṃ hetu, gatiupadhikālapayogā ṭhānaṃ, ekameva pāṇaṃ ghātentesupi "imassa cetanā nirayagāminī bhavissatī"ti pajānāti; saṃkilesanti hānabhāgiyadhammaṃ, vodānanti visesabhāgiyadhammaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "ahampi dasabalasadiso"ti abhimānaṃ pahāya, kammaṃ hetu vipākassāti saddahanto nirayagāminiṃ cetanaṃ na pavattetvā, hānabhāgiyaṃ saṃkilesaṃ vajjetvā visesabhāgiyaṃ vodānaṃ upasampādetvā, akampiyaṭṭhena upatthambhakaṭṭhena ca balabhūtena ñāṇena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Ekakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-3|↑↑↑]]

Ekakaniddese pañcaviññāṇāni dassanasavanādimattāneva, kusalākusalapaṭivijānanaṃ samādhivipassanā ca manodvārikajavaneyeva labbhati; supinante ca cetiyavandanadhammassavanādīni karontassa kusalo, pāṇātipātādīni karontassa akusalo, taṃ kammaṃ savipākaṃ; asekkhā pahīnavipallāsattā supinaṃ na passanti. Tasmā yoginā "cakkhunā diṭṭhamatteneva kusalaṃ akusalaṃ vā hotī"ti gāhaṃ pahāya, manodvārikajavane pāṇātipātādiakusalaṃ akaronto, supinantepi cetiyavandanadhammassavanādikusalaṃ upasampādetvā, supine katampi kammaṃ savipākanti sallakkhento vipallāsappahānāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

##### The Exposition of the Ones ^2-16-11-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-761]]

The five kinds of consciousness are simply not roots, simply without roots, simply dissociated from roots, simply having conditions, simply conditioned, simply immaterial, simply worldly, simply subject to mental taints, simply able to be bound by fetters, simply able to be bound by ties, simply able to be swept by floods, simply able to be bound by yokes, simply able to be obstructed by hindrances, simply able to be wrongly grasped, simply able to be clung to, simply able to be defiled, simply neutral, simply having objects, simply not mental factors, simply results, simply taken up at birth and able to be clung to, simply undefiled but connected with defilement, simply not with thought and with examination, simply not without thought but with examination only, simply without thought and without examination, simply not accompanied by joy, simply to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development, simply having causes to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development, simply leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling, simply belonging to one neither in training nor beyond training, simply limited, simply of the sense sphere, simply not of the fine-material sphere, simply not of the immaterial sphere, simply included in a sphere, simply not outside any sphere, simply unfixed, simply not leading out, simply arisen and knowable by mind-consciousness, simply impermanent, simply overcome by aging. ^2-761

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-762]]

"The five kinds of consciousness have arisen bases and arisen objects": they arise when the basis has arisen and the object has arisen.
"They have bases arisen before them and objects arisen before them": they arise when the basis has arisen before and the object has arisen before.
"They have internal bases and external objects": the bases of the five kinds of consciousness are internal; their objects are external.
"They have unbroken bases and unbroken objects": they arise when the basis is unbroken and the object is unbroken.
"They have different bases and different objects": the basis and object of eye-consciousness are one thing; the basis and object of ear-consciousness are another; the basis and object of nose-consciousness are another; the basis and object of tongue-consciousness are another; the basis and object of body-consciousness are another. ^2-762

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-763]]

"They do not experience one another's field and range": ear-consciousness does not experience the field and range of eye-consciousness, nor does eye-consciousness experience the field and range of ear-consciousness. Nose-consciousness does not experience the field and range of eye-consciousness, nor does eye-consciousness experience the field and range of nose-consciousness. Tongue-consciousness does not experience the field and range of eye-consciousness, nor does eye-consciousness experience the field and range of tongue-consciousness. Body-consciousness does not experience the field and range of eye-consciousness, nor does eye-consciousness experience the field and range of body-consciousness. Of ear-consciousness… of nose-consciousness… of tongue-consciousness… eye-consciousness does not experience the field and range of body-consciousness, nor does body-consciousness experience the field and range of eye-consciousness. Ear-consciousness does not experience the field and range of body-consciousness, nor does body-consciousness experience the field and range of ear-consciousness. Nose-consciousness does not experience the field and range of body-consciousness, nor does body-consciousness experience the field and range of nose-consciousness. Tongue-consciousness does not experience the field and range of body-consciousness, nor does body-consciousness experience the field and range of tongue-consciousness. ^2-763

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-764]]

"They do not arise without bringing-to-mind": they arise for one who brings to mind.
"They do not arise without attention": they arise for one who attends.
"They do not arise uninterruptedly": they do not arise in unbroken succession.
"They do not arise not-before and not-after": they do not arise at a single moment. ^2-764

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-765]]

Nose-consciousness does not arise in immediate succession to the arising of eye-consciousness, nor does eye-consciousness arise in immediate succession to the arising of nose-consciousness. Ear-consciousness does not arise in immediate succession to the arising of body-consciousness, nor does body-consciousness arise in immediate succession to the arising of ear-consciousness.
