#### Abyākataniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abyākataṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe āgatapaṭipāṭiyāva vibhattaṃ. Sabbavāresu cettha avijjāmūlakā nayā parihīnā; kasmā? Avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbassa abhāvato — kusalacittesu hi avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbaṃ kusalamūlaṃ atthi, cakkhuviññāṇādīsu natthi; sahetukesu pana kiñcāpi atthi, evaṃ santepi idha pacchinnattā na gahitaṃ. Visesato panettha cakkhuviññāṇādīsu taṇhāṭṭhānaṃ upādānaṭṭhānañca parihīnaṃ, taṇhāṭṭhānārahassa balavadhammassa abhāvā adhimokkharahitattā ca; sesāhetukesu taṇhāṭṭhānameva parihīnaṃ; sahetukesu pana pasādasabbhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne pasādena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Evamettha kusalākusalavipākesu cakkhuviññāṇādīsu saṅkhāraviññāṇanāmachaṭṭhāyatanaphassavedanāmūlakā cha cha nayā, sesāhetukesu adhimokkhamūlakena saddhiṃ satta satta, sahetukesu pasādamūlakena saddhiṃ aṭṭha aṭṭha nayā veditabbā. Cakkhuviññāṇādīsu ca catunnampi catukkānaṃ ādivārova vutto; tatiyacatutthavārā asambhavatoyeva na vuttā — rūpamissakā hi te, na ca cakkhuviññāṇādīni rūpaṃ samuṭṭhāpenti. Arūpāvacaravipākasmiṃ pana vāradvayameva labbhati.

#### Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Idāni aparena pariyāyena ekacittakkhaṇe paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ puna "katame dhammā kusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha "avijjāpaccayā"ti upanissayapaccayataṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ; teneva niddesavāre "tattha katamā avijjā"ti avibhajitvā "tattha katamo avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"ti vibhattaṃ — kusalacetanāsaṅkhāto hi saṅkhāroyeva tasmiṃ samaye cittena sahajāto hoti, na avijjā. Tattha lokiyakusalassa heṭṭhā suttantabhājanīye vuttanayeneva avijjā paccayo hoti; yasmā pana appahīnāvijjo avijjāya pahānatthaṃ lokuttaraṃ bhāveti, tasmā tassāpi samatikkamavasena paccayo hoti — avijjāvatoyeva hi kusalāyūhanaṃ hoti, na itarassa. Tebhūmakakusale ca sammohavasenapi samatikkamabhāvanāvasenapi āyūhanaṃ labbhati, lokuttare samucchedabhāvanāvasena. Ayaṃ pana viseso — yathā heṭṭhā ekekakusale catunnaṃ catukkānaṃ vasena nava soḷasakā laddhā, tathā idha na labbhanti; avijjāya avigatasampayuttaaññamaññapaccayābhāvato upanissayavasena panettha paṭhamacatukkameva labbhati, tampi paṭhamavārameva dassetvā saṃkhittaṃ.

#### Abyākataniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abyākataniddese vipākakiriyacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Cakkhuviññāṇādīsu avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbassa abhāvā avijjāmūlakā nayā parihīnā; taṇhāṭṭhānārahassa balavadhammassa abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhānaṃ upādānaṭṭhānañca parihīnaṃ; sahetukesu pana pasādena taṇhāṭṭhāne padaṃ pūritaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "sabbaṃ me cittaṃ kusalaṃ vā akusalaṃ vā"ti ekantasaññaṃ pahāya, dassanasavanādīsu abyākatacittānaṃ suddhavipākabhāvadassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "diṭṭhe diṭṭhamattaṃ sute sutamattaṃ — na tāva ettha rāgo na doso"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddese avijjā kusalassa upanissayapaccayavasena paccayo dassito. Na hi ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjā uppajjati; appahīnāvijjo pana avijjāya pahānatthaṃ lokuttaraṃ bhāveti, tasmā samatikkamavasena avijjā tassāpi paccayo hoti — avijjāvatoyeva hi kusalāyūhanaṃ hoti, na itarassa. Tasmā yoginā "avijjāvā ahaṃ, kiṃ me kusalabhāvanāyā"ti nibbedaṃ pahāya, "avijjāya pahānatthameva ayaṃ bhāvanā"ti ñatvā vīriyaṃ upasampādetvā, attano avijjaṃyeva bhāvanāya kāraṇaṃ karonto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `AI_translation/english/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

328. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because unwholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind-consciousness element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-328

329. Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-329

330. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when the mind element has arisen — functional, neither wholesome nor unwholesome and not the result of action — accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a tangible thing as its object… or the mind-consciousness element has arisen — functional, neither wholesome nor unwholesome and not the result of action — accompanied by joy, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object… or the mind-consciousness element has arisen — functional, neither wholesome nor unwholesome and not the result of action — accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-330
