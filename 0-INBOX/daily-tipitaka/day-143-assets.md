### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye tisso paṭisambhidā lokiyā, atthapaṭisambhidā pana nibbānārammaṇānaṃ maggaphalañāṇānaṃ vasena lokiyalokuttaramissakā. Kusalākusalavipākakiriyānaṃ vasena catūhi vārehi vibhattaṃ; kusalākusalavāresu ekekasmiṃ cittaniddese catasso catasso, vipākakiriyavāresu pana vipākakiriyānaṃ atthena saṅgahitattā dhammapaṭisambhidaṃ chaḍḍetvā tisso tissova paṭisambhidā vibhattā. Paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā itarāsaṃ kiccaṃ jānāti, sayaṃ pana tāsaṃ kiccaṃ kātuṃ na sakkoti, bahussutadhammakathiko viya appassutadhammakathikassa. Pariyosāne tāsaṃ uppattiṭṭhānabhūtaṃ khettaṃ dassitaṃ – sekkhānaṃ kāmāvacarakusalato catūsu ñāṇasampayuttesu cittuppādesu, asekkhānaṃ kiriyato catūsu; atthapaṭisambhidā pana maggaphalakālepi uppajjati.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva catunnaṃ paṭisambhidānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu niruttipaṭisambhidā saddameva ārammaṇaṃ karotīti parittārammaṇā paccuppannārammaṇā bahiddhārammaṇā ca; itarā pana paccavekkhitabbassa atthassa dhammassa ñāṇassa ca vasena parittamahaggatappamāṇārammaṇā, atītānāgatapaccuppannārammaṇā, ajjhattabahiddhārammaṇā ca honti. Dhammapaṭisambhidā paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā ca maggaṃ paccavekkhaṇakāle maggārammaṇā, maggaṃ garuṃ katvā paccavekkhantassa maggādhipatino. Idhāpi tisso paṭisambhidā lokiyā, atthapaṭisambhidā lokiyalokuttarā.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake niruttipaṭisambhidā paccuppannaṃ saddameva ārammaṇaṃ karoti, itarā pana ajjhattampi bahiddhāpi atītādibhedampi atthaṃ dhammaṃ ñāṇañca paccavekkhanti. ## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/pi-2-english-plain-zeroshot.md`)

There are three analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding.
Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because unwholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a tangible thing as its object… the resultant mind-consciousness element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion there is contact, there is feeling, there is perception, there is intention, there is mind, there is thought, there is examination, there is equanimity, there is one-pointedness of mind, there is the mind faculty, there is the equanimity faculty, there is the life faculty; and whatever other immaterial, dependently arisen things there are on that occasion. These things are neutral. Knowledge with regard to these things is the analytical knowledge of meaning. The language by which those things are made known — knowledge with regard to the expression of the language of those principles is the analytical knowledge of language.
