## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Jhāna)

[[#^toc-12-1|↑↑↑]]

Mātikaṃ paṭipāṭiyā bhājento "imissā diṭṭhiyā"tiādīhi padehi sikkhattayasaṅkhātaṃ sabbaññubuddhasāsanameva dasseti. Tattha pātimokkhanti sikkhāpadasīlaṃ; yo naṃ pāti rakkhati, taṃ āpāyikādīhi dukkhehi mokkheti mocayatīti pātimokkhaṃ; tañca sīlaṃ sabbesaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ patiṭṭhā ādi ca. Yathā hi nagaravaḍḍhakī paṭhamaṃ nagaraṭṭhānaṃ sodheti, evameva yogāvacaro āditova sīlaṃ visodhetvā aparabhāge samathavipassanāmaggaphalanibbānāni sacchikaroti. So ājīvaṃ parisodhetvā anācārāgocare pahāya, aṇumattānipi vajjāni aṭṭhasaṭṭhiyojanasatasahassubbedhasinerupabbatasadisāni katvā bhayato passanto, indriyesu guttadvāro bhojane mattaññū pubbarattāpararattaṃ jāgariyānuyogamanuyutto satisampajaññena sabbakiccakārī, vivittaṃ appasaddaṃ appanigghosaṃ paṭisallānasāruppaṃ senāsanaṃ bhajitvā, pallaṅkaṃ ābhujitvā ujuṃ kāyaṃ paṇidhāya parimukhaṃ satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā, abhijjhaṃ byāpādaṃ thinamiddhaṃ uddhaccakukkuccaṃ vicikicchañcāti ime pañca nīvaraṇe cetaso upakkilese paññāya dubbalīkaraṇe pahāya cittaṃ parisodheti. Evaṃ pahīnanīvaraṇo vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṃ savicāraṃ vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ, tato sampasādapītisukhacittekaggatāsaṅkhātāni aṅgāni gahetvā dutiyatatiyacatutthajjhānāni upasampajja viharati; "vivicceva kāmehī"tiādīsu pana yaṃ vattabbaṃ siyā, taṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe rūpāvacaraniddese vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Jhāna)

[[#^toc-12-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye jhānassa pubbabhāgo vitthārena dassito — sīlavisuddhi, ājīvapārisuddhi, indriyasaṃvaro, jāgariyānuyogo, satisampajaññaṃ, vivittasenāsanapariggaho, nīvaraṇappahānañca. Tasmā yoginā aṇumattānipi vajjāni aṭṭhasaṭṭhiyojanasatasahassubbedhasinerupabbatasadisāni katvā bhayato passantena, anācārāgocaraṃ pahāya, ājīvaṃ sodhetvā, "idaṃ me pāpaṃ na karaṇīya"nti sabbaṃ dussīlyaṃ pariccajitvā, indriyesu guttadvārena bhojane mattaññunā satisampajaññena ca kusalā dhammā upasampādetabbā; gatapaccāgatavattaṃ pūrento kammaṭṭhānaṃ avissajjetvā vivittaṃ appasaddaṃ appanigghosaṃ senāsanaṃ bhajitvā, pallaṅkaṃ ābhujitvā ujuṃ kāyaṃ paṇidhāya parimukhaṃ satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā, abhijjhaṃ byāpādaṃ thinamiddhaṃ uddhaccakukkuccaṃ vicikicchañcāti pañca nīvaraṇe pahāya, tehi cetaso upakkilesehi cittaṃ parisodhetvā, vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānādīni upasampajja cittaṃ ekaggaṃ katvā pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

Therein, what is mindfulness? The mindfulness, recollection, remembering, mindfulness, bearing in mind, keeping in mind, not floating away, not forgetting; mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness — this is called "mindfulness". ^2-524

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-525]]

"Clearly comprehending": therein, what is clear comprehension? The wisdom, understanding, investigation, close investigation, investigation of things, discerning, discrimination, differentiation, erudition, proficiency, subtlety, clarification, consideration, close examination, breadth, cleverness, guidance, insight, clear comprehension, the goad; wisdom, the faculty of wisdom, the power of wisdom, the sword of wisdom, the tower of wisdom, the light of wisdom, the radiance of wisdom, the lamp of wisdom, the jewel of wisdom; non-delusion, investigation of things, right view — this is called "clear comprehension". So he is endowed with this mindfulness and this clear comprehension… furnished with them. Thus a monk goes forward mindful and clearly comprehending, returns mindful and clearly comprehending, looks ahead mindful and clearly comprehending, looks away mindful and clearly comprehending, bends mindful and clearly comprehending, stretches mindful and clearly comprehending; he acts mindful and clearly comprehending; he acts mindful and clearly comprehending when carrying the outer robe, the bowl, and the robes; he acts mindful and clearly comprehending when eating, drinking, chewing, and tasting; he acts mindful and clearly comprehending when defecating and urinating; he acts with clear comprehension when walking, standing, sitting, sleeping, waking, speaking, and keeping silent. ^2-525

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-526]]

"Secluded": even if a dwelling place is nearby, if it is not crowded with householders and renunciants, then it is secluded. Even if a dwelling place is far away, if it is not crowded with householders and renunciants, then it is secluded. ^2-526

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-527]]

"Dwelling place": a bed is a dwelling place, a chair is a dwelling place, a mattress is a dwelling place, a pillow is a dwelling place, a monastery is a dwelling place, a lean-to is a dwelling place, a mansion is a dwelling place, a watchtower is a dwelling place, a pavilion is a dwelling place, a cave-dwelling is a dwelling place, a cave is a dwelling place, the foot of a tree is a dwelling place, a bamboo thicket is a dwelling place. Or wherever monks retire — all that is a dwelling place. ^2-527

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-528]]

"Resorts to a secluded dwelling place": he resorts to this secluded dwelling place, resorts to it fully, frequents it, frequents it closely, frequents it thoroughly. Therefore it is said, "resorts to a secluded dwelling place". ^2-528

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-529]]

"Forest": having gone out beyond the boundary post, all that is forest. ^2-529

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-530]]

"The foot of a tree": the foot of a tree is just the foot of a tree. A mountain is just a mountain. A glen is just a glen. A mountain cave is just a mountain cave. A charnel ground is just a charnel ground. The open air is just the open air. A heap of straw is just a heap of straw. ^2-530

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-531]]

"Remote forest": this is a designation for distant dwelling places. "Remote forest": this is a designation for dwelling places in forest thickets. "Remote forest": this is a designation for frightening dwelling places. "Remote forest": this is a designation for hair-raising dwelling places.
