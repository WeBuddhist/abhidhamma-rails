## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Chandādhipateyyasuddhikasuññatā

[[#^toc-2-8-8|↑↑↑]]

Chandādhipateyyaṃ suññatañca missayitvā lokuttaravipākaṃ dassetuṃ chandādhipateyyasuddhikasuññatā vuttā. Tebhūmakakusalaṃ parittaaggiṭṭhāne uṇhabhāvanibbutakālo viya – kammakkhaṇe aññattha vipākakkhaṇanti nānākkhaṇatāya attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ na labhāpeti; lokuttaraṃ pana mahantaṃ aggikkhandhaṃ viya – tāya paññāya avūpasantāya maggānantarameva phalaṃ uppajjati, tasmā taṃ attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ labhāpeti. Chandādīnaṃ catunnaṃ adhipatiyānaṃ saddhiṃ suññatādiṭṭhānesu yojitā vīsati mahānayā chandādhipateyyādivasena catugguṇā honti; evaṃ catūhi nayasahassehi bhājetvā paṭhamamaggavipāko dasito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1480]]
#### Dutiyādimaggavipāko

[[#^toc-2-8-9|↑↑↑]]

Dutiyādimaggānaṃ vipākaṃ dassetuṃ dutiyādimaggavipāko vutto. Tattha catutthaphalaniddese viseso – 'aññindriyaṃ'ti na vuttaṃ, 'aññātāvindriyaṃ'ti vuttaṃ; aññātāvīti catūsu saccesu niṭṭhitañāṇakiccassa indriyaṃ, arahattaphale ṭhitassa. Tīṇi lokuttarindriyāni evaṃ vibhajjanti – ekañhi 'anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ' ekaṃ ṭhānaṃ gacchati sotāpattimaggaṃ, ekaṃ 'aññindriyaṃ' heṭṭhā tīṇi phalāni upari tayo maggeti cha ṭhānāni gacchati, ekaṃ 'aññātāvindriyaṃ' ekaṃ ṭhānaṃ gacchati arahattaphalaṃ. Sabbesupi maggaphalesu atthato aṭṭha aṭṭha indriyānīti catusaṭṭhi lokuttarindriyāni kathitāni honti.

### Akusalavipākaabyākataṃ

[[#^toc-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākāni pañca cakkhusotaghānajivhākāyaviññāṇāni, ekā manodhātu, ekā manoviññāṇadhātūti imāni satta cittāni pāḷito ca atthato ca kusalavipākacittehi sadisāni. Kevalañhi tāni kusalakammapaccayāni imāni akusalakammapaccayāni; tāni iṭṭhaiṭṭhamajjhattesu ārammaṇesu vattanti, imāni aniṭṭhaaniṭṭhamajjhattesu. Tattha sukhasahagataṃ kāyaviññāṇaṃ, idha dukkhasahagataṃ. Upekkhāsahagatā manoviññāṇadhātu akusalacittena kamme āyūhite kammakammanimittagatinimittesu aññataraṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā catūsu apāyesu paṭisandhi hutvā, dutiyavārato paṭṭhāya yāvatāyukaṃ bhavaṅgaṃ hutvā, pañcasu ṭhānesu vipaccati.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1484]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1485]]

#### Akusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni

[[#^toc-2-9-1|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni pāḷito ca atthato ca kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇehi sadisāni; kevalañhi tāni kusalakammapaccayāni, imāni akusalakammapaccayāni, tāni iṭṭhaiṭṭhamajjhattesu ārammaṇesu vattanti, imāni aniṭṭhaaniṭṭhamajjhattesu. Vatthudvārārammaṇāni nibaddhāneva honti, vatthādisaṅkamanaṃ natthi. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhupasādaṃ vatthuṃ katvā aniṭṭhe aniṭṭhamajjhatte ca rūpārammaṇe dassanakiccaṃ sādhayati; sotaviññāṇādīni sotapasādādīni vatthuṃ katvā savanaghāyanasāyanaphusanakiccāni sādhayanti. Kāyaviññāṇaṃ pana aniṭṭhe ārammaṇe dukkhasahagataṃ uppajjati, cakkhudvārādīsu catūsu upekkhāvedanā bhājitā.

#### Akusalavipākamanodhātu

[[#^toc-2-9-2|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākamanodhātu ekāva hoti. Sabhāvasuññatanissattaṭṭhena manoyeva dhātu; akusalavipākapañcaviññāṇānaṃ anantaraṃ rūpādivijānanalakkhaṇā, aniṭṭhaaniṭṭhamajjhattārammaṇānaṃ sampaṭicchanarasā, tathābhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā, akusalavipākacakkhuviññāṇādiapagamapadaṭṭhānā. Vatthu nibaddhaṃ hadayavatthumeva hoti, dvārārammaṇāni anibaddhāni; sampaṭicchanakiccameva hetaṃ hoti, pañcadvāre pañcasu ārammaṇesu sampaṭicchanaṃ hutvā vipaccati. Jhānaṅgamaggaṅgāni na labbhanti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1484]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1387]]

#### Akusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu

[[#^toc-2-9-3|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu ekāva upekkhāsahagatā hoti, na kusalavipāke viya somanassasahagataṃ ca upekkhāsahagataṃ ca duvidhā. Sā ahetukavipākā, chaḷārammaṇavijānanalakkhaṇā, santīraṇādirasā, tathābhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā, hadayavatthupadaṭṭhānā. Ekādasavidhenāpi akusalacittena, ṭhapetvā uddhaccasahagataṃ, kamme āyūhite kammakammanimittagatinimittesu aññataraṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā catūsu apāyesu paṭisandhi hutvā, dutiyavārato paṭṭhāya yāvatāyukaṃ bhavaṅgaṃ hutvā, aniṭṭhaaniṭṭhamajjhattārammaṇāya pañcaviññāṇavīthiyā santīraṇaṃ hutvā, balavārammaṇe chasu dvāresu tadārammaṇaṃ hutvā, maraṇakāle cuti hutvāti pañcasu ṭhānesu vipaccati.

## Akusalavipākaabyākataṃ

[[#^toc-1-9|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākābyākatacittuppāde akusalakammānaṃ phalaṭṭhena tayo akusalavipākacittavisesā saṅgahīyanti. Akusalavipākāni aniṭṭhārammaṇesu uppajjanti, kammavipākato. Imesu akusalavipākesu aniṭṭhārammaṇaṃ paccupaṭṭhitaṃ sante "idaṃ akusalakammānaṃ phalaṃ" ti sammapajānanaṃ katvā upekkhaṃ bhāveti, saṃvegaṃ vā uppādeti kusalakammānaṃ āsevanāya.

### Akusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni

[[#^toc-1-9-1|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca ahetukakusalavipākacittāni aniṭṭhārammaṇe saṅgahiyanti. Aniṭṭhārammaṇaṃ vijāniyamāne "idaṃ akusalakammavipākaṃ" ti sammapajānitvā, kusalakammānaṃ āsevanāya saṃvegaṃ uppādeti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-13-4-0]]

### Akusalavipākamanodhātu

[[#^toc-1-9-2|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākamanodhātu aniṭṭhārammaṇe sampaṭicchanavasena uppajjati. "Idaṃ akusalavipākaṃ" ti sammapajānitvā, pāpakammaṃ vajjitvā kusalakammānaṃ āsevanāya ussāho hoti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-13-4-0]]

---

### Akusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu

[[#^toc-1-9-3|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu aniṭṭhārammaṇe santīraṇavasena uppajjati. Domanassaṃ vā upekkhā vā sahagataṃ akusalavipākaṃ anubhavantassa "idaṃ akusalakammaphalaṃ" ti dassanaṃ cittapariyodāpanāya hoti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-13-4-0]]

##### Resultant of the Second and Subsequent Paths. ^1-1-8-9-0

Which phenomena are indeterminate?
When one cultivates a supramundane jhāna leading to liberation, reducing defilements, for the weakening of sensual desire and ill will, for the attainment of the second stage of liberation...pe...for the complete abandonment of sensual desire and ill will, for the attainment of the third stage of liberation...pe...for the complete abandonment of passion for form, passion for the formless, conceit, restlessness, and ignorance, for the attainment of the fourth stage of liberation, secluded from sensual desires...pe...enters and abides in the first jhāna, with difficult practice, slow comprehension; at that time, contact arises...pe...the faculty of final knowledge arises...pe...non-distraction arises...pe...these phenomena are wholesome.
As a result of that very supramundane wholesome jhāna having been performed and cultivated, its resultant state, secluded from sensual desires...pe...enters and abides in the first jhāna, with difficult practice, slow comprehension, emptiness; at that time, contact arises...pe...the faculty of one who knows arises...pe...non-distraction arises...pe...or whatever other incorporeal, conditionally arisen phenomena are present at that time – these phenomena are indeterminate. ^1-553

What is contact at that time?
Whatever contact, touching, complete touching, the state of having completely touched, is present at that time – this is contact at that time...pe.... ^1-554

What is the faculty of one who knows at that time?
The knowledge, wisdom, understanding, investigation, thorough investigation, investigation of phenomena, discernment, observation, close observation, erudition, skill, proficiency, analysis, consideration, examination, insight, intelligence, leading, insight, comprehension, goad, wisdom, wisdom faculty, power of wisdom, sword of wisdom, height of wisdom, light of wisdom, glory of wisdom, splendor of wisdom, jewel of wisdom, non-delusion, investigation of phenomena, right view, the investigation of phenomena as an enlightenment factor, a path factor, included in the path, of those who have come to know these phenomena – this is the faculty of one who knows at that time...pe...or whatever other incorporeal, conditionally arisen phenomena are present at that time – these phenomena are indeterminate.

#### Indeterminate Resultant of Unwholesome Kamma ^1-1-9-0

##### The Five Resultant Unwholesome Sense-Consciousnesses ^1-1-9-1-0

Which states are indeterminate?
When, as the result of unwholesome kamma having been wrought, having been stored up, eye-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, having visible form as its object; ear-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, having sound as its object; nose-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, having smell as its object; tongue-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, having taste as its object; body-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by pain, having tangible object as its object, then at that time there is contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, pain, one-pointedness of mind, mind faculty, pain faculty, life faculty; or whatever other immaterial phenomena there are at that time, arisen dependently – these phenomena are indeterminate. ^1-556

What is contact at that time? Whatever contact, touching, full touching, the state of having fully touched there is at that time – this is contact at that time. ^1-557

What is feeling at that time? Whatever bodily unpleasantness, bodily pain, unpleasant painful sensation born of body-contact, unpleasant painful feeling born of body-contact there is at that time, born of contact with the appropriate body-consciousness element – this is feeling at that time. ^1-558

What is pain at that time? Whatever bodily unpleasantness, bodily pain, unpleasant painful sensation born of body-contact, unpleasant painful feeling born of body-contact there is at that time – this is pain at that time. ^1-559

What is the pain faculty at that time? Whatever bodily unpleasantness, bodily pain, unpleasant painful sensation born of body-contact, unpleasant painful feeling born of body-contact there is at that time – this is the pain faculty at that time; or whatever other immaterial phenomena there are at that time, arisen dependently – these phenomena are indeterminate.
At that time there are four aggregates, two sense bases, two elements, three nutriments, three faculties, one contact, one body-consciousness element, one mind-object base, one mind-object element; or whatever other immaterial phenomena there are at that time, arisen dependently – these phenomena are indeterminate. ^1-560
