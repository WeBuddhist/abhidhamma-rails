## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Appamaññā)

[[#^toc-13-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye padabhājanīyaṃ dassento, yasmā idaṃ kammaṭṭhānaṃ dosacaritassa sappāyaṃ, tasmā mettāya vatthubhūtaṃ puggalaṃ dassetuṃ "seyyathāpi nāma ekaṃ puggalaṃ piyaṃ manāpa"nti vuttaṃ. Piyatāya hi byāpādassa pahānaṃ hoti, manāpatāya udāsīnatā na saṇṭhāti hirottappañca paccupaṭṭhāti, tato hirottappānupālitā mettā na parihāyati; evaṃ appanāppattāya vasībhāvaṃ upagatāya mettāya majjhattaverisaṅkhātepi sabbe satte anukkamena pharati. Karuṇāya pana duggataṃ durupetaṃ, muditāya jotibhāve ṭhitaṃ piyaṃ manāpaṃ, upekkhāya neva manāpaṃ na amanāpaṃ puggalaṃ vatthuṃ katvā dassitaṃ. Sesaṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe vuttaṃ, bhāvanāvidhānampi visuddhimagge vitthārato kathitaṃ.

##### Loving-Kindness ^2-13-1-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-643]]

And how does a monk dwell pervading one direction with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness? Just as, on seeing one person who is dear and pleasing, he would feel loving-kindness, in the same way he pervades all beings with loving-kindness.
Therein, what is loving-kindness? The friendliness towards beings, being friendly, the state of friendliness, the liberation of mind through loving-kindness — this is called "loving-kindness".
Therein, what is the mind? The mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the mind". This mind is accompanied by this loving-kindness, arisen together with it, conjoined with it, associated with it. Therefore it is said, "with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness". ^2-643

"With a mind accompanied by sympathetic joy": therein, what is sympathetic joy? The gladness towards beings, being glad, the state of gladness, the liberation of mind through sympathetic joy — this is called "sympathetic joy".
Therein, what is the mind? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the mind". This mind is accompanied by this sympathetic joy, arisen together with it, conjoined with it, associated with it. Therefore it is said, "with a mind accompanied by sympathetic joy". ^2-669

##### Equanimity ^2-13-1-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-673]]

And how does a monk dwell pervading one direction with a mind accompanied by equanimity? Just as, on seeing one person who is neither pleasing nor displeasing, he would be equanimous, in the same way he pervades all beings with equanimity.
Therein, what is equanimity? The equanimity towards beings, being equanimous, the state of equanimity, the liberation of mind through equanimity — this is called "equanimity".
Therein, what is the mind? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the mind". This mind is accompanied by this equanimity, arisen together with it, conjoined with it, associated with it. Therefore it is said, "with a mind accompanied by equanimity". ^2-673
