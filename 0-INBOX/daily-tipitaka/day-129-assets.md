### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Maggaṅga)

[[#^toc-11-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye "ariyo"ti avatvā "aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo"ti vuttaṃ; evaṃ avuttepi ayaṃ ariyo eva, yathā muddhābhisittāya deviyā kucchismiṃ jāto putto "rājaputto"ti avuttepi rājaputtoyeva. Pañcaṅgikavārepi "aṭṭhaṅgiko"ti avuttepi aṭṭhaṅgikova veditabbo, lokuttaramaggo hi pañcaṅgiko nāma natthi; "pañcaṅgiko maggo"ti pana atirekakiccadassanatthaṃ gahitaṃ, imāni pañca kārāpakaṅgāneva micchāvācādīni pajahanti, sammāvācādayo pana sayaṃ virativasena pūrenti. Vitaṇḍavādissa vādo mahāsaḷāyatana-uparipaṇṇāsa-subhaddasuttādīhi bhinditvā "aṭṭhaṅgikova lokuttaramaggo"ti dassito. Nayā panettha aṭṭhavīsati nayasahassāni vibhattāni, vipāke pana kusalato tiguṇā nayā kātabbā.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Maggaṅga)

[[#^toc-11-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva maggaṅgānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana sabbānipetāni appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattito appamāṇārammaṇāneva, na maggārammaṇāni na maggādhipatīni; nibbānassa pana bahiddhādhammattā bahiddhārammaṇāni nāma honti. Evametasmiṃ pañhāpucchakepi nibbattitalokuttarāneva maggaṅgāni kathitānīti.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Maggaṅga)

[[#^toc-11-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye "ariyo"ti avuttepi ayaṃ ariyova, lokuttaramaggo ca aṭṭhaṅgikova. Yasmiṃ samaye micchāvācaṃ pajahati sammāvācaṃ pūreti, tasmiṃ samaye pañca kārāpakaṅgāneva micchāvācaṃ pajahanti, sammāvācā pana sayaṃ virativasena pūreti; evaṃ micchākammante micchāājīve ca. Tasmā yoginā "pañcaṅgikova maggo"ti vitaṇḍagāhaṃ pahāya, catūhi vacīduccaritehi tīhi kāyaduccaritehi micchājīvato ca ekekaṃ viratiṃ ariyaṃ anāsavaṃ lokuttaraṃ maggaṅgaṃ katvā, sammādiṭṭhiādīni aṭṭha maggaṅgāni upasampādetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Maggaṅga)

[[#^toc-11-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake maggaṅgāni nibbattitalokuttarāneva, appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattattā appamāṇārammaṇāni bahiddhārammaṇāni ca, na pana maggārammaṇāni na maggādhipatīni. ##### The Triads ^2-11-3-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-506]]

They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. Right intention is associated with pleasant feeling; seven path factors may be associated with pleasant feeling or may be associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They may be results; they may be things subject to result. They are not taken up at birth and not able to be clung to. They are undefiled and not connected with defilement. Right intention is without thought but with examination only; seven path factors may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination. Right intention is accompanied by joy and accompanied by happiness, not accompanied by equanimity; seven path factors may be accompanied by joy, may be accompanied by happiness, or may be accompanied by equanimity. They are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. Their causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. They may be leading to dismantling; they may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling. They may belong to one in training; they may belong to one beyond training. They are measureless. They have measureless objects. They are refined. They may be fixed in rightness; they may be unfixed. They do not have the path as their object; they may have the path as their cause; they may have the path as their dominant factor; they may not be describable either as having the path as their cause or as having the path as their dominant factor. They may be arisen; they may be not arisen; they may be due to arise. They may be past; they may be future; they may be present. They are not describable as having a past object, a future object, or a present object. They may be internal; they may be external; they may be both internal and external. They have external objects. They are invisible and offer no resistance. ^2-506

##### The Pairs ^2-11-3-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-507]]

Right view is a root; seven path factors are not roots. They have roots. They are associated with roots. Right view is both a root and has roots; seven path factors are not describable as being both roots and having roots; they have roots but are not roots. Right view is both a root and associated with roots; seven path factors are not describable as being both roots and associated with roots; they are associated with roots but are not roots. Seven path factors are not roots yet have roots; right view is not describable either as being a non-root that has roots or as being a non-root without roots.
They have conditions. They are conditioned. They are invisible. They offer no resistance. They are immaterial. They are beyond the world. They can be known by one means; they cannot be known by another.
They are not mental taints. They are without mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints. They are not describable either as being mental taints and subject to mental taints, or as being subject to mental taints but not mental taints. They are not describable either as being mental taints and associated with mental taints, or as being associated with mental taints but not mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints. They are without mental taints.
They are not fetters… they are not ties… they are not floods… they are not yokes… they are not hindrances… they are not wrong grasping… they have objects. They are not minds. They are mental factors. They are associated with mind. They are conjoined with mind. They are originated by mind. They arise together with mind. They follow along with mind. They are conjoined with and originated by mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and arise together with mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and follow along with mind. They are external. They are not derived. They are not taken up at birth.
They are not clinging… they are not defilements… they are not to be abandoned by seeing. They are not to be abandoned by meditative development. Their causes are not to be abandoned by seeing. Their causes are not to be abandoned by meditative development. Right intention is without thought; seven path factors may be with thought or may be without thought. Right intention is with examination; seven path factors may be with examination or may be without examination. Right intention is with joy; seven path factors may be with joy or may be without joy. Right intention is accompanied by joy; seven path factors may be accompanied by joy or may be not accompanied by joy. Right intention is accompanied by happiness; seven path factors may be accompanied by happiness or may be not accompanied by happiness. Right intention is not accompanied by equanimity; seven path factors may be accompanied by equanimity or may be not accompanied by equanimity. They do not belong to the sense sphere. They do not belong to the fine-material sphere. They do not belong to the immaterial sphere. They are not included in any sphere. They may be leading out; they may be not leading out. They may be fixed; they may be unfixed. They are unsurpassed. They are free from conflict.
