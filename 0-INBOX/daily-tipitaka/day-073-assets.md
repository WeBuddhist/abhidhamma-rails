## Pāli source verses

##### Saṃyojanagocchakaṃ ^1-4-2-4-0

1477. Katame dhammā saṃyojanā? Dasa saṃyojanāni – kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ, mānasaṃyojanaṃ, diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ, vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ, sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ, bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ, issāsaṃyojanaṃ, macchariyasaṃyojanaṃ, avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ. Kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Mānasaṃyojanaṃ catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ catūsu diṭṭhigatasampayuttesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ vicikicchāsahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ catūsu diṭṭhigatasampayuttesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Issāsaṃyojanañca macchariyasaṃyojanañca dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjanti. Avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ sabbākusalesu uppajjati – ime dhammā saṃyojanā. ^1-1477

1478. Katame dhammā no saṃyojanā. Ṭhapetvā saṃyojane avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no saṃyojanā. ^1-1478

1479. Katame dhammā saṃyojaniyā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā saṃyojaniyā. ^1-1479

1480. Katame dhammā asaṃyojaniyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā asaṃyojaniyā. ^1-1480

1481. Katame dhammā saṃyojanasampayuttā? Uddhaccasahagataṃ mohaṃ ṭhapetvā avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā saṃyojanasampayuttā. ^1-1481

1482. Katame dhammā saṃyojanavippayuttā? Uddhaccasahagato moho, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā saṃyojanavippayuttā. ^1-1482

1483. Katame dhammā saṃyojanā ceva saṃyojaniyā ca? Tāneva saṃyojanāni saṃyojanā ceva saṃyojaniyā ca. ^1-1483

1484. Katame dhammā saṃyojaniyā ceva no ca saṃyojanā? Ṭhapetvā saṃyojane avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā saṃyojaniyā ceva no ca saṃyojanā. Asaṃyojaniyā dhammā na vattabbā – saṃyojanā ceva saṃyojaniyā cātipi, saṃyojaniyā ceva no ca saṃyojanātipi. ^1-1484

1485. Katame dhammā saṃyojanā ceva saṃyojanasampayuttā ca? Yattha dve tīṇi saṃyojanāni ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā saṃyojanā ceva saṃyojanasampayuttā ca. ^1-1485

1486. Katame dhammā saṃyojanasampayuttā ceva no ca saṃyojanā? Ṭhapetvā saṃyojane, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ – ime dhammā saṃyojanasampayuttā ceva no ca saṃyojanā. Saṃyojanavippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – saṃyojanā ceva saṃyojanasampayuttā cātipi, saṃyojanasampayuttā ceva no ca saṃyojanātipi. ^1-1486

1487. Katame dhammā saṃyojanavippayuttā saṃyojaniyā? Uddhaccasahagato moho, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā saṃyojanavippayuttā saṃyojaniyā. ^1-1487

1488. Katame dhammā saṃyojanavippayuttā asaṃyojaniyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā saṃyojanavippayuttā asaṃyojaniyā. Saṃyojanasampayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – saṃyojanavippayuttā saṃyojaniyātipi, saṃyojanavippayuttā asaṃyojaniyātipi. ^1-1488

##### Ganthagocchakaṃ ^1-4-2-5-0

1489. Katame dhammā ganthā? Cattāro ganthā – abhijjhākāyagantho, byāpādo kāyagantho, sīlabbataparāmāso kāyagantho, idaṃsaccābhiniveso kāyagantho. Abhijjhākāyagantho aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Byāpādo kāyagantho dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati. Sīlabbataparāmāso kāyagantho ca idaṃsaccābhiniveso kāyagantho ca catūsu diṭṭhigatasampayuttesu cittuppādesu uppajjanti – ime dhammā ganthā. ^1-1489

1490. Katame dhammā no ganthā? Ṭhapetvā ganthe, avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no ganthā. ^1-1490

1491. Katame dhammā ganthaniyā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā ganthaniyā. ^1-1491

1492. Katame dhammā aganthaniyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā aganthaniyā. ^1-1492

1493. Katame dhammā ganthasampayuttā? Cattāro diṭṭhigatasampayuttacittuppādā , cattāro diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatacittuppādā, etthuppannaṃ lobhaṃ ṭhapetvā, dve domanassasahagatacittuppādā, etthuppannaṃ paṭighaṃ ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā ganthasampayuttā. ^1-1493

1494. Katame dhammā ganthavippayuttā? Catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppanno lobho, dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppannaṃ paṭighaṃ, vicikicchāsahagato cittuppādo, uddhaccasahagato cittuppādo, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā ganthavippayuttā. ^1-1494

1495. Katame dhammā ganthā ceva ganthaniyā ca? Teva ganthā ganthā ceva ganthaniyā ca. ^1-1495

1496. Katame dhammā ganthaniyā ceva no ca ganthā? Ṭhapetvā ganthe avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā ganthaniyā ceva no ca ganthā. Aganthaniyā dhammā na vattabbā – ganthā ceva ganthaniyā cātipi, ganthaniyā ceva no ca ganthātipi. ^1-1496

1497. Katame dhammā ganthā ceva ganthasampayuttā ca? Yattha diṭṭhi ca lobho ca ekato uppajjanti – ime dhammā ganthā ceva ganthasampayuttā ca. ^1-1497

1498. Katame dhammā ganthasampayuttā ceva no ca ganthā? Aṭṭha lobhasahagatacittuppādā dve domanassasahagatacittuppādā, etthuppanne ganthe ṭhapetvā – ime dhammā ganthasampayuttā ceva no ca ganthā. Ganthavippayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – ganthā ceva ganthasampayuttā cātipi, ganthasampayuttā ceva no ca ganthātipi. ^1-1498

1499. Katame dhammā ganthavippayuttā ganthaniyā? Catūsu diṭṭhigatavippayuttalobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppanno lobho, dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppannaṃ paṭighaṃ, vicikicchāsahagato cittuppādo, uddhaccasahagato cittuppādo, tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā ganthavippayuttā ganthaniyā. ^1-1499

1500. Katame dhammā ganthavippayuttā aganthaniyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā ganthavippayuttā aganthaniyā. Ganthasampayuttā dhammā na vattabbā – ganthavippayuttā ganthaniyātipi, ganthavippayuttā aganthaniyātipi. ^1-1500

##### Nīvaraṇagocchakaṃ ^1-4-2-8-0

1503. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇā? Cha nīvaraṇā – kāmacchandanīvaraṇaṃ, byāpādanīvaraṇaṃ, thinamiddhanīvaraṇaṃ, uddhaccakukkuccanīvaraṇaṃ, vicikicchānīvaraṇaṃ, avijjānīvaraṇaṃ . Kāmacchandanīvaraṇaṃ aṭṭhasu lobhasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati, byāpādanīvaraṇaṃ dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati, thinamiddhanīvaraṇaṃ sasaṅkhārikesu akusalesu uppajjati, uddhaccanīvaraṇaṃ uddhaccasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati, kukkuccanīvaraṇaṃ dvīsu domanassasahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati, vicikicchānīvaraṇaṃ vicikicchāsahagatesu cittuppādesu uppajjati, avijjānīvaraṇaṃ sabbākusalesu uppajjati – ime dhammā nīvaraṇā. ^1-1503

1504. Katame dhammā no nīvaraṇā? Ṭhapetvā nīvaraṇe avasesaṃ akusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, rūpañca, nibbānañca – ime dhammā no nīvaraṇā. ^1-1504

1505. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇiyā? Tīsu bhūmīsu kusalaṃ, akusalaṃ, tīsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṃ, sabbañca rūpaṃ – ime dhammā nīvaraṇiyā. ^1-1505

1506. Katame dhammā anīvaraṇiyā? Cattāro maggā apariyāpannā, cattāri ca sāmaññaphalāni, nibbānañca – ime dhammā anīvaraṇiyā. ^1-1506

1507. Katame dhammā nīvaraṇasampayuttā? Dvādasa akusalacittuppādā – ime dhammā nīvaraṇasampayuttā. ^1-1507

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Saṃyojanagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṃyojanagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yattha dve tīṇi saṃyojanāni ekato uppajjantīti dasadhā saṃyojanānaṃ ekato uppatti veditabbā. Kāmarāgo catudhā ekato uppajjati – mānasaṃyojanaavijjāsaṃyojanehi ceva, diṭṭhisaṃyojanaavijjāsaṃyojanehi ca, sīlabbataparāmāsaavijjāsaṃyojanehi ca, avijjāsaṃyojanamatteneva ca saddhinti. Paṭigho tidhā, māno ekadhā. Tathā vicikicchā. Iminā nayena atthuddhāro veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1930]]

#### Ganthagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Ganthagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese ganthānaṃ atthuddhāraṃ āsavagocchakānaṃ vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Tattha tīṇi akusalamūlāni lobho doso moho, tadekaṭṭhā ca kilesāti sahajekaṭṭhapahānekaṭṭhabhāvena atthuddhāro kato. Iminā upāyena sesagocchakesupi desanānayo catūsu bhūmīsu kusalesupi akusalesupi ca veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1929]]

#### Oghagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Oghagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese oghānaṃ atthuddhāraṃ āsavagocchakānaṃ vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Tattha catubhūmakakusalaṃ kusalamūlehi pariyādiyitvā, taṃsampayuttacatukkhandhavasena pariyādiyitvā, tīṇaṃ kammadvārānaṃ vasena pariyādiyitvā dassesi dhammarājā. Akusalepi eseva nayo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1929]]

#### Yogagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Yogagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yogānaṃ atthuddhāraṃ āsavagocchakānaṃ vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Iminā upāyena sesagocchakesupi desanānayo veditabbo – kusalādīnaṃ atthuddhāro catūsu bhūmīsu kusalesu akusalesu vipākesu kiriyāsupi ca mūlakhandhadvāranayehi paricchinditvā atthuddhāravasena dassito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1929]]

#### Nīvaraṇagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-5-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Nīvaraṇagocchakānaṃ atthuddhāraniddese yattha dve tīṇi nīvaraṇāni ekato uppajjantīti aṭṭhadhā nīvaraṇānaṃ ekato uppatti veditabbā. Kāmacchando duvidhā ekato uppajjati – asaṅkhārikacittesu uddhaccanīvaraṇaavijjānīvaraṇehi, sasaṅkhārikesu thinamiddhauddhaccaavijjānīvaraṇehi saddhiṃ. Byāpādo catudhā ekato uppajjati; uddhaccaṃ ekadhā; vicikicchā ekadhāva ekato uppajjatīti.

#### Saṃyojanagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṃyojanaatthuddhāre dasa saṃyojanāni ekato uppattivasena uddhaṭāni — kāmarāgo catudhā (mānadiṭṭhisīlabbataparāmāsaavijjāsaṃyojanehi saha), paṭigho tidhā, māno vicikicchā ca ekadhā ekato uppajjati. #### Ganthagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Ganthaatthuddhāre cattāro ganthā (abhijjhākāyagantho byāpādakāyagantho sīlabbataparāmāsakāyagantho idaṃsaccābhinivesakāyagantho) āsavānaṃ vuttanayeneva uddhaṭā. Tayo akusalamūlā (lobho doso moho) tadekaṭṭhā ca kilesā sahajekaṭṭhapahānekaṭṭhabhāvena dassitā, yehi kāyo cittañca ganthiyati. Tasmā yoginā ime ganthe "idha uppajjantī"ti ñatvā, abhijjhābyāpāde mettāsantosena, parāmāse sammādiṭṭhiyā, ganthe chindanto, visaṃyuttaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Oghagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Oghaatthuddhāre cattāro oghā (kāmogho bhavogho diṭṭhogho avijjogho) āsavānaṃ vuttanayeneva uddhaṭā — catubhūmakaṃ kusalaṃ kusalamūlehi, taṃsampayuttacatukkhandhavasena, tīṇaṃ kammadvārānaṃ vasena ca pariyādiyitvā dhammarājā dassesi. Yehi oghehi sattā saṃsārasāgare vuyhanti. Tasmā yoginā ime oghe "idha pavattantī"ti ñatvā, kāmabhavesu taṇhaṃ, diṭṭhiṃ, avijjañca ariyamaggaplavena taritvā, thale patiṭṭhitacittena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-1929]]

#### Yogagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Yogaatthuddhāre cattāro yogā (kāmayogo bhavayogo diṭṭhiyogo avijjāyogo) āsavānaṃ vuttanayeneva uddhaṭā — catūsu bhūmīsu kusalākusalavipākakiriyāsu mūlakhandhadvāranayehi paricchinditvā dassitā. Yehi yogehi sattā vaṭṭe yojiyanti. Tasmā yoginā ime yoge "idha yojentī"ti ñatvā, kāmayogaṃ bhavayogaṃ diṭṭhiavijjāyogañca maggañāṇena visaṃyojetvā, yogakkhemaṃ nibbānaninnacittena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-1929]]

#### Nīvaraṇagocchakaṃ (Atthuddhāra)

[[#^toc-4-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Nīvaraṇaatthuddhāre nīvaraṇāni ekato uppattivasena uddhaṭāni — kāmacchando duvidhā (asaṅkhārike uddhaccaavijjānīvaraṇehi, sasaṅkhārike thinamiddhauddhaccaavijjānīvaraṇehi saha), byāpādo catudhā, uddhaccaṃ vicikicchā ca ekadhā ekato uppajjati. Etāni cittaṃ āvaritvā samādhiṃ paññañca nivārenti. Tasmā yoginā "imasmiṃ cittuppāde ime nīvaraṇā ekato uppajjantī"ti ñatvā, kāmacchandaṃ asubhena, byāpādaṃ mettāya, thinamiddhaṃ ālokasaññāya, uddhaccakukkuccaṃ samathena, vicikicchaṃ dhammavavatthānena vinodetvā, nīvaraṇavimuttaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/en-dhammasangani-ai.md`)

Which phenomena are fetters? The ten fetters: sensual craving, ill-will, conceit, wrong view, doubt, adherence to rites and rituals, craving for existence, envy, avarice, ignorance. Sensual craving arises in the eight greed-rooted consciousnesses. Ill-will arises in the two hate-rooted consciousnesses. Conceit arises in the four greed-rooted consciousnesses disjoined from wrong view. Wrong view arises in the four greed-rooted consciousnesses conjoined with wrong view. Doubt arises in the doubt-rooted consciousness. Adherence to rites and rituals arises in the four greed-rooted consciousnesses conjoined with wrong view. Craving for existence arises in the four greed-rooted consciousnesses disjoined from wrong view. Envy and avarice arise in the two hate-rooted consciousnesses. Ignorance arises in all unwholesome consciousnesses – these phenomena are fetters. ^1-1477

Which phenomena are not fetters? Excluding the fetters, the remaining unwholesome phenomena, wholesome phenomena in the four planes, resultant phenomena in the four planes, functional phenomena in the three planes, material phenomena, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not fetters. ^1-1478

Which phenomena are capable of being fettered? Wholesome phenomena in the three planes, unwholesome phenomena, resultant phenomena in the three planes, functional phenomena in the three planes, and all material phenomena – these phenomena are capable of being fettered. ^1-1479

Which phenomena are not capable of being fettered? The four supramundane paths, the four supramundane fruits, and Nibbāna – these phenomena are not capable of being fettered. ^1-1480

Which phenomena are associated with fetters? Excluding ignorance accompanied by restlessness, the remaining unwholesome phenomena – these phenomena are associated with fetters. ^1-1481

Which dhammas are disjoined from fetters? Delusion accompanied by restlessness, wholesome consciousness in the four planes, resultant consciousness in the four planes, functional indeterminate consciousness in the three planes, matter, and Nibbana – these dhammas are disjoined from fetters. ^1-1482

Which dhammas are both fetters and conducive to fetters? The fetters themselves are both fetters and conducive to fetters. ^1-1483
