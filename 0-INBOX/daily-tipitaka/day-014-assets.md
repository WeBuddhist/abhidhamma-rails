## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-summaries.md`)

#### Catasso paṭipadā (Rūpāvacara)

[[#^toc-2-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacarajjhānaṃ nāma paṭipadākamena sijjhati. Sā pana dukkhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā, dukkhā paṭipadā khippābhiññā, sukhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā, sukhā paṭipadā khippābhiññāti catubbidhā. Tattha ādito kilese vikkhambhentassa kasirapaṭipattitāya dukkhā, appanāparivāse maggapātubhāvamandatāya dandhābhiññāti evaṃ catasso paṭipadā yathārahaṃ veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1092]]

#### Cattāri ārammaṇāni

[[#^toc-2-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Jhānaṃ nāma yathā paṭipadābhedena evaṃ ārammaṇabhedenāpi catubbidho hoti – parittaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, parittaṃ appamāṇārammaṇaṃ, appamāṇaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, appamāṇaṃ appamāṇārammaṇanti. Tattha avaḍḍhite ārammaṇe parittārammaṇatā, vipule vaḍḍhite appamāṇārammaṇatā. Jhānassa pana uparijjhānapaccayasamatthatāya hīnapaṇītabhāvena parittaappamāṇārammaṇatā veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1100]]

#### Soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Idāni ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissakaṃ soḷasakkhattukanayaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha paṭipadācatukkañca ārammaṇacatukkañca aññamaññaṃ yojetvā soḷasa jhānanayā nibbattanti. Paṭhama nayo catūhi kāraṇehi hīno, soḷasamo pana catūhi kāraṇehi paṇītoti evaṃ jhānuppattikāraṇattā puggalajjhāsayavasena ca ayaṃ desanā katā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1102]]

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-1-practice.md`)

### Catasso paṭipadā — Soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Paṭipadācatukke cattāro paṭipadā vibhajiyanti: dukkhā dandhābhiññā, dukkhā khippābhiññā, sukhā dandhābhiññā, sukhā khippābhiññāti. Imesu catūsu paṭipadāsu yā yassa sattassa sappāyā hoti tāya paṭipadāya jhānaṃ bhāvetabbaṃ. Indriyānaṃ tīkhabhāvena vā mudubhāvena jhānakkhippaṃ vā dandhaṃ paṭilabhati; tasmā sattassa indriyaparopariyattaṃ ñatvā anucchavikā paṭipadā sevitabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-5-6-0]]

---

### Cattāri ārammaṇāni

[[#^toc-1-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Ārammaṇacatukke cattāri ārammaṇāni vibhajiyanti: parittaṃ, appamāṇaṃ, paṇītaṃ, majjhimaṃ ca. Kasiṇassa ārammaṇaṃ parittato vā appamāṇato vā bhāvetvā jhānaṃ paṭilabhituṃ sakkā hoti. Yathā ārammaṇassa visāraṇena ca sankocanaṃ ca sikkhamāno yogī kasiṇārammaṇaṃ sampādeti, tathā ārammaṇaṃ yathāvato sikkhitabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-4-5-7-0]]

---

### Soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Soḷasakkhattukaṃ catukkanayena catasso paṭipadā catussoḷasakkhattu samāpajjanāya vibhajiyati. Sotāpannādīnaṃ pañca jhānāni catukkanayena soḷasakkhattu samāpajjitvā jhānassa vasī hoti. Evaṃ jhānaṃ anupubbena vasibhāvaṃ ānetvā vipassanāya ādhāraṃ karoti.

When one develops the path to rebirth in the fine-material realms, secluded from sensual pleasures... one abides having attained the first jhāna, through pleasant practice, with swift discernment, limited, and with a boundless object, using the earth kasiṇa. At that time, contact is present... undistractedness is present... these states are wholesome. ^1-199
