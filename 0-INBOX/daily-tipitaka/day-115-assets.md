#### Abyākataniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abyākataṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe āgatapaṭipāṭiyāva vibhattaṃ. Sabbavāresu cettha avijjāmūlakā nayā parihīnā; kasmā? Avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbassa abhāvato — kusalacittesu hi avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbaṃ kusalamūlaṃ atthi, cakkhuviññāṇādīsu natthi; sahetukesu pana kiñcāpi atthi, evaṃ santepi idha pacchinnattā na gahitaṃ. Visesato panettha cakkhuviññāṇādīsu taṇhāṭṭhānaṃ upādānaṭṭhānañca parihīnaṃ, taṇhāṭṭhānārahassa balavadhammassa abhāvā adhimokkharahitattā ca; sesāhetukesu taṇhāṭṭhānameva parihīnaṃ; sahetukesu pana pasādasabbhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne pasādena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Evamettha kusalākusalavipākesu cakkhuviññāṇādīsu saṅkhāraviññāṇanāmachaṭṭhāyatanaphassavedanāmūlakā cha cha nayā, sesāhetukesu adhimokkhamūlakena saddhiṃ satta satta, sahetukesu pasādamūlakena saddhiṃ aṭṭha aṭṭha nayā veditabbā. Cakkhuviññāṇādīsu ca catunnampi catukkānaṃ ādivārova vutto; tatiyacatutthavārā asambhavatoyeva na vuttā — rūpamissakā hi te, na ca cakkhuviññāṇādīni rūpaṃ samuṭṭhāpenti. Arūpāvacaravipākasmiṃ pana vāradvayameva labbhati.

##### The Exposition of the Neutral ^2-6-2-12-0

306. Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because sense-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, resultant eye-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-306

307. Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding eye-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition".
Therein, what is the mentality that has consciousness as condition? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations — this is called "the mentality that has consciousness as condition".
Therein, what is the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding eye-consciousness element — this is called "the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition".
Therein, what is the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition? The contact, touching, being in contact, state of being in contact — this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental experience that is neither comfortable nor uncomfortable, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant experience born of mind-contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has feeling as condition? Setting aside feeling: the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has feeling as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering". ^2-307
