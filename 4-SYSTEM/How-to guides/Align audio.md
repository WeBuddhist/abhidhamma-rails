# Workflow: Transcribing Dhamma Voice Videos with oTranscribe

This guide outlines the process of taking a YouTube video from Dhamma Voice, transcribing it using oTranscribe, and syncing the output back into your Obsidian vault.

## Phase 1: Preparation & Setup

### 1. Open oTranscribe

- Navigate to [otranscribe.com](https://otranscribe.com/ "null").
    
- Click the **"Start Transcribing"** button.
    

### 2. Source the Video

- Go to [dhammavoice.lk](https://www.dhammavoice.lk/index.shtml "null").
    
- Find the specific teaching or sermon you wish to transcribe.
    
- Copy the YouTube video link (right-click the video and select "Copy video URL").
    
- In oTranscribe, click **"Choose video file"** (or use the YouTube link option) and paste the URL.
    

## Phase 2: Transcribing & Formatting

### 3. Prepare Obsidian Text

- Open your **Obsidian Vault**.
    
- Locate the existing notes or draft text you wish to use as a baseline.
    
- Copy the text and paste it into the main text area of oTranscribe.
    

### 4. Insert Timestamps

Timestamps allow you to jump to specific moments in the audio.

- As you listen, pause or play the video using `ESC`.
    
- To insert a timestamp for a line of text, press **`Ctrl + J`** (or `Cmd + J` on Mac).
    
- **Pro Tip:** Ensure every new paragraph or significant line has a timestamp at the beginning for easy navigation later.
    

## Phase 3: Exporting & Saving

### 5. Export as oTranscribe Format

To preserve the interactive nature of the timestamps and the connection to the video:

- Click the **Settings (gear icon)** or the **Export** button in the top right of the oTranscribe toolbar.
    
- Select **"oTranscribe format (.otr)"**.
    
- This file contains your text, the timestamps, and the reference to the video source.
    

## Phase 4: Obsidian Integration

### 6. Upload to Obsidian Vault

- Locate the downloaded `.otr` file on your computer.
    
- Open your Obsidian file explorer.
    
- Drag and drop the `.otr` file into the following path:
    
    - `1. Sources` > `Audio`
        
- **Note:** While Obsidian may not render the `.otr` file as a native note, keeping it in this folder ensures you have a backup of the interactive transcription session that can be re-loaded into oTranscribe at any time.
    

## Shortcut Summary

|   |   |
|---|---|
|**Action**|**Shortcut**|
|Play / Pause|`ESC`|
|Insert Timestamp|`Ctrl + J`|
|Rewind / Fast Forward|`F1` / `F2`|
|Bold Text|`Ctrl + B`|
|Italicize Text|`Ctrl + I`|
