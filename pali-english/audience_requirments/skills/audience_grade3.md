# Audience Profile: Grade 3 Students

## Audience

Children approximately 8–9 years old.

---

## Translation Goal

Communicate the main meaning using language appropriate for young children.

---

## Terminology

Avoid technical Buddhist vocabulary whenever possible.

For terms covered by `pali-english/audience_requirments/termbase/termbase-pi-1-audience_grade3.md`, use the **Translation** column, keyed by **Sense Tag**. Every occurrence of the same Sense Tag must use identical wording across the text.

Examples (lemma, Sense Tag → Beginner Translation):

* citta, `mind` → mind
* vedanā, `feeling_aggregate` → how something feels — good, bad, or neutral
* saṅkhāra, `volitional_formations` → the mind's habits and intentions that shape actions
* dhamma, `phenomenon` → thing that arises and passes away
* kilesa, `defilement` → a harmful habit of mind

For lemmas not yet in the termbase (e.g. saññā → recognizing things), choose a simple, consistent phrasing and reuse it for that sense everywhere it occurs.

Use familiar words.

---

## Style

* Very simple
* Short sentences
* Concrete language

---

## Explanatory Additions

Moderate simplification is permitted.

Do not distort the meaning.

---

## Priority

1. Understanding
2. Simplicity
3. Core meaning
4. Accuracy
