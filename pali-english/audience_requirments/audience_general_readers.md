# Audience Profile: General Readers

## Audience

Readers with little or no Buddhist background.

---

## Translation Goal

Make the text understandable without prior knowledge of Buddhist terminology.

---

## Terminology

Prefer understandable English over technical vocabulary.

Examples:

* citta → mind
* vedanā → feeling
* saññā → perception
* saṅkhāra → mental formations

Retain only highly established Buddhist terms:

* Dhamma
* Nibbāna
* Buddha

---

## Style

* Clear
* Modern
* Natural English

---

## Explanatory Additions

Minor clarification through wording is permitted.

Do not add information absent from the source.

---

## Priority

1. Understanding
2. Accuracy
3. Readability
4. Consistency
