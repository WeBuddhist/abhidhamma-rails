# Audience Profile: Scholarly / Academic

## Audience

* Buddhist Studies researchers
* Pāli scholars
* Translators
* Graduate students
* Academic readers

---

## Translation Goal

Maximize doctrinal precision and terminological consistency.

Assume readers are familiar with Buddhist technical vocabulary.

---

## Terminology

Use Canonical Translation exactly as provided by the termbase.

Examples:

* dhamma → phenomenon
* vedanā → feeling
* saññā → perception
* citta → consciousness
* saṅkhāra → formations

Do not simplify terminology.

---

## Style

* Formal
* Precise
* Technical
* Minimal interpretation

---

## Explanatory Additions

Do not add explanations.

Do not paraphrase technical terms.

---

## Priority

1. Accuracy
2. Consistency
3. Readability
