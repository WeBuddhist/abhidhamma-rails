# The Book of Mind-States (Dhammasaṅgaṇī)

## Mātikā (the list of topics)

### Tikamātikā (Groups of Three)

1. Things that are good for the mind. / Things that are not good for the mind. / Things that are neither.

2. Things that come with a good feeling. / Things that come with an uncomfortable feeling. / Things that come with a feeling that is neither good nor bad.

3. Things that are results from before. / Things that can produce results later. / Things that are neither results nor can produce results.

4. Things that are clung to, and that people can cling to. / Things that are not clung to now, but that people can still cling to. / Things that are not clung to, and that people cannot cling to.

5. Things that are dirtied, and that can make the mind dirty. / Things that are not dirtied, but that can still make the mind dirty. / Things that are not dirtied, and that cannot make the mind dirty.

6. Things that come with thinking about something and looking at it closely. / Things that come with looking closely only, without first thinking about it. / Things that come with neither thinking about it nor looking closely.

7. Things that come together with joy. / Things that come together with a good feeling. / Things that come together with a calm, even feeling.

8. Things given up by seeing the truth clearly for the first time. / Things given up slowly through practice. / Things not given up by either of these.

9. Things whose causes are given up by seeing the truth clearly for the first time. / Things whose causes are given up slowly through practice. / Things whose causes are not given up by either of these.

10. Things that build up more rebirth. / Things that break down rebirth. / Things that do neither.

11. Things belonging to someone who is still learning. / Things belonging to someone who has finished learning. / Things that are neither.

12. Small things. / Big, far-reaching things. / Things with no limit at all.

13. Things that have something small as their focus. / Things that have something big and far-reaching as their focus. / Things that have something with no limit as their focus.

14. Low things. / Middle things. / The best things.

15. Things that are wrong, and stuck being wrong. / Things that are right, and stuck being right. / Things that are not stuck either way.

16. Things that have the path to freedom as their focus. / Things that are caused by the path to freedom. / Things that are led by the path to freedom.

17. Things that have already arisen. / Things that have not yet arisen. / Things that are sure to arise.

18. Things from the past. / Things from the future. / Things happening right now.

19. Things that focus on something from the past. / Things that focus on something from the future. / Things that focus on something happening right now.

20. Things inside a person. / Things outside a person. / Things both inside and outside a person.

21. Things that focus on something inside a person. / Things that focus on something outside a person. / Things that focus on something both inside and outside a person.

22. Things that can be seen and that can be felt by the senses hitting against them. / Things that cannot be seen but that can be felt by the senses hitting against them. / Things that cannot be seen and cannot be felt by the senses hitting against them.
