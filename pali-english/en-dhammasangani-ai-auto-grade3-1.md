##### Breaking Down the Words ^1-1-1-1-0

1. What good things are there? When a good mind starts in our everyday world, with a happy feeling and knowing, thinking about something we see, hear, smell, taste, touch, or think about — at that time there is touch, feeling, recognizing, choosing, mind, putting the mind onto something, keeping the mind on something, a feeling of joy, comfort, the mind staying on one thing, trust, trying hard, remembering, calm focus, wisdom, right seeing, right aiming, right trying, right remembering, right calm focus, powers of trust, trying hard, remembering, calm focus, and wisdom, not wanting too much, not being mean, not being foolish, not grabbing, not wishing harm, feeling shame about wrong, worrying about doing wrong, calm in body-mind things, calm in the mind, lightness, softness, ease of use, fitness, straightness, remembering, knowing what we are doing, calm peace, clear seeing, trying hard, not getting confused, and other good mind things that come from causes — these are good things. ^1-1

2. What is touch at that time? The touch that is touching and being touched — that is touch at that time. ^1-2

3. What is feeling at that time? The happy feeling in the mind from mind-touch — that is feeling at that time. ^1-3

4. What is recognizing at that time? The recognizing that comes from mind-touch — that is recognizing at that time. ^1-4

5. What is choosing at that time? The choosing and willing that comes from mind-touch — that is choosing at that time. ^1-5

6. What is the mind at that time? The mind that knows and thinks at that time — that is the mind at that time. ^1-6

7. What is putting the mind onto something at that time? The mind placing itself on something in a right way — that is putting the mind onto something at that time. ^1-7

8. What is keeping the mind on something at that time? The mind staying with and looking at something — that is keeping the mind on something at that time. ^1-8

9. What is a feeling of joy at that time? The joy, gladness, and light feeling in the mind — that is a feeling of joy at that time. ^1-9
