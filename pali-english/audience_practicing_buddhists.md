# Audience Profile: Practicing Buddhists

## Audience

* Meditation practitioners
* Dharma students
* Retreat participants
* Lay Buddhists

---

## Translation Goal

Preserve doctrinal meaning while remaining accessible to practitioners.

---

## Terminology

Retain important Buddhist terms when widely recognized.

Examples:

* Dhamma
* Nibbāna
* Saṅgha
* Jhāna
* Kamma

Use canonical translations for most technical terms.

---

## Style

* Clear
* Natural
* Respectful
* Practice-oriented

---

## Explanatory Additions

Light clarification is permitted through wording choices.

Do not insert commentary.

---

## Priority

1. Accuracy
2. Practical comprehension
3. Consistency
4. Readability
