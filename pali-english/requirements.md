# Pāli-to-English Translation Requirements

## Objective

Translate Pāli Buddhist texts into English while preserving meaning, doctrinal accuracy, and consistency.

The translation must faithfully represent the source text while remaining understandable according to the active audience profile.

Audience-specific adaptation is controlled by the selected audience profile, not by this document.

---

# Translation Workflow

For each translation unit:

1. Analyze the source text.
2. Identify relevant lemmas.
3. Consult the termbase.
4. Determine the appropriate sense.
5. Select the corresponding Canonical Translation.
6. Apply audience adaptation rules.
7. Produce the final translation.

---

# Authority Order

When sources conflict, use the following order:

1. Source Pāli Text
2. Translation Termbase
3. Commentary Notes
4. Section Summary
5. Previous Translation Decisions

Do not override the source text unless required to resolve ambiguity.

---

# Sense Resolution

Many Pāli terms have multiple senses.

Examples include:

* dhamma
* citta
* saṅkhāra
* viññāṇa
* vedanā
* saññā

When multiple senses exist:

1. Examine immediate context.
2. Examine surrounding passages.
3. Examine section summary.
4. Examine commentary notes.
5. Select the most appropriate sense.

Do not assume a fixed translation for a lemma.

---

# Use of the Termbase

The termbase is authoritative.

For each identified sense:

* use the corresponding Canonical Translation as the semantic basis
* preserve doctrinal distinctions
* maintain consistency throughout the document

Do not invent new doctrinal meanings.

Do not merge distinct doctrinal concepts.

---

# Context Usage

Use contextual information to determine meaning.

Context may include:

* nearby sentences
* chapter topic
* doctrinal category
* summary files
* commentary files

Context may guide interpretation.

Context must not introduce new content into the translation.

---

# Commentary Usage

Commentary may be used to:

* resolve ambiguity
* identify doctrinal references
* distinguish competing interpretations

Commentary must not be translated into the output.

Commentary must not replace the source text.

Commentary must not add information to the translation.

---

# Summary Usage

Section summaries may be used to:

* identify subject matter
* establish doctrinal context
* improve consistency across sections

Summaries must not be translated.

Summaries must not be inserted into the output.

---

# Terminology Consistency

When the same doctrinal sense appears repeatedly:

* maintain consistent rendering
* avoid stylistic variation

Consistency takes priority over synonym variety.

Do not alternate between multiple English words merely for style.

---

# Repeated Formulae

Pāli literature frequently contains repeated passages.

When wording and meaning remain unchanged:

* preserve translation consistency
* avoid unnecessary variation

Identical source passages should normally produce identical translations.

---

# Enumerations and Lists

Preserve all doctrinal structures.

Examples:

* aggregates
* sense bases
* elements
* dependent origination sequences
* path factors
* classifications
* taxonomies

Do not convert structured lists into narrative prose.

---

# Grammatical Fidelity

Preserve distinctions that affect meaning.

Examples:

* singular and plural
* conditional and unconditional statements
* negation
* comparison
* causation
* temporal relationships

Do not add distinctions absent from the source.

Do not remove distinctions present in the source.

---

# Translation Style

Translate meaning rather than individual words.

Literal translation is preferred when it accurately conveys meaning.

Moderate restructuring is permitted when required for natural English.

Do not paraphrase unnecessarily.

Do not simplify doctrinal content unless instructed by the active audience profile.

---

# Handling Uncertain Cases

When uncertainty remains after consulting all available resources:

1. Select the most defensible interpretation.
2. Prefer established doctrinal usage.
3. Maintain consistency with nearby passages.

Do not invent unsupported meanings.

---

# Prohibited Actions

Do not:

* summarize
* omit source content
* add explanatory material
* add commentary
* insert translator notes
* modernize doctrine
* harmonize contradictory passages
* introduce personal interpretations
* rewrite the text as an explanation

Translate only what is present in the source text.

---

# Formatting Rules

Preserve:

* headings
* numbering
* section identifiers
* list structure
* paragraph boundaries

Unless explicitly instructed otherwise, retain the structure of the source text.

---

# Output Requirements

Output only the translated text.

Do not include:

* explanations
* reasoning
* notes
* commentary
* glossary entries
* translation analysis

Return the translation and nothing else.
