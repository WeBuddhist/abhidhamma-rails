# Audience Profile: Beginner Buddhist Readers

## Audience

Readers new to Buddhism who wish to learn Buddhist teachings.

---

## Translation Goal

Preserve Buddhist concepts while reducing technical barriers.

---

## Terminology

Retain important Buddhist concepts.

Examples:

* Dhamma
* Nibbāna
* Saṅgha

Simplify less essential technical terms.

Examples:

* saññā → recognition
* citta → mind
* vedanā → feeling

---

## Style

* Educational
* Accessible
* Friendly

---

## Explanatory Additions

Use wording that naturally communicates meaning.

Do not insert commentary.

---

## Priority

1. Understanding
2. Accuracy
3. Learning value
4. Readability
