### The Seventh Method ^3-8-0

#### Explanation of the Terms for What Is Dissociated by Way of What Is Associated ^3-8-1-0

Those states that are associated with the aggregate of feeling … with the aggregate of perception … with the aggregate of mental formations … with the aggregate of consciousness … with the mind base; and the states that are dissociated from those states: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states dissociated? Those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and they are partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-306

Those states that are associated with the eye-consciousness element … with the mind element … with the mind-consciousness element; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from one element. ^3-307

Those states that are associated with the mind faculty; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and they are partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-308

Those states that are associated with the equanimity faculty; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from five elements. ^3-309

Those states that are associated with consciousness conditioned by formations … with contact conditioned by the six sense bases … with feeling conditioned by contact … with contact … with feeling … with perception … with volition … with consciousness … with attention; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and they are partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-310

Those states that are associated with decision; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from one element. ^3-311

Those states that are associated with states associated with feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant … with states accompanied by equanimity; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from five elements. ^3-312

Those states that are associated with states with applied thought and sustained thought; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from one element. ^3-313

Those states that are associated with states that are consciousness … with states that are mental factors … with states associated with the mind … with states joined with the mind … with states joined with and arising from the mind … with states joined with, arising from and existing together with the mind … with states joined with, arising from and accompanying the mind; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and they are partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-314

Those states that are associated with states with applied thought … with states with sustained thought; and the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from one element. ^3-315

Those states that are associated with states accompanied by equanimity; and the states that are dissociated from those states: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states dissociated? Those states are not dissociated, even partly, from any aggregate or any sense base, but they are dissociated from five elements.
