#### Explanation of the Terms for What Is Excluded by Way of What Is Included ^3-3-1-0

Those states that, together with the eye base … with the touch base … with the eye element … with the touch element, are included in terms of the aggregates but are excluded in terms of the sense bases and excluded in terms of the elements: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states excluded? Those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-171

Those states that, together with the eye-consciousness element … with the ear-consciousness element … with the nose-consciousness element … with the tongue-consciousness element … with the body-consciousness element … with the mind element … with the mind-consciousness element, are included in terms of the aggregates but are excluded in terms of the sense bases and excluded in terms of the elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, eleven sense bases and twelve elements. ^3-172

Those states that, together with the eye faculty … with the ear faculty … with the nose faculty … with the tongue faculty … with the body faculty … with the femininity faculty … with the masculinity faculty, are included in terms of the aggregates but are excluded in terms of the sense bases and excluded in terms of the elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-173

Those states that, together with lamentation … with states that are visible and have impact, are included in terms of the aggregates but are excluded in terms of the sense bases and excluded in terms of the elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-175

Those states that, together with states that are invisible but have impact, are included in terms of the aggregates but are excluded in terms of the sense bases and excluded in terms of the elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, ten sense bases and sixteen elements. ^3-176
