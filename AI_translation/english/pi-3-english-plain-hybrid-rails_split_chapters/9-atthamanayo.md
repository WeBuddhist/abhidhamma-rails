### The Eighth Method ^3-9-0

#### Explanation of the Terms for What Is Associated by Way of What Is Dissociated ^3-9-1-0

Those states that are dissociated from the aggregate of material form: with how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are they associated? With none. ^3-317

Those states that are dissociated from the aggregate of feeling … from the aggregate of perception … from the aggregate of mental formations … from the aggregate of consciousness … from states with conflict … from states without conflict: with how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are they associated? With none.
The mental-objects base, the mental-objects element, then life and mind-and-matter;
the six sense bases, birth, aging and death; and two in the groups of three are not found.
Seven in the first intermediate section, and ten in the clusters and the later part;
fourteen, and six at the end: so these forty-seven states
are not found in this analysis, since the question is empty.
