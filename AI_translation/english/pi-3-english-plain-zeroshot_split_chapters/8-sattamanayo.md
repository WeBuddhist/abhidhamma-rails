### The Seventh Method ^3-8-0

#### Explanation of What Is Dissociated, by Way of What Is Associated ^3-8-1-0

Take the states that are associated with the aggregate of feeling… with the aggregate of perception… with the aggregate of mental formations… with the aggregate of consciousness… with the mind base. The states that are dissociated from those states — from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are they dissociated? Those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and partly from one sense base and one element. ^3-306

Take the states that are associated with the eye-consciousness element… with the mind element… with the mind-consciousness element; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-307

Take the states that are associated with the mind faculty; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and partly from one sense base and one element. ^3-308

Take the states that are associated with the faculty of equanimity; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from five elements. ^3-309

Take the states that are associated with consciousness conditioned by formations… with contact conditioned by the six sense bases… with feeling conditioned by contact… with contact… with feeling… with perception… with intention… with mind… with attention; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and partly from one sense base and one element. ^3-310

Take the states that are associated with decision; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-311

Take the states that are associated with states associated with feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant… with states accompanied by equanimity; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from five elements. ^3-312

Take the states that are associated with states with applied thought and sustained thought; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-313

Take the states that are associated with states that are mind… with mental factors… with states associated with mind… with states joined with mind… with states joined with and arising from mind… with states joined with, arising from and existing together with mind… with states joined with, arising from and accompanying mind; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements; and partly from one sense base and one element. ^3-314

Take the states that are associated with states with applied thought… with states with sustained thought; the states dissociated from those states… are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-315

Take the states that are associated with states accompanied by equanimity. The states that are dissociated from those states — from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are they dissociated? Those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from five elements.
Four aggregates and one sense base;
seven among the elements, and two from the faculties;
three from dependent origination, and likewise the five headed by contact;
decision and attention; three among the sets of three;
seven in the intermediate sections, and two joined with mind;
applied thought, sustained thought, and what goes with equanimity.
The seventh: the Explanation of What Is Dissociated, by Way of What Is Associated, is complete. ^3-316
