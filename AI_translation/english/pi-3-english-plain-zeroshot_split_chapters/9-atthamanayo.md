### The Eighth Method ^3-9-0

#### Explanation of What Is Associated, by Way of What Is Dissociated ^3-9-1-0

The states that are dissociated from the aggregate of material form — with how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states associated? With none. ^3-317

The states that are dissociated from the aggregate of feeling… from the aggregate of perception… from the aggregate of mental formations… from the aggregate of consciousness… from states with conflict… from states without conflict — with how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states associated? With none.
The base of mental objects, the element of mental objects, then life, mind-and-matter;
the six sense bases, birth, aging and death; and two among the sets of three are not found.
Seven in the first intermediate section, ten in the clusters, and in the later intermediate section
fourteen, and six at the end: these forty-seven states
are not found in this analysis, because the question for them comes up empty.
The eighth: the Explanation of What Is Associated, by Way of What Is Dissociated, is complete. ^3-318
