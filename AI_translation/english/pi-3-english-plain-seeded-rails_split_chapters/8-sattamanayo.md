### Seventh Method ^3-8-0

#### Explanation of the Term "What Is Dissociated from What Is Associated" ^3-8-1-0

Take the states that are associated with the aggregate of feeling… with the aggregate of perception… with the aggregate of formations… with the aggregate of consciousness… with the mind base; then the states that are dissociated from those states: from how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states dissociated? Those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one base and one element. ^3-306

Take the states that are associated with the eye-consciousness element… with the mind-element… with the mind-consciousness element; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from one element. ^3-307

Take the states that are associated with the mind faculty; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one base and one element. ^3-308

Take the states that are associated with the equanimity faculty; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from five elements. ^3-309

Take the states that are associated with consciousness with formations as condition… with contact with the six sense bases as condition… with feeling with contact as condition… with contact… with feeling… with perception… with volition… with consciousness… with attention; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one base and one element. ^3-310

Take the states that are associated with decision; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from one element. ^3-311

Take the states that are associated with states associated with a feeling of neither suffering nor happiness… with states accompanied by equanimity; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from five elements. ^3-312

Take the states that are associated with states with thinking and with holding the thought; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from one element. ^3-313

Take the states that are associated with states that are consciousness… with states that are mental factors… with states associated with mind… with states joined with mind… with states joined with and arising from mind… with states joined with, arising from and existing together with mind… with states joined with, arising from and accompanying mind; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one base and one element. ^3-314

Take the states that are associated with states with applied thinking… with states with sustained thought; then the states that are dissociated from those states… those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from one element. ^3-315

Take the states that are associated with states accompanied by equanimity; then the states that are dissociated from those states: from how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states dissociated? Those states are not dissociated from any aggregate or any base, but are dissociated from five elements.
Four aggregates, and one base;
seven among the elements, and two from the faculties;
three from dependent origination, and likewise the five with contact;
decision, attention; three among the groups of three;
seven from the intermediate sections, and two joined with mind;
thinking, holding the thought, and what goes with equanimity.
