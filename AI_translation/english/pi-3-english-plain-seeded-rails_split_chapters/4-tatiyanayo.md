### Third Method ^3-4-0

#### Explanation of the Term "What Is Included by What Is Excluded" ^3-4-1-0

The states that are excluded from the aggregate of feeling… from the aggregate of perception… from the aggregate of formations… from the truth of the origin… from the truth of the path in terms of aggregates, but included in terms of bases and included in terms of elements: in how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states included? Leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, those states are included in three aggregates, one base and one element. ^3-179

The states that are excluded from the truth of the ending in terms of aggregates, but included in terms of bases and included in terms of elements… those states are included in four aggregates, one base and one element. ^3-180

The states that are excluded from the life faculty in terms of aggregates, but included in terms of bases and included in terms of elements… leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, those states are included in two aggregates, one base and one element. ^3-181
