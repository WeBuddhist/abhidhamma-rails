### The Third Method ^3-4-0

#### Explanation of the Term "Included by Way of the Excluded" ^3-4-1-0

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from the aggregate of feeling … from the aggregate of perception … from the aggregate of mental formations … from the truth of the origin … from the truth of the path, but are included together with it in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements: in how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states included? Those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in three aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-179

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from the truth of cessation, but are included together with it in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states are included in four aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-180

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from the life faculty, but are included together with it in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in two aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-181

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from the femininity faculty … from the masculinity faculty … from the pleasure faculty … from the pain faculty … from the joy faculty … from the sadness faculty … from the equanimity faculty … from the faith faculty … from the energy faculty … from the mindfulness faculty … from the concentration faculty … from the wisdom faculty … from the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known" … from the faculty of final knowledge … from the faculty of one who has final knowledge … from ignorance … from formations conditioned by ignorance … from contact conditioned by the six sense bases … from feeling conditioned by contact … from craving conditioned by feeling … from clinging conditioned by craving … from existence as action [from existence as action conditioned by clinging (Siamese edition)], but are included together with it in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in three aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-182

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from birth … from aging … from death … from absorption, but are included together with it in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in two aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-183

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from sorrow … from pain … from sadness … from despair … from a foundation of mindfulness … from a right effort … from an immeasurable state … from the five faculties … from the five powers … from the seven factors of awakening … from the noble eightfold path … from contact … from feeling … from perception … from intention … from decision … from attention … from states that are roots … from states that are both roots and have roots (sahetukehi) … from states that are both roots and associated with roots, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in three aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-184

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from states without conditions … from unconditioned states, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states are included in four aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-185

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from states that are taints … from states that are both taints and subject to taints … from states that are both taints and associated with taints, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in three aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-186

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from states that are fetters … ties … floods … yokes … hindrances … misapprehensions … from states that are both misapprehensions and misapprehended, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in three aggregates, one sense base and one element. ^3-187

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from states that are mental factors … from states associated with mind … from states joined with mind … from states joined with mind and arising from it … from states joined with mind, arising from it and existing together with it … from states joined with mind, arising from it and accompanying it, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in one aggregate, one sense base and one element. ^3-188

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from states existing together with mind … from states accompanying mind, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements … those states are included in no aggregate, but in one sense base and one element. ^3-189

The states that, in terms of aggregates, are excluded from states that are clinging … from states that are defilements … from states that are both defilements and lead to defilement … from states that are both defilements and defiled … from states that are both defilements and associated with defilements, but are included together with them in terms of sense bases and included in terms of elements: in how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states included? Those states, leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, are included in three aggregates, one sense base and one element.
