### The Seventh Method ^3-8-0

#### Explanation of the Term "Dissociated by Way of the Associated" ^3-8-1-0

Take the states that are associated with the aggregate of feeling … with the aggregate of perception … with the aggregate of mental formations … with the aggregate of consciousness … with the mind base; then the states that are dissociated from those states: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states dissociated? Those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-306

Take the states that are associated with the eye-consciousness element … with the mind element … with the mind-consciousness element; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-307

Take the states that are associated with the mind faculty; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-308

Take the states that are associated with the equanimity faculty; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from five elements. ^3-309

Take the states that are associated with consciousness conditioned by formations … with contact conditioned by the six sense bases … with feeling conditioned by contact … with contact … with feeling … with perception … with intention … with mind … with attention; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-310

Take the states that are associated with decision; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-311

Take the states that are associated with states associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling … with states accompanied by equanimity; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from five elements. ^3-312

Take the states that are associated with states with applied thought and with sustained thought; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-313

Take the states that are associated with states that are mind … with states that are mental factors … with states associated with mind … with states joined with mind … with states joined with mind and arising from it … with states joined with mind, arising from it and existing together with it … with states joined with mind, arising from it and accompanying it; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from four aggregates, one sense base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one sense base and one element. ^3-314

Take the states that are associated with states with applied thought … with states with sustained thought; then the states that are dissociated from those states … those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from one element. ^3-315

Take the states that are associated with states accompanied by equanimity; then the states that are dissociated from those states: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states dissociated? Those states are dissociated from no aggregate and no sense base, but from five elements.
Four aggregates, and one sense base;
seven among the elements, and two from among the faculties;
three from dependent origination, and likewise the five beginning with contact;
decision, attention; three in the groups of three;
seven from the intermediate sections, and two joined with mind;
applied thought, sustained thought, and what is accompanied by equanimity.
Here ends the seventh: the explanation of the term "dissociated by way of the associated". ^3-316
