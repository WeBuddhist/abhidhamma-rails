### The Second Method ^3-3-0

#### Explanation of the Term "Excluded by Way of the Included" ^3-3-1-0

The states that are included together with the eye base … with the touch base … with the eye element … with the touch element in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states excluded? Those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-171

The states that are included together with the eye-consciousness element … with the ear-consciousness element … with the nose-consciousness element … with the tongue-consciousness element … with the body-consciousness element … with the mind element … with the mind-consciousness element in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, eleven sense bases and twelve elements. ^3-172

The states that are included together with the eye faculty … with the ear faculty … with the nose faculty … with the tongue faculty … with the body faculty … with the femininity faculty … with the masculinity faculty in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-173

The states that are included together with existence without perception … with one-constituent existence in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, three sense bases and nine elements. ^3-174

The states that are included together with lamentation … with states that are visible and have impact in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-175

The states that are included together with states that are invisible but have impact in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, ten sense bases and sixteen elements. ^3-176

The states that are included together with visible states in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements … those states are excluded from four aggregates, two sense bases and eight elements. ^3-177

The states that are included together with states with impact … with derived states in terms of aggregates, but are excluded in terms of sense bases and excluded in terms of elements: from how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states excluded? Those states are excluded from four aggregates, eleven sense bases and seventeen elements.
Ten sense bases, seventeen elements,
seven faculties, existence without perception, one-constituent existence;
lamentation, what is visible and has impact,
what is invisible, and again [punareva (pī.)] what has impact, and the derived.
Here ends the second: the explanation of the term "excluded by way of the included". ^3-178
