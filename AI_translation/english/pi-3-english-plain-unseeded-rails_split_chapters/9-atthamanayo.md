### The Eighth Method ^3-9-0

#### Explanation of the Term "Associated by Way of the Dissociated" ^3-9-1-0

The states that are dissociated from the aggregate of material form: with how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states associated? None. ^3-317

The states that are dissociated from the aggregate of feeling … from the aggregate of perception … from the aggregate of mental formations … from the aggregate of consciousness … from states with conflict … from states without conflict: with how many aggregates, how many sense bases and how many elements are those states associated? None.
The mental-objects base, the mental-objects element, then life, mind-and-matter;
the six sense bases, birth, aging and death; and two in the groups of three are not found.
Seven in the first intermediate section, ten in the clusters, in the later section
fourteen, and six at the end: these forty-seven states
are not found in this analysis, since the question comes up empty.
Here ends the eighth: the explanation of the term "associated by way of the dissociated". ^3-318
