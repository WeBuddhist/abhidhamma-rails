## kusala

### Contextual definition

*Kusala* sabhāvato anavajja-sukhavipāka-lakkhaṇaṃ — yaṃ sāvajjarahitaṃ sukhavipākanibbattakañca tādisaṃ cetasikadhammasamūhassa nāmaṃ. Kusala-saddo idha tīsu atthesu pavattati: ārogya, anavajja, sukhavipāka. Vacanattho cha-pakāro: (1) *kusā* vuccanti kucchitā pāpā dhammā, te salayanti calayanti kampenti viddhaṃsentīti kusalā; (2) attanā *kusā* hutvā akusalasaṅkhāte kuse lunantīti kusalā; (3) kucchitānaṃ sānato tanukaraṇato osānakaraṇato ñāṇaṃ *kusaṃ* nāma, tena kusena lātabbāti kusalā. Ñāṇavippayuttānampi kusalānaṃ yonisomanasikāramūlattā ñāṇena pavattanaṃ siddhaṃ.

### Attestations

#### dhammasangani-atthakatha

> Idāni kusalā dhammātiādīnaṃ mātikāpadānaṃ ayamanupubbapadavaṇṇanā – ‘kusala’-saddo tāva ārogyaanavajjachekasukhavipākesu dissati. Ayañhi "kacci nu bhoto kusalaṃ, kacci bhoto anāmaya"ntiādīsu ārogye dissati. … Svāyamidha ārogyepi anavajjepi sukhavipākepi vattati.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-511)

> Vacanattho panettha – kucchite pāpake dhamme salayanti calayanti kampenti viddhaṃsentīti kusalā. Kucchitena vā ākārena sayantīti kusā. Te akusalasaṅkhāte kuse lunanti chindantīti kusalā. … Tena kusena lātabbāti kusalā; gahetabbā pavattetabbāti attho. … Yathā vā kusā ubhayabhāgagataṃ hatthappadesaṃ lunanti, evamimepi uppannānuppannabhāvena ubhayabhāgagataṃ kilesapakkhaṃ lunanti.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-515)

#### dhammasangani-mulatiika

> Ñāṇavippayuttānampi hi ñāṇaṃ upanissayapaccayo hotīti sabbepi kusalā dhammā kusena ñāṇena pavattetabbāti kusalā.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-80)

#### dhammasangani-anutiika

> Ñāṇavippayuttāpi hi ñāṇeneva pavattiyanti hitasukhahetubhūtāya pavattiyā paññavantānaṃ paṭipattibhāvato. Na hi antarena yonisomanasikāraṃ kusaluppatti atthīti.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-72)
