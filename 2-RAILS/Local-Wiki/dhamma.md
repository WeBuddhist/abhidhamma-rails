## dhamma

### Contextual definition

*Dhamma*-saddo bahūsu atthesu dissati: *pariyatti* (scripture), *hetu* (cause), *guṇa* (virtue), *nissattanijjīvatā* (no-being-no-soul, i.e. mere phenomenal nature). Kusalattikādīsu pana *nissattanijjīvatāyameva* vaṭṭati — anūpādiyamāna-satta-jīva-paramaññatta-sabhāvamatta. Vacanattho: *attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentīti dhammā*; *dhāriyanti vā paccayehi*, *dhārīyanti vā yathāsabhāvato*. Mūlaṭīkāyaṃ vibhāvīyate: nāmavasena viññātāviññāte sabhāvadhamme aññe viya katvā "attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentī"ti vutto — kattukamma-paramattha-bheda-rahito, kappanā-siddha-bheda-mattaṃ. Sappaccayadhammesu *dhārīyanti paccayehī*ti visesāyaṃ vutto.

### Attestations

#### dhammasangani-atthakatha

> Dhammasaddo panāyaṃ pariyattihetuguṇanissattanijjīvatādīsu dissati. Ayañhi "dhammaṃ pariyāpuṇāti suttaṃ geyya"ntiādīsu pariyattiyaṃ dissati. "Hetumhi ñāṇaṃ dhammapaṭisambhidā"tiādīsu hetumhi.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-512)

> "Tasmiṃ kho pana samaye dhammā honti", "dhammesu dhammānupassī viharatī"tiādīsu nissattanijjīvatāyaṃ. Svāyamidhāpi nissattanijjīvatāyameva vaṭṭati.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-514)

> Attano pana sabhāvaṃ dhārentīti dhammā. Dhāriyanti vā paccayehi, dhārīyanti vā yathāsabhāvatoti dhammā.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-515)

#### dhammasangani-mulatiika

> Sattādigāhakānaṃ cittānaṃ gocarā sattādayo viya paññāya upaparikkhiyamānā na nissabhāvā, kintu attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentīti dhammā. Na ca dhāriyamānasabhāvā añño dhammo nāma atthi. … Aññathā pana avabodhetuṃ na sakkāti nāmavasena viññātāviññāte sabhāvadhamme aññe viya katvā "attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentī"ti vuttaṃ. Sappaccayadhammesu visesaṃ dassento "dhārīyanti vā paccayehī"ti āha.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-81)

#### dhammasangani-anutiika

> Na cātiādinā "sabhāvaṃ dhārentī"ti ettha paramatthato kattukammassa ca bhedo natthi, kappanāsiddho eva pana bhedoti dasseti. … Ettha ca paṭhamo attho saṅkhatāsaṅkhatadhammavasena vutto, dutiyo saṅkhatavasena, tatiyo saṅkhatāsaṅkhatapaññattidhammavasenāti daṭṭhabbaṃ.
> (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-73)
