## Mātikā

[[#^toc-1|↑↑↑]]

Ayaṃ mātikā abhidhammakathāya ādinā ṭhitā, tikamātikā dukamātikāti duvidhā. Tattha tikā dvāvīsati, dukā sataṃ abhidhammamātikāya, dvācattālīsa suttantikadukā ca – evaṃ sabbepete pañcadasahi paricchedehi vavatthitā, ekena tikānaṃ catuddasahi ca dukānaṃ. Sappadesā ca nippadesā cāti dve koṭṭhāsā honti. Sattannaṃ pakaraṇānaṃ mātikā nāma esā yā jinavacanabhūtā sabbaññubuddhena desitā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-509]]

### Tikamātikā

[[#^toc-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Tikamātikā ādipadena laddhanāmo kusalattiko, sabbapadehi laddhanāmo vedanāttikādayo, dvāvīsati tikā – ekaṃ paricchedaṃ katvā ṭhitā. Tattha 'kusala'-saddo ārogye anavajje sukhavipāke ca vattati; idha pana anavajjasukhavipākalakkhaṇo. 'Dhamma'-saddo sabhāvadhāraṇādiatthajotakattena vuccati; nissattanijjīvatāyaṃ dissati. Kusalā dhammā anavajjasukhavipākalakkhaṇā; akusalā sāvajjadukkhavipākalakkhaṇā; abyākatā avipākalakkhaṇā. Evaṃ imināva nayena sesatikānampi nayo veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-507]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-511]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-515]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-542]]

### Dukamātikā

[[#^toc-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Dukamātikāyaṃ hetugocchakādayo satta gocchakā, cūḷantaradukādayo, mahantaradukā, upādānagocchako, kilesagocchako, piṭṭhidukāti catuddasahi paricchedehi vavatthitā sataṃ dukā abhidhammamātikāya. Tato aparā dvācattālīsa suttantikadukā nāma. Gocchakāti cha cha dukā aññamaññasambandhena kaṇṇikā viya ghaṭā viya hutvā ṭhitā; cūḷantaradukā pana aññamaññaṃ asambandhā dukasāmaññato uccinitvā visuṃ visuṃ gocchakantare ṭhapitā. Evaṃ dukā symbols sappadesā nippadesāti dvedhā honti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-509]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-543]]

#### Hetugocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Hetugocchake 'hetu'-saddo mūlaṭṭhena dissati – rāgadosādihetusaṅkhātānaṃ dhammānaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Cha dukā – hetū, sahetukā, hetusampayuttāti padattikena, tato aparā tayo dukā paṭhamadukaṃ dutiyatatiyehi saddhiṃ yojetvā vuttā – te aññamaññasambandhena kaṇṇikāva ghaṭāva hutvā ṭhitā 'hetugocchako'ti vuccanti. 'Na hetū kho pana dhammā sahetukāpi ahetukāpī'ti osānaduke 'kho panā'ti padena atirekattho saṅgahito veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-543]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-544]]

#### Cūḷantaradukaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Cūḷantaradukesu satta dukā aññamaññaṃ asambandhā – gocchakantare ṭhapitattā aññehi ca mahantaradukehi cūḷakattā 'cūḷantaradukā'ti veditabbā. Sappaccayā paccayehi nipphāditā dhammā; appaccayā asaṅkhatā ca uppādaṭhitibhaṅgesu paccayarahitā. Rūpino ruppanalakkhaṇaṃ yesaṃ atthīti; arūpino tadrahitā. Lokiyā vaṭṭapariyāpannā; lokuttarā vaṭṭato uttīrṇā. Kenaci viññeyyā cakkhuviññāṇādīsu kenaci ekena vijānitabbā – evaṃ dvinnampi padānaṃ atthanānattato duko hoti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-545]]

#### Āsavagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Āsavagocchake 'āsava'-saddo cirapārivāsiyaṭṭhena madirādayo viya vuccati, cakkhutopi manato yāva bhavaggaṃ savantīti vā, āyataṃ saṃsāradukkhaṃ savanti pasavantītipi vā. Sāsavā āsavānaṃ ārammaṇabhūtā dhammā; anāsavā āsavarahitā. Sesaṃ hetugocchake vuttanayeneva yojetabbaṃ – kiñcāpi hetugocchakasadiso osānaduko idha na vutto, atha kho ayañca añño ca bhedo tattha vuttanayeneva veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-546]]

#### Saṃyojanagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṃyojanagocchake saṃyojanā yaṃ puggalaṃ vaṭṭasmiṃ saṃyojenti bandhantīti; dasa saṃyojanānaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Saṃyojaniyā ārammaṇabhāvaṃ upagantvā saṃyojanasambandhane saṃyojanānaṃ hitā dhammā – saṃyojanassa ārammaṇapaccayabhūtānametaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Na saṃyojaniyā asaṃyojaniyā. Sesapadāni hetugocchake vuttanayenevahetugocchake vuttanayeneva yojetabbāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-547]]

#### Ganthagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Ganthagocchake ganthā yaṃ cutipaṭisandhivasena vaṭṭasmiṃ ganthenti ghaṭentīti; kāyaganthaādīnaṃ catunnaṃ ganthānaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Ganthaniyā ārammaṇakaraṇavasena ganthehi ganthitabbā dhammā. Sesapadāni hetugocchake vuttanayeneva yojetabbāni – yathā idha evaṃ ito paresupi vuttāvasesaṃ tattha tattha vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-548]]

#### Oghagocchakaṃ 

[[#^toc-1-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Oghagocchake oghā yaṃ vaṭṭasmiṃyeva ohananti osīdāpentīti; cattāro oghā – kāmogho bhavogho diṭṭhogho avijjāoghoti, yesaṃ yassa saṃvijjanti so vaṭṭe majjatīti. Oghaniyā ārammaṇaṃ katvā atikkamanīyato oghehi atikkamitabbā dhammā – oghānaṃ ārammaṇadhammā eva veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-549]]

#### Yogagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Yogagocchake yogā vaṭṭasmiṃ yojentīti; cattāro yogā kāmayoga-bhavayoga-diṭṭhiyoga-avijjāyogasaṅkhātā. Yoganiyā oghaniyā viya ārammaṇakaraṇavasena yogehi yujjitabbā dhammā veditabbā. Sesapadāni oghagocchake vuttanayeneva yojetabbāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-550]]

#### Nīvaraṇagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Nīvaraṇagocchake nīvaraṇā cittaṃ nīvaranti pariyonandhantīti; pañca nīvaraṇāni – kāmacchandanīvaraṇādīni, yesaṃ yassa saṃvijjanti tassa cittaṃ āvaraṇena pariyonaddhaṃ hoti. Nīvaraṇiyā saṃyojaniyā viya ārammaṇabhāvaṃ upagantvā nīvaraṇānaṃ hitā dhammā – nīvaraṇassa ārammaṇapaccayabhūtānametaṃ adhivacanaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-551]]

#### Parāmāsagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Parāmāsagocchake parāmāsā dhammānaṃ yathābhūtaṃ aniccādiākāraṃ atikkamitvā 'nicca'ntiādivikāravasena parato āmasantīti; diṭṭhisaṅkhātassa ekassa parāmāsassa adhivacanaṃ. Parāmaṭṭhā parāmāsehi ārammaṇakaraṇavasena āmasitā dhammā. Iti pañca dukā aññamaññasambandhena 'parāmāsagocchako'ti vuccanti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-552]]

#### Mahantaradukaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Mahantaradukesu catuddasapadāni: sārammaṇā ārammaṇaṃ aggahetvā appavattito saha ārammaṇena pavattā; anārammaṇā ārammaṇarahitā. Cittā cintanaṭṭhena vicittaṭṭhena vā; cetasikā avippayogavasena cetasi niyuttā. Cittasaṃsaṭṭhā nirantarabhāvūpagamanena cittena saṃsaṭṭhā; cittasamuṭṭhānā cittaṃ samuṭṭhānaṃ etesanti. Cittasahabhuno cittena sahabhavantā; cittānuparivattino cittaṃ anuparivattantā. Ajjhattikā attano santāne pavattā; upādā bhūtehi upādiyanteva gahitā. Evaṃ mahantaradukesu catuddasahi padehi dhammaviseso vavatthito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-553]]

#### Upādānagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-11|↑↑↑]]

Upādānagocchake upādānā bhusaṃ ādiyanti daḷhaggāhaṃ gaṇhantīti; cattāro upādānā – kāmupādāna-diṭṭupādāna-sīlabbatupādāna-attavādupādānasaṅkhātā. Upādāniyā ārammaṇakaraṇavasena upādānehi ādātabbā dhammā. Sesapadāni hetugocchake vuttanayeneva yojetabbāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-554]]

#### Kilesagocchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Kilesagocchake attho saṃkiliṭṭhattike vuttanayeneva veditabbo – kilesā saṃkilesehi samannāgatā santānaṃ kiliñjanti parikiliñjantīti vā. Aṭṭha dukā – kilesā, saṃkilesikā, saṃkiliṭṭhā, kilesasampayuttāti padacatukkena vasena, tato aparā cattāro dukā aññamaññasambandhena ṭhitā – evaṃ sabbe 'kilesagocchako'ti vuccanti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-555]]

#### Piṭṭhidukaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Piṭṭhidukesu aṭṭhārasa dukā abhidhammamātikāya pariyosāne ṭhapetattā 'piṭṭhidukā'ti nāmaṃ labhanti. Kāmāvacarā kāme avacarantā dhammā; rūpāvacarā rūpajhānesu avacarantā; arūpāvacarā arūpajhānesu avacarantā. Pariyāpannā tebhūmakavaṭṭe antogadhā; niyyānikā vaṭṭamūlaṃ chindantā nibbānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā vaṭṭato niyyantā. Niyatā cutiyā anantaraṃ phaladāne niyatā; aniyatā tathā niyamābhāvato aniyatā. Saraṇā rāgādīhi sampayogavasena pahānaṃ labhantā dhammā; araṇā tenākārena raṇarahitā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-556]]

#### Suttantikadukamātikā

[[#^toc-1-2-14|↑↑↑]]

Suttantikadukesu dvācattālīsa dukā suttantatantiyā gahitā. Vijjābhāgino sampayogavasena vijjaṃ bhajantā – tattha aṭṭha vijjā vipassanāñāṇaṃ manomayiddhi cha abhiññāti; avijjābhāgino avijjaṃ bhajantā – catasso avijjā dukkhapaṭicchādakādikā. Vijjūpamā anajjhottharaṇabhāvena kilesandhakāraṃ viddhaṃsetuṃ asamatthā; vajirūpamā nissesaṃ viddhaṃsanasamatthatāya vajiraṃ upamā etesanti. Adhivacanādayo tayo dukā atthato ninnānākaraṇā, byañjanamevettha nānaṃ. Nāmaṃ nāmakaraṇaṭṭhena namanaṭṭhena ca; rūpaṃ ruppanaṭṭhena. Sīlādīnaṃ vipatti-sampadā-visuddhidukā saṅvegādidukā ca vividhehi padehi ṭhitā; vijjāvimuttidukena khaye-anuppādañāṇadukena ca niṭṭhiṃ gacchantī suttantikadukamātikā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-557]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-563]]

## Cittuppādakaṇḍaṃ

[[#^toc-2|↑↑↑]]

Dhammasaṅgaṇīpakaraṇe paṭhamakaṇḍaṃ 'cittuppādakaṇḍ'nti vuccati, 'cittavibhatti'tipi. Tattha kāmāvacarakusalato aṭṭha, akusalato dvādasa, kusalavipākato soḷasa, akusalavipākato satta, kiriyato ekādasa; rūpāvacarakusalato pañca, vipākato pañca, kiriyato pañca; arūpāvacarakusalato cattāri, vipākato cattāri, kiriyato cattāri; lokuttarakusalato cattāri, vipākato cattārīti ekūnanavuti cittāni saṅkhaṃ gacchanti. Etaṃ kaṇḍaṃ mātikāpadabhājanīyavasena duvidhaṃ – tikamātikādisaṅgahena paṭhamā mātikā, padabhājanīyaṃ pana kāmāvacarakusalato ārabhitvā sabbacittuppādānaṃ vitthāradesanā. Vācanāmaggato atirekachabhāṇavāraṃ; vitthāriyamānaṃ pana anantamaparimāṇaṃ hoti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-31]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-507]]

### Kāmāvacarakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Mātikāya saṅgahite dhamme pabhedato dassetuṃ 'katame dhammā kusalā'ti padabhājanīyaṃ āraddhaṃ; tattha paṭhamaṃ kāmāvacarakusalaṃ dassitaṃ. Tassa niddese dhammavavatthānavāro saṅgahavāro suññatavāroti tayo mahāvārā honti; dhammavavatthānavāre uddesaniddesavasena dvidhā. Uddesavāre pucchā samayaniddeso dhammuddeso appanāti cattāro paricchedā – 'katame dhammā kusalā'ti pucchā kathetukamyatāpucchāsaṅkhātā, pabhedato dhammānaṃ desanaṃ dīpetuṃ vuttā. 'Yasmiṃ samaye kāmāvacaraṃ kusalaṃ cittaṃ uppanna'ntiādinā samayaniyamitena cittena phassavedanādayo dhamme santatisamūhakiccārammaṇaghanavasena bodheti. Saṅkhepato ñāṇavibhattidesanāvasena catubbidhaṃ, asaṅkhārasasaṅkhāravibhattito aṭṭheva kāmāvacarakusalacittāni honti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-565]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-577]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1008]]

#### Padabhājanī

[[#^toc-2-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Kāmāvacarakusalassa padabhājanīye tayo mahāvārā – dhammavavatthānavāro saṅgahavāro suññatavāro. Dhammavavatthānavāre uddesaniddesavasena dve bhāgā; uddesavāre cattāro paricchedā – pucchā samayaniddeso dhammuddeso appanāti. 'Katame dhammā kusalā'ti pañcavidhesu pucchāsu kathetukamyatāpucchāsaṅkhātā – pabhedavantesu dhammesu pabhedo dassiyamāno suviññeyyo hotīti ghanavinibbhogapaṭisambhidāñāṇāvahattā 'dhammā kusalā'ti padānukkamo kato. Niddesavāre pana phassavedanādinaṃ nānāpadesavisesā, yevāpanakachandādayo, koṭṭhāsavāro suññatavāro ca paricchinnā vitthāritā; aggahitaggahaṇena phassapañcakaṃ vitakkavicārādayo ca sesadhammasamūhena saha padapaṭipāṭiyā niddiṭṭhā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-565]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-573]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-574]]

#### Koṭṭhāsavāro

[[#^toc-2-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tasmiṃ kho pana samaye cattāro khandhā hontīti saṅgahavāro āraddhaṃ. So uddesaniddesapaṭiniddesānaṃ vasena tividho hoti. Tattha uddesavāre cattāro khandhātiādayo tevīsati koṭṭhāsā honti. Tesaṃ evamattho veditabbo – yasmiṃ samaye kāmāvacaraṃ paṭhamaṃ mahākusalacittaṃ uppajjati, ye tasmiṃ samaye cittaṅgavasena uppannā, ṭhapetvā yevāpanake, pāḷiāruḷhā atirekapaṇṇāsadhammā, te sabbepi saṅgayhamānā rāsaṭṭhena cattārova khandhā honti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-961]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-962]]

#### Suññatavāro

[[#^toc-2-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tasmiṃ kho pana samaye dhammā hontīti suññatavāro āraddhaṃ. So uddesaniddesavasena dvidhā vavatthito. Tattha uddesavāre ‘dhammā hontī’ti iminā saddhiṃ catuvīsati koṭṭhāsā honti. Sabbakoṭṭhāsesu ca ‘cattāro dve tayo’ti gaṇanaparicchedo na vutto. Kasmā? Saṅgahavāre paricchinnattā. Tattha paricchinnadhammāyeva hi idhāpi vuttā. Na hettha satto vā bhāvo vā attā vā upalabbhati. Dhammāva ete dhammamattā asārā apariṇāyakāti imissā suññatāya dīpanatthaṃ vuttā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-972]]

### Rūpāvacarakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Idāni rūpāvacarakusalaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha rūpūpapattiyā maggaṃ bhāvento 'vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehī'ti kāyavivekacittavivekavikkhambhanavivekavasena nīvaraṇāni vikkhambhitvā, savitakkaṃ savicāraṃ vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamajjhānanti evaṃ catukkapañcakajjhānāni pathavikasiṇādīni ārammaṇāni kasiṇaparikammaṃ katvā nibbatteti. Ettha 'jhāna'ntipadena ārammaṇūpanijjhānaṃ adhippetaṃ. Paṭipadāya sādhetabbato puggalapubbaṅgamā desanā katā. 'Tasmiṃ samaye phasso hotī'tiādīsu chapaṇṇāsa dhammā bhūmantaravasena mahaggatā rūpāvacarāti ayamettha viseso.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1011]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1024]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1028]]

#### Catukkanayo

[[#^toc-2-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacarakusale catukkanayo nāma savitakkasavicārādibhedo catubbidho jhānapabhedo. Tattha paṭhamajjhānaṃ vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṃ savicāraṃ vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ upasampajja viharati pathavīkasiṇādīsu aññataraṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā. Evaṃ dutiyādīni jhānanukkamena catukkajjhānāni nibbattenti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1011]]

#### Pañcakanayo

[[#^toc-2-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañcakanayo pana puggalajjhāsayavasena ceva desanāvilāsena ca bhājito. Tattha vitakkavicārānaṃ visuṃ vūpasamanavasena paṭhamajjhānato paraṃ avitakkaṃ vicāramattaṃ dutiyajjhānaṃ, tato avitakkaavicāraṃ tatiyajjhānanti evaṃ pañca jhāniyāni nibbattenti. Ayamettha viseso – yaṃ catukkanaye dutiyaṃ taṃ idha tatiyanti evamānupubbiyaṃ veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1075]]

#### Catasso paṭipadā (Rūpāvacara)

[[#^toc-2-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacarajjhānaṃ nāma paṭipadākamena sijjhati. Sā pana dukkhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā, dukkhā paṭipadā khippābhiññā, sukhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā, sukhā paṭipadā khippābhiññāti catubbidhā. Tattha ādito kilese vikkhambhentassa kasirapaṭipattitāya dukkhā, appanāparivāse maggapātubhāvamandatāya dandhābhiññāti evaṃ catasso paṭipadā yathārahaṃ veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1092]]

#### Cattāri ārammaṇāni

[[#^toc-2-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Jhānaṃ nāma yathā paṭipadābhedena evaṃ ārammaṇabhedenāpi catubbidho hoti – parittaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, parittaṃ appamāṇārammaṇaṃ, appamāṇaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, appamāṇaṃ appamāṇārammaṇanti. Tattha avaḍḍhite ārammaṇe parittārammaṇatā, vipule vaḍḍhite appamāṇārammaṇatā. Jhānassa pana uparijjhānapaccayasamatthatāya hīnapaṇītabhāvena parittaappamāṇārammaṇatā veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1100]]

#### Soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Idāni ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissakaṃ soḷasakkhattukanayaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha paṭipadācatukkañca ārammaṇacatukkañca aññamaññaṃ yojetvā soḷasa jhānanayā nibbattanti. Paṭhama nayo catūhi kāraṇehi hīno, soḷasamo pana catūhi kāraṇehi paṇītoti evaṃ jhānuppattikāraṇattā puggalajjhāsayavasena ca ayaṃ desanā katā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1102]]

#### Aṭṭhakasiṇaṃ soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Idāni yasmā āpokasiṇādīsupi etāni jhānāni uppajjanti, tasmā tesaṃ dassanatthaṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tesu sabbo pāḷinayo ca atthavibhāvanā ca cittagaṇanā ca vārasaṅkhepo ca pathavīkasiṇe vuttanayeneva veditabbo. Bhāvanānayo pana kasiṇaparikammaṃ ādiṃ katvā sabbo visuddhimagge pakāsitova.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1108]]

#### Abhibhāyatanāni parittāni

[[#^toc-2-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Evaṃ aṭṭhasu kasiṇesu rūpāvacarakusalaṃ niddisitvā, idāni yasmā samānepi ārammaṇe bhāvanāya asamānaṃ imesu aṭṭhasu kasiṇesu aññampi abhibhāyatanasaṅkhātaṃ rūpāvacarakusalaṃ pavattati, tasmā taṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha ajjhattaṃ arūpasaññīti alābhitāya vā anatthikatāya vā ajjhattarūpe parikammasaññāvirahito. Bahiddhā rūpāni passatīti bahiddhā aṭṭhasu kasiṇesu kataparikammatāya parikammavasena ceva appanāvasena ca tāni bahiddhā aṭṭhasu kasiṇesu rūpāni passati.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1113]]

#### Catasso paṭipadā (Rūpāvacara 2)

[[#^toc-2-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abhibhāyatanesu suddhikanayapaṭipadābhedo pathavīkasiṇe vuttanayeneva ekekasmiṃ abhibhāyatane veditabbo. Kevalañcettha ārammaṇacatukkaṃ ārammaṇadukaṃ hoti, soḷasakkhattukañca aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ. Sesaṃ tādisameva. Evamettha ekekasmiṃ abhibhāyatane eko suddhikanavako, cattāro paṭipadānavakā, dve ārammaṇanavakā, ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissake aṭṭha navakāti pannarasa navakāti catūsupi abhibhāyatanesu samasaṭṭhi navakā veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Dve ārammaṇāni

[[#^toc-2-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Parittaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ appamāṇaṃ parittārammaṇanti idha parittānīti āgatattā appamāṇārammaṇatā na gahitā, parato appamāṇānīti āgatattā parittārammaṇatā. Aṭṭhakathāyaṃ pana vuttaṃ – ‘imasmiṃ ṭhāne cattāri cattāri ārammaṇāni aggahetvā dve dveva gahitāni . Kiṃ kāraṇā? Catūsu hi gahitesu desanā soḷasakkhattukā hoti, satthārā ca heṭṭhā soḷasakkhattukā desanā kilañjamhi tile pattharantena viya vitthārato kathitā. Tassa imasmiṃ ṭhāne aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ desanaṃ kātuṃ ajjhāsayo. Tasmā dve dveyeva gahitānīti veditabbānīti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1115]]

#### Aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Idāni ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissakaṃ aṭṭhakkhattukanayaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha abhibhāyatanesu ārammaṇacatukkaṃ ārammaṇadukaṃ hoti, soḷasakkhattukañca aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ. Sesaṃ tādisameva. Evamettha ekekasmiṃ abhibhāyatane ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissake aṭṭha navakā veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Idampi aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-11|↑↑↑]]

Idampi aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ heṭṭhā vuttasādiseneva parittābhibhāyatanavasena pavattaṃ, kevalañhi ārammaṇe suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇabhedo viseso. Suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇānīti parisuddhāparisuddhavaṇṇāni; catūsu imesu ārammaṇesu parittaṃ vitakkacaritavasena āgataṃ, appamāṇaṃ mohacaritavasena, suvaṇṇaṃ dosacaritavasena, dubbaṇṇaṃ rāgacaritavasena – etesañhi etāni sappāyāni honti. Ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissakanayena paṭipadācatukkañca ārammaṇadukaṃ missantā aṭṭhakkhattukanayo veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1116]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Appamāṇāni

[[#^toc-2-2-12|↑↑↑]]

Appamāṇānīti vaḍḍhitappamāṇāni; avaḍḍhitānaṃ parittānaṃ viseso. Tattha yathā mahagghaso puriso ekaṃ bhattavaḍḍhitakaṃ labhitvā taṃ na mahantato passati, evameva ñāṇuttaro puggalo visadaññāṇo 'kimettha samāpajjitabbaṃ, na idaṃ appamāṇaṃ, na mayhaṃ cittekaggatākaraṇe bhāro'ti tāni appamāṇāni ārammaṇāni abhibhavitvā samāpajjati – saha nimittuppādenevettha appanaṃ nibbattetīti attho. Heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva suddhikanayapaṭipadābhedo panettha abhibhāyatane veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1114]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Catasso paṭipadā (Rūpāvacara 3)

[[#^toc-2-2-13|↑↑↑]]

Appamāṇāni ārammaṇāni katvā paṭipadābhedena jhānadesanā dassetuṃ imasmiṃ naye catasso paṭipadā ārabhiyantiādi āraddhaṃ. Catasso paṭipadā dukkhā dandhābhiññā, dukkhā khippābhiññā, sukhā dandhābhiññā, sukhā khippābhiññāti; tā ādikilesavikkhambhanakasiratavasena dukkhā, vipākamaggappātubhāvamandatāya dandhābhiññāti evaṃ yathārahaṃ veditabbā. Pañcakanayena paṭhamamhā catutthajjhānakamena tato pañcamajjhānantogadhāni sabbāni jhānāni catūsu paṭipadāsu niyyojitāni honti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1092]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Dve ārammaṇāni (Rūpāvacara 2)

[[#^toc-2-2-14|↑↑↑]]

Appamāṇāni abhibhāyatanārammaṇāni katvā dve ārammaṇāni dassetuṃ imasmiṃ nayo āraddho. Tattha 'appamāṇāni'ti āgatattā parittārammaṇatā na gahitā, parato āgatā appamāṇārammaṇatāyeva dve dveva gahitā – parittaṃ appamāṇārammaṇaṃ appamāṇaṃ appamāṇārammaṇanti. Heṭṭhā vuttānaṃ parittārammaṇānaṃ dve ārammaṇāni nāmato samānāni atthato pana parittappamāṇabhinnattā visabhāgāni – imasmiṃ ṭhāne niyyamitāni ārammaṇāni aṭṭhakkhattukadesanāya kāraṇaṃ honti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1115]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Aparampi aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-15|↑↑↑]]

Aparampi aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ nāma appamāṇāni ārammaṇāni katvā ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissakanayena nibbattetuṃ desitaṃ. Paṭipadācatukkañca ārammaṇadukaṃ ca aññamaññaṃ yojetvā aṭṭha jhānanayā nibbattanti; imasmiṃ ṭhāne parittaṃ na gahitaṃ, appamāṇārammaṇadvayantveva gahitaṃ – paṭipadābhede hi parittaappamāṇārammaṇāni cattāri yojetvā soḷasakkhattukaṃ hoteyya, satthārā pana imasmiṃ ṭhāne aṭṭhakkhattukāyeva desanaṃ kātuṃ ajjhāsayoti heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1115]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1117]]

#### Idampi aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ (Rūpāvacara 2)

[[#^toc-2-2-16|↑↑↑]]

Idampi aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ appamāṇāni ārammaṇāni suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇānīti visesīkatāni katvā desitaṃ. Yathāpi hi purimā suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇavasena parittāni ārammaṇāni aṭṭhakkhattukan'ti vuttaṃ, evameva idampi appamāṇāni suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇāni ārammaṇāni aṭṭhakkhattukaṃ veditabbaṃ. Pañcamaābhibhāyatanādīsu pana nīlānīti sabbasaṅgāhikavasena, nīlavaṇṇāni nidassanāni nibhāsānīti suvisuddhatthadassanatthaṃ vuttaṃ; suvisuddhavaṇṇavasena hi imāni cattāri abhibhāyatanāni pavattanti.

#### Tīṇi vimokkhāni soḷasakkhattukāni

[[#^toc-2-2-17|↑↑↑]]

Tīṇi vimokkhāni soḷasakkhattukāni nāma rūpāvacarakusalaṃ vimokkhavaseneva pavattamānaṃ dassetuṃ desitaṃ. Kenaṭṭhena vimokkho? Adhimuccanaṭṭhena – paccanīkadhammehi suṭṭhu vimuccanaṭṭhena, ārammaṇe ca abhirativasena suṭṭhu vimuccanaṭṭhena; pituaṅke vissaṭṭhaaṅgapaccaṅgassa dārakassa sayanaṃ viya aniggahitabhāvena nirāsaṅkatāya ārammaṇe pavattīti attho. Tattha rūpī rūpāni passatīti ajjhattabahiddhāvatthukesu kasiṇesu jhānapaṭilābho; ajjhattaṃ arūpasaññīti bahiddhāva paṭiladdhajjhānatā; subhanti vimuttaṃ suvisuddhesu nīlādīsu vaṇṇakasiṇesu jhānāni. Iti kasiṇanti vā abhibhāyatananti vā vimokkhoti vā rūpāvacarajjhānameva – ārammaṇassa sakalaṭṭhena kasiṇaṃ, abhibhavanaṭṭhena abhibhāyatanaṃ, adhimuccanaṭṭhena paccanīkadhammehi ca vimuccanaṭṭhena vimokkho nāmāti. Ekekasmiṃ pana vimokkhe pañcavīsati navakāti katvā tīsu soḷasakkhattukāni honti.

#### Cattāri brahmavihārajhānāni soḷasakkhattukāni

[[#^toc-2-2-18|↑↑↑]]

Cattāri brahmavihārajhānāni soḷasakkhattukāni nāma mettāsahagatamādīni cattāri jhānāni dassetuṃ desitāni. Mettā hitākārappavattilakkhaṇā byāpādūpasamo sampatti; karuṇā dukkhāpanayanākārappavattilakkhaṇā vihiṃsūpasamo sampatti; muditā pamodanalakkhaṇā arativūpasamo sampatti; upekkhā sattesu majjhattākārappavattilakkhaṇā paṭighānunayavūpasamo sampatti – iti seṭṭhaṭṭhena niddosabhāvena brahmavihārāti vuccanti. Appamāṇā hi sattā etāsaṃ gocarabhūtā pamāṇaṃ aggahetvā sakalapharaṇavaseneva pavattāti appamaññātipi vuccanti. Pathavīkasiṇe pañcavīsati navakā santi, idha pana purimāsu tīsu mettākaruṇāmuditāsu tikacatukkajjhānikavasena somanassāvippayogābhāvato sattakā honti, upekkhāya catutthajjhānavasena upekkhāvedanāsampayogato ekakāva; parittārammaṇatā ca nabahusattārammaṇavasena, appamāṇārammaṇatā bahusattārammaṇavasena hotīti ayaṃ viseso.

#### Asubhajhānaṃ soḷasakkhattukaṃ

[[#^toc-2-2-19|↑↑↑]]

Asubhajhānaṃ soḷasakkhattukaṃ nāma rāgacaritasattānaṃ ekantahitaṃ nānārammaṇesu ekekajjhānavaseneva pavattamānaṃ rūpāvacarakusalaṃ dassetuṃ desitaṃ. Tāni dasa asubhāni – uddhumātakaṃ vinīlakaṃ vipubbakaṃ vicchiddakaṃ vikkhāyitakaṃ vikkhittakaṃ hatavikkhittakaṃ lohitakaṃ puḷavakaṃ aṭṭhikanti; imāni sarīrasabhāvappattivasena dasadhā honti, rāgacaritabhedavasena ca dasadhā – uddhumātakaṃ sarīrasaṇṭhānarāgino sappāyaṃ, vinīlakaṃ sarīravaṇṇarāgino, aṭṭhikaṃ dantasampattirāgino sappāyanti evaṃ. Catutthajjhānavasena upekkhābrahmavihāre viya, asubhesu paṭhamajjhānavasena ekekasmiṃ pañcavīsati ekakā honti – asubhārammaṇassa avaḍḍhanīyattā hi jhānūpacārova paṭilabbhati, na uttariṃ. Paritte uddhumātakaṭṭhāne uppannanimittaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, mahante appamāṇārammaṇanti veditabbaṃ.

### Arūpāvacarakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Idāni arūpāvacarakusalaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha arūpūpapattiyā maggaṃ bhāvento sabbaso rūpasaññānaṃ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṃ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṃ amanasikārā – kusalavipākakiriyavasena pañcadasannaṃ rūpasaññānaṃ, dasannaṃ paṭighasaññānaṃ, catucattālīsāya nānattasaññānaṃ virāgā nirodhā ca – ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ ākiñcaññāyatanaṃ nevasaññānāsaññāyatananti cattāri arūpajhānāni nibbatteti. Ussāhajananatthaṃ palobhanatthañca sotūnaṃ bhagavā imāsaṃ samāpattīnaṃ vaṇṇaṃ kathesi. Bhūmantaravasena arūpāvacaratā idha viseso.

#### Cattāri arūpajhānāni soḷasakkhattukāni

[[#^toc-2-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Cattāri arūpajhānāni soḷasakkhattukāni arūpāyatanajhānānaṃ ārammaṇapaṭipadāmissakanayena desitāni. Paṭhame ākāsānañcāyatane tīhi padehi ussāhajananatthaṃ palobhanatthañca vaṇṇo kathito – 'rūpasaññānaṃ samatikkamā'ti pañcadasannaṃ jhānasaṅkhātānaṃ aṭṭhannaṃ ca ārammaṇasaṅkhātānaṃ rūpasaññānaṃ sabbākārena virāgā nirodhā; 'paṭighasaññānaṃ atthaṅgamā'ti dasannaṃ cakkhādivatthu-rūpādārammaṇapaṭighātasamuṭṭhitānaṃ saññānaṃ pahānaṃ; 'nānattasaññānaṃ amanasikārā'ti catucattālīsāya kāmāvacaramanodhātumanoviññāṇadhātusaṅgahitānaṃ saññānaṃ anāvajjanaṃ. Evaṃ tīhi padehi ākāsānañcāyatanaṃ vaṇṇayitvā taṃ ārammaṇaṃ samatikkamitvā ākāse pavattaviññāṇārammaṇaṃ viññāṇañcāyatanaṃ, tadapagamārammaṇaṃ ākiñcaññāyatanaṃ, saṅkhārāvasesasukhumabhāvappattaṃ nevasaññānāsaññāyatananti evaṃ ārammaṇātikkamato catasso arūpasamāpattiyo anupubbena paṇītatarā honti. Ekekasmiṃ āyatane catutthajhānavasena pañcavīsati ekakā; ārammaṇacatukkena ca catasso paṭipadāhi ca yojetvā soḷasakkhattukā honti.

### Tebhūmakakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Sabbānipetāni tebhūmakakusalāni hīnādinā pabhedena vattanti, tasmā tesaṃ tesaṃ taṃ pabhedaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha hīnaṃ majjhimaṃ paṇītaṃ – imāni āyūhanavasena veditabbāni; yassa āyūhanakkhaṇe chando vā vīriyaṃ vā cittaṃ vā vīmaṃsā vā hīno hoti, taṃ hīnaṃ nāma, yassa majjhimā paṇītā ca, taṃ tathā. Chandaṃ dhuraṃ katvā āyūhitaṃ chandādhipateyyaṃ nāma; vīriyādhipateyye eseva nayo. Tebhūmakesu kusalesu kāmāvacarakusalaṃ tihetukampi duhetukampi; rūpāvacarārūpāvacaraṃ pana tihetukameva ñāṇasampayuttameva. Tebhūmakakusalaṃ vaṭṭasmiṃ cutipaṭisandhiyo ācināti vaḍḍhetīti ācayagāmī nāma hoti.

#### Kāmāvacarakusalaṃ (Tebhūmaka)

[[#^toc-2-4-1|↑↑↑]]

Tebhūmake kāmāvacarakusale hīnaṃ āyūhanakkhaṇe chandādike hīne lāmakanti, majjhimaṃ majjhime, paṇītaṃ paṇīte veditabbaṃ; yaṃ kattukāmatāsaṅkhātaṃ chandaṃ dhuraṃ katvā āyūhitaṃ taṃ chandādhipateyyaṃ nāma – evaṃ vīsati mahānayā yojitabbā. Kāmāvacarakusalaṃ tihetukampi duhetukampi hoti ñāṇasampayuttavippayuttavasena; adhipatinā sahāpi uppajjati vināpi. Ārammaṇādhipati sahajātādhipatīti dvepi adhipatayo labbhanti – ārammaṇādhipati pana imesu ñāṇasampayuttesu atthi, na ñāṇavippayuttesu. Tasmā ñāṇasampayuttesu vuttaparimāṇato vīsatiguṇo cittanavakavārabhedo veditabbo, ñāṇavippayuttesu pana soḷasaguṇoti.

#### Rūpāvacarakusalaṃ (Tebhūmaka)

[[#^toc-2-4-2|↑↑↑]]

Tebhūmake rūpāvacarakusale āyūhanavasena hīnamajjhimapaṇītabhedo chandādhipateyyādayo ca vīsati mahānayā yathāsambhavaṃ yojitabbā. Rūpāvacaraṃ pana tihetukameva ñāṇasampayuttameva – ñāṇavippayuttassa rūpāvacarajhānanibbattanasamatthatāyeva abhāvā. Adhipatisampannameva hoti, adhipatinā vinā anuppajjamānattā. Idha pana kāmāvacaravisesena ārammaṇādhipati na labbhati, sahajātādhipatiyeva labbhati – jhānārammaṇesu tadaṃsapaccayakaraṇāsamatthatāya. Tasmā vuttaparimāṇato vīsatiguṇoyeva cittanavakavārabhedo, na soḷasaguṇo.

#### Arūpāvacarakusalaṃ (Tebhūmaka)

[[#^toc-2-4-3|↑↑↑]]

Tebhūmake arūpāvacarakusale rūpāvacaravuttanayeneva āyūhanavasena hīnamajjhimapaṇītabhedo chandādhipateyyādayo ca vīsati mahānayā yojitabbā. Arūpāvacarampetaṃ tihetukameva ñāṇasampayuttameva; sahajātādhipatiyeva labbhati, na ārammaṇādhipati. Rūpāvacarato viseso pana etthāpatthibhūmārammaṇaviseso – arūpāvacarajhānāni ārammaṇātikkamakamena paṭilabbhantīti, yathā heṭṭhā vuttaṃ. Iti tebhūmakesu tīsu kusalabhūmīsu kāmāvacara ñāṇasampayuttavippayuttubhayasaṅgahitaṃ, rūpāvacararūpāvacara pana ñāṇasampayuttameva – ayaṃ mukhyavisesoti veditabbo.

### Lokuttarakusalaṃ

[[#^toc-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Evaṃ bhavattayasampattinibbattakaṃ kusalaṃ dassetvā idāni sabbabhavasamatikkamanāya lokuttarakusalaṃ dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā kusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. 'Lokaṃ taratīti lokuttaraṃ' – vaṭṭato niyyātīti niyyānikaṃ apacayagāmiṃ ca. Jhānaṃ bhāvetīti ekacittakkhaṇikaṃ appanājhānaṃ uppādeti vaḍḍheti. Diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānāya paṭhamāya bhūmiyā pattiyāti sotāpattiphalaṃ, samucchedavivekavasena viviccā. Paṭipadāya nānattaṃ dassetuṃ dukkhapaṭipadā dandhābhiññā, sukhāpaṭipadā khippābhiññāoti vuttaṃ. Phassādīsu anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvoti cattāro dhammā adhikā – maggaṭṭhakaṃ hoti. Etaṃ lokuttarakusalaṃ tebhūmakakusalaṃ upādāya apacayagāmī.

### Dvādasa akusalāni

[[#^toc-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Idāni akusaladhammapadaṃ bhājetvā dassetuṃ katame dhammā akusalātiādi āraddhaṃ. Akusalassāyaṃ bhūmibhedo natthi – tasmā ekantaṃ kāmāvacarampi samānaṃ 'kāmāvacara'nti na vuttaṃ. Dvādasasu akusalacittesu diṭṭhigatasampayuttā cattāro, diṭṭhivippayuttā cattāro, paṭighasampayuttā dve, vicikicchāsahagatā ca uddhaccasahagatā cāti dve apaṇṇakā – evaṃ dvādasa akusalacittuppādā. Tesu micchādiṭṭhi ayāthāvaditthilakkhaṇā ayonisomanasikārapadaṭṭhānā; lobho ārammaṇaggahaṇalakkhaṇo; moho aññāṇalakkhaṇo sabbākusalānaṃ mūlaṃ. Assaddhiyacitte pasādo natthi, asatiyacitte sati natthi, andhabālacitte paññā natthi – tasmā saddhāsatipaññā na gahitā. Ṭhapetvā uddhaccasahagataṃ, sesāni ekādaseva kamme āyūhite catūsu apāyesu paṭisandhiṃ ākaḍḍhanti.

### Abyākatavipāko

[[#^toc-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni abyākatapadaṃ bhājetvā dassetuṃ katame dhammā abyākatātiādi āraddhaṃ. Catubbidhaṃ abyākataṃ – vipākaṃ kiriyaṃ rūpaṃ nibbānanti. Tattha kusalavipākaṃ parittaṃ ahetukaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīhi ārabhitvā dassetuṃ kāmāvacarassa kusalassa kammassa katattā upacitattā cakkhuviññāṇaṃ uppajjatītiādi vuttaṃ. Tattha cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhusannissitarūpavijānanalakkhaṇaṃ; manodhātu rūpādivijānanalakkhaṇā sampaṭicchanarasā; manoviññāṇadhātu santīraṇādikiccā chaḷārammaṇavijānanalakkhaṇā hadayavatthupadaṭṭhānā. Tato aṭṭha mahāvipākacittāni dassetuṃ puna katame dhammā abyākatātiādi āraddhaṃ; tesaṃ attho heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva, viseso pana alobho abyākatamūlanti dassetuṃ vuttaṃ. Rūpāvacarārūpāvacaravipākāni kusalasadisattā kusalānugatikaṃ katvā bhājitāni.

#### Kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇāni

[[#^toc-2-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Kusalavipākapañcaviññāṇānaṃ vatthudvārārammaṇāni nibaddhāneva honti, vatthādisaṅkamanaṃ natthi. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhusannissitarūpavijānanalakkhaṇaṃ, rūpamattārammaṇarasaṃ, rūpābhimukhabhāvapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ; eseva nayo sotaviññāṇādīsupi. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhupasādaṃ vatthuṃ katvā iṭṭhe iṭṭhamajjhatte ca catusamuṭṭhānikarūpārammaṇe dassanakiccaṃ sādhayati; sotaviññāṇādīni sotapasādādīni vatthuṃ katvā savanaghāyanasāyanaphusanakiccāni. Cakkhudvārādīsu catūsu upekkhāvedanā bhājitā, kāyaviññāṇe pana ghaṭṭanādhikyena iṭṭhe sukhasahagataṃ aniṭṭhe dukkhasahagataṃ uppajjati.

#### Kusalavipākamanodhātu

[[#^toc-2-7-2|↑↑↑]]

Manodhātu sabhāvasuññatanissattaṭṭhena manoyeva dhātu; cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ anantaraṃ rūpādivijānanalakkhaṇā, rūpādīnaṃ sampaṭicchanarasā, tathābhāvapaccupaṭṭhānā, cakkhuviññāṇādiapagamapadaṭṭhānā. Vatthu nibaddhaṃ hadayavatthumeva; dvārārammaṇāni pana anibaddhāni – pañcadvāre ārammaṇānaṃ saṅkamantesu sampaṭicchanaṃ hutvā vipaccati, kusalavipākesu cakkhuviññāṇādīsu niruddhesu taṃsamanantarā tāneva ṭhānappattāni rūpārammaṇādīni sampaṭicchati. Jhānaṅgampi pañcaviññāṇasote patitvā gatattāyeva na uddhaṭaṃ; maggaṅgāni ca alābhatoyeva.

#### Kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātusomanassasahagatā

[[#^toc-2-7-3|↑↑↑]]

Paṭhamā kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu iṭṭhārammaṇasmiṃyeva pavattati, tasmā somanassavedanā hoti. Dvīsu ṭhānesu vipaccati – pañcadvāre kusalavipākacakkhuviññāṇādianantaraṃ vipākamanodhātuyā taṃ ārammaṇaṃ sampaṭicchitvā niruddhāya santīraṇakiccaṃ sādhayamānā, chasu dvāresu ca balavārammaṇe javanena gahitārammaṇaṃ gahetvā tadārammaṇabhāvena. Tadārammaṇaṃ patiṭṭhamānaṃ bhavaṅgassa vāraṃ adatvā ekaṃ dve cittavāre pavattitvā bhavaṅgameva otarati. Ubhayatthāpi pañcaviññāṇasote patitvā gatattāyeva jhānaṅgamaggaṅgāni na uddhaṭāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1388]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1389]]

#### Kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātuupekkhāsahagatā

[[#^toc-2-7-4|↑↑↑]]

Dutiyā kusalavipākamanoviññāṇadhātu iṭṭhamajjhattārammaṇe pavattati, tasmā upekkhāvedanā hoti; padāni ca manodhātuniddesasadisāneva. Pañcasu ṭhānesu vipaccati – jaccandhajaccabadhirajaccaeḷajaccummattakaubhatobyañjanakanapuṃsakānaṃ paṭisandhiggahaṇakāle paṭisandhi hutvā, paṭisandhiyā vītivattāya yāvatāyukaṃ bhavaṅgaṃ hutvā, iṭṭhamajjhatte pañcārammaṇavīthiyā santīraṇaṃ hutvā, balavārammaṇe chasu dvāresu tadārammaṇaṃ hutvā, maraṇakāle cuti hutvāti. Lakkhaṇādito duvidhāpi manoviññāṇadhātu ahetukavipākā, chaḷārammaṇavijānanalakkhaṇā, santīraṇādirasā, hadayavatthupadaṭṭhānāti veditabbā.

#### Aṭṭhamahāvipākā

[[#^toc-2-7-5|↑↑↑]]

Aṭṭhamahāvipākacittesu kammadvārakammapathapuññakiriyavatthubhedo natthi – aviññattijanakato avipākadhammato tathā appavattito ca. Karuṇāmuditā sattārammaṇattā na santi – ekantaparittārammaṇāni hi kāmāvacaravipākāni; viratiyo ca 'pañca sikkhāpadāni kusalānevā'ti vacanato na santi. Asaṅkhārasasaṅkhāravidhānaṃ kusalato ceva paccayabhedato ca veditabbaṃ; hīnassa kusalassa vipākaṃ hīnaṃ, majjhimassa majjhimaṃ, paṇītassa paṇītaṃ. Catūsu ṭhānesu vipaccanti – paṭisandhiyaṃ bhavaṅge cutiyaṃ tadārammaṇeti. Sabbaññubodhisattā pacchimapaṭisandhiggahaṇe paṭhamena somanassasahagatatihetukaasaṅkhārikamahāvipākacittena paṭisandhiṃ gaṇhantīti vuttaṃ.

#### Rūpāvacaravipākā

[[#^toc-2-7-6|↑↑↑]]

Rūpāvacaravipākacittāni kusalānugatikaṃ katvā bhājitāni – yathā hatthiassapabbatādīnaṃ chāyā attano mūlasadisāva honti, tathā rūpāvacaravipākaṃ attano kusalasadisameva hoti, na sadisampi asadisampi. Kāmāvacarakammañca yadā kadāci vipākaṃ deti, rūpāvacaraṃ pana anantarāyena dutiyasmiṃyeva attabhāve vipākaṃ detītipi kusalānugatikaṃ katvā bhājitaṃ. Paṭipadādibhedo hīnapaṇītamajjhimabhāvo ca etesaṃ jhānāgamanato veditabbo. Chandādīnaṃ aññataraṃ dhuraṃ katvā anuppādanīyattā niradhipatikāneva etāni; sesaṃ kusale vuttanayeneva.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1473]]

#### Arūpāvacaravipākā

[[#^toc-2-7-7|↑↑↑]]

Arūpāvacaravipākacittānipi rūpāvacarasadisanayeneva kusalānugatikāni katvā bhājitāni – attano kusalasadisamevāti. Kāmāvacaravipākaṃ viya attano kusalena sadisampi asadisampi na honti; anantarāyena dutiyasmiṃyeva attabhāve vipākaṃ denti. Paṭipadādibhedo ca hīnapaṇītamajjhimabhāvo ca arūpajhānāgamanato veditabbo. Chandādīnaṃ dhuraṃ katvā anuppādanīyattā niradhipatikāneva etāni; sesaṃ sabbaṃ kusale vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha#^1-1473]]

### Lokuttaravipāka-paṭhamamaggavipākā

[[#^toc-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Lokuttaravipākampi kusalasadisattā kusalānugatikameva katvā bhājitaṃ. Tebhūmakakusalaṃ cutipaṭisandhivasena vaṭṭaṃ ācināti vaḍḍhetīti 'katattā upacitattā'ti tattha vuttaṃ; lokuttaraṃ pana tena ācitampi apacināti, sayampi cutipaṭisandhivasena na ācināti – tenettha 'katattā bhāvitattā'ti vuttaṃ. Maggāgamanaṃ dhuraṃ phalassa āgataṭṭhāne; suññataanimittaappaṇihitanāmāni maggānugatikaṃ phalaṃ labhati. Tebhūmakakusalānañhi añño āyūhanakālo añño vipaccanakālo – na sakkā attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ labhāpetuṃ. Lokuttarāni pana tāya paññāya avūpasantāya apaṇṇakaṃ maggānantarameva vipākaṃ paṭilabhanti – tasmā attano vipākaṃ adhipatiṃ labhāpetuṃ sakkonti.

### Akusalavipākaabyākataṃ

[[#^toc-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Akusalavipākāni pañca cakkhusotaghānajivhākāyaviññāṇāni, ekā manodhātu, ekā manoviññāṇadhātūti imāni satta cittāni pāḷito ca atthato ca kusalavipākacittehi sadisāni. Kevalañhi tāni kusalakammapaccayāni imāni akusalakammapaccayāni; tāni iṭṭhaiṭṭhamajjhattesu ārammaṇesu vattanti, imāni aniṭṭhaaniṭṭhamajjhattesu. Tattha sukhasahagataṃ kāyaviññāṇaṃ, idha dukkhasahagataṃ. Upekkhāsahagatā manoviññāṇadhātu akusalacittena kamme āyūhite kammakammanimittagatinimittesu aññataraṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā catūsu apāyesu paṭisandhi hutvā, dutiyavārato paṭṭhāya yāvatāyukaṃ bhavaṅgaṃ hutvā, pañcasu ṭhānesu vipaccati.
