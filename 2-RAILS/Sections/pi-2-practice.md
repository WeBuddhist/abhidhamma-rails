## Khandhavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-1|↑↑↑]]

Khandhavibhaṅge pañca khandhā rāsilakkhaṇena koṭṭhāsalakkhaṇena ca vibhattā. Yassa yogino "rūpaṃ ruppanaṭṭhena rūpaṃ, vedanā vedayitalakkhaṇā, saññā sañjānanalakkhaṇā, saṅkhārā āyūhanalakkhaṇā, viññāṇaṃ vijānanalakkhaṇa"nti pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ sabhāvo pākaṭo hoti, so "na ettha koci satto na puggalo, rāsimattameva ida"nti passanto attasaññaṃ pajahati. Tasmā yoginā pañcasu khandhesu "netaṃ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā"ti anupassitvā, khandhesu attagāhaṃ akaraṇena pāpaṃ pahāya, aniccadukkhānattalakkhaṇāropanena vipassanaṃ upasampādetvā, cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ. Ayañhi khandhavibhaṅgo attadiṭṭhisamugghātanatthaṃ desito.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ

[[#^toc-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye pañca khandhā ekādasasu okāsesu — atītānāgatapaccuppanna-ajjhattabahiddhā-oḷārikasukhuma-hīnapaṇīta-dūresantikavasena — vibhattā. Ayaṃ ekādasavidho vibhāgo yogino sabbapariyādānatthaṃ upakappati: yaṃ kiñci rūpaṃ vā vedanā vā, atītaṃ vā anāgataṃ vā, attano vā parassa vā, oḷārikaṃ vā sukhumaṃ vā, sabbaṃ tadekajjhaṃ abhisaññūhitvā "khandho"ti passati. Tasmā yoginā ekekasmiṃ okāse attasaññaṃ apanetvā, "sabbaṃ idaṃ khandhamattaṃ aniccaṃ vipariṇāmadhamma"nti sammasitvā, kāmarāgaṃ paṭighañca tesu ṭhānesu pahāya, sabbaso pariggahaṃ paṭṭhapetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-6]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-7]]

#### Rūpakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandhaniddese "ruppanaṭṭhena rūpa"nti vuttaṃ — sītuṇhādivasena vikāraṃ āpajjanato rūpaṃ nāma. Atītaṃ rūpaṃ niruddhaṃ vigataṃ vipariṇataṃ atthaṅgataṃ, anāgataṃ ajātaṃ anuppannaṃ, paccuppannaṃ jātaṃ uppannaṃ samuṭṭhitaṃ. Evaṃ ruppanasabhāvaṃ passanto yogī sarīre "subha"nti saññaṃ pajahati. Tasmā yoginā attano parassa ca kāye cattāro mahābhūte upādārūpañca "idaṃ ruppati vipariṇamatī"ti pariggaṇhitvā, kāyasmiṃ rāgaṃ mānañca pahāya, asubhānupassanaṃ aniccānupassanañca upasampādetvā, kāye anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-8]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-9]]

#### Vedanākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Vedanākkhandhaniddese sukhā dukkhā adukkhamasukhā ca vedanā kāyikacetasikavasena vibhattā; tāsaṃ atītādibhāvo santativasena khaṇādivasena ca veditabbo. Oḷārikasukhumaniddese akusalā vedanā sadarathaṭṭhena dukkhavipākaṭṭhena ca oḷārikā, kusalā niddarathaṭṭhena sukhavipākaṭṭhena ca sukhumā. Yathā ca vedanā khaṇattayapariyāpannā uppajjitvā nirujjhati, evaṃ passanto tattha nābhinandati nāppaṭikkosati. Tasmā yoginā sukhāya vedanāya rāgaṃ, dukkhāya paṭighaṃ, adukkhamasukhāya avijjañca pahāya, "vedanā aniccā saṅkhatā paṭiccasamuppannā"ti sallakkhetvā, vedanānupassanaṃ bhāvetvā, vedanāsu anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Saññākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhaniddese cakkhusamphassajādivasena cha saññā vuttā — purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva, manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi. Paṭighasamphassajā nāma sappaṭighe vatthumhi sappaṭighe ārammaṇe ca uppannā. Saññā hi nimittaṃ gaṇhāti; tato ca papañco vaḍḍhati. Tasmā yoginā dvāresu ārammaṇaṃ āpāthagataṃ "saññāmattaṃ ida"nti ñatvā, nimittaggāhena anusārena uppajjamānaṃ kāmasaññaṃ byāpādasaññaṃ vihiṃsāsaññañca pahāya, nekkhammasaññaṃ abyāpādasaññaṃ avihiṃsāsaññañca upasampādetvā, "aniccā saññā"ti anupassanena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-60]]

#### Saṅkhārakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese "cetanā"ti heṭṭhimakoṭiyā padhānasaṅkhāravasena vuttaṃ — cetanā hi āyūhanaṭṭhena pākaṭattā padhānā, tāya gahitāya taṃsampayuttasaṅkhārā gahitāva honti. Cetanā ca kammaṃ nāma; tasmā ayaṃ khandho kammassa mūlaṃ dasseti. Tasmā yoginā "cetanāhaṃ, bhikkhave, kammaṃ vadāmī"ti ñatvā, akusalacetanāya kāyavacīmanoduccaritaṃ pahāya, kusalacetanāya dānasīlabhāvanākammaṃ upasampādetvā, sabbasaṅkhāre "aniccā vipariṇāmadhammā"ti passanto saṅkhāresu nibbindanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Viññāṇakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Viññāṇakkhandhaniddese cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāneva, manoviññāṇaṃ hadayavatthukampi avatthukampi; sabbaṃ catubhūmakaṃ viññāṇaṃ. Na sakkā viññāṇaṃ "manosamphassaja"nti niddisituṃ — viññāṇameva hi mano nāma. Yassa yogino "viññāṇaṃ paccayuppannaṃ, cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca uppajjati cakkhuviññāṇa"nti pākaṭaṃ hoti, so "attā viññātā"ti gāhaṃ pajahati. Tasmā yoginā viññāṇe attagāhaṃ pahāya, "paṭiccasamuppannaṃ ida"nti sallakkhetvā, cittānupassanaṃ bhāvetvā, viññāṇe anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye satthā khandhe bhājento tike gahetvā dukesu pakkhipi, duke gahetvā tikesu pakkhipi, ubhatovaḍḍhananīhārena āhari, avasāne bahuvidhena dassesi — iti cattāro mahāvārā. Etaṃ pana bahudhā desanaṃ puggalajjhāsayena ceva desanāvilāsena ca katanti veditabbaṃ: yassa yena ākārena paṭivijjhanaṃ sappāyaṃ, tassa tenākārena desesi. Tasmā yoginā "nāyaṃ dhammo ekeneva nayena gahetabbo"ti ñatvā, attano cittassa sappāyaṃ kammaṭṭhānanayaṃ gahetvā, mānaṃ diṭṭhiparāmāsañca pahāya, nānānayehi khandhe sammasanto paññaṃ upasampādetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Rūpakkhandho (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye rūpakkhandhaniddeso dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ rūpakaṇḍe vitthāritanayeneva veditabbo — tattha ekavidhādivasena rūpassa vibhāgo vitthārito. Tasmā yoginā heṭṭhā rūpakaṇḍe pariggahitaṃ rūpassa ruppanasabhāvaṃ idhāpi anussaritvā, "sabbaṃ rūpaṃ cattāri mahābhūtāni upādārūpañcā"ti paricchinditvā, rūpe subhasaññaṃ niccasaññañca pahāya, kāye satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-116]]

#### Vedanākkhandho (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye vedanākkhandho ekavidhena phassasampayuttato, duvidhena sahetukāhetukato, tividhena jātito, catubbidhena bhūmantarato, pañcavidhena indriyato, chabbidhena vatthuto, sattavidhādivasena ca bhedato dassito. Catubhūmikavedanā ca cakkhudvārādīsu tīhi ākārehi labbhati — upanissayavasena, samatikkamavasena, bhāvanāvasena. Tattha yathā ekacco jātijarābyādhimaraṇaṃ bhayato disvā "na sakkā nipajjitvā niddāyantena jātiādīhi muccituṃ, āditova vīriyaṃ kātuṃ sīlaṃ sodhetuṃ vaṭṭatī"ti cintetvā arahattaṃ pāpuṇāti, evaṃ yoginā saṃvegaṃ upanissayaṃ katvā sīlaṃ sodhetabbaṃ, uppanne kilese samatikkamitvā, nāmarūpaṃ pariggaṇhitvā tīṇi lakkhaṇāni āropetvā vipassanāpaṭipāṭiyā saṅkhāre sammasitvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayo vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti; saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā "cittasampayutto"ti vuttaṃ; viññāṇakkhandhaniddese "cakkhuviññāṇa"ntiādi vuttaṃ, na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ manosamphassajanti niddisituṃ. Iti ekasmiṃ nayamhi gahite sesā tenupāyena gahitāva honti. Tasmā yoginā ekasmiṃ khandhe sammasanañāṇaṃ paṭiladdhaṃ sesesupi upanetvā, "sabbe pañca khandhā aniccā dukkhā anattā"ti ekarasena passanto, khandhesu attagāhaṃ pahāya, sabbaso anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchane pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kusalākusalābyākatādivasena pañhā pucchitvā vissajjitā. Ārammaṇattikesu cattāro khandhā kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha rajjantassa dussantassa muyhantassa vā parittārammaṇā honti, saṃvarantassa sammasantassa vā tatheva; maggaphalanibbānāni paccavekkhantassa appamāṇārammaṇā. Attano khandhe ārabbha ajjhattārammaṇā, paresaṃ ārabbha bahiddhārammaṇā. Iti ekasmiṃyeva ārammaṇe rajjanaṃ vā saṃvaraṇaṃ vā hoti. Tasmā yoginā "ārammaṇe patiṭṭhitaṃ me cittaṃ rajjati vā saṃvarati vā"ti sallakkhetvā, ārammaṇesu rāgadosamohe pahāya, tesuyeva saṃvaraṃ sammasanañca upasampādetvā, ajjhattabahiddhā khandhe samaṃ passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.
