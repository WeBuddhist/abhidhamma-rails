## Tikamātikā

Aṭṭhasāliniyaṃ Buddhaghosācariyo mātikāpadabhājanīyassa paṭhamabhāgaṃ — kusalattikādīni dvāvīsati tikāni saddhiṃ satta dukasatehi — pañcadasaparicchedavasena vavatthāpeti. Tikamātikā nāma kusalattikādivasena ādimhi ṭhitā, hetudukādayo dukā tato paraṃ vuttā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-507)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-509)

Sappadesanippadesavasena tikadukā duvidhā: nava tikā ekasattati ca dukā sappadesā, terasa tikā ekasattati ca dukā nippadesāti. Kusalattikādayopi nippadesā honti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-510)

Kusalasaddo cattāresu atthesu dissati: ārogye, anavajje, cheke, sukhavipāke ca. Idha pana ārogyaanavajjasukhavipāka-tisu eva atthesu pavattati. Dhammasaddo pana pariyatti-hetu-guṇa-nissattanijjīvatāsu dissati, idha pana nissattanijjīvatāyameva pavattati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-511)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-512)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-514)

Kusalānaṃ vacanattho — kucchite pāpake dhamme salayanti calayanti kampenti viddhaṃsentīti kusalā; kusena ñāṇena lātabbāti vā kusalā. Akusalā kusalapaṭipakkhavacanena vuccanti, na kusalābhāvamattavacanena. Abyākatā ‘‘kusalākusalabhāvena akathitā’’ti. Lakkhaṇato kusalā anavajjasukhavipākalakkhaṇā, akusalā sāvajjadukkhavipākalakkhaṇā, abyākatā avipākalakkhaṇāti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-515)

Codako ‘‘kusalā dhammā’’ti ettha kusala-dhamma-saddānaṃ ekatthatā vā nānatthatā vāti vimaṃsati. Aṭṭhakathāyaṃ ubhayapakkho akāraṇaṃ vutto — yathānumativohārasiddhito kusalasaddo anavajjasukhavipākasaṅkhātassa atthassa jotako, akusalasaddo sāvajjadukkhavipākatthajotako, abyākatasaddo avipākatthajotako, dhammasaddo pana sabbesaṃ sabhāvadhāraṇādiatthasāmaññaṃ dīpetīti. Imināva nayena sesatikadukānampi anupubbapadavaṇṇanā veditabbāti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-516)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-517)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-518)

Tato vedanāttike sukha-dukkha-saddānaṃ pana atthavibhāgaṃ dasseti — idha pana sukhavedanāyameva dukkhavedanāyañca daṭṭhabbo. Sampayuttapadasseva attho ekuppādatādīhi pakārehi yuttoti veditabbo. Sesatikā imināva sappadesena sampīḷetvā vaṇṇitā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-519)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-520)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-521)

Sanidassanattike sanidassanā ‘‘sahanidassanena’’ sappaṭighā ‘‘sahapaṭighena’’ti vuttā. Tatiyapadaṃ ubhayapaṭikkhepena pavattati. Evaṃ tikamātikāya anupubbapadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541)
