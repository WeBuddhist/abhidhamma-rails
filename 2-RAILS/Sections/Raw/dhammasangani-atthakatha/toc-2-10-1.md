## Kiriyāmanodhātu

Kiriyamanodhātucittassa ekaggatāniddese pavattiṭṭhitimattameva labbhati; dubbalattā saṇṭhiti avaṭṭhitiādīni na labbhanti. Sesaṃ sabbaṃ vipākamanodhātuniddese vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ, aññatra uppattiṭṭhānā — tañhi vipākamanodhātucittaṃ pañcaviññāṇānantaraṃ uppajjati.

(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-1488)

Idaṃ pana kiriyamanodhātucittaṃ pañcadvāre vaḷañjanakappavattikāle sabbesaṃ pure uppajjati. Cakkhudvāre tāva iṭṭhaiṭṭhamajjhattaaniṭṭhaaniṭṭhamajjhattesu rūpārammaṇesu yena kenaci pasāde ghaṭṭite taṃ ārammaṇaṃ gahetvā āvajjanavasena purecārikaṃ hutvā bhavaṅgaṃ āvaṭṭayamānaṃ uppajjati; sotadvārādīsupi eseva nayo. Kiriyamanodhātucittaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ.
