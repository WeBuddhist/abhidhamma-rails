## Tikamātikā

Mūlaṭīkāyaṃ ācariyo aṭṭhakathāya ‘‘sabbapadehi laddhanāmo’’ti vacanaṃ vyākhyāti — vedanāsaddo tīsupi padesu vijjamāno, padasamudāyo eva idha padanti vuccatīti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-77)

Dukānaṃ aññamaññasambandho ‘‘ganthato ca atthato cā’’ti vutto — hetupada-sahetukapadādīhi sambandhattā, hetuattha-sahetukatthādīhi sambandhattā ca; gocchakavibhāgo kaṇṇikā viya ghaṭā viya hutvā saṅgahito. Sappadesanippadesavibhāgo asaṅgahitapadesabhāvābhāvavasena ñāyatīti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-78)

Kusalasaddassa cha atthe aṭṭhakathāyaṃ vuttā, mūlaṭīkā tesaṃ paccuddhāraṇaṃ karoti — ‘‘kacci nu bhoto anāmaya’’nti anāmayattho, bāhitikasutte vutto kusalakāyasamācāro anavajjatthena vutto, bodhipakkhiyadhammā ‘‘kusalā dhammā’’ti anavajjatthe eva. ‘‘Kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ samādānahetu’’ti puññavipākanibbattakakammaṃ kusalanti vuttaṃ. ‘‘Dhammā hontī’’ti suññadhammatā vā sabhāvamattatā vā gahetabbā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-79)

Kusalassa vacanatthe ‘‘salayanti calayanti kampenti viddhaṃsentī’’ti aṭṭhakathāya vutte purimassa pacchimaṃ atthavacananti vā, salanassa tīṇi atthadīpanāni tadaṅgappahānādīhi yojetabbāni vā. Ñāṇavippayuttānampi kusaluppatti vinā yonisomanasikāraṃ na hoti, tasmā sabbe kusalā ñāṇena pavattetabbāti ‘‘kusena lātabbā’’ti attho yujjati. Ubhayabhāgagataṃ saṃkilesapakkhaṃ sammappadhānadvayavasena chindantīti vā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-80)

Dhammasaddassa ‘‘attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentī’’ti vacanaṃ — sattādigāhakacittānaṃ gocarā sattādayo viya na nissabhāvā, paccayehi dhāriyantīti vā yathāsabhāvato dhārīyantīti vā ettha attho dīpīyate; lakkhīyantīti vā dhārīyantīti veditabbaṃ.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-81)

Akusalasaddo na kusalābhāvamattavacanaṃ na ca kusalehi tadaññamattavacanaṃ, atha kho kusalapaṭipakkhavacanaṃ. Tassa tappaṭipakkhattā ca ‘‘abyākatā’’ti tatiyo rāsi vutto, tato akusalo eko rāsīti viññāyati. Loke ‘‘amittā’’ti, sāsane ‘‘alobho’’ti, idhāpi akusala-saddo tappaṭipakkhavacanaṃ siddho.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-82)

Abyākatasaddo na avipākavacanena akathitavasena akusalā vā kusalā vā āpajjanti — anuvattamānavacanavacanīyabhāvena akathitassa abyākatabhāvato. Apicāyaṃ vi-saddo virodhavacano, ā-saddo abhimukhabhāvappakāsano. Byākatā kusalākusalā lakkhaṇavirodhato vināsakavināsitabbatāya ca vuttā; abyākatā ca te lakkhaṇato kusalākusalā viya viruddhā na honti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-83)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-84)

Anavajjasukhavipākalakkhaṇāti ettha anavajjavacanena pavattisukhatā kusalānaṃ, sukhavipākavacanena vipākasukhatā dīpitā. Purimena attasuddhi, pacchimena visuddhavipākatā; purimena kusalaṃ akusalasabhāvato nivatteti, pacchimena abyākatasabhāvato. Sāvajjadukkhavipākalakkhaṇāti akusalānaṃ; avipākalakkhaṇāti abyākatānaṃ.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-85)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-86)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-87)

Codako ‘‘kusalā dhammā’’ti chakkacatukkabhāvāpattidosaṃ dasseti, mūlaṭīkā taṃ paṭisedheti — māsasaddo viya eko saddo bhinnatthe vattumarahati, tasmā tikadukānaṃ chakkacatukkabhāvo nāpajjati. Padānaṃ asambandhaṃ siddhaṃ katvā chakkacatukkabhāvāpatti coditā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-88)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-89)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-90)

Evaṃ tikamātikāpadavaṇṇanā mūlaṭīkāya niṭṭhitā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-124)
