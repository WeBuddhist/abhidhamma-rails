## Tikamātikā

Anuṭīkāyaṃ ‘‘sabbapadehī’’ti tīhi padehi laddhanāmo, avayavadhammenāpi samudāyassa apadisitabbato yathā ‘‘samaṃ cuṇṇa’’nti. Codako vibhattiantasseva padabhāvaṃ sallakkhetvā codeti, itaro padasamudāyassa padabhāvaṃ pakaroti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-70)

Hetugocchakadvayasambandho ubhayekapadavasena yathā paṭhamadukasambandhā dutiyatatiyadukā, evaṃ āsavagocchakādīsupi nayo dīpīyate. Sappadesanippadesatā tikadukānaṃ asaṅgahitadhammāpadesayuttatāya niraṅgavasena ñāyatīti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-71)

Anavajjattho avajjavirahattho. Ñāṇavippayuttānampi kusalānaṃ ñāṇena pavattanaṃ yonisomanasikāramūlattā siddhaṃ, vinā yonisomanasikāraṃ kusaluppatti natthi. Sammappadhānadvayaṃ viya ubhayabhāgagataṃ saṃkilesapakkhaṃ chindantīti vā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-72)

‘‘Sabhāvaṃ dhārentī’’ti ettha kattukammabhedo paramatthato natthi, kappanāsiddho eva pana bhedoti dassīyate. Saṅkhatāsaṅkhatadhammā paṭhamavasena, saṅkhatā dutiyavasena, saṅkhatāsaṅkhatapaññattidhammā tatiyavasena vuttāti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-73)

Kusalapaṭisedhanaṃ kusalābhāvomattaṃ na hoti — sattāpaṭisedho hi abhāvoti, akusalavacanena sabhāvadhammo abodhiyamāno akusalā na vutto siyā, anabyākataggahaṇañca āpajjeyya, dukacatukkabhāvopi āpajjeyya, taṃ pana na hoti, tasmā akusalasaddo tappaṭipakkhavacanaṃ eva.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-74)

A-kāro na-atthattayasseva jotako na hoti — taṃyoganivattiyā (ahetukā), taṃsambandhibhāvanivattiyā (appaccayā), taṃsabhāvanivattiyā (anidassanā), taggayhabhāvanivattiyā (anāsavā), taṃkiccanivattiyā (appaṭighā, anārammaṇā), tabbhāvanivattiyā (arūpino, acetasikā), tabbhāvamattanivattiyā (amanusso), taṃsambhāvanaguṇanivattiyā (asamaṇo), tadappabhāvanivattiyā (anāmayā), taṃsadisabhāvanivattiyā (anuppannā), tadapariyosānanivattiyā (asekkhā) – evamanekesaṃ atthānaṃ jotako, idha pana pārisesena tappaṭipakkhavacanatā eva niyatā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-75)

Tappaṭipakkhabhāvo viruddhasabhāvattā tadantogadho, tappaheyyabhāvo pana kusalākusalesu niyato, na kusalā akusalehi pahātabbā, mahābalavatāya pana kusalāyeva akusale tadaṅgavikkhambhanasamucchedavasena pajahanti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-76)

Abyākatavacanena avipākatthā bodhitā eva. Kusalākusalānaṃ ahaṃ avipākārahā na hontīti vacanaṃ ‘‘aññāpakasādhanīyavacano bhagavā’’ti nivāretabboti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-77)

Anavajjavacanaṃ visesanabhāvena gahitaṃ — sukhavipākasaṅkhātassa kusalabhāvasamattassa kusalānaṃ agarahitabbatāsaṅkhātaṃ visesaṃ dasseti. Pavattisukhatāsukhavipākatāattasuddhivisuddhavipākatāakusalābyākatasabhāvanivattirasapaccupaṭṭhānapadaṭṭhānavisesadīpanena padadvayapariggaho vipulappayojano. Saṅkhāradukkhūpasamasukhassa vipākatā na sambhavati, tasmā sukha-saddo idha iṭṭhapariyāyavacananti veditabbaṃ.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-78)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-79)

Sāvajjadukkhavipākalakkhaṇāti akusalānaṃ. Avajja-saddo rāgādīsu niruḷho, tasmā sāvajjavacaneneva akusalānaṃ kusalābyākatehi viseso dassito. Vipākadhammatāpaṭisedhavasena abyākatānaṃ avipākārahasabhāvo lakkhaṇaṃ.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-80)

Chakkacatukkabhāvānāpatti dassīyate — tīhi dhammasaddehi vuccamāno sabhāvadhāraṇatthena abhinno, jātiādibhedena pana bhinno; māsa-saddo viya bhinnajātiyatthavacanīyatāya rūpābhedepi atthabhedo upapajjati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-81)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-82)

Evaṃ anuṭīkāyaṃ tikamātikāpadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-144)
