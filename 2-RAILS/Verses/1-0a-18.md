## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha atitatikassa tinam padanam attham evamdasseti: *atita dhamma* nama attano sabhavam — uppadadikkhanam va — patva atikkatanta, te kalam pi sabhavam pi atikkamimsu ti "atita"; *anagata dhamma* nama tadubhayam pi — kalo ca sabhavo ca — na agata, anuppatta; *paccuppanna dhamma* nama tam tam karanam paticca uppanna, paticca-samuppade vuttanayena paccaya-samuppanna ti lakkhanena niyutta.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-537)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatiika atthakathayam "attano sabhavan"ti padam sphutataram vibhajati: *sabhavo* nama kakkhalaphusanadisabhavo — pathavidhaturiyam kakkhalatttam, apodhatuyam paggharanam ityadi — imina lakkhananena atita ye te sabhavam patva atikkatanta. Ayam vibhago atikkanta-saddena kalam ca sabhavanca ubhayam sangahitanti dassetu katam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-119)

## Consensus

Dve tikaranani (atthakatha, mulatiika) imasmim tike sammatam: (1) *atita dhamma* nama sabhavam ca kalam ca atikkatanta — "sabhavo" idha kakkhalaphusanadisabhavo, na kevalam kalapariyapanno; (2) *anagata dhamma* nama kalam ca sabhavam ca anapatta — tadubhayeneva "anagata" vuccanti; (3) *paccuppanna dhamma* nama tam tam karanam paticca idani uppanna — paccaya-samuppatti-lakkhanana.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-537)
