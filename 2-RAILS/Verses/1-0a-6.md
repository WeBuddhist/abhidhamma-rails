## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha savitakkasavicara ti sampayogavasena vyakaroti: saha vitakkena vattamana savitakka, saha vicarena savicara --- sampayogena ekuppadadi-bhavena sahabhavo idha adhippeto. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525) Savitakka ca te savicara cati savitakkasavicara ti dvandvasamaso. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525) Avitakkaavicara ti ubhayarahita --- vitakkanca vicaranca ubhayam vipayutta. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525) Vicaramatta ti: vitakkavicaresuvicaro va matta pamanum etesam --- vicaro uttarim vitakkena sampayogam na gacchanti, vitakkabhave vigatepi vicaro yeva titthati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525) Avitakka ca te vicaramatta cati avitakkavicara-matta ti kammadhara-ya-samaso: avitakka eva, vicaramatta eva. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika matta-saddassa pamana-vacakatan adi-to gahetva vicaro va matta etesam ti attham samvanneti: avitakkanam jhanadhamminam vicaro yeva pamanam, na vitakko. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102) Aparam attham ca dasseti: matta-saddo visesa-nivatti-attho --- savitakkasavicara hi vitakkavisitten vicaren savicara, ete pana vicara-mattena vitakkasankhata-visesarahitena, tasma vicaramatta ti vuccanti, vicara-mattavanto ti attho. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102) Avitakkavacanam ca vicara-mattavacanena avitakkatte siddhe annesa-mpi avitakkanam atthibhava-jotanattha-m vuttanti niyameti --- avitakka hi vicaramatta ca santi avicara cati nivattetabba gahetabba ca honti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102) Pada-nukkama-bhedo ca pakasito: visesanavisesitabba-bhavo pana yathakamam hoti ti --- dhammesu nivattetabba-gahetabba-bhava yena yena pakarena labbhanti, tena tena pakarena visesana-visesitabba-bhavo sambhavati ti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102) Dutiye paragraphe mulatika savicara duvidhati pakaseti: savitakka ca avitakka ca --- tesu avitakke nivattetum adipadam vuttam, avicara ca duvidhata savitakka avitakka ca --- tesu savitakke nivattetum tatiyapadam vuttam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-103) Ye dvihipi nivattita avitakka savicara ca avicara ca, tesu avitakkavicara-matta ti avitakkasavicaresu vitakko ca vicaro ca yathavuttatuppadesu sakka vinnatunti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-103)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika matta-saddassa tividhattham vitthara-yati: (a) pamana-vacakam --- silamattakam, param viya mattayaadisu viya; (b) mariyada-vacako --- vicaro hettha jhananggesu hetthi-ma-mariyado, na pathamajjhana-upacara-jjhanesu viya vitakko; (c) isadattho --- mattasukha-pariccagaadisu viya, vitakka-rahita bhava-naya atisukhumabhuta-vicaratta isam vicara ca avitakkavicara-matta ti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-120) Vicara-mariyadata vitakkabhavena jatati anutika niyameti: avitakka hutva vicara-mariyada-jhananggesu vicara-hetthi-ma-kotika ti avitakka-gahanam katam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-120) Dutiye paragraphe anutika mulatika-vacanam parisodhetva pakaseti: avitakka savitakka ca savicara avicara cati avitakka-savicara savitakka avicara ti yojetabbam --- ubhayeka-desa-dassanena itarampi pakasetu nti adhippayena. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-121) Vitakkabhavena ete vicaramatta ti ayampi attho visesa-nivatti-atthamyeva matta-saddam gahetva vutto --- vicaramatta ti hi vicara-mattavanto ti vinnayamanatta tad-anna-visesa-viraha-sammannato nivattetva vitakka-visesa-viraha-sankhate avitakka-saddo sanni-dhapito viseseti dutiyajjhana-dhamme ti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-121)

## Consensus

Sabbani tini pakkharanani imamattham anumodanti: (1) savitakkasavicara ti sampayogavasena sahabhavena vitakka-vicara-sampayutta dhamma --- ekuppadadi-bhavo adhippeto (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525); (2) vicaramatta ti matta-saddo vicarass-eva pamana-tam va visesanivattitam va dipeti --- vitakkena saha sampayogam na gacchanti ti (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-120); (3) avitakkavicara-matta ti kammadharaya-samaso --- avitakka eva vicara-matta cati (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102); (4) avitakkaavicara ti ubhayarahita --- vitakkanca vicaranca ubhayam vipayutta dhamma (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525); (5) avitakkavacanam vicara-mattavacanena avitakkatte siddhe annasam-pi avitakkanam atthibhava-jotanattham (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-120).

## Divergences

- **matta-saddassa attho** --- Atthakatha matta ti pamana-vacakameva gahetva vicaro va matta etesam ti niddisati; Mulatika dvidha dasseti: pamana-vacakam va visesanivatti-attho va; Anutika tisso ariyani (pamana-, mariyada-, isadattha-) vibhajati.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-120)

- **padanukkama-bhedo** --- Atthakatha avitakka-vicara-matta ti kammadharaya-samaso; Mulatika visesana-visesitabba-bhavo yathakamam hotiti padanukkamo kato dipeti.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-525)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-102)
