## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha micchattatikaM evaM vibhajati: *micchatta* ti musasabhava, *hitasukhavaha me bhavissanti* ti evaM asisitapi tatha abhavato ca asubhadisu subhadiviparitappavattito ca musasabhava; *vipakadane sati khandhabhedanantarameva* phalanti; iti *micchatta ca niyata ca* ti *micchattaniyata*.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534)

*Sammattaniyata* ti vuttaviparitena atthena sammasabhava, sammatta ca te niyata ca anantarameva phaladanena. *Aniyata* ti ubhayathapi na niyata.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika *micchattaniyata* padaM visesayati: matughatadisu pavattamanapi te dhamma hitasukhaM icchanta pavattanti, tasma asisita; asubhadivipariyasadalhata anantariyakammaniyatamicchidittthisu pavatti hoti, tasma viparitappavattika; *micchasabhava* iti *musasabhava*. Anekesu anantariyesu katesu balavantatamo vipaccati - na ca ekantavipakajanakata sabbasu niyata - tasma *vipakadane sati* iti aha.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-113)

Codakassa saddhammaM "ubhayathapi na niyata" tiadinaa vivaranena mulatika anantariyakanam phalakalaaniyameneva niyatataM sadheti: asamanajatikena cetopaenidhivasena upaghataakena ca phalanivattetuM na sakka, aviciphalatta tesaM. Samanasabhavesu ekena vipake dinne itarani dutiyaM tatiyampi patisandhiM na karonti, na samatthatavighatatta.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-114)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-115)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika *vipariyasadalhataya* ti vacanaM uddharati: lokiyasadhujanehipi atijigucchaniiyesu aanantariyakammanatthikaavadadisu pavatti vina vipallasabalavabhavena na hotiti. *Vipakadane sati* tiaadina satipi kalaniyame sasankavacanena yathavuttanayena niyatataya atippasango dunnivaro. Balavataa aanantariyena asamanajatikena anivattaniyavipakataM, samanajatikena ca anantaraM vipakuppaaadanasamatthataya avihantabbataM anantariyakanam dasseti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-132)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-133)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tini pakkharanim imamattaM anumodanti: (1) *micchattaniyata dhamma* ti musasabhava asisitaviparita anantariyakammani ca micchaditthiyo ca, ye khandhabhedanantarameva phalanti -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-113); (2) *sammattaniyata dhamma* ti sammasabhava anantarameva phaladayaka -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-115); (3) *aniyata dhamma* ti ubhayathapi na niyata -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534); (4) anantariyakanam niyatata phalakaalaniyameneva, na phaladaananiyameneva -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-113) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-132).

## Divergences

- **niyatataya vinicchayo** -- atthakatha *vipakadane sati khandhabhedanantarameva* iti ekantavacanena niyatataM sadheti; mulatika *ekantavipakajanakata* iti na sabbasu niyata, balavantatamassa vipaccanaM na itaraanaM, iti sasankavacanena phalakaalaniyameneva niyatataM vibhajati.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-113)

- **aniyatanam anivattaniyata** -- mulatika asamanajatikena cetopaenidhivasena anantariyakanam phalaM nivattetuM na sakka iti "naapajjati" tiadinaa sadheti; anutika codako adhippayaM ajananto atippasangameva codeti, itaro attano adhippayaM vivaranena avikaroti iti bhumikaaM dipeti.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-114)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-133)

*Aniyata dhamma* (NOM.PL.M; *aniyata* = ubhayathapi na niyata -- atthakatha (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-534); na micchattaniyata na sammattaniyata, vipakappavattiyaM appaniyata).
