## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Ajjhattattike atthakatha *ajjhatta*-saddassa caturo atthe niddisati — gocarajjhatta, niyakajjhatta, ajjhattajjhatta, visayajjhatta —; idhādhippetattho pana niyakajjhatto yeva: attano santāne pavattā pāṭipuggalikā dhammā *ajjhattā* nāma. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) Tato bāhirabhutā dhammā, indriyabaddhā vā anindriyabaddhā vā, *bahiddhā* nāma. Tatiyapadaṃ *ajjhattabahiddhā* tadubhayavasena vuttaṃ — ubhinnaṃ saṅgahaṃ karoti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) *Evaṃ pavattamānā mayaṃ attā*ti gahaṇaṃ, attānaṃ adhikāraṃ katvā pavattanti *ajjhattā*ti vacanatthassa mūlabhāvo dīpito. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatīkā *ajjhatta*-saddassa vacanatthaṃ vibhajati: *adhi* + *atta* ti samāsavisaye adhikāratthaJca pavattiyatthañca gahetvā pavattati — *attānaṃ adhi* ajjhattāti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-120) *Evaṃ cakkhādibhāvena phusanaādibhāvena ca ekasantatipariyāpannatāvasena pavattamānā* ti atthakathāvacanassa attho vibhāvīyate — ajjhattā dhammā sakasantānaparicchinnatāvasena vuttā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-120) Ajjhatta-saddassa ajjhattika-saddena asamānatā ca saddato atthato ca dīpitā: *ajjhattika-saddo* saparasantānikesupi cakkhādīsu pavattati, *ajjhatta-saddo* pana sakasantānikesveva pavattati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-121) Visayajjhattassa atthassa ca mulatīkā *issariyaṭhāna*-bhāvaṃ phalasamāpattiyā sambandhanena vibhāveti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-122)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutīkā atthakathāya *ajjhattasuttaādīsu cittaṃ na pakkhanda* ti-ādi-vacanassa heṭhāpāḷiṃ sākhīkaraṇatthāya pubbapadāni vibhajati — jhānā vuṭhahitvā suññataṃ ajjhattaṃ bahiddhā ajjhattabahiddhā ca manasikaroto cittaṃ na pakkhandatiti vuttassa yathāpāḷivinayena dīpanaṃ karoti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-136) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-137) Tattha *ajjhattasuttaādīsu* ti ajjhattaṃ bahiddhā ajjhattabahiddhā ca suññatāya āneñje ca; *paṭhamajjhānādisamaadhinimitte* ti pādakabhūtapaṭhamajjhānādisamadhinimitte. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-138) Ajjhattajjhattavācakassa saddassa ca tatthattho sakasantānaparicchinnatāto — tena ajjhattikasaddassapi samānatthbhāvo dīpīyate. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-139) Visayajjhattassa atthassa ca pāḷi mahāsaccakasuttato (ma. ni. 1.387) sākhīkaritā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-140) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-141) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-142)

## Consensus

Sabbāpi tīṇi pakkharanaāni (atthakathā, mulatīkā, anutīkā) imasmiṃ ajjhattattike imaṃ sammatiṃ dhārenti: (1) *ajjhatta*-saddassa idhādhippeto attho *niyakajjhatto* — attano santāne pavattā pāṭipuggalikā dhammā ajjhattā ti; (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-120) (2) *bahiddhā* dhammā tatobāhirabhutā, indriyabaddhā vā anindriyabaddhā vā; (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) (3) *ajjhattabahiddhā* tatiyapadaṃ tadubhayasaṅgahavasena vuttaṃ; (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) (4) *adhi*-saddo samāsaviṭhāne attānaṃ adhikāratthaṃ dīpeti, tasmā sakasantānapariyāpannatā ajjhattabhāvassa lakkhanaṃ. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-120) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-139)

## Divergences ⨑

- **Ajjhatta-saddo caturattho** — Atthakathā ajjhatta-saddassa caturo atthe (gocarajjhatta, niyakajjhatta, ajjhattajjhatta, visayajjhatta) vitthārena nidassati, idhādhippeto niyakajjhatto ti niddhāraṇamattameva karoti ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) Mulatīkā pana ajjhattikasaddena ajjhattasaddassa visesaṃ saddato atthato ca vibhajati, saparasantānikesupi ajjhattikasaddo pavattati ajjhattasaddo na ti adhikavibhāgaṃ karoti ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-121) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-139)

- **Visayajjhattassa attho** — Atthakathā visayajjhattaṃ *issariyaṭhāna* ti phalasamāpattiyā sambandheneva nidassati ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-539) Mulatīkā *cittissarā hi buddhā bhagavanto* ti kāraṇaṃ vitthāreti, phalasamāpattiyā issariyaṃ *nibbattaṃ* ti ca vuttaṃ, tassā ca buddhānaṃ *visayatā* saccakasutteneva dīpetabbā ti katvā atthavisesaṃ añjati ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-122) Anutīkā mahāsaccakasuttato pāḷiṃ sākhīkaroti ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-140) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-141)
