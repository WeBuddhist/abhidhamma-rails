## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Sanidassanattike atthakathā tīṇi padāni yathānukkamaṃ vibhajati. *Daṭhabbabhāvasaṅkhātena saha nidassanenāti sanidassanā* — cakkhuviññāṇagocarabhāvo yeva *daṭhabbabhāvo*, tena saha nidassanaṃ yesaṃ atthīti *sanidassanā*. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) *Paṭihananabhāvasaṅkhātena saha paṭighenāti sappaṭighā* — aññamaññapatanaṃ paṭihananabhāvo, tena saha pavattanti *sappaṭighā*. Sanidassanā ca te sappaṭighā cāti *sanidassanasappaṭighā*. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) *Natthi etesaṃ daṭhabbabhāvasaṅkhātaṃ nidassananti anidassanā*, te ca sappaṭighā cāti *anidassanasappaṭighā* dutiyapadaṃ. Tatiyapadaṃ *anidassanaappaṭighā* ubhayapaṭikkhepena vuttaṃ — na nidassanā na sappaṭighā ti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) Tikamātikāpadavaṇṇanā idha niṭhitā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-542)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatīkā atthakathāvacanassa *daṭhabbabhāvo cakkhuviññāṇagocarabhāvo* ti vuttassa vibhāvanatthāya: *yesaṃ daṭhabbabhāvo atthi, te sanidassanā* — cakkhuviññāṇagocarabhāvo yeva daṭhabbabhāvo, aññehi *anidassanehi aññaṃ viya katvā* rūpāyatanassa nidassanabhāvo upacarito. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) *Dhammasabhāvasāmaññena* hi ekībhūtesu dhammesu *yo nānattakaro sabhāvo, so añño viya katvā upacarituṃ yutto* — tena nidassanabhāvo rūpāyatanato anaññopi añño viya katvā vuttaṃ. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) Paṭihananabhāvassa ca lakkhaṇaṃ: *sayaṃ ca nissayavasena ca sampattānaṃ asampattānañca paṭimukhabhāvo aññamaññapatanaṃ* — yena byāpārādivikārapaccayantarasahitesu cakkhādīnaṃ visayesu vikāruppatti hotīti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutīkā mulatīkāya vuttaṃ *aññehi anidassanehi aññaṃ viya katvā* ti vacanaṃ vibhājeti: *silāputtakassa sarīra* nti viya upamāvasena — dhammasabhāvasāmaññena ekībhūtesūpi rūpāyatanato viseso nidassanabhāvena kato, tadaññadhammavisesakaro nidassanabhāvo rūpāyatanato anaññopi añño viya katvā upacarito ti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143) *Atthaviseso sāmaññavisesatthabhedo* ti — sāmaññena ekībhūtesveva atthavisesena nānattaṃ dasseti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143) Paṭihananabhāvassa ca lakkhaṇaṃ vitthārīkataṃ: *byāpārādīti cittakiriyāvāyodhātuvipphāravasena akkhipaṭalādīnaṃ heṭhā upari ca saṃsīdanalaṅghanādippavatti*; *vikāruppatti visadisuppatti, visayassa iṭhāniṭhabhāvena anuggaho upaghāto cā* ti attho. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143)

## Consensus

Sabbāpi tīṇi pakkharaṇāni imasmiṃ sanidassanattike imaṃ sammatiṃ dhārenti: (1) *sanidassana*-saddo *daṭhabbabhāva* = *cakkhuviññāṇagocarabhāva* ti lakkhaṇena niddiṭhho, rūpāyatanaṃ yeva sanidassanasappaṭighaṃ; (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) (2) *sappaṭigha*-saddo *paṭihananabhāva* = *aññamaññapatana* ti lakkhaṇena niddiṭhho; (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143) (3) tatiyapadaṃ *anidassanaappaṭighā* ubhayapaṭikkhepena vuttaṃ — na nidassanā na sappaṭighā ti; (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) (4) nidassanabhāvo rūpāyatanassa yeva visesalakkhaṇaṃ, aññehi dhammehi visesetuṃ añño viya katvā upacarito. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143)

## Divergences ⨑

- **Nidassanabhāvassa upacaranakāraṇaṃ** — Atthakathā *daṭhabbabhāvasaṅkhātena saha nidassanenāti sanidassanā* ti vacanatthassa mūlaṃ dīpeti; hetu-vibhāgaṃ vitthāreti nāti saṅkhittamattameva ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) Mulatīkā *dhammasabhāvasāmaññena ekībhūtesu dhammesu yo nānattakaro sabhāvo, so añño viya katvā upacarituṃ yutto* ti hetu-vibhāgaṃ vitthāreti ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) Anutīkā *silāputtakassa sarīra* nti dīpanī upamā-vasena upacaranakāraṇaṃ iddhikaṃ karoti ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143)

- **Paṭihananabhāvassa vitthāro** — Atthakathā *paṭihananabhāva = aññamaññapatana* ti saṅkhittamattameva niddisati ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-541) Mulatīkā *sayaṃ sampattānaṃ asampattānañca paṭimukhabhāvo* ti vibhajati ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-123) Anutīkā *cittakiriyāvāyodhātuvipphāravasena akkhipaṭalādīnaṃ saṃsīdanalaṅghanādippavatti* ti vitthatataraṃ vibhajati, *visadisuppatti iṭhāniṭhabhāvena anuggaho upaghāto cā* ti ca yojeti ⨑. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-143)
