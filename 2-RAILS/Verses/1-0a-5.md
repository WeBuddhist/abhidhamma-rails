## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Aṭṭhakathā *saṃkilesa*-saddaṃ „vibādhati, upatāpetī’ti dvīhi kiriyāpadehi vyākaroti: saṃkileso nāma yo dhamme vibādhati upatāpeti cāti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) *Saṃkiliṭṭhā* ti saṃkilasena samannaāgatā — kilesena saha uppannatāya kilena samāyutṭā dhammāti attho. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) *Saṃkilesikā* ti duvidhaṃ byākaroti: (a) attānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattanena saṃkilesaṃ arahantīti, (b) saṃkilese niyuttā taṃ ārammaṇabhāvaṃ nātikkamantiiti — ubhayatthopi kilesassa ārammaṇapaccayabhūtatā dīpitā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) Dvandvasamāso *saṃkiliṭṭha-saṃkilesikā* ti: saṃkiliṭṭhā ca te saṃkilesikā cāti yojanā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) Sesapadadvayaṃ (Kha, Ga) aṭṭhakathā purimattike (kusalattike) vuttanayeneva veditabbanti anudesanāmattameva deti, tato uttari na vitthārayati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mūlaṭīkā *saṃkileso* ti dasa kilesavatthuāni adhippetānīti niyāmeti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101) *Saṃkiliṭṭhā* ti tehi kilesehi vibādhitā upatāpitā ca — saṃkilesasampayogena ekuppādādīhi ninnānattaṃ nāma ekībhāvaṃ gatāti visadīhi viya sappīādayo vidūsitā malīnāti upamāya dīpeti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101) Tasmā aṭṭhakathāvacanaṃ „saṃkilesena samannaāgatā saṃkiliṭṭhā’ti sampayogattham sādhukameva kīlayatīti mūlaṭīkā aṭṭhakathāyaṃ ādhāraṃ karoti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101) *Saṃkilesaṃ arahantī* ti laddhuṃ arahantīti — ārammaṇabhāvena taṃ gaṇhantīti attho, ārammaṇabhāvānatikkamanatoti aṭṭhakathāpadaṃ saṃkilesassa ārammaṇatāṃ anatikkamanameva dasseti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101) *Saṃkilesika*-saddassa pavatti vatthayugikasuṅkasālikasaddānaṃ viya sodapekkhā-vacanatā: yathā suṅkagāhassa sālikā vatthayugikasaddo, evaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattantīnāṃ kilesānaṃ niyojitā saṃkilesikāti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anuṭīkā *saṃkilesa*-saddassa paricayavacanaṃ cha-padena deti: vibādhanaṃ, pīḷanaṃ, kilamanaṃ, upatāpo, parīḷāho, appassaddhidabhāvo — iti saṃkileso aṭṭhakathāya „vibādhati upatāpetī’ti dvīhi vacanehi veditabbaṃ atirittaṃ atthantaraṃ ca pakāseyya. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-119) *Vidūsitā malīnā* ti ca anuṭīkā visesayati: na kevalaṃ kilesasampayuttā dhammā, api ca keci akilesasabhāvāpi aniṭṭhapalā gārayhhā ca jātā honti — tato saṃkilesikā-saddo ārammaṇabhāvena kilesasambaddhatāmeva gacchati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-119)

## Consensus

Sabbāni tīṇi pakkhaṃraṇāni imamattham anumodanti: (1) *saṃkileso* nāma kilesānaṃ vibādhana-upatāpana-kammaṃ, dasa kilesavatthuāsu pavatto (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101); (2) *saṃkiliṭṭhā* ti saṃkilesena samannaāgatā, kilesasampayogena ekuppādādibhāvaṃ gatā dhammā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101); (3) *saṃkilesikā* ti ārammaṇabhāvena saṃkilesaṃ arahanti, taṃ ārammaṇabhāvaṃ nātikkamantiiti (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101); (4) *saṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikā* ti dvandvasamāso, saṃkiliṭṭhā ca te saṃkilesikā cāti yojanā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524); (5) sesapadadvayaṃ (Kha, Ga) purimattike vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524).

## Divergences

- **saṃkilesassa paricchedanā** — Aṭṭhakathā „vibādhati, upatāpetī’ti dvipadamattameva deti ⚔️; Anuṭīkā vibādhanaṃ, pīḷanaṃ, kilamanaṃ, upatāpo, parīḷāho, appassaddhidabhāvo iti chabbidhena saṃkilesaṃ vitthārayati ⚔️.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-119)

- **saṃkilesikā-saddassa pakāsanā** — Aṭṭhakathā „attānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattanena saṃkilesaṃ arahantī’ti, „saṃkilese niyuttā taṃ ārammaṇabhāvaṃ nātikkamantiiti’ ca duvidhaṃ dīpeti ⚔️; Mūlaṭīkā vatthayugikasuṅkasālikasaddānaṃ viya saṃkilesika-saddassa pavattinayaṃ upamāya pakāseti ⚔️.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-524)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-101)
