## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakathā *ācaya* iti padam vibhajati: *kammakilesehi āciyatīti ācayo* — patisandhicutigatippavattānam etam nāmam. *Ācayagāmino* iti dve atthā dīpenti: (1) tassa ācayassa kāranam hutvā nipphādanakabhāvena tam ācayam gacchantīti; (2) yassa puggalassa pavattanti tam ācayam gamentīti — sāsavakusalākusalānam etam adhivachanam. *Apacayo* iti nibbānam adhippeti — ācayasaṅkhātā cayā apetattā apacayo. *Apacayagāmino* ti: tam nibbānam ārammanam katvā pavattanato apacayam gacchantīti — ariyamaggānametam adhivachanam. Atthakathā upamāyapi dīpeti: pākāram itthakavaddhakī viya ācayagāmino, tena citam citam itthakam viddhaṃsayamāno puriso viya apacayagāmino. Tatiyapadam ubhayapatikkhepena vuttam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-529)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatīkā *tam ārammanam katvā*ti idam catukiccasādhanavasena ārammaناkaranam sandhāya vuttanti niyameti — itarathā gotrabhuphalapaccavekkhanādīnampi apacayagāmitā āpajjeyyāti. Atha vā *hetubhāvena apacayam nibbānam gacchantīti apacayagāmino* ti dutiyam atthavikappampi dasseti — nibbānassa samudayapahānasamudayanirodhānam adhigamaadhigantabbabhāvato hetuhetuphalabhāvo magganibbānānam yujjati. Purimā ācayagāmī maggasassampayuttā vuttā, pacchimā maggā kevalā. Purime ācayagāmī viya ariyamaggasīsena sabbalokuttarakusalacittuppādā apacayagāmī gahetabbā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-108)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutīkā *ārammaṇakaraṇam* iti catukiccasādhanam sandhāya vuttanti sādheti: sāmaññajotanāya visese avatthānato ariyamaggadhammānamyeva ca apacayagāmibhāvato; *cattāro maggā apariyāpannā apacayagāmino*ti (dha. sa. 1021) vuttam. *Paccavekkhanādīnanti* vodānādayo saṅganhāti. Hetubhāvenāti *sampāpakahetubhāvena* ti — ñāpako kārako sampāpakoti tividho hi hetu. Yathā lokiyakusalākusalacittuppādā nirayādimanussabhāvādigāmipatipādābhāvato ācayagāmino, na micchāditthi ādimattā, evam lokuttarakusalacittuppādā nibbānagāmipatipādābhāvato apacayagāmino, na ariyamaggadhammā mattā iti vibhāveti. Tattha ariyamaggasseva nibbānagāmipatipādābhāvo paribyatto, tasmā tasseva apacayagāmibhāvo yutto, sesadhammā tadanuvattakatāya saṅgahitā. *Anunāsikalopo* iti *ācaya-saddassa* sānunāsikapadam byattayavasena vuttam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-126)

## Consensus

Sabbāni tīni vyākaranāni (atthakathā, mūlatīkā, anutīkā) imam attham samanujānanti: (1) *ācaya* iti padam *kammakilesehi āciyamānam patisandhicutigatippavattam* sandhāyāha, *ācayagāmino* ti sāsavakusalākusalānam adhivachanam; (2) *apacayo* ti nibbānam adhippeti, *apacayagāmino* ti ariyamaggānam adhivachanam, tam nibbānam catukiccasādhanavasena ārammanam katvā pavattanato; (3) apacayagāmī padena ariyamaggasīsena sabbalokuttarakusalacittuppādā saṅgayhanti, na ariyamaggadhammā eva; (4) tatiyapadam *nevācayagāmināpacayagāmino* ubhayapatikkhepena vuttam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-529)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-108)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-126)

## Divergences ⚑

- **apacayagāmino: ārammaṇakaraṇam vā hetubhāvam** — Atthakathā *tam nibbānam ārammanam katvā pavattanato* iti padhānato katheti ⚑; Mulatīkā *catukiccasādhanavasena ārammaṇakaraṇam* sandhāya vuttanti niyameti, gotrabhupaccavekkhanādīnam asaṅgaham dasseti ⚑; Anutīkā sāmaññajotanāya visese avatthānato ariyamaggadhammānova paribyattato catukiccasādhanādhippāyo iti sakadetam samādhati ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-529)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-108)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-126)
