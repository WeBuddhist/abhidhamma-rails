## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Aṭṭhakathā parittārammaṇattikassa tīṇi padāni ekasūtrena vyākhyāti: *parittārammaṇā dhammā* nāma te dhammā yesaṃ parittaṃ ārammaṇaṃ; parittaṃ ārammaṇaṃ etesanti parittārammaṇā — bahubbīhisamāso, parittaṭṭhāne yathoktaparittadhammā ārammaṇaṃ. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-532) Sesapadadvayepi (mahaggatārammaṇā, appamāṇārammaṇā) eseva nayo — mahaggatā ārammaṇaṃ etesanti mahaggatārammaṇā; appamāṇā ārammaṇaṃ etesanti appamāṇārammaṇā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-532)

## Consensus

Ekameva pakkharaṇaṃ (aṭṭhakathā) imaṃ tikkaṃ nissāya pavattati. Aṭṭhakathā tīni padāni bahubbīhisamāsena vyākhyāti: parittaṃ ārammaṇaṃ etesanti *parittārammaṇā*, mahaggatā ārammaṇaṃ etesanti *mahaggatārammaṇā*, appamāṇā ārammaṇaṃ etesanti *appamāṇārammaṇā*; sesapadadvayepi eseva nayo. Ārammaṇaviseso parittārammaṇattikassa pavibhāgahetuṃ karoti — ārammaṇaṃ katamesaṃ parittaṃ mahaggatādi, tato yathāvuttanāmakaraṇaṃ. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-532)
