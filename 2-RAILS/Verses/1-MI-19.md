## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha atitirammanatikassa attham evamdasseti: *atitirammanatika dhamma* nama ye dhamma atitam arammanam etesam — tesam arammanam atite pavattam; "atita-arammana"ti tappurisasamaso, arammana-kalaviseso eva padabhedassa hetu. Sesapadadvayepi — *anagatarammanani*, *paccuppannarammanani* — eseva nayo pavattati: ye dhamma annagatam arammanam etesam te "anagatarammanani"; ye dhamma paccuppannam arammanam etesam te "paccuppannarammanani". Sabbapadesu arammanam-kalaviseso eva paticchedo, dhammanam arammanavijjamana-visayatta.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-538)

## Consensus

Ekameva tikaranam (atthakatha) imasmim tike sajjhayam karoti: *atitirammanani/anagatarammanani/paccuppannarammanani dhamma* nama arammanassa kalavisesena vibhatta — atite anagata paccuppanne ca arammane etesam iti tappurisasamasena gahita dhamma. Sesapadadvayepi ekameva nayam anugacchanti, arammanakalam eva bhedo.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-538)
