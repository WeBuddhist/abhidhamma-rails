## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha *dassanena* iti padam *sotapattimaggena* iti niyameti — so hi pathamam nibbānam passatīti ''dassanan''ti vuccati. Gotrabhucittam kiñcāpi pubbam nibbānam passati, tathāpi kattabbakiccassa kilesappahanassa akatatta dassananti na vuccati; tassa nanam maggassa avajjanattane titthatīti. *Bhāvanāya* iti padam *sesamaggattayena* iti niyameti — dutiya-tatiya-catuttha-magga pathamam aggena ditthasmiṃyeva nibbānadhamme bhāvanāvasena uppajjanti, aditthapobbam kiñci na passantīti bhāvanāti vuccanti. *Neva dassanena na bhāvanāya* iti tatiyapadam ubhayapatikkhepavasena vuttam — purimadvinnampi pahānanam patikkhepavasena sesadhammā gahitāti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-527)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika *nibbānam dassanato* iti atthakathāvacana nibbānārammanatam sandhāya vuttam, na nibbānapatavijjhanam sandhāyāti vibhāveti. Dhammacakkhu punappunam nibbattanena bhāvanābhāvam appattam dassanam nāmāti gotrabhussa dassanabhāvāpatti na yujjatīti dīpeti. *Ubhayapatikkhepavasenā* iti padassa attham vibhajati: tatiyapadena dvīhi padehi vuttadhammapatikkhepavasena pahāyakapatikkhepavasena ca sesadhammā gahitā; añño samucchedavasena pahāyako nāma natthi, tasmā appahātabbā eva gahitā, na pahātabbā aññena kenacīti. Nanasāmaggiyā sammāpatapādāya pahātabbā vikkhambhitā cāti aññehi vikkhambhitesupi punappavattisambhavā, tasmā te tatiyarāsimhi asaṅgayhantīti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-105)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika atthakathāvacanaṃ *nibbānārammanatam sandhāyāhā* ti anugganhāti — nibbānapatavijjhanam sandhāya na vuttam, itarathā gotrabhussa dassanabhāvāpatti acodanīyā siyāti adhippāyam vibhāveti. Sotāpattimaggova ukkamsagativijānanena nibbānapatavijjhanam dassananti; gotrabhucittam tādisammadassanam nāpajjatīti. Tatiyamaggādīnampi dhammacakkhupariyāyasabbhāvato *bhāvanābhāvam appatta*nti vuttam — bhāvanā vaddhana, sā bahulaṃ uppattiyā hotīti punappunam nibbattanena dassitā. *Neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbā* iti tatiyapadassa niyojanam: lokiyasamathavipassanā yathābalam kāmacchandādīnam vikkhambhanavasena pahāyakā, tathāpi te samucchedavasena pahātabbānam āpattiyā tatiyarāsimhi asaṅgayhantīti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-124)

## Consensus

Sabbāni tīni vyākaranāni (atthakathā, mūlatīkā, anutīkā) imam attham samanujānanti: (1) *dassanena* iti padam sotāpattimaggam eva adhippeti, gotrabhucittam nibbānam passatīti vuttakālepi dassanabhāvam nāpajjatīti; (2) *bhāvanāya* iti padam sesam maggattayam (dutiya-tatiya-catuttha-maggā) sandhāyāha, te pathamam aggena dittheyeva nibbāne bhāvanāvasena pavattantīti; (3) *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbā* iti tatiyapadam ubhayapatikkhepavasena vuttam — dassanabhāvanāhi appahātabbā eva te dhammā, na aññena pahāyakena pahātabbā ca, na samucchedavasena pahātabbāti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-527)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-105)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-124)

## Divergences ⚑

- **gotrabhucittassa dassanabhāvāpatti** — Atthakathā kattabbakiccassa kilesappahanassābhāvā gotrabhucittam dassananti na vuccatīti niddesamattam karoti ⚑; Mūlatīkā imam dhammacakkhūpattibhāvova nādhigato, bhāvanābhāvo ca appatto, tasmā dassanabhāvo na sijjhatīti saddāyuttim ca atthāyuttiñca vibhājeti ⚑; Anutīkā ukkamsagativijānanena nibbānapatavijjhanasallakkhanavasena eva dassananti niyameti ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-527)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-105)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-124)

- **tatiyapadassa gahanānayo** — Atthakathā *ubhayapatikkhepavasena* iti sāmañño niddeso karoti ⚑; Mūlatīkā pahātabbapadam patisedhavasena na yojetvā dassanabhāvanāpādehi yuttena pahātabbahetukapadena visum yojanato sesadhammā *appahātabbā eva* iti visesam dīpeti ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-527)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-105)
