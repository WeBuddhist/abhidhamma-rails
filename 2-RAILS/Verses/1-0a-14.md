## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Aṭṭhakathā hīnattikaM evaṃ vibhajati: *hīnā* ti lāmakā, yo ca te akusalā dhammā; *hīnappaṇītānaṃ majjhe bhavā* ti majjhimā; *avasetā tebhūmakā dhammā uttamaṭṭhena atappakaṭṭhena ca paṇītā, lokuttaraā dhammā* ti paṇītā.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533)

Iti hīna-saddo lāmakabhāvaṃ, paṇīta-saddo uttamaṭṭhena atappakaṭṭhena lokuttaravisesāṃ dīpeti; majjhimā-padaṃ ubhinnaṃ antarabhāvato nibbattaṃ.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mūlaṭīkā *atappakaṭṭhena* iti vacanaṃ vibhajati: lokuttaradhammā divasampi paccavekkhiyāmānā tittiṃ na janenti; samāpajjiyāmānāpi phaladhammā tittijanakā na honti. Tasmā *atappakaṭṭhena* iti aticchaparikkhayarahitatāya paṇītatāyeva lakkhanaṃ dīpeti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-112)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anuṭīkā mūlaṭīkāvacanaṃ santatatatāya asecanakabhāvato tittiṃ na janentīti uddharati. Ettha ca *pamāṇakarehi* tiādi atthavikappo *atappakaṭṭhena* tiādikaya hīnattikapadavaṇṇanāya parato bahūsu potthakesu likhīyati yathāṭhane ānetvā vattabbo ti pasāretvā dīpeti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-131)

## Consensus

Sabbāpi tīṇi pakkharaniṃ imamattaṃ anumodanti: (1) *hīnā dhammā* ti akusalā lāmakasabhāvā dhammā; (2) *majjhimā dhammā* ti hīnappaṇītānaṃ antarabhāvino dhammā; (3) *paṇītā dhammā* ti tebhūmakānaṃ avasetā uttamaṭṭhena atappakaṭṭhena ca lokuttarā dhammā — (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533); (4) lokuttaradhammānaṃ paṇītatāyā lakkhaṇaṃ *atappakaṭṭha* iti, yaṃ divasampi paccavekkhiyāmānā tittiṃ na janentīti — (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-112); (5) asecanakabhāvato santatatatāya ca tesaṃ tittirahitatāyeva ca nibbiseso anumodiyo — (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-131).

## Divergences ⚡

- **paṇītānaṃ lakkhaṇaṃ** — aṭṭhakathā *uttamaṭṭhena atappakaṭṭhena* iti ubhayaṭṭhena paṇītatāṃ dīpeti, lokuttaradhammā tebhūmakānaṃ avasetā ca paṇītāti ⚡; mūlaṭīkā *atappakaṭṭhena* ti phaladhammānaṃ tittirahitatāmeva pradhānaṃ karoti, pamāṇakarehitiādiṃ atirekayati ⚡.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-112)

## Disambiguated Restatement (original language)

*Hīnā dhammā* (NOM.PL.M; *hīnā* = lāmakā, sc. akusalā — aṭṭhakathā “lāmakā akusalā dhammā” (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533); *dhammā* sabhāvadhāraṇādinā atthena).

*Majjhimā dhammā* (NOM.PL.M; *majjhimā* = hīnappaṇītānaṃ majjhe bhavā — aṭṭhakathā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533); antarabhāvavacanaṃ, na parimāṇavacanaṃ).

*Paṇītā dhammā* (NOM.PL.M; *paṇītā* = avasetā tebhūmakā dhammā uttamaṭṭhena atappakaṭṭhena ca, lokuttarā ca — aṭṭhakathā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-533); *atappakaṭṭhena* iti phaladhammānaṃ divasampi tittirahitatā ⚡ — mūlaṭīkā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-112); asecanakabhāvato santatatatāyā ca — anuṭīkā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-131)).
