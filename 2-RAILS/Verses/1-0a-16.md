## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha maggaarammanattikaM evaM vibhajati: *maggo* ti nibbanaM maggati gavesati, kilese va marento gacchatiti; *maggaarammanaa* ti maggo aarammanam etesanti -- sc. nibbanaM aarammanam yesaM te. *Maggahetuka* ti atthangikopi maggo paccayatthena etesaM hetuti; maggasampayutta va hetu magge va hetuti maggahetu; te etesaM hetutipI maggahetuka. SammaaditThhi sayaM maggo ceva hetu ca, iti maggo hetu etesanti maggahetuka. *Maggaadhipatino* ti abhibhavitvaa pavattanatthena maggo adhipati etesanti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika *magga* ti paripunnnamaggakiccatta cattaaro ariyamaggaava idha vuttaati dipeti. *Maggahetuka* padassa tayo nayaa vibhajati: nikkhepakamdepaaliyaa ye maggapaccayaM labhanti te maggahetuka (pathamo nayo); *maggasampayutta va hetu* ti ariyamaggasamangissa alobhadayo maggahetu (dutiyo nayo); *sammadittthi sayaM maggo ceva hetu caa* ti tatiyasangaho. Hetubahutaavasena tatiyo nayo idha dutiyo vutto. Iti asangahitasanganavanasena patipatiya tayo naya vuttaa.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-116)

Maggaadhipatino padaM mulatika vibhajati: sahajaataadhipati pubbaabhisankaaravasena jeththakabhaave pavattamaano sahajaate attano vase anuvattayamaano abhibhavitvaa pavattati; aarammanaddhipati tadaarammmane dhamme tatheva anuvattayamaano abhibhavitvaa aarammanabhaavena pavattati, na paccuppanabhaavena. Tasmaa adhipatidvayampi *maggaadhipatino* sangahitaM. Paaliyaami *maggo adhipati maggaadhipatii* ti saruupekasesavasena samaanasaddatthavasena vaa sangahitoti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-117)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika *maggakiccaM parinnadi* ti uddharitvaa atthangikammaggasammaadiTThimaggasampayuttaalobhaaadosaasankhaatehi maggahetuhii maggasampayuttakhandhasesaamaggangassammaaditThinam sahetukabhaavadassanato tinnnaM nayaanaM asangahitasanganahanavasenati aha. *Hetubahutaavasena* ti bahuhetukassa pathamanayassa anantaraM bahuhetukataasamannena nikkhepaakandapaliyaM tatiyaM vuttanayo idha dutiyaM vutto. *Pubbaabhisankaaravasena* ti pavattiviseso hi purimapurimaanaM cittacetasikaanaM uttaruttaresu visesaadhaanam bhaavanaapubbaabhisankaaroti. Patthaanavacana (pattha. 2.16.11) *maggo adhipati maggaadhipatii* ti saruupekasesavasena samaanasaddatthavasena saaddheti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-134)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tiini pakkharaniM imamattaM anumodanti: (1) *maggo* idha = cattaaro ariyamaggaa, paripunnamaggakiccatta -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-116); (2) *maggaarammanaa* ti nibbanaM aarammanam etesaM dhammaanaM -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535); (3) *maggahetuka* ti tayo nayaa -- maggapaccayalabino, maggasampayuttaalobhaadayo, sammadiththi sayaM maggo ca hetu ca -- asangahitasanganahanaavasena vuttaa -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-116) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-134); (4) *maggaadhipatino* ti sahajaataadhipati ca aarammanaddhipati ca sangahitaa, abhibhavitvaa pavattanatthena -- (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-117).

## Divergences

- **maggahetukaanam nayakamo** -- atthakatha tayo naye patipatiya vatteti, tatiyaM nayaM (sammadiththi sayaM maggo ca hetu ca) antimo aharati; mulatika hetubahutaavasena tatiyaM nayaM idha dutiyaM vuttanti upanissayaM dipeti, nikkhepaakandapaliyaM vuttanayabhumikaaM vibhajati.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-116)

- **maggaadhipatinam sangaho** -- atthakatha *abhibhavitvaa pavattanatthena* iti saamanno attho dasseti, nikkhepaakande udaaharanavaaasena aagataM atthanayaM gahetvaaa; mulatika sahajaataadhipati-aarammanaddhipatiubhayaM sangahitaM, paccuppanabhaavena na aarammanaddhipativacananti visesaM dipeti, patthaanaM uddharitvaa saruupekasesavasena samaanasaddatthavasena vaa ti dvihi nayehi sadheti.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-117)

## Disambiguated Restatement (original language)

*Maggaarammanaa dhammaa* (NOM.PL.M; *magga* = ariyamaggo nibbanaM maggamaano -- atthakatha (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-535); *aarammanaa* = aarammanam etesaM iti bahubbihi, sc. nibbanaM aarammanam yesaM dhammaanaM te).

*Maggahetuka dhammaa* (NOM.PL.M; *maggahetuka* tayo nayehi: (i) maggapaccayaM labhantaa; (ii) maggasampayuttaalobhaadayo hetuu; (iii) sammadiththi sayaM maggo ca hetu ca -- anutika asangahitasanganahanavasena (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-134); nayakame divergence -- mulatika (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-116)).
