## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha imesam tinnam padanam atthanam vibhagam evam dasseti: *uppanna dhamma* nama uppadato patthaya yava bhanga uddham panna gata pavatta, te "uppanna"ti vuccanti — uppado ca pavattanca imasmim pade sangahitam. *Anuppanna dhamma* nama na uppanna; atita ca anagata ca imasmim pade sangahita. *Upadino dhamma* nama pariniththitakaranekadesatta — karanassa ekadeso nibbattito — avassam uppajjissantiti lakkhanena niyutta; te neva uppanna neva anuppanna, kincapi anagata, na pana "anuppanna"ti padena sangahita, yato tesam uppado niyato.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-536)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatiika atthakathayam "anuppanna"ti padassa atthavibhagam nipunataram vibhajati. "Anuppanna"ti etena sabbo uppannabhavo patisiddho, na kevalam uppannadhammabhavo patisiddho — tena atitipi anuppannapadena sangahitabbam siya. Yadi atita "anuppanna"ti pade sangahita siyum, tarahi "anuppanno dhammo uppannassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo"ti suttantidesana na yujjeyya — sa ca vuttatta atita anuppannapada bahiddha thapetabba. Upadinoti: anagatavipaka-katattarupanam karanassa ekadeso atite anagate va purima-nipphannattha te avassam uppajjissanti — imina nayena "upadino"ti sammatam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-118)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika "uppanna-saddo" duvidhesu atthesu pavattati iti vibhaveti: (1) uppadaadim patipajjamana dhamma (uppada-tthiti-bhangappatta); (2) uppadaadim patva vigata dhamma — iti ubhayasmimpi pavattati. Tena yadi purimattho eva gahiyeyya, atita sangahita na honti. Tamattham dassetu "anuppanna"tiadimaha — imina anuppannapadena atitipi uppannabhavena sangahita honti, na kevalam paccuppanna. "Sabbo uppannabhavo"ti vacanam etam dipeti: uppannadhammabhavo vuttamanadhammabhavamattam, tena atita na sangahita; atitipi hi uppannabhavena (uppadaadippattiya) sangayhanti. Upadino dhamme pana anutika "phalanibbattito karanassa puretaram nibbatti pariniththitasaddena vuccati, na tassa hutva vigatabhavo"ti suceti — yato Metteyyassa uppajjanaka-phalam pi "upadino"ti vuccati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-135)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tini tikaranani (atthakatha, mulatiika, anutika) imam attham sadharanam karonti: (1) *uppanna dhamma* nama uppadato yava bhanga pavattamana dhamma — te paccuppanna; (2) *upadino dhamma* nama pariniththitakaranekadesattha avassam uppajjissanti — te niyata-anagata, na pana anuppannapadena sangahita; (3) *anuppanna dhamma* nama upadino bahira — ekacco anagato ca tassa sangahavisayo, mulatiikato atita pi bahiddha; (4) pariniththita-saddo imasmim thanhe karanassa phalanibbattipubbabhaga-siddhim dipeti, na tassa hutva vigatabhavam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-536)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-118)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-135)

## Divergences

- **anuppanna-padassa ussaro (scope of anuppanna)** — Atthakatha "na uppanna"ti samannho niddeso mattam va karoti, atitinam sangahavisayam na sphutam dasseti; Mulatiika atita anuppannapada bahiddha thapetabba ti suttantidesanaya yuktivasena sadheti — "sabbo uppannabhavo patisiddho, na uppannadhammabhavomattam"ti viseseti; Anutika pana viparitam "uppannabhavena atitipi sangahita"ti vadati, uppannapadassa duvidhatthatta.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-536)
