## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

*Upadinna*-saddo: arammanaranavasena tanhaaditthiihi upetena kammuna aadinno, phalabhavena gahiitoti upadinno. *Upadaniya*-saddo: aarammanabhaavam upagantvaa upaadaanasambhandhena upaadaananam hitaati upaadaaniya -- upaadanassa aarammanapaccayabhuutaanam etam adhivacananam. *Upadinnupadaniya*-samaaso: upadinno ca te upadaaniya caati upadinnupadaniya -- saasavakammanibbattaanam rupaarupa-dhammaanametam adhivacananam. Iti imina nayena sesapadadvayempi patisedhasahito attho veditabbo.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-523)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika *upadinna*-saddassa attham anupubbato vibhajati. *Upeta*-saddassa sambandham: catubbidhaupaadaanabhuutaahi tanhaaditthiihi upetam, tehi ca aarammanaranavasena upetam, na samannagamavasena. *Adinno*-saddassa yojanam: sabbatebhuumakadhammanam upaadanaarammanattte yehi vipaakakatattaaruupani amehi nibbattattaa amehaakam etaani phalaaaniti ganhantehi viya aadinnaani, taani tebhuumakakammaani kammabhaavena ekattam upanetvaa upetanti idha gahitaani. Tena ca nibbattaani vipaakakatattruuruupani upadinnaa dhammati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-99)
Aparo attho: *upa*-saddo upetam diipeti -- upagamanam sasaadhanaum vadati; upagamananca upaadaanupayo, tena upagatam upetam. Yamaasati upaadaane na hoti, tam "upaadaanapaccayaa bhavo"ti evam vuttam tebhuumakakammam paccayabhaavena purimajaaatuppannena upaadaanena upagatattaa "upeta"nti vuccati. Tena upetena kammuna punabbhavassa aaadaanam hoti -- "kammuna hi saasavena sattaa aaadiyanti punabbhavam". So ca upapattibhavo tebhuumakavipaakakatattaarupasangaho "bhavapaccayaa jaati"ti ettha jatiivacane samavaruddho iti upaadinnavacanena upapattibhavo vuccati. *Upadaaniya*-saddassa attho: aarammanabhaavam upagantvaa upaadaanena upaadatabbati vaa upaadaaniye niyuttaati vaa upaadaniya -- upaadannaaanupaadinnabhedena duvidham.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-100)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika mulatikay atthavibhavanam anupubbato vibhajati. *Phalabhavenaa*-ti iti aaadaanappakaaravacananam. *Upetasaddasambandhi*na upaya-saddena vuccamanaahi catubbidhaupadaanabhuutaahi tanhaaditthiihi upetam, tehi ca aarammanaranavasena upetam iti. *Upetanti hi upetabbatthe vuccamaane avassam upetakiriyaa upetaa ca naayatiti*. "Ruupadhaatuyaa kho pana, gahapati, yo chando yo raago ya nandii yaa tanhaa ye upayupaadaanaa, cetaso adhitthaanaa abhinivesaa anusayaa"tiaadiisu "upayo"ti tanhaadittthiyo adhippetaati aaha "upaya...pe...ditthiihii"ti. *Yathaasambhavanti* aarammanaranavasenaatiaadinaa atthakathayam vuttaatthesu yo yo sambhavati yojetum, so soti attho.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-117)
*Upetam diipetii*ti yathaa "pacariyo"ti ettha pagato acariyo pacariyoti pa-saddo pagatam diipeti, evam upa-saddo upetam diipeti eva. *Paccayabhaavena*ti etena purimanibbattim viseseti -- tena sahajataassapi upaadaanassa sangaho kato hoti. *Dhaatukathaayam pakaasita*nti "upapattibhavo pancahi khandhehi ekaadassaayyatanehi sattarasahi dhatuuhi sangahito"ti evamaadinam sandhayaaha. *Avisesetva*ti upadinnaanupaadinnaavisesam akatva. *Upadaananam aarammanabhaavanativattanato upaadaanehi upaadatabbati vaa upaadaniye niyuttati vaa ka-kaarassa ya-kaaram katvaa*ti evamettha attho datthabbo.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-118)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tini pakkharanani (atthakatha, mulatika, anutika) imamattam anumodanti: (1) *upadinna*-saddo aarammanaranavasena tanhaaditthiihi upetena kammuna gahitatam sandahehi -- saasavakammanibbattaanam ruupaaruupadhammanam adhivacananam; (2) *upadaniya*-saddo aarammanabhaavam upagantvaa upaadaanena upaadaatabbataayam pavattati -- upaadaanassa aarammanapaccayabhuutaanam adhivacananam; (3) *upadinnupaadaaniya*-samaaso kammadharayasaamaasanena "upaadinnaa ca te upaadaaniya caati" ti attham darasseti; (4) sesapadadvayam patisedhasahitam: *anupaadinnupaadaaniya* upaadaanena na gahitaa yeteva upaadaaniyaa; *anupaadinnanupaadaniya* ubhayabhaavanapi patikkhittaa.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-523)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-99)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-100)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-117)

## Divergences

- **upadinna -- atthavinicchayo: aarammanaranavasena va purimajatuppannena upaadaanena upagatakammavasena** -- Atthakatha "aarammanaranavasena tanhaaditthiihi upetena kammuna aadinno"ti aarammanaranavasena tanha-ditthiihi upatam kammam pathamaato niddisati; Mulatika pana aparaatthayojanaya "upa-saddo upetam diipeti -- yamaasati upaadaane na hoti, tam tebhuumakakammam paccayabhaavena purimajaaatuppannena upaadaanena upagatattaa upetan"ti -- purimajatuppannena upaadaanena kammassa upagatattam pathamaato dasseti.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-523)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-100)

- **upadinna -- sabbapaccayuppannanam upaadinnattapatti** -- Mulatika "sabbapaccayuppannanam upaadinnattam aapajjatiiti? Naapajjati bodhaneyyajjhaasayavasena desanaapavatitto"ti upadinnavacananam niyamitaatthassa paricchedanam vibhajati; Anutika pana "naaapajjati saamaannaajotanaya visese avatthaanato visesatthinaa ca viseso anupayujjatiiti tam pana visesam vuttappakaaram niyametva dassetum bodhaneyya"tiaadi vuttanti -- anutika saamaannavuttito visessaparichedo imameva lakkhanam sandahati.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-99)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-117)
