## Synthesis (original language)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

*Kusalā dhammā* — kusalā nāma anavajjā sukhavipākā ca, te attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentīti dhammā. Kusala-saddo idha ārogya-anavajja-sukhavipāka-tisu eva atthesu pavattati, dhamma-saddo nissattanijjīvatāyameva. *Akusalā dhammā* — akusalā ‘‘kusalapaṭipakkhā’’ti vutto, lakkhaṇato sāvajjadukkhavipākā. *Abyākatā dhammā* — kusalākusalabhāvena akathitā, lakkhaṇato avipākā. Tisu padesu dhamma-saddo sabbesaṃ sabhāvadhāraṇādiatthasāmaññaṃ dīpeti, ādipadāni (kusalā, akusalā, abyākatā) attano attano atthavisesaṃ dassenti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-511)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-514)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-515)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-518)

Vacanattho: kucchite pāpake dhamme salayanti calayanti kampenti viddhaṃsentīti **kusalā**; kucchitena vā ākārena sayantīti kusā, te akusalasaṅkhāte kuse lunantīti kusalā; kusena ñāṇena lātabbāti vā kusalā. Attano pana sabhāvaṃ dhārentīti **dhammā**; dhāriyanti vā paccayehi yathāsabhāvato vā. Mittapaṭipakkhā amittā viya **akusalā** kusalapaṭipakkhā. Na byākatāti **abyākatā**, kusalākusalabhāvena akathitāti attho.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-515)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mūlaṭīkā kusalassa vacanatthe aṭṭhakathāya vacanaṃ vibhajati — salanaṃ, calanaṃ, kampanaṃ, viddhaṃsananti purimapacchimabhāvena vā tadaṅgappahānādīhi yojanavasena vā gahetabbāni. Ñāṇavippayuttānampi kusaluppatti vinā yonisomanasikāraṃ na hoti, tasmā ‘‘kusena lātabbā’’ti attho sabbe kusale anugacchati. Dhammasaddassa ‘‘sabhāvaṃ dhārentī’’ti vacane kappanāsiddhabhedo, paramatthato bhedo nāma natthi. Akusala-saddo kusalābhāvamattavacanaṃ vā tadaññamattavacanaṃ vā na hoti, atha kho kusalapaṭipakkhavacanaṃ. Vi-saddo virodhavacano, ā-saddo abhimukhabhāvappakāsano — byākatā kusalākusalā viruddhā, abyākatā pana viruddhā na honti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-80)

Anavajjasukhavipākalakkhaṇāti ettha anavajjavacanena pavattisukhatā ca attasuddhi ca, sukhavipākavacanena vipākasukhatā ca visuddhavipākatā ca dīpīyante. Sāvajja-dukkhavipāka-lakkhaṇā akusalānaṃ; avipāka-lakkhaṇā abyākatānaṃ.

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anuṭīkā ‘‘anavajjattho avajjavirahattho’’ti vibhāveti. A-kāro na-atthattayasseva jotako nahoti, anekesu atthesu (taṃyoga-, taṃsambandhibhāva-, taṃsabhāva-, taggayhabhāva-, taṃkicca-, tabbhāva-, tabbhāvamatta-, taṃsambhāvanaguṇa-, tadappabhāva-, taṃsadisabhāva-, tadapariyosāna-nivattiyā) pavattati. Pārisesena pana idha akusala-saddo kusalapaṭipakkha-vacanatā eva niyatā, kasmā? Kusalavippayuttādīnaṃ akusalabhāvo na yujjatīti suvidūrabhāvato. Tappaṭipakkhabhāvo viruddhasabhāvattā tadantogadho, tappaheyyabhāvo kusalākusalesu niyato — na hi kusalā akusalehi pahātabbā, kusalāyeva akusale tadaṅgavikkhambhanasamucchedavasena pajahantīti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-72)

### Consensus

Sabbāpi tīṇi pakkharaṇāni (aṭṭhakathā, mūlaṭīkā, anuṭīkā) imamattaṃ anumodanti: (1) *kusalā dhammā* nāma anavajja-sukhavipāka-lakkhaṇāva (blameless and producing pleasant result); (2) *akusalā dhammā* nāma sāvajja-dukkhavipāka-lakkhaṇā, akusala-saddo pana kusala-paṭipakkha-vacanaṃ (designating the opposite), na kusalābhāvamatta-vacanaṃ vā kusalehi tadaññamatta-vacanaṃ vā; (3) *abyākatā dhammā* nāma avipāka-lakkhaṇā, abyākata-saddo na kusalākusalānaṃ avipākatāyāpattiṃ janayati; (4) *dhammā* idha nissattanijjīvatā-vasena (no-being-no-soul sense) sabhāvadhāraṇattho gahito; (5) kusalattikassa tīṇi padāni sappadesato nānatthāni honti yathā loke ‘‘amittā’’ti vacanaṃ.

### Divergences

- **Kusalavacanatthassa pradhānaviṣayo** — Aṭṭhasālinī ‘‘salayanti calayanti kampenti viddhaṃsentī’’ti caturpadenidhārentī kusalānaṃ akusala-vidhāmsana-kammaṃ pradhānaṃ karoti ⚑; Mūlaṭīkā ‘‘kusena ñāṇena lātabbā’’ti yonisomanasikāra-mūlatā-pradhānakaṃ visesayati, ñāṇavippayuttānampi kusalānaṃ ñāṇaṃ upanissayapaccayoti adhikaṃ vadati ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-515)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-80)

## Disambiguated verse (original language)

*Kusalā dhammā* (NOM.PL.M; *kusalā* in the threefold sense of *anavajja-sukhavipāka* — namely blameless, productive of pleasant result, and to be picked-up by the knowledge that defines them as such per the aṭṭhakathā ⚑ and mūlaṭīkā ⚑; *dhammā* in the sense *nissattanijjīva* — "states" excluding the senses "scripture", "cause", and "virtue").

*Akusalā dhammā* (NOM.PL.M; *akusalā* in the sense **opposite of *kusala*** — *kusala-paṭipakkha-vacana* — neither a mere negation nor a mere designation of-something-other, per the unanimous reading of the three commentaries; the a- prefix here is *tappaṭipakkha-nivattiyā* per anuṭīkā ⚑; *dhammā* same sense as above).

*Abyākatā dhammā* (NOM.PL.M; *abyākatā* in the sense ***kusalākusala-bhāvena akathita*** "not declared as either *kusala* or *akusala*" per aṭṭhakathā ⚑ — derivable also as *byā-* "directed in opposition" + negation per the mūlaṭīkā's alternative analysis of vi+ā ⚑; lakkhaṇato *avipāka* "having no fruition"; *dhammā* same sense as above).
