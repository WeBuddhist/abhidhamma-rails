## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha *sekkha dhamma*-ssa atthanam tini nidassanani deti: (1) tisu sikkhasuu jatati sekkha -- silasamadhipannasankhatasu tisu sikkhasuu uppannatta te dhamma sekkha; (2) sattannam sekkhanam ete -- sekkhapuggalanam (catumaggahetthimaphalattaya-vasena sattannam) santaniyati sekkha; (3) apariyositasikkhatta sayameva sikkhantiti sekkha -- sikkhaphalabhavam anadhigatatta sikkha-kiccam karontiti sekkha. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530) *Asekkha dhamma* -- upari sikkhitabbabhavato na sekkha-ti asekkha; vuddhippatta va sekkha-ti asekkha; arahattaphaladhammnam etam adhivacanam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530) *Nevasekkhanasekkha dhamma* -- tatiyapadam ubhayapatikkhepena vuttam, ubhayasmimpi asantatta. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatiika atthakathaya sekkha-vacanattham vibhajati. Satta sekkha sikkhanasiilatiti -- ye dhamma sikkhanti, te sikkhanasila; akkharattho pana sikkha etesam silanti sekkha. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109) *Asekkha*-saddo yattha sekkhabhavaasanka atthi tattheva patisedhaya vuttam; tasma lokiyanibbanesu asekkhabhavanapatti datthabbaa. Silasamadhipannasankhata sikkha parisudha, upakkilesanam arammanabhavampi anupagamanato catumaggahetthimaphalattayadhammesu vijjanti. Arahattaphaladhamma pana parinitthitasikkhakiccatta sikkha-kiccam na karonti, sikkhaphalabhaveneva pavattanti, tasma ta na sikkhavacanam arahanti, arahattaphaladhamma ca asekkhati vuccanti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109) Vuddhippattati atthe: arahattamaggadhamma arahattaphalabhavam apanna kusalasukhato vipakasukham santatarataya panitataranti vuddhippatta asekkhati vuccanti, na tu sekkhadhammesu kesanci vuddhippattanam asekkhataapatti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-110)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutiika mulatikaya "lokiyesu asekkhabhavanapatti datthabbaa"ti vacanam arabbha panham codeti: kalyanaaputhujjanassapi siladidha sekkha-ti vuccanti kim na? (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-127) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-128) Uttaram deti: na, pariyayabhavato. Nippariyayena hi sekkha-asekkhabhavo yathasambhavam maggaphaladhammesu evati, lokiyesu sekkhabhavaasankabhavato asekkhabhavanapatti vutta. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-129) Arahattaphaladhamma hetthimaphaladhamma viya sikkhaphalabhaavena pavattanato sekkha siyum -- tanca codanam manasikatva "parinitthitasikkhakiccatta"ti mulatika vyakasi; "tamsadisesu tabboharo"ti ca vuttam, yatha "amanusso"ti sadiso asekkha nama hoti. Vuddhippattasekkhati annattha a-karo vuddhiatthepi dassito. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-129)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tini pakkharanani imam attham anumodanti: (1) *sekkha dhamma* nama tisu sikkhasuu jata silasamadhipannasampayutta catumaggahetthimaphalattayadhamma, sikkhanasiilattta sayameva sikkha-kiccam karonta (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109); (2) *asekkha dhamma* nama arahattaphaladhamma eva, parinitthitasikkhakiccatta upari sikkhitabbabhavo natthi (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109); (3) lokiya dhamma, nibbanananca, na sekkha na asekkha -- nippariyayena sekkha-asekkhabhavo maggaphaladhammesu evati (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-129); (4) *nevasekkhanasekkha*-padam ubhayapatikkhepena vuttam (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530).

## Divergences ⚑

- **sekkha-vacanatthassa padhanavijayo** -- Atthakatha tini atthani (tisu sikkhasuu jata; sattannam sekkhanam ete; apariyositasikkhatta sikkhanti) samanabhavena dasseti ⚑; Mulatiika "akkharattho pana sikkha etesam silanti sekkha"ti iti akkharattham padhanam katva avasesa attha tassa nidassanani va anugamino vati vibhajati ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109)

- **asekkha-saddo: vuddhippatta-atthassa yojanaththanam** -- Atthakatha "vuddhippatta va sekkha asekkha"ti arahattaphaladhammaanam adhivacanattam salakkheti, kasma vuddhippatta iti na vibhajati ⚑; Mulatiika vibhajati: arahattamaggadhamma arahattaphalabhavam apanna vipakassukhassa panitatara vuddhippatta honti, na hetthimaphaladhammaanam ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-110)

## Disambiguated Restatement (original language)

*Sekkha dhamma* (NOM.PL.M; *sekkha* -- tini atthani: (a) tisu sikkhasuu jata "born within the three trainings" per atthakatha ⚑; (b) sattannam sekkhanam ete "belonging to the seven sekkha-persons" per atthakatha ⚑; (c) apariyositasikkhatta sayameva sikkhanti "training on their own account because training is not yet complete" per atthakatha ⚑; akkharattho pana sikkha etesam silanti per mulatiika ⚑; referent: catumaggahetthimaphalattayadhamma per mulatiika ⚑; *dhamma* = sabhavadhirana).
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109)

*Asekkha dhamma* (NOM.PL.M; *asekkha* -- upari sikkhitabbabhavato na sekkha "not-sekkha because there is nothing further to train in" per atthakatha ⚑; vuddhippatta va sekkha per atthakatha ⚑ -- vibhajati mulatiika: vuddhippatta = arahattamaggadhamma arahattaphalabhavam apanna, santatarataya panitatara ⚑; referent: arahattaphaladhamma per atthakatha ⚑ mulatiika ⚑; *dhamma* same sense).
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-110)

*Nevasekkhanasekkha dhamma* (NOM.PL.M; *nevasekkhanasekkha* -- ubhayapatikkhepena vuttam "stated by the negation of both" per atthakatha ⚑; nippariyayena sekkha-asekkhabhavo maggaphaladhammesu eva, tasma lokiya dhamma nibbanananca na sekkha na asekkha per mulatiika ⚑ anutiika ⚑; *dhamma* same sense).
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-530)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-109)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-129)
