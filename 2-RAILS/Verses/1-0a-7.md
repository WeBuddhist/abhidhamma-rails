## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha pitisahagatata ti ekuppadadi-bhavena sahabhava-ppatti ya piti, taya saha gatati vyakaroti: pitisahagata piti-sampayuttati attho. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526) Sesapadadvayampi (sukhasahagata, upekkhasahagata) eseva nayo. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526) Upekkhati ettha adukkhamasukha vedana vutta: sa hi sukha-dukkha-kara-pavattim upekkha-ti, majjhattakarasanthitatta tenakara-va pavattati --- evam upekkha-saddo adukkha-masukha-vedana-vacano. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526) Ayam tiko veddanattikato padadvayameva gahetva nippitikassa sukhassa sappitikasukha-to visesa-dassanavasena vutto: pitisahagata vs. sukhasahagata ti anam nana-ttam vedana-ttikato atirittam tiko pakaseti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika upekkhatta-m evam pakaseti: vedayamanapi majjhattavedana sukhakare dukkhakarekara ca udisina hotiti attho --- atha va upeta yutta sukha-dukkhanam aviruddha ikkha anubhavanams upekkha. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104) Visesa-dassana-vase nati nanatta-dassanavasena: yadi hi pitisahagata eva sukhasahagata siyum, pitisahagatati eteneva siddhatta sukhasahagatati idam na vattabbam siya; tato tikam purentena dukkhasahagatam vattabbam siya --- evanca sati vedanattiko evayanti vuttavacanam apajjati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104) Tasma pitisahagatati vatva sukhasahagatati vadanto piti-vippayuttampi sukkham atthiti --- tatiya-jjhana-kaya-vinnana-sampayuttam sukkham sappitikasukha-to bhinnam katva dasseti ti adhippayo. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104) Atha va piti-sukhanam dubbitnneya-nanattanam nanatta-dassanattha-m ayam tiko vutto: pitisahagatati ettha hi sukhekadeso sanggahito, na piti; sukhasahagatati ettha piti sangahita, na sukham. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika upekkhaya nayam vibhaventi: vedayamanati anubhavamana; sukhakare ti itthakaare, itthanubbhavanakare va; udasinati nati-apanati-rahita; sukha-dukkhanam aviruddha tesam bya-vadha-yika-bhavato --- sukha-dukkha-ni viya hi sukkha-dukkhanam anantaram pavattanato byava-dha-yika-bhuta na tehi virujjhati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-122) Piti-sahagata sukha-sahagata ca annaman-nam bhinnati --- tasma pitisahagatayeva na sukhasahagata, sukhasahagatapi na pitisahagata. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-122) Dutiye paragraphe anutika savasesam niravase-sam ca sukkha-pitio sangahetva pavattehi padhama-dutiyapadehi yo pitisahagato dhammaviseso tam sukkham, yo ca sukhasahagato dhamma-viseso sa pititi anam annam samsattha-bhave padantara-sanggahita-bhava-dipanatosiddho. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-123) Catutthajjhana-sukkham atipanitasukkha nti olaarikaggato nIharitvA tassa panitabhavam dassetu-m ayam tiko vuttoti keci vadanti --- tadetam sabbesam sukhavedana-sampayutta-dhammanam idha sukhasahagatati vuttatta vicaretabbam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-123)

## Consensus

Sabbani tini pakkharanani imamattham anumodanti: (1) pitisahagata, sukhasahagata, upekkha-sahagata ti sahagata-saddo ekupp-ada-di-bhavena sampayoga-vasena sahabhavam adhipeti (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526); (2) upekkha ti adukkhama-sukha vedana, sukhakara-dukkhakara-pavattim upekkha-titi majjhattakarasanthita (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-122); (3) ayam tiko vedanattikato padadvayameva gahetva nippitikassa sukhassa sappitikasukha-to visesam dasseti (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104); (4) pitisahagata ti ettha sukhekadeso sanggahito, na piti; sukhasahagata ti ettha piti sanggahita, na sukkham --- piti-sukkhanam annam-annam samsattha-bhave padantara-sangaha (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-123).

## Divergences

- **upekkha-saddassa vyutpatti** --- Atthakatha sukha-dukkha-kara-pavattim upekkha-ti majjhattakarasanthita tenakara-va pavattati ti kamma-patham dasseti; Anutika upeta yutta sukha-dukkhanam aviruddha ikkha anubhavanati aparatt ham pi gahetva aviruddha-bhavena pakaseti.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-122)

- **tikassa karanam** --- Atthakatha nippitikassa sukhassa sappitikasukha-to visesadassanavasenati ekadesa-visesam pakaseti; Mulatika piti-sukhanam dubbitnneya-nanattam ti dubbitnneyanana-ttam aparaatt ham pi (piti ettha sukhekadeso, sukhe piti) dasseti; Anutika keci catuttthajjhana-sukkham atipanitasukkha nti vadanti --- tat-etam vicaretabbam.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-526)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-104)
