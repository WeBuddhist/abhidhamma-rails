## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

*Vipaka*-saddo: kusalakusalanam paka-ti vipaka. *Vipakabhavamaapannanam arupadhammananetam adhivacananam* -- vipakanikutthi padam arupyadhammameva sandhaya vuttam, na rupadhamma, kasmaa? Yatha saliibijaadinam phalani tamsadisani nibbattani vipakkani naamaati vipakanirutti labhanti, na muulankurapattakhandhanaalani -- evameva kusalakusalanam phalani arupadhammasabhavena saarammanabhavena sukkakaadibhavena ca tamsadisani vipakkabhavamaapannaniti vipakanirutti labhanti, na rupadhamma kammanibbattaapi kammaasamiidisati. *Vipakadhammadhamma*-saddo: vipakasabhavadhamma yatha jaatijaraasabhava jaatijaraapakativa satta jaatidhamma jaraadhammaati vuccanti evam vipakajanakathena vipakasabhavaa vipaakapakativaa dhamma. Tatiyapadam ubhayasabhavapatikkepavasena vuttam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-522)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika *vipakkabhavamaapannanam arupadhammananam* iti vacanam vibhajati: yatha saaliibijaadinam phalani tamsadisani nibbattani vipakkani naama honti vipakanirutti ca labhanti, na muulankurapattakhandhanaalani -- evameva kusalakusalanam phalani arupadhammasabhavena saarammanabhavena sukkakaadibhavena ca tamsadisani vipakkabhavamaapannaaniti vipakanirutti labhanti, na rupadhamma kammanibbattaapipi kammaasamiidisaati dassetuam vuttam. *Jaatijara-sabhava*-ti jaayanajiranasabhavaa. *Vipaakapakatika*-ti vipaccanapakativa. Vipaccanasabhavata ca anupacchinnaavijaatanahaamaanaasantaane sabyaaparataa; tena abhinnaaadikusalanam bhaavanaapahaatabbaadiiakusalanam ca vipaakaaanuppaaadepi vipaakadhammataa siddhaa hoti. Vipakkabhavanti cettha bhava-saddena sabhavo eva vutto. *Ubhayasabhavapatikkhepavasena* iti tatiyapadassa yojanam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-98)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika vipaakanirutti-vacananam vibhajati: *vipakanirutti ca labhanti*-ti tesu vipakasaddassa nirulhataum dasseti. *Sukkakanhaadii*-ti aadi-saddena akanhaasukkaaphassaadiibhavo pariggahito. Sati pana paaka-saddassa phalapariyaayabhaave ruupam viya na niihiino pakkaum viya visittho pakoti vipakoti evam vaa ettha attho datthabboti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-115)
*Sabyaaparataa*-ti saussaahataa. *Santaane sabyaaparataa*-ti etena cittappayogasankhaatena kiriyaabhavena vipaakadhammanam santaanavisesam aaha "yato yasmim cittuppaade kusalakusala cetana, tamsantaane eva tassa vipaakuppatti"ti. Ettha ca "sabyaaparataa"ti etena aavajjanadvayam vipaakam ca nivatteti, "anupacchinnaavijaatanahaamaane"ti imina avasittham kiriyam nivatteti. Ubhayenapi anusayasahaayasaussaahataalaakkhana vipaakadhammadhammaa ti dasseti. Lokuttarakusalanampi hi anusaya upanissaya honti. *Nirussaahasantabhaavalaakkhana vipaakaa*, ubhayaviparitalaakkhana nevavapaakanavipaakadhammadhammaa ti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-116)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tini pakkharanani (atthakatha, mulatika, anutika) imamattam anumodanti: (1) *vipaka*-saddo kusalakusalanam paka-ti nipphattiphalabhavo; (2) *vipakabhavamaapannanam arupadhammanam*-iti vacanam: rupadhamma kammanibbattaapi vipakaanirati labhanti, na arupadhammasadisa phalasabhavena -- tasmaa vipakapadam arupadhammanameva adhivacananam; (3) *vipakadhammadhamma*-saddo vipakajanakathena vipakasabhavadhamma sandheti, analogena jaatijaraadhammaanuppaadehi; (4) tatiyapadam *nevavapaakanavipaakadhammadhamma* ubhayasabhavapatikkhepena vuttam; (5) vipaakadhammanam laakkhanavinicchaye: nirussaahasantabhaavalaakkhana vipaka, anusayasahaayasaussaahataalaakkhana vipaakadhamma.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-522)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-98)

## Divergences

- **vipakadhammadhamma -- sabyaaparataa-vibhago** -- Atthakatha *vipakadhammadhamma*-padassa yojanam "vipakajanakathena vipakasabhavaa vipakapakatikaa dhamma"ti niddeseti, santipavicaranam na vibhajati; Anutika pana "anupacchinnaavijaatanahaamaanasantaane sabyaaparataa"ti santaanavisesam vivarati -- yato yasmin cittuppaade kusalakusala cetana, tamsantaane eva tassa vipaakuppatti -- tena abhinnaaadikusalanam bhaavanaapahaatabaadiiakusalanam ca vipaakaaanuppaaadepi vipaakadhammataa siddhaa hoti.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-522)
