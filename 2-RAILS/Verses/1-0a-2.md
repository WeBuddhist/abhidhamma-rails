## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

*Sukha*-saddo bahuatthesu vattati: sukhavedanayam, sukhamule, sukharammanam, sukhahetumhi, sukhapaccayatthane, abyabajjhe, nibbane ca; idha pana *sukhaya vedanaya sampayutta dhamma* iti padam sukhavedanayameva datthabba, na annesu atthesu.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-519)
*Dukkha*-saddo dukkhavedanayam, dukkhavatthusmim, dukkharammanam, dukkhapaccaye, dukkhapaccayatthane ca dissati; idha *dukkhaya vedanaya sampayutta dhamma* iti padam dukkhavedanayameva niyantabbam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-520)
Vacanattho: sukhayatiti *sukha*; dukkhayatiti *dukkha*; na dukkha na sukhati *adukkhamasukha*, yattha ma-karo padasandhivasena vutto. *Vedana*-saddo vedayitamattameva dipeti: arammanarasa vedayanti anubhavantiti vedana; tasu itthanubbhavanalakkhana *sukha*, anitthanubbhavanalakkhana *dukkha*, ubhayaviparitanubbhavanalakkhana *adukkhamasukha*. *Sampayutta*-saddo tisupi padesu ekupppadatadini samam pakarehi yuttoti attho, yatha Kathavatthupaliaya vuttam: ekuppada ekaniroddha ekavatthuka ekarammanati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-521)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatika sukha-saddassa bahuvuttiyam vibhajati: sukhindriyattho sukha-saddo kayasukkham kayanuggaham satavisesam gahetva pavatto, na pana satasamannam gahetva pavattasukha-saddo viya. Sukhamulasukhahetesu phalupaccarena, sukharammaṇasukhapaccayattthanesu sukhasahacariyaya, abyapajjinibbanesu dukkkhapagamabhavena sukhapariyayo vuttoti datthabboti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-95)
*Sukha* iti vedanasaddamapekkhitva sukhabhavamattassa appaakasanena napumsakalinggata na kata. Vipaakaavipaakabhedaya lakkhnanam dvidha vibhajati: vipaka sukhavedana sabhaavato itthassa anubhavanalakkhana; itara sabhaavato sankappato ca itthassa itthakarassa va anubhavanalakkhanati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-96)
*Sampayutta*-saddassa attham vibhajanto aha: sama ekibhavuupagata viya yutta, samam va saha yuttati yojetabbam; ekuppadati eko samano uppado etesanti ekuppada, samanapaccayehi sahuppattikati attho. Ekanirodhadii-lakkhanani anupubbato niyamiyanti; *ekarammanatii*-vacane pana pancavinnanasampatticchanadinam ekasmin arammanam uppajjamanana bhinnavatthukana sampayuttatia apajjeyyati, tannivaranattham vuttam.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-97)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutika mulatiikaya sukhindriyatthanam sukha-saddassa saamaaññavisesavibhagam sampadetvaa dasseti: samaaññassa bhedapariyaadaanatoo bhedassa ca saamaññapariccaagatoti adhippayo -- visesasaamaññaani avayavasamudaaya viya aññamaññato bhinnani. Itthaasuuuti sukhopekkhaanam viparinamaaññanasankharadukkhataya anitthabhavopi atthiiti viseseti; sa akusalavipakabhuta anitthapi atthiiti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-111)
Sankharadukkhadisu dukkha-saddappavatti aadi-saddena "thitisukham viparinamaadukkham, akusalam kayakammam dukkhudrayam dukkhavipaka"ntiaaadike sanganhati; dukkhasaddappavatti vuttanayeneva yojetabba. *Itthanubbhavana*-lakkhanassa vipaakaavipaakabhedanukuulatthaaya vibhaavo: vipaaka sabhaavato itthassa anubhavanalakkhana, itara sabhaavato sankappato ca itthassa va itthakarassa va anubhavanalakkhanati.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-112)
*Sampayutta*-saddassa catulakkhanam anupubbato vibhavayanto: asamanapaccayehi ekajjham uppattito samanapaccayehi ekajjham uppatti saatisayati ukkaamsagativijanaanavasena "samanapaccayehi sahuppattikati attho"ti vuttam. Ekavatthukati kappentassa sambandho; ekarammaana-vacane pana pancavinnanasampatticchanadinam bhinnavatthukaanam sampayuttataa aapajjeyyati nidassanato vuttam. Vedanaattikassa kusalattikanantarattam dassetva vedanaasampayogavibhagadassanattham -- kusala dhamma siya sukhaya sampayutta siya adukkhamasukhaya, akusala siya sukhaya siya dukkhaya siya adukkhamasukhaya -- Sammasambhudddhena vuttanti aha.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-113)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-114)

## Consensus

Sabbapi tini pakkharanani (atthakatha, mulatika, anutika) imamattam anumodanti: (1) *sukha*-saddo idha sukhavedanayameva niyamito, na annattha -- *dukkha*-saddo dukkhavedanayameva, na dukkhavatthadiatthesu; (2) *vedana*-saddo vedayitamattam dipeti, arammanarasa anubbhavanam; (3) lakkhato *sukha* itthanubbhavanalakkhana, *dukkha* anitthanubbhavanalakkhana, *adukkhamasukha* ubhayaviparitanubbhavanalakkhana; (4) *sampayutta*-saddo ekupppadatadini -- ekuppada-ekaniroddha-ekavatthuka-ekarammanatta -- samam yuttoti attho; (5) adukkhamasukhayam ma-karo padasandhivasena vutto.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-521)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-95)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-96)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-112)

## Divergences

- **sukha vedana -- lakkhana-vibhago vipakaavipaakabhedena** -- Atthakatha sukhaya vedanaya lakkhanam "itthanubbhavanalakkhana"ti ekam niddisati, vipaakaavipaakabhedam na vibhajati; Mulatika pana "vipaka sabhaavato itthassa anubhavanalakkhana, itara sabhaavato sankappato ca itthassa itthakarassa va anubhavanalakkhana"ti dvidha vibhajati.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-521)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-96)

- **sampayuttassa catulakkhanam -- anupubbavinicchayo** -- Atthakatha ekupppadatadinam catunnam lakkhanam anupubbam Kathaavatthupaalivaacanena yojetva deseti, na vikappe; Mulatika pana patilomagativijaanavasenapana ekarammanati pathamam vatva anupubbam itarani ca yojetva duvidhavikappena vibhajati.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-521)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-97)
