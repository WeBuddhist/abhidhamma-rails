## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakatha *paritta*-saddo vyakhyati: samantato khanditatta appamattakam parittam -- "parittam gomayapindan"tiadisu viya loke appamattakavacanam. Imepi kamavacaradhamma appanubhavataya paritta, paritta viyati paritta; kamavacaradhammanam etam adhivacanam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531) *Mahaggatā dhammā* -- kilesavikkhambhanasamatthataya vipulaphalataya dighasantataya ca mahantabhavam gata, mahantehi va ularacchandaviriyacittapannehi gata patipannatipi mahaggatā; rūpārūpāvacarānam adhivacanam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531) *Appamāṇā dhammā* -- pamāṇakarā rāgādayo dhammā pamāṇam nāma; ārammaṇato vā sampayogato vā natthi etesam pamāṇam, pamāṇassa ca patipakkhāti appamāṇā; lokuttaradhammanam adhivacanam. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatiika atthakathaya tini thanani vibhajati. *Paritta* -- kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatadiihi paritta (tini lakkhanani: kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatā, vipulaphalatā, dighasantatā -- imepi tayo kusala mahaggatānam lakkhanā, vipākakiriyesu dighasantatāva); pamānakarehi vā olārikehi kāmataṇhādīhi paricchinnāti paritta. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111) *Mahaggatā* -- tehi aparicchinnattā sukhumehi rūpataṇhādīhi paricchinnā pamāṇamahattam gatā pavattāti mahaggatā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111) *Appamāṇā* -- aparicchinnā appamāṇā. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutiika mulatikaya "kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatā"ti vacanam nidassanamattam datthabbanti vibhajati -- vitakkādivikkhambhanasamatthatāpi hettha labbhati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130) Akusalaviddhaṃsanarasattā kusalānam tattha sātisayakiccayuttataṃ parittadhammehi mahaggatānam pakāsetuṃ "kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatāyā"ti vuttam. Vipākakiriyesu dighasantatāva, na kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatā vipulaphalatā ca -- vipākesupi "vipulam phalam vipulaphala"nti atthe gayhamāne vipulaphalatā labbhateva, sopi ekasesanayena atthakathāyam vuttoti veditabbo. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130) *Pamāṇakarā* dhammā -- guṇato pamāṇam karontā viya pavattantīti olārikā kilesā; tehi parito khanditā paricchinnāti paritta; satipi kehici paricchinnatte mahāpamāṇabhāvena gatā pavattāti mahaggatā; paricchedakarānam kilesānam sukhumānampi agocarattā na kathañcipi paricchinnā vītikkantāti aparicchinnā appamāṇā -- yato te "apariyāpannā"tipi vuccanti. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130)

## Consensus

Sabbāpi tini pakkharanani imam attham anumodanti: (1) *paritta dhammā* nāma kāmāvacaradhammā, olārikehi pamāṇakarehi kilesāhi paricchinnattā appānubhāvatāya paritta (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111); (2) *mahaggatā dhammā* nāma rūpārūpāvacaradhammā, pamāṇakarānam olārikakilesānam agocarā sukhumehi paricchinnā mahantabhāvam gatā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111); (3) *appamāṇā dhammā* nāma lokuttaradhammā, pamāṇakarānam sukhumakilesānampi agocarattā aparicchinnā (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130); (4) *mahaggatā*-lakkhanā tayo (kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatā, vipulaphalatā, dighasantatā) kusalamahaggatānam sādhāranā, vipākakiriyesu ekasesanayena dighasantatāva (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111) (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130).

## Divergences ⚑

- **mahaggatā-lakkhanānam vipākakiriyesu yojanā** -- Atthakatha tayo lakkhanē (kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatā, vipulaphalatā, dighasantatā) sabbe samānabhāvena mahaggatānam adhivacanam karoti ⚑; Mulatiika vibhajati: vipākakiriyesu dighasantatāva, na kilesavikkhambhanasamatthatā; Anutiika ekadesena anumodati: "vipulam phalam vipulaphala"nti atthe vipākesupi vipulaphalatā labbhati, sopi ekasesanayena atthakathāyam vuttoti ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-111)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130)

- **paritta-vacanattho** -- Atthakatha parittam "samantato khanditatta appamattakam" iti loke parittam-saddassa vohāram mulam karoti ⚑; Anutiika "pamāṇakarā" kilesā etesam simākārā iti pariccheda-nayena vyakhyati -- tehi parito khanditā paricchinnāti parittāti ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-531)
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-130)
