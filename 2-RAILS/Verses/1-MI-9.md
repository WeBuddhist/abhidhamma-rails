## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Atthakathā *dassanena pahātabbahetukā* iti samāsam vibhajati: *dassanena pahātabbo hetu etesam* iti atthena pahātabbahetukā. Tatiyapadassa *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā* iti attham vibhajanto āha: *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbo hetu etesam atthīti* iti atthena gahetabbam, na *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbo hetu etesanti* iti. Itarathā hi ahetukānam aggahanam bhaveyya — ahetukānam hi hetu natthi yo dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbo siyā. Sahetukesupi hetuvajjānam pahānam āpajjeyya, na hetūnam; hetuyeva hi etesam *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbo* iti vutto, na te dhammā. Ubhayampi cetam anadhippetam, tasmā yo hetu dassanabhāvanāpahātabbo so atthi etesanti ayamattho gahetabbo.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-528)

### dhammasangani-mulatiika

Mulatīkā ^1-106 āha: *evamattham aggahetvā* iti padam atthāyuttito ca saddāyuttito ca aggahetabbatam dasseti. Appahātabbahetumattāni gayhamānāni ahetukā asaṅgahitā siyum — atthāyutti; pahātabbasaddassa niccasāpekkhattā ca na hetusaddena samāso upapajjetīti saddāyutti. Tasmā *pahātabba-saddo patisedhavasena na yojetabbo, yesam aññapadatthe samāso, tabbisesanam atthīti* idam patisedhavasena yojetvā *dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbo hetu etesam nevatthīti* attho gahetabbo. Evam purimapadadvayasaṅgahitadhammapatisedhanena tadaññadhammanidassanametam hoti, na ahetukapadam viya hetuvirahappakāsanena.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-106)

Mulatīkā ^1-107 ahetukānam asaṅgaham sādheti: purimasmiñhi atthe hetūnam dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbatā patikkhittā; patikkhepo ca pahātabbāsaṅkāsabbhāve hoti; pahātabbāsaṅkā ca hetumhi sati siyā; ahetukānam pana hetuyeva natthi yo dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbo siyā, tasmā tesam patikkhepo na sambhavati. Atha vā ahetukānam aggahanam pākatameva, tasmā *hetuyeva hi tesam natthī*tiādi gahetabbatthasseva kāranam vadati. Tatiyarāsibhāvena vuttānam dhammānam anekesu sahetukāhetukā, tesu yo dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbo siyā, so hetu natthi.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-107)

### dhammasangani-anutiika

Anutīkā *appahātabbahetumattesū*ti padam *appahātabbahetukamattesu* iti atthena vibhaveti — sabbo kusalābyākatadhammo yathādhippetattho saṅgahito. *Samāso na upapajjati asamatthabhāvato* ti vibhajati: *pahātabbasaddo patisedhavasena yojito sati* hetusaddena na samāso sādheti. *Ubhinnanti* dve pahātabbahetusaddā visum visum yojetabbā — dassanena pahātabbahetukā, bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukāti ca tehi patisedhaṃ ca visum yojetvā tatiyarāsi gahitoti. Evañca purimapadadvayasaṅgahitadhammapatisedhanena tadaññadhammadassanameva hoti, ahetukapadam viya hetuvirahappakāsanam na hoti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-125)

## Consensus

Sabbāni tīni vyākaranāni (atthakathā, mūlatīkā, anutīkā) imam attham samanujānanti: (1) *dassanena pahātabbahetukā* iti samāsassa attho *dassanena pahātabbo hetu etesam atthīti* — hetu eva pahātabbo, na dhammā sayameva; (2) tatiyapadassa *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā* iti attho *dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbo hetu etesam nevatthīti* — na tu *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbo hetu* iti atthena, yathā ahetukānam aggahanam āpajjeyya; (3) ahetukā dhammā tatiyarāsimhi na asaṅgayhanti, tesam hetu natthi yo dassanabhāvanāhi pahātabbo siyāti.
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-528)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-106)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-mulatiika.md#^1-107)
(1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-anutiika.md#^1-125)

## Divergences ⚑

- **tatiyapadassa vyākhyānapaddhati** — Atthakathā *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbo hetu etesam atthīti* iti atthe thitvā ahetukānam aggahanam bhaveyyāti parisosanam karoti, na saddāyuttim vibhajati ⚑; Mūlatīkā atthāyuttiñca saddāyuttiñca ubhayampi vibhājeti — *pahātabbasaddassa niccasāpekkhattā sambandhīsaddato pahāyakato aññam patisedham apekkhamānassa hetusaddena samāso na yujjatīti* saddāyutti, ahetukānam asaṅgaham atthāyuttito ca sādheti ⚑.
  (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-528)
