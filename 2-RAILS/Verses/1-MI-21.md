## Synthesis (per commentator)

### dhammasangani-atthakatha

Ajjhattārammaṇattike atthakathā saṅkhittena dīpeti: *anantarattiko* — idaṃ tikattayaṃ teyeva tippakārepi dhamme (ajjhattā, bahiddhā, ajjhattabahiddhā dhammā) ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattanavasena vuttaṃ. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-540) *Anantarattika*-saddo "anantare" atthena paccupaṭhito — purimattikasseva (ajjhattattikassa) tippakāre dhamme ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattanaṃ imasmiṃ tikattaye adhippetaṃ, tasmā idaṃ tikaṃ *ārammaṇattika* ti vuccati. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-540)

## Consensus

Ekameva pakkharaṇaṃ (atthakathā) imasmiṃ tikattaye pavattati. Tassā sammati: (1) *ajjhattārammaṇattika* ajjhattattikassa anantare ṭhitaṃ, tasseva tippakārepi dhamme (ajjhattā, bahiddhā, ajjhattabahiddhā) ārammaṇaṃ katvā pavattanavasena vuttaṃ; (2) *ārammaṇa*-padena imasmiṃ tike dhammasambandho ārammaṇabhāvasāmaññena niddiṭhho. (1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md#^1-540)
