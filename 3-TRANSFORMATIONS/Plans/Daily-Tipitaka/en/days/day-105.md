# Day 105 — Only One Is Unwholesome

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The chapter closes today with the sorting. Two long verses: first the triads, then the pairs. Are the faculties wholesome, unwholesome, or *abyākata* — indeterminate, neither wholesome nor unwholesome? What object does each one take? Is it a root cause? Can it be defiled?

One count is worth carrying out of the chant. Of the twenty-two faculties, exactly one is unwholesome: *domanassindriya*, the faculty of displeasure — the mind's own unpleasant feeling. Ten are indeterminate. The rest can go either way. Then the realms. Ten faculties are *lokiya*, mundane — of this world. Three are *lokuttara*, supramundane — above the world. Nine are mixed, at home in both. Those nine include faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can this small effort really be the same one that goes all the way?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### MISSAKA — Mixed

**Pronunciation:** *mis-sa-ka* — three syllables. Hold the double "s" for a beat.

**In today's passage:** The teachers sort the twenty-two faculties by realm. Ten work only in the sense sphere. Three are supramundane only — above the world. The other nine are *lokiyalokuttaramissaka*: mixed, at work in both. Mind, life, equanimity, joy, and the five we can train.

**Why it matters:** Mixed means you are not waiting on a different set of tools. The mindfulness you spend on something small today is the same faculty that finishes the work. Ordinary use is not a lesser version of it. It is practice on the same instrument.
