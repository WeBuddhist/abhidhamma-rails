# Day 170 — Gods by Purity

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today the map shows the worlds themselves. The sense sphere (*kāmāvacara*) runs from the lowest hell up to the highest sense-pleasure gods. The fine-material sphere (*rūpāvacara*) runs from the Brahma world to the Akaniṭṭha gods. Above it is the formless sphere (*arūpāvacara*). The paths, their fruits, and the unconstructed element belong to none of them.

Then the verses ask what leads to each world. Kings and queens are gods (*deva*) by convention. Beings in the heavens are gods by rebirth. Arahants (fully freed ones) are gods by purity. Giving (*dāna*), moral discipline (*sīla*), and the observance day (*uposatha*) lead to good human or heavenly births. *Jhāna* (meditative absorption) leads to the Brahma worlds. Each world has its own life span, counted in years or in eons.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which act of giving or restraint today could I do with a full heart?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VISUDDHIDEVA — God by Purity

**Pronunciation:** *vi-sud-dhi-de-va* — five syllables; "dh" is a "d" with a puff of breath. Say "vi-SUD-dhi-DAY-va".

**In today’s passage:** The teaching names three kinds of gods. Kings and queens are gods only by custom. Beings born in heaven are gods by rebirth. Arahants are gods by purity. The commentary says all the other gods should honour them, because they are pure of every defilement.

**Why it matters:** Giving, moral discipline, and the observance day can lead to a heavenly birth. The teaching asks us to do these things well. It also asks us not to be content with heaven alone. So give, keep your precepts, and aim for the purity that even the gods honour.
