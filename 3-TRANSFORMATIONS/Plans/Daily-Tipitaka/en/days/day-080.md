# Day 80 — The Second Turning

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Today the fifth heap closes the first pass, and a second pass begins. Consciousness (*viññāṇa*) gets the same eleven-fold sweep: any consciousness at all — past, future, present, inner, outer, coarse, fine, low, high, far, near — gathered together as the aggregate of consciousness.

Then the chapter turns. The commentary says the same ground is covered three times, in three "turnings" (*parivaṭṭa*): the discourse way, the Abhidhamma way, and the question way. Today we cross from the first into the second. The same five heaps are now taken apart by strict analysis. Material form (*rūpa*) comes first, and the text keeps it short. Book I already laid it out in full, so it is not repeated here. The day ends on a plain line: "This is called the aggregate of form."

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I look at the same familiar thing a second way, and see something new?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VIÑÑĀṆA — Consciousness

**Pronunciation:** *vin-YĀ-ṇa* — the "ññ" is like the "ny" in "canyon".

**In today's passage:** *Viññāṇa* means "consciousness" — the bare knowing of an object. Today it completes the five heaps. The commentary makes one careful point: unlike the other heaps, consciousness cannot be called "born of mind-contact," because consciousness *is* the mind. It is named instead by its door — eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, and so on.

**Why it matters:** Consciousness feels like the one solid "me" behind everything. But it too is only a heap, and it arises by conditions: eye plus form, and eye-consciousness appears. Seeing that loosens the last and deepest grip — the sense that there is a knower standing behind the knowing.
