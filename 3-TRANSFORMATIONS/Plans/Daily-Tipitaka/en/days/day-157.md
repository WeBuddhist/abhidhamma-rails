# Day 157 — Three Fires

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The threes continue. Today's verses name the three taints, the three fetters, and three sets of craving. There are three quests: for sense pleasure, for existence, and for a "holy life" built on wrong views. There are three fears: of birth, of aging, and of death. Doubt about the past, the present, and the future is called darkness.

Three wrong ideas about pleasure and pain come next. Some say it all comes from past action. Some say a creator made it. Some say it has no cause at all. Then lust, hate, and delusion appear under new names: impediments, stains, and fires. The commentary says each one burns the person in whom it arises.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which of the three fires is burning in me right now?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### AGGI — Fire

**Pronunciation:** *ag-gi* — two short syllables, with a double "g". Say "AG-gi".

**In today’s passage:** The verses name three fires: the fire of lust, the fire of hate, and the fire of delusion. The commentary says they are called fire because they burn the one in whom they arise. It tells of a young nun so overcome by lust that she died where she stood.

**Why it matters:** We often think lust or anger hurts others first. This image says they burn us first. When you feel the heat of craving or anger, treat it like a flame near your hand. You do not need to fight it. Just stop adding fuel, and it cools.
