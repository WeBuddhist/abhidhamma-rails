# Day 131 — Mindfulness Steadies the Approach

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today the text slows down and defines the qualities the meditator brings to the cushion. First among them is *sati* (mindfulness) — remembering, staying present, not letting the object float away. Alongside it come energy and clear knowing.

With these in place, the passage returns to the five hindrances and shows the mind cleansing itself of each one — of wanting, ill will, dullness, restlessness, and doubt. Each is named, understood, and released. This is not suppression; it is seeing an obstacle clearly enough that it loosens its own grip. Mindfulness is what holds the whole process steady, the way a steady hand holds a lamp so you can see what you are doing.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I notice one hindrance today without either feeding it or fighting it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SATI — Mindfulness

**Pronunciation:** *sa-ti* — a short "a" like the "u" in "cut", then "ti".

**In today’s passage:** *Sati* is mindfulness — the mind’s power to stay with its object and not forget it. Today’s text defines it carefully and places it at the front of the qualities that make calm possible. Without it, the mind drifts and the hindrances slip back in.

**Why it matters:** Mindfulness is less about effort and more about presence. When you remember to notice what is happening, harmful states lose their cover — you cannot let go of what you never saw. A little more remembering today is a little more of your own mind returned to you.
