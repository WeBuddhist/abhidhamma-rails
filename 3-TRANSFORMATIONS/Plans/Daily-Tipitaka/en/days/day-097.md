# Day 97 — It Starts as a Thought

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Two things happen today. First the chapter finishes its last set of six. Sense desire, ill will, and cruelty are each defined — and the definition is a surprise. Each one is named first as a kind of thinking: thought, intention, fixing the mind, aiming it. Ill will gets a longer entry, with ten grounds for resentment. But it still begins as a thought.

Then the chapter starts over from the Abhidhamma side, and the six become eighteen. Eye, sights, eye-consciousness; ear, sounds, ear-consciousness; and so on through all six doors. The same ground, counted more finely. Notice the order of the two halves. The first tells you where harm begins — in something small enough to catch. The second lays out the bare machinery underneath, with nobody at the controls.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Where did that thought start, and did I notice?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VITAKKA — Thought

**Pronunciation:** *vi-tak-ka* — "vi-TAK-ka"; hold the double "k" for a beat.

**In today's passage:** *Vitakka* is thought — the mind reaching out and landing on something. Today it does the defining work. Sense desire, ill will, and cruelty are each described first as thinking connected with that thing. Not as a mood that grabs you. Not as an act you have already done. As a thought.

**Why it matters:** This puts the trouble early, while it is still small. By the time ill will is a hot feeling, it is hard to move. As a thought it is light. The list runs the other way too — renunciation, goodwill, and harmlessness also begin as thoughts, and a thought can be chosen.
