# Day 104 — Twenty-Two Rulers

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

A new chapter opens today — the fifth of the eighteen. It counts twenty-two *indriya*, faculties: powers of mind and body, each in charge of its own small patch. Eye, ear, nose, tongue, body. Mind. Life. Two that mark a woman or a man. Five kinds of feeling. Then faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom. Then three that appear only on the noble path.

This chapter skips the sutta method — the first one to do so. The teachers say why: the suttas never list all twenty-two in a row. Sometimes two, sometimes three, sometimes five. So it goes straight to the Abhidhamma list, defines each one, then opens the question section. The order of the list is itself a lesson. Body first, because that is what we can see. Then life, which holds it together. Then feeling, because whatever is felt is pleasant or painful. Then the five we can train.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Of these twenty-two, which five are mine to train?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### INDA — Ruler

**Pronunciation:** *in-da* — two syllables. Say "IN-dah."

**In today's passage:** The word *indriya* (faculty) is built on *inda*, a ruler. Each faculty does a ruler's work in its own small province. The teachers put it plainly: the eye's rule shows up in seeing. Sharp eye, sharp seeing. Dull eye, dull seeing. Nothing outside the faculty settles that.

**Why it matters:** Twenty-two rulers, and you chose almost none of them. But five grow stronger with use — faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom. Some of your mind is simply given to you. Some of it is yours to train. Today, use one of the five.
