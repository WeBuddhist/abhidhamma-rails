# Day 169 — Born with Knowledge

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

We stay with the moment of rebirth today. Some beings are born with three roots: non-greed, non-hate, and non-delusion (*alobha / adosa / amoha*). Their birth is associated with knowledge (*ñāṇasampayutta*). Others are born with two roots, without knowledge. The rest are born with no roots at all.

Then the view widens to the higher worlds. In the material-form element, beings are born with five aggregates but only five bases. The non-percipient beings (*asaññasatta*) are stranger still. At rebirth they have one aggregate only: material form. They have no mind, no feeling, and no perception. The immaterial element is the reverse. There, four aggregates of mind appear, and no material form.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *If I was born able to understand, how will I use that gift today?*

## 4. 💡 Reading for Meaning

**Verses 1013–1019.** *(Full text rendered in the WeBuddhist app from the verse library; the first and last verses of the range are printed below so the plan-importer can find the right entries.)*

**v. 1013.** At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, for whom do three roots appear? For the gods of the sense sphere, for humans of the first eon, and for womb-born beings who have roots and are associated with knowledge, at the moment of rebirth three roots appear: non-greed as a resultant root, non-hate as a resultant root, non-delusion as a resultant root. At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, these three roots appear for them. At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, for whom do two roots appear? For the gods of the sense sphere, for humans of the first eon, and for womb-born beings who have roots but are dissociated from knowledge, at the moment of rebirth two roots appear: non-greed as a resultant root, non-hate as a resultant root. At the moment of rebirth in the sense-pleasure element, these two roots appear for them. The remaining beings appear without roots.

…

**v. 1019.** At the moment of rebirth in the immaterial element, which four aggregates appear? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — at the moment of rebirth in the immaterial element, these four aggregates appear. *(…)* At the moment of rebirth in the immaterial element, which one feeling… one perception… one intention… one mind appears? The mind-consciousness element — at the moment of rebirth in the immaterial element, this one mind appears.

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ÑĀṆASAMPAYUTTA — Associated with Knowledge

**Pronunciation:** *ñā-ṇa-sam-pa-yut-ta* — six syllables; "ñ" is like the "ny" in "canyon". Say "NYAA-na-sam-pa-YUT-ta".

**In today’s passage:** *Ñāṇa* is knowledge, and *sampayutta* means joined with. A birth associated with knowledge has all three good roots, non-delusion among them. Such a being has the faculty of wisdom (*paññā*) from the first moment of life.

**Why it matters:** The teaching says that if we were born with knowledge, we should develop the faculty of wisdom. It is a seed, not a full-grown tree. Use it today. Pause, look at what is going on in your mind, and see it clearly before you act.
