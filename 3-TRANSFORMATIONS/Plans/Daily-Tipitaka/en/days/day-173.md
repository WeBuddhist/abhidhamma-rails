# Day 173 — The Book of Analysis Is Complete

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today the Vibhaṅga, the Book of Analysis, comes to its end. The last verses sort the twelve groups four more ways. Which are born of past action and can be clung to? Which come with thought (*vitakka*), and which come without it? Which are material form, and which are not? Last of all: which are mundane (*lokiya*, of this world), and which are supramundane (*lokuttara*, above the world)?

The answer to the last question closes the book. Material form is always mundane. The four mental aggregates may be either. The commentary says that with this, all eighteen chapters of the Book of Analysis are complete. Tomorrow a new book begins.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What in me today is still of this world, and what is reaching beyond it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### PARIÑÑĀ — Full Understanding

**Pronunciation:** *pa-riñ-ñā* — three syllables; "ññ" is like the "ny" in "canyon". Say "pa-RIN-nyaa".

**In today’s passage:** Every list in this chapter is to be fully understood (*pariññā*). The commentary names three steps: knowing a thing, weighing it up, and letting go of what should go. The origin of suffering and the three unwholesome roots are what we let go.

**Why it matters:** Eighteen chapters of lists can seem dry. But the aim is to understand our own mind so well that greed, hatred, and delusion lose their hold. Take one step home today. Notice one feeling, see it clearly, and let it pass.
