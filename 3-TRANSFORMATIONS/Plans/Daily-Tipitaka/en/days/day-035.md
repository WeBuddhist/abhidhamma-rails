# Day 35 — Results We Did Not Choose

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Today the chant crosses two borders. First it completes the first path's fruit and touches the higher ones — the fruits of the second, third, and fourth paths. At the summit stands the arahant's faculty of final knowledge (*aññātāvindriya*): the knowing that the work is done.

Then the tone changes. From verse 556 come the results of unwholesome kamma: seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting, and touching that meet unwished-for objects — including plain bodily pain. The machinery is the same as in the wholesome results; only the kamma behind it differs. The teaching is steady here: when a hard moment lands, know it as a result, not a command — and let that knowing stir care for what we plant next.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *When something unpleasant arrives today, can I meet it as a result — not a verdict?*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### INDRIYA — Faculty

**Pronunciation:** in-DRI-ya — three syllables. Say "in-dree-ya."

**In today's passage:** An *indriya* is a faculty — a power that governs its own province, as a minister governs a district. Today's range holds the highest and the humblest: the arahant's faculty of final knowledge, and the pain faculty (*dukkhindriya*) — bodily pain doing its plain work inside an unwholesome result.

**Why it matters:** The text lists pain as a faculty, not a punishment. It governs its moment, then passes. Seen that way, pain loses some of its verdict: less blame, more care, and a clearer eye on what we plant next.
