# Day 13 — Fourfold and Fivefold Jhāna

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Yesterday opened the *fine-material sphere* with the first three *jhānas* (deep focus states). Today the catalogue covers the same path twice. First with the *fourfold method* (four jhāna steps). Then with the *fivefold method* (five jhāna steps). The teaching counts both ways. Real minds drop the factors in different orders.

In the four-step pass, the second jhāna drops both *vitakka* (thinking) and *vicāra* (holding the thought) at once. In the five-step pass, *vitakka* drops first, then *vicāra*. Same path, different stride. Verse 175 closes that pass. Verse 176 opens the next angle: the four *modes of progress* — easy or hard, slow or quick.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *When my own focus deepens, which factor falls away first — the search, the holding, or the joy?*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### PAÑCAKANAYO — Fivefold Method

**Pronunciation:** pun-cha-ka-NA-yo — five syllables; "c" is a soft "ch" as in "church". Stress the fourth syllable.

**In today's passage:** Verses 167–175 walk through the same jhānas the fourfold method just covered, but split into five steps. The reason is human: some minds let go of *vitakka* (thinking) before *vicāra* (holding the thought), so they need a step between the first and second jhāna. The teaching counts both ways so each kind of practitioner finds their own pace.

**Why it matters:** This is the teaching being kind. The same goal, two routes — because not every mind moves the same way. When you sit, do not measure yourself against a fixed map. Notice what falls away first, and follow that. The path adjusts to you; you do not have to bend to fit it.
