# Day 86 — Formations, and a Round Closes

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

A short day with two landmarks. The fourth aggregate opens: formations (*saṅkhāra*). And the text marks it differently from the ones before. Feeling and perception were each "associated with contact"; formations are "associated with mind." The commentary explains why — contact is itself one of the formations, so the heap cannot be named by what it contains. It is named by the mind it accompanies.

Then, four verses later, the range ends on a single word: *Dukamūlakaṃ* — "the dyad-rooted section." That marks the close of the first of four great rounds through this material. The Buddha took the twos and folded them into the threes; now that whole pass is done, and three more ways of turning the same ground are still to come.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is my mind putting together right now, without my noticing?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAṄKHĀRA — Formations

**Pronunciation:** *saṅ-KHĀ-ra* — "saṅ" as in "sung"; the "kh" is a breathy "k".

**In today's passage:** *Saṅkhāra* means "formations" — what the mind puts together. It is the whole working crew of mental factors, led by volition. Today's first verse calls this heap "associated with mind", not "associated with contact". The reason is simple: contact sits inside the crew. You cannot name the crew by naming one member.

**Why it matters:** This is the busiest of the five heaps, and the one that makes karma. What you are putting together right now — the wish to read, the attention, the effort — all sits here. Seeing a heap of factors instead of "me deciding" lifts some of the weight. It does not lift the responsibility.
