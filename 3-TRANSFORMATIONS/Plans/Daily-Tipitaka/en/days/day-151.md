# Day 151 — Like Seeks Like

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today's verses walk through five more of the Buddha's ten powers. He knows how past, present, and future actions ripen, and what conditions let them ripen or hold them back. He knows which way of life leads where — to suffering or to freedom. He knows the world's many elements. And he knows the different *adhimutti* (inclinations) of beings, and the strength of their faculties.

One line stands out for daily life. Beings of low inclination seek out others like themselves, and beings of fine inclination seek out the fine. The commentary adds that it is the heart's inclination that draws people together. The virtuous gather with the virtuous; the wise, with the wise. The company we seek both shows and shapes who we are becoming.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Whose company do I seek — and what does that choice say about where I am heading?*

**v. 815.** And what is the disposition of beings? "The world is eternal", or "the world is not eternal", or "the world is finite", or "the world is infinite", or "the soul and the body are the same", or "the soul is one thing and the body another", or "a Tathāgata exists after death", or "a Tathāgata does not exist after death", or "a Tathāgata both exists and does not exist after death", or "a Tathāgata neither exists nor does not exist after death" — thus beings rely either on the view of existence or on the view of non-existence. Or, avoiding both these extremes, they have gained the conforming acceptance with regard to things that are dependently arisen through specific conditionality, and the knowledge of them as they really are. This is the disposition of beings.

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ADHIMUTTI — Inclination

**Pronunciation:** *a-dhi-mut-ti* — four syllables. Say "a-dhi-MUT-ti".

**In today’s passage:** *Adhimutti* is inclination — the direction the heart leans. The Buddha’s fifth power is knowing the inclinations of beings. Today’s verses note that people of low inclination seek each other out, and people of fine inclination do the same.

**Why it matters:** You can learn something about your own inclination by looking at what, and whom, you are drawn to. The pull works both ways. Choosing good company, good talk, and good influences slowly tilts the heart toward the good. That is a small daily choice with a long reach.
