# Day 9 — Analyzing the Wholesome Mind

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Having named and defined all 56 factors of the first wholesome mind in the Padabhājanī, the Dhammasaṅgaṇī now turns a new lens on the same mind-moment: the *Koṭṭhāsavāro* — the Analysis by Portion.

Verse 58 opens with an overview: at that same moment, there are four aggregates (*khandhā*), two sense-bases (*āyatanāni*), two elements (*dhātuyo*), three nutriments (*āhārā*), eight faculties (*indriyāni*), five-factored jhāna (*jhānaṃ*), the five-factored path (*maggo*), seven powers (*balāni*), and three roots (*hetū*). Then verses 59 through 88 define each of these groupings in turn — working through the four aggregates, two bases, two elements, three nutriments, eight faculties, and closing with the five factors of jhāna.

The analytical method here is the same as the Padabhājanī: precise, exhaustive, non-overlapping definitions. But where the Padabhājanī listed factors individually, the Koṭṭhāsavāro groups them into the standard Abhidhamma categories and asks: which of these *portions* are present?

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I sense any one of these groupings — even just "feeling" as one of the four aggregates — arising in the present moment of listening?*

## 4. Reading for Meaning

### Analyzing by Portion — Aggregates, Bases, Elements, Nutriments, Faculties, and Jhāna

At that same moment, there are **four aggregates**, two sense-bases, two elements, three nutriments, eight faculties, five-factored jhāna, the five-factored path, seven powers, three roots, one contact, one feeling, one perception, one intention, one mind — one feeling-aggregate, one perception-aggregate, one formations-aggregate, one consciousness-aggregate; one mind-base, one mind-faculty, one mind-consciousness-element; one mind-object-base, one mind-object-element; or whatever other co-arisen immaterial phenomena there are at that time — **these phenomena are wholesome.** *(v. 58)*

---

**What at that time are the four aggregates?** The feeling-aggregate, the perception-aggregate, the formations-aggregate, the consciousness-aggregate. *(v. 59)*

**What at that time is the feeling-aggregate?** Whatever at that time is the pleasant mental feeling born of mind-contact — this at that time is the feeling-aggregate. *(v. 60)*

**What at that time is the perception-aggregate?** Whatever at that time is the perceiving, the state of having perceived — this at that time is the perception-aggregate. *(v. 61)*

**What at that time is the formations-aggregate?** Contact, intention, applied thought, sustained thought, rapture, one-pointedness, the faculty of faith, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of life, right view, right thought, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration, the power of faith, the power of energy, the power of mindfulness, the power of concentration, the power of wisdom, the power of moral shame, the power of moral dread, non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion, non-covetousness, non-ill-will, right view, moral shame, moral dread, tranquillity of the mental body, tranquillity of mind, lightness of the mental body, lightness of mind, pliancy of the mental body, pliancy of mind, wieldiness of the mental body, wieldiness of mind, proficiency of the mental body, proficiency of mind, uprightness of the mental body, uprightness of mind, mindfulness, clear comprehension, serenity, insight, effort, non-distraction — or whatever other co-arisen immaterial phenomena there are, excluding the feeling-, perception-, and consciousness-aggregates — this at that time is the formations-aggregate. *(v. 62)*

**What at that time is the consciousness-aggregate?** Whatever at that time is mind, manas, mentality, heart, the pure mind, mind-base, mind-faculty, consciousness, the corresponding mind-element — this at that time is the consciousness-aggregate. *(v. 63)*

---

**What at that time are the two sense-bases?** The mind-base and the mind-object-base. *(v. 64)*

**What is the mind-base?** Whatever at that time is mind, manas, mentality, consciousness — this at that time is the mind-base. *(v. 65)*

**What is the mind-object-base?** The feeling-aggregate, the perception-aggregate, the formations-aggregate — this at that time is the mind-object-base. *(v. 66)*

---

**What at that time are the two elements?** The mind-consciousness-element and the mind-object-element. *(v. 67)*

**What is the mind-consciousness-element?** Whatever at that time is mind, manas, consciousness — this at that time is the mind-consciousness-element. *(v. 68)*

**What is the mind-object-element?** The feeling-aggregate, the perception-aggregate, the formations-aggregate — this at that time is the mind-object-element. *(v. 69)*

---

**What at that time are the three nutriments?** The contact-nutriment, the mental-volition-nutriment, the consciousness-nutriment. *(v. 70)*

**What is the contact-nutriment?** Whatever at that time is contact, the touching — this at that time is the contact-nutriment. *(v. 71)*

**What is the mental-volition-nutriment?** Whatever at that time is intention, the intending — this at that time is the mental-volition-nutriment. *(v. 72)*

**What is the consciousness-nutriment?** Whatever at that time is mind, consciousness — this at that time is the consciousness-nutriment. *(v. 73)*

---

**What at that time are the eight faculties?** The faculty of faith, the faculty of energy, the faculty of mindfulness, the faculty of concentration, the faculty of wisdom, the faculty of mind, the faculty of joy, the faculty of life. *(v. 74)*

*(Verses 75–82 define each faculty in turn, using the same formulas as the Padabhājanī.)*

The faculty of faith is faith, trust, confidence. The faculty of energy is the mental exertion, the striving, the vigor. The faculty of mindfulness is mindfulness, recollection, the holding. The faculty of concentration is the stability, steadiness, non-distraction of mind. The faculty of wisdom is wisdom, knowing, investigation of phenomena, right view. The faculty of mind is mind, consciousness. The faculty of joy is the pleasant mental feeling born of mind-contact. The faculty of life is the life-span of those immaterial phenomena, their duration, their continuance. *(vv. 75–82)*

---

**What at that time is five-factored jhāna?** Applied thought, sustained thought, rapture, pleasure, one-pointedness of mind. *(v. 83)*

**What at that time is applied thought?** Whatever at that time is thinking, applied thought, the directing of the mind onto its object — this at that time is applied thought. *(v. 84)*

**What at that time is sustained thought?** Whatever at that time is exploring, sustained consideration, continuous following with the mind — this at that time is sustained thought. *(v. 85)*

**What at that time is rapture?** Whatever at that time is rapture, delight, rejoicing, the mind's jubilation — this at that time is rapture. *(v. 86)*

**What at that time is pleasure?** Whatever at that time is the pleasant mental feeling born of mind-contact — this at that time is pleasure. *(v. 87)*

**What at that time is one-pointedness of mind?** Whatever at that time is the stability, steadiness, non-distraction of mind, right concentration — this at that time is one-pointedness of mind. **This at that time is five-factored jhāna.** *(v. 88)*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### KHANDHA — Aggregate

**Pronunciation:** KHAN-dha — the "kh" is an aspirated "k"; "dha" rhymes with "duh." Two syllables, first stressed.

**Literal sense:** *Khandha* means heap, mass, or aggregate — a group of phenomena collected under a single category. The Dhammasaṅgaṇī identifies four immaterial aggregates present in any mind-moment: the feeling-aggregate (*vedanākkhandha*), the perception-aggregate (*saññākkhandha*), the formations-aggregate (*saṅkhārakkhandha*), and the consciousness-aggregate (*viññāṇakkhandha*). The physical world (*rūpa*) constitutes the fifth aggregate, but it is not analyzed here since the object of today's passage is the mind-moment itself.

**In the practice:** The *pi-1-practice.md* rail notes that understanding the four aggregates is the antidote to self-view (*sakkāyadiṭṭhi*): when one sees that what we call "I" or "me" is in fact these four heaps — none of which is a self, none of which belongs to a self — the foundation of wrong view begins to dissolve. The Koṭṭhāsavāro teaches this not abstractly but through direct analysis of the very mind-moment that arose just now, in the practice.

**Why it matters:** Every time the word "aggregate" appears in chanting or reading, the Abhidhamma is pointing to the same insight: experience can be fully accounted for without positing a self. This is the analytical heart of *anattānupassanā* — contemplation of non-self.

## 6. Chanting in Pāli

##### Koṭṭhāsavāro

**58.** Tasmiṃ kho pana samaye cattāro khandhā honti,
dvāyatanāni honti, dve dhātuyo honti,
tayo āhārā honti, aṭṭhindriyāni honti,
pañcaṅgikaṃ jhānaṃ hoti, pañcaṅgiko maggo hoti,
satta balāni honti, tayo hetū honti,
eko phasso hoti, ekā vedanā hoti,
ekā saññā hoti, ekā cetanā hoti, ekaṃ cittaṃ hoti,
eko vedanākkhandho hoti, eko saññākkhandho hoti,
eko saṅkhārakkhandho hoti, eko viññāṇakkhandho hoti,
ekaṃ manāyatanaṃ hoti, ekaṃ manindriyaṃ hoti,
ekā manoviññāṇadhātu hoti,
ekaṃ dhammāyatanaṃ hoti, ekā dhammadhātu hoti;
ye vā pana tasmiṃ samaye aññepi atthi
paṭiccasamuppannā arūpino dhammā —
**ime dhammā kusalā.** ^1-58

**59.** Katame tasmiṃ samaye cattāro khandhā honti?
Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho,
saṅkhārakkhandho, viññāṇakkhandho. ^1-59

**60.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye vedanākkhandho hoti?
Yaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cetasikaṃ sātaṃ cetasikaṃ sukhaṃ
cetosamphassajaṃ sātaṃ sukhaṃ vedayitaṃ
cetosamphassajā sātā sukhā vedanā —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye vedanākkhandho hoti.** ^1-60

**61.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye saññākkhandho hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye saññā sañjānanā sañjānitattaṃ —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye saññākkhandho hoti.** ^1-61

**62.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye saṅkhārakkhandho hoti?
Phasso cetanā vitakko vicāro pīti cittassekaggatā
saddhindriyaṃ vīriyindriyaṃ satindriyaṃ samādhindriyaṃ
paññindriyaṃ jīvitindriyaṃ
sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi
saddhābalaṃ vīriyabalaṃ satibalaṃ samādhibalaṃ paññābalaṃ
hiribalaṃ ottappabalaṃ
alobho adoso amoho anabhijjhā abyāpādo sammādiṭṭhi
hirī ottappaṃ
kāyapassaddhi cittapassaddhi
kāyalahutā cittalahutā
kāyamudutā cittamudutā
kāyakammaññatā cittakammaññatā
kāyapāguññatā cittapāguññatā
kāyujukatā cittujukatā
sati sampajaññaṃ samatho vipassanā paggāho avikkhepo;
ye vā pana tasmiṃ samaye aññepi atthi
paṭiccasamuppannā arūpino dhammā
ṭhapetvā vedanākkhandhaṃ ṭhapetvā saññākkhandhaṃ
ṭhapetvā viññāṇakkhandhaṃ —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye saṅkhārakkhandho hoti.** ^1-62

**63.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye viññāṇakkhandho hoti?
Yaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cittaṃ mano mānasaṃ hadayaṃ paṇḍaraṃ
mano manāyatanaṃ manindriyaṃ viññāṇaṃ
viññāṇakkhandho tajjāmanoviññāṇadhātu —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye viññāṇakkhandho hoti.**
Ime tasmiṃ samaye cattāro khandhā honti. ^1-63

**64.** Katamāni tasmiṃ samaye dvāyatanāni honti?
Manāyatanaṃ, dhammāyatanaṃ. ^1-64

**65.** Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye manāyatanaṃ hoti?
Yaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cittaṃ mano mānasaṃ hadayaṃ paṇḍaraṃ
mano manāyatanaṃ manindriyaṃ viññāṇaṃ
viññāṇakkhandho tajjāmanoviññāṇadhātu —
**idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye manāyatanaṃ hoti.** ^1-65

**66.** Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye dhammāyatanaṃ hoti?
Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho —
**idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye dhammāyatanaṃ hoti.**
Imāni tasmiṃ samaye dvāyatanāni honti. ^1-66

**67.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye dve dhātuyo honti?
Manoviññāṇadhātu, dhammadhātu. ^1-67

**68.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye manoviññāṇadhātu hoti?
Yaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cittaṃ mano mānasaṃ hadayaṃ paṇḍaraṃ
mano manāyatanaṃ manindriyaṃ viññāṇaṃ
viññāṇakkhandho tajjāmanoviññāṇadhātu —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye manoviññāṇadhātu hoti.** ^1-68

**69.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye dhammadhātu hoti?
Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye dhammadhātu hoti.**
Imā tasmiṃ samaye dve dhātuyo honti. ^1-69

**70.** Katame tasmiṃ samaye tayo āhārā honti?
Phassāhāro, manosañcetanāhāro, viññāṇāhāro. ^1-70

**71.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye phassāhāro hoti?
Yo tasmiṃ samaye phasso phusanā saṃphusanā saṃphusitattaṃ —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye phassāhāro hoti.** ^1-71

**72.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye manosañcetanāhāro hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye cetanā sañcetanā cetayitattaṃ —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye manosañcetanāhāro hoti.** ^1-72

**73.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye viññāṇāhāro hoti?
Yaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cittaṃ mano mānasaṃ hadayaṃ paṇḍaraṃ
mano manāyatanaṃ manindriyaṃ viññāṇaṃ
viññāṇakkhandho tajjāmanoviññāṇadhātu —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye viññāṇāhāro hoti.**
Ime tasmiṃ samaye tayo āhārā honti. ^1-73

**74.** Katamāni tasmiṃ samaye aṭṭhindriyāni honti?
Saddhindriyaṃ, vīriyindriyaṃ, satindriyaṃ,
samādhindriyaṃ, paññindriyaṃ,
manindriyaṃ, somanassindriyaṃ, jīvitindriyaṃ. ^1-74

**75–82.** *(The faculty of faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, wisdom, mind, joy, and life are defined — each using its Padabhājanī formula.)*
**Imāni tasmiṃ samaye aṭṭhindriyāni honti.** ^1-82

**83.** Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye pañcaṅgikaṃ jhānaṃ hoti?
Vitakko, vicāro, pīti, sukhaṃ, cittassekaggatā. ^1-83

**84.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye vitakko hoti?
Yo tasmiṃ samaye takko vitakko saṅkappo
appanā byappanā cetaso abhiniropanā sammāsaṅkappo —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye vitakko hoti.** ^1-84

**85.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye vicāro hoti?
Yo tasmiṃ samaye cāro vicāro anuvicāro upavicāro
cittassa anusandhānatā anupekkhanatā —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye vicāro hoti.** ^1-85

**86.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye pīti hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye pīti pāmojjaṃ āmodanā pamodanā
hāso pahāso vitti odagyaṃ attamanatā cittassa —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye pīti hoti.** ^1-86

**87.** Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sukhaṃ hoti?
Yaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cetasikaṃ sātaṃ cetasikaṃ sukhaṃ
cetosamphassajaṃ sātaṃ sukhaṃ vedayitaṃ
cetosamphassajā sātā sukhā vedanā —
**idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sukhaṃ hoti.** ^1-87

**88.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye cittassekaggatā hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye cittassa ṭhiti saṇṭhiti avaṭṭhiti
avisāhāro avikkhepo avisāhaṭamānasatā
samatho samādhindriyaṃ samādhibalaṃ sammāsamādhi —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye cittassekaggatā hoti.**
**Idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye pañcaṅgikaṃ jhānaṃ hoti.** ^1-88
