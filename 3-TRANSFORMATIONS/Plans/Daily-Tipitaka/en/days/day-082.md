# Day 82 — Every Angle on One Feeling

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Feeling continues, and today the counting opens right out. A single long verse runs feeling through nearly every set of three in the whole Abhidhamma: is it a result, or productive of result? Clung-to, or not? Stained, or clean? With thinking, or without? To be abandoned by seeing, by developing, or by neither? Small, expanded, or measureless? Learner, adept, or neither? Past, future, present? Inner, outer, or both?

The commentary explains the method behind this: the Buddha took the threes and folded them into the twos, took the twos and folded them into the threes, grew both together, and finally taught by sevens, twenty-fours, thirties, and more. Not showing off — meeting different minds where they were. The day closes on arising: there is feeling arisen, not arisen, and about to arise.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which of these questions could I honestly ask of the feeling I have right now?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### PARIVAṬṬA — Turning (A Pass Through the Material)

**Pronunciation:** *pa-ri-VAṬ-ṭa* — four beats; the "ṭṭ" is a hard, tongue-back "t".

**In today's passage:** *Parivaṭṭa* means a "turning" or revolution — one full pass over the same ground. The commentary says the Buddha analysed the aggregates in three turnings: the discourse way, the Abhidhamma way, and the question way. Today sits deep inside the second, where feeling is turned through set after set.

**Why it matters:** Repetition here is not padding — it is method. You do not understand a feeling by naming it once. You come back, ask another question, and see a face of it you had missed. That is how a list becomes insight: not one clever look, but many patient ones.
