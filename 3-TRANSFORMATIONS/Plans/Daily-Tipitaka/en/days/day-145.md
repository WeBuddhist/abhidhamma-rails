# Day 145 — Owning Our Actions

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today's single verse gives the grounds of knowledge in fours. It opens with a set that sums up the whole path. First comes knowing that one's actions are one's own. Then comes knowledge that fits the truths, then the knowledge of one on the path, and then the knowledge of one who has reached its fruit.

The first of these is *kammassakatā* (the knowledge that action is one's own). It is the simple, grounded view that what I do shapes what I become. The rest of the fours go on to sort wisdom by where it works and what it leads to. But the list starts with owning one's actions on purpose. Every deeper insight rests on this plain footing.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which of my actions today am I most ready to own?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### KAMMASSAKATĀ — Owning One’s Actions

**Pronunciation:** *kam-mas-sa-ka-tā* — five syllables. Say "kam-MAS-sa-ka-taa".

**In today’s passage:** *Kammassakatā* is the knowledge that action is one’s own. Today’s verse puts it first among the fours. The commentary adds that almost all good wisdom short of the path counts as this knowledge, and that countless people have reached *nibbāna* standing on it.

**Why it matters:** Owning your actions is not about blame. It is about power. If what you do shapes what you become, then each choice today matters. That view makes it easier to do a little less harm and a little more good, because you can see that it counts.
