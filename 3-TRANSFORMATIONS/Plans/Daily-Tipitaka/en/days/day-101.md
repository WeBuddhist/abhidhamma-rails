# Day 101 — Go for the Cause

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The third and fourth truths today, and then the chapter starts again from the Abhidhamma side. The ending of suffering (*nirodha*) is defined without mystery: the complete fading of that same craving, giving it up, letting it go, not leaning on it. And it is given a place. Craving ends where it arose — at the eye, the ear, the mind, at whatever in the world is lovely and pleasing.

Then the path, with its eight factors from right view through right concentration. The teachers add two images worth keeping. Cut a bitter gourd vine at the root and the whole vine withers by itself. And: a dog bites the stick thrown at it, while a lion turns to look for who threw it. Other teachers go at the pain; the Buddhas go at the cause.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Am I biting the stick, or looking for who threw it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### NIRODHA — Ending

**Pronunciation:** *ni-ro-dha* — "ni-ROH-dha"; the "dh" is a "d" with breath after it.

**In today's passage:** *Nirodha* is the ending of suffering, the third truth. Today it is defined as the complete fading of craving, and nothing is added to that. Many names are given for the same one thing: letting go, release, not leaning, the ending of greed, of hate, of delusion.

**Why it matters:** Notice what is not said. Nobody is told to end suffering directly. Suffering ends because its cause ends. That is the lion's move — turn and look for who threw the stick. Whatever is hard for you today, the useful question is not how to stop the feeling, but what keeps feeding it.
