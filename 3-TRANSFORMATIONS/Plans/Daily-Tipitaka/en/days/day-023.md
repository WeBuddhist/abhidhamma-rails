# Day 23 — The Greed-Mind

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

For weeks the chant has stayed inside wholesome minds — sense-sphere, fine-material, formless, supramundane. Today it crosses into the other half of the field. From v. 365 begins *Dvādasa akusalāni* — the *Twelve Unwholesome Consciousnesses*. These are the twelve everyday mind-states that move the world toward suffering. Eight are rooted in greed, two in hatred, two in confusion.

V. 365 opens with the first of the twelve. A mind accompanied by joy, joined to wrong view, taking in a sight, a sound, a smell, a taste, a touch, or a thought. The factors inside it look strangely familiar — contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, initial application, sustained application, zest, happiness, one-pointedness. The same factors as a wholesome mind, but joined to greed, delusion, and false view. Verses 366–387 then name each one in turn, ending today at the *power of shamelessness*.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Where in my day does this mind — pleased, sure of itself, and a little bit wrong — show up?*

## 4. Reading for Meaning

**Verses 365–387.** *(Full text rendered in the WeBuddhist app from the verse library; the first and last verses of the range are printed below so the plan-importer can find the right entries.)*

**v. 365.** Which phenomena are unwholesome? When unwholesome consciousness arises, accompanied by joy and associated with wrong view, having a visible object, or a sound object, or a smell object, or a taste object, or a tangible object, or a mental object, or whatever it may be based on, on that occasion there is contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, initial application, sustained application, zest, happiness, one-pointedness of mind; faculty of energy, faculty of concentration, faculty of mind, faculty of joy, life faculty; wrong view, wrong intention, wrong effort, wrong concentration; power of energy, power of concentration, power of shamelessness, power of fearlessness of wrongdoing; greed, delusion, covetousness, wrong view, shamelessness, fearlessness of wrongdoing; calm, exertion, non-distraction; or whatever other immaterial, conditionally arisen phenomena there are on that occasion – these phenomena are unwholesome.

…

**v. 387.** What on that occasion is the power of shamelessness? The absence of conscientious scruple on that occasion when scruples ought to be felt, the absence of conscientious scruple at attaining to bad and unwholesome states — this is the power of shamelessness that there then is.

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### LOBHA — Greed

**Pronunciation:** LO-bha — two syllables; the "bh" is one soft breath. Stress the first.

**In today's passage:** *Lobha* is the first of the three unwholesome roots, and the root of eight of the twelve unwholesome minds. The commentary calls its mark *ārammaṇaggahaṇa* — "grabbing the object." Today's first citta is greed paired with joy and wrong view — a mind that reaches for a pleasing thing and tells itself a story that makes the reaching look right.

**Why it matters:** Greed is not only the big wants. It is the small daily grab — the next bite, the next scroll, the next clever reply. Naming it as a root, not a mood, gives a practitioner somewhere to stand. You don't have to fight every grab. You can watch the root, and let the reach soften on its own.
