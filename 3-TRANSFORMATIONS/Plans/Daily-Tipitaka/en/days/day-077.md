# Day 77 — The Book Concludes

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Today the Dhammasaṅgaṇī reaches its end. The last pairs are gentle ones. First the feeling pairs: which states are carried by happiness (*sukha*)? — the bright, joyful minds, wholesome and unwholesome alike — and then those carried by calm.

The very last sortings lift the eye upward. Which states are unsurpassed (*anuttara*)? The four noble paths, their four fruits, and Nibbāna — nothing stands higher. And which are "with conflict," still stirred by the defilements? The twelve unwholesome minds. Their pair is the conflict-free (*araṇa*): everything at peace, up to Nibbāna itself. On that quiet note the whole book closes — "The treatise Dhammasaṅgaṇī is concluded" — completing the first book of the Abhidhamma and this stage of our journey.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *After all these lists, can I rest the mind for one moment in what is simply conflict-free?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ARAṆA — Conflict-Free (At Peace)

**Pronunciation:** *A-ra-ṇa* — three light beats; the "ṇa" is a soft "n".

**In today's passage:** *Araṇa* means "without conflict" — free of the inner war that the defilements stir up. It is the last note of the book, set against *saraṇa*, the mind "with conflict" (the twelve unwholesome states). Everything not at war belongs here, all the way up to Nibbāna.

**Why it matters:** The whole Dhammasaṅgaṇī has sorted the mind into hundreds of pieces. It ends by pointing past all of them, to a mind at peace with itself. That peace is not far away. Each time greed or anger settles and does not fire back, you have touched a moment of *araṇa* — the very thing the long list was pointing toward.
