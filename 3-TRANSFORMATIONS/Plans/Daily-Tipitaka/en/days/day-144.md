# Day 144 — Grounds of Knowledge

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

A new chapter opens on *ñāṇa* (knowledge). It is laid out like a ladder. First knowledge is described in groups of one, then of two, then of three, and so on up to ten. The second half of the chapter then explains each group in turn.

Today's verses give the first three groups. The first looks closely at the five kinds of sense consciousness — seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting, touching — and lists what they are and are not. The second sorts *paññā* (wisdom) into pairs, such as worldly wisdom and wisdom beyond the world. The third gives threes, like wisdom that comes from thinking things through, from hearing the teaching, and from meditation. The commentary calls each of these a "ground of knowledge", because many good things grow from it.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Where does most of what I understand come from — thinking, hearing, or practice?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ÑĀṆAVATTHU — Ground of Knowledge

**Pronunciation:** *ñā-ṇa-vat-thu* — four syllables. The "ñ" is the "ny" in "canyon". Say "nyaa-na-VAT-thu".

**In today’s passage:** *Ñāṇavatthu* means a ground of knowledge. The commentary explains that knowledge is called a "ground" because many good results grow from it, the way plants grow from soil. Today’s verses give the grounds of knowledge in ones, twos, and threes.

**Why it matters:** It helps to see understanding as soil, not a trophy. You do not win it once and keep it forever. You tend it — by reflecting, by listening, by practising — and good things grow from it. A little tending each day is enough.
