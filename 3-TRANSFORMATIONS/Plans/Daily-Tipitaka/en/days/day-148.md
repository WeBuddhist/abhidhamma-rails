# Day 148 — Three Kinds of Skill

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today's verses explain the groups of three. Wisdom grows in three ways: by thinking things through, by hearing the teaching, and by meditation. It shows up in giving, in keeping precepts, and in meditation. It supports higher virtue, higher mind, and higher wisdom.

One set of three is very practical. *Kosalla* (skill) comes in three kinds. Skill in gain is knowing which ways of paying attention make bad states fade and good states grow. Skill in loss is knowing the ways that do the opposite. Skill in means is knowing what to do in an urgent moment. The rest of the day sorts wisdom by its objects — small or vast, past or future, inside or outside. The thread is the same. Wisdom is a skill you practise, not a fact you hold.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What way of paying attention helps my mind most — and what way harms it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### KOSALLA — Skill

**Pronunciation:** *ko-sal-la* — three syllables. Say "ko-SAL-la".

**In today’s passage:** *Kosalla* means skill. The verses name three: skill in gain, skill in loss, and skill in means. Skill in gain is seeing which ways of attending let bad states fade and good states grow. Skill in means is the right move in an urgent moment.

**Why it matters:** This turns wisdom into something you can practise today. Watch what happens when you attend to things a certain way. If worry grows, that is a lesson. If calm grows, that is one too. Learning your own mind like this means a little less harm and a little more good.
