# Day 30 — The Fruit of the Path

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Today the resultant (*vipāka*) minds climb to their peak. So far the results have been ordinary — bare senses, receiving, investigating. Now they rise.

Verses 499–500 give the results of fine-material jhāna (*rūpāvacara*) — the mind reborn in a meditation heaven, the fruit of deep focus. Verses 501–504 give the results of formless jhāna (*arūpāvacara*), higher still. The commentary uses a simple image: as a shadow matches the shape that casts it, each result mirrors the very mind that made it. Then, from verse 505, the chant reaches the highest result of all — *lokuttara*, the supramundane (above the world). This is the *fruit* mind of the first path: the instant of release that follows seeing Nibbāna and dropping wrong view for good. The long list of results ends on its summit.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What might the quiet I build in practice be quietly building in return?*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### LOKUTTARA — Supramundane (above the world)

**Pronunciation:** lo-kut-TA-ra — four syllables. Stress the third; "lok-" rhymes with "oak."

**In today's passage:** *Lokuttara* means *supramundane* — literally "above the world." It names the highest result in today's chant: the *fruit* mind of the first path. The commentary explains that ordinary good kamma feeds the round of rebirth, but the supramundane mind does the opposite — it *unwinds* that round. Its result follows the path-mind at once, like a fruit appearing the instant the flower opens.

**Why it matters:** The whole long list of results has been climbing toward this — release. We may be far from it, yet the chant shows the direction the path actually runs: not toward more, but toward letting go. Even a small loosening of a grip today leans the same way the path leans.
