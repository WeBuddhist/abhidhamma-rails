# Day 147 — Just Seeing

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today's verses finish the look at the five kinds of sense consciousness. They make a striking point. Seeing, by itself, does not know anything as good or bad. It does not choose, move, or judge. It is only the bare touch of an object. All the liking, disliking, and deciding happens a moment later, in the mind.

Then the verses begin the group of two. They sort *paññā* (wisdom) into pairs: worldly wisdom and wisdom beyond the world, wisdom with taints and wisdom without them, and many more. The whole day points one way. The gap between seeing and reacting is small, but it is real. That gap is where a wiser choice can begin.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I catch the moment between seeing something and reacting to it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### PAÑÑĀ — Wisdom

**Pronunciation:** *pañ-ñā* — two syllables. The "ññ" is a soft "ny", as in "canyon". Say "PUN-nyaa".

**In today’s passage:** *Paññā* is wisdom — the mind’s power to see things as they are. Today the verses begin sorting it in pairs: worldly wisdom and wisdom beyond the world, wisdom with taints and without. Just before that, they show how little plain sense-seeing knows on its own.

**Why it matters:** If seeing alone does not judge, then much of our upset is added later. Wisdom is noticing that step. You do not have to stop feeling. You only have to see the moment a reaction is being built, and choose not to build it higher.
