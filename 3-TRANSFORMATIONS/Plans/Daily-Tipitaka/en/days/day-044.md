# Day 44 — The Life Faculty

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The walk through *derived material form* (matter built on the four elements) goes on. After the tangible, the text reaches three faculties hidden in the body: the faculty of being female, the faculty of being male, and the life faculty (*jīvitindriya*). These are not a self. They are bits of matter, made from causes, that shape the body and keep it alive.

Then the text sorts matter by whether it is the *base* for each kind of sense-knowing. The lesson underneath stays the same. Every part of the body is borrowed and made from causes. Life itself is a faculty that must be fed and renewed — not a thing you simply own.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What keeps this body alive — and is any of it truly under my control?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### JĪVITINDRIYA — The Life Faculty

**Pronunciation:** *JEE-vi-tin-dri-ya* — "j" as in "jam," the "ī" held long.

**In today's passage:** *Jīvitindriya* is the life faculty. It is the force that keeps the body alive and holds it together while it lasts. The text lists it as one more kind of matter — part of the body, not a self behind it.

**Why it matters:** Life is a faculty, not a thing you own. It must be fed and renewed, moment by moment. When you see this, the belief "my life is mine" grows lighter. Life is on loan, and it is short. That truth is not grim. It gently asks you to use the time well — a little less harm, a little more good.
