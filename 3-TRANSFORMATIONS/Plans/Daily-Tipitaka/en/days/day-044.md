# Day 44 — The Life Faculty

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The walk through *derived material form* continues. After the tangible, the text reaches three faculties hidden in the body: the faculty of femininity, the faculty of masculinity, and the life faculty (*jīvitindriya*). These are not a "self." They are conditioned bits of matter that shape the body and keep it alive moment to moment.

From there the text begins sorting matter by whether it serves as the *basis* for each kind of sense-awareness. The teaching underneath stays the same. Every part of the body is borrowed, conditioned, and kept going only by causes. Life itself is a faculty that must be fed and renewed — not a possession that is simply "mine."

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What keeps this body alive — and is any of it truly under my control?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### JĪVITINDRIYA — The Life Faculty

**Pronunciation:** *JEE-vi-tin-dri-ya* — "j" as in "jam," the "ī" held long.

**In today's passage:** Among the derived kinds of matter is *jīvitindriya*, the life faculty — the force that keeps the body's matter alive and holds it together for as long as it lasts. It sits in the list beside the faculties of femininity and masculinity, as part of the body, not behind it.

**Why it matters:** Seeing life as a *faculty* — a conditioned thing that must be fed and renewed — loosens the deep sense that "I" simply own my life. Life is borrowed and fragile, kept up by causes moment to moment. Resting in that truth brings a gentle urgency to use this short life well: a little less harm, a little more good.
