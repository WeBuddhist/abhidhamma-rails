# Day 167 — Where Greed Cannot Reach

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Yesterday's map climbs higher today. In the immaterial element (*arūpadhātu*, the formless world), there is no material form at all. Only four aggregates remain, with one kind of mind. Then come the things not included (*apariyāpanna*) in any world. There are only six roots here, and not one of them is unwholesome.

Next the verses ask a new question of each group. Is it included in the sense-pleasure element, or not? Material form always is. So are the three unwholesome roots: greed, hatred, and delusion. The commentary explains the word. "Included" means held inside a world, by birth and by place. "Not included" means not held inside any world.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Where in my day am I still held by greed, and where am I free of it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### APARIYĀPANNA — Not Included

**Pronunciation:** *a-pa-ri-yā-pan-na* — six syllables. Say "a-pa-ri-YAA-pan-na".

**In today’s passage:** *Pariyāpanna* means included — held inside a world. *Apariyāpanna* is its opposite: not included in any world. Today it covers the path (*magga*) and the ending (*nirodha*) of suffering. Wholesome roots are found there, but no unwholesome root at all.

**Why it matters:** Even the high formless worlds still carry greed and delusion with them. A better birth is not yet freedom. So the teaching points to what no world includes: the path that greed cannot enter. Each time you let go of a grasping thought, you step that way.
