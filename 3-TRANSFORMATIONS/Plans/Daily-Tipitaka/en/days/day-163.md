# Day 163 — Gain and Loss

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today begins with the eight *lokadhamma*, worldly conditions: gain and loss, fame and disrepute, praise and blame, happiness and pain. The mind is struck when it grabs the good ones and pushes against the bad ones. The commentary says no one is free of them — even Buddhas meet them.

Next come eight faults of a monk who is charged with a wrong. He says "I don't remember," attacks the one who charged him, changes the subject, or threatens to quit. Then come the nines: nine grounds of resentment and nine stains of a person. Nine things grow from craving, one after another: seeking, gain, deciding, desire, holding on, owning, stinginess, guarding, and at last fights and lies. The nines end with "I am," which the verses call an agitation.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which of the eight worldly conditions shakes me most?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### LOKADHAMMA — Worldly Condition

**Pronunciation:** *lo-ka-dham-ma* — four syllables. Say "LO-ka-DHAM-ma".

**In today’s passage:** A *lokadhamma* is a worldly condition — one of eight that come to everyone: gain and loss, fame and disrepute, praise and blame, happiness and pain. The verse says the harm is not in the conditions themselves. It is the passion at gain and the push against loss that strike the mind.

**Why it matters:** We cannot stop praise or blame from coming. We can change how hard they hit. Remember that these eight come and go for everyone, even the Buddha. When praise comes, enjoy it lightly. When blame comes, you can still stay steady.
