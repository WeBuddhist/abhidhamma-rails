# Day 34 — The Great Fire

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Today the chant stays inside one section and lets it unfold. Aspiration still leads; the four modes of progress still sort the work; the three names — emptiness, signless, undirected — keep passing from path to fruit across all the jhānas. Counted out, the combinations run into the thousands.

The commentary gives this day its picture. Ordinary good kamma is like a small fire: by the time its result ripens — somewhere else, sometime later — the warmth is gone. The supramundane is a great mass of fire: the fruit arises at once, while wisdom still blazes. That is why this fruit alone can keep its leader's name.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What in my practice is a small fire — and what could become a great one?*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### PHALA — Fruit

**Pronunciation:** PHA-la — two syllables. "ph" is a breathy "p," not an "f."

**In today's passage:** *Phala* is the fruit: the resultant mind that follows the path-moment with nothing in between. Today's verses show that fruit keeping its path's shape — its mode of progress, its name, its leader — the way a fruit keeps the flavor of its tree.

**Why it matters:** Everything we do is planting. Most fruit comes later, and we do not choose when. But we choose the planting. Each kind act today is a seed already leaning toward its fruit — and the chant says the best fruit of all comes quickest.
