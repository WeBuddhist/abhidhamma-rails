# Day 129 — Right View Leads

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

When the path arises in a single moment of deep insight, it can appear as five factors rather than eight — right view, right intention, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration. The factors of speech, action, and livelihood have already done their work by keeping conduct clean, so here they rest.

Today's verses also ask a sharp question: among the factors, which one is a *root*? The answer is right view. Right view — seeing clearly what leads to suffering and what leads out of it — is the root the other factors grow from and lean on. Get the seeing right, and right intention, effort, mindfulness, and concentration all have something true to serve.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Where is my seeing clear, and where am I still guessing?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAMMĀDIṬṬHI — Right View

**Pronunciation:** *sam-mā-diṭ-ṭhi* — "diṭṭhi" rhymes roughly with "kitty".

**In today’s passage:** *Sammādiṭṭhi* is right view — knowing suffering, its cause, its ending, and the way to its ending. Today’s text calls it a *root*: the factor the whole path grows from. It is also named "the awakening factor of investigation of things", so right view is active looking, not just belief.

**Why it matters:** Right view is where practice starts and where it steadies. When you can see honestly what harms and what helps, good choices get easier and forcing yourself gets rarer. A little more clear seeing today is a little less harm tomorrow.
