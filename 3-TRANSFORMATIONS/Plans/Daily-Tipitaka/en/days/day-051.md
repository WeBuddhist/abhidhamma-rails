# Day 51 — Born of Kamma

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

A new section opens: the fourfold detailed sorting (*Catukkaniddeso*). It groups matter four ways at once — for example, whether it is derived, grasped, and produced by past action (*kamma*). The opening verse is striking. Much of the body — the senses, the faculties of sex and life, and more — is "produced by kamma." It is the bodily fruit of choices made long ago.

So the body you carry is, in part, a result you inherited from your own past deeds. This is not for blame but for clarity. If past action shaped this body, then present action is shaping what comes next — so today's kindness and restraint matter.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *If past deeds shaped this body, what am I shaping now?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### KAMMAJA — Born of Kamma

**Pronunciation:** *KAM-ma-ja* — "j" as in "jam."

**In today's passage:** The fourfold sorting marks out matter that is *kammassa katattā*, "produced by kamma" — born of past action. The senses, the faculties of sex and life, and certain other matter arise as the bodily fruit of earlier deeds.

**Why it matters:** Seeing part of the body as *kammaja* — a result you inherited — changes how you hold it. It is not a random possession, and not fully under your control; it is an effect with causes. That view cools both pride and blame. And it points forward: present deeds are seeding the future, so a little less harm and a little more good today is never wasted.
