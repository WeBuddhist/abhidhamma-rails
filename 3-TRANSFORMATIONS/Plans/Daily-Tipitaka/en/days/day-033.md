# Day 33 — When Aspiration Leads

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

A new thread enters the weave: predominance (*adhipati*) — a mind led by one strong factor. Today's verses take aspiration (*chanda*), the wish to act, and set it at the head of the supramundane path.

Then the chant asks a fine question: can a fruit inherit its path's leader? For ordinary good kamma, no — it ripens later, in another moment or another life, so its leadership lapses. But the supramundane fruit follows its path at once, while faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom are still warm. So a path led by aspiration yields a fruit led by aspiration. What leads the cause can lead the result — when nothing falls between them.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is leading my mind right now — and where is it leading?*

**v. 529.** Which phenomena are indeterminate? When one cultivates supramundane jhána, leading to liberation, tending to the decrease (of defilements), for the abandonment of wrong views, for the attainment of the first stage, having quite secluded from sensual pleasures... he enters and abides in the first jhána, with difficult practice, slow comprehension, and with dominance of desire, at that time there is contact... non-distraction... these phenomena are wholesome. Due to the doing and developing of that same supramundane wholesome jhána, its resultant, having quite secluded from sensual pleasures... he enters and abides in the first jhána, with difficult practice, slow comprehension, as emptiness with dominance of desire, at that time there is contact... non-distraction... these phenomena are indeterminate.

**v. 539.** Which states are indeterminate? When one cultivates supramundane jhāna, leading to liberation, leading to cessation of accumulation, for the abandonment of wrong views, for the attainment of the first stage of awakening, secluded from sensual pleasures... the first jhāna, entering and abiding with difficult practice, slow direct knowledge, emptiness, dominated by desire, at that time, contact... non-distraction... these states are wholesome. As the resultant of that very supramundane wholesome jhāna having been done and developed, secluded from sensual pleasures... the first jhāna, entering and abiding with difficult practice, slow direct knowledge, signless, dominated by desire, at that time, contact... non-distraction... these states are indeterminate.

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### CHANDA — Aspiration

**Pronunciation:** CHAN-da — two syllables. "chan" as in "chant."

**In today's passage:** *Chanda* is the wish to act — here rendered aspiration, the first of four factors that can lead the path (*adhipati*, predominance). Today's verses pair it with emptiness: a path with aspiration at its head gives a fruit with aspiration at its head.

**Why it matters:** Wanting is not always the enemy. Craving binds, but the wish to be free is itself a strength the path can ride. Notice what you want today. Wanting can be pointed — toward less harm, more good, a clearer mind.
