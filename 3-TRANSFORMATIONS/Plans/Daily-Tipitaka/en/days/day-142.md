# Day 142 — Seeing Results Clearly

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today's verses stay in one long section on *vipāka* (results). These are states of mind that arise because of past actions — like the simple act of seeing, hearing, or feeling that follows from good or bad deeds done before. One by one the text walks through these results, from the five senses up to the calm of meditation and the fruits of the path.

A pattern repeats. For results, only three of the four knowledges apply. The knowledge of principles drops out, because a result is counted as meaning, not as a cause. It is simply what has come to be. Seeing this clearly is its own kind of wisdom. Much of what happens to you is the ripening of what was done before. You can meet it without adding blame or panic.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I meet what is happening now as a result, and put my effort into what I do next?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ATTHA — Meaning

**Pronunciation:** *at-tha* — two syllables. The "th" is a "t" with a breath, not the "th" in "thin".

**In today’s passage:** *Attha* here means meaning in a special sense: the result, what follows from a cause. The commentary lists five kinds — anything that arises from conditions, *nibbāna*, the sense of what is said, the results of action, and functional states. Today’s verses treat knowing results as knowing meaning.

**Why it matters:** Seeing results as results changes how you meet hard days. The pain or pleasure arriving now grew from causes already planted. You cannot undo those, but you can choose what you plant next. That shift, from blaming to understanding, is a quiet kind of wisdom.
