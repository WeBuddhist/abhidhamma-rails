# Day 172 — Known Only by the Mind

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today the verses sort the twelve groups in new ways. First: what can be seen, heard, sensed, or cognized? Sights are seen. Sounds are heard. Smells, tastes, and touches are sensed. But feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness are never seen, heard, or sensed. They are only cognized, by the mind.

Then come three old questions from the start of the Abhidhamma. Is each thing wholesome (*kusala*), unwholesome (*akusala*), or indeterminate (*abyākata*, neither wholesome nor unwholesome)? Does it come with pleasant, painful, or neutral feeling? Is it a result (*vipāka*), a cause of results, or neither? The commentary says all of this was explained earlier, in the question sections.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What in my mind right now can only I see — and is it wholesome?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VIÑÑĀTA — Cognized

**Pronunciation:** *viñ-ñā-ta* — three syllables; "ññ" is like the "ny" in "canyon", held a moment. Say "vin-NYAA-ta".

**In today’s passage:** *Viññāta* means cognized — known by the mind. It is the last of four words: seen, heard, sensed, cognized. Everything in the twelve groups can be cognized. But feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness can only be cognized. No eye or ear can reach them.

**Why it matters:** No one else can see your anger or your kindness directly. Only you can know them from the inside. So the practice starts at home. Look inward and notice what is there. A mind that knows itself does less harm.
