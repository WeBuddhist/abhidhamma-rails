# Day 10 — The Path Within Every Wholesome Moment

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Yesterday's Koṭṭhāsavāro worked through aggregates, bases, elements, nutriments, faculties, and jhāna. Today we complete it, with verses 89 through 120 — the five-factored path (*maggo*), the seven powers (*balāni*), the three wholesome roots (*hetū*), and then the re-analysis of each individual factor in its "one" form: one contact, one feeling, one perception, one intention, one mind, and so on through one mind-consciousness-element and one mind-object-element.

The centerpiece today is the five-factored path embedded within the first wholesome mind. Right view, right thought, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration — these are not something reserved for meditators on retreat. The Dhammasaṅgaṇī shows that they are structurally present in every moment of sense-sphere wholesome consciousness. You do not need to be in jhāna to have the path factors present; they arise whenever a genuinely wholesome mind-moment arises.

The passage closes at verse 120 with the formal seal: "Koṭṭhāsavāro" — the Analysis by Portion is complete.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Right view, right thought, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration — can I sense any trace of these in the mind that is chanting right now?*

## 4. Reading for Meaning

### The Path, the Powers, and the Roots

**What at that time is the five-factored path?** Right view, right thought, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration. *(v. 89)*

**What at that time is right view?** Whatever at that time is wisdom, knowing, inquiry, investigation of phenomena, discernment — the light of wisdom, the jewel of wisdom, non-delusion, right view — this at that time is right view. *(v. 90)*

**What at that time is right thought?** Whatever at that time is thinking, applied thought, intention, the directing of the mind onto its object, right thought — this at that time is right thought. *(v. 91)*

**What at that time is right effort?** Whatever at that time is the mental exertion of energy, the setting forth, the striving, the vigor, the strength, the robustness, right effort — this at that time is right effort. *(v. 92)*

**What at that time is right mindfulness?** Whatever at that time is mindfulness, recollection, the holding, the not floating away, the not forgetting, right mindfulness — this at that time is right mindfulness. *(v. 93)*

**What at that time is right concentration?** Whatever at that time is the stability, steadiness, settling, non-distraction of mind, right concentration — this at that time is right concentration. **This at that time is the five-factored path.** *(v. 94)*

---

**What at that time are the seven powers?** The power of faith, the power of energy, the power of mindfulness, the power of concentration, the power of wisdom, the power of moral shame, the power of moral dread. *(v. 95)*

*(Verses 96–102 define each power using its Padabhājanī formula.)*

The power of faith is faith, trust, confidence. The power of energy is the striving, the vigor. The power of mindfulness is mindfulness, the holding, the not forgetting. The power of concentration is the stability, non-distraction of mind. The power of wisdom is wisdom, investigation of phenomena, right view. The power of moral shame is the shrinking back from the attainment of bad, unwholesome phenomena. The power of moral dread is the dreading of the attainment of bad, unwholesome phenomena. **These at that time are the seven powers.** *(vv. 96–102)*

---

**What at that time are the three roots?** Non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion. *(v. 103)*

**What at that time is non-greed?** Whatever at that time is non-greed, the not-grasping, non-attachment, non-covetousness, non-greed as a wholesome root — this at that time is non-greed. *(v. 104)*

**What at that time is non-hatred?** Whatever at that time is non-hatred, the not-hating, non-ill-will, non-hatred as a wholesome root — this at that time is non-hatred. *(v. 105)*

**What at that time is non-delusion?** Whatever at that time is wisdom — investigation of phenomena, right view, non-delusion as a wholesome root — this at that time is non-delusion. **These at that time are the three roots.** *(v. 106)*

---

**What at that time is the one contact?** Whatever at that time is contact, the touching, the state of being touched — this at that time is the one contact. *(v. 107)*

**What at that time is the one feeling?** The pleasant mental feeling born of mind-contact — this at that time is the one feeling. *(v. 108)*

**What at that time is the one perception?** The perceiving, the state of having perceived — this at that time is the one perception. *(v. 109)*

**What at that time is the one intention?** The intending, the state of having intended — this at that time is the one intention. *(v. 110)*

**What at that time is the one mind?** Whatever at that time is mind, manas, mentality, consciousness — this at that time is the one mind. *(v. 111)*

**What at that time is the one feeling-aggregate?** The pleasant mental feeling born of mind-contact — this at that time is the one feeling-aggregate. *(v. 112)*

**What at that time is the one perception-aggregate?** The perceiving, the state of having perceived — this at that time is the one perception-aggregate. *(v. 113)*

**What at that time is the one formations-aggregate?** Contact, intention, applied thought, sustained thought, rapture, one-pointedness — and all the other co-arisen immaterial phenomena, excluding the feeling-, perception-, and consciousness-aggregates — this at that time is the one formations-aggregate. *(v. 114)*

**What at that time is the one consciousness-aggregate?** Mind, manas, mentality, consciousness — this at that time is the one consciousness-aggregate. *(v. 115)*

**What at that time is the one mind-base?** Mind, manas, consciousness — this at that time is the one mind-base. *(v. 116)*

**What at that time is the one mind-faculty?** Mind, consciousness — this at that time is the one mind-faculty. *(v. 117)*

**What at that time is the one mind-consciousness-element?** Mind, consciousness — this at that time is the one mind-consciousness-element. *(v. 118)*

**What at that time is the one mind-object-base?** The feeling-aggregate, the perception-aggregate, the formations-aggregate — this at that time is the one mind-object-base. *(v. 119)*

**What at that time is the one mind-object-element?** The feeling-aggregate, the perception-aggregate, the formations-aggregate — this at that time is the one mind-object-element. Or whatever other co-arisen immaterial phenomena there are — **these phenomena are wholesome.**

**Koṭṭhāsavāro.** *(v. 120)*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### SAMMĀDIṬṬHI — Right View

**Pronunciation:** SAM-mā-diṭ-ṭhi — four syllables; the double "ṭṭh" is a retroflex stop, held a beat. Stress on the first syllable of each compound element: SAM-mā, DIṬṬ-hi.

**Literal sense:** *Sammā* means "right, correct, complete, in accordance with truth." *Diṭṭhi* means "view, seeing, the way one looks at things." Together: right view — the correct seeing of things as they are. The Dhammasaṅgaṇī equates sammādiṭṭhi with the full list of wisdom-synonyms: *paññā pajānanā vicayo pavicayo dhammavicayo… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi* — wisdom, knowing, investigation of phenomena, non-delusion. It is not a separate faculty added on top of wisdom; it *is* wisdom, named from the angle of correctness.

**In today's passage:** The five-factored path appears as one of the koṭṭhāsā — the portions — present within the first wholesome mind. Sammādiṭṭhi is the first of the five path-factors, and in the Abhidhamma analysis it shares its definition entirely with *paññindriyaṃ* (the faculty of wisdom) and *paññābalaṃ* (the power of wisdom) and *amoha* (non-delusion). These are not four different things; they are one reality named from four different angles.

**In the practice:** The *pi-1-practice.md* rail notes that understanding the Koṭṭhāsavāro's teaching on the five-factored path leads to a profound recognition: the Noble Eightfold Path is not something we do after developing the mind; it is present in every moment the mind is genuinely wholesome. Cultivating sammādiṭṭhi means cultivating the conditions for these path-factors to arise naturally.

## 6. Chanting in Pāli

##### Koṭṭhāsavāro *(continued)*

**89.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye pañcaṅgiko maggo hoti?
Sammādiṭṭhi, sammāsaṅkappo,
sammāvāyāmo, sammāsati, sammāsamādhi. ^1-89

**90.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye sammādiṭṭhi hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye paññā pajānanā vicayo pavicayo
dhammavicayo sallakkhaṇā upalakkhaṇā paccupalakkhaṇā
paṇḍiccaṃ kosallaṃ nepuññaṃ vebhabyā cintā upaparikkhā
bhūrī medhā pariṇāyikā vipassanā sampajaññaṃ patodo
paññā paññindriyaṃ paññābalaṃ paññāsatthaṃ
paññāpāsādo paññāāloko paññāobhāso
paññāpajjoto paññāratanaṃ
amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammādiṭṭhi hoti.** ^1-90

**91.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye sammāsaṅkappo hoti?
Yo tasmiṃ samaye takko vitakko saṅkappo
appanā byappanā cetaso abhiniropanā sammāsaṅkappo —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammāsaṅkappo hoti.** ^1-91

**92.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye sammāvāyāmo hoti?
Yo tasmiṃ samaye cetasiko vīriyārambho
nikkamo parakkamo uyyāmo vāyāmo ussāho ussoḷhī
thāmo dhiti asithilaparakkamatā anikkhittachandatā
anikkhittadhuratā dhurasampaggāho
vīriyaṃ vīriyindriyaṃ vīriyabalaṃ sammāvāyāmo —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammāvāyāmo hoti.** ^1-92

**93.** Katamā tasmiṃ samaye sammāsati hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye sati anussati paṭissati sati
saraṇatā dhāraṇatā apilāpanatā asammussanatā
sati satindriyaṃ satibalaṃ sammāsati —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammāsati hoti.** ^1-93

**94.** Katamo tasmiṃ samaye sammāsamādhi hoti?
Yā tasmiṃ samaye cittassa ṭhiti saṇṭhiti avaṭṭhiti
avisāhāro avikkhepo avisāhaṭamānasatā
samatho samādhindriyaṃ samādhibalaṃ sammāsamādhi —
**ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye sammāsamādhi hoti.**
**Ayaṃ tasmiṃ samaye pañcaṅgiko maggo hoti.** ^1-94

**95.** Katamāni tasmiṃ samaye satta balāni honti?
Saddhābalaṃ, vīriyabalaṃ, satibalaṃ,
samādhibalaṃ, paññābalaṃ,
hiribalaṃ, ottappabalaṃ. ^1-95

**102.** Katamaṃ tasmiṃ samaye ottappabalaṃ hoti?
