# Day 168 — What Appears at Birth

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The lists of what is included end today. Then a new section asks: at the moment of rebirth (*upapatti*) in the sense-pleasure element, what appears?

Everyone born there gets all five aggregates at once. But the bases differ. Gods and the first humans of a world-age may start with eleven. The sound base is never there at the first moment. A being born in the womb starts with only seven. It has no eye, ear, nose, or tongue base at that moment. Then the verses count the faculties (*indriya*): fourteen for a god born with knowledge, down to four for some beings born without roots. One thing is the same for everyone. Only one truth appears at birth: the truth of suffering (*dukkha*).

Today's question to carry into the chant: *If every birth brings suffering, can I meet today's small pains with patience?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### UPAPATTI — Rebirth

**Pronunciation:** *u-pa-pat-ti* — four syllables; hold the double "tt" a little. Say "u-pa-PAT-ti".

**In today’s passage:** *Upapatti* is rebirth — arising in a new life. The verses look at the very first moment of a life and list what appears in it. The list changes from being to being. The truth that appears never changes: it is suffering.

**Why it matters:** The teaching asks us to see this clearly and not to take delight in rebirth. No birth comes without aging, tiredness, or worry. Seeing that, you can hold your own troubles more lightly. You can hold other people's troubles more gently too.
