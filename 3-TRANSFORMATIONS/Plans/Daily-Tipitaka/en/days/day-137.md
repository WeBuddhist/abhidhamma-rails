# Day 137 — Measureless, and Steady

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today's verses complete the four measureless states with *upekkhā* (equanimity). This is a mind that meets all beings evenly. It wishes them neither harm nor special favour. The passage then defines loving-kindness once more.

The chapter turns next to the Abhidhamma's analysis. Here the same states are shown as *kusala* (wholesome) states of mind. They arise within jhāna and are aimed at beings. What began as a warm wish is now mapped as an exact quality you can train. The pairing matters. Heart and clarity are not rivals. Care without balance burns out; balance without care goes cold. Held together, these states have no natural ceiling — which is part of why they are called measureless.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I care deeply about someone and still hold them with an even, unclutching heart?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### APPAMAÑÑĀ — Measureless State

**Pronunciation:** *ap-pa-mañ-ñā* — "ññ" is a soft "ny", as in "canyon".

**In today’s passage:** *Appamaññā* means a "measureless" state of heart. The text names four — loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity. Today it closes the set with equanimity. Then it shows them as wholesome states of mind.

**Why it matters:** These states are measureless because they set no limit on who deserves good will. That is their power against an old habit of the mind — drawing lines between us and them, worthy and unworthy. Widen the circle even a little, and that habit loosens. A less divided heart is a lighter one to carry.
