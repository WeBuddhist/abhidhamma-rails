# Day 88 — Still in Training

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Formations finish today. The verses run the heap through its last sets, and one of them names where you stand: a state can belong to a learner (*sekha*), to an adept (*asekha*), or to neither. A learner is anyone still training — still working on conduct, mind and wisdom. An adept has finished that work.

Most of us are in the first group, and the text treats that as a real, named position, not a shortfall. Being a learner is a status in the system, with its own states of mind. The day closes plainly: "This is called the aggregate of formations." Four of the five heaps have now been turned in this second pass. Only consciousness is left.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What am I actually in training for, today?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SEKHA — Learner

**Pronunciation:** *SE-kha* — two beats; the "kh" is a breathy "k".

**In today's passage:** *Sekha* means "learner" — one still in training. Its partner is *asekha*, the adept, who has nothing left to train. Today's verses sort formations by which of the two they belong to, or neither. The training meant here is the familiar three: conduct, mind, and wisdom.

**Why it matters:** "Learner" is not a polite word for beginner. It is a position on the path, and it holds everyone from the first day to the last step before the end. That is worth resting in. You are not waiting to start being a practitioner — being in training *is* the practice.
