# Day 56 — Abandoned by Development

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The laying-out continues. Yesterday named what is "abandoned by seeing" — the fetters cut in a flash at the first awakening. Today names what is "abandoned by development" (*bhāvanāya pahātabbā*): the greed, hate, and delusion that remain. These are not removed in one stroke. They are worn down slowly, by long practice, stage after stage.

It is an honest and kind teaching. Most of the work is gradual. Then the chapter sorts states another way — by whether their object lies in the past, the future, or the present. The lesson under both: defilement is not one solid enemy but many states, each with its own conditions, each able to be known and, in time, released.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I meet what remains in me with patience — knowing it is worn away slowly, not all at once?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### PAHĀTABBA — What Is To Be Abandoned

**Pronunciation:** *pa-HĀ-tab-ba* — "ā" held long.

**In today's passage:** The chapter splits the defilements by *how* they go. Some are *dassanena pahātabba* — "to be let go by seeing," cut at the first insight. The rest are *bhāvanāya pahātabba* — "to be let go by development," worn away by long practice of the path.

**Why it matters:** This sets an honest pace. One insight does not end all craving and anger. Most of it fades bit by bit, through patient effort. Knowing this guards you from two traps: thinking one breakthrough ends the work, and losing heart when old habits return. You just keep walking the path, and what is "let go by development" slowly thins.
