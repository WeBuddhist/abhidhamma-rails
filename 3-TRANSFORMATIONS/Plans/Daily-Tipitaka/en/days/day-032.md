# Day 32 — Twenty Great Methods

## 1. Today's Chanting Guide

Today the second door opens. Yesterday the fruit took its names from emptiness. Now the undirected (*appaṇihita*) — the mind that has let go of craving's aims — does the same work. It passes the three names to its fruit, one mode of progress at a time.

Then verse 528 opens out into the Twenty Great Methods. One supramundane moment (a mind above the world) is called by twenty names: jhāna (deep meditative focus), path, mindfulness, right effort, faculty, power, awakening factor, truth, calm, and more. Not twenty things — one act of growing the mind, doing twenty jobs at once. The chant shows how much work one moment of awakening does.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What if one good act today were allowed to do many jobs?*

## 5. Pāli Word of the Day

### BHĀVANĀ — Cultivation

**Pronunciation:** bhaa-va-NAA — three syllables. "bh" is a breathy "b."

**In today's passage:** Every verse turns on one verb: *bhāveti*, "one cultivates." *Bhāvanā* is that work — growing the mind the way a farmer grows a field. The Twenty Great Methods show what one moment of supramundane cultivation contains: it is jhāna, path, mindfulness, power, calm — twenty names, one seed.

**Why it matters:** Minds are grown, not forced. No farmer shouts at a seed; they water it daily. A little less harm, a little more good, a little more knowing of our own mind — that daily watering is bhāvanā, and today's chant is part of it.
