# Day 83 — Perception Takes Its Turn

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Feeling finishes today, and perception (*saññā*) steps into the same machine. The last feeling verses sort it by time — past, future, present — closing a long stretch that has turned one aggregate over and over.

Then the text starts again from the top, with a new subject. "As a single category: perception is associated with contact." The same counts follow: by root, by kind, by plane, by faculty, by door. If it feels familiar, that is the point. The commentary says once you have the method for one aggregate, the others come with it — the insight you win on feeling is meant to be carried straight across. So today is less about new information than about doing the same seeing on a different part of yourself.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I look at how I label things as plainly as I looked at how they feel?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAÑÑĀ — Perception

**Pronunciation:** *SAÑ-ñā* — the "ññ" is like the "ny" in "canyon".

**In today's passage:** *Saññā* means "perception" — the mind's act of recognising and labelling what it meets. Today it takes over from feeling as the subject of the analysis. Like feeling, it is counted first as one thing: perception comes with contact. Nothing is perceived without something touching a sense door.

**Why it matters:** Perception is where a bare sight becomes "my car", "her tone", "a threat". The label arrives so fast it feels like part of the object. Watching perception work — seeing the naming happen — puts a small gap between what is there and the story about it. That gap is where choice lives.
