# Day 171 — No Becoming Lasts

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The life spans reach their peak today. Gods of the formless worlds live twenty, forty, sixty, and at last eighty-four thousand eons (*kappa*, world-ages). Then a short verse stops the counting. Lifted by merit, beings may reach even the peak of existence. Yet when their life runs out, they fall back to a bad destination. No becoming is permanent. So the wise develop the highest path, for release from aging and death.

Next the verses ask what to do with each group. Every one is to be known and fully understood. The origin (*samudaya*) of suffering is to be abandoned. The path is to be developed. The ending of suffering is to be realised. A last question asks which things take an object (*ārammaṇa*). Material form never does. The four mental aggregates always do.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *If no becoming lasts, what am I building my happiness on?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### BHAVA — Becoming

**Pronunciation:** *bha-va* — two syllables; "bh" is a "b" with a puff of breath. Say "BHA-va".

**In today’s passage:** *Bhava* is becoming — any state of existence, from a human life to the highest formless world. Today's verse says it plainly: no existence is permanent. Even gods who live eighty-four thousand eons pass away when their life ends.

**Why it matters:** We work hard to make one state last: a good mood, a good job, a healthy body. The teaching asks us to see how short a human life is. Then we can drop heedlessness (*pamāda*) and put our effort into the path. A little practice today counts.
