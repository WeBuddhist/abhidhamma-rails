# Day 103 — Feeding It or Seeing It

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The chapter closes today. Two more passes over the four truths, then the question-and-answer section: are they wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate; what objects do they take; and finally the pairs — root cause or not, with a root cause or without.

One line from the teachers is worth keeping. Two of the truths belong to the world and two go beyond it. Suffering and its origin are worldly. The ending and the path are not. And there is a plain test for which side your mind is on right now. When you relish something — chew on it, want more of it — the mind has joined the origin. When you look back at an experience and see it clearly, the mind has joined the path. Same mind, same day, same object. Two directions. The Analysis of the Truths ends here.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Right now — am I feeding this, or seeing it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ASSĀDA — Relishing

**Pronunciation:** *as-sā-da* — "as-SAA-da"; hold the double "s" for a beat.

**In today's passage:** *Assāda* is relishing — the mind chewing on something it enjoys. The teachers use it here to sort the truths. Whatever you relish, your mind is working on the origin side. Whatever you look back at and see plainly, your mind is on the path side.

**Why it matters:** This is a test you can run in a second, with no Abhidhamma at all. Just ask which one you are doing. Both use the same mind and the same object. The only difference is whether you are feeding it or seeing it. Small distinction. It decides a great deal.
