# Daily Notifications

The short push-notification copy that accompanies each day. Filed by day to match `../../<lang>/days/`.

## Per-file structure

```
day-NNN.md
---
day: NNN
language: <tag>
status: draft
---

(headline — short, warm, one line)

(body — one short sentence, anchored to the day's passage, inviting the practitioner to sit down for today's session)
```

## Tone

Warm. Unhurried. Never urgency-driven. Never marketing copy. The notification is the gentle morning bell, not a sales pitch.

## Cadence

One notification per day per language. Plan-close days carry a slightly longer notification that names the plan that has just finished and the one that is about to begin.
