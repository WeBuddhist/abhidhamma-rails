# Aspiration (Closing Dedication)

**IMINĀ PUÑÑAKAMMENA, MĀ ME BĀLA-SAMĀGAMO;**
**SATAṂ SAMĀGAMO HOTU, YĀVA NIBBĀNAPATTIYĀ.**
_By the power of this meritorious deed, may I not suffer the company of unwise people. May I be blessed with the company of wise people, until Deliverance is won._

**IDAṂ ME PUÑÑA KAMMAṂ ĀSAVAKKHAYAṂ VAHAṂ HOTU**
**SABBADUKKHĀ PAMUCCATU NIBBĀNASSA PACCAYO HOTU!**
_May this meritorious deed of mine conduce to the destruction of cankers, and lead to freedom from all suffering; may it be a condition for spiritual Liberation._

**SĀDHU! SĀDHU! SĀDHU!** 🙏
