# Schedule

The 200-day journey, organised into plans of a few days each, mapped against a real-world calendar that ends at the ITCC chanting gathering in Bodhgayā, December 2026.

## Files in this folder

- `calendar.md` — the master day-to-date table. `day-001` … `day-200` mapped to calendar dates, plus the date of each plan boundary and major milestone.
- `milestones.md` — the major moments along the journey: kick-off date, mid-point, the run-up to Bodhgayā, the gathering itself.

The granular plan files — what each plan covers and which day numbers it spans — live in `../plans/`. The `calendar.md` table cross-references each plan by its plan-ID and day-range, so the two views stay in sync.
