# Vandanā (Homage)

**NAMO TASSA BHAGAVATO ARAHATO SAMMĀ SAMBUDDHASSA**
_Homage to him, the Blessed One, the Perfect One, the Supremely Enlightened One._

**BUDDHAṂ VANDĀMI, DHAMMAṂ VANDĀMI, SAṄGHAṂ VANDĀMI, AHAṂ VANDĀMI SABBADĀ.**
_I pay homage to the Buddha, to the Dhamma, to the Saṅgha — always._

**DUTIYAMPI BUDDHAṂ VANDĀMI, DHAMMAṂ VANDĀMI, SAṄGHAṂ VANDĀMI, AHAṂ VANDĀMI SABBADĀ.**
_For the second time, I pay homage to the Buddha, to the Dhamma, to the Saṅgha — always._

**TATIYAMPI BUDDHAṂ VANDĀMI, DHAMMAṂ VANDĀMI, SAṄGHAṂ VANDĀMI, AHAṂ VANDĀMI SABBADĀ.**
_For the third time, I pay homage to the Buddha, to the Dhamma, to the Saṅgha — always._
