# Contemporary English Abhidhamma — Terminology

The locked English rendering of every Pāli technical term used under this brief. Once a row is `complete`, its rendering does not change — changing it means revising every published output that uses it.

This file is the authoritative source for the **English column** of `../../Daily-Tipitaka/terminology.md`; the two files must stay in sync.

## How to add a term

1. Confirm the term has a sense page in `2-RAILS/Local-Wiki/`.
2. Confirm the term has an entry in `2-RAILS/Glossaries/glossary-pi-en.md` with the attested senses.
3. Pick the rendering that fits §1.3 of `brief.md` (preserve form where form carries meaning; naturalise the connective tissue).
4. Add the row below with `status: draft`.
5. After Pāli-reviewer and readability-reviewer sign-off, mark `complete`.
