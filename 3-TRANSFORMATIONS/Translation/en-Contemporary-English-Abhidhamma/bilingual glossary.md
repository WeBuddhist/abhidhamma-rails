## Notes on derivations

### apacayagāmiṃ / upacayagāmiṃ

No attested rendering exactly matches the requirements for this pair. "Reducing" (24) is too terse and loses the accumulation reference. "Leading_to_cessation_of_accumulation" (15) is accurate but too verbose for the formulaic mātikā context. Derived "leading to diminution" / "leading to accumulation" as a parallel pair that is concise, natural, and captures the Abhidhamma meaning precisely — apacaya = diminution of accumulated kamma; upacaya = accumulation of kamma.
