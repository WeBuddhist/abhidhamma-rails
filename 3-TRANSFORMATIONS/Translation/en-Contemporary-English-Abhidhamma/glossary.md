# Translation glossary — en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma

This glossary captures the per-track keyword renderings for the en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma translation track. It is the working glossary the translation skill loads on every run. Keyword selection is governed by `requirements.md` §1.3 (technical terms) and §1.2 (form preservation). Where the consolidated glossary's most-frequent attested rendering meets the requirements, it is used unchanged; where the requirements override the attested rendering, the override is recorded as a `derived` row with the derivation paragraph in **Notes on derivations** below.

This first version is scoped to the Tikamātikā (^1-0a-1 .. ^1-0a-22). Keywords for the remaining triads, dyads, and the body of the Dhammasaṅgaṇī will be added incrementally as translation work advances through the table of contents.

## Notes on derivations

### dhamma

The attested rendering `states` (44× across both English translations in the corpus) is rejected for this track. The requirements at `requirements.md` §1.3 directly mandate `phenomenon` for analytical Abhidhamma contexts:

> `dhamma` is rendered _phenomenon_ in Abhidhamma analytical contexts, _teaching_ in dispensation contexts, _quality_ in mind-state contexts. The first occurrence in any new text carries a footnote that names the three senses and points at `2-RAILS/Local-Wiki/dhamma (phenomenon).md`, `dhamma (teaching).md`, etc.

The Local-Wiki article [[dhamma]] confirms that the relevant sense in the Tikamātikā is *nissattanijjīvatā* ("no-being-no-soul" — mere phenomenal nature), which is exactly the sense `phenomenon` is chosen to capture. The aṭṭhakathā excludes the *pariyatti* (scripture), *hetu* (cause), and *guṇa* (virtue) senses for this context, leaving only the phenomenal-nature sense — and `phenomenon` makes that selection visible in English. `States` flattens the philosophical commitment.

This rendering will be fed back into `2-RAILS/Glossaries/pi-en.md` as a new row with source `en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma` and frequency 0 — until the translation file actually attests it.
