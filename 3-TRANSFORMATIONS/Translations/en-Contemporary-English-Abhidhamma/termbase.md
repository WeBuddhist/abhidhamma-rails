# Translation termbase — en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma

This termbase captures the per-track keyword renderings for the en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma translation track. It is the working bilingual glossary the translation skill loads on every run. Keyword selection is governed by `requirements.md` §1.3 (technical terms) and §1.2 (form preservation). Where Bhikkhu Bodhi's curated or in-text rendering meets the requirements, it is used unchanged; where the requirements override the attested rendering, the override is recorded as a `derived` row with the derivation paragraph in **Notes on derivations** below.

**Pāli keyword set.** Every headword in `2-RAILS/Bilingual-Glossaries/Raw/pi-en-rd.md` is included — Rhys Davids' translation has the broadest Pāli term coverage of any translation in the repo. Headwords are deduplicated against the gloss-file lemmatization (function words, particles, and lemmas with <3 occurrences excluded by the upstream extractor).

**English renderings.** Drawn from Bhikkhu Bodhi (BPS Pariyatti 1993/1999), whose modern English register matches the contemporary-English contract in `requirements.md`. RD's renderings (1900-era Victorian English) are not used as fallbacks.

Origin tiers (see `tier_counts` in frontmatter):

- **`BB curated`** — headword present in BB's Pāli-English back-of-book glossary (`pi-en-bb.md`). Direct match, or normalized match (compound markers `+`/`-` stripped).
- **`BB in-text (BB Ch.N)`** — headword not in BB's curated glossary; harvested by regex from BB's prose paragraphs in `1-SOURCES/References/A comprehensive manual of Abhid - Bikkhu Bodhi.md`. Pattern: `<English phrase> \\(*pāli term*\\)`. The chapter where the gloss appears is tagged. When multiple in-text glosses exist, the most-frequent one is chosen; ties broken by earliest chapter.
- **`derived`** — rendering overridden per `requirements.md`. See **Notes on derivations**.
- **`TODO`** — neither BB curated nor BB in-text harvest produced a rendering. The Pāli term is recorded here so the gap is visible; the Pāli reviewer fills it during the next review pass.

## Notes on derivations

### dhamma

The attested rendering `states` (from RD) is rejected for this track. The requirements at `requirements.md` §1.3 directly mandate `phenomenon` for analytical Abhidhamma contexts:

> `dhamma` is rendered _phenomenon_ in Abhidhamma analytical contexts, _teaching_ in dispensation contexts, _quality_ in mind-state contexts. The first occurrence in any new text carries a footnote that names the three senses and points at `2-RAILS/Local-Wiki/dhamma (phenomenon).md`, `dhamma (teaching).md`, etc.

BB-curated does not list `dhamma` itself as a separate Pāli-English glossary headword — BB treats it as in-text vocabulary (rendered *phenomenon* throughout the manual's prose). The RD attestation flattens the philosophical commitment that *phenomenon* makes visible; the Local-Wiki article [[dhamma]] confirms the *nissattanijjīvatā* sense is the operative one in the Tikamātikā context. This rendering is fed back into `2-RAILS/Bilingual-Glossaries/pi-en.md` as a new row with source `en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma` once the translation file actually attests it.
