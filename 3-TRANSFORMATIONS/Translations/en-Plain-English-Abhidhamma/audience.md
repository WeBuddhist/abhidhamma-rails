## 3. Reading Level and Register

- **Target reading level:** Inferred **8th grade / Flesch-Kincaid ≤ 9**, matching the Daily Tipitaka plan requirement that governs the §1 and §5 prose built on this translation.
- **Sentence length:** Short. The Abhidhamma's threefold and tenfold lists resist this; the translation handles them by keeping each list item minimal rather than by re-flowing the list into prose.
- **Pāli retention:** Technical Pāli is *not* retained in the running translation — English renderings are used throughout (see `termbase.md`). Pāli terms appear only in the Daily Tipitaka layer above this one, always glossed on first use.

## 4. What This Audience Needs

- Structural repetition preserved, because the repetition *is* the teaching method (the commentary's *tayo parivaṭṭa* — three turnings).
- No academic register, no Latinate chains where a short word will do.
- Grammatical completeness — the dropped copulas currently in the threefold lists ("feeling that wholesome") fail this audience specifically, since a second-language reader cannot repair the sentence from context as easily.
